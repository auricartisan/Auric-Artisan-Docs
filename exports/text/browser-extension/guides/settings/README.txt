==============================================================================
SETTINGS
==============================================================================
How to open the extension's Settings view, how it saves, and what each of its
six sections holds.
Area: Browser extension › Settings   Updated: 2026-09-25

Settings is a two-pane view inside the popup, side panel or separate window:
the section names on the left and the controls on the right.

OPEN AND CLOSE IT
=================

  - Choose Settings (the sliders button) in the header, or
  - search for a setting by name and choose it: Settings opens at that section
    with the control focused.

A plain open always starts at Appearance. Move between sections with the arrow
keys, `Home` and `End`. "Saved as you change them": every change is stored the
moment you make it, and open tabs pick up changes without a reload. Press
`Esc` or choose Back to the tools to close Settings.

SECTIONS
========

  Section:       Appearance
  What it holds: Theme, accent colour, popup size, language
  Page:          Appearance [appearance.txt]

  Section:       Pickers
  What it holds: The website pickers: master switch, designed controls, 13
                 types, Never on list
  Page:          Pickers [pickers.txt]

  Section:       Right-click
  What it holds: The custom menu, stepping aside, browser menu items, quick
                 bar, layout, row height, sites, permissions
  Page:          Right-click [right-click.txt]

  Section:       Tools
  What it holds: Copy format, colour names, APCA, history, sound, deep audit,
                 automatic outlines
  Page:          Tools [tools.txt]

  Section:       Data
  What it holds: Storage use, backup and restore, copy and paste settings,
                 clearing and resetting
  Page:          Data [data.txt]

  Section:       Shortcuts
  What it holds: The keyboard map, changing shortcuts in the browser,
                 replaying the tour
  Page:          Shortcuts [shortcuts.txt]
