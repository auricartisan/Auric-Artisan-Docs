==============================================================================
GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Plain-language definitions of the colour, contrast, print and workspace terms
used in the Basic Color Tools and Colour Tools documentation.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

Terms are in alphabetical order.

  Term:    Achromatopsia
  Meaning: Complete absence of colour vision; everything appears in shades of
           grey.

  Term:    ACEScg
  Meaning: A linear, wide-gamut RGB space used in film and visual-effects
           rendering.

  Term:    Adobe RGB (1998)
  Meaning: A wider-gamut RGB space used in photography and print workflows,
           especially for greens and cyans.

  Term:    APCA
  Meaning: Accessible Perceptual Contrast Algorithm, a contrast method
           proposed for WCAG 3 that accounts for which colour is text and
           which is background.

  Term:    APCA Lc
  Meaning: APCA's lightness-contrast value, from about −108 to +106; its size,
           together with font size and weight, predicts readability.

  Term:    Analogous
  Meaning: A harmony of hues next to each other on the colour wheel, typically
           about 30° apart.

  Term:    Black point
  Meaning: The darkest colour a printing process can actually produce, which
           is never a pure black.

  Term:    CCT
  Meaning: Correlated colour temperature, in kelvin; an estimate of how warm
           or cool a light-like colour appears.

  Term:    Chroma
  Meaning: How colourful a colour is compared with a grey of the same
           lightness.

  Term:    Chromaticity
  Meaning: The colour quality of a light apart from its brightness, usually
           given as CIE x and y.

  Term:    CIE 1931 chromaticity diagram
  Meaning: A horseshoe-shaped chart of all colours people can see, used to
           compare gamuts.

  Term:    CIELAB (Lab)
  Meaning: A CIE colour space with lightness (L) and two opponent axes (a
           green–red, b blue–yellow), designed for measuring colour
           difference.

  Term:    CIELuv
  Meaning: A CIE perceptual space often used for lights and displays.

  Term:    CMYK
  Meaning: The four process inks of printing: cyan, magenta, yellow and black
           (key).

  Term:    Colour harmony
  Meaning: A set of hues chosen by their positions on the colour wheel, such
           as complementary or triadic.

  Term:    Colour space
  Meaning: A system of numbers for describing colours, such as sRGB, Lab or
           OKLCH.

  Term:    Colour stop
  Meaning: A colour fixed at a position along a gradient.

  Term:    Complementary
  Meaning: Two hues opposite each other on the colour wheel.

  Term:    Contrast ratio
  Meaning: The WCAG 2.x measure of luminance difference between two colours,
           from 1:1 to 21:1.

  Term:    CVD
  Meaning: Colour-vision deficiency, often called colour blindness.

  Term:    D50, D65
  Meaning: Standard white points: D65 is average daylight (used by sRGB and
           screens); D50 is used in print and for CSS lab() and lch().

  Term:    Deuteranopia
  Meaning: A red–green colour-vision deficiency caused by missing
           green-sensitive cones; the most common type.

  Term:    Display P3
  Meaning: A wide-gamut RGB space used by many modern phones and monitors.

  Term:    Dock
  Meaning: Where a minimised panel is kept, out of view, until you restore it.

  Term:    Dot gain
  Meaning: The darkening of mid-tones in print as ink spreads into the paper.

  Term:    Easing
  Meaning: How the pace of a blend changes along a gradient, such as ease in
           or ease out.

  Term:    ΔE (delta E)
  Meaning: A number for how different two colours look. ΔE 76 is the simple
           Lab distance; ΔE2000 (CIEDE2000) is the more accurate modern
           formula.

  Term:    Focused panel
  Meaning: The panel you selected last; it sits in front and receives keyboard
           shortcuts and Save to Library.

  Term:    Gamut
  Meaning: The range of colours a colour space, screen or printer can
           reproduce.

  Term:    Gamut clipping
  Meaning: Replacing a colour that is outside a gamut with the nearest colour
           inside it.

  Term:    HEX code
  Meaning: A six-digit (or three-digit) hexadecimal way of writing an sRGB
           colour, such as #D3AF37.

  Term:    HSL, HSV, HWB
  Meaning: Cylindrical versions of RGB: hue with saturation and lightness,
           saturation and value, or whiteness and blackness.

  Term:    Hue
  Meaning: The attribute that makes a colour red, yellow, green, blue and so
           on, given as an angle from 0° to 360°.

  Term:    Interpolation
  Meaning: Calculating the colours between two stops; the colour space used
           changes how the middle looks.

  Term:    JzAzBz
  Meaning: A perceptual colour space designed to work for high dynamic range
           as well as normal images.

  Term:    k-means
  Meaning: A clustering method used to find the main colours of an image by
           grouping similar pixels.

  Term:    Level (Basic, Advanced, Both)
  Meaning: The Catalogue filter that shows simpler tools, advanced tools, or
           all of them.

  Term:    Linear RGB
  Meaning: RGB with the gamma curve removed, so the numbers are proportional
           to light energy.

  Term:    Luminance (relative)
  Meaning: How bright a colour is on a 0 (black) to 1 (white) scale, used by
           WCAG contrast.

  Term:    Metamerism
  Meaning: When two colours match under one light and differ under another.

  Term:    Monochromatic
  Meaning: A set of colours with one hue and different lightness.

  Term:    Named colour system
  Meaning: A catalogue of colours with names or codes, such as Pantone, RAL,
           NCS or the CSS named colours.

  Term:    OKLab, OKLCH
  Meaning: A modern perceptual colour space (and its lightness, chroma, hue
           form) where equal steps look roughly equal; supported in CSS.

  Term:    Panel
  Meaning: A movable, resizable window inside the page that holds one tool.

  Term:    Protanopia
  Meaning: A red–green colour-vision deficiency caused by missing
           red-sensitive cones; reds also look darker.

  Term:    Rec.2020
  Meaning: A very wide-gamut RGB space defined for ultra-high-definition and
           HDR video.

  Term:    Role palette
  Meaning: A palette where each colour has a job, such as canvas, ink, primary
           or focus, chosen so pairs meet a contrast target.

  Term:    Saturation
  Meaning: How intense a colour is relative to its brightness, in HSL or HSV
           terms.

  Term:    Soft-proofing
  Meaning: Previewing on screen how a design is likely to look when printed.

  Term:    sRGB
  Meaning: The standard RGB colour space of the web and most screens.

  Term:    Stage
  Meaning: The surface in the Workspace view where panels open.

  Term:    TAC
  Meaning: Total area coverage: the maximum combined percentage of CMYK inks a
           press allows.

  Term:    Tetradic, square
  Meaning: Four hues spaced around the colour wheel; in these tools both use
           four hues 90° apart.

  Term:    Triadic
  Meaning: Three hues 120° apart on the colour wheel.

  Term:    Tritanopia
  Meaning: A rare blue–yellow colour-vision deficiency caused by missing
           blue-sensitive cones.

  Term:    WCAG
  Meaning: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines. Their contrast levels: AA
           needs 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text and interface
           parts; AAA needs 7:1 for normal text.

  Term:    XYZ, xyY
  Meaning: CIE tristimulus values that other colour spaces are built on, and
           the chromaticity-plus-luminance form of them.

  Term:    YCbCr, YUV, YIQ
  Meaning: Luma and colour-difference encodings used in video and broadcast.
