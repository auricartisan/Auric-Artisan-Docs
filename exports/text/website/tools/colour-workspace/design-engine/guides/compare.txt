==============================================================================
COMPARE STYLES AND INDUSTRIES
==============================================================================
Run your brief through every style or every industry at once, sort the
results, and adopt the one you prefer.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

Because a system is generated almost instantly, the tool can build many at
once. The Compare view keeps your brief and varies one thing, the style or the
industry, so you can see every option side by side.

OPEN COMPARE
============

Select Compare every style → at the bottom of The brief. ← Back to the system
returns to the tab you were on.

The heading says what is being compared, for example Healthcare at AAA,
through all ten styles., and the range of scores.

THE CONTROLS
============

  Control:      Vary
  Options:      Style, Industry
  What it does: Varies the style (ten cards) or the industry (one card per
                industry setting, including Auto).

  Control:      Order by
  Options:      Score, Chroma, Listed
  What it does: Sorts by score, by how colourful the primary is, or in the
                list order.

Compare uses your contrast target, density and other overrides, but ignores
your seed colour so that each style or industry shows its own hue.

EACH CARD
=========

  - The name, on screen if it matches your current system, and the score
    (colour-coded).
  - The primary ramp, and chips for primary, accent, success, warning and
    danger.
  - The heading font drawn in its weight, with the body font, type ratio and
    icon family.
  - A sample Continue button and a tile showing the corner and shadow.
  - A footer with the corner style and harmony, audit clean or N failing
    (light and dark together), and Use.

When sorted by score, the highest-scoring card is highlighted.

ADOPT A RESULT
==============

Select Use on a card. Your brief switches to that style or industry, the
system regenerates, and you return to your tab with a message such as Using ….

THINGS TO KNOW
==============

  - The score measures contrast headroom, surviving chroma and text hierarchy,
    not taste; the page says as much under the cards.
  - Compare is built only while the view is open, so it does not slow the rest
    of the tool.
