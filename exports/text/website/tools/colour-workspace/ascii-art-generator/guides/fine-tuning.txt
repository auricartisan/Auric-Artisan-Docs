==============================================================================
FINE TUNING
==============================================================================
Every setting behind the Fine tuning button — character set and custom ramp,
shape, tone, sampling and dithering, edges, type, filters and advanced
options.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

Select Fine tuning at the bottom of the rail to open eight more groups. Each
starts folded; select a group's heading to open it. Every change re-converts
the art a moment later.

Changing anything here clears the highlight on the Look buttons, since the art
no longer matches a named look.

CHARACTER SET
=============

A menu of seven sets, each listed light to dark:

  Set      | Characters                                         | Count
  ---------+----------------------------------------------------+------
  Standard | space `.:-=+*#%@`                                  | 10
  Detailed | space and 69 punctuation marks, letters and digits | 70
  Blocks   | space `░▒▓█`                                       | 5
  Minimal  | space `.:#@`                                       | 5
  Symbols  | space `·•●◉⬤`                                      | 6
  Lines    | space and box-drawing characters `─│┌┐└┘` and more | 23
  Braille  | space and all 255 Braille patterns                 | 256

Custom ramp lets you type your own characters, lightest first and darkest
last, up to 200. It needs at least two characters; with fewer, the set chosen
in the menu is used. Start with a space so the lightest areas stay empty, for
example ` .oO@`.

The Look strip in the rail always shows the set actually in use, custom ramp
included.

SHAPE
=====

Character cells are taller than they are wide, so without correction the art
comes out stretched.

  Control:      Match the output font
  Range:        on or off
  Default:      on
  What it does: Measures the output font and sets Cell aspect to match it.

  Control:      Cell aspect
  Range:        0.2 to 1.2
  Default:      set by the font
  What it does: Width of a cell divided by its height. Lower values give fewer
                rows.

  Control:      Lock row count
  Range:        on or off
  Default:      off
  What it does: Fixes the number of rows instead of working it out from the
                picture.

  Control:      Rows
  Range:        10 to 300
  Default:      50
  What it does: The fixed row count, when locked.

Moving Cell aspect by hand works best with Match the output font off; while it
is on, choosing another output font measures it again.

TONE
====

  Control:      Brightness model
  Range:        Luminosity (Rec.601), Average, Lightness (HSL)
  Default:      Luminosity
  What it does: How a pixel's colour becomes a single brightness. Luminosity
                weights green most, as the eye does.

  Control:      Gamma
  Range:        0.2 to 3.0
  Default:      1.0
  What it does: Above 1 lifts the mid-tones (lighter); below 1 deepens them.

  Control:      Contrast
  Range:        0 to 1
  Default:      0
  What it does: Stretches the picture's contrast before conversion.

  Control:      Saturation
  Range:        0 to 2
  Default:      0
  What it does: Boosts colour before conversion. Mainly affects From image
                colour.

SAMPLING
========

  Control:      Sampling method
  Options:      Area average (SAT), Bilinear, Point / Nearest
  Default:      Area average
  What it does: How the pixels under each cell become one value. Area
                averaging uses every pixel and is the most faithful; point
                sampling is fastest but can shimmer on fine detail.

  Control:      Dithering
  Options:      None, Floyd-Steinberg, Ordered (Bayer)
  Default:      None
  What it does: Spreads the rounding error between neighbouring cells so
                smooth gradients show as texture instead of bands.

  Control:      Bayer matrix
  Options:      Bayer 4×4, Bayer 8×8
  Default:      8×8
  What it does: The pattern size for ordered dithering.

Floyd–Steinberg gives an organic, grainy texture; ordered dithering gives a
regular cross-hatch.

EDGES
=====

  Control:      Sobel glyph selection
  Range:        on or off
  Default:      on
  What it does: Finds edges in the picture and, at strong edges, swaps in a
                thin line character of similar brightness.

  Control:      Edge weight
  Range:        0 to 1
  Default:      0.65
  What it does: How strongly edges steer the choice of character.

The line characters it can use are the backtick, `-`, `/`, `|` and the
backslash, and only those present in the current set. So it has most effect
with Detailed, a little with Standard (which has `-`), and none with Blocks,
Minimal, Symbols, Lines or Braille. The character is chosen by brightness, not
by the direction of the edge.

The Sobel edge field chart in Analysis shows where the edges are.

TYPE
====

  Control:      Output font
  Range:        monospace, Courier New, Consolas, Monaco
  Default:      monospace
  What it does: Font of the HTML, Canvas and SVG renders and of downloads.

  Control:      Size
  Range:        6 to 32 pixels
  Default:      12
  What it does: Character size for the Canvas and SVG renders and downloads.

  Control:      Line height
  Range:        0.8 to 2.0
  Default:      1.0
  What it does: Row spacing.

  Control:      Kerning
  Range:        −2 to 4
  Default:      0
  What it does: Extra space between characters, in pixels.

  Control:      Render mode
  Range:        Text (plain), HTML (color spans), Canvas (rasterised), SVG
                (vector)
  Default:      Text
  What it does: The same choice as the Render chips above the art.

On screen, the Text and HTML renders are scaled to fit the stage whatever size
you choose.

FILTERS
=======

Eight check boxes, applied to the finished characters:

  Filter     | Effect
  -----------+------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Flip X     | Mirrors left to right.
  Flip Y     | Mirrors top to bottom.
  Rotate 90° | Turns the art a quarter turn clockwise.
  Border     | Surrounds the art with `#`.
  Frame      | Surrounds the art with a double-line box.
  Brighten   | Moves each Standard-set character one step towards `@`.
  Darken     | Moves each Standard-set character one step towards a space.
  Noise      | Replaces a share of the characters with random Standard-set characters.

Noise amount (0 to 1, default 0.1) sets that share.

Filters work on the characters, not the picture, so:

  - Brighten, Darken and Noise use the Standard characters; with other sets,
    Brighten and Darken leave characters they do not know unchanged, and Noise
    mixes Standard characters in;
  - the HTML render does not show filters at all, although Copy and the TXT
    download include them;
  - with From image colour, shape-changing filters (Rotate 90°, Border, Frame)
    make the Canvas and SVG renders fall back to one colour, and the flips can
    leave colours in their original places.

ADVANCED
========

  Control:      Thin out
  Range:        0 to 0.95
  Default:      0
  What it does: Drops characters from runs of the same character. Lines become
                shorter, so the picture skews; use it for effect, not to save
                space.

  Control:      Seed
  Range:        a whole number
  Default:      2654435769
  What it does: Fixes the random choices of Noise and the position of the
                ordered-dither pattern, so the same seed gives the same
                result.
