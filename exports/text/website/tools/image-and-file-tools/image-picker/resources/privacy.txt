PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What the Image Picker does with your pictures, and when it uses the network.
Area: Website › Tools › Image and file tools › Image Picker   Updated: 2026-09-25

PICTURES FROM YOUR DEVICE STAY IN YOUR BROWSER
==============================================

  - A picture you drop or choose is read by the page itself and drawn to a
    canvas in your browser. The tool does not upload it or send it anywhere.
  - The extraction, the readouts, the charts and the batch analysis are all
    worked out on your device.
  - Downloads are created in the browser and saved by your browser's normal
    download.
  - The clipboard is written only when you select a copy button or press `H`
    or `J`, or when you click a swatch or pin.

PICTURES FROM A WEB ADDRESS
===========================

When you use Load URL, your browser requests the image from the address you
typed, exactly as it would when you open that address. The site that hosts the
image sees that request. The Image Picker does not send the image anywhere
afterwards.

NOTHING IS KEPT AFTER YOU LEAVE
===============================

The picture, the palette and the pins are not stored. Reset, reloading or
leaving the page clears them. The only thing remembered is whether you closed
the Automate this line, which is kept in your browser.

WHAT AN EXPORT CONTAINS
=======================

The JSON export contains the picture's file name (or the last part of its
address), its sizes, your settings, the palette and your pins. It does not
contain the picture itself.

THE PAGE ITSELF
===============

The Image Picker is a normal page on auricartisan.com. Site-wide features such
as sign-in, preferences and any consent-based services follow the site's own
privacy policy and your cookie choices; see Help
[../../../../../help/README.txt].
