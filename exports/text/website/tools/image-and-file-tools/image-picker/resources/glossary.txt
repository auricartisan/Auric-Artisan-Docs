==============================================================================
IMAGE PICKER GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Definitions of the terms used in the Image Picker.
Area: Website › Tools › Image and file tools › Image Picker   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    CIELAB
  Meaning: A colour space with lightness L* and two colour axes, a* and b*,
           designed so distances track perceived difference

  Term:    Cluster
  Meaning: A group of similar sampled pixels, represented in the palette by
           their average colour

  Term:    Contrast ratio
  Meaning: The WCAG 2 measure of brightness difference between two colours,
           from 1:1 to 21:1

  Term:    CORS
  Meaning: Cross-origin resource sharing: a server's permission for other
           sites to read its images' pixels

  Term:    D65
  Meaning: A standard daylight white at about 6500 K, used here as the white
           for CIELAB

  Term:    Eyedropper
  Meaning: The live readout of the pixel under the pointer on the preview

  Term:    Harmony
  Meaning: A pair of colours whose hues sit at a classic colour-wheel angle

  Term:    Hex
  Meaning: A colour written as `#RRGGBB`, two hexadecimal digits each for red,
           green and blue

  Term:    HSL
  Meaning: Hue (0° to 360°), saturation and lightness (0% to 100%): a
           cylindrical form of RGB

  Term:    k-means++
  Meaning: A clustering method that picks well-spread starting colours, then
           moves each cluster to the average of its nearest pixels

  Term:    LCH
  Meaning: CIELAB written as lightness, chroma (colourfulness) and hue angle

  Term:    Merge threshold
  Meaning: The RGB distance below which two clusters are combined

  Term:    Palette
  Meaning: The extracted colours, sorted by their share of the picture

  Term:    Pin
  Meaning: An exact pixel colour kept by clicking the preview; up to 24

  Term:    Population
  Meaning: A cluster's share of the sampled pixels

  Term:    Quality
  Meaning: How densely the picture is sampled, from Fast to Max detail

  Term:    Relative luminance
  Meaning: The WCAG measure of a colour's brightness, from 0 for black to 1
           for white

  Term:    Sample
  Meaning: One pixel read from the working copy for the extraction

  Term:    Tone band
  Meaning: Shadows, Midtones or Highlights, by relative luminance

  Term:    WCAG
  Meaning: The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, which define the contrast
           ratio and its ratings

  Term:    Working copy
  Meaning: The picture shrunk to fit 800 × 500 pixels, which the tool samples
           and the eyedropper reads
