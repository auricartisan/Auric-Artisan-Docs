==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING THE M-GRADIENT PALETTE
==============================================================================
Symptoms you may meet in the M-Gradient Palette, why they happen, and what to
do.
Area: Website › Tools › Image and file tools › M-Gradient Palette   Updated: 2026-09-25

BUILDING THE RAMP
=================

  Symptom:    That is not a colour. and the hex box reverts
  Why:        The value is not three or six hex digits
  What to do: Type a value such as `F2B134` or `#abc`; eight-digit hex with
              alpha is not accepted

  Symptom:    Some steps have a gold underline
  Why:        Their chroma was reduced to fit sRGB
  What to do: Expected with saturated ends in OKLCH. Choose less saturated
              ends, or accept the boundary colours

  Symptom:    Neighbouring steps are identical
  Why:        The step easing has only five positions
  What to do: Choose another easing, or five steps

  Symptom:    The middle of the ramp is grey or muddy
  Why:        sRGB and linear RGB pass near grey between very different hues
  What to do: Use OKLCH, short hue; see the Spaces tab

  Symptom:    The ramp passes through unexpected hues
  Why:        A long hue route, or HSL, goes round the colour wheel
  What to do: Use a short hue route

  Symptom:    One bar is much taller than the rest
  Why:        A visible jump at that step
  What to do: Try another route or easing; check largest jump

  Symptom:    A preset gave a poor ramp
  Why:        Presets make no claim to be good ramps
  What to do: Adjust the ends, route or easing

THE SPACES TAB
==============

  Symptom:    No row is marked the evenest
  Why:        Two or more rows tie
  What to do: Choose any of the tied rows

  Symptom:    Choosing a row does nothing
  Why:        The table is a comparison, not a control
  What to do: Select that route and easing on the Ramp tab

CONTRAST
========

  Symptom:    APCA Lc is negative
  Why:        The text is lighter than the step
  What to do: Expected: the sign shows polarity; the Good for column uses the
              size

  Symptom:    WCAG passes but APCA says not for text, or the reverse
  Why:        The two measure different things
  What to do: Treat both as evidence; choose steps that satisfy the standard
              you work to

  Symptom:    The simulation changed the colours but not the contrast figures
  Why:        Figures always use the true colours
  What to do: Expected

EXPORTS
=======

  Symptom:    The CSS gradient looks different from the ramp
  Why:        It carries only the two ends and the space, not steps or easing
  What to do: Use the custom properties for exact steps

  Symptom:    The CSS gradient looks like an sRGB blend in one browser
  Why:        That browser does not support interpolation in a named space
  What to do: Use the custom properties, or an SVG

  Symptom:    The SVG's middle differs from the ramp
  Why:        Between stops, the program showing the SVG blends in its own
              space
  What to do: Add more steps so the stops are closer

  Symptom:    The browser refused the clipboard.
  Why:        Clipboard access was blocked
  What to do: Allow clipboard access for the site, or use Save the SVG and
              Save the reading

  Symptom:    This browser will not take text from the clipboard.
  Why:        The browser cannot copy text
  What to do: Use a current browser, or the save buttons

  Symptom:    Pasted custom properties have no effect
  Why:        They were pasted outside a CSS rule
  What to do: Put them inside a rule such as `:root { … }`
