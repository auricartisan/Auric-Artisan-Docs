TABS
====

  Tab:      Ramp
  Contents: Ends, presets, routes, steps, easing, colour-vision view; the
            ramp, the step differences, figures and hue circle

  Tab:      Spaces
  Contents: Thirty route and easing combinations measured; three ramps drawn
            with their least chromatic step

  Tab:      Contrast
  Contents: Text colour; evidence table; every step checked by WCAG 2.2 and
            APCA; samples

  Tab:      Data
  Contents: The 15 entries every figure rests on, filterable by status; the

  Tab:      Export
  Contents: Six copy and save buttons, previews, the size table and the
            reading

  Tab:      Reference
  Contents: Eight sources and the arithmetic table

RAMP TAB — CONTROLS
===================

  Control:      Start swatch and hex box
  What it does: The first colour
  Values:       Any colour; hex as 3 or 6 digits, `#` optional
  Default:      `#0B3D5C`

  Control:      End swatch and hex box
  What it does: The last colour
  Values:       As above
  Default:      `#F2B134`

  Control:      Swap the ends
  What it does: Exchanges start and end
  Values:       —
  Default:      —

  Control:      A starting point
  What it does: Sets both ends to a preset pair
  Values:       25 presets, listed below
  Default:      Choose a pair…

  Control:      Interpolate in
  What it does: The route
  Values:       OKLCH, short hue; OKLCH, long hue; sRGB; Linear RGB; HSL,
                short hue; HSL, long hue
  Default:      OKLCH, short hue

  Control:      Steps
  What it does: Number of colours, ends included
  Values:       2 to 32
  Default:      12

  Control:      Easing
  What it does: Where the steps land
  Values:       linear, ease-in, ease-out, ease-in-out, step
  Default:      linear

  Control:      Show it as seen by
  What it does: Colour-vision view for the display
  Values:       Normal vision, Protan, Deutan, Tritan
  Default:      Normal vision

EASING CURVES
=============

  Easing      | Position of step at fraction t along the ramp
  ------------+---------------------------------------------------------
  linear      | t
  ease-in     | t²
  ease-out    | t × (2 − t)
  ease-in-out | 2t² for the first half; −1 + (4 − 2t) × t for the second
  step        | t rounded to the nearest quarter

RAMP TAB — READOUTS
===================

  Readout: Note above the ramp
  Meaning: Steps, route and easing

  Readout: The ramp
  Meaning: One button per step; select to copy the hex; gold underline when
           chroma was reduced

  Readout: End labels
  Meaning: Hex of the first and last steps

  Readout: ΔE₀₀ between adjacent steps
  Meaning: One bar per gap, with a mean line; stronger colours at 2 and 5

  Readout: mean ΔE₀₀
  Meaning: Average step difference

  Readout: evenness
  Meaning: Standard deviation of the step differences; highlighted below 15%
           of the mean

  Readout: largest jump
  Meaning: Largest step difference and where it is

  Readout: moved to fit sRGB
  Meaning: Steps with reduced chroma; highlighted when above 0

  Readout: Hue circle
  Meaning: Steps in OKLCH hue (angle) and chroma (radius); spokes every 30°;
           amber outline for reduced steps

Figures have two decimal places.

SPACES TAB
==========

  Element:  Note
  Contents: Ends and step count

  Element:  Route table
  Contents: Route, Easing, Mean ΔE₀₀, Min, Max, Evenness, What happens; 30
            rows

  Element:  Footnote
  Contents: The evenest row, and how much the mean and evenness vary

  Element:  The desaturated middle, drawn
  Contents: sRGB, Linear RGB and OKLCH, short hue at linear easing, each with
            evenness and least chromatic step

CONTRAST TAB
============

  Element:  Text colour
  Contents: The colour checked against every step; default `#FFFFFF`

  Element:  Why two figures
  Contents: WCAG 2.2 is a ratio; APCA is perceptual and signed

  Element:  Evidence table
  Contents: Five fixed pairs: Text on background, It printed, APCA gives, Out
            by

  Element:  Your ramp, checked both ways
  Contents: Step, WCAG 2, Rating, APCA Lc, Good for, The old figure

  Element:  Footnote
  Contents: How far the old figure is off on this ramp, and steps it wrongly
            passed at Lc 60

  Element:  Samples
  Contents: "The quick brown fox" on the first, middle and last steps

  Element:  WCAG 2.2 was already right
  Contents: Reference values: 21.00, 4.54, 7.00

WCAG ratings: AAA 7:1 and above, AA 4.5:1, AA large 3:1, fail below. APCA
bands by absolute Lc: any text 90, body text 75, large text 60, headlines only
45, non-text only 30, not for text below.

DATA TAB
========

Status buttons: All (15), Measured (4), Computed (6), This tool's choice (4),
Absent (1). Select an entry to expand it.

  Status:  Measured
  Entries: ΔE₀₀ between adjacent steps, evenness, the route × easing table,
           steps moved to fit sRGB

  Status:  Computed
  Entries: OKLab and OKLCH, the sRGB transfer function, relative luminance,
           the WCAG 2 ratio, APCA Lc, CIELAB

  Status:  This tool's choice
  Entries: How out-of-gamut steps are brought back, the five easings, calling
           a ramp even, the 25 presets

  Status:  Absent
  Entries: Colour-vision deficiency below full severity

What was corrected, and what it printed lists four corrections, each with was
and now: the APCA scale factor, the colour-blindness citation, "six
interpolation spaces", and "chromaticity paths".

EXPORT TAB
==========

  Button                     | Output
  ---------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------
  Copy the CSS gradient      | `linear-gradient` in the route's space, from the two ends
  Copy the custom properties | `--ramp-01` to `--ramp-NN`, one per step
  Copy the hex list          | One hex per line
  Copy the SVG               | 600 × 80 SVG with one stop per step
  Save the SVG               | `ramp.svg`
  Save the reading           | `ramp-reading.json`
