==============================================================================
M-GRADIENT PALETTE GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Definitions of the terms used in the M-Gradient Palette.
Area: Website › Tools › Image and file tools › M-Gradient Palette   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    APCA
  Meaning: Advanced Perceptual Contrast Algorithm; a lightness contrast
           measure in Lc units, signed by polarity

  Term:    Chroma
  Meaning: How colourful a colour is, its distance from grey; in OKLCH, the C
           value

  Term:    CIEDE2000 (ΔE₀₀)
  Meaning: The CIE's standard formula for how different two colours look

  Term:    CIELAB
  Meaning: A colour space of lightness and two colour axes; used here only to
           compute ΔE₀₀

  Term:    Clipping
  Meaning: Forcing each channel into 0 to 255 separately, which also shifts
           hue; not used by this tool

  Term:    Colour-vision deficiency
  Meaning: Reduced ability to tell some colours apart; simulated here as full
           protanopia, deuteranopia or tritanopia

  Term:    Contrast ratio
  Meaning: The WCAG 2 measure of luminance difference, from 1:1 to 21:1

  Term:    Custom property
  Meaning: A CSS variable such as `--ramp-01`, holding one step's colour

  Term:    Deutan
  Meaning: Deuteranopia: no working medium-wavelength (green) cones

  Term:    Easing
  Meaning: A curve that decides where along the route the steps land

  Term:    Evenness
  Meaning: The standard deviation of the differences between neighbouring
           steps; lower is more even

  Term:    Gamut
  Meaning: The range of colours a space or device can show

  Term:    Gamut mapping
  Meaning: Bringing an out-of-range colour back inside, here by reducing
           chroma at fixed lightness and hue

  Term:    HSL
  Meaning: Hue, saturation and lightness: a cylinder built from sRGB

  Term:    Hue route
  Meaning: Short or long way round the hue circle between the two ends

  Term:    Interpolation
  Meaning: Choosing colours part of the way between two others

  Term:    Lc
  Meaning: The unit of APCA lightness contrast

  Term:    Linear RGB
  Meaning: Red, green and blue as amounts of light, without the sRGB curve

  Term:    Long hue
  Meaning: The route round the hue circle that goes the longer way

  Term:    Mean ΔE₀₀
  Meaning: The average difference between neighbouring steps

  Term:    OKLab
  Meaning: A perceptual colour space published by Björn Ottosson in 2020

  Term:    OKLCH
  Meaning: OKLab in polar form: lightness L, chroma C and hue H

  Term:    Protan
  Meaning: Protanopia: no working long-wavelength (red) cones

  Term:    Ramp
  Meaning: A row of steps between two colours

  Term:    Relative luminance
  Meaning: The WCAG measure of brightness, from 0 for black to 1 for white

  Term:    Short hue
  Meaning: The route round the hue circle that goes the shorter way, at most
           180°

  Term:    sRGB
  Meaning: The standard colour space for web and consumer screens

  Term:    Step
  Meaning: One colour of the ramp

  Term:    Tritan
  Meaning: Tritanopia: no working short-wavelength (blue) cones

  Term:    WCAG
  Meaning: The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines; version 2.2 defines the
           contrast ratio used here
