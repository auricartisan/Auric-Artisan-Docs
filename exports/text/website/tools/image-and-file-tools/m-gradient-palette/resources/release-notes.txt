CURRENT VERSION — GRADIENT RAMP
===============================

The tool was rebuilt as the Gradient Ramp workbench, with six tabs: Ramp,
Spaces, Contrast, Data, Export and Reference. The developer reference that
describes this version in the site's documentation library is dated 6 June
2026.

What changed for you:

  - APCA figures are now correct. The earlier contrast readout used the wrong
    scale and left out two parts of the APCA algorithm, so it reported
    contrast about 1.4 times too high: 161.80 for black on white, where APCA
    gives 106.04. Across 2,704 grey-on-grey pairs, 520 cleared its Lc 60 line
    that real APCA fails. If you chose text colours with the earlier version,
    check them again.
  - Both contrast figures side by side. Each step shows the WCAG 2.2 ratio and
    the APCA Lc together, and the old figure for comparison.
  - The colour-vision simulation is correctly attributed to Machado, Oliveira
    and Fernandes (2009); the simulation itself is unchanged.
  - "Six spaces" is now four spaces and six routes. Short and long hue are
    routes in the same space.
  - The hue plot is a circle. A ramp crossing 0° no longer draws a line across
    the whole plot.
  - The route table marks the measured evenest row, and none when rows tie,
    instead of starring every row under a fixed threshold.
  - Steps brought inside sRGB are counted and marked.
  - The reading records the ramp, its measurements and both contrast figures,
    naming the APCA version.

WCAG 2 contrast, CIEDE2000, OKLab and the route comparison were already
correct and are unchanged.
