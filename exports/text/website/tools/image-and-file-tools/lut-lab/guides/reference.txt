TABS
====

  Tab:      Lab
  Contents: Scene, look, .cube file, overlay switch, share link; the before
            and after pair and what the transform moved

  Tab:      Method
  Contents: Five explanations: Sample order, Interpolation, Clipping, Round
            trip, Linear light

  Tab:      Looks
  Contents: A card per look with a test strip and figures; the two withdrawn
            features

  Tab:      Data
  Contents: The 17 entries every figure rests on, filterable by status

  Tab:      Export
  Contents: Kind, size, file details, save and copy buttons, the file preview

  Tab:      Reference
  Contents: The ten sources, filterable by whether they run

LAB TAB — THE SCENE
===================

  Control:      Neutral wedge
  What it does: 16-step grey ramp over red, green and blue ramps, 320 × 180
  Default:      Selected

  Control:      Colour patches
  What it does: 24 flat patches, six hues at four lightness levels
  Default:      —

  Control:      Skin tones
  What it does: Six skin-like tones on a mid-grey surround
  Default:      —

  Control:      Saturation field
  What it does: Hue across, chroma from none at the bottom to strong at the
                top
  Default:      —

  Control:      Drop an image
  What it does: Loads your picture, scaled to fit 480 × 270, as the scene
  Default:      —

  Control:      Back to the drawn scenes
  What it does: Removes your picture and returns to the drawn scenes
  Default:      Shown with your picture

LAB TAB — THE LOOK
==================

  Control: Look menu
  Values:  None, Teal and orange, Bleach bypass, Film soft, Mono contrast,
           Log-ish lift, Reinhard tone-map
  Default: Teal and orange

  Control: Strength
  Values:  0% to 100%, step 1%
  Default: 100%

  Control: Tone-map exposure
  Values:  0.25× to 8×, step 0.05; active only for Reinhard tone-map
  Default: 2.00×

  Control: Note
  Values:  The pair is showing a look this page computes. or The pair is
           showing the loaded file. The looks are paused.
  Default: —

The look menu and Strength are disabled while a loaded file is shown.

LAB TAB — A .CUBE FILE
======================

  Control:      Drop a .cube
  What it does: Loads a dropped `.cube`, or opens a chooser (`.cube`, plain
                text) when selected or on `Enter` or `Space`

  Control:      File card
  What it does: Title, kind, size, domain, reachable, and notes or errors

  Control:      Back to the looks
  What it does: Shows the look again, keeping the file loaded; visible while
                the file is shown

  Control:      Clear the file
  What it does: Removes the file and shows the look

LAB TAB — SHOW
==============

  Control:      What the transform crushed
  What it does: Hatches clipped pixels on the After image: pink for driven to
                white, blue for driven to black
  Default:      On

  Control:      Copy a link to this setting
  What it does: Copies an address that restores the settings
  Default:      —

LAB TAB — READOUTS
==================

  Readout: Label above the pair
  Meaning: The transform shown

  Readout: Before caption
  Meaning: Pixel count of the scene

  Readout: After caption
  Meaning: Share of pixels clipped

  Readout: mean ΔE₀₀
  Meaning: Average CIEDE2000 difference, before to after

  Readout: 95th percentile
  Meaning: Difference that 95% of pixels are at or below

  Readout: largest
  Meaning: Largest difference on any pixel

  Readout: clipped
  Meaning: Share of pixels with a channel driven to 0 or 255; red above 1%

  Readout: the old test said
  Meaning: Share of output pixels with any channel at 0 or 255

  Readout: Hue circle
  Meaning: 72 test colours at L* 60, C* 40: hollow before, filled after, pink
           ring if clipped

  Readout: largest hue shift
  Meaning: Largest hue turn, degrees, signed

  Readout: mean hue shift
  Meaning: Average absolute hue turn, degrees

  Readout: largest L shift*
  Meaning: Largest lightness change, signed

  Readout: clipped on the circle
  Meaning: Test colours clipped, out of 72

  Readout: Caption count
  Meaning: Test colours pulled inside sRGB before the test: 13 of 72

ΔE figures have two decimal places, percentages two, hue and lightness shifts
one.

LOOKS TAB
=========

Each card: name, Stylisation or Transform badge, an 18-colour before and after
strip, a description, mean ΔE₀₀, max hue and clipped on the hue ring, and Show
it on the pair.

  Look              | Kind        | mean ΔE₀₀ on the ring | max hue | clipped
  ------------------+-------------+-----------------------+---------+--------
  Teal and orange   | Stylisation | 1.2                   | +3.5°   | 0/72
  Bleach bypass     | Stylisation | 7.9                   | −12.9°  | 0/72
  Film soft         | Stylisation | 1.9                   | −7.7°   | 0/72
  Mono contrast     | Stylisation | 22.6                  | +177.8° | 0/72
  Log-ish lift      | Stylisation | 16.9                  | −25.2°  | 0/72
  Reinhard tone-map | Transform   | 6.6                   | −25.1°  | 0/72

These are the figures at full strength, and for the tone-map at the default
exposure of 2×. Mono contrast removes colour entirely, so its hue figure has
no useful meaning.

Withdrawn: ACEScct to ACEScg and Export 32-bit float samples, each with why
and what it would take, and a table of the ACEScct decode for nine input
codes.

METHOD TAB
==========

  Section:  Sample order
  Contents: The index order, and the eight rows of a 2 × 2 × 2 identity

  Section:  Interpolation
  Contents: The seven blends of trilinear interpolation; worst identity error
            at sizes 2, 5, 9, 17, 33 and 65

  Section:  Clipping
  Contents: Seven test pixels judged by the old test and the clip test

  Section:  Round trip
  Contents: Write-and-read-back difference at 17 and 33 of the current
            transform; identity accuracy at 16, 17, 18, 33, 52 and 65

  Section:  Linear light
  Contents: The tone-map at six input codes, in linear light and in the old
            placement

DATA TAB
========

Status buttons: All (17), Verbatim (5), Computed (5), Stand-in (4), Absent
(3). Select an entry to expand it.

  Status:  Verbatim
  Entries: The .cube format, sRGB, CIE 1931 observer and CIELAB, CIEDE2000,
           Reinhard tone reproduction

  Status:  Computed
  Entries: Trilinear interpolation, clipping detection, the hue sweep, what a
           transform moves, the export round trip

  Status:  Stand-in
  Entries: The five stylisations, the 8-bit path, the tone-map exposure, the
           drawn scenes

  Status:  Absent
  Entries: ACEScct to ACEScg, 32-bit float export, PQ, BT.2020 and the ACES
           primaries

Loading your own picture changes the scenes entry to Verbatim, naming your
file.

EXPORT TAB
==========

  Control        | Values                                             | Default
  ---------------+----------------------------------------------------+--------
  Kind           | 3D cube, 1D curve                                  | 3D cube
  Size           | 2 to 65, step 1                                    | 33
  rows           | Size³ for 3D, size for 1D                          | 35937
  file size      | Size of the text, in KB                            | —
  file name      | `auric-`name`-`size`.cube`, with `-1d` for a curve | —
  Grid note      | Whether grid points land on 8-bit values           | —
  Save the .cube | Downloads the file                                 | —
  Copy it        | Copies the file text                               | —
  Hue sweep, CSV | Downloads `auric-lut-lab-hue-sweep.csv`            | —
  Report, JSON   | Downloads `auric-lut-lab-report.json`              | —

.CUBE KEYWORDS
==============

  Keyword        | Read               | Written
  ---------------+--------------------+-----------------------------
  `TITLE`        | Yes                | `"Auric Artisan — ` name `"`
  `LUT_3D_SIZE`  | Yes                | For 3D
  `LUT_1D_SIZE`  | Yes                | For 1D
  `DOMAIN_MIN`   | Yes; default 0 0 0 | `0.0 0.0 0.0`
  `DOMAIN_MAX`   | Yes; default 1 1 1 | `1.0 1.0 1.0`
  `#` comments   | Ignored            | Header of about 12 lines
  Other keywords | Ignored            | —

Data rows are written with six decimal places, red index changing fastest.

SHARE LINK PARAMETERS
=====================

  Parameter: `scene`
  Meaning:   Drawn scene
  Values:    `wedge`, `patches`, `skin`, `sweep`

  Parameter: `look`
  Meaning:   Look
  Values:    `none`, `teal-orange`, `bleach-bypass`, `film-soft`,
             `mono-contrast`, `log-lift`, `reinhard`

  Parameter: `s`
  Meaning:   Strength
  Values:    0 to 1

  Parameter: `e`
  Meaning:   Tone-map exposure
  Values:    0.25 to 8

  Parameter: `n`
  Meaning:   Export size
  Values:    2 to 65

  Parameter: `k`
  Meaning:   Export kind
  Values:    `3D`, `1D`

MESSAGES
========

  Message                                | When
  ---------------------------------------+---------------------------------
  Loaded name, with a note count if any  | A `.cube` was read
  Loaded name at width×height            | Your picture was loaded
  That image could not be read           | The picture could not be decoded
  A reading error, such as empty file    | The `.cube` could not be read
  Cleared the loaded file                | Clear the file was selected
  Back to the drawn scenes               | Your picture was removed
  Saved file name                        | A `.cube` was saved
  Copied the .cube / Link copied         | A copy succeeded
  The clipboard refused                  | Clipboard access was blocked
  Saved the hue sweep / Saved the report | A CSV or JSON was saved
