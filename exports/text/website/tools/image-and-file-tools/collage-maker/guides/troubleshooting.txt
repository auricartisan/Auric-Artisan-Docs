==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING THE COLLAGE MAKER
==============================================================================
Symptoms you may meet in the Collage Maker, why they happen, and what to do.
Area: Website › Tools › Image and file tools › Collage Maker   Updated: 2026-09-25

PICTURES WILL NOT LOAD
======================

  Symptom:    Nothing happens after dropping a file
  Why:        The file is not an image type, so it is ignored
  What to do: Convert it to JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF and try again

  Symptom:    Could not read followed by a file name
  Why:        Your browser could not decode that file
  What to do: Save it again as JPG or PNG in another program; HEIC and camera
              RAW files are not opened by every browser

  Symptom:    The page slows down with many pictures
  Why:        Every change is measured from pixels, once per picture for the
              overlap figure
  What to do: Use fewer or smaller pictures, or resize very large photographs
              before adding them

A PICTURE IS IN THE WRONG PLACE OR MISSING
==========================================

  Symptom:    A picture cannot be clicked
  Why:        It is hidden, or another picture is on top of it
  What to do: Select it in the Layers · top first list; use Show or Bring to
              front

  Symptom:    A picture moved partly off the canvas after changing Shape
  Why:        Changing the shape does not move pictures
  What to do: Use Arrange in a grid, Centre, or drag it back; watch the
              outside the canvas card

  Symptom:    Dragging jumps in steps
  Why:        Snap to a 10 px grid is on
  What to do: Clear the switch, or use the arrow keys for 1 px nudges

  Symptom:    Arrow keys scroll the page instead of nudging
  Why:        The canvas does not have keyboard focus
  What to do: Click the canvas once, then use the arrow keys

GRID ARRANGEMENTS
=================

  Symptom:    No arrangement fits at this padding and gap.
  Why:        Padding and gap leave no room for any grid
  What to do: Reduce Padding or Gap on the Studio tab

  Symptom:    A row in the Layout table reads refused
  Why:        That grid's cells come out at zero or less
  What to do: Reduce padding or gap, or choose a grid with fewer columns or
              rows

  Symptom:    Every row shows the same Covered figure
  Why:        No picture is larger than its cell, so each keeps its own size
  What to do: Switch on Enlarge a small picture to fill its cell, or use
              larger pictures

  Symptom:    Pictures overlap after applying a grid
  Why:        Fill the cell, crop scales pictures past their cells without
              trimming them
  What to do: Use Fit inside the cell, or check the Overlap column first

  Symptom:    Rotated pictures became straight
  Why:        Applying an arrangement sets every picture's rotation to 0°
  What to do: Rotate again afterwards, or undo

UNDO AND REDO
=============

  Symptom:    One key press undid two steps
  Why:        With the canvas focused, the shortcut can be handled twice
  What to do: Use the Undo and Redo buttons, or click outside the canvas
              before pressing the shortcut

  Symptom:    `Ctrl` + `Z` does nothing
  Why:        A slider, menu or text box has focus
  What to do: Click an empty part of the page, or use the Undo button

  Symptom:    Early steps can no longer be undone
  Why:        The history keeps 60 steps
  What to do: Save a session file at points you may want to return to

  Symptom:    A picture in that step is no longer loaded.
  Why:        The step refers to a picture whose image the page no longer
              holds
  What to do: Add the picture again

EXPORTS
=======

  Symptom:    The JPEG has dark or unexpected empty areas or corners
  Why:        JPEG cannot store transparency
  What to do: Clear Transparent background before saving a JPEG, or save a PNG

  Symptom:    Part of a picture is missing from the export
  Why:        It lies outside the canvas, or the rounded corners clip it
  What to do: Check outside the canvas; reduce Corner radius

  Symptom:    A picture looks soft in a large export
  Why:        It was scaled above its own size, or the export scale enlarges
              it
  What to do: Use a larger original, or a lower Scale

  Symptom:    The browser could not encode that.
  Why:        The image was too large for the browser, or encoding failed
  What to do: Lower Scale and try again

  Symptom:    Nothing on the canvas yet.
  Why:        There are no pictures
  What to do: Add pictures first

  Symptom:    This browser will not take an image from the clipboard.
  Why:        The browser cannot copy images
  What to do: Use Save the PNG instead

  Symptom:    The browser refused the clipboard.
  Why:        Clipboard permission was blocked
  What to do: Allow clipboard access for the site, or save the file

SESSIONS
========

  Symptom:    That is not a session file this page wrote.
  Why:        The file is not JSON, or lacks the canvas and picture lists
  What to do: Choose a `collage-session.json` saved by this tool

  Symptom:    Loading a session changed only the canvas
  Why:        No pictures were loaded when the session was read
  What to do: Add the pictures, then load the session again

  Symptom:    Pictures took each other's positions
  Why:        They were added in a different order from the original
  What to do: Clear the canvas, add the pictures in their original order, and
              load the session again

  Symptom:    The session reopened without the pictures
  Why:        A session never contains image data
  What to do: Keep the original picture files with the session

STILL STUCK
===========

Reload the page to start fresh; nothing you loaded was stored, so reloading
clears the canvas and history. For help with the site in general, see the help
pages at Help [../../../../../help/README.txt].
