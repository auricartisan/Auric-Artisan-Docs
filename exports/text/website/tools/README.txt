==============================================================================
WEBSITE TOOLS
==============================================================================
Every tool on auricartisan.com, grouped by what it helps you do, with its
address and a link to its documentation.
Area: Website › Tools   Updated: 2026-09-25

Auric Artisan's tools run in your browser at https://auricartisan.com. Most
need no account: what you make is kept in your browser, and you can export it
as files. Each tool below has its own folder of documentation with a quick
start, task guides, a complete reference, troubleshooting and a glossary.

The tools fall into five groups. If you are not sure which one you need, start
with Which tool for which job.

ACCESSIBILITY AND VISION
========================

Check colour contrast, audit whole websites, work through WCAG, and see colour
the way people with different vision do. Group overview: Accessibility and
vision [accessibility-and-vision/README.txt].

  Tool:         Analyzer [accessibility-and-vision/analyzer/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/analyzer/
  What it does: Audits a website's colours, contrast and accessibility, and
                keeps the reports.

  Tool:         Contrast Checker
                [accessibility-and-vision/contrast-checker/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/contrast-checker/
  What it does: Grades a text and background pair with WCAG 2.x ratios and
                APCA.

  Tool:         Accessibility Checklist
                [accessibility-and-vision/accessibility-checklist/README.txt]
  Address:      /resources/accessibility-checklist/
  What it does: 50 WCAG 2.2 checks with the law by jurisdiction and an
                inspector.

  Tool:         Accessibility Compliance
                [accessibility-and-vision/accessibility-compliance/README.txt]
  Address:      /accessibility-compliance/
  What it does: Where this address takes you and what it offers.

  Tool:         Vision Lab
                [accessibility-and-vision/vision-simulation/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/vision-simulation/
  What it does: Shows a colour, palette or image through 78 vision conditions.

  Tool:         Ishihara Plate Generator
                [accessibility-and-vision/ishihara-plate-generator/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/ishihara-plate-generation/
  What it does: Makes, checks and exports Ishihara-style plates.

COLOUR WORKSPACE
================

Build palettes and design systems, convert and study colours, and choose fonts
and icons. Group overview: Colour workspace [colour-workspace/README.txt].

  Tool:         Basic Color Tools
                [colour-workspace/basic-colour-tools/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/basic-tools/
  What it does: A panel workspace of colour tools, plus the site-wide Colour
                Tools.

  Tool:         Color Science Lab
                [colour-workspace/colour-science-lab/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/color-science-lab/
  What it does: Workbench, Compare, Gamut audit and Illuminants views with 45
                panel tools.

  Tool:         Personalization Generator
                [colour-workspace/personalisation-generator/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/personalization-gen/
  What it does: Turns a colour pool and a brief into palettes, type, UI pairs,
                tokens and a brand studio.

  Tool:         Design System Generator
                [colour-workspace/design-engine/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/design-engine/
  What it does: Generates, checks and exports a complete design system.

  Tool:         ASCII Art Generator
                [colour-workspace/ascii-art-generator/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/ascii/
  What it does: Turns a picture or a word into character art.

  Tool:         Font Library [colour-workspace/font-library/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/font/
  What it does: Browse, compare and embed fonts from the Auric font CDN.

  Tool:         Icon Library [colour-workspace/icon-library/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/icon/
  What it does: Browse, customise and export icons from the Auric icon CDN.

COLORIMETRY AND RENDERING
=========================

Light sources, adaptation, gamuts, HDR and tone. Group overview: Colorimetry
and rendering [colorimetry-and-rendering/README.txt].

  Tool:         Illuminants [colorimetry-and-rendering/illuminants/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/general/colorimetry/illuminants/
  What it does: Standard illuminants, their spectra, white points and colour
                temperature.

  Tool:         Chromatic Adaptation
                [colorimetry-and-rendering/chromatic-adaptation/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/general/colorimetry/chromatic-adaptation-dynamic/
  What it does: Adapts colours and images from one white point to another.

  Tool:         Gamut Mapping [colorimetry-and-rendering/gamut-map/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/general/colorimetry/gamut-map/
  What it does: Brings out-of-gamut colours into a target space.

  Tool:         Gamut Lab [colorimetry-and-rendering/gamut-lab/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/general/gamut-and-rendering/gamut/
  What it does: Compares colour spaces on chromaticity diagrams and slices.

  Tool:         HDR Gamut Plot
                [colorimetry-and-rendering/hdr-gamut-plot/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/general/gamut-and-rendering/hdr-gamut-plot/
  What it does: Plots HDR and wide-gamut colour.

  Tool:         Tonal Steps [colorimetry-and-rendering/tonal-steps/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/general/gamut-and-rendering/tonal-steps-tints-shades/
  What it does: Builds tint, tone and shade ramps.

  Tool:         Tone Mapping
                [colorimetry-and-rendering/tone-mapping/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/general/gamut-and-rendering/tone-mapping/
  What it does: Compares tone-mapping operators on HDR frames.

PERCEPTION AND SPECTRAL
=======================

How the eye and brain see colour, psychophysics experiments, and colour from
dyes and materials. Group overview: Perception and spectral
[perception-and-spectral/README.txt].

  Tool:         Visual Pathway
                [perception-and-spectral/neuroscience/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/general/perception-and-neuroscience/neuroscience/
  What it does: Follows a stimulus from the cones to the visual cortex.

  Tool:         Perception and Vision
                [perception-and-spectral/perception-and-vision/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/general/perception-and-neuroscience/perception-vision/
  What it does: Colour-vision simulation over your own image.

  Tool:         Psychophysical Experiment Engine
                [perception-and-spectral/psychophysical-experiment-engine/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/general/perception-and-neuroscience/psychophysical-experiment-engine/
  What it does: Measures thresholds with psychophysical methods.

  Tool:         Dye in Solution
                [perception-and-spectral/industrial-dye-chemistry/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/general/spectral-tools/industrial-dye-chemistry/
  What it does: Dye colour against pH, concentration and path length.

  Tool:         Material Under Light
                [perception-and-spectral/material-lighting/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/general/spectral-tools/material-lighting/
  What it does: Renders materials under chosen light sources.

  Tool:         Collage Maker [image-and-file-tools/collage-maker/README.txt]

  Tool:         ICC Profile Parser
                [image-and-file-tools/icc-profile-parser/README.txt]

  Tool:         Image Picker [image-and-file-tools/image-picker/README.txt]

  Tool:         M-Gradient Palette
                [image-and-file-tools/m-gradient-palette/README.txt]

  Tool:         SVG Recolour [image-and-file-tools/svg-recolour/README.txt]
  Address:      /tool/general/tools/svg-recolor/
  What it does: Maps and replaces the colours in an SVG.

WHICH TOOL FOR WHICH JOB
========================

  You want to…: Check that text is readable on its background
  Use:          Contrast Checker
                [accessibility-and-vision/contrast-checker/README.txt]

  You want to…: Audit a whole website for colour and accessibility
  Use:          Analyzer [accessibility-and-vision/analyzer/README.txt]

  You want to…: Work through WCAG 2.2 success criteria
  Use:          Accessibility Checklist
                [accessibility-and-vision/accessibility-checklist/README.txt]

  You want to…: See your design as people with colour-vision deficiencies do
  Use:          Vision Lab
                [accessibility-and-vision/vision-simulation/README.txt]

  You want to…: Build a palette, shades or a gradient quickly
  Use:          Basic Color Tools
                [colour-workspace/basic-colour-tools/README.txt]

  You want to…: Generate a whole design system with tokens
  Use:          Design System Generator
                [colour-workspace/design-engine/README.txt]

  You want to…: Take colours from a photograph
  Use:          Image Picker [image-and-file-tools/image-picker/README.txt]

  You want to…: Make a perceptually even ramp
  Use:          M-Gradient Palette
                [image-and-file-tools/m-gradient-palette/README.txt] or Tonal
                Steps [colorimetry-and-rendering/tonal-steps/README.txt]

  You want to…: Understand a colour profile file
  Use:          ICC Profile Parser
                [image-and-file-tools/icc-profile-parser/README.txt]

  You want to…: Find a font or icon for a project
  Use:          Font Library [colour-workspace/font-library/README.txt] or
                Icon Library [colour-workspace/icon-library/README.txt]

  You want to…: Learn how light and white points change colour
  Use:          Illuminants [colorimetry-and-rendering/illuminants/README.txt]
                and Chromatic Adaptation
                [colorimetry-and-rendering/chromatic-adaptation/README.txt]

The floating kits [../kits/README.txt] — a calculator, unit converter, notes
and more — and the colour libraries [../library/README.txt] are documented in
their own sections.
