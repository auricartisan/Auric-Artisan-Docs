THE SPECTRUM CSV
================

The file starts with `#` header lines that state everything a reader needs to
compare it with another spectrum:

  - the time; the sampling (380–780 nm at 5 nm, 81 samples); the observer (CIE
    1931 2°, not interpolated); the illuminant;
  - the bath's pH and path length;
  - for each component: name (and "(disabled)" if muted), concentration, pKa,
    both forms' λmax and ε, whether the band shape is MEASURED or SYNTHESISED,
    the base fraction at this pH and the isosbestic point;
  - the peak absorbance and whether it is inside the Beer–Lambert linear
    range;
  - which white point the swatch and the L*a*b* use;
  - the colour inconstancy (ΔE₀₀) to each other illuminant;
  - statements that the file is not a metamerism index, that F11 is not held,
    that it is not a fastness test, and that it holds no hazard data;
  - the tolerance sweep's median and 95th percentile, if you ran one.

Then 81 rows with these columns:

  Column                                   | Meaning
  -----------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------
  `wavelength_nm`                          | 380 to 780 in steps of 5
  `absorbance`                             | The whole bath's absorbance
  `transmittance`                          | 10 to the power of minus the absorbance
  `eps_acid`, `eps_base`                   | The selected dye's two forms, molar absorptivity
  `observer_x`, `observer_y`, `observer_z` | The CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions

THE BATH JSON
=============

  Field:    `tool`, `generated`
  Contents: The tool name and time

  Field:    `sampling`
  Contents: Range, step and number of samples, with a note that nothing is
            interpolated

  Field:    `illuminant`, `illuminant_not_held`
  Contents: The light used, and F11 with why it is not held and what it would
            take

  Field:    `bath`
  Contents: `ph` and `path_cm`

  Field:    `components`
  Contents: For each dye: name, what it stands for, provenance, enabled, pKa,
            concentration in mol/L, base fraction, both forms' λmax and ε,
            isosbestic point

  Field:    `beer_lambert`
  Contents: Peak absorbance, its wavelength, whether it is inside the linear
            range, and the limit

  Field:    `colour`
  Contents: sRGB hex (un-adapted), L*a*b*, the white it was taken against,
            gamut flag, dominant wavelength, purity and note

  Field:    `colour_inconstancy`
  Contents: ΔE₀₀ to each other illuminant, each marked `is_metamerism: false`

  Field:    `sensitivity`
  Contents: The sweep result, or `null`

  Field:    `not_held`
  Contents: Every register entry marked absent, with why and what it would
            take

  Field:    `register`
  Contents: Every register entry with its source and status

  Field:    `spectrum`
  Contents: Wavelengths, absorbance and transmittance arrays

LINKS
=====

A link rebuilds the bath: `ph`, `path`, `ill` (`d65`, `d50`, `a` or `e`) and
`dyes`, a comma-separated list of `id:pKa:concentration-in-µM`, for example:

    https://auricartisan.com/tool/general/spectral-tools/industrial-dye-chemistry/?ph=3.50&path=1.00&ill=d65&dyes=weak-acid:3.50:34.00,anthraquinone-blue:11.80:40.00

Dye identifiers: `weak-acid`, `mid-range`, `high-range`, `azo-red`,
`anthraquinone-blue`, `yellow`. A link does not carry muted state, measured
spectra or sweep tolerances.

You should now have a file that anyone can read without the page, with its
light, observer and data sources stated.
