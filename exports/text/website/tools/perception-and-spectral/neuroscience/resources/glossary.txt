==============================================================================
VISUAL PATHWAY GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Terms used in the Visual Pathway tool, each explained briefly.
Area: Website › Tools › Perception and spectral › Neuroscience   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Alias
  Meaning: A false, coarser pattern that appears when a screen is asked to
           draw stripes finer than its pixels can carry

  Term:    Carrier
  Meaning: What a grating varies: colour at constant L + M (Chromatic) or
           lightness in grey (Luminance)

  Term:    Centre-surround
  Meaning: A receptive field that compares a small central region with a
           larger surrounding one

  Term:    Colourmap
  Meaning: The ramp of colours used to draw a map's values, here Viridis,
           Inferno or Grayscale

  Term:    Complex-cell energy
  Meaning: The combined response of two filters a quarter-cycle apart; it
           responds to stripes regardless of their exact position

  Term:    Cone signals (L, M, S)
  Meaning: The responses of the long-, medium- and short-wavelength-sensitive
           cones to a colour

  Term:    Cone table
  Meaning: A table of cone spectral sensitivities by wavelength; the page's
           built-in one is its own, and you can install another

  Term:    Cycles per degree (c/deg)
  Meaning: How many light-dark cycles of a pattern fall within one degree of
           visual angle

  Term:    Degrees per pixel
  Meaning: The angle one screen pixel covers at your viewing distance:
           atan(pixel pitch ÷ distance)

  Term:    Deuteranopia
  Meaning: Colour vision without M cones

  Term:    Dichromacy
  Meaning: Colour vision with only two of the three cone types working

  Term:    Difference of Gaussians (DoG)
  Meaning: A centre blur minus a weighted surround blur, the standard model of
           a retinal centre-surround cell

  Term:    Divisive normalisation
  Meaning: Dividing a response by the local average response, so each part of
           a map is read relative to its neighbourhood

  Term:    DKL space
  Meaning: The Derrington–Krauskopf–Lennie opponent space, built on cone
           contrast against an adapting white; the tool's axes are not DKL

  Term:    Gabor filter
  Meaning: A sine-wave stripe pattern inside a Gaussian envelope, the standard
           model of a V1 simple cell

  Term:    Hunt-Pointer-Estévez matrix
  Meaning: A published 3 × 3 matrix that converts CIE XYZ to cone signals,
           scaled so D65 white gives equal L, M and S

  Term:    Hypercolumn map
  Meaning: A map of the preferred orientation at each point, drawn here as a
           colour wheel

  Term:    LGN
  Meaning: The lateral geniculate nucleus, the relay between retina and cortex
           where colour is carried in opponent channels

  Term:    Luminance (relative)
  Meaning: The lightness-related quantity Y of CIE XYZ, from 0 for black to 1
           for white; the retina and V1 stages read it

  Term:    Nyquist limit
  Meaning: The finest frequency a sampled grid can carry: half a cycle per
           pixel, converted here to c/deg

  Term:    Opponent channel
  Meaning: A signal formed by comparing cone signals, such as L − M (red
           against green)

  Term:    Pixel pitch
  Meaning: The physical width of one screen pixel, in millimetres

  Term:    Protanopia
  Meaning: Colour vision without L cones

  Term:    RMS contrast
  Meaning: The root-mean-square spread of luminance values across an image

  Term:    Scale bar
  Meaning: The bar under the map giving the minimum and maximum the colours
           stand for

  Term:    Spatial frequency
  Meaning: How finely a pattern repeats across space, here in cycles per
           degree

  Term:    sRGB
  Meaning: The standard colour space of web content, with a defined transfer
           curve and primaries (IEC 61966-2-1)

  Term:    Stand-in
  Meaning: A register status: values chosen by the tool to be plausible, not
           taken from a publication

  Term:    Tritanopia
  Meaning: Colour vision without S cones

  Term:    V1, V2, V4
  Meaning: Areas of visual cortex: V1 analyses orientation and spatial
           frequency, V2 boundaries and texture, V4 colour and form

  Term:    Verbatim, Computed, Absent
  Meaning: Register statuses: taken unchanged from a source; derived from
           verbatim data by stated arithmetic; not held

  Term:    Viewing distance
  Meaning: The distance from your eyes to the screen
