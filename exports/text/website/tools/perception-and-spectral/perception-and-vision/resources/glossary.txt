==============================================================================
PERCEPTION AND VISION GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Terms used in the Perception Vision tool, each explained briefly.
Area: Website › Tools › Perception and spectral › Perception and Vision   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Acuity (Snellen)
  Meaning: Sharpness of vision written as a fraction such as 20/40: the viewer
           sees at 20 feet what a typical eye sees at 40

  Term:    Anomalous trichromacy
  Meaning: Colour vision with all three cone types but one of them shifted,
           giving weaker colour discrimination than normal

  Term:    Bradford transform
  Meaning: A published method for predicting how colours shift when the light
           source changes, used here to relight the scene

  Term:    Cataract
  Meaning: Clouding of the eye's lens, which scatters light and lowers
           contrast

  Term:    CIEDE2000 (ΔE00)
  Meaning: The CIE's current formula for how different two colours look;
           larger is more different

  Term:    Clamped
  Meaning: A colour pushed back inside the sRGB range because the simulation
           produced a value a screen cannot show

  Term:    Cone signals (L, M, S)
  Meaning: The responses of the long-, medium- and short-wavelength-sensitive
           cones

  Term:    Daltonising
  Meaning: Recolouring an image to move information into colour differences a
           dichromat can see

  Term:    Deutan
  Meaning: Relating to the M (medium-wavelength) cones; deuteranopia is the
           absence of M cones

  Term:    Dichromacy
  Meaning: Colour vision with only two working cone types

  Term:    Illuminant (D65, D50, A)
  Meaning: A standard light source: D65 and D50 are daylights, A is tungsten
           (incandescent) light

  Term:    Mesopic vision
  Meaning: Vision in twilight, when both rods and cones contribute

  Term:    Neutral drift
  Meaning: How far the simulation pulls a grey away from grey, in code values

  Term:    Projection
  Meaning: The operation that removes one colour axis, replacing the missing
           cone's signal with a combination of the others

  Term:    Protan
  Meaning: Relating to the L (long-wavelength) cones; protanopia is the
           absence of L cones

  Term:    Relative luminance
  Meaning: The lightness-related quantity Y of CIE XYZ, from 0 (black) to 1
           (white)

  Term:    Rods
  Meaning: Photoreceptors that work in dim light and do not distinguish colour

  Term:    Scotoma
  Meaning: An area of lost vision in the visual field

  Term:    Scotopic vision
  Meaning: Night vision with rods only

  Term:    Severity
  Meaning: How strongly a simulation is applied, from 0 (none) to 1 (full)

  Term:    Spatial simulation
  Meaning: A simulation that depends on neighbouring pixels or position
           (Cataract, Central loss, Acuity)

  Term:    sRGB
  Meaning: The standard colour space of web content, with a defined transfer
           curve and primaries

  Term:    Tritan
  Meaning: Relating to the S (short-wavelength) cones; tritanopia is the
           absence of S cones

  Term:    WebGL2
  Meaning: A browser graphics interface that lets the page compute on the
           graphics card
