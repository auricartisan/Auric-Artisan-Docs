==============================================================================
MATERIAL UNDER LIGHT GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Colour, light and reflectance terms used in the Material Under Light tool,
each explained briefly.
Area: Website › Tools › Perception and spectral › Material Lighting   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Azimuth
  Meaning: The angle between the light and the eye measured around the surface
           normal (Between them)

  Term:    Blackbody (Planckian radiator)
  Meaning: An ideal hot object whose light depends only on its temperature,
           given by Planck's law

  Term:    BRDF (lobe)
  Meaning: A model of how a surface spreads reflected light over directions

  Term:    CIE 1931 2° observer
  Meaning: The standard colour-matching functions describing average human
           colour vision

  Term:    CIE L*a*b*
  Meaning: A colour space with lightness L* and opponent axes a* (red–green)
           and b* (yellow–blue)

  Term:    CIEDE2000 (ΔE₀₀)
  Meaning: The CIE's recommended formula for how different two colours look

  Term:    Conductor
  Meaning: A metal; its colour comes entirely from specular reflection tinted
           by its reflectance

  Term:    Correlated colour temperature (CCT)
  Meaning: The temperature of the blackbody whose colour is closest to a
           light's, in kelvin

  Term:    CRI (colour rendering index)
  Meaning: A CIE score of how faithfully a light renders a set of test
           colours; withdrawn from this tool

  Term:    D-series daylight (D50, D55, D65, D75)
  Meaning: CIE standard daylights reconstructed from a published basis, named
           for their temperature

  Term:    Dielectric
  Meaning: A non-metal; a faint colourless highlight plus coloured diffuse
           reflection

  Term:    Diffuse reflection
  Meaning: Light that enters a material, scatters and leaves in many
           directions, carrying the material's colour

  Term:    Exposure normalisation
  Meaning: Scaling so a perfect white diffuser in the same light and geometry
           reads Y = 1

  Term:    F0
  Meaning: The reflectance of a surface seen straight on, set here by the
           refractive index

  Term:    Illuminant A
  Meaning: The CIE standard for incandescent light: a blackbody at 2856 K

  Term:    Illuminant E
  Meaning: A theoretical light with equal power at every wavelength

  Term:    JzAzBz (Jz)
  Meaning: A colour space for high-dynamic-range signals; Jz is its lightness

  Term:    McCamy's formula
  Meaning: A published approximation for computing CCT from chromaticity

  Term:    Metamerism
  Meaning: Two materials with different spectra matching under one light but
           not under another

  Term:    Microfacet model
  Meaning: A lobe that treats a surface as tiny mirror facets with a spread of
           tilts

  Term:    Reference illuminant
  Meaning: The light a source is compared with: a blackbody below 5000 K, a
           CIE daylight at or above

  Term:    Reflectance spectrum
  Meaning: The fraction of light a material reflects at each wavelength, from
           0 to 1

  Term:    Refractive index (n)
  Meaning: How strongly a material bends light; sets a dielectric's F0

  Term:    Rendering shift
  Meaning: The ΔE₀₀ of one material between a source and its reference light

  Term:    Roughness
  Meaning: How spread out a surface's reflection is; low is glossy, high is
           matte

  Term:    Specular reflection
  Meaning: Mirror-like reflection from the surface itself

  Term:    Spectral power distribution (SPD)
  Meaning: A light's power at each wavelength

  Term:    sRGB
  Meaning: The standard colour space of screens and the web

  Term:    TM-30 (Rf, Rg)
  Meaning: An IES method scoring colour fidelity (Rf) and gamut (Rg);
           withdrawn from this tool

  Term:    Un-adapted swatch
  Meaning: A colour shown as the light actually leaving the surface, without
           compensating for the light's colour
