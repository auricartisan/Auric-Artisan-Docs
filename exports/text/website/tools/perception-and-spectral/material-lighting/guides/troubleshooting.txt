==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING MATERIAL UNDER LIGHT
==============================================================================
Symptoms, causes and fixes for Material Under Light: black samples, disabled
controls, clipping, refused files and links.
Area: Website › Tools › Perception and spectral › Material Lighting   Updated: 2026-09-25

THE SPHERE AND SWATCH ARE BLACK
===============================

Why: you chose Conductor with the Oren-Nayar lobe. A conductor has no diffuse
term, and Oren-Nayar has no specular term, so nothing is reflected.

What to do: choose Dielectric, or a specular lobe such as GGX, Beckmann, Ward
or Ashikhmin-Shirley.

THE TEMPERATURE SLIDER DOES NOTHING OR IS GREYED OUT
====================================================

Why: it is used only by Blackbody at a temperature you choose and Daylight at
a temperature you choose. Every other light has its own fixed definition.

What to do: choose one of those two options.

I SET A DAYLIGHT BELOW 4000 K BUT THE READOUT SAYS 4000 K
=========================================================

Why: the CIE daylight model is defined from 4000 K upwards, so lower values
are raised to 4000 K. The exports record this.

What to do: use Blackbody at a temperature you choose for warmer sources.

"MEASURED TEMPERATURE" IS HIGHLIGHTED AND MENTIONS MCCAMY
=========================================================

Why: above 7000 K the colour-temperature formula is outside its accurate
range; the error band grows to ±139 K and beyond.

What to do: nothing; the reference chosen above 5000 K is a daylight either
way. Treat the temperature as approximate.

"CLIPPED TO SRGB" APPEARS, OR "INSIDE SRGB" READS "NO" OR "THE HIGHLIGHT IS NOT"
==============================================================================

Why: the material's colour, or its highlight, is outside what sRGB can show;
the swatch and sphere show the nearest displayable colour. Saturated materials
such as the default gold, and bright highlights on shiny surfaces, often do
this.

What to do: nothing is wrong; the L*a*b* values are computed before clipping.
Raise Roughness or change the angles to dim the highlight.

MOVING "SEEN FROM" OR "BETWEEN THEM" DOES NOT CHANGE THE SPHERE
===============================================================

Why: the sphere is always drawn seen head-on, lit from Light from. The view
angles set the geometry of the single point the readouts and swatches
describe.

What to do: read the swatch and the readouts, which do change.

THE REFRACTIVE INDEX SLIDER IS GREYED OUT
=========================================

Why: it applies only to dielectrics.

What to do: select Dielectric.

EVERY MATERIAL LOOKS SIMILAR UNDER E OR A HIGH-TEMPERATURE LIGHT
================================================================

Why: the presets are smooth invented curves, and the lobe mixes in a
colourless highlight on dielectrics.

What to do: compare L*a*b* values rather than swatches, or load a measured
reflectance.

MY REFLECTANCE FILE IS REFUSED
==============================

  Message: No two-column numeric rows in that file
  Fix:     Make sure each data line has two numbers: wavelength and
           reflectance

  Message: Not installed: fewer than eight rows
  Fix:     Supply at least eight rows

  Message: Not installed: covers X to Y nm; at least 420 to 680 is needed …
  Fix:     Extend the data to cover 420–680 nm

A file in percent is converted automatically. A file mixing fractions and
percentages is read as percent throughout.

THE MATERIAL MENU SHOWS ONLY MY FILE
====================================

Why: a loaded reflectance replaces the twenty presets until you remove it.

What to do: select Go back to the presets on the Data tab.

A FILE CHOOSER OPENS WHEN I CLICK THE SPECTRUM
==============================================

Why: the plot is also a drop zone for reflectance files; clicking it opens a
chooser.

What to do: cancel it if you did not mean to load a file.

THE SWEEP RESULT IS OUT OF DATE
===============================

Why: the sweep is not rerun when the scene changes.

What to do: select Run the sweep again on the Metrics tab before exporting.

A LINK DID NOT RESTORE MY MEASURED MATERIAL
===========================================

Why: links carry the light, preset material, surface, lobe and angles only.

What to do: load the file again after opening the link.
