==============================================================================
GAMUT MAPPING PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What the Gamut Mapping lab does with your colours and settings.
Area: Website › Tools › Colorimetry and rendering   Updated: 2026-09-25

  - All mapping runs in your browser. Colours, slices, volumes and batch lists
    are computed on your device and not sent anywhere.
  - Exports are created locally and saved through your browser's download.
  - The share link contains the spaces, method, L*, resolution, sampler,
    strength and base colour. Anyone with the link sees those settings.
  - Clipboard. Copy writes the link to your clipboard only when you select it.
  - Save to Library stores a snapshot in your Auric Artisan Library.
  - The page loads the site's shared features like every other page on
    auricartisan.com; see the site's privacy policy.
