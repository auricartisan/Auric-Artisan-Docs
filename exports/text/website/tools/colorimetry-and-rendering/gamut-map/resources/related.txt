TOOLS
=====

  Tool: Gamut Lab [../../gamut-lab/README.txt]
  Why:  13 RGB spaces on the chromaticity diagram, their transfer curves and
        volumes

  Tool: HDR Gamut Plot [../../hdr-gamut-plot/README.txt]
  Why:  Rec.709, P3 and Rec.2020 with HDR transfer curves and perceptual
        planes

  Tool: Chromatic Adaptation [../../chromatic-adaptation/README.txt]
  Why:  Moving colours between white points with eight transforms

  Tool: ICC Profile Parser
        [../../../image-and-file-tools/icc-profile-parser/README.txt]
  Why:  Inspect the ICC profiles whose lookup tables implement rendering
        intents

  Tool: Contrast Checker
        [../../../accessibility-and-vision/contrast-checker/README.txt]
  Why:  Check that a mapped colour still has enough contrast
