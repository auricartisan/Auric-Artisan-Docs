LAB TAB
=======

Controls
--------

  Control:      Working color space
  What it does: Chooses the space shown in the panel and adds it to the
                diagram (up to five drawn)
  Values:       The 13 spaces and Custom
  Default:      sRGB / BT.709

  Control:      Diagram mode
  What it does: How the main canvas draws
  Values:       CIE 1931 xy; CIE 1976 u′v′; 3-D Wireframe (Lab)
  Default:      CIE 1931 xy

  Control:      Standard observer
  What it does: Shown with two options, but only the CIE 1931 2° observer is
                used
  Values:       CIE 1931 2°; CIE 1964 10°
  Default:      CIE 1931 2°

  Control:      Spectral locus
  What it does: Draws the locus
  Values:       On or off
  Default:      On

  Control:      Grid
  What it does: Draws the grid
  Values:       On or off
  Default:      On

  Control:      MacAdam ellipses
  What it does: Draws 24 illustrative ellipses (a stand-in, see Limits)
  Values:       On or off
  Default:      Off

  Control:      λ labels
  What it does: Labels wavelengths on the locus
  Values:       On or off
  Default:      On

  Control:      Show all library spaces
  What it does: Draws every space, including imported ones
  Values:       On or off
  Default:      Off

  Control:      WebGL chromaticity fill
  What it does: Fills the locus with approximate colours using WebGL 2
  Values:       On or off
  Default:      Off

  Control:      Reset view
  What it does: Returns to CIE 1931 xy, default overlays, no probe
  Values:       Button
  Default:      —

  Control:      Export PNG
  What it does: Downloads the diagram as `gamut-<mode>.png`
  Values:       Button
  Default:      —

  Control:      Export SVG
  What it does: Downloads the diagram as `gamut-<mode>.svg`
  Values:       Button
  Default:      —

  Control:      Primaries (xy) R, G, B, W
  What it does: The working space's primaries and white; editable for Custom
  Values:       Numbers, 4 decimals
  Default:      From the space

  Control:      White point XYZ
  What it does: The white as X, Y, Z with Y = 1
  Values:       —
  Default:      From the space

  Control:      Solve from primaries
  What it does: Builds the Custom space from the fields and derives its
                matrices
  Values:       Button
  Default:      —

  Control:      Transfer function
  What it does: Curve shown on the EOTF / OETF canvas
  Values:       sRGB, Display P3, Rec. 2020, PQ (ST 2084), HLG, Adobe RGB (γ
                563/256), ProPhoto (ROMM)
  Default:      sRGB

  Control:      Volume comparison first list
  What it does: Reference space
  Values:       sRGB, Display P3, Rec. 2020, ACEScg
  Default:      sRGB

  Control:      Volume comparison second list
  What it does: Space to compare
  Values:       Active space, sRGB, Display P3, Rec. 2020, ACEScg
  Default:      Active space

  Control:      Compute volume
  What it does: Measures both volumes and their overlap
  Values:       Button
  Default:      —

  Control:      Gamut slice mode
  What it does: Slice space
  Values:       Constant L* (CIELAB); Constant L (OKLab)
  Default:      Constant L*

  Control:      Level
  What it does: Slice lightness
  Values:       0 to 100
  Default:      50

Readouts
--------

  Readout:       Chromaticity diagram
  What it shows: Drawn spaces as triangles, the locus, overlays and the probe
                 marker with its coordinates. Click or drag to probe

  Readout:       Vertex readout
  What it shows: x, y, Y, a chip with sRGB HEX, CIELAB (L*, a*, b*) and a tick
                 or cross per drawn space

  Readout:       RGB ↔ XYZ matrices
  What it shows: RGB → XYZ, XYZ → RGB, Area (xy) and Area % sRGB

  Readout:       EOTF / OETF curve
  What it shows: The chosen transfer curve, its defining document and an
                 equation

  Readout:       Volume metrics
  What it shows: Both volumes (Lab³) with spread and box, overlap of A and of
                 B in u′v′, and Jaccard

  Readout:       Gamut slice — Lab / OKLab
  What it shows: Up to five drawn spaces outlined at the slice level

  Readout:       3-D gamut wireframe
  What it shows: Wireframes of the drawn spaces in CIELAB, at a fixed viewing
                 angle

TRANSFER TAB
============

  Control:      Plot
  What it does: Which curves and axis
  Values:       Relative, 0 to 1; Absolute, cd/m² (PQ, log scale 1 to 10,000)
  Default:      Relative

  Control:      Fidelity
  What it does: Filters the rows
  Values:       All, Exact, Partial, Corrected (with counts)
  Default:      All

Rows give each distinct curve, the spaces that use it, a fidelity tag, the
defining document and, where relevant, what it used to be or why it is
partial.

COMPARE TAB
===========

  Control          | What it does             | Values                        | Default
  -----------------+--------------------------+-------------------------------+-----------
  Measure in       | The share column's plane | u′v′ share; xy share (legacy) | u′v′ share
  Samples (n)      | Halton samples per space | 5,000 to 200,000, step 5,000  | 40k
  Measure them all | Measures every space     | Button                        | —

Table columns: Space, White, Lab volume ± spread, vs sRGB, share.

DATA TAB
========

  Dataset                                | Status
  ---------------------------------------+------------------------------------------
  Primaries and white points, 13 spaces  | Verbatim
  Transfer functions, 10 distinct curves | Computed
  HLG opto-electronic transfer           | Synthesised (stand-in): inverse OETF only
  Colour matching functions, 2°          | Verbatim
  Spectral locus                         | Computed, 380–715 nm
  RGB ↔ XYZ matrices                     | Computed
  Gamut volume in CIELAB                 | Computed, Halton sampling
  MacAdam ellipses                       | Synthesised (stand-in)

Status chips filter the list. Install a table ("Drop a CSV or JSON") does not
load files in the current version.

EXPORT TAB
==========

  Control:      What to take
  What it does: This space, Every space, The comparison, The diagram, A link
                to this view

  Control:      As
  What it does: Formats that fit the choice: JSON; JSON; CSV or JSON; SVG or
                PNG; Link

  Control:      Download (names the file) or Copy the link
  What it does: Saves the file, or copies the link

  Control:      Copy to clipboard
  What it does: Copies text payloads (hidden for PNG and link)

  Control:      Exactly what you will get
  What it does: Previews the payload and its size

  Control:      Bring one in, Add to the library
  What it does: Imports a space from JSON

  Control:      Library table
  What it does: Name, Type (Standard or Custom), White, Transfer, and Load /
                Del

  Choice: This space, JSON
  File:   `gamut-lab-<space>.json`

  Choice: Every space, JSON
  File:   `gamut-lab-spaces.json`

  Choice: The comparison, CSV
  File:   `gamut-comparison.csv` with Name, Area (xy), Area % sRGB, Volume
          (Lab³), Transfer, White Point

  Choice: The comparison, JSON
  File:   `gamut-comparison.json` with area, area %, volume, spread, box, u′v′
          share, transfer and white per drawn space

  Choice: The diagram, SVG
  File:   `gamut-diagram.svg`

  Choice: The diagram, PNG
  File:   `gamut-diagram.png`, at the canvas size

  Choice: A link to this view
  File:   A URL encoding the drawn spaces, diagram mode, overlays and other
          view settings

The comparison covers only the spaces currently drawn on the diagram, not the
whole library. A link longer than about 1,800 characters shows a warning that
some clients truncate long links.

REFERENCE TAB
=============

  Control                                        | What it does
  -----------------------------------------------+-----------------------------------
  Standards, Formulas, Citations, Research notes | Switch sections
  Filter                                         | Searches all four; `Esc` clears it

Standards are grouped as display and broadcast, cinema and VFX, photography
and print, legacy, and colorimetry foundations. Formulas cover xy and u′v′,
matrix derivation, the sRGB, PQ and HLG curves, shoelace area, CIELAB volume
and overlap. Citations list 22 references. Research notes include a computed
table of each space's area in xy and u′v′, % of sRGB and % of the locus.

SAVE TO LIBRARY
===============

The floating Save to Library button stores a snapshot of the diagram in your
Auric Artisan Library.
