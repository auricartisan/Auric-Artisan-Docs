==============================================================================
ANALYZER KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
==============================================================================
The keys the Analyzer responds to, in the URL field, the Sections picker, the
workspace panels and the token meter.
Area: Website › Tools › Accessibility and vision   Updated: 2026-09-25

The Analyzer can be used entirely from the keyboard. `Tab` and `Shift` + `Tab`
move between controls as usual; the keys below are the Analyzer's own.

ANYWHERE ON THE ANALYZER PAGE
=============================

  Keys:         `Ctrl` + `P`
  What they do: Open the Sections picker, in Simple or Advanced; replaces the
                print shortcut on this page

IN THE URL FIELD
================

  Keys    | What they do
  --------+-------------------------------------
  `Enter` | Start the audit, the same as Analyze

IN THE SECTIONS PICKER
======================

  Keys             | What they do
  -----------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------
  Type any text    | Filter the sections by name
  `Up` / `Down`    | Move to the row above or below, as the rows are laid out
  `Left` / `Right` | Move across columns, when the filter field is empty
  `Enter`          | Open the highlighted section (or scroll to it) and close the picker
  `Space`          | Open or close the highlighted section and keep the picker open
  `Esc`            | Close the picker

ON A PANEL
==========

  Keys:         `Left` / `Right` on the panel grip
  What they do: Move the panel one place earlier or later in the workspace

  Keys:         `Enter` or `Space` on Width, Focus, Collapse, Close
  What they do: Use that button

ON THE TOKEN METER
==================

  Keys               | What they do
  -------------------+---------------------
  `Enter` or `Space` | Open the token sheet

DISCLOSURES AND CARDS
=====================

Folding cards and disclosures, such as Why this score?, Cause chain and the
A11y+ cards, open and close with `Enter` or `Space`.

For shortcuts that work across the whole site, see Keyboard shortcuts
[../../../../site-features/keyboard-shortcuts/README.txt].
