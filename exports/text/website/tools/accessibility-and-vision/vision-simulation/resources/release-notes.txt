User-facing history of the Vision Lab, from the public changelog and the
current page.
Area: Website › Tools › Accessibility and vision   Updated: 2026-09-25

Dated entries come from the public changelog at
https://auricartisan.com/library/changelog/. The first section describes the
page as it is now.

CURRENT PAGE (SEPTEMBER 2026)
=============================

  - The lab opens on a Workbench that takes one condition apart, with
    Interface, Palette and Image specimens, a severity dial, a choice of
    Brettel, Viénot or Machado, and measurements of what collapses.
  - Five views sit behind one bar: Workbench, Catalogue, Compare, Palette
    audit and Analytics.
  - Compare is a contact sheet with a normal-vision control tile, ordering by
    impact or severity, and a choice of 3, 4 or 5 columns.
  - Palette audit checks every pair of up to 24 colours under 17 colour
    conditions and suggests lightness fixes.
  - Analytics shows the collection as a matrix of categories against severity,
    with JSON and CSV exports.
  - Floating panels are arranged from a compact Vision Control dock.
  - The medical notice appears once, at the foot of the lab.

V0.9.8 — 2 MAY 2026
===================

  - Vision Simulation moved into the Unified Simulation system as a
    panel-based workflow.

V0.9.6 — 13 APRIL 2026
======================

  - Improved protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia simulation models, with
    revised coefficients validated against published datasets.
  - A multi-model comparison view for side-by-side analysis.
  - Expanded exports for reproducible experiments, with CSV and JSON outputs.
