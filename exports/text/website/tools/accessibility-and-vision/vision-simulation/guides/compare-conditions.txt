==============================================================================
COMPARE CONDITIONS
==============================================================================
Lay one source out under many vision conditions at once on the Compare contact
sheet, and find the condition that costs the most.
Area: Website › Tools › Accessibility and vision   Updated: 2026-09-25

Compare is a contact sheet: the same source shown under several conditions
side by side, each tile measured the same way. Use it to answer "which of
these conditions hurts my design most?"

OPEN THE SHEET
==============

  - Select Compare in the view bar.
  - From the Workbench, select Compare this to add the current condition.
  - Press `C` (outside text fields) to jump to Compare.

THE TILES
=========

The first tile is Normal vision, marked "control", so you always have the
original to compare against. Every other tile shows:

  - the source under that condition;
  - "N pairs lost of 45", the number of reference colour pairs that stop being
    distinguishable;
  - "CTA" and a contrast ratio: the contrast of the mock-up's call-to-action
    button label on its fill, under the condition;
  - the condition's severity score and name;
  - a grip to reorder it ("Reorder … — drag, or use the arrow keys") and × to
    remove it.

Select Add a condition (it says how many are available to choose from) to add
another tile.

THE CONTROLS
============

  Control:          Severity for every tile
  What it does:     One severity for all tiles
  Options or range: 0–100%
  Default:          100%

  Control:          Order
  What it does:     How tiles are sorted
  Options or range: By impact, By severity, As arranged
  Default:          —

  Control:          Columns
  What it does:     Tiles per row
  Options or range: 3, 4, 5
  Default:          —

  Control:          Copy link to this sheet
  What it does:     Copies a link that reopens this sheet
  Options or range: —
  Default:          —

  Control:          Send the worst to the workbench
  What it does:     Opens the most damaging condition in the Workbench
  Options or range: —
  Default:          —

Order and Columns are under Sheet options. A heading names the set, for
example "Colour vision · 8 conditions", and a sentence names the condition
that costs the most, for example "Achromatopsia costs the most: 23 of 45
reference pairs stop being tellable apart."

REORDER WITH THE KEYBOARD
=========================

Focus a tile's grip, then press `Left` or `Up` to move it one place earlier,
`Right` or `Down` to move it later, and `Home` or `End` to send it to the
start or end.

A WAY TO USE IT
===============

  1. Put your design's colours in the Workbench as the base colour, or load
     your screenshot as the Image specimen.
  2. Open Compare and set Order to By impact.
  3. Read the first few tiles: these are the conditions to design for.
  4. Select Send the worst to the workbench to study the worst one in detail.
  5. Select Copy link to this sheet to share the comparison.

The sheet is remembered in this browser between visits.
