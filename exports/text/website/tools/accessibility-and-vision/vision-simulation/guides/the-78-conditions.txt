THE 78 CONDITIONS
==============================================================================
Every condition the Vision Lab simulates, by category, with its kind, severity
score, summary and prevalence as the cards show them.
Area: Website › Tools › Accessibility and vision   Updated: 2026-09-25

The Vision Lab simulates 78 conditions in 11 categories. This page lists them
as the cards show them.

HOW TO READ THE TABLES
======================

  - Severity is an estimated visual-impact score from 1 to 10. It orders the
    collection; it is not a clinical grade and does not rank anyone's sight.
  - Kind is Per-colour (a colour-in, colour-out transform: every colour has an
    exact answer) or Field (an effect on the visual field, such as blur, lost
    regions or glare). The 17 per-colour conditions are the Catalogue's Basic
    level; the 61 field conditions are Advanced.
  - Summary and Prevalence are the short texts on each card, with
    abbreviations such as "/yr" and "HTN" spelled out here. Prevalence figures
    are approximate, and some cards say "Educational" or "N/A" for conditions
    that are teaching aids rather than diagnoses.
