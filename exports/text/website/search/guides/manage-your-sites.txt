==============================================================================
MANAGE YOUR SITES
==============================================================================
How to see every site on your Search Console account, switch between them,
read how the crawler treats a site, and remove one.
Area: Website › Search   Updated: 2026-09-25

Your sites in the Search Console's side rail lists every site on your account.
It stays available even while a site is waiting to be verified, because it is
where you add, prove and remove sites.

THE LIST
========

The heading says how many sites the account has, for example 2 sites on this
account. Removing one takes it off this console, not out of the index.

Each row shows:

  - The site's name and its status, such as verified or pending. The site the
    console is showing is marked open.
  - For a verified site: how many pages are indexed, how many are excluded,
    and when it was last crawled, for example 120 indexed · 8 excluded ·
    crawled 3 hours ago.
  - For an unverified site: not proved yet — nothing is crawled until it is.
  - Open, to make it the site the console shows.
  - Remove, to take it off your console.

You can also switch sites from the switcher in the console header, which
appears when the account has more than one site.

HOW THE CRAWLER TREATS THE OPEN SITE
====================================

Below the list, a card named after the open site shows:

  - The ownership line, for example Ownership proven by file on the site,
    2026-09-20.
  - Last crawl — when, and the HTTP status the site answered with.
  - Pace — how long the crawler waits between requests to the site.
  - robots.txt — Found, with when it was read, or None.
  - Could not be fetched — how many addresses failed, with See which, and why,
    which opens Coverage. This appears only when there are failures.
  - The crawler is being turned away. — shown when the crawler cannot get in
    at all, with the reason.

REMOVE A SITE
=============

  1. Select Remove on the site's row.
  2. A dialog asks Remove this property? and explains what happens:
     - Its pages stay in the index. They were crawled from the open web, they
       keep being crawled, and searching still finds them.
     - You stop seeing them in the console.
     - Your search history for the site is deleted: impressions, clicks and
       positions. It does not come back.
     - You can add the site again later, but you must prove you own it again,
       and the history starts from then.
  3. Select Remove from my console to go ahead, or Keep it to cancel. `Escape`
     or selecting outside the dialog also cancels.

The page then says the site is no longer in your console. Its pages are still
in the index.

KEEP PAGES OUT OF THE INDEX
===========================

Removing a site from the console does not remove its pages from the index. To
keep pages out, tell the crawler on the site itself: disallow them in
robots.txt, or add a noindex robots meta tag or `X-Robots-Tag` header. See
Request a crawl [request-a-crawl.txt].
