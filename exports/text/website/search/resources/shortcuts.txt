==============================================================================
SEARCH SHORTCUTS
==============================================================================
Keyboard shortcuts on the Auric Artisan search page and in the Search Console.
Area: Website › Search   Updated: 2026-09-25

GETTING TO SEARCH
=================

  Keys or action: `Ctrl`+`K`
  What it does:   Opens Spotlight from any page. Choose View all results for
                  your query to open the search page.

  Keys or action: Right-click selected text, Search this site
  What it does:   Searches the site for the selected text in a new tab.

IN THE SEARCH FIELD
===================

  Keys:         `↓` / `↑`
  What it does: Moves through the suggestions, or through the results when no
                suggestions are open. Wraps round at the ends.

  Keys:         `Home` / `End`
  What it does: Jumps to the first or last suggestion or result, once one is
                highlighted. Otherwise they move the text cursor.

  Keys:         `Enter`
  What it does: Opens the highlighted suggestion or result. With nothing
                highlighted, runs the search and saves it as recent.

  Keys:         `Escape`
  What it does: Closes the suggestions. Pressed again, clears the query.

OUTSIDE THE SEARCH FIELD
========================

  Keys      | What it does
  ----------+------------------------------------------------------------------
  `↓` / `↑` | Selects the next or previous result and shows why it ranks there.
  `Enter`   | Opens the selected result.
  `End`     | Goes to the bottom of the page once every result is shown.

These keys do nothing while you are typing in another field, or when `Ctrl`,
`Alt` or a command key is held.

SEARCH CONSOLE
==============

  Keys:         `↓` / `→` and `↑` / `←`
  What it does: Move between sections in the side rail when it has focus.

  Keys:         `Home` / `End`
  What it does: The first or last section in the side rail.

  Keys:         `Enter`
  What it does: Adds the site in Domain or URL prefix, or submits the address
                in Add a page or a sitemap.

  Keys:         `Escape`
  What it does: Closes the inspection panel or the remove dialog.
