==============================================================================
KITS GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Terms used across the kit guides, from launcher and Focus mode to tool
visibility mode.
Area: Website › Kits   Updated: 2026-09-25

Kit — A floating panel that opens over any page of the site and does one job,
such as calculating or finding a page.

Page utilities bar — The launcher bar in the bottom-left corner of every page.
Closed, it is a single round button labelled Page utilities; open, it shows
one labelled launcher per kit.

Launcher — The labelled button in the page utilities bar that opens and closes
one kit, for example Feature or Library.

Hub — The round button that opens and closes the page utilities bar. While the
bar is open it reads Close.

Panel — The floating window a kit opens in.

Title bar — The strip across the top of a kit panel, holding the kit's name
and its controls. You drag it to move the panel.

Modeless — Describes a panel that does not block the page behind it. You can
keep using the page while a modeless kit is open.

Focus mode — A title-bar setting that keeps a kit open when you open another
kit and opens it again on the next page you load. In the Library Kit it is
called Keep open.

Expanded — The larger size a kit takes after you select Resize.

Minimised — A kit shrunk to its title bar (or, for the Feature Kit, to a small
round icon).

Kit theme — A light or dark setting that belongs to one kit and can differ
from the site theme.

Accent colour — The highlight colour chosen in the site settings panel. It
colours the gold details in the kits.

Tool visibility mode — The site-wide setting chosen in the Advanced Kit:
Basic, Advanced, Everything or Custom. It decides which tools the site shows.

Page status — A label such as Beta, Stable or Experimental that says how
finished a page is. The Status Kit shows it.

Universal Library — Everything you have saved from the site's tools, kept in
your browser and shown in the Library Kit.

Saved item — One entry in the Universal Library, such as a palette, a
gradient, a report or an image.

Update notice — The Update available message the Update Kit shows at the top
of the page when a newer release of the site is out.
