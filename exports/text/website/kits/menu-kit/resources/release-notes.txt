THE MENU KIT AS OF 24 SEPTEMBER 2026
====================================

  - Seven tabs (Tools, Design, Analyze, Account, Library, Company, Trust) with
    collapsible, remembered sections.
  - Recommended and Most Used sections at the top of the Tools tab.
  - A search that turns into a ranked results list with quick actions and
    keyboard navigation.
  - A You are here marker on the current page's tile.
  - The site status chip in the title bar.
  - Shared kit controls: Focus mode, per-kit theme, Resize, Minimize, Close,
    and a labelled Menu launcher in the page utilities bar.

Changes not recorded in the public history are not listed here.
