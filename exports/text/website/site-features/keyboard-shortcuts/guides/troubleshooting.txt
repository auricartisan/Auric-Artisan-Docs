==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
==============================================================================
Why a site shortcut might not work, and what to do.
Area: Website › Site features › Keyboard shortcuts   Updated: 2026-09-25

A SHORTCUT DOES NOTHING
=======================

Possible causes and fixes:

  - You are typing in a field. `Alt` letter shortcuts and `?` are ignored in
    text fields. Click an empty part of the page, or press `Esc` to leave the
    field, then try again.
  - The page is still loading. Most panels load a moment after the page. Wait,
    then try again.
  - The feature is not on this page. A few simple pages (for example sign-in
    pages) do not load every panel.
  - Another panel has focus. Some panels use keys for themselves while open;
    close the panel first.

THE BROWSER DID SOMETHING ELSE INSTEAD
======================================

Why: browsers and operating systems reserve some combinations. For example,
`Ctrl` + `Shift` + `D` can bookmark all tabs, `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `A` can
search tabs, and `Cmd` + `M` minimises a window on a Mac. The site claims its
shortcuts where the browser allows it, but some browsers keep certain
combinations for themselves.

What to do: use the on-screen control instead (the navigation dock for split
view, the page utilities bar for kits and Settings).

`?` DOES NOT OPEN THE GUIDE
===========================

Why: on some keyboard layouts, `?` needs a modifier the site does not accept,
such as `Alt Gr`.

What to do: use the Guide button in the header.

`ALT` SHORTCUTS OPEN THE BROWSER'S MENU BAR
===========================================

Why: in some browsers on Windows, pressing and releasing `Alt` alone moves
focus to the menu bar.

What to do: hold `Alt` while pressing the second key; the site's shortcut
takes effect.

`ALT` + `+` DOES NOT ENLARGE TEXT
=================================

Why: on many keyboards `+` needs `Shift`.

What to do: press `Alt` + `=` instead, which does the same.
