==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING COOKIE CONSENT
==============================================================================
Fixes for common problems with the cookie notice and preferences.
Area: Website › Site features › Cookie consent and privacy   Updated: 2026-09-25

THE CONSENT BAR KEEPS COMING BACK
=================================

Why: your choice is kept in this browser's storage. It is lost if the browser
clears site data on exit, if you use a private window, or if storage is
blocked for the site.

What to do: allow auricartisan.com to keep site data, or accept that a private
window asks each time.

I CANNOT FIND THE COOKIE SETTINGS IN THE SETTINGS PANEL
=======================================================

Why: the consent bar mentions Settings, but the controls are on the Cookie
Policy page.

What to do: open https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/#cookies-preferences.

I PRESSED ACCEPT ALL OR REJECT ALL ON THE COOKIE POLICY PAGE AND NOTHING CHANGED
==============================================================================

Why: on that page, those buttons only set the switches.

What to do: select Save Preferences.

MY SETTINGS SURVIVED REJECT ALL
===============================

Why: your preferences (theme, accent, language and the Settings panel's
choices) are kept in your browser's local storage so that the features you use
keep working. Rejecting optional cookies does not reset them.

What to do: use Reset all in the Settings panel, or clear the site's data in
your browser, to remove them.

THE BAR DOES NOT APPEAR ON THE SIGN-IN PAGE
===========================================

Why: the notice is not shown over sign-in and verification pages. It appears
on the next ordinary page.
