==============================================================================
EYE REST GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Terms used in the eye rest guides.
Area: Website › Site features › Eye rest   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Reminder interval
  Meaning: How long a page is open before a reminder appears: 20, 35, 45 or 60
           minutes.

  Term:    Rest
  Meaning: The few seconds (5 to 30) during which the screen dims and invites
           you to look away.

  Term:    Reminder card
  Meaning: The small card that appears when the interval ends: "Time to look
           away".

  Term:    Horizon
  Meaning: The line across the rest screen that shrinks to show how much of
           the rest is left.

  Term:    Dawn
  Meaning: The brightening of the whole screen that marks the end of a rest.

  Term:    Session
  Meaning: The life of a browser tab; Not this session lasts until the tab is
           closed.

  Term:    20-20-20
  Meaning: A common screen habit: every 20 minutes, look about 20 feet away
           for about 20 seconds.
