==============================================================================
EYE REST PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What eye rest stores and sends.
Area: Website › Site features › Eye rest   Updated: 2026-09-25

  - Your on or off choice and your interval are kept in this browser, as part
    of your settings. Turn these off is remembered in this browser too, and
    Not this session in this tab only.
  - Eye rest does not record when you rest, how often you skip, or anything
    about your activity, and it sends nothing over the network.
  - The cookie policy lists the eye rest preference under Functional storage.
