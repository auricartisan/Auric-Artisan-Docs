==============================================================================
GUIDE AND PAGE OUTLINE GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Terms used in the guide and page outline guides.
Area: Website › Site features › Guide and page outline   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Guide
  Meaning: The help panel for the current page, opened with `?`.

  Term:    Guided tour
  Meaning: A step-by-step walk through a page's important areas.

  Term:    Stop
  Meaning: One step of a tour, highlighting one area of the page.

  Term:    Site-wide stops
  Meaning: Tour stops about the header, search, library, theme and footer, the
           same on every page.

  Term:    Recommended path
  Meaning: A short recipe in the guide for a common job.

  Term:    Section rail
  Meaning: The pinned "On this page" row of section links on long company,
           legal and trust pages.

  Term:    Folded section
  Meaning: A section of a legal page that shows only its heading until opened.
