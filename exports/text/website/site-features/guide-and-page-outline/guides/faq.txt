==============================================================================
GUIDE AND PAGE OUTLINE FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers about the guide, tours and section rail.
Area: Website › Site features › Guide and page outline   Updated: 2026-09-25

WILL THE GUIDE POP UP BY ITSELF?
================================

No. It opens only when you press `?` or select a Guide button.

IS THE TOUR THE SAME ON EVERY PAGE?
===================================

No. Each kind of page (home, analyzer, collections, search, workspace,
library, tools, company and policy pages) has its own stops, plus a few
site-wide stops shared by all.

DOES FINISHING ONE PAGE'S TOUR MARK OTHERS AS FINISHED?
=======================================================

No. Progress is kept separately for each page.

WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE THREE OUTLINES?
==================================================

The guide explains the page and tours it. The section rail is the pinned row
of section links on long company, legal and trust pages. The navigation dock's
Page outline lists the headings of any page.

IS THE GUIDE AVAILABLE IN HINDI?
================================

Yes. When the site is in Hindi, the guide and its tours are shown in Hindi.
