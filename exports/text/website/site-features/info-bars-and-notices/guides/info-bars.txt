==============================================================================
THE INFO BARS
==============================================================================
Use the welcome, announcement and quick-links bars, pause them, open their
links and hide them.
Area: Website › Site features › Info bars and notices   Updated: 2026-09-25

THE WELCOME BAR
===============

A single-line bar that shows one message at a time and cross-fades to the next
every six seconds. Its seven messages introduce the platform: colour science,
colour-space tooling, accessibility, vision simulation, palettes, research
tools such as Ishihara plates, and what comes next. Each message links to the
welcome article on the blog. Hover a message for a short tooltip.

THE ANNOUNCEMENT BAR
====================

Also one message at a time, every six seconds. It carries the latest platform
news, for example:

  - "New library updates and workflow improvements are in progress — read the
    roadmap." (to the roadmap article)
  - "Every tool works offline — install Auric Artisan as an app from the
    Download page." (to the Download page)
  - "Early access: accounts, pricing, and sync are rolling out in stages." (to
    the roadmap article)

Highlighted messages are featured news.

THE QUICK-LINKS BAR
===================

A row of cards that scrolls continuously across the page:

  Card          | Subtitle               | Goes to
  --------------+------------------------+--------------------------
  Documentation | Guides & API reference | `/library/documentation/`
  Changelog     | What's new lately      | `/library/changelog/`
  Timeline      | Milestones & history   | `/company/timeline/`
  Blog          | Posts & site news      | `/library/blog/`
  Learn         | Tutorials & resources  | `/library/learn/`

PAUSING AND MOTION
==================

  - Point at the welcome or announcement bar to pause it on the current
    message. It resumes when you move away.
  - Rotation also pauses while the tab is hidden.
  - With Reduce motion on (Settings › Comfort) or your device's reduced-motion
    setting, messages change instantly instead of fading.

HIDE A BAR
==========

Select the ✕ at the end of the bar (Hide this bar (re-enable in Settings)).
The bar is hidden on every page, and its switch in Settings › Highlights turns
off.

  | Note: The tooltip on the ✕ says "Turn it back on in Settings → Comfort". The
  | switches are in the Highlights section.

THE BAR'S RIGHT-CLICK MENU
==========================

Right-click a bar, or a message in it, to open a small menu headed with the
bar's name ("Welcome messages", "Site announcements" or "Site tools"). Under
This bar:

  Button    | What it does
  ----------+---------------------------------------------------------------
  Open      | Goes to the message you right-clicked, or the bar's first link
  Copy link | Copies that link's address
  Hide bar  | Hides this bar everywhere
  Settings  | Opens the Settings panel (on the Comfort section)

`Esc`, a click elsewhere, a scroll or resizing the window closes the menu.
