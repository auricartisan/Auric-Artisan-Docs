==============================================================================
NOTICES AND STATUS MESSAGES
==============================================================================
The hero notice, demo chips, first-visit welcome, loading screen, and the
short status messages the site shows.
Area: Website › Site features › Info bars and notices   Updated: 2026-09-25

HERO NOTICE
===========

A highlighted notice banner in the homepage's opening section. Select its ✕
(Dismiss (re-enable in Settings → Comfort)) to hide it. Switch it back on with
Hero notice in Settings › Highlights.

DEMO CHIPS
==========

Small labels with an information mark that sit above sections showing a live
demo, marking them as "live" or "demo" content. Select the ✕ on any chip (Hide
demo chips (re-enable in Settings → Highlights)) to hide every demo chip on
the site. Switch them back on with Live demo chips in Settings › Highlights.

FIRST-VISIT WELCOME
===================

The first time you open the site in a browser, a short animated welcome plays
over the page for about a second (shorter if your device asks for reduced
motion). Click it, or press `Esc`, `Enter` or `Space`, to skip it. It plays
only once per browser. The cookie consent bar appears after it.

LOADING SCREEN
==============

While a page is loading, a brief screen with the Auric Artisan logo and the
line "Preparing local color tools." may show, with a progress bar. It
disappears when the page is ready.

CONNECTION MESSAGES
===================

A small message appears at the edge of the screen when your connection
changes:

  Message: Offline mode
  Meaning: Your device has lost its connection. The message stays while you
           are offline. Saved pages and tools keep working.

  Message: Back online
  Meaning: The connection has returned.

APP MESSAGES
============

When the site is installed as an app or prepared for offline use, you may see:

  Message: Update ready — "Refresh to use the newest offline cache." (Refresh
           / Later)
  Meaning: A new version of the site is ready

  Message: Saving for offline — N%
  Meaning: An offline download you asked for is in progress

  Message: Full offline app ready
  Meaning: The offline download has finished

  Message: Auric Artisan installed
  Meaning: The app was installed

  Message: N files ready / Could not open file
  Meaning: Files opened with the installed app

These are explained in Download and offline
[../../../download-and-offline/README.txt]. Website updates can also be
announced by the Update Kit; see Kits [../../../kits/README.txt].

CONFIRMATION MESSAGES
=====================

Many actions show a short confirmation that fades after a couple of seconds,
for example "Settings exported", "Page URL copied" or "Theme: Dark". They are
announced to screen readers without moving focus. Each feature's reference
page lists its messages.

NOTIFICATIONS
=============

The site does not send system or push notifications. Everything above appears
on the page itself.
