==============================================================================
SCREEN RECORDER FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers about the screen recorder.
Area: Website › Site features › Screen recorder   Updated: 2026-09-25

ARE MY RECORDINGS UPLOADED?
===========================

No. They are recorded and stored in your browser on this device. They leave it
only when you download them.

ARE RECORDINGS SAVED IN MY LIBRARY?
===================================

No. Recordings have their own list, My recordings, separate from the library
of palettes and assets.

CAN I RECORD OTHER WEBSITES OR APPS?
====================================

Yes, with Screen or window: your browser lets you pick any screen, window or
tab to share.

WHY MP4 IN ONE BROWSER AND WEBM IN ANOTHER?
===========================================

The recorder uses MP4 where the browser can record it, because it plays almost
everywhere. Otherwise it records WebM. The dialog tells you which.

CAN I KEEP RECORDING WHILE I MOVE TO ANOTHER PAGE ON THE SITE?
==============================================================

No. Leaving or reloading the page ends the recording; the browser asks you to
confirm first. Record with Screen or window if you need to move between pages,
and pick the browser window.

DOES THE CUSTOM CURSOR APPEAR IN RECORDINGS?
============================================

Yes, when you record the page itself. Switch it off in Settings › Comfort for
a clean system pointer, and use Highlight the cursor if you want the pointer
emphasised.
