==============================================================================
HEADER AND NAVIGATION PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What the header, drawer and page utilities bar remember, and what they send.
Area: Website › Site features › Header and navigation   Updated: 2026-09-25

  - The header's menus and the drawer do not store anything themselves.
  - The Theme and Language switches in the header and drawer store your choice
    in this browser, the same way the Settings panel does.
  - The header checks whether you are signed in, so it can show your account
    menu instead of Sign in. For visitors who are not signed in, no account
    request is made.
  - The page utilities bar does not store whether it was open.
  - The footer's social links open other websites (Product Hunt, LinkedIn,
    GitHub, X) in a new tab only when you select them. Explore our portfolio,
    LEACB opens the studio's own portfolio site.
