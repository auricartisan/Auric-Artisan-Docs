==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING THE HEADER AND NAVIGATION
==============================================================================
Fixes for common problems with the header, the drawer, the page utilities bar
and the footer.
Area: Website › Site features › Header and navigation   Updated: 2026-09-25

THE HEADER SHOWS A MENU BUTTON INSTEAD OF COMPANY, TOOLS AND LEARN
==================================================================

Why: your window is narrower than 960 pixels, or your browser is zoomed in far
enough to make the page that narrow.

What to do: use the drawer, or widen the window or zoom out (`Ctrl` + `-`) to
see the full header.

A HEADER MENU CLOSED BEFORE I COULD CLICK AN ENTRY
==================================================

Why: menus close when keyboard focus or a click moves outside them.

What to do: open the menu again and select the entry directly. On a touch
screen, use the drawer, whose sections stay open until you close them.

I CANNOT SEE THE BOTTOM OF A LONG MENU
======================================

Why: tall menus scroll inside themselves.

What to do: scroll inside the menu. The SCROLL DOWN hint shows while more
entries are below.

THE PAGE DOES NOT SCROLL WHILE THE DRAWER IS OPEN
=================================================

Why: this is intended, so that swiping inside the drawer does not move the
page behind it.

What to do: close the drawer with the menu button, `Esc`, or a tap on the
dimmed area.

THE FLOATING BUTTONS DISAPPEARED
================================

Why: the page utilities button and other floating buttons are hidden while the
mobile drawer, the screen saver or an eye rest is showing.

What to do: close the drawer, move the mouse or press a key to leave the
screen saver, or finish or end the eye rest.

THE THEME BUTTONS IN THE DRAWER DO NOT MATCH WHAT I SEE
=======================================================

Why: the drawer shows the stored choice: Auto follows your device, so a dark
page can show Auto selected.

What to do: select Light or Dark to fix the theme regardless of your device.

THE LIBRARY BUTTON DOES NOTHING
===============================

Why: the library panel loads a moment after the page.

What to do: wait until the page has finished loading and select it again, or
use `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `L`.
