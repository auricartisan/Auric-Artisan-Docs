==============================================================================
NAVIGATION DOCK REFERENCE
==============================================================================
Every control of the navigation dock with its label, action and availability.
Area: Website › Site features › Navigation dock   Updated: 2026-09-25

CONTROLS
========

  Control:         Grip
  Label (tooltip): Drag to move
  Action:          Drag vertically; drag across the middle to change side
  Phone:           Hidden

  Control:         Back
  Label (tooltip): Back
  Action:          Browser history back
  Phone:           Shown

  Control:         Forward
  Label (tooltip): Forward
  Action:          Browser history forward
  Phone:           Shown

  Control:         Reload
  Label (tooltip): Reload
  Action:          Reloads the page
  Phone:           Shown

  Control:         Outline
  Label (tooltip): Page outline
  Action:          Opens or closes On this page
  Phone:           Shown

  Control:         Split view
  Label (tooltip): Open split view
  Action:          Opens split view
  Phone:           Shown

  Control:         Record
  Label (tooltip): Record screen (Alt+Shift+R)
  Action:          Opens the recorder setup
  Phone:           Shown

  Control:         Copy
  Label (tooltip): Copy link
  Action:          Copies the current address; shows a tick
  Phone:           Shown

  Control:         Share
  Label (tooltip): Share
  Action:          Share sheet, or copies the address and shows a tick
  Phone:           Shown

  Control:         Top
  Label (tooltip): Scroll to top
  Action:          Smooth scroll to the top; ring shows progress
  Phone:           Shown

  Control:         Bottom
  Label (tooltip): Scroll to bottom
  Action:          Smooth scroll to the end
  Phone:           Shown

  Control:         Flip
  Label (tooltip): Flip dock side
  Action:          Moves the dock to the other edge
  Phone:           Hidden

  Control:         Collapsed line or button
  Label (tooltip): Show navigation (Navigation)
  Action:          Opens the dock
  Phone:           Button on some phones

Back and Forward are greyed out when there is no page in that direction, in
browsers that report this (such as Chrome and Edge). In other browsers they
are always available.

STATES AND SIZES
================

  State                           | Appearance
  --------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------
  Open (desktop)                  | Vertical pill of buttons, 12 pixels from the edge
  Collapsed (desktop)             | Thin gold line, 3 by 64 pixels, 4 pixels from the edge
  Open (700 px and narrower)      | Horizontal bar centred at the bottom, scrolls sideways
  Collapsed (700 px and narrower) | 44-pixel round button, or hidden on the narrowest screens

REMEMBERED SETTINGS
===================

  Setting | Values                   | Default
  --------+--------------------------+-------------
  Side    | Left, right              | Left
  Height  | 10% to 90% of the window | 50% (middle)

Both are kept in this browser and apply on every page.

TIMINGS
=======

  Behaviour                                          | Time
  ---------------------------------------------------+------------------
  Stays open after a page loads (desktop)            | About 1.6 seconds
  Collapses after the pointer leaves                 | About 0.5 seconds
  Closes after opening from its round button (phone) | About 5 seconds
  Copied tick shows for                              | About 1 second
