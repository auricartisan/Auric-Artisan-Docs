==============================================================================
NAVIGATION DOCK FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers about the navigation dock.
Area: Website › Site features › Navigation dock   Updated: 2026-09-25

CAN I TURN THE DOCK OFF?
========================

There is no switch to remove it. It collapses to a thin line when you are not
using it, and you can move it wherever it is least in the way.

CAN I KEEP THE DOCK OPEN ALL THE TIME?
======================================

No. On desktop it opens when you point at it or tab into it, and collapses
when you move away.

DOES THE DOCK WORK IN THE INSTALLED APP?
========================================

Yes. It is especially useful there, where the browser's own Back, Forward and
Reload buttons may not be visible.

IS THE DOCK THE SAME AS THE HEADER MENUS?
=========================================

No. The header takes you to other pages; the dock moves you around the page
you are on and through your history.

WHY IS THERE NO DOCK INSIDE SPLIT VIEW?
=======================================

Each pane of split view has its own Back, Forward and Reload buttons, so the
dock stays out of the panes.
