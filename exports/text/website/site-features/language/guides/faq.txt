LANGUAGE FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers about English and Hindi on Auric Artisan.
Area: Website › Site features › Language   Updated: 2026-09-25

WHICH LANGUAGES ARE AVAILABLE?
==============================

English and Hindi.

IS THE HINDI VERSION A SEPARATE WEBSITE?
========================================

No. Every page has one address, and the same page is shown in the language you
chose.

DOES SWITCHING LANGUAGE CHANGE MY OTHER SETTINGS?
=================================================

No. Theme, accent, text size and your other settings stay as they are.

DOES MY ACCOUNT REMEMBER THE LANGUAGE?
======================================

The language is kept in your browser, not in your account. Choose it once on
each browser you use, or open a link with `?lang=hi`.

WHY ARE SOME BRAND NAMES WRITTEN IN DEVANAGARI?
===============================================

The site writes its own brand and product names in Devanagari so that Hindi
pages read in one script. Names of other companies and tools that you would
type into a search engine stay in Latin letters.

CAN I READ AN ARTICLE IN HINDI AND A TOOL IN ENGLISH?
=====================================================

Not at the same time. The language applies to the whole site in this browser.
