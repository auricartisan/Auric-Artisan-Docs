==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING LANGUAGES
==============================================================================
Fixes for common problems when using the site in Hindi.
Area: Website › Site features › Language   Updated: 2026-09-25

THE PAGE STAYS IN ENGLISH AFTER I CHOOSE HINDI
==============================================

Why: the Hindi text is downloaded when you switch. On a slow or broken
connection it may not arrive.

What to do: check your connection and reload the page; the site remembers
Hindi and translates again on load. In the installed app offline, Hindi is
available only if you prepared the offline version while the site was in
Hindi.

SOME WORDS ARE STILL IN ENGLISH
===============================

Why: some names and terms are kept in English on purpose (see What is
translated [what-is-translated.txt]), and a few messages made up of several
live values are not yet translated.

SWITCHING TO ENGLISH RELOADED THE PAGE AND I LOST WHAT I TYPED
==============================================================

Why: returning to English needs a reload.

What to do: before switching back, finish or copy your text. Many tool pages
keep their inputs in the page address, so a reload restores them (see
Shareable links [../../shareable-links/README.txt]).

DEVANAGARI TEXT LOOKS LIKE BOXES OR A PLAIN FONT
================================================

Why: your device has no Devanagari font, or not one of those the site asks
for.

What to do: install a Devanagari font such as Noto Sans Devanagari, or add the
Hindi language pack in your operating system's language settings.

THE SITE OPENED IN HINDI ALTHOUGH I NEVER CHOSE IT
==================================================

Why: you followed a link that ended in `?lang=hi`, which switches the site to
Hindi and remembers it.

What to do: select the globe button, or choose English in Settings.

SEARCH DOES NOT FIND ANYTHING WHEN I TYPE IN HINDI
==================================================

Why: page search matches the English text of pages.

What to do: search with English words. Spotlight commands can be found in
either language.
