==============================================================================
SETTINGS PANEL FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers to common questions about the Settings panel.
Area: Website › Site features › Settings panel   Updated: 2026-09-25

DO I NEED AN ACCOUNT TO USE SETTINGS?
=====================================

No. Every setting works signed out and is kept in this browser. The only
exception is the Ads section, whose controls need a paid plan.

DO MY SETTINGS FOLLOW ME TO ANOTHER DEVICE?
===========================================

Not automatically. Export them with Export and load the file on the other
device with Import.

DOES THE ACCENT COLOUR CHANGE THE COLOURS INSIDE THE TOOLS?
===========================================================

No. The accent recolours the site's interface: links, buttons, focus rings,
tabs, kit launchers and tooltip borders. Colours you are working with in a
tool, and colours shown in libraries, are not changed.

WHY IS THE DEFAULT GOLD DARKER IN THE LIGHT THEME?
==================================================

Plain gold on a near-white page has too little contrast to read comfortably,
so the light theme uses a darker gold for the default accent. Colours you pick
yourself are used as chosen. The Accent accessibility readout shows how your
accent performs.

WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN RESTORE SECTION DEFAULTS AND RESET ALL?
======================================================================

Restore section defaults resets only the section you are in. Reset all resets
every section after asking you to confirm. Neither removes your saved colours.

IS LANGUAGE INCLUDED IN AN EXPORTED SETTINGS FILE?
==================================================

No. The language is kept separately. Choose it again on the new browser, or
open any page with `?lang=hi` added to its address (see Language
[../../language/README.txt]).

CAN I OPEN THE PANEL ON A SPECIFIC SECTION?
===========================================

Yes, from a few places: the right-click menu's Keyboard shortcuts opens
Shortcuts, the clock strip's All settings opens Clock, and an info bar's
Settings opens Comfort.

DOES REDUCE MOTION AFFECT THE CUSTOM CURSOR?
============================================

No. The custom cursor follows your operating system's reduced-motion
preference, not this switch. To remove it, switch off Custom cursor in
Comfort.

WHY DOES THE PANEL LOOK DIFFERENT ON MY PHONE?
==============================================

On screens narrower than about 900 pixels, the section list becomes a strip
across the top of the panel that you scroll sideways, and the control counts
are hidden. All the same settings are there.
