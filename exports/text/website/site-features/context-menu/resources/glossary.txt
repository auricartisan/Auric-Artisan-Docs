==============================================================================
RIGHT-CLICK MENU GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Terms used by the right-click menu and its guides.
Area: Website › Site features › Right-click menu   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Context
  Meaning: The thing you right-clicked: a page, link, image, selection or
           colour.

  Term:    Context menu
  Meaning: A menu that opens on right-click and offers actions for what is
           under the pointer.

  Term:    Quick bar
  Meaning: The row of one-click buttons at the top of the menu. Holds up to
           six pinned actions.

  Term:    Pin
  Meaning: To add an action to the quick bar with the star on its row.

  Term:    Group
  Meaning: A set of related actions (Page, Colour tools, View, Site) shown as
           one row that opens a submenu.

  Term:    Submenu
  Meaning: The small panel that opens beside a group row, listing that group's
           actions.

  Term:    Filter
  Meaning: The field that narrows the menu to actions whose names match what
           you type.

  Term:    Focused layout
  Meaning: The default layout: your context's actions, then one row per group.

  Term:    Everything layout
  Meaning: A layout that lists every group's actions in full, with no
           submenus.

  Term:    Markdown link
  Meaning: Text written as `[label](address)`, which many editors turn into a
           link.

  Term:    HEX, RGB, HSL
  Meaning: Common ways of writing a colour: a hex code, red-green-blue values,
           or hue-saturation-lightness.

  Term:    OKLCH
  Meaning: A way of writing colour as lightness, chroma and hue that follows
           human perception closely.

  Term:    Alt text
  Meaning: The text alternative of an image, read by screen readers and shown
           when the image cannot load.

  Term:    Share sheet
  Meaning: Your device's own panel for sending a link to an app or contact.
