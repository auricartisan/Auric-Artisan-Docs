==============================================================================
MENU PREFERENCES AND TURNING IT OFF
==============================================================================
Change the right-click menu's layout and row height, and make your browser's
own menu the default.
Area: Website › Site features › Right-click menu   Updated: 2026-09-25

The gear button at the bottom right of the menu, Customise this menu, replaces
the action list with the menu's preferences. Every change is saved as soon as
you make it; the footer confirms "Saved". Select Done, the gear again, or
press `Esc` to return to the actions. Typing in the filter field also returns
to the actions.

QUICK BAR
=========

Shows your pinned actions as chips, each with an X to unpin it, and a Reset
chip that restores the default five. See Quick bar and pinning
[quick-bar-and-pinning.txt].

LAYOUT
======

  Option:                Focused (default)
  Description on screen: What you clicked, then one row per group
  What it looks like:    Your context's actions, then a More section with one
                         row per group that opens a submenu

  Option:                Everything
  Description on screen: Every group open in one list
  What it looks like:    Every group's actions listed in full, each under its
                         own heading, with no submenus

Choose Everything if you prefer to see all actions at once and do not mind a
longer menu.

ROW HEIGHT
==========

  Option             | Effect
  -------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------
  Standard (default) | Comfortable rows, easier to hit with a pointer or a finger
  Compact            | Shorter rows, so more actions fit in the same space

RIGHT-CLICK ON THIS SITE
========================

This choice decides which menu a plain right-click opens.

  Option              | Description on screen
  --------------------+----------------------------------
  This menu (default) | Hold Shift for the browser’s menu
  The browser’s menu  | Hold Shift for this one

Make your browser's menu the default
------------------------------------

  1. Right-click the page and select the gear.
  2. Under Right-click on this site, select The browser’s menu.
  3. The footer confirms "Right-click now opens the browser’s menu".

From now on a plain right-click opens your browser's menu everywhere on the
site. To reach the site menu, hold `Shift` while you right-click. You can also
rest your pointer on the thin line at the right edge of the window.

Switch the site menu back on
----------------------------

  1. Hold `Shift` and right-click the page. The site menu opens.
  2. Select the gear, then This menu under Right-click on this site.

ONE-OFF USE OF THE BROWSER'S MENU
=================================

Without changing any preference:

  - hold `Shift` while you right-click; or
  - choose Browser’s own menu at the foot of the site menu, then right-click
    again.

Right-clicking in a text field, drop-down or editable area always opens the
browser's menu.

WHERE PREFERENCES ARE KEPT
==========================

Your layout, row height, right-click choice and quick-bar pins are kept in
this browser and apply on every page of the site. They are not part of the
Settings panel's export file.
