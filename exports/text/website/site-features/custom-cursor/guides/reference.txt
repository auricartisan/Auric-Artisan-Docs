==============================================================================
CUSTOM CURSOR REFERENCE
==============================================================================
Every shape and effect of the custom cursor, and where each appears.
Area: Website › Site features › Custom cursor   Updated: 2026-09-25

SHAPES
======

  Shape:        Default
  Looks like:   Round ring with a dot
  Appears over: Ordinary page areas

  Shape:        Pointer
  Looks like:   Larger ring with a soft glow
  Appears over: Links, buttons and other clickable items

  Shape:        Text
  Looks like:   Tall, narrow I-beam
  Appears over: Text and text fields

  Shape:        Grab
  Looks like:   Rounded square
  Appears over: Things you can drag

  Shape:        Grabbing
  Looks like:   Smaller rounded square
  Appears over: While dragging

  Shape:        Precision
  Looks like:   Small ring
  Appears over: Sliders and vector graphics such as charts and diagrams

  Shape:        Canvas
  Looks like:   Small dashed, spinning ring
  Appears over: Drawing and graphics areas in tools

  Shape:        Resize
  Looks like:   Double-headed arrow, turned to the direction
  Appears over: Resize handles and split-view dividers (left-right, up-down or
                diagonal)

  Shape:        Move
  Looks like:   Four-way arrow
  Appears over: Things you can move in any direction

  Shape:        Zoom in / Zoom out
  Looks like:   Plus or minus in the ring
  Appears over: Images and areas you can zoom

  Shape:        Help
  Looks like:   Question mark in the ring
  Appears over: Abbreviations and help items

  Shape:        Copy
  Looks like:   Small plus at the ring's corner
  Appears over: Items that copy when clicked

  Shape:        Crosshair
  Looks like:   Hairline cross in a tight ring
  Appears over: Pickers and crosshair areas

  Shape:        Not allowed
  Looks like:   Red ring with a slash
  Appears over: Actions that are not allowed

  Shape:        Disabled
  Looks like:   Faint, grey ring
  Appears over: Disabled controls

  Shape:        Loading
  Looks like:   Dashed, spinning ring
  Appears over: While the page or a tool is busy

EFFECTS
=======

  Effect: Magnetic glow
  When:   Over clickable items and draggable items

  Effect: Shrink
  When:   On click

  Effect: Stretch
  When:   During fast movement

  Effect: Fade to half
  When:   When idle, or when the pointer leaves the window

  Effect: Adaptive colour
  When:   Always: the ring takes the hue of the surface under it, lightened or
          darkened to stand out

  Effect: Accent colour
  When:   Over grey and neutral surfaces

  Effect: Dark or light dot
  When:   Chosen from the brightness of the surface under it

SETTING
=======

  Control                            | Values  | Default
  -----------------------------------+---------+--------
  Settings › Comfort › Custom cursor | On, off | On
