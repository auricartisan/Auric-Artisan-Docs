==============================================================================
THEME FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers about the site's themes.
Area: Website › Site features › Theme   Updated: 2026-09-25

IS THERE A HIGH-CONTRAST THEME?
===============================

There is no separate theme, but Settings › Comfort › Boost contrast
strengthens text and borders in both themes. See Accessibility aids
[../../accessibility-aids/README.txt].

DOES THE THEME CHANGE COLOURS IN THE TOOLS?
===========================================

No. The theme changes the site's interface. Colours you are working with,
swatches and images are shown as they are.

DOES MY ACCOUNT REMEMBER MY THEME?
==================================

The site theme is kept in your browser, not in your account. Your account
dashboard has its own theme preference; see Account and billing
[../../../account-and-billing/README.txt].

CAN I SET A DIFFERENT THEME FOR ONE KIT?
========================================

Yes. Use the sun or moon button in that kit's title bar. It affects only that
kit.

WHY DOES THE PAGE NOT FLASH WHEN IT LOADS?
==========================================

The site reads your saved theme before it draws the page, so it starts in the
right theme.
