==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING FEEDBACK
==============================================================================
Fixes for common feedback problems.
Area: Website › Site features › Feedback   Updated: 2026-09-25

THE PROMPT NEVER APPEARS
========================

Why: it needs 20 minutes of active time on one page, it does not appear on
sign-in, checkout, payment or legal pages, it waits at least a week between
prompts, and it stops for good after Don't ask again.

What to do: use the Feature Kit's Feedback panel whenever you want to give
feedback.

I CHOSE DON'T ASK AGAIN BY MISTAKE
==================================

Why: the choice is kept in this browser.

What to do: use the Feature Kit's Feedback panel, which is always available.
Clearing the site's data in your browser also resets the choice.

CONTINUE IS GREYED OUT
======================

Why: it needs a star rating or a comment.

SEND REPORT IS GREYED OUT
=========================

Why: What happened? is required.

PRESSING A NUMBER CHANGED MY RATING
===================================

Why: the keys `1` to `5` set the rating when the comment box does not have
focus.

What to do: click in the comment box before typing numbers.

I WAS OFFLINE WHEN I SENT FEEDBACK
==================================

It is kept on your device and sent automatically when you are back online.
