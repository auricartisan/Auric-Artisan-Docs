==============================================================================
FEEDBACK FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers about giving feedback on Auric Artisan.
Area: Website › Site features › Feedback   Updated: 2026-09-25

DO I HAVE TO GIVE MY NAME OR EMAIL?
===================================

No. Send anonymously is always available and sends your feedback without
contact details.

WILL I GET A REPLY?
===================

Only if you add contact details and the team decides to follow up. A bug
report is not a promise of a fix, but reports are reviewed.

CAN I ATTACH A SCREENSHOT OR RECORDING?
=======================================

Not in the feedback panel. You can describe the problem, or contact the team
through https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/.

WHY DOES THE SITE WAIT SO LONG BEFORE ASKING?
=============================================

So that it asks people who have actually used the site, and does not interrupt
short visits.

DOES THE PROMPT APPEAR ON EVERY DEVICE?
=======================================

The timing and your choices are kept per browser, so each browser counts
separately.
