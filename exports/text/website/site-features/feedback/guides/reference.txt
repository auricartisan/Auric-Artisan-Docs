==============================================================================
FEEDBACK REFERENCE
==============================================================================
Every feedback screen, button, timing and message.
Area: Website › Site features › Feedback   Updated: 2026-09-25

WHEN THE AUTOMATIC PROMPT MAY APPEAR
====================================

  Condition                    | Value
  -----------------------------+-------------------------------------------
  Active time needed           | 20 minutes with the page visible
  Pages where it never appears | Sign-in, checkout, payment and legal pages
  Minimum gap between prompts  | 7 days on this device

QUIET PERIODS
=============

  Outcome                              | No automatic prompt for
  -------------------------------------+------------------------
  Prompt shown (whatever happens next) | 7 days
  Maybe later                          | 7 days
  Dismissed (× or `Esc`)               | 14 days
  Feedback, survey or report sent      | 90 days
  Don't ask again                      | Ever, on this device

QUICK RATING SCREEN
===================

  Control                         | Notes
  --------------------------------+-----------------------------------------
  Stars 1 to 5                    | Also keys `1` to `5`
  Comment box                     | Question and hint change with the rating
  Maybe later                     | Postpone
  Don't ask again                 | Stop automatic prompts
  Report a bug / Something broke? | Opens the bug form
  Full survey                     | Opens the survey
  Continue                        | Goes to the contact step

CONTACT STEP
============

  Control              | Notes
  ---------------------+-----------------------------------------------
  Add contact details  | Shows Name and Email
  Send anonymously     | Sends without contact details
  Name — optional      | Up to 120 characters
  Email — optional     | Up to 254 characters; checked for a valid form
  Back / Send feedback | Return, or send with details

SURVEY
======

Six optional questions; Not now and Send answers; counter "N of 6 answered".

BUG REPORT
==========

  Field                         | Required | Notes
  ------------------------------+----------+----------------------------------
  What happened?                | Yes      | Enables Send report
  What did you expect instead?  | No       | —
  How can we reproduce it?      | No       | —
  How badly does it affect you? | No       | Minor, Normal (default), Blocking

Attached automatically: page address, window size, screen size, browser and
platform, language, theme, and recent page errors where available.

MESSAGES
========

  Message: You have been here a little while — how is it going?
  When:    Automatic prompt

  Message: How is Auric Artisan working for you?
  When:    Panel opened on request

  Message: Would you like us to contact you about this feedback?
  When:    Contact step

  Message: That email does not look right. Correct it, clear it, or go back to
           send anonymously.
  When:    Invalid email

  Message: Thank you for your feedback
  When:    Rating sent

  Message: Your responses have been received.
  When:    Survey sent

  Message: Thank you for the report
  When:    Bug report sent

  Message: Restored what you had written earlier.
  When:    A bug-report draft was restored

  Message: Sending…
  When:    While sending
