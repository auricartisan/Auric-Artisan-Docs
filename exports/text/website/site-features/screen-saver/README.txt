==============================================================================
SCREEN SAVER
==============================================================================
The optional idle screen that fills the window with a colour from the site's
colour atlas after a period of inactivity.
Area: Website › Site features › Screen saver   Updated: 2026-09-25

The screen saver is an optional idle screen. When you have not touched the
page for a while, it fills the window with a single colour from the site's
colour atlas, laid out as seven bands from its darkest shade to its lightest,
with the colour itself sitting on its own step. A readout names the colour and
gives its hex code, its OKLCH value, the mood it reads as, the art movement
associated with it, and its contrast against black. A new colour arrives every
twelve seconds, and a small clock shows the time. Move the mouse, press a key,
scroll or touch the screen, and the page comes back exactly as you left it.

The screen saver is off by default. You choose whether it runs and how long
the page must be idle first (1, 5, 10, 20 or 30 minutes).

WHO IT IS FOR
=============

People who leave the site open on a second screen or in a studio, and anyone
who enjoys a slow tour of the site's colour atlas.

WHERE TO FIND IT
================

Settings (`Ctrl` + `,`) › Comfort › Screen saver.

QUICK START
===========

  1. Press `Ctrl` + `,` and select Comfort.
  2. Switch on Screen saver.
  3. Under Start after, select 1m to try it.
  4. Leave the page alone for a minute or so.
  5. The screen fills with a colour and its readout. Wait twelve seconds to
     see the next one.
  6. Move the mouse. The page returns.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
===============

  - Turn the screen saver on or off.
  - Choose how long the page must be idle before it starts.
  - Read the name, hex, OKLCH, mood, movement and contrast of a new colour
    every twelve seconds.
