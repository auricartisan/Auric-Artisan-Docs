SCREEN SAVER LIMITS AND ACCURACY
==============================================================================
What the screen saver's values mean and how precise they are.
Area: Website › Site features › Screen saver   Updated: 2026-09-25

  - Values. The OKLCH values are shown to two decimal places for lightness and
    chroma and to whole degrees for hue. The contrast against black uses the
    WCAG 2 formula, to two decimal places.
  - Shades. The seven bands are drawn in OKLCH where your browser supports it;
    very colourful shades may be outside what your screen can show and are
    drawn as close as the screen allows.
  - Mood and movement. "reads as" and "movement" are descriptive notes from
    the colour atlas, not measurements.
  - Timing. The idle check runs every ten seconds, so the screen saver can
    start up to about ten seconds after your chosen delay.
  - Scope. It runs only in the page's own tab and does not replace your
    operating system's screen saver or power settings.
