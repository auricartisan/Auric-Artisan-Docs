SCREEN SAVER PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What the screen saver stores and downloads.
Area: Website › Site features › Screen saver   Updated: 2026-09-25

  - Your on or off choice and delay are kept in this browser, as part of your
    settings.
  - The screen saver watches for activity only to know when you are idle. It
    does not record or send what you do.
  - The first time it starts, it downloads a small list of atlas colours from
    auricartisan.com. Nothing is sent in return.
