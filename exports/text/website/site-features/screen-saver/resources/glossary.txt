==============================================================================
SCREEN SAVER GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Terms used in the screen saver guides.
Area: Website › Site features › Screen saver   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Idle
  Meaning: No mouse, keyboard, scroll, wheel or touch activity on the page.

  Term:    Colour atlas
  Meaning: The site's collection of 8,192 named colours with mood and movement
           notes.

  Term:    OKLCH
  Meaning: A way of writing colour as lightness (L), chroma (C) and hue (H)
           that follows human perception.

  Term:    Lightness step
  Meaning: One of the seven bands, each a fixed OKLCH lightness from 0.22 to
           0.94.

  Term:    Chroma
  Meaning: How colourful a colour is, from grey (0) upwards.

  Term:    Contrast on black
  Meaning: The WCAG contrast ratio between the colour and pure black.
