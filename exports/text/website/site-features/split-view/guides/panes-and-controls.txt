==============================================================================
PANES AND THEIR CONTROLS
==============================================================================
Use each pane's tab, history buttons, address bar and More menu for theme,
viewport, zoom, focus and more.
Area: Website › Site features › Split view   Updated: 2026-09-25

Each pane has the same row of controls in the toolbar.

THE PANE TAB
============

The tab at the start of each row names the pane: by default the page's own
title (for example "Basic Tools"). A dot shows its state: amber and pulsing
while the page loads, green when it is ready.

Rename a pane: double-click its tab, type a name and press `Enter` (or click
elsewhere). Press `Esc` to cancel. An empty name returns to the page title.
Your name stays on the pane while you browse in it, until you close split
view.

BACK, FORWARD AND RELOAD
========================

Each pane has its own history. Back and Forward move through the pages that
pane has shown; Reload reloads just that pane.

THE ADDRESS BAR
===============

Each pane has an address bar showing its page's address on the site, for
example `/library/palette/`.

To load another page:

  1. Select the address bar. A list of destinations opens, grouped under Home,
     Tools, Libraries, Account, Company, Legal and your Recent pages.
  2. Type to narrow the list: page names, addresses and keywords all match
     (for example `oklch` finds the Colour Science Lab).
  3. Use `Up` and `Down` to choose and `Enter` to load, or click an entry.

You can also type an address that is not in the list: a site path such as
`/tool/icon/` (the list offers Go to that path) or a full `https://` address
(Open external URL). Press `Ctrl` + `Enter` to open the typed address in a new
browser tab instead. Press `Esc` to close the list, and again to put back the
current address.

Loading a new page in the live pane means closing split view will take you to
that page. The companion's address bar tooltip reads "Edit the companion
pane's URL and press Enter to load it."

Pages you load are added to Recent (up to 10 site pages), and split view uses
your most recent companion page the next time it opens.

THE MORE MENU
=============

Select the three dots (More pane controls) at the end of a pane's row:

  Control:      Theme
  What it does: Cycles Auto, Light and Dark for this pane only. Light and dark
                apply instantly.

  Control:      Viewport
  What it does: Cycles desktop (full pane width), tablet (820 pixels) and
                phone (400 pixels). The page inside reflows to that width.

  Control:      Zoom
  What it does: Cycles 100%, 125%, 150% and 75% for this pane.

  Control:      Focus this pane
  What it does: Moves keyboard focus into the pane and outlines it (`Alt` +
                `1` for live, `Alt` + `2` for companion). Select again to
                clear.

  Control:      Maximize
  What it does: Gives this pane the whole area. Select again to restore.

  Control:      Open in new tab
  What it does: Opens this pane's page in a new browser tab.

  Control:      Copy URL
  What it does: Copies this pane's page address, without the split details.

Controls that differ from their default (a forced theme, a device width, a
zoom other than 100%) are highlighted. The menu stays open while you cycle
theme or zoom; click elsewhere or press `Esc` to close it.

Your own site theme setting is not changed by a pane's theme. When you switch
a pane back to Auto, it reloads to follow your site theme again.

KEYBOARD INSIDE A PANE
======================

Keyboard shortcuts pressed inside a pane (such as `Esc`, `Alt` + `S` or `Ctrl`
+ `Shift` + `D`) still reach split view.
