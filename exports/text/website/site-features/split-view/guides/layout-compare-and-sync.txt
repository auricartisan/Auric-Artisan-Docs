==============================================================================
LAYOUT, COMPARE AND SYNC
==============================================================================
Change the split layout, resize and swap panes, compare pages visually, sync
scrolling, route links and watch performance.
Area: Website › Site features › Split view   Updated: 2026-09-25

These are the global controls at the right of the split view toolbar.

SIDE BY SIDE OR STACKED
=======================

Select Toggle layout or press `Alt` + `V` to switch between panes side by side
and panes one above the other.

RESIZE THE PANES
================

  - Drag the divider between the panes. A badge follows the pointer showing
    the split as two percentages, for example "62 | 38".
  - The divider snaps when you pass near 25%, 33%, 50%, 66% or 75%.
  - A pane cannot be made smaller than 80 pixels.
  - Double-click the divider, select Equal split, or press `Alt` + `E` to
    return to 50 | 50.

The sizes you choose are remembered separately for side-by-side and stacked
layouts.

SWAP THE PANES
==============

Select Swap panes or press `Alt` + `S`. The panes trade places; both pages
keep running, with nothing reloaded.

MAXIMISE ONE PANE
=================

Use Maximize in a pane's More menu to give it the whole area, and select it
again to restore.

COMPARE TWO PAGES
=================

Compare overlays the two panes and highlights what differs:

  1. Load the two pages you want to compare, for example the same page with
     one pane in light theme and the other in dark, or two palettes.
  2. Select Compare.
  3. A note appears: "Compare mode — matching areas go dark, differences light
     up. Scroll is synced."

Areas where the two pages are identical turn black; anything different shows
in colour. Scroll sync is switched on while comparing, and returns to your
previous choice when you leave. Select Compare again to leave.

MIRROR
======

Mirror loads the live pane's page into the companion, so you can show the same
page twice, for example at two widths or in two themes.

ROUTE LINKS
===========

When Route links is on (highlighted), links you click in the live pane open in
the companion pane instead, so the live pane stays put. Whether it is on or
off, holding `Alt` while clicking a link in either pane opens it in the other
pane.

SCROLL SYNC
===========

When Scroll sync is on (highlighted), scrolling one pane scrolls the other to
the same relative position (for example, halfway down both pages).

PERFORMANCE CHIP
================

The first global control shows the frame rate, for example "58 fps", with a
coloured health dot.

  - Point at it (or focus it) to see the Performance panel: FPS, Frame time,
    CPU busy (long tasks), Event-loop lag, JS heap (RAM) where the browser
    reports it, DOM nodes · live and DOM nodes · pane 2.
  - Click it to pin the panel open; click again to unpin.

Eco mode
--------

Two full pages at once can be heavy. When readings stay poor for a few seconds
(frame rate below 32, CPU busy above 65%, or lag above 400 milliseconds),
split view switches both panes, and the page behind, to the site's
low-performance mode, which turns off decorative effects. The panel then reads
"Eco mode active — panes switched to the low performance tier until readings
recover." When readings stay good for a few seconds, normal mode returns.
Otherwise it reads "Optimizer on standby — kicks in automatically when FPS
drops or lag builds up."
