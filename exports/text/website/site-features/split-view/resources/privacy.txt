==============================================================================
SPLIT VIEW PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What split view stores and what it writes into the page address.
Area: Website › Site features › Split view   Updated: 2026-09-25

  - Stored in your browser: your recent pane destinations (up to 10 site
    pages) and the divider sizes.
  - In the address bar: while open, the address describes both panes,
    including each page's remembered state (scroll position and values
    entered). Anyone you send the address to sees those pages and values.
  - Not sent anywhere: split view runs entirely in your browser. Opening an
    external address in a pane loads that website directly, as a normal visit
    would.
  - Performance readings are measured in your browser and shown only to you.
