==============================================================================
INVOICES AND RECEIPTS
==============================================================================
Find your Auric Artisan payment history, open an invoice, print it or save it
as PDF, and understand what the receipt is for tax purposes.
Area: Website › Account and billing   Updated: 2026-09-25

Every payment, refund and chargeback on your account is listed in the
dashboard, and each one has its own printable invoice page.

FIND YOUR PAYMENT HISTORY
=========================

  1. Open the dashboard and select Plan & Usage (or choose Billing & plan in
     the header account menu).
  2. Scroll to Invoices & Payment History.

The table lists Date, Description (Payment, Refund or Chargeback, with the
plan and term), Amount, Status (Paid, Failed or Refunded) and a View link. It
shows 25 rows at a time; use <- Newer** and **Older -> to page through more.

With no purchases yet it reads "No payments yet. Receipts appear here after
your first purchase."

OPEN AN INVOICE
===============

  1. Select View on a row.
  2. The invoice page opens at https://auricartisan.com/auth/billing/invoice/
     with that invoice's reference in the address.

The invoice shows:

  Part:     Header
  Contents: Auric Artisan — "Color science platform & creative tools", the
            website and the support email address

  Part:     Invoice #, Date, Status
  Contents: The invoice reference, the date and Paid, Failed or Refunded

  Part:     From
  Contents: Auric Artisan, "Internet · Worldwide", support@auricartisan.com

  Part:     Billed to
  Contents: Your name and email address

  Part:     Line items
  Contents: What was bought (the plan and its term) and the amount, then the
            Total

  Part:     Payment note
  Contents: "Payment processed via" the provider (Razorpay), and the payment
            reference

  Part:     Footer
  Contents: "Auric Artisan is operated under Indian law. Questions? Email
            support@auricartisan.com and quote the invoice number above."

PRINT OR SAVE AS PDF
====================

  1. Open the invoice.
  2. Select Print / Save as PDF.
  3. In your browser's print dialog, choose a printer, or choose Save as PDF
     (the wording depends on your browser).

SHARE OR KEEP THE LINK
======================

Select Copy invoice URL to copy the invoice's address; the button briefly
reads Copied. Only you can open it: the page needs you to be signed in to the
account that made the payment.

Select ← Back to billing to return to the dashboard.

RECEIPTS AND GST
================

The Subscription and Billing Policy explains that Auric Artisan is not
currently registered for GST. That means:

  - no GST or other tax is added at checkout — the price you see is the total
    you pay;
  - the receipts issued are payment receipts, not GST tax invoices: they carry
    no GSTIN and no tax component;
  - they cannot be used to claim input tax credit.

If you need a GST invoice for a business purchase, the policy asks you to wait
until registration is in place rather than buying on the assumption that one
will follow.

  | Note: The invoice page has a section for GST details ("This is a tax invoice.
  | GST is inclusive in the total.") that appears only when an invoice actually
  | carries tax. Under the current policy you should not expect to see it.

YOUR RECEIPT EMAIL AND ORDER REFERENCE
======================================

The contact page's Billing topic says your order or invoice ID "is in your
receipt email. It starts with order_." Quote it whenever you contact Auric
Artisan about a payment. The confirmation page after checkout shows the same
Order reference with a copy button.

MESSAGES ON THE INVOICE PAGE
============================

  Message:    Invoice not found — "We couldn't load this invoice. It may have
              been removed, or you may need to sign in."
  What to do: Sign in to the account that paid, then open the invoice from
              your payment history.

  Message:    "Missing invoice id."
  What to do: Open the invoice from the View link rather than a shortened
              address.

  Message:    "That invoice could not be found on your account."
  What to do: The invoice belongs to another account, or the reference is
              wrong.

  Message:    "We could not load this invoice. Try again later."
  What to do: A temporary problem. Try again.

  Message:    "Network error loading the invoice."
  What to do: Check your connection.
