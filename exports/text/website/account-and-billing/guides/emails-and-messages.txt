==============================================================================
EMAILS AND MESSAGES
==============================================================================
The emails your Auric Artisan account sends, where replies to your messages
arrive, and why there is no in-account inbox.
Area: Website › Account and billing   Updated: 2026-09-25

Your Auric Artisan account has no message inbox of its own. Everything the
site needs to tell you arrives by email, at the address on your account, and
replies to anything you send through the contact form arrive by email too.

EMAILS THE ACCOUNT SENDS
========================

  Email:              Verification link
  When it arrives:    After you sign up with email and password, or when you
                      ask for a fresh link
  What to do with it: Open it within 24 hours and select Confirm email
                      ownership. See Verify your email [verify-email.txt].

  Email:              Welcome
  When it arrives:    Once your account is usable — straight away for Google
                      or GitHub sign-ups, after verification for email
                      sign-ups
  What to do with it: Nothing; it lists places to start: the tools, the Learn
                      library and your dashboard. Replying reaches a person.

  Email:              Password reset link
  When it arrives:    When you use Forgot password?
  What to do with it: Open it within one hour. See Passwords [passwords.txt].

  Email:              Receipt
  When it arrives:    After a purchase
  What to do with it: Keep it; it carries your order reference, which starts
                      with `order_`.

  Email:              Contact form confirmation
  When it arrives:    When you send a message through the contact page
  What to do with it: Nothing; it confirms the message arrived.

  Email:              Reply to your message
  When it arrives:    When Auric Artisan answers
  What to do with it: Reply in the same thread.

Email is delivered through Resend, named on the Security page as a third-party
provider.

WHERE REPLIES COME FROM
=======================

The contact page routes each topic to one of three addresses, and replies come
from the same one:

  Address                    | Topics
  ---------------------------+--------------------------------------
  hello@auricartisan.com     | Using a tool, Billing, Something else
  developer@auricartisan.com | A bug, API or integration
  security@auricartisan.com  | Security, Privacy or data

Invoices name support@auricartisan.com for questions about an invoice.

See Contact and support [../../../help/contact-and-support.txt] for reply
times and how to write a useful message.

BEWARE OF LOOK-ALIKES
=====================

Auric Artisan asks you to pay only through the Razorpay checkout on
auricartisan.com. If an email asks you to pay somewhere else, or asks for your
password or backup codes, do not act on it; report it through the contact
page.

CHANGE THE EMAIL ADDRESS ON THE ACCOUNT
=======================================

The email address cannot be changed from the dashboard yet. Its field in
Profile is read-only and says "Email changes are not supported yet — contact
support." Write through the contact page from your current address.
