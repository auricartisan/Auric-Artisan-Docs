==============================================================================
SIGN IN AND SIGN OUT
==============================================================================
Sign in to Auric Artisan with your email or username, with Google or GitHub,
or with a second factor, and understand how long a sign-in lasts.
Area: Website › Account and billing   Updated: 2026-09-25

The sign-in page is https://auricartisan.com/auth/. Select Sign in in the site
header to open it; it returns you to the page you were on once you are in.

SIGN IN WITH YOUR EMAIL OR USERNAME
===================================

  1. Open https://auricartisan.com/auth/. The Sign In tab is selected and the
     panel reads Welcome back.
  2. In Email or username, type either the email address on the account or
     your username (without the @).
  3. Type your Password. Select the eye button to show it.
  4. Optional: tick Remember me to stay signed in on this browser (see How
     long a sign-in lasts).
  5. Select Sign In.
  6. The first time you submit, the Auric human check appears: repeat the
     three-mark sequence and select Verify sequence, then select Sign In
     again. How the check works is described in Sign up [sign-up.txt].
  7. If the account has two-step verification, the Second factor. panel
     appears; see Sign in with a second factor.

Result: the message "Identity confirmed. Opening your workspace…" appears and
the site opens:

  - the page you were trying to reach, if you came from a link that needed
    sign-in; otherwise
  - the dashboard, if your account has no username yet; otherwise
  - the home page.

Form messages
-------------

  Message:    "Enter your email or username."
  What to do: The first field is empty.

  Message:    "Enter a valid email address."
  What to do: You typed an @, so the field is checked as an email address, and
              it is not a valid one.

  Message:    "Usernames are at least 3 characters." or "Use letters, numbers
              and underscores only."
  What to do: Without an @, the field is checked as a username.

  Message:    "Password is required."
  What to do: The password field is empty.

  Message:    "Complete the Auric human check before continuing."
  What to do: A check is on screen but not completed.

Wrong credentials, a locked account or a server problem show a message at the
top of the panel. See Troubleshooting [troubleshooting.txt].

SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE OR GITHUB
=============================

  1. On the Sign In tab, under or continue with, select Google or GitHub.
  2. Complete the provider's sign-in if asked.

Result: you return to Auric Artisan signed in. Use the same provider you
signed up with; an email address that already has a password account cannot be
signed in with a different provider (the message is "This email already has an
account. Sign in with the method already connected to it.").

SIGN IN WITH A SECOND FACTOR
============================

If two-step verification is on, a second panel appears after your password:

  1. The panel Second factor. says "Enter the current six-digit code. This
     sign-in expires in 10:00", with a countdown.
  2. Open your authenticator app and type the current 6-digit code in
     Verification Code.
  3. Select Verify.

Result: "Second factor confirmed. Opening your workspace…"

If you do not have your authenticator:

  1. Select Use a backup code.
  2. Type one of your backup codes in Backup Code (format
     `XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`).
  3. Select Verify. Each backup code works once and is used up only when it is
     accepted.

Select ← Back to code entry to return to the 6-digit code, or Cancel sign-in
to start again. If the 10-minute countdown runs out, the page returns to the
sign-in form with "That sign-in request expired. Start again."

Details: Two-step verification [two-step-verification.txt].

HOW LONG A SIGN-IN LASTS
========================

  Choice:                  Remember me ticked
  How long you stay signed in:Up to 30 days on this browser. The Cookie Policy
                           lists the session cookie as lasting "up to 30 days,
                           or until sign-out or expiry". A sign-in that goes
                           unused for seven days ends sooner.

  Choice:                  Remember me not ticked
  How long you stay signed in:Until you close the browser, and no longer than
                           24 hours.

  Choice:                  Google or GitHub
  How long you stay signed in:Treated as a normal sign-in without Remember me.

Signing in on one device does not sign you out anywhere else. Each browser is
a separate signed-in device, listed under Security & Privacy › Signed-in
devices.

Some actions end your sign-ins on purpose:

  - Changing your password signs you out everywhere, including the browser you
    changed it in. Sign in again with the new password.
  - Resetting a forgotten password also ends all existing sign-ins.
  - Revoking a device, or Sign out everywhere, ends those sessions
    immediately.
  - Deleting the account signs you out everywhere.

IF YOU ARE ALREADY SIGNED IN
============================

Opening the sign-in page while signed in sends you straight on to the page you
asked for, or the home page. If your email address is not verified yet, the
page shows Confirm your inbox. instead — see Verify your email
[verify-email.txt].

THE ACCOUNT MENU
================

When you are signed in, the header shows your photo, first name and plan
instead of Sign in. Select it to open the account menu:

  Item           | Goes to
  ---------------+-------------------------------------------------------
  Signed in as   | Shows your name and email address
  Dashboard      | "Overview and recent activity" — the dashboard
  Profile        | "Identity and profile photo" — the Profile section
  Billing & plan | "Subscription and invoices" — the Plan & Usage section
  API keys       | "Manage developer access" — the Plan & Usage section
  Sign out       | "End this session securely"

SIGN OUT
========

  1. Select your account chip in the header.
  2. Select Sign out.

Result: this browser's session ends. Other devices stay signed in; to end them
too, use Sign out everywhere in Security & Privacy [security-and-privacy.txt].
