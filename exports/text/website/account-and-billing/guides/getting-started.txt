==============================================================================
GETTING STARTED WITH THE DASHBOARD
==============================================================================
Open the Auric Artisan account dashboard, learn its layout, and complete the
first five tasks for a new account.
Area: Website › Account and billing   Updated: 2026-09-25

The account dashboard is one page with four sections. It shows what the
account holds — your work, the public page that shows it, what you are paying
for, and who is signed in — and it is where you change all of it.

OPEN THE DASHBOARD
==================

  1. Sign in at https://auricartisan.com/auth/ (see Sign in and sign out
     [sign-in.txt]).
  2. Open https://auricartisan.com/auth/dashboard/, or select your account
     chip in the header and choose Dashboard.

If you are not signed in, the dashboard sends you to the sign-in page and
brings you back afterwards.

  | Tip: After you sign in, the site takes you to the dashboard automatically if
  | your account has no username yet, so you can claim one. Otherwise it returns
  | you to the page you came from, or to the home page.

THE SCREEN, TOP TO BOTTOM
=========================

The masthead
------------

  Part:          Auric Artisan · Account
  What it shows: The page kicker

  Part:          @yourhandle
  What it shows: Your username. It reads @unclaimed until you choose one, with
                 a Claim your handle link beneath.

  Part:          Profile photo
  What it shows: Your photo, or your initial, or an empty outline.
                 Double-click it to open the photo viewer.

  Part:          Plan
  What it shows: Your plan name, for example Apprentice. A status is added
                 when it is not simply active, for example "Artisan · Past
                 due".

  Part:          Email address
  What it shows: The address you sign in with

  Part:          Join date
  What it shows: "Joined today", or "Member since" and the month and year

  Part:          Open Studio
  What it shows: Opens your saved work in the Library

  Part:          Upgrade
  What it shows: Opens the pricing page. On a paid plan it reads Manage and
                 opens Plan & Usage instead. It is hidden on Industrial Pro,
                 the highest tier.

The section rail
----------------

Four tabs switch the sections without leaving the page:

  Tab:           Overview
  What it holds: A statement of the account, your saved work, suggested next
                 steps and quick links to tools
  Badge:         —

  Tab:           Profile
  What it holds: Your name, username, bio, contact details, photo, and the
                 doorway to your public portfolio
  Badge:         —

  Tab:           Plan & Usage
  What it holds: Your plan, usage meters, invoices, and (on plans with API
                 access) keys and credits
  Badge:         Number of API keys

  Tab:           Security & Privacy
  What it holds: Password, two-step verification, cookies, signed-in devices,
                 data export and the danger zone
  Badge:         Number of signed-in devices

A badge turns green while its count is zero. With the rail focused, the `Left`
and `Right` arrow keys move between tabs, and `Home` and `End` jump to the
first and last.

Each section has its own address, so you can bookmark it: `#overview`,
`#identity` (Profile), `#plan` and `#security`. Older addresses still work and
land in the right place:

  Old address: `#profile`, `#portfolio`,
               https://auricartisan.com/auth/profile/
  Opens:       Profile

  Old address: `#billing`, `#api-keys`,
               https://auricartisan.com/auth/billing/,
               https://auricartisan.com/auth/api-keys/
  Opens:       Plan & Usage

  Old address: `#settings`, `#sessions`,
               https://auricartisan.com/auth/settings/,
               https://auricartisan.com/auth/sessions/
  Opens:       Security & Privacy

  Old address: https://auricartisan.com/auth/portfolio/
  Opens:       The Portfolio Builder at https://auricartisan.com/portfolio/

The "Finish setting up" panel
-----------------------------

If your account is missing something the site needs — usually a username,
sometimes a display name — a floating panel titled Your account is nearly
there appears. It is not a blocking dialog: you can keep using the page, press
`Esc`, or select Later. It returns after a week if the detail is still
missing. It stays out of the way while the Profile section is open, because
that section holds the same fields.

In the panel:

  1. Type a username after the @ (it is pre-filled with a suggestion when
     possible). A tick and Available, or a cross and the reason, appears as
     you type.
  2. Type Your name if it is asked for.
  3. If your email is not verified, select Send it again to get a new
     verification link.
  4. Select Save.

YOUR FIRST FIVE TASKS
=====================

  1. Claim your username. Select Claim your handle in the masthead, or open
     Profile, type it in Username and select Save changes. It becomes the
     address of your public portfolio and can be used to sign in. See Profile
     section [profile.txt].
  2. Verify your email. Until you do, billing, API keys and device approval
     stay locked. See Verify your email [verify-email.txt].
  3. Add a name and photo. The Finish setting up card in the Overview side
     column tracks these; it disappears when all four items are done.
  4. Turn on two-step verification. Open Security & Privacy, select Enable
     2FA, scan the code, and save your backup codes. See Two-step verification
     [two-step-verification.txt].
  5. Check your plan. Open Plan & Usage to see your plan and usage. New
     accounts are on the free Apprentice plan with 100 starter tokens. See
     Plans and pricing [plans-and-pricing.txt].
