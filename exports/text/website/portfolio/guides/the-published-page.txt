==============================================================================
THE PUBLISHED PAGE
==============================================================================
What visitors see at a published portfolio address and at a snapshot link.
Area: Website › Portfolio   Updated: 2026-09-25

AT YOUR ADDRESS
===============

A published portfolio lives at `https://auricartisan.com/p/` followed by your
address. It opens quickly: the portfolio appears first, and the site's header,
footer, cursor and right-click menu arrive a moment later.

Visitors see:

  - your header and sections, in your chosen typeface, paper, rhythm and
    accent;
  - your saved palettes in the Work / Projects section as swatches they can
    select, and copy as CSS or JSON;
  - Copy link and, if you allowed it, PDF;
  - the view count, if you chose to show it.

The right-click menu on a portfolio
-----------------------------------

On a portfolio page, the site's right-click menu offers actions for readers:

  Action                           | What it does
  ---------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------
  Copy link to this portfolio      | Copies the address.
  Save this portfolio as PDF       | Opens the print dialog.
  Email and the owner's first name | Starts an email, when the portfolio shows an email address.
  Reading mode (`Alt` + `R`)       | Switches the site's reading mode.
  Make your own portfolio          | Opens the Portfolio Builder.

AT A SNAPSHOT LINK
==================

A snapshot link opens https://auricartisan.com/portfolio/public/ with the
portfolio carried in the address. The page shows Opening portfolio…, then the
portfolio with Copy link and PDF, and a footer: "Built with Auric Artisan —
Colour intelligence, accessibility tools, and a portfolio builder." with Make
your own.

Messages a visitor may see:

  Message: "This page shows a shared portfolio, and this link is missing its
           data. Ask for a fresh link from the portfolio builder."
  Why:     The link was cut short.

  Message: "This browser cannot decompress the link. Try a current Chrome,
           Edge, Firefox or Safari."
  Why:     An old browser.

  Message: "This portfolio could not be opened. It may have been built by a
           newer version of the editor."
  Why:     The link could not be read.

RESULT
======

Visitors get a fast, readable page that works on any device, and a way to
copy, save or contact you.
