==============================================================================
STYLE AND THEME
==============================================================================
Choose a typeface, paper, rhythm and accent for your portfolio, with the
accent graded for contrast.
Area: Website › Portfolio   Updated: 2026-09-25

Open the Style tab. A portfolio's look is four choices, each from a named,
checked set rather than free colour pickers, so the page stays readable.

TYPEFACE
========

  Choice             | Headings         | Body text
  -------------------+------------------+---------------------
  Fraunces (default) | Fraunces (serif) | Manrope (sans-serif)
  Newsreader         | Newsreader       | Newsreader
  Space Grotesk      | Space Grotesk    | Space Grotesk
  Manrope            | Manrope          | Manrope

PAPER
=====

The page's background and text colour.

  Choice:     Cream (default)
  Background: `#FBFAF7`
  Notes:      Dark text.

  Choice:     White
  Background: `#FFFFFF`
  Notes:      Dark text.

  Choice:     Sand
  Background: `#F2EDE3`
  Notes:      Dark text.

  Choice:     Ink
  Background: `#14151A`
  Notes:      A dark page with light text.

  Choice:     No background
  Background: None — "Nothing behind the page"
  Notes:      No fill, padding or rounded corner. Text follows the viewer's
              own light or dark theme. For pages that draw their own
              background, such as a Raw HTML hero.

All the greys the page uses for secondary text are chosen to clear AA contrast
on each of the four papers.

RHYTHM
======

The spacing and roundness of the page.

  Choice         | Feel
  ---------------+----------------------------------
  Tight          | Close spacing, square corners.
  Even (default) | Moderate spacing, softly rounded.
  Airy           | Generous spacing, well rounded.

ACCENT
======

The accent colours links, headings and buttons. Under Accent, from your
library, the Style tab lists the palettes and scales you have saved in your
Library, newest first, as swatches.

  - Each swatch is graded against the paper you chose. Hover to see, for
    example, "#8A6D16 — 4.70:1 on Cream (AA)".
  - Swatches below 4.5:1 are marked as weak ("below AA for text"). You can
    still pick one, for borders and rules.
  - If no step of a palette clears 4.5:1, the pane says "Nothing in this
    palette clears 4.5:1 on" the paper, and suggests a darker step or a
    different paper.
  - With no saved palettes, it says: Save a palette from any colour tool and
    its steps show up here as accents.

Under the swatches, a line grades your current accent: "Your accent clears AA
on" the paper, "at any size", or "Below 4.5:1 on" the paper, with the advice
that it is "Fine for a rule or a border, not for words".

You can also set the accent with the colour picker in the Header panel.

RESET
=====

Reset to defaults returns to Fraunces, Cream, Even and the default accent
`#8A6D16`.

FROM THE COMMAND FIELD
======================

Every choice is also a command: type, for example, "paper" and choose Paper —
Ink, or "typeface" for Typeface — Newsreader.

RESULT
======

Your portfolio has a consistent, readable look, and the accent is one you made
yourself and know passes contrast.
