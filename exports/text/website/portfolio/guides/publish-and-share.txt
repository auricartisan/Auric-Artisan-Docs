==============================================================================
PUBLISH AND SHARE
==============================================================================
Publish your portfolio at your own address or as a private link, update it,
control search, the index, PDF and views, and unpublish.
Area: Website › Portfolio   Updated: 2026-09-25

Select Publish in the top bar. The dialog, headed Portfolio and Put this
online, shows Not published until your page is live.

If Publish is greyed out, the Check pane has blocking findings to fix first;
see Preview and check [preview-and-check.txt].

CHOOSE WHO CAN SEE IT
=====================

  Choice:        Public at an address
  What it means: "Anyone with the link can open it, and it stays current —
                 publish again and the same address shows the new version."

  Choice:        Only people I send the link to
  What it means: "No address on the site, and search engines never see it."
                 The builder gives you a snapshot link instead: "The link is
                 the portfolio" — it needs no account, works offline, never
                 updates, and can be too long to paste in some places if the
                 page has several images.

PUBLISH AT AN ADDRESS
=====================

  1. Choose Public at an address.
  2. In Your address, after `auricartisan.com/p/`, type the name you want.
     - Lower case letters, numbers and hyphens, 2 to 31 characters.
     - While you type, the field says Checking…, then whether the address is
       available; a tick shows when it is yours to take.
     - Under the field, Or take offers suggestions based on your name.
  3. Look at the side panel:
     - What a shared link shows — the card people see when your address is
       shared in a chat or social app: your name, headline and avatar, "Drawn
       from your header, and refreshed every time you publish."
     - Ready to go — a checklist: Address is yours to take, how many sections
       will be published, Name is set, Headline is set. A missing item says
       what to do, for example A headline is what the share card shows.
  4. Select Publish (it reads Publishing…). The line under it says "It goes
     live at once, and you can take it down whenever."
  5. The builder confirms Published at and your address.

WHEN IT IS LIVE
===============

The dialog title becomes Live since and the date, and shows:

  Part:         Your address
  What it does: For example `auricartisan.com/p/your-name`.

  Part:         Copy
  What it does: Copies the address (Copied).

  Part:         Open
  What it does: Opens the live page.

  Part:         QR code / Hide code
  What it does: Shows a QR code "For a slide or a card" that "Points at the
                same address, so it keeps working after you republish."

  Part:         Since you published
  What it does: "This is exactly what visitors are seeing." — or "You have
                edits here that are not published yet." / "You have changed a
                setting that is not published yet."

  Part:         Publish these changes
  What it does: Appears when there is something new to publish. Updates the
                live page at the same address.

  Part:         Reach
  What it does: The number of views and "views since" the date you published —
                "Counted as a number and nothing else — no visitor records, no
                addresses."

The top bar also shows your address and whether it is live.

SETTINGS
========

Select Search, the Portfolio Index, PDF and views to open the switches. A
summary beside it reads, for example, "In search · In the index · …".

  Switch:       Let search engines list it
  Default:      On
  What it does: Off keeps it out of Google while the link still works for
                anyone you send it to.

  Switch:       List it in the Portfolio Index
  Default:      On
  What it does: Lists it at https://auricartisan.com/portfolios/, "searchable
                by craft, place and colour". Needs Let search engines list it
                on.

  Switch:       Allow a PDF download
  Default:      On
  What it does: Visitors get a PDF button for a print-clean copy of the page.

  Switch:       Show the view count
  Default:      On
  What it does: Shows views at the foot of the page. You always see the count
                in the dialog.

Change a switch, then select Publish these changes for it to take effect.

TAKE IT DOWN
============

Under Take it down: "The address stops working at once. It also becomes free
for someone else to take — so if you might come back, leave it up and switch
off the search listing instead."

  1. Select Unpublish. It changes to Yes, take it down for a few seconds.
  2. Select Yes, take it down to confirm.
  3. The builder confirms Unpublished. The address no longer shows anything.

Your draft stays in the builder; you can publish again later, at the same
address if it is still free.

SHARE A PRIVATE LINK
====================

  1. Choose Only people I send the link to.
  2. Select Copy the link. "Nothing is stored on the site."
  3. Paste the link to the people you choose.

CLOSE
=====

Select Close (or press `Escape`).

RESULT
======

Your portfolio is live at your own address, or shared privately, and you
control whether search engines, the Portfolio Index and PDF downloads can
reach it.
