==============================================================================
HEADER AND AVATAR
==============================================================================
Fill in the portfolio header, follow your profile or override it, choose what
to show, and set an avatar.
Area: Website › Portfolio   Updated: 2026-09-25

The Header panel at the top of the Content column controls the top of your
portfolio: "The top of your portfolio. Leave a field blank to follow your
profile."

THE FIELDS
==========

  Field:       Name
  Limit:       80 characters
  Show switch: Always shown
  Notes:       Blank follows your profile's display name.

  Field:       Headline / tagline
  Limit:       120 characters
  Show switch: Always shown
  Notes:       Also used on your share card.

  Field:       Email
  Limit:       120 characters
  Show switch: Show — off by default
  Notes:       The address you publish can differ from the one you sign in
               with.

  Field:       Location
  Limit:       80 characters
  Show switch: Show — on by default
  Notes:       Placeholder "City, Country".

  Field:       Website
  Limit:       200 characters
  Show switch: Show — on by default
  Notes:       —

  Field:       Avatar (image address)
  Limit:       500 characters
  Show switch: Show — on by default
  Notes:       Or pick a picture (below).

  Field:       Accent
  Limit:       A colour picker
  Show switch: —
  Notes:       The page's accent colour, also set from Style.

Following your profile
----------------------

A blank field is not "no value" — it means "use my profile". The profile's
value appears as grey placeholder text in the empty field, so you can see what
the page will say. Type into the field to use a different value just for your
portfolio; clear it to go back to following your profile. Changes to your
profile then reach the portfolio automatically.

A Show switch that is off keeps that detail off the page, off exports and out
of anything you publish or share, even if the field has a value.

Use the built-in header
-----------------------

The Use the built-in header switch turns the whole header block (name, avatar,
headline and details) on or off. Turn it off when your page has its own
opening — for example a Raw HTML hero — so it is not preceded by a second one.
While it is off, the Avatar, Location and Email switches are greyed out.

CHOOSE AN AVATAR
================

  1. Double-click the avatar circle in the Header panel, or double-click your
     picture in the preview ("Double-click to choose a picture").
  2. Pick an image file.
  3. The builder resizes it to a small square, stores it inside your portfolio
     and confirms Avatar updated.

Because the picture is stored in the portfolio, it travels with every export,
snapshot link and publish. Resizing also removes hidden photo information
(such as camera and location data) from the file.

Messages you may see:

  - Turn the built-in header on first. — the header is switched off.
  - That picture is too large even shrunk. Try a smaller one.
  - That file could not be read as a picture.

You can also paste an image address into Avatar instead. With no avatar, the
page shows your initial.

  | Note: A snapshot link has little room, so a large avatar is left out of
  | snapshot links (the initial is shown instead). Published pages keep it.

RESULT
======

The top of your portfolio says exactly who you are, with only the details you
chose to show.
