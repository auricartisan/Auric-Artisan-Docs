==============================================================================
PORTFOLIO SHORTCUTS
==============================================================================
Keys and pointer actions in the Portfolio Builder, on published portfolios and
in the Portfolio Index.
Area: Website › Portfolio   Updated: 2026-09-25

BUILDER
=======

  Keys or action: `Ctrl` + `K` or the command field
  Effect:         Opens the list of builder commands; type, then `Enter`.

  Keys or action: `Enter`
  Effect:         Finishes a one-line field you are typing in the preview.

  Keys or action: `Escape`
  Effect:         Finishes editing in the preview; closes the templates
                  dialog, the section picker, full-screen preview and the
                  publish dialog.

  Keys or action: `Enter` in the section picker's search
  Effect:         Adds the first matching section type.

  Keys or action: Double-click the avatar
  Effect:         Choose a picture.

  Keys or action: Drag a card's handle (⠿)
  Effect:         Reorder sections.

The Undo and Redo buttons show `Ctrl` + `Z` and `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `Z` in
their tooltips; if the keys do not respond, use the buttons or the Undo and
Redo commands.

PUBLISHED PORTFOLIO
===================

  Keys or action: Right-click
  Effect:         Copy link to this portfolio, Save this portfolio as PDF,
                  Email the owner, Reading mode, Make your own portfolio.

  Keys or action: `Alt` + `R`
  Effect:         Reading mode.

PORTFOLIO INDEX
===============

  Keys     | Effect
  ---------+----------------------------------------------------
  `/`      | Focus the search box.
  `Escape` | Clear the search box, or close an open filter menu.

See also Site features [../../site-features/README.txt] for site-wide
shortcuts.
