THE LIVE QUOTE
==============

Beside the campaign builder, the Live estimate panel prices your campaign as
you build it. Every choice updates it at once, and the price shown is the
price you are asked to pay.

Your campaign shows:

  Line:    Summary
  Meaning: Format, zone, days and share, for example Leaderboard · Run of site
           (everywhere) · 30 days @ 50%.

  Line:    Base run
  Meaning: The price before discounts.

  Line:    Flight discount
  Meaning: The saving for a longer flight. Shown only when there is one.

  Line:    Promo
  Meaning: The promo code saving, with the code. Shown only when there is one.

  Line:    Tax
  Meaning: Shown only when tax applies.

  Line:    Total
  Meaning: What you pay.

Estimated delivery shows:

  Figure:  impressions
  Meaning: How many times the ad is expected to be shown: the zone's daily
           views, times the days, times your share of voice.

  Figure:  est. uniques
  Meaning: Roughly how many different people see it.

  Figure:  est. clicks
  Meaning: Impressions times the format's typical click-through rate.

  Figure:  eff. CPM
  Meaning: The effective price per thousand impressions, before tax.

These are estimates. Your flight report shows what was actually delivered.

WHAT CHANGES THE PRICE
======================

  - Format: each has a daily rate, from ₹299 ($4) to ₹649 ($8) a day. See
    Formats and placements [formats-and-placements.txt].
  - Zone: busier zones cost more a day. Run of site (everywhere) costs the
    most and reaches the most views.
  - Flight length: longer flights have a lower daily rate. 1 week is the
    standard rate; 2 weeks save 5%, 1 month saves 15% and 3 months save 28%.
  - Share of voice: 25% share, 50% share or 100% takeover of the eligible ad
    renders in your zone. A larger share costs more and delivers more
    impressions.
  - Promo code: a valid code takes a percentage off the total after the flight
    discount.
  - Currency: INR ₹ or USD $. Each currency has its own rates.

SEND THE REQUEST
================

The button at the foot of the quote panel reads Request campaign with the
total and (pay on approval) once every step is complete. The note under it
reads Nothing is charged here · we confirm the campaign, then send a Razorpay
link for the price shown.

Sending the request books nothing and charges nothing.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
=================

  1. Confirmation. Auric Artisan confirms availability and sends a Razorpay
     payment link to your billing email within one business day.
  2. Payment. You pay the price shown in your quote through the Razorpay link.
  3. Creative review. Before launch, your creative and destination are
     reviewed for policy compliance.
  4. Launch. The campaign starts at 00:00 IST on your start date, after
     creative approval, and runs for the flight you chose.
  5. Transparency. While it runs, the campaign is listed in the Ad Library
     [ad-library.txt].
  6. Flight report. At the end, you receive a report of impressions,
     MRC-viewable impressions, clicks and effective CPM, measured by the ad
     system rather than estimated.

The request carries your campaign details as text: the campaign and brand
names, format, zone, dates, share of voice, goal, frequency cap, audiences,
final destination address, quoted total and estimated impressions. Your
artwork is not attached to the request, so keep your file, or your studio
settings, ready for when Auric Artisan replies.

REFUNDS
=======

From the refund policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/refund/:

  - An advertising booking is refundable in full at any time before the
    campaign start date.
  - Once a campaign has started, refunds for the remaining, undelivered part
    are handled case by case, based on the booking terms shown at purchase.
