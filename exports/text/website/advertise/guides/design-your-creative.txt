==============================================================================
DESIGN YOUR CREATIVE
==============================================================================
How to design an ad in the AdsSense creative studio or upload finished
artwork, the specifications for each format, and the creative policy.
Area: Website › Advertise   Updated: 2026-09-25

The third step of the campaign builder, Creative, is where you make the ad
itself. Under Creative source, choose Design it here to use the built-in
creative studio, or Choose a file to upload finished artwork. The Live preview
always shows your creative at the exact ratio of the format you chose, for
example 728×90 · Leaderboard, with the Sponsored label.

DESIGN IT IN THE STUDIO
=======================

The studio renders your text and colours as a crisp vector image at the exact
size of your format.

  Field:         Headline
  What it is:    The main line.
  Limit:         40 characters
  If left empty: Your brand name

  Field:         Supporting line
  What it is:    One clear reason to click.
  Limit:         60 characters
  If left empty: "Reach people who work in color"

  Field:         Call to action
  What it is:    The button text.
  Limit:         20 characters
  If left empty: "LEARN MORE"

  Field:         Creative description
  What it is:    A description of the visual for screen readers.
  Limit:         120 characters
  If left empty: A description made from your brand name

  Field:         Background
  What it is:    The background colour.
  Limit:         Colour picker
  If left empty: #FFF8EC

  Field:         Accent
  What it is:    The colour of the border, the side bar or mark, and the
                 button.
  Limit:         Colour picker
  If left empty: #D3AF37

  Field:         Text
  What it is:    The colour of the headline and supporting line.
  Limit:         Colour picker
  If left empty: #1F2937

The button text is set in dark or white automatically, to stand out on the
accent colour.

The contrast check
------------------

Under the colours, a line checks the headline against the background, for
example Headline contrast 13.90:1 — passes WCAG AA ✓ with the default colours.
Below 4.5:1 it reads below WCAG AA, consider darker text. The check is advice;
it does not stop you continuing.

The layout for each format
--------------------------

  - Leaderboard: an accent bar on the left, the headline and supporting line
    beside it, and the call-to-action button on the right.
  - Panels & Search Strip: an accent bar, the headline and supporting line,
    and an accent dot on the right.
  - Medium Rectangle and Native In-Feed Card: a centred accent mark, headline,
    supporting line and button.

Every studio design has a thin accent border and a small AD mark in the
top-right corner. If you change format, the design is redrawn at the new size.

UPLOAD FINISHED ARTWORK
=======================

  1. Select Choose a file.
  2. Drop your artwork on Drop your artwork here, or select it to choose a
     file from your device.
  3. The file card shows the name, the pixel size and the file size. Remove
     clears it.

  Rule             | Detail
  -----------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------
  File types       | PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG
  Largest file     | 300 KB
  Recommended size | The size of your format, shown as Recommended size for this format

If the artwork matches the format's shape, the page says Ready to use ·
matches the 728×90 format. (with your format's size). If not, it says the
artwork will be cropped to the format's size, and suggests uploading the
recommended ratio for the sharpest result. A ratio within about 3% of the
format counts as a match.

SPECIFICATIONS FOR EACH FORMAT
==============================

The Creative specs section of the advertise page gives this guidance:

  Format:  Leaderboard
  Artwork: 728×90; SVG, or PNG or WebP at 2×; under 150 KB
  Notes:   Runs in-content on articles, tools and hubs.

  Format:  Medium Rectangle
  Artwork: 300×250; SVG, or PNG or WebP at 2×; under 150 KB
  Notes:   The highest-engagement display unit.

  Format:  Native In-Feed Card
  Artwork: Card artwork 720×600, a title of up to 60 characters and a category
           line
  Notes:   Sits inside the content grids.

  Format:  Panels & Search Strip
  Artwork: 320×100
  Notes:   Shows inside the site kits, Spotlight and search results.

"At 2×" means twice the listed pixel size, for sharp display on high-density
screens.

THE CREATIVE POLICY
===================

Every creative is reviewed before launch. The advertise page states the rules:

  - Creatives are clearly labelled Sponsored.
  - They never imitate the site's interface. Creatives that pretend to be
    buttons or system dialogs are rejected.
  - They never auto-play sound.
  - They never track visitors across sites.

When you send the request, you also accept the creative and acceptable-use
policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/acceptable-use-policy/.

ACCESSIBILITY TIPS
==================

  - Aim for a headline contrast of at least 4.5:1; the studio's check tells
    you where you stand.
  - Always fill in Creative description, so screen-reader users know what the
    ad shows.
  - Keep the call to action short and specific.
