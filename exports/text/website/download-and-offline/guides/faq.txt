==============================================================================
DOWNLOAD AND OFFLINE FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers about installing Auric Artisan and using it offline.
Area: Website › Download and offline   Updated: 2026-09-25

IS THERE A DESKTOP APP TO DOWNLOAD?
===================================

The app is installed from your browser, not downloaded as a separate program.
It then runs in its own window. The browser extension and the VS Code
extensions are separate products.

IS THE APP FREE?
================

Yes. Installing and using it offline costs nothing. Every tool is free on
every plan.

DOES THE INSTALLED APP NEED AN ACCOUNT?
=======================================

No. An account is needed only for account features, which also need a
connection.

DO MY SAVED PALETTES WORK OFFLINE?
==================================

Yes. Your library is kept on your device and works offline.

DOES THE APP UPDATE ITSELF?
===========================

Yes, from the website. When a new version is ready you are asked to refresh;
see Updates [updates.txt].

CAN I INSTALL IT ON MORE THAN ONE DEVICE?
=========================================

Yes. Install and prepare it on each device. Your library and settings stay on
each device separately, unless you use account features that sync them.

HOW MUCH SPACE DOES IT NEED?
============================

It depends on the profile. Lite is small; Full can take more than 100 MB. The
Storage used figure on the Download page shows your actual use.

WHICH BROWSERS WORK BEST?
=========================

For installing: Chrome and Edge on desktop and Android, and Safari on iPhone
and iPad (Add to Home Screen) and on macOS (Add to Dock). For offline use: any
current browser with service workers on a secure connection.
