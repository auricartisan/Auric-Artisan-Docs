TIMELINE
==============================================================================
A summary of Auric Artisan's company timeline page, from the first planning
milestone in June 2025 to version 0.9.11 in August 2026.
Area: Website › Company and legal   Updated: 2026-09-25

Official page: https://auricartisan.com/company/timeline/ — "How we got here".
The page says Auric Artisan "started as a colour-science engine and became a
platform", marks the turning points, and fills in everything between them. For
every change in a release, the Changelog
(https://auricartisan.com/library/changelog/) carries the detail from version
0.9.0 onward.
