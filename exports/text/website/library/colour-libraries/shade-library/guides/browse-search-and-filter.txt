SEARCH
======

Search by id, hex, token or method… filters as you type. Every word must
appear in the scale's id, its name, its base colour, its method, its base hue
name (red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple, pink), its token names,
or any of its hex codes. The Filter inside results box adds more words; Clear
empties it. Press `/` outside a text field to jump to the search box.

Examples:

  - `ff724c` — scales based on, or containing, that colour.
  - `tailwind blue` — Tailwind-like scales with a blue base.
  - `950` — scales that have a `950` token.

FILTERS
=======

  Filter:  Base hue
  Options: All hues, Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Cyan, Blue, Purple, Pink
  Meaning: The OKLCH hue of the scale's base colour.

  Filter:  Chroma
  Options: Any chroma, Muted (< 0.07)**, **Balanced** (0.07 to 0.15), **Vivid
           (> 0.15)
  Meaning: Average OKLCH chroma of the tokens.

  Filter:  Lightness
  Options: Any lightness, Dark (below 0.40), Mid (0.40 to 0.70), Light (above
           0.70)
  Meaning: Average OKLCH lightness of the tokens.

  Filter:  Method
  Options: All methods, Accessible Ui, Brand System, Duotone Shift, Ink Paper,
           Material Like, Neutral System, Oklch Ramp, Tailwind Like,
           Temperature Shift, Vivid Product
  Meaning: How the scale was generated.

SORT BY
=======

  Option                        | Order
  ------------------------------+--------------------------------------
  Sort: original                | Collection order (default).
  Lightness up / Lightness down | Average OKLCH lightness.
  Chroma up / Chroma down       | Average OKLCH chroma.
  Hue (rainbow)                 | Base colour's hue from 0°.
  Score high to low             | Highest recorded quality score first.
  Tokens high to low            | Most tokens first.
  Random                        | Shuffled.

BUTTONS
=======

  - Shuffle — sort Random.
  - Open a random scale — opens any scale on Inspect.
  - Reset — clears searches, filters and sort.
  - Export visible — opens Export.
  - Back to top — scrolls to the first card.

THE GRID
========

The counter reads, for example, 1–24 of 1,148 scales, or Nothing to show; the
grid then says No shade systems match — Try clearing a filter, or searching a
broader method, hex or token.

Cards: select a swatch to copy its hex; Copy copies all hex codes,
comma-separated (Shade hexes copied); Save saves or unsaves (Shade saved /
Shade removed); Inspect, or anywhere else on the card, opens it. With the
keyboard, `Enter` or `Space` on a focused swatch opens the scale.

A NOTE ON EVENNESS
==================

The page's introduction points to evenness — whether a scale's steps are
equally spaced — as the property that matters most, and reports that 8.2% of
scales are genuinely even. The current filter row does not include an evenness
filter. To judge evenness for one scale, open it on Inspect and read Min
adjacent contrast and the Token contrast ladder: large jumps between
neighbouring steps show up there.
