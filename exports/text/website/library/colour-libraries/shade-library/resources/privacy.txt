WHAT STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER
==========================

  What                                         | How to remove it
  ---------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------
  Saved scale ids (up to 500)                  | Unsave each one, or clear this site's data.
  A copy of each saved scale in your Library   | Unsave it, or remove it in the Library.
  Whether you dismissed the Automate this note | Clear this site's data.

Searches and filters are handled in your browser and are not remembered after
you leave.

WHAT USES THE NETWORK
=====================

  - On opening, the page downloads the collection from auricartisan.com.
  - Downloads are created in your browser. Download all in the design tokens
    dialog may fetch a small helper from auricartisan.com.
  - Share links carry the scale in the link; nothing is uploaded.

Advertising and analytics follow your consent choices. See Company and legal
