==============================================================================
GRADIENT LIBRARY GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Definitions of the terms used in the Gradient Library.
Area: Website › Library › Colour libraries › Gradient Library   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Achromatopsia
  Meaning: Complete colour blindness: only shades of grey are seen.

  Term:    Angle
  Meaning: The direction of a linear gradient, in degrees, as in CSS: `0deg`
           runs bottom to top, `90deg` left to right.

  Term:    Banding
  Meaning: Visible steps or edges in a gradient that should look smooth.

  Term:    Complexity
  Meaning: Simple, Detailed or Extreme: how elaborate a gradient is, backed by
           a complexity score.

  Term:    Contrast ratio
  Meaning: The WCAG 2 measure of the lightness difference between two colours,
           from 1:1 to 21:1.

  Term:    Design tokens
  Meaning: Named values in a format a design system or platform reads.

  Term:    Easing
  Meaning: The curve controlling how fast a blend moves between two stops.

  Term:    Interpolation space
  Meaning: The colour space in which in-between colours are calculated: for
           example sRGB, linear RGB, LCH, OKLab or OKLCH. "Short" and "long"
           say which way round the hue circle the blend travels.

  Term:    OKLab, OKLCH
  Meaning: A modern perceptual colour space, and its lightness–chroma–hue
           form. Equal distances in it look roughly equally different, which
           is why the banding check measures in OKLab.

  Term:    Scheme
  Meaning: The colour family a gradient belongs to, such as Aurora, Thermal or
           Terrain.

  Term:    Score
  Meaning: A quality score from 0 to 1 recorded for each gradient.

  Term:    sRGB
  Meaning: The standard colour space of the web. Plain CSS gradients blend in
           it.

  Term:    Step ratio
  Meaning: The largest colour change between neighbouring samples divided by
           the average change. 1 is perfectly even; the check calls 3.2 and
           above compression and 5.5 and above a step.

  Term:    Stop
  Meaning: One colour at one position along a gradient.

  Term:    WCAG
  Meaning: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines. AA text needs 4.5:1 contrast
           (3:1 for large text); AAA needs 7:1.
