WHAT STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER
==========================

  What:             Saved gradients (up to 240)
  Why:              To fill the Saved tab.
  How to remove it: Unsave each one, or clear this site's data.

  What:             A copy of each saved gradient in your Library
  Why:              So it appears with your other saved items.
  How to remove it: Unsave it, or remove it in the Library.

  What:             Whether you dismissed the Automate this note
  Why:              So it stays hidden.
  How to remove it: Clear this site's data.

The stops you type into Will your gradient band? are measured in your browser
and are never sent anywhere. Searches and filters are not remembered after you
leave.

WHAT USES THE NETWORK
=====================

  - On opening, the page downloads the collection's index from
    auricartisan.com.
  - Opening a gradient downloads its detailed metrics from auricartisan.com.
  - Downloads are created in your browser. Download all in the design tokens
    dialog may fetch a small helper from auricartisan.com to build the ZIP.
  - Share links carry the gradient in the link itself; nothing is uploaded.
