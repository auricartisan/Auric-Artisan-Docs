==============================================================================
BROWSE, SEARCH AND FILTER
==============================================================================
Narrow the 8,192-colour atlas with the two search boxes, eight filters and six
sort orders, and move around the grid.
Area: Website › Library › Colour libraries › Color Library   Updated: 2026-09-25

The Browse the atlas section and the Library tab hold everything you need to
narrow the atlas and scan it. This page covers each control in the order you
meet it.

SEARCH
======

The first search box reads Search by hex (#a6e223) or id….

  1. Select the box, or press `/` anywhere on the page (outside a text field)
     to jump to it.
  2. Type part of a hex code, an atlas id, or a label. The grid updates a
     moment after you stop typing.

A colour matches when every word you type appears in at least one of these:
its hex code, its atlas id, its emotion, its art movement, its mood or its
design usage. Matching ignores case and accepts part-words, so `a6e` matches
`#A6E223`, and `rom` matches both romantic and Romanticism.

  | Note: Ids match as text, so typing `12` also finds ids such as `120`, `1203`
  | and `4812`.

FILTER INSIDE RESULTS
=====================

The second row, Filter inside results, has its own box (hex, emotion, mood,
usage…) and a Clear button. It works exactly like the first search box; the
words in both boxes are combined, and every word must match. Use it to add a
narrowing word without editing the first search. Clear empties only this
second box.

THE EIGHT FILTERS
=================

The filters sit at the top of the Library tab. Each is a drop-down list;
choose a value to keep only matching colours.

  Filter:        Hue
  Options:       All hues, Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Cyan, Blue, Purple,
                 Pink, Neutral / Gray
  What it tests: The colour's HSL hue angle, in fixed ranges (see the
                 Reference [reference.txt]). Neutral / Gray keeps colours with
                 very low chroma, and the hue options skip them.

  Filter:        Lightness
  Options:       Any lightness, Dark (L < 35)**, **Mid (35–70)**, **Light (>
                 70)
  What it tests: The colour's CIE L* lightness, from 0 (black) to 100 (white).

  Filter:        Chroma
  Options:       Any chroma, Muted, Balanced, Vivid
  What it tests: The colour's OKLCH chroma: Muted below 0.07, Balanced 0.07 to
                 0.15, Vivid above 0.15.

  Filter:        Emotion
  Options:       All emotions and ten labels
  What it tests: The colour's emotion label.

  Filter:        Art movement
  Options:       All movements and eight movements
  What it tests: The art movement it is associated with.

  Filter:        AI mood
  Options:       All moods and ten moods
  What it tests: The colour's mood label.

  Filter:        Design usage
  Options:       All usages and ten usages
  What it tests: The use it was labelled for, such as `typography` or
                 `ui-surface`.

  Filter:        WCAG
  Options:       Any WCAG, AA on white, AAA on white, AA on black, AAA on
                 black
  What it tests: The contrast ratio on white or black: AA is 4.5:1 or more,
                 AAA is 7:1 or more.

All filters combine with AND, and they also combine with the search boxes and
the requirement chips.

  | Note: The Chroma filter's bands are not the same as the Vivid and Muted
  | requirement chips. The chip Vivid needs chroma 0.18 or more, the filter Vivid
  | needs more than 0.15. Use whichever cut suits you.

SORT
====

Sort by decides the order of the grid:

  Option         | Order
  ---------------+---------------------------------------------------
  Sort: original | The atlas's own order, by id. This is the default.
  Hue (rainbow)  | By HSL hue, from 0° (red) round to 360°.
  Lightness ↑    | Darkest first, by OKLCH lightness.
  Lightness ↓    | Lightest first.
  Chroma ↓       | Most colourful first.
  Luminance ↓    | Highest relative luminance first.
  Random         | A random order.

SHUFFLE, RANDOM AND RESET
=========================

  - Shuffle sets the sort to Random and reorders the grid. Select it again for
    a new order.
  - Open a random colour picks any one of the 8,192 colours, ignoring your
    filters, and opens it on The colour tab.
  - Reset empties both search boxes, sets every filter back to its "all" or
    "any" option and the sort back to Sort: original. It does not clear the
    requirement chips; select a highlighted chip to turn it off.
  - Export visible switches to the Export tab, where you can download every
    colour that currently matches. See Copy and export [copy-and-export.txt].

MOVING AROUND THE GRID
======================

  - The grid scrolls continuously. There are no pages.
  - Under the grid, a line such as 1–48 of 2,998 colours says which colours
    you are looking at and how many match. When nothing matches it reads
    Nothing to show, and the grid shows No colors match these filters with the
    hint Try clearing the search or selecting "Any" on filters.
  - Back to top returns to the first colour.
  - The Library tab's badge shows the number of matching colours.
  - Select a tile, or focus it and press `Enter` or `Space`, to open the
    colour on The colour tab.
  - Select the star on a tile to save or unsave it without opening it. See
    Save colours [save-colours.txt].

RESULT
======

The grid shows only the colours that match your chips, searches and filters,
in the order you chose, and the counter under it tells you how many there are.
