CONTROL BAR
===========

  Control:      Ground swatches
  What it does: Set the background.
  Values:       `#0D0D0D`, `#1D2A3E`, `#FFFCF7`, `#1B4D3E`
  Default:      `#1D2A3E`

  Control:      Colour picker
  What it does: Any other ground.
  Values:       Any colour
  Default:      —

  Control:      Swap
  What it does: Puts the current foreground on the ground and moves the caret
                to the old ground.
  Values:       —
  Default:      —

  Control:      Target
  What it does: The contrast threshold.
  Values:       3:1, 4.5:1, 7:1
  Default:      4.5:1

  Control:      Territory
  What it does: Share of the map that clears the target.
  Values:       0% to 100%
  Default:      —

THE MAP
=======

  Element: Horizontal axis
  Meaning: Hue: 0° red, 60° yellow, 120° green, 180° cyan, 240° blue, 300°
           magenta, 360°.

  Element: Vertical axis
  Meaning: OKLCH lightness, 0 to 1.0.

  Element: Colour at each point
  Meaning: The most colourful sRGB colour at that hue and lightness.

  Element: Lines
  Meaning: 3:1 (dashed amber), 4.5:1 body (solid green), 7:1 AAA (dashed pale
           blue).

  Element: Dimmed area
  Meaning: Fails the current target.

  Element: Caret
  Meaning: The current foreground. Starts at hue 190°, lightness 0.78.

  Element: Pins
  Meaning: Your pasted colours; failures have a line to the target.

  Input            | Effect
  -----------------+-------------------------------------------
  Press and drag   | Moves the caret.
  `Left` / `Right` | Hue −5° / +5° (×5 with `Shift`).
  `Up` / `Down`    | Lightness +0.02 / −0.02 (×5 with `Shift`).
  `Home` / `End`   | Lightness 1 / 0.

READOUT
=======

  Item:    Specimen
  Meaning: "Readable at a glance" and a paragraph in the foreground on the
           ground.

  Item:    Ratio
  Meaning: WCAG 2 contrast, two decimals.

  Item:    Verdict
  Meaning: clears AAA for body text (≥ 7), clears AA for body text (≥ 4.5),
           large text and UI only (≥ 3), fails every text threshold.

  Item:    Foreground, Ground
  Meaning: Hex codes.

  Item:    Lightness
  Meaning: Foreground OKLCH lightness.

  Item:    Hue
  Meaning: Foreground hue in degrees.

  Item:    APCA
  Meaning: Lc, rounded.

  Item:    Vision margin
  Meaning: Worst-case separation under ten simulated conditions, 0 to 1.

  Item:    To the line
  Meaning: Lightness distance to the target line, or unreachable at this hue.

  Item:    Advice
  Meaning: Inside / outside / unreachable sentence.

PICKS
=====

  Control:      Heading
  What it does: N that work on this ground, with the target and ground named.

  Control:      Pick card
  What it does: Specimen, ratio and hex. Select to move the caret to it.

  Control:      Copy as CSS
  What it does: Copies a `:root` block with `--ground` and `--fg-01` …
                `--fg-12`; shows Copied or Copy failed.

Up to 12 picks, one per 30° of hue, each 10% of lightness inside the line.

OR BRING YOUR OWN
=================

  Element   | Meaning
  ----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Paste box | Hex codes starting with `#`, 3 to 6 digits; up to 24; duplicates removed.
  Colour    | Swatch and hex.
  Here      | Contrast on the ground.
  Lightness | OKLCH lightness as a percentage.
  Needs     | nothing, +N% lightness, or unreachable.

THE INDEX
=========

  Control:      Lookup box
  What it does: A record id (`acc_…`) or a number.

  Control:      Look up / `Enter`
  What it does: Shows the record.

  Control:      Record
  What it does: Specimen; Pair, Contrast, APCA, Auric SD, Recommended for;
                seed, index and batch.

  Control:      Pairs that pass / Everything
  What it does: Browse filter. Default: Pairs that pass.

  Control:      Count
  What it does: N shown · scanned M of 5,000,000.

  Control:      Pair card
  What it does: Select to load the pair into the map.
