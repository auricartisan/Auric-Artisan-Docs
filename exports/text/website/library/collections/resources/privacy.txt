WHAT STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER
==========================

The Collections page remembers a few things in your browser's local storage.
None of it is sent to Auric Artisan or saved to your account.

  What:             Pinned tools
  Why:              To show Pinned, Pinned first and your Start here picks.
  How to remove it: Unpin each tool, or clear this site's data.

  What:             Tool opens (count and last time)
  Why:              To build Most used and part of Recommended.
  How to remove it: Clear this site's data.

  What:             Grid or list view
  Why:              To keep your chosen layout.
  How to remove it: Choose the other view, or clear this site's data.

  What:             Density and columns
  Why:              To keep your card-menu layout choices.
  How to remove it: Select Reset in the card menu.

  What:             Tool-visibility setting
  Why:              To show the tool set you chose in the Advanced Kit.
  How to remove it: Change it in the Advanced Kit.

Because these are stored per browser, a private window starts with none of
them and forgets them when it closes.

WHAT USES THE NETWORK
=====================

  - The page downloads the tool catalogue and card artwork from
    auricartisan.com when it loads.
  - Copy link writes to your device's clipboard only; nothing is sent.
  - The page can show advertising. Advertising and analytics on the site
    follow your cookie and consent choices.
