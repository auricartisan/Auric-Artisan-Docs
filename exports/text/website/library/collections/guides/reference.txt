PAGE SECTIONS
=============

FILTER AND SORT PANEL
=====================

The panel is headed Filter and sort and has three sections you can open or
close.

  Section:      Sort by
  Control:      Sort list
  What it does: Chooses the order of the grid. See the sort table below.
  Default:      —

  Section:      Category
  Control:      Tick boxes with counts
  What it does: Shows only tools in the ticked categories. None ticked means
                all.
  Default:      Open, none ticked

  Section:      Topics
  Control:      Tick boxes with counts
  What it does: Shows only tools with any ticked topic. Up to 14 of the most
                common topics are listed.
  Default:      Closed, none ticked

A Pinned tick box appears first under Category once you have pinned at least
one tool.

Sort orders
-----------

  Option:           Title A to Z
  Card-menu button: Title
  Order:            Alphabetical by tool name. First in the list, and the
                    order Reset returns to.

  Option:           Category A to Z
  Card-menu button: Category
  Order:            Alphabetical by category.

  Option:           Newest first
  Card-menu button: Newest
  Order:            By date, newest first. Tools have no dates, so this keeps
                    catalogue order.

  Option:           Oldest first
  Card-menu button: Oldest
  Order:            By date, oldest first. Tools have no dates, so this keeps
                    catalogue order.

  Option:           Recommended
  Card-menu button: Recommended
  Order:            By a score built from your pins, your opens, core subjects
                    and description depth.

  Option:           Most used
  Card-menu button: Most
  Order:            By how often and how recently you opened each tool from
                    this page.

  Option:           Pinned first
  Card-menu button: Pinned
  Order:            Pinned tools first, then the rest.

  Option:           Most metadata
  Card-menu button: Most metadata
  Order:            Tools with the fullest descriptions and most tags first.

When the page first opens, the grid is in catalogue order (the order of the
tool catalogue below), even though the Sort list shows Title A to Z. Choose
any order to apply it.

RESULTS BAR
===========

  Control:      Search tools...
  What it does: Filters as you type. Every word must match the tool's name,
                description, category, address or tags. Case does not matter;
                part-words match.
  Default:      Empty

  Control:      Grid
  What it does: Shows cards side by side.
  Default:      On

  Control:      List
  What it does: Shows one tool per row.
  Default:      Off

  Control:      Result count
  What it does: Shows `N tools`, or `N of M tools` while filtering.
  Default:      —

CARDS
=====

  Part:                  Artwork
  What it shows or does: An illustration for the tool.

  Part:                  Category line
  What it shows or does: The tool's category, above its name.

  Part:                  Name and description
  What it shows or does: The tool's name and one-sentence summary.

  Part:                  Free to use
  What it shows or does: Shown on every card: every tool is free on every
                         plan.

  Part:                  Pin button
  What it shows or does: Pins or unpins the tool. Its label reads "Pin" or
                         "Unpin" followed by the tool's name.

  Part:                  Tooltip
  What it shows or does: On hover: Category, Mode (Basic, Advanced or Both),
                         Route (the tool's address) and topic tags.

  Part:                  Selecting the card
  What it shows or does: Opens the tool and counts one open towards Most used.

LOADING
=======

  Behaviour: First view
  Detail:    12 tools.

  Behaviour: Scrolling to the end of the grid
  Detail:    Loads the next 12 automatically.

  Behaviour: Status line
  Detail:    `Showing 12 of 14 tools`, then `All 14 tools shown` (numbers
             vary).

  Behaviour: Fallback button
  Detail:    Load N more tools, shown only if automatic loading is
             unavailable.

  Behaviour: No results
  Detail:    No matching tools — "Try a shorter search, clear the topic chip,
             or switch the category back to all tools."

  Behaviour: Load failure
  Detail:    The count reads Unable to load and the grid shows "Unable to load
             tools."

FLOATING SEARCH BUTTON
======================

Appears on the right-hand side of the screen once the main controls have
scrolled out of view. Its label is "Search and sort tools".

  Control                       | What it does
  ------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------
  Search tools...               | Same search as the main box; the two stay in step.
  Sort                          | Same list as the main Sort list.
  Category                      | Same tick boxes as the main panel (open by default).
  Topics                        | Same tick boxes as the main panel (closed by default).
  `Escape` or selecting outside | Closes the panel.

PLANS SECTION
=============

  Control:      Billing switch
  What it does: Shows monthly or annual prices. The Save 22% badge sits beside
                it.
  Values:       Monthly, Annual
  Default:      Monthly

  Control:      Currency buttons
  What it does: Shows prices in US dollars or Indian rupees. The page picks
                one for your region on your first visit; your own choice is
                remembered.
  Values:       USD $, INR ₹
  Default:      Set by region

The plans are Apprentice, Artisan, Specialist and Industrial Pro. The section
lists "No credit card to start", "Every tool free on every plan" and "Cancel
anytime · 7-day money-back". For plan details, see Account and billing
[../../../account-and-billing/README.txt].
