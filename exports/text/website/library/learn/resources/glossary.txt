==============================================================================
LEARN GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Definitions of the terms used on the Learn page and in Learn articles' page
furniture.
Area: Website › Library › Learn   Updated: 2026-09-25

Each article also ends with its own glossary, The terms, defined, for the
colour-science terms it teaches. This page covers the terms of the Learn
library itself.

  Term:    Article number
  Meaning: An article's place in the course, from 1 to 113.

  Term:    Category
  Meaning: One of seven groups used to filter the course: Foundations,
           Physics, Vision, Colorimetry, Digital, Design, Print.

  Term:    Course order
  Meaning: The order the articles were written to be read in, from what light
           is to printing.

  Term:    Continue your journey
  Meaning: The personalised suggestions at the end of each article.

  Term:    Docked
  Meaning: A demo panel placed beside the article so both can be seen.

  Term:    Lab, live demo
  Meaning: An interactive demonstration inside an article, opened with Live
           demo.

  Term:    Pin
  Meaning: A mark that keeps an article at the front of Continue your journey.

  Term:    Quick check
  Meaning: The short quiz at the end of an article.

  Term:    Read
  Meaning: An article you have scrolled at least 55% of the way through, in
           this browser.

  Term:    RSS
  Meaning: A feed format that lets a news reader show new articles
           automatically.
