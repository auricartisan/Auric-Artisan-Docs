==============================================================================
YOUR FIRST HOUR
==============================================================================
A guided first hour on the website — finding your way, a first contrast check,
a colour ramp with tokens, a page audit, vision simulation, saving work and
setting preferences.
Area: Getting started   Updated: 2026-09-25

This tour takes about an hour and needs no account. Each stop names the
controls as they appear on screen, says roughly how long it takes, and ends
with what you should see. Links point to the full documentation for each tool.

STOP 1 — FIND YOUR WAY AROUND (5 MINUTES)
=========================================

  1. Open https://auricartisan.com/. On your very first visit a short welcome
     animation plays; press `Esc`, `Enter` or `Space`, or click, to skip it.
  2. When the cookie bar appears at the bottom, choose Accept All, Reject All
     or Customise. Every tool works whichever you pick. See Privacy basics
     [privacy-basics.txt].
  3. Look at the header. From left to right it gives you the home link,
     Search, and the Company, Tools and Learn menus, followed by icon buttons
     for the app, the browser extension, VS Code, search, the guide, the
     library, language and theme, and finally Sign in and Create account.
  4. Press `Ctrl` + `K` and type "contrast". Open the Contrast Checker from
     the results, then come back.
  5. Press `?` (while not typing in a field) to open the Guide. It offers
     quick paths such as Run a quick audit, Build color assets, Check
     accessibility and Find a feature fast. Close it with `Esc`.

Result: you can reach any tool from search, the menus or the guide.

STOP 2 — A FIRST CONTRAST CHECK (5 MINUTES)
===========================================

  1. On the home page, scroll to Pick two colours, watch the verdict move.
  2. Choose a Text swatch and a Background swatch.
  3. Read the contrast ratio and the Normal text and Large text verdicts (AA,
     AAA or fail).
  4. Select Open the full checker to continue in the Contrast Checker with
     your own colours.

Result: you know whether a colour pair is readable, and why. WCAG (the Web
Content Accessibility Guidelines) sets 4.5:1 as the AA minimum for normal text
and 3:1 for large text. Full guide: Contrast Checker
[../website/tools/accessibility-and-vision/contrast-checker/README.txt].

STOP 3 — ONE BRAND COLOUR TO A TOKEN FILE (10 MINUTES)
======================================================

  1. On the home page, find One brand color to a shippable system.
  2. Enter your brand colour in Brand colour (or type a hex value in the field
     beside it).
  3. Watch the three steps update: Accessible ramp (nine steps measured
     against the page surface), Vision check (the ramp through colour-vision
     models) and Export tokens.
  4. Under Export tokens, choose CSS, Tailwind or JSON, then select Copy
     tokens.
  5. Paste the tokens into your project.

Result: a readable colour scale in a format your build reads. For a complete
system (type, spacing, radius, shadow and motion as well as colour), continue
in the Design System Generator
[../website/tools/colour-workspace/design-engine/README.txt].

STOP 4 — AUDIT A WEB PAGE (15 MINUTES)
======================================

  1. Open Analyze URLs from Tools › Core tools
     (https://auricartisan.com/tool/analyzer/).
  2. Paste the address of a page you own or are allowed to test. The Terms of
     Service and Acceptable Use Policy only permit analysis of sites you own
     or are authorised to test.
  3. Run the audit and stay in Simple mode for your first run.
  4. Work through Overview, then the fixes, then the report, as the Guide's
     "Run a quick audit" path suggests.
  5. Export a report if you want to keep one.

Result: a list of accessibility and colour issues on the page, with suggested
fixes. Runs that need Auric Artisan's servers, such as whole-site crawls, show
their token cost before you start and need an account. Full guide: Analyzer
[../website/tools/accessibility-and-vision/analyzer/README.txt].

STOP 5 — SEE IT THROUGH OTHER EYES (10 MINUTES)
===============================================

  1. Open Vision Simulation
     (https://auricartisan.com/tool/vision-simulation/).
  2. Load an image or screenshot of your design.
  3. Switch between protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia and check that
     colour-coded information is still distinguishable.

Result: you can spot colour pairs that collapse for people with colour-vision
deficiency. The simulations are models for design and learning, not a medical
test. Full guide: Vision Simulation
[../website/tools/accessibility-and-vision/vision-simulation/README.txt].

STOP 6 — SAVE AND FIND YOUR WORK (5 MINUTES)
============================================

  1. In any tool you used, use its save or export control to keep a palette,
     gradient or report.
  2. Open Library from the header (the button described as "Saved palettes and
     exports").
  3. Find what you saved.

Result: your work is kept in My Library in this browser. It stays on this
device and is not uploaded unless you publish it. See Library
[../website/library/README.txt] and Library Kit
[../website/kits/library-kit/README.txt].

STOP 7 — MAKE THE SITE YOURS (5 MINUTES)
========================================

  1. Press `Ctrl` + `,` to open Settings.
  2. Under Appearance, choose a Theme (Light, Dark or System), an accent
     colour, a Language and a Text size.
  3. Under Comfort, try Reduce motion, Boost contrast or Reading mode (`Alt` +
     `R`).
  4. Close with `Esc`.

Result: the whole site follows your preferences on this device. See Light and
dark theme [light-and-dark-theme.txt], Language [language.txt] and Settings
panel [../website/site-features/settings-panel/README.txt].

STOP 8 — OPTIONAL: CREATE AN ACCOUNT (5 MINUTES)
================================================

If you want to publish a portfolio, run server-backed crawls, or keep a paid
plan across devices, create a free account: Create an account (optional)
[create-an-account.txt]. Then open the dashboard at
https://auricartisan.com/auth/dashboard/ and follow Finish setting up in the
side column.
