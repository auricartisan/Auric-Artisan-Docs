CHOOSE WHERE TO START
==============================================================================
A goal-by-goal map from what you want to do to the Auric Artisan tool or
product that does it.
Area: Getting started   Updated: 2026-09-25

Pick the row that matches what you want to do. Each row names the tool as it
appears on the site, its address, and the documentation for it. Nothing in
this table needs an account unless the row says so.
