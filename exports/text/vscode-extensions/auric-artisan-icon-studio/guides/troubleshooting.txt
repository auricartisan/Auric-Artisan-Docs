==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
==============================================================================
Symptoms, causes and fixes for common problems with Auric Artisan Icon Studio.
Area: VS Code extensions › Auric Artisan Icon Studio   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Symptom:        No search results.
  Why it happens: A collection or category filter is narrowing the search, or
                  you are in Likes or Recent.
  What to do:     Select Reset filters, switch to Library, and try a simpler
                  word or synonym.

  Symptom:        A style or skin tone option is missing.
  Why it happens: Styles differ by collection; the Style control only appears
                  when one collection with several styles is chosen. Only some
                  emoji support skin tones.
  What to do:     Choose a single collection to see its styles.

  Symptom:        For you chip is missing.
  Why it happens: Recommendations are turned off.
  What to do:     Turn on `auricIconStudio.recommendations.enabled`.

  Symptom:        For you is empty.
  Why it happens: There is no editor context and no likes or usage yet.
  What to do:     Open a file, and like or use a few icons.

  Symptom:        Insert copied the icon instead.
  Why it happens: No editor was available to insert into.
  What to do:     Open and focus a writable text editor, then insert again.

  Symptom:        "VS Code could not apply the edit."
  Why it happens: The editor is read-only or rejected the change.
  What to do:     Use Copy, or open a writable file.

  Symptom:        A Material icon shows as a word in my app.
  Why it happens: Material output needs the Material font; the Studio's
                  preview font is not installed in your project.
  What to do:     Load the matching Material Icons font and styles in your
                  application.

  Symptom:        I cannot save a Material icon or emoji as SVG.
  Why it happens: They are font glyphs and characters, not SVG files.
  What to do:     Use Copy or As… for a text format.

  Symptom:        The output needs editing.
  Why it happens: Smart insert picks a format; it does not integrate the icon
                  into your code for you.
  What to do:     Use As… for a different format and check the insertion
                  point.

  Symptom:        The Modern, Drawn or Playful library view disappeared.
  Why it happens: Icon Studio is hosting those collections.
  What to do:     Turn off `auricIconStudio.hostFamilyLibraries` to show their
                  views again.

  Symptom:        `Ctrl` + `Alt` + `I` does not run Icon Studio.
  Why it happens: The editor is not focused, another extension uses the same
                  keys, or Icon Studio's shortcuts stepped aside because you
                  kept the separate collection views.
  What to do:     Focus the editor, check Keyboard Shortcuts for conflicts,
                  and review `hostFamilyLibraries`.

  Symptom:        Hovering an Auric icon ID shows no Icon Studio preview.
  Why it happens: The owning collection extension is installed and shows its
                  own preview, or previews are off.
  What to do:     This is expected; or turn on `auricIconStudio.hoverPreview`.

  Symptom:        Emoji look different from other screenshots.
  Why it happens: Emoji are drawn by your operating system's emoji font.
  What to do:     This is expected; newer emoji may not appear with older
                  fonts.

  Symptom:        An error says the icon catalog failed to load.
  Why it happens: The installed extension files are damaged or incomplete.
  What to do:     Reinstall the extension from the Marketplace.

RESET PERSONALISATION
=====================

Run Auric Icon Studio: Clear Likes, Recents and Learned Recommendations and
confirm with Clear.

STILL STUCK
===========

Note your VS Code version, the Icon Studio version, the collection and icon
ID, and the steps that reproduce the problem. Remove private code and paths
from screenshots, then contact support at
https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/.
