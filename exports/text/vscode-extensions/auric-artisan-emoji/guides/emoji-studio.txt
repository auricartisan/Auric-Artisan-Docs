==============================================================================
USE EMOJI STUDIO
==============================================================================
Search, browse and insert with Emoji Studio: views, categories, skin tones,
quick filters, layouts, cards, suggestions and the Workspace overview.
Area: VS Code extensions › Auric Artisan Emoji   Updated: 2026-09-25

SEARCH
======

Type in Search (placeholder "Search 1,914 emoji — name, mood, :shortcode: or
U+1F680"). Press `/` or `Ctrl` + `K` (`Cmd` + `K`) to focus it.

You can search by:

  - name or keyword, such as `developer` or `thanks`;
  - an exact emoji;
  - a shortcode, such as `:rocket:`;
  - a code point, such as `U+1F680`;
  - common Hindi words, such as `दिल`, `रॉकेट` or `धन्यवाद`.

VIEWS
=====

  Tab          | Shows
  -------------+------------------------------------------------------------------
  01 Library   | Every emoji matching the search and filters.
  02 For you   | Favorites first, then the emoji you use most, then curated picks.
  03 Favorites | Emoji you starred. Press `F` or the heart on a card.
  04 Recent    | Everything you inserted or copied, most recent first.

In the narrow sidebar, Favorites and Recent buttons at the bottom switch
views, and Back to library returns.

FILTERS
=======

  Filter:  Category
  Options: All or one of nine: Smileys & Emotion, People & Body, Animals &
           Nature, Food & Drink, Travel & Places, Activities, Objects,
           Symbols, Flags. More / Fewer expands the chip list.

  Filter:  Skin tone
  Options: Yellow (standard) or Light, Medium light, Medium, Medium dark,
           Dark. A note shows how many emoji it applies to.

  Filter:  Quick filter (full Studio)
  Options: All, Curated, Skin tone (tone-capable emoji only), Newest.

  Filter:  Density (full Studio)
  Options: Compact, Comfortable, Showcase.

Clear filters resets search and filters. The results line reads, for example,
"42 emoji for "heart"".

LAYOUT
======

In the full Studio, choose Grid or List. Cards load as you scroll ("144 of
1,914 loaded — keep scrolling"); use Load more if it appears.
`auricEmoji.showNames` shows emoji names on cards when there is room.

CARDS
=====

Each card shows the emoji and, space permitting, its name. On hover or
keyboard focus (always visible in the sidebar and on touch screens):

  Action | What it does
  -------+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Insert | Inserts into the active or last active editor, even if your click action is Copy.
  Copy   | Copies in your copy format.
  …      | Opens the inspector.
  Heart  | Adds to or removes from favorites.

Selecting the emoji itself (or pressing `Enter` on it) runs your click action
(`auricEmoji.clickAction`): Insert (default), Copy, or Insert and copy.
`Shift` + `Enter` copies.

KEYBOARD IN THE GRID
====================

  Key                              | Action
  ---------------------------------+----------------------------------------------
  Arrow keys                       | Move between cards
  `Enter`                          | Click action
  `Shift` + `Enter`                | Copy
  `Space`                          | Add to the sequence
  `Backspace` (outside text boxes) | Remove the last sequence item
  `F`                              | Favorite or unfavorite
  `I`                              | Open or close the inspector
  `Escape`                         | Close the open panel, then clear the sequence
  `?`                              | Open the keyboard map

`Tab` reaches each card's buttons. Text boxes keep their normal editing keys.

SUGGESTED FOR YOU
=================

The Suggested for you rail (headed "For {file}" when a file is open) lists
emoji based on the file and your recent use, each with a reason such as In
favorites, Used 3× or Curated. Select Refresh for new suggestions.

WORKSPACE OVERVIEW
==================

Expand Workspace overview · active file & quick tools:

  Part:     Hero
  Contents: "Every emoji. Exactly where you type." with Quick insert, Replace
            shortcodes and Keyboard map →.

  Part:     Active file
  Contents: The target file and language, line and cursor count, skin tone,
            completion state and click action.

  Part:     Active file buttons
  Contents: Insert {emoji} again, Replace :shortcodes: in selection, Clear
            history.

  Part:     Metrics
  Contents: Emoji (with category count), Skin-tone ready, Favorites (kept on
            this machine only), Recent (ranked by use and recency).

Clear history removes recent emoji and last-emoji recall. Favorites are kept.

STUDIO SETTINGS
===============

  - Customize Emoji Studio (gear) and Settings in the footer open Settings
    filtered to this extension.
  - `auricEmoji.closeStudioAfterInsert` closes the full Studio after a
    successful insertion.
  - `auricEmoji.gridDensity` sets the default density.
  - `auricEmoji.statusBar.enabled` shows or hides the Emoji status bar button.

RESULT
======

You can find any emoji quickly, in your tone, and insert or copy it with one
click or key.
