==============================================================================
GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Definitions of the terms used in Auric Artisan Theme and Theme Studio.
Area: VS Code extensions › Auric Artisan Theme   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Accent
  Meaning: The single colour the whole theme is keyed to. Washes, borders,
           selections, the focus ring and the cursor are derived from it.

  Term:    Alpha
  Meaning: Transparency. Alpha slots accept an opacity value, written as the
           last two digits of `#rrggbbaa`.

  Term:    APCA Lc
  Meaning: A perceptual contrast value from the draft WCAG 3 work. It accounts
           for polarity; a negative value means light text on a dark
           background.

  Term:    Audit
  Meaning: The contrast check of 81 foreground and background pairs in the
           active theme.

  Term:    Canvas
  Meaning: The editor background, slot `bg.base`.

  Term:    Chrome
  Meaning: The workbench bars and frames around the editor: status bar,
           activity bar, title bar, side bar and tabs.

  Term:    Colour slot
  Meaning: One named colour the theme is built from, such as `syntax.keyword`.
           There are 107.

  Term:    Contrast guard
  Meaning: The step that nudges colours along OKLCH lightness until they meet
           your minimum contrast. Controlled by Enforce contrast.

  Term:    Contrast ratio
  Meaning: The WCAG measure of luminance difference, from 1:1 to 21:1.

  Term:    Customisation JSON
  Meaning: The portable document holding your changed dials and colour
           overrides.

  Term:    Dial
  Meaning: One theme setting, such as Syntax palette or Depth. There are 37,
           all under `auricTheme.*`.

  Term:    Family
  Meaning: A set of variants sharing the same neutral surfaces, such as
           Obsidian or Porcelain.

  Term:    High contrast
  Meaning: The two builds designed for VS Code's high-contrast modes, one dark
           and one light.

  Term:    Look
  Meaning: A named combination of dial values, such as Clarity, that you apply
           in one click.

  Term:    OKLCH
  Meaning: A colour model with Lightness, Chroma (colourfulness) and Hue that
           matches human perception closely, so changing hue keeps colours
           looking equally strong.

  Term:    Override
  Meaning: A colour you set for a slot by hand. It wins over the dials and is
           stored per variant.

  Term:    Polarity
  Meaning: Whether a theme is dark (light text on dark) or light (dark text on
           light).

  Term:    Preset
  Meaning: A named, saved copy of your dials and overrides, kept on this
           machine.

  Term:    Semantic highlighting
  Meaning: Colouring based on what a language server knows about a symbol, in
           addition to TextMate grammar colouring.

  Term:    Shipped
  Meaning: The colour a slot has in the variant as published, before your
           changes.

  Term:    TextMate token
  Meaning: A piece of code classified by a grammar, such as a string or
           keyword, and coloured by the theme.

  Term:    Theme Studio
  Meaning: The editor-tab interface for customising the theme, with six tabs.

  Term:    Variant
  Meaning: One of the 54 complete themes: a family plus an accent.

  Term:    WCAG AA / AAA
  Meaning: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines levels. AA needs 4.5:1 for
           body text (3:1 for large text and control edges); AAA needs 7:1.
