Privacy

What the Auric Artisan search page and Search Console keep in your browser, what they send, and how to clear it.

Site search

Site search runs on your device. The page downloads the site's index and the list of portfolios once, and matches your queries in the browser. What you type in site search is not sent anywhere.

These are saved in this browser only:

WhatWhyHow to clear
Your six most recent searchesTo offer them on the start screen and in Spotlight.Select Clear next to Recent.
Which results you have openedTo nudge those pages up in your future results.Clear this site's data in your browser.
The Show the score on every result settingTo remember your choice.Untick the setting, or clear site data.

A private window starts with none of these and forgets them when you close it.

Web search

With the globe on, the page sends to Auric Artisan's search service:

  • Your query, and the words you type, for suggestions.
  • When you open a web result: the query, which result you chose and its position in the list.

Clicks are used to improve ranking and to give site owners their click counts in the Search Console. Site icons in the results are fetched through Auric Artisan, so other services are not told which sites appear in your results. Web results open other websites in a new tab; those sites have their own privacy terms.

The Search Console

  • The console needs an Auric Artisan account, so that the sites you add stay yours.
  • Your sites, their verification and their reports are tied to your account.
  • Impressions, clicks, positions, countries and devices are counts of searches on Auric Artisan's web search. They are not shown to you with any searcher's identity.
  • Removing a site from your console deletes its search history for good. The site's pages stay in the index, because they were crawled from the open web. To keep pages out of the index, use robots.txt or noindex on the site itself.

Sponsored units

The search page can show clearly labelled Sponsored units. For how advertising works on Auric Artisan and what it does and does not track, see Advertise. Paid subscribers browse without ads.
