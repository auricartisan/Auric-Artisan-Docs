Getting started with advertising

A tour of the Auric Artisan advertise page and how to send your first campaign request.

Before you start

Have these ready:

  • The address you want clicks to go to.
  • Your brand or company name.
  • An email address for receipts and campaign updates.
  • Finished artwork, if you have it. If not, you can design the ad on the page.

You do not need an Auric Artisan account.

A tour of the page

https://auricartisan.com/advertise/ is one long page. A row of links near the top jumps to each part: Campaign builder, Live placements, Formats, Ad Library and Policy & measurement.

  • The introduction — Put your work in front of people who build with color., with Create a campaign and View live ads. Figures beside it describe the audience, such as the estimated daily ad views and the number of Learn guides.
  • Engine telemetry — this page, right now — four live counts for the ads on the page you are reading: units mounted, impressions, MRC-viewable and clicks. They are measured in your browser, not modelled.
  • See a live placement — real ad units, served the same way your campaign would be. A unit with no ad to show disappears.
  • Formats & benchmarks — a table of the four formats with size, position, typical click-through rate, what each is best for and the starting price.
  • Create your campaign — the six-step builder, with the live quote beside it.
  • The Ad Library — a preview and a link to every ad that runs on the site.
  • Creative specs — the size and file guidance for each format, and the rules creatives must follow.
  • Serving policy & measurement — how ads are served and measured.
  • Questions before you book? — Contact us for custom flights, bulk buys or studio-made creatives.

Send your first request

  1. Select Create a campaign.
  2. In Basics, enter a Campaign name, your Brand / company and the Destination URL. Choose a goal under What should this campaign do?, for example Site traffic. Select Continue.
  3. In Format, keep the Recommended format or choose another. Select Continue.
  4. In Creative, type a headline and supporting line in the studio, or choose Choose a file to upload artwork. Watch the Live preview. Select Continue.
  5. In Audience, choose where the ad runs and the Frequency cap. Select Continue.
  6. In Budget, choose a Flight length, Start date and Share of voice. The quote on the side updates as you go. Select Review campaign.
  7. In Review, check the Campaign summary, enter your Billing email and tick the policy box.
  8. Select Request campaign in the quote panel. The button shows the total and (pay on approval).

The page confirms: Request received. We confirm availability and send a Razorpay payment link within 1 business day — your campaign goes live after payment.

Your draft saves itself in this browser as you work, so you can leave and come back. It is cleared once the request is sent.
