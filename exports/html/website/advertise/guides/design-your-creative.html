Design your creative

How to design an ad in the AdsSense creative studio or upload finished artwork, the specifications for each format, and the creative policy.

The third step of the campaign builder, Creative, is where you make the ad itself. Under Creative source, choose Design it here to use the built-in creative studio, or Choose a file to upload finished artwork. The Live preview always shows your creative at the exact ratio of the format you chose, for example 728×90 · Leaderboard, with the Sponsored label.

Design it in the studio

The studio renders your text and colours as a crisp vector image at the exact size of your format.

The contrast check

Under the colours, a line checks the headline against the background, for example Headline contrast 13.90:1 — passes WCAG AA ✓ with the default colours. Below 4.5:1 it reads below WCAG AA, consider darker text. The check is advice; it does not stop you continuing.

The layout for each format

  • Leaderboard: an accent bar on the left, the headline and supporting line beside it, and the call-to-action button on the right.
  • Panels & Search Strip: an accent bar, the headline and supporting line, and an accent dot on the right.
  • Medium Rectangle and Native In-Feed Card: a centred accent mark, headline, supporting line and button.

Every studio design has a thin accent border and a small AD mark in the top-right corner. If you change format, the design is redrawn at the new size.

Upload finished artwork

  1. Select Choose a file.
  2. Drop your artwork on Drop your artwork here, or select it to choose a file from your device.
  3. The file card shows the name, the pixel size and the file size. Remove clears it.
RuleDetail
File typesPNG, JPG, WebP or SVG
Largest file300 KB
Recommended sizeThe size of your format, shown as Recommended size for this format

If the artwork matches the format's shape, the page says Ready to use · matches the 728×90 format. (with your format's size). If not, it says the artwork will be cropped to the format's size, and suggests uploading the recommended ratio for the sharpest result. A ratio within about 3% of the format counts as a match.

"At 2×" means twice the listed pixel size, for sharp display on high-density screens.

The creative policy

Every creative is reviewed before launch. The advertise page states the rules:

  • Creatives are clearly labelled Sponsored.
  • They never imitate the site's interface. Creatives that pretend to be buttons or system dialogs are rejected.
  • They never auto-play sound.
  • They never track visitors across sites.

When you send the request, you also accept the creative and acceptable-use policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/acceptable-use-policy/.

Accessibility tips

  • Aim for a headline contrast of at least 4.5:1; the studio's check tells you where you stand.
  • Always fill in Creative description, so screen-reader users know what the ad shows.
  • Keep the call to action short and specific.
