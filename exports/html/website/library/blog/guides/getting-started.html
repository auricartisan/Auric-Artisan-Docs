Getting started

Read the Auric Artisan blog page and its posts, print a post, and follow new posts by RSS.

The blog page

Open https://auricartisan.com/library/blog/.

  • The heading Blog and the line "Occasional posts about what is being built and why — roadmaps, product notes, and the odd essay on colour", followed by a count such as Three so far.
  • The newest post, shown large: its date (for example 27 July 2026), its category, its title, its opening paragraph, an illustration and Read the post.
  • Earlier — the other posts, newest first, each with date, category, title and a one-line summary.
  • A footer that points to the changelog for release detail and Learn for longer colour-science writing, and an RSS link.

If the list of posts cannot be loaded, the page shows The blog could not be loaded. and a plain list of All posts still links to every post.

Reading a post

Each post has:

PartWhat it does
Date, Author, CategoryWhen it was written, by whom, and what kind of post it is.
PrintPrints the post.
← Back to BlogReturns to the blog page.
OverviewA short summary of the post.
Table of ContentsLinks to each section.
SectionsThe post itself. Each section heading can be selected to fold the section away or open it again.

Some older posts open with a dated update note, for example to say that much of a roadmap has since shipped.

Follow new posts

Add https://auricartisan.com/library/blog/rss.xml to your news reader. New posts appear there automatically.

Result

You can read every post, keep it on paper, and hear about the next one.
