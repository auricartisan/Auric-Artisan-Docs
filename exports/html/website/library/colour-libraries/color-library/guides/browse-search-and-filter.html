Browse, search and filter

Narrow the 8,192-colour atlas with the two search boxes, eight filters and six sort orders, and move around the grid.

The Browse the atlas section and the Library tab hold everything you need to narrow the atlas and scan it. This page covers each control in the order you meet it.

Search

The first search box reads Search by hex (#a6e223) or id….

  1. Select the box, or press / anywhere on the page (outside a text field) to jump to it.
  2. Type part of a hex code, an atlas id, or a label. The grid updates a moment after you stop typing.

A colour matches when every word you type appears in at least one of these: its hex code, its atlas id, its emotion, its art movement, its mood or its design usage. Matching ignores case and accepts part-words, so a6e matches #A6E223, and rom matches both romantic and Romanticism.

Note: Ids match as text, so typing 12 also finds ids such as 120, 1203 and 4812.

Filter inside results

The second row, Filter inside results, has its own box (hex, emotion, mood, usage…) and a Clear button. It works exactly like the first search box; the words in both boxes are combined, and every word must match. Use it to add a narrowing word without editing the first search. Clear empties only this second box.

The eight filters

The filters sit at the top of the Library tab. Each is a drop-down list; choose a value to keep only matching colours.

FilterOptionsWhat it tests
HueAll hues, Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Cyan, Blue, Purple, Pink, Neutral / GrayThe colour's HSL hue angle, in fixed ranges (see the Reference). Neutral / Gray keeps colours with very low chroma, and the hue options skip them.
LightnessAny lightness, Dark (L < 35)**, **Mid (35–70)**, **Light (> 70)The colour's CIE L* lightness, from 0 (black) to 100 (white).
ChromaAny chroma, Muted, Balanced, VividThe colour's OKLCH chroma: Muted below 0.07, Balanced 0.07 to 0.15, Vivid above 0.15.
EmotionAll emotions and ten labelsThe colour's emotion label.
Art movementAll movements and eight movementsThe art movement it is associated with.
AI moodAll moods and ten moodsThe colour's mood label.
Design usageAll usages and ten usagesThe use it was labelled for, such as typography or ui-surface.
WCAGAny WCAG, AA on white, AAA on white, AA on black, AAA on blackThe contrast ratio on white or black: AA is 4.5:1 or more, AAA is 7:1 or more.

All filters combine with AND, and they also combine with the search boxes and the requirement chips.

Note: The Chroma filter's bands are not the same as the Vivid and Muted requirement chips. The chip Vivid needs chroma 0.18 or more, the filter Vivid needs more than 0.15. Use whichever cut suits you.

Sort

Sort by decides the order of the grid:

OptionOrder
Sort: originalThe atlas's own order, by id. This is the default.
Hue (rainbow)By HSL hue, from 0° (red) round to 360°.
Lightness ↑Darkest first, by OKLCH lightness.
Lightness ↓Lightest first.
Chroma ↓Most colourful first.
Luminance ↓Highest relative luminance first.
RandomA random order.

Shuffle, random and reset

  • Shuffle sets the sort to Random and reorders the grid. Select it again for a new order.
  • Open a random colour picks any one of the 8,192 colours, ignoring your filters, and opens it on The colour tab.
  • Reset empties both search boxes, sets every filter back to its "all" or "any" option and the sort back to Sort: original. It does not clear the requirement chips; select a highlighted chip to turn it off.
  • Export visible switches to the Export tab, where you can download every colour that currently matches. See Copy and export.

Moving around the grid

  • The grid scrolls continuously. There are no pages.
  • Under the grid, a line such as 1–48 of 2,998 colours says which colours you are looking at and how many match. When nothing matches it reads Nothing to show, and the grid shows No colors match these filters with the hint Try clearing the search or selecting "Any" on filters.
  • Back to top returns to the first colour.
  • The Library tab's badge shows the number of matching colours.
  • Select a tile, or focus it and press Enter or Space, to open the colour on The colour tab.
  • Select the star on a tile to save or unsave it without opening it. See Save colours.

Result

The grid shows only the colours that match your chips, searches and filters, in the order you chose, and the counter under it tells you how many there are.
