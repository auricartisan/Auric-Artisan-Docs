Save palettes

Save palettes, find them on the Saved tab, and reach them from your Library.

Save a palette

Either:

  • select Save on the palette's card in the grid (it changes to Saved); or
  • open the palette on Inspect and select Save to library under Quick export (the page confirms Saved).

The Saved tab's badge goes up by one.

See your saved palettes

Select the Saved tab. Your palettes appear as cards, the most recently saved first. The cards work like cards in the grid: select a swatch to copy it, Copy to copy all five, Inspect to open the palette, or Saved to unsave it.

A note above the cards says your palettes are Also saved to your Library workspace with image previews, with a link.

With nothing saved, the tab shows No saved palettes yet and explains how to save one.

Remove a palette

Select Saved on its card, or Unsave under Quick export on Inspect (the page confirms Removed). The palette leaves the Saved tab and your Library.

In your Library

Each saved palette is also added to your Library as a palette named, for example, Palette pal_abc, described by its method and colour count, with a picture of its five colours. Open your Library from any page with Ctrl + Shift + L. See the Library Kit docs.

Limits

  • The Saved tab keeps up to 500 palettes. Saving more drops the oldest from the list.
  • Saved palettes are kept in this browser, not in your account. They do not follow you to another browser or device, and clearing this site's data removes them.

Because every palette is fixed by its id, writing down an id such as pal_abc is a permanent way to keep a palette: search for the id at any time to get the same five colours.

Result

Your chosen palettes are one tab away on the Palette Library and also in your Library next to everything else you have saved.
