Privacy

What the Gradient Library keeps in your browser and what it fetches over the network.

What stays in your browser

WhatWhyHow to remove it
Saved gradients (up to 240)To fill the Saved tab.Unsave each one, or clear this site's data.
A copy of each saved gradient in your LibrarySo it appears with your other saved items.Unsave it, or remove it in the Library.
Whether you dismissed the Automate this noteSo it stays hidden.Clear this site's data.

The stops you type into Will your gradient band? are measured in your browser and are never sent anywhere. Searches and filters are not remembered after you leave.

What uses the network

  • On opening, the page downloads the collection's index from auricartisan.com.
  • Opening a gradient downloads its detailed metrics from auricartisan.com.
  • Downloads are created in your browser. Download all in the design tokens dialog may fetch a small helper from auricartisan.com to build the ZIP.
  • Share links carry the gradient in the link itself; nothing is uploaded.

Advertising and analytics follow your consent choices. See Company and legal.
