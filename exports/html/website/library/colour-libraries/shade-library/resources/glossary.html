Shade Library glossary

Definitions of the terms used in the Shade Library.

TermMeaning
Auto textThe better of near-black (#111111) or white text for a token: near-black on tokens with luminance above 0.5, white otherwise.
BaseThe colour a scale is built around, usually near its middle.
Contrast ratioThe WCAG 2 lightness-difference measure, 1:1 to 21:1. 4.5:1 is AA for body text; 3:1 for large text.
Even stepsA scale in which no step between neighbouring tokens is much larger than the average step.
Min adjacent contrastThe contrast between the two most similar neighbouring tokens.
OKLCHA perceptual colour space: lightness (0 to 1), chroma (colourfulness) and hue angle.
ProfileThe Shade summary badge: Neutral, Hue Shift, Light, Deep or Balanced.
Scale (shade system, ramp)One colour taken through lightness in named steps.
ShadeA darker version of a colour, towards black.
TintA lighter version of a colour, towards white.
TokenA named step in a scale, such as 500.
