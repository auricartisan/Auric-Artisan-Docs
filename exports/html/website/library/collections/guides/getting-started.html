Getting started with Collections

Tour the Collections page and find your first tool.

Collections is the page that lists every Auric Artisan tool. This guide walks through the page from top to bottom and ends with you opening a tool you found by search.

Open the page

Go to https://auricartisan.com/collections/, or open the Tools menu in the site header and choose All Tools. You do not need to sign in.

The screen, top to bottom

Hero

The page opens with the heading "All Auric Artisan tools, engines & generators in one place." Under it, three counters show:

  • Tools — how many tools the catalogue is currently showing.
  • Categories — how many different categories those tools belong to.
  • Topics — how many different topic tags those tools carry.

The counters follow your tool-visibility setting (see Show basic or advanced tools), so they can be lower than the full catalogue of 30 tools.

Beside the heading, three quick links lead to Tools (this page), Learn (the Learn library) and Pricing (the plans page).

Start here

Start here is a shortlist of three featured tools shown as large cards in a slider. Each card shows the tool's name, a one-line description and its category.

  • The slider moves on by itself, one card at a time. A thin bar along the bottom fills up to show when the next card is due.
  • It pauses while your pointer is over it or while it has keyboard focus.
  • Use the previous and next arrow buttons, the dots under the slider, or the Left and Right arrow keys (with the slider focused) to move between cards. A counter such as 01 / 03 shows where you are.
  • If your system asks for reduced motion, the slider does not move by itself.

Tools you pin come first in this shortlist.

All tools

All tools is the full catalogue. On a wide screen it has two columns:

  • On the left, the Filter and sort panel with three sections you can open and close: Sort by, Category and Topics. On wide screens this panel stays in view as you scroll.
  • On the right, the results: a Search tools... box, a Grid / List switch, and the cards.

On a narrower screen (about 900 pixels wide or less), the filter panel sits above the results instead of beside them.

A line above the grid reports the count, for example 14 tools, or 3 of 14 tools when a search or filter is active.

A card

Each card shows:

  • the tool's artwork;
  • its category above the name, for example Colorimetry;
  • the tool's name and a one-sentence description;
  • the line Free to use at the foot, because every tool is free on every plan;
  • a pin button in the corner.

Hover over a card to see a tooltip with more detail: the category, the Mode (Basic, Advanced or Both), the tool's address on the site, and its topic tags.

Below the catalogue

Further down the page:

  • Featured Highlights — two short notes on key capabilities (Accessibility & Analysis, Creative Generation).
  • The plans section, headed "Every tool here is free. Upgrade for superpowers." It shows the four plans with a Monthly / Annual switch and a USD $ / INR ₹ currency switch. See Account and billing for what each plan includes.
  • A closing banner, Explore All Collections, with links back to this page and to the full pricing page.

Your first search

  1. Scroll to All tools.
  2. Select the Search tools... box and type palette.
  3. Watch the grid update as you type. The count above the grid changes to show how many tools match.
  4. Under Category in the Filter and sort panel, tick Generators.
  5. The grid now shows only generator tools whose details mention "palette".
  6. Select a card, for example Basic Tools, to open it.

You now have the tool open. Use your browser's back button to return to Collections. Your pins and your grid or list choice are remembered in this browser for next time.
