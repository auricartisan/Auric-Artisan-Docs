Troubleshooting Collections

Fix common problems on the Collections page, from missing tools to lost pins.

Each entry gives the symptom, the reason, and what to do.

A tool I know exists is not in the grid

Why: The site's tool-visibility setting hides tools that do not match it. The default setting, Basic, shows 14 of the 30 tools; advanced tools such as Illuminants or LUT Lab are hidden. A Custom setting hides every tool you switched off.

What to do: Open the Advanced Kit and choose Everything, or switch the tool back on in Custom. The grid updates at once. See Show basic or advanced tools.

The grid is empty and says "No matching tools"

Why: Your search words, ticked categories and ticked topics together match nothing. Every search word must appear in a tool's details, and a tool must pass the category and topic filters as well.

What to do:

  1. Shorten the search, or try the other spelling (color or colour).
  2. Untick all Topics.
  3. Untick all Category boxes, including Pinned.

The grid is not in alphabetical order, although Sort shows "Title A to Z"

Why: When the page first opens, the grid is in the catalogue's own order. The Sort list shows its first option, Title A to Z, but that order has not been applied yet.

What to do: Choose a different order and then Title A to Z again, or right-click a card and select Title under Sort. The card menu's Reset button also applies Title A to Z.

"Newest first" and "Oldest first" do not change anything

Why: Tools have no publication dates, so both orders keep the catalogue's own order.

What to do: Use Title A to Z, Category A to Z, Recommended, Most used, Pinned first or Most metadata instead.

My pins or usage history have gone

Why: Pins and opens are stored only in this browser. They are not saved to your account and do not follow you to another browser or device. They are lost if you clear this site's data, use a private window, or your browser clears storage automatically.

What to do: Pin the tools again. To keep pins, avoid clearing site data for auricartisan.com and do not rely on private windows.

"Most used" does not show a tool I use every day

Why: Only opens made by selecting a card on the Collections page count. Opening a tool from the header menu, a bookmark, a search result or a link does not.

What to do: Open the tool from its Collections card, or pin it so it always appears under Pinned and at the top of Pinned first.

Right-clicking a card shows the site's normal menu, not the card menu

Why: The card menu loads a moment after the page. It also only answers right-clicks on cards inside All tools, not on the Start here slider or the rest of the page. Some privacy or performance settings can stop the page's extra features from loading.

What to do: Wait for the page to finish loading and right-click a card in the All tools grid. If it still does not appear, reload the page.

The Columns setting makes cards tiny, or seems to do nothing

Why: 2, 3 and 4 force that many columns at every screen width, so on a phone the cards become narrow. Columns have no effect in list view, which always shows one tool per row.

What to do: Choose Auto under Columns, or switch View to Grid to see the column change.

The featured slider keeps moving while I read it

Why: Start here plays automatically.

What to do: Move your pointer over the slider or give it keyboard focus to pause it. If you prefer no movement at all, turn on your system's reduced-motion setting; the slider then stays still.

The page says "Unable to load tools."

Why: The page could not fetch the tool catalogue, usually because the network connection dropped or the page was loaded from an old offline copy.

What to do: Check your connection and reload the page. Meanwhile, you can reach the tools from the site header's Tools menu.

Prices show in the wrong currency

Why: On your first visit the page chooses a currency for your region.

What to do: Select USD $ or INR ₹ above the plans. Your choice is remembered.
