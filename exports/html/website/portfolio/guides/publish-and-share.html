Publish and share

Publish your portfolio at your own address or as a private link, update it, control search, the index, PDF and views, and unpublish.

Select Publish in the top bar. The dialog, headed Portfolio and Put this online, shows Not published until your page is live.

If Publish is greyed out, the Check pane has blocking findings to fix first; see Preview and check.

Choose who can see it

ChoiceWhat it means
Public at an address"Anyone with the link can open it, and it stays current — publish again and the same address shows the new version."
Only people I send the link to"No address on the site, and search engines never see it." The builder gives you a snapshot link instead: "The link is the portfolio" — it needs no account, works offline, never updates, and can be too long to paste in some places if the page has several images.

Publish at an address

  1. Choose Public at an address.
  2. In Your address, after auricartisan.com/p/, type the name you want.
    • Lower case letters, numbers and hyphens, 2 to 31 characters.
    • While you type, the field says Checking…, then whether the address is available; a tick shows when it is yours to take.
    • Under the field, Or take offers suggestions based on your name.
  3. Look at the side panel:
    • What a shared link shows — the card people see when your address is shared in a chat or social app: your name, headline and avatar, "Drawn from your header, and refreshed every time you publish."
    • Ready to go — a checklist: Address is yours to take, how many sections will be published, Name is set, Headline is set. A missing item says what to do, for example A headline is what the share card shows.
  4. Select Publish (it reads Publishing…). The line under it says "It goes live at once, and you can take it down whenever."
  5. The builder confirms Published at and your address.

When it is live

The dialog title becomes Live since and the date, and shows:

PartWhat it does
Your addressFor example auricartisan.com/p/your-name.
CopyCopies the address (Copied).
OpenOpens the live page.
QR code / Hide codeShows a QR code "For a slide or a card" that "Points at the same address, so it keeps working after you republish."
Since you published"This is exactly what visitors are seeing." — or "You have edits here that are not published yet." / "You have changed a setting that is not published yet."
Publish these changesAppears when there is something new to publish. Updates the live page at the same address.
ReachThe number of views and "views since" the date you published — "Counted as a number and nothing else — no visitor records, no addresses."

The top bar also shows your address and whether it is live.

Settings

Select Search, the Portfolio Index, PDF and views to open the switches. A summary beside it reads, for example, "In search · In the index · …".

SwitchDefaultWhat it does
Let search engines list itOnOff keeps it out of Google while the link still works for anyone you send it to.
List it in the Portfolio IndexOnLists it at https://auricartisan.com/portfolios/, "searchable by craft, place and colour". Needs Let search engines list it on.
Allow a PDF downloadOnVisitors get a PDF button for a print-clean copy of the page.
Show the view countOnShows views at the foot of the page. You always see the count in the dialog.

Change a switch, then select Publish these changes for it to take effect.

Take it down

Under Take it down: "The address stops working at once. It also becomes free for someone else to take — so if you might come back, leave it up and switch off the search listing instead."

  1. Select Unpublish. It changes to Yes, take it down for a few seconds.
  2. Select Yes, take it down to confirm.
  3. The builder confirms Unpublished. The address no longer shows anything.

Your draft stays in the builder; you can publish again later, at the same address if it is still free.

Share a private link

  1. Choose Only people I send the link to.
  2. Select Copy the link. "Nothing is stored on the site."
  3. Paste the link to the people you choose.

Close

Select Close (or press Escape).

Result

Your portfolio is live at your own address, or shared privately, and you control whether search engines, the Portfolio Index and PDF downloads can reach it.
