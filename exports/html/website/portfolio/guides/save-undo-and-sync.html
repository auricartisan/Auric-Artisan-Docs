Save, undo and sync

How the Portfolio Builder saves your draft, keeps it on your account, and lets you undo and redo.

Autosave

There is no Save button. Every change is saved automatically. The top bar shows the state:

LabelMeaning
SavedKept in this browser. Tooltip: "Kept in this browser. Sign in and it follows you."
Saved to your accountKept in this browser and on your account. Tooltip: "Kept in this browser and on your account".

Saving your draft never changes what visitors see. Only Publish does that.

Draft sync

When you are signed in, your draft is also kept on your account, so it follows you to another browser or device.

  • Open the builder on another device and it loads the newer copy: Loaded your portfolio from your account. (when this browser had nothing) or Loaded the newer draft from your account.
  • A brand-new, untouched starter page never overwrites real work on your account.
  • The builder does not replace a draft you are in the middle of editing.
  • If the account copy cannot be reached, the builder keeps working in this browser and tries again later.

Undo and redo

  • Select Undo or Redo in the top bar ("Undo (Ctrl+Z)", "Redo (Ctrl+Shift+Z)"), or use the commands Undo and Redo in the command field.
  • Undo covers adding, editing, moving, hiding, duplicating and deleting sections, bulk actions, templates and imports.
  • The buttons are greyed out when there is nothing to undo or redo.

Many messages remind you, for example Section removed — Ctrl+Z to undo.

Keep a copy yourself

To keep a backup or move a portfolio between accounts, use Export › Export JSON, and Import JSON… to bring it back. See Export and import.

Result

Your work is never lost to a closed tab, follows you when you are signed in, and any change can be taken back.
