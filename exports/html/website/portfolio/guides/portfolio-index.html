The Portfolio Index

Browse, search and filter the Portfolio Index by name, craft, place and colour, and share a search.

The Portfolio Index at https://auricartisan.com/portfolios/ is "Every portfolio, in one place": portfolios made with the Portfolio Builder and portfolios people keep on their own sites, "searched together by name, craft, place and colour". Anyone can browse it; no account is needed.

The page

  • The heading, with Publish yours (opens the Portfolio Builder) and Add one you host (opens the Search Console's Portfolios tab).
  • Four counts: In the index, Made here, From elsewhere, Verified.
  • The search box.
  • Source buttons, Filters, the results, and How a portfolio gets in.

Search

Type into Search by name, craft, place or a colour like #d3af37, or press / anywhere on the page to jump to it. Results update as you type; Escape clears the box.

Words are matched against names, headlines, crafts, places and descriptions, with allowance for typing mistakes and word beginnings. You can also use:

You typeIt means
"exact words"Those words together, in that order.
-wordLeave out results with that word.
A hex colour such as #d3af37Portfolios whose colours are nearest to it come first (colours are compared perceptually, in OKLab).
tag:branding or tag:"brand identity"A craft.
in:lisbonA place.
made:here / made:elsewhereBuilder portfolios / portfolios on other sites.
is:verified or verifiedVerified portfolios only.

Under the box, Try offers example tokens — such as made:here, is:verified and a colour — built from what is in the index; select one to put it in the box.

Filter

ControlOptions
Source buttonsAll, Made here, Elsewhere, each with a count.
FiltersOpens the filter row; a number shows how many are on.
Verified onlyOnly portfolios whose owner proved the site is theirs.
ColourColour family: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Teal, Blue, Violet, Pink, Neutral.
CraftThe crafts in the index (· none yet when there are none).
LocationThe places in the index.

Every count reflects your other filters, so you can see what each choice would leave. Active filters are shown as removable chips.

Sort and view

SortOrder
Best matchBy relevance to your search.
Recently updatedFeatured portfolios first, then most recently updated.
NewestNewest first.
Name, A to ZAlphabetical.
Most viewedMost viewed first.

Grid and List switch the layout. In the grid, a featured portfolio at the top gets a larger spotlight card.

The cards

Each card shows the portfolio's colours as a cover, its source (Made here or Elsewhere), a Verified mark ("The owner proved it is theirs") or Reviewed for a checked external entry, an avatar or initials, the name and place, the headline, up to four crafts, where it lives, when it was updated, and Open portfolio. External portfolios open in a new tab. The list view shows the same facts in a row.

Results come 24 at a time; Show N more of M loads the next batch.

Share a search

The address of the page updates with your search, filters, sort and view, so you can copy it to share exactly what you are looking at, and the browser's Back button steps back through your changes.

How a portfolio gets in

The page explains three steps:

  1. Publish, or add an address — "Builder portfolios are copied in within fifteen minutes of publishing. Portfolios on other sites are added in the Search Console."
  2. Proof lists it at once — an address on a site you have verified goes in straight away, marked Verified; anything else waits for someone on the Auric Artisan team to review it.
  3. Found by what it holds — name, craft, place and colour.

"Leave whenever you like: switch off “List it in the Portfolio Index” the next time you publish." See List your portfolio.

The index is searched in your browser and needs JavaScript. Without it, every builder portfolio is still at its own address.
