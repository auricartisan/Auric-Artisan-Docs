Acceptable Use Policy

A plain-language summary of Auric Artisan's Acceptable Use Policy — what you may do, what is prohibited, the web-analysis rules, and how the policy is enforced.

Official page: https://auricartisan.com/legal/acceptable-use-policy/ — version 2.2, effective 27 April 2026, last updated 23 May 2026. The official policy is authoritative.

Key points

  • Use Auric Artisan for colour science, learning, theme generation and creative exploration.
  • Use web analysis only on sites you own or are authorised to test.
  • Do not use security analysis for intrusion, scanning abuse, bypassing controls or harm.
  • Vision simulations and Ishihara-style plates are informational, not medical diagnostics.
  • Respect intellectual property, privacy, security and the law.

What you may do (section 2)

  • Explore colour science and compare models.
  • Read and reference the articles for learning and creative work.
  • Build palettes, themes and design material.
  • Create and export themes for legitimate sites, apps and brands.
  • Review sites for performance, responsiveness, accessibility signals, palettes, security posture and interface quality.
  • Run analysis on sites you own, manage or have permission to evaluate.
  • Use the simulations for non-certified personal, academic, creative or technical research.

What is prohibited (section 3)

  • Anything unlawful.
  • Using analysis or reports to attack, exploit, disrupt or access systems without authorisation.
  • Bypassing security controls, rate limits or access restrictions.
  • Reverse engineering, decompiling, scraping, copying or bulk-extracting the platform.
  • Submitting sites, files or content you have no right to analyse.
  • Uploading malware, spam, deceptive content, harmful scripts or illegal material.
  • Harassment, threats, hate speech, discrimination, impersonation or abuse of communication features.
  • Presenting outputs as certified, medical-grade, legal, governmental or safety-critical results.
  • Abusing free access, automated requests or public endpoints in ways that degrade the platform.

Web analysis and security testing (section 4)

Authorisation is required; no intrusive activity (exploitation, credential testing, denial of service, unauthorised scanning); no claims of a certified audit unless independently verified; respect third parties; treat findings as guidance.

Vision simulations and Ishihara-style tools (section 5)

Simulations are model-based approximations. Ishihara-style plates are not medical-grade. Do not use them to diagnose, screen, certify or make medical decisions; consult qualified professionals.

Communication, collaboration and content (sections 6–7)

Where sharing or feedback features exist: no abusive or misleading content, no impersonating Auric Artisan, staff, partners, institutions or other users, no spam, phishing or harassment. You are responsible for what you submit, for having the rights to it, and for how you use outputs.

Enforcement (section 8)

Pricing pages alone are not an invitation to probe billing systems. Auric Artisan may limit, throttle, block or disable access; remove content; suspend accounts; and report illegal activity — without prior notice where needed for security, availability, legal compliance or safety.

Reporting (section 9)

Report behaviour, content or activity that may break the policy through the contact page.
