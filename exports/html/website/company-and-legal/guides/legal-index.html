Legal index

What is on Auric Artisan's Legal and Policies index page, with one line on each of the twelve policies.

Official page: https://auricartisan.com/legal/ — Legal & Policies: "Every document, policy, and term governing the use of Auric Artisan — clear, current, and in one place."

The index header shows "All policies effective: April 27, 2026", "Last updated: May 23, 2026" and "Version: 2.2". Several policies have been updated since then; each policy page shows its own date and version. See How to read the policies.
