Timeline

A summary of Auric Artisan's company timeline page, from the first planning milestone in June 2025 to version 0.9.11 in August 2026.

Official page: https://auricartisan.com/company/timeline/ — "How we got here". The page says Auric Artisan "started as a colour-science engine and became a platform", marks the turning points, and fills in everything between them. For every change in a release, the Changelog (https://auricartisan.com/library/changelog/) carries the detail from version 0.9.0 onward.

The page loads its entries as you read. If it cannot load them it says "The timeline could not be loaded." — refresh to try again.
