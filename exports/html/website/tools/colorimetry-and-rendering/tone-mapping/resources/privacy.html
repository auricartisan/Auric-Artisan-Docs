Tone Mapping privacy

What the Tone Mapping lab does with your images and settings.

  • Your image stays on your device. An uploaded image is decoded and processed in your browser, and is never sent to a server.
  • Exports are created locally and saved through your browser's download, or copied to your clipboard when you choose.
  • The page address is updated as you work to record the operator, exposure, source encoding, display peak, output encoding and target primaries. Copying or sharing the address shares those settings, never your image.
  • Stored A and B frames exist only in the open page.
  • Save to Library stores an image of the mapped frame in your Auric Artisan Library.
  • The page loads the site's shared features like every other page on auricartisan.com; see the site's privacy policy.
