Image Picker glossary

Definitions of the terms used in the Image Picker.

TermMeaning
CIELABA colour space with lightness L* and two colour axes, a* and b*, designed so distances track perceived difference
ClusterA group of similar sampled pixels, represented in the palette by their average colour
Contrast ratioThe WCAG 2 measure of brightness difference between two colours, from 1:1 to 21:1
CORSCross-origin resource sharing: a server's permission for other sites to read its images' pixels
D65A standard daylight white at about 6500 K, used here as the white for CIELAB
EyedropperThe live readout of the pixel under the pointer on the preview
HarmonyA pair of colours whose hues sit at a classic colour-wheel angle
HexA colour written as #RRGGBB, two hexadecimal digits each for red, green and blue
HSLHue (0° to 360°), saturation and lightness (0% to 100%): a cylindrical form of RGB
k-means++A clustering method that picks well-spread starting colours, then moves each cluster to the average of its nearest pixels
LCHCIELAB written as lightness, chroma (colourfulness) and hue angle
Merge thresholdThe RGB distance below which two clusters are combined
PaletteThe extracted colours, sorted by their share of the picture
PinAn exact pixel colour kept by clicking the preview; up to 24
PopulationA cluster's share of the sampled pixels
QualityHow densely the picture is sampled, from Fast to Max detail
Relative luminanceThe WCAG measure of a colour's brightness, from 0 for black to 1 for white
SampleOne pixel read from the working copy for the extraction
Tone bandShadows, Midtones or Highlights, by relative luminance
WCAGThe Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, which define the contrast ratio and its ratings
Working copyThe picture shrunk to fit 800 × 500 pixels, which the tool samples and the eyedropper reads
