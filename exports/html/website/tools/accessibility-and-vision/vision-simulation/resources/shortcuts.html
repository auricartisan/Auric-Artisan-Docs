Vision Lab keyboard shortcuts

Every key the Vision Lab responds to.

The lab's single-key shortcuts work anywhere on the page except while you are typing in a field, and not with Ctrl, Alt or Cmd held. Press ? to see them on screen.

Navigation

KeyAction
/Focus the search field
EscClear the search (while it has focus)
?Show or hide the keyboard shortcuts overlay
FFavourites
AAnalytics
CCompare

Panels

KeysAction
ROpen a random simulation
TTile all panels
1 to 5Bring panel 1 to 5 forward
Shift + click, or Ctrl + click, on a cardDuplicate a panel that is already open

Cards and tiles

KeysWhereAction
Enter or SpaceA focused Catalogue cardOpen its panel
Left / UpA Compare tile's gripMove the tile one place earlier
Right / DownA Compare tile's gripMove the tile one place later
Home / EndA Compare tile's gripMove the tile to the start or end

For shortcuts that work across the whole site, see Keyboard shortcuts.
