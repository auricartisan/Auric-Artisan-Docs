Troubleshooting the Accessibility Checklist

Common problems with the Accessibility Checklist, why they happen, and what to do.

My progress has gone

  • Why: marks and screenshots live only in this browser on this device. They are lost if you cleared this site's data, used a private window, switched browser, profile or device, or selected Reset (which does not ask first).
  • What to do: there is no server copy to restore from. In future, copy the Markdown or print before clearing anything.

Reset cleared everything without asking

  • Why: in the current version, Reset clears all marks and screenshots immediately, even though its description says it asks first.
  • What to do: nothing can be recovered. Export before you reset.

Screenshots will not attach

  • Why: screenshots need your browser's database storage, which some private windows block. Only image files are accepted.
  • What to do: use a normal window, and attach PNG, JPEG or other image files.

The single-key shortcuts do nothing

  • Why: they only work while focus is in the index or the pane, not with Ctrl, Alt or Cmd held, and not while the screenshot dialog is open. Space marks Pass only from a row, the pane or the check title. During the site's first-visit guide tour, the tour may take the arrow keys.
  • What to do: select a row first, then press the key.

I cannot find a check

  • Why: a filter is on, Open only is hiding passed checks, or the area is folded.
  • What to do: look at the chip beside the meter; select All 50, switch off Open only, and unfold the area.

Previous check or Next check is greyed out

  • Why: you are at the start or end of the visible list.
  • What to do: change the filter or unfold areas to reach more checks.

The printout is missing checks

  • Why: passed and not-applicable checks are left out of the printout by design. An active filter may also limit it.
  • What to do: select All 50 and switch off Open only before printing. For counts and a full list of untouched checks, use Copy as Markdown.

Export the report did not download a file

  • Why: it opens the browser's print dialog instead.
  • What to do: choose Save as PDF in the print dialog.

Copy as Markdown failed

  • Why: the browser refused clipboard access. The message says "Could not reach the clipboard — the browser refused it."
  • What to do: select the button again after clicking on the page, and check your browser's clipboard permission for auricartisan.com.

"What a claim actually needs →" does nothing

  • Why: the link does not switch tabs in the current version.
  • What to do: select the Reference tab.

Remove says "Remove this one?"

  • Why: deleting a screenshot needs a second press within four seconds.
  • What to do: press Remove this one? to confirm, or wait for it to reset.

On a phone, the check fills the screen

  • Why: below 1080 pixels wide the checklist shows one check at a time.
  • What to do: tap All checks, or use your browser's Back.

Redacted areas are still visible in stored screenshots

  • Why: redaction applies only to the marked-up copy used for thumbnails and printing; the original is kept unchanged.
  • What to do: crop or blur sensitive information before attaching the screenshot.

"Check a pair" does not open a contrast checker

  • Why: the link on check c1 opens the tools collection page.
  • What to do: open the Contrast Checker directly.
