Related tools and articles

Tools and Learn articles related to Perception Vision.

Tools in this group

  • Neuroscience — the same cone and dichromacy maths, followed through the retina, LGN, V1, V2 and V4.
  • Psychophysical Experiment Engine — measure a contrast threshold, the quantity a contrast sensitivity simulation would need.
  • Material Lighting — how an illuminant changes a material's colour, spectrally rather than by adaptation.

Other tools on the site

  • Vision simulation — the Vision Lab, for checking designs under many visual conditions.
  • Contrast Checker — check the contrast of a colour pair against accessibility guidelines.
  • Ishihara plate generator — generate pseudo-isochromatic plates.
