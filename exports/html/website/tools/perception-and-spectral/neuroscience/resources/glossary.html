Visual Pathway glossary

Terms used in the Visual Pathway tool, each explained briefly.

TermMeaning
AliasA false, coarser pattern that appears when a screen is asked to draw stripes finer than its pixels can carry
CarrierWhat a grating varies: colour at constant L + M (Chromatic) or lightness in grey (Luminance)
Centre-surroundA receptive field that compares a small central region with a larger surrounding one
ColourmapThe ramp of colours used to draw a map's values, here Viridis, Inferno or Grayscale
Complex-cell energyThe combined response of two filters a quarter-cycle apart; it responds to stripes regardless of their exact position
Cone signals (L, M, S)The responses of the long-, medium- and short-wavelength-sensitive cones to a colour
Cone tableA table of cone spectral sensitivities by wavelength; the page's built-in one is its own, and you can install another
Cycles per degree (c/deg)How many light-dark cycles of a pattern fall within one degree of visual angle
Degrees per pixelThe angle one screen pixel covers at your viewing distance: atan(pixel pitch ÷ distance)
DeuteranopiaColour vision without M cones
DichromacyColour vision with only two of the three cone types working
Difference of Gaussians (DoG)A centre blur minus a weighted surround blur, the standard model of a retinal centre-surround cell
Divisive normalisationDividing a response by the local average response, so each part of a map is read relative to its neighbourhood
DKL spaceThe Derrington–Krauskopf–Lennie opponent space, built on cone contrast against an adapting white; the tool's axes are not DKL
Gabor filterA sine-wave stripe pattern inside a Gaussian envelope, the standard model of a V1 simple cell
Hunt-Pointer-Estévez matrixA published 3 × 3 matrix that converts CIE XYZ to cone signals, scaled so D65 white gives equal L, M and S
Hypercolumn mapA map of the preferred orientation at each point, drawn here as a colour wheel
LGNThe lateral geniculate nucleus, the relay between retina and cortex where colour is carried in opponent channels
Luminance (relative)The lightness-related quantity Y of CIE XYZ, from 0 for black to 1 for white; the retina and V1 stages read it
Nyquist limitThe finest frequency a sampled grid can carry: half a cycle per pixel, converted here to c/deg
Opponent channelA signal formed by comparing cone signals, such as L − M (red against green)
Pixel pitchThe physical width of one screen pixel, in millimetres
ProtanopiaColour vision without L cones
RMS contrastThe root-mean-square spread of luminance values across an image
Scale barThe bar under the map giving the minimum and maximum the colours stand for
Spatial frequencyHow finely a pattern repeats across space, here in cycles per degree
sRGBThe standard colour space of web content, with a defined transfer curve and primaries (IEC 61966-2-1)
Stand-inA register status: values chosen by the tool to be plausible, not taken from a publication
TritanopiaColour vision without S cones
V1, V2, V4Areas of visual cortex: V1 analyses orientation and spatial frequency, V2 boundaries and texture, V4 colour and form
Verbatim, Computed, AbsentRegister statuses: taken unchanged from a source; derived from verbatim data by stated arithmetic; not held
Viewing distanceThe distance from your eyes to the screen
