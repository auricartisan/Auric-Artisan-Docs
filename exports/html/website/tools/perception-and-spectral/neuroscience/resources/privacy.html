Privacy

What the Visual Pathway keeps in your browser and what, if anything, uses the network.

What stays in your browser

  • Every calculation: the stimulus, the five stages, the readouts and the colour list are computed in the page.
  • A cone table you install is read from your disk by the page. It is not uploaded and is forgotten when you reload.
  • The colours you type in the Export tab stay in the page.

What leaves the page, and only when you ask

  • Download the CSV, Save the map and Download the settings create files through your browser's normal download.
  • Copy to clipboard and Copy a link to this view write to your clipboard.
  • A copied link contains your settings in its address (for example the base colour and viewing distance). Anyone you send it to can read them.

The rest of the page

The tool page loads the site's usual pages, styles and scripts, and, depending on your cookie choices, advertising and consent-based analytics, like the rest of auricartisan.com. See the Privacy Policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/ and the Cookie Policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/.
