Troubleshooting Material Under Light

Symptoms, causes and fixes for Material Under Light: black samples, disabled controls, clipping, refused files and links.

The sphere and swatch are black

Why: you chose Conductor with the Oren-Nayar lobe. A conductor has no diffuse term, and Oren-Nayar has no specular term, so nothing is reflected.

What to do: choose Dielectric, or a specular lobe such as GGX, Beckmann, Ward or Ashikhmin-Shirley.

The Temperature slider does nothing or is greyed out

Why: it is used only by Blackbody at a temperature you choose and Daylight at a temperature you choose. Every other light has its own fixed definition.

What to do: choose one of those two options.

I set a daylight below 4000 K but the readout says 4000 K

Why: the CIE daylight model is defined from 4000 K upwards, so lower values are raised to 4000 K. The exports record this.

What to do: use Blackbody at a temperature you choose for warmer sources.

"Measured temperature" is highlighted and mentions McCamy

Why: above 7000 K the colour-temperature formula is outside its accurate range; the error band grows to ±139 K and beyond.

What to do: nothing; the reference chosen above 5000 K is a daylight either way. Treat the temperature as approximate.

"clipped to sRGB" appears, or "Inside sRGB" reads "no" or "the highlight is not"

Why: the material's colour, or its highlight, is outside what sRGB can show; the swatch and sphere show the nearest displayable colour. Saturated materials such as the default gold, and bright highlights on shiny surfaces, often do this.

What to do: nothing is wrong; the L*a*b* values are computed before clipping. Raise Roughness or change the angles to dim the highlight.

Moving "Seen from" or "Between them" does not change the sphere

Why: the sphere is always drawn seen head-on, lit from Light from. The view angles set the geometry of the single point the readouts and swatches describe.

What to do: read the swatch and the readouts, which do change.

The Refractive index slider is greyed out

Why: it applies only to dielectrics.

What to do: select Dielectric.

Every material looks similar under E or a high-temperature light

Why: the presets are smooth invented curves, and the lobe mixes in a colourless highlight on dielectrics.

What to do: compare L*a*b* values rather than swatches, or load a measured reflectance.

My reflectance file is refused

MessageFix
No two-column numeric rows in that fileMake sure each data line has two numbers: wavelength and reflectance
Not installed: fewer than eight rowsSupply at least eight rows
Not installed: covers X to Y nm; at least 420 to 680 is needed …Extend the data to cover 420–680 nm

A file in percent is converted automatically. A file mixing fractions and percentages is read as percent throughout.

The material menu shows only my file

Why: a loaded reflectance replaces the twenty presets until you remove it.

What to do: select Go back to the presets on the Data tab.

A file chooser opens when I click the spectrum

Why: the plot is also a drop zone for reflectance files; clicking it opens a chooser.

What to do: cancel it if you did not mean to load a file.

The sweep result is out of date

Why: the sweep is not rerun when the scene changes.

What to do: select Run the sweep again on the Metrics tab before exporting.

A link did not restore my measured material

Why: links carry the light, preset material, surface, lobe and angles only.

What to do: load the file again after opening the link.
