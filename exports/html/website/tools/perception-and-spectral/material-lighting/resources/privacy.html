Privacy

What Material Under Light keeps in your browser and what uses the network.

What stays in your browser

  • Every calculation: spectra, the sphere, the readouts, the reference comparison and the sweep are computed in the page.
  • Reflectance files you load are read from your disk by the page. They are not uploaded and are forgotten when you reload.

What leaves the page, and only when you ask

  • Save the spectrum, Download the spectrum and Download the scene create files through your browser's normal download. They include the name of any reflectance file you loaded.
  • Copy a link to this scene writes a link to your clipboard. It contains your scene settings, never your files.

The rest of the page

The tool page loads the site's usual pages, styles and scripts, and, depending on your cookie choices, advertising and consent-based analytics, like the rest of auricartisan.com. See the Privacy Policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/ and the Cookie Policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/.
