Get started with the navigation dock

Open the navigation dock and learn what each of its buttons does.

Open the dock

When a page loads on a computer, the dock shows fully for a moment and then tucks itself away into a thin gold line at the edge of the window. To bring it back:

  • point at the line; or
  • click it; or
  • move keyboard focus into it with Tab.

It tucks away again about half a second after your pointer leaves, unless the page outline is open or a button in the dock has keyboard focus.

On a phone, open the page utilities bar at the bottom-left and select Navigate.

The buttons

From top to bottom (left to right on a phone):

ButtonWhat it does
Grip (six dots)Drag to move the dock. Tooltip: "Drag to move". Not shown on phones.
BackGoes back one page. Greyed out when there is nothing to go back to.
ForwardGoes forward one page. Greyed out when there is nothing ahead.
ReloadReloads the page.
Page outlineOpens the On this page list of headings.
Open split viewOpens split view with this page (see Split view).
Record screen (Alt+Shift+R)Opens the screen recorder (see Screen recorder).
Copy linkCopies the page's address. The icon briefly becomes a tick.
ShareOpens your device's share sheet; without one, copies the link and shows a tick.
Scroll to topScrolls smoothly to the top. The ring around it shows how far down you are.
Scroll to bottomScrolls smoothly to the end of the page.
Flip dock sideMoves the dock to the other edge. Not shown on phones.

Hover or focus a button to see its name in a tooltip.

Note: Copy link and Share copy the address exactly as it is in the address bar, which can include the page's remembered state, such as your scroll position and the values you typed into a tool. See Shareable links.

Try it

  1. Open a long page, such as https://auricartisan.com/company/about/.
  2. Scroll halfway down. Point at the dock: the ring around Scroll to top is half full.
  3. Select Page outline. The heading of the section you are reading is highlighted.
  4. Select the first heading. The page scrolls up to it.
  5. Select Copy link and paste the address somewhere. Opening it later returns you to the same place on the page.
