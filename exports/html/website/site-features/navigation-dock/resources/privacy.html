Navigation dock privacy

What the navigation dock stores and what its buttons share.

  • The dock remembers only which side of the window it is on and how high it sits, in this browser.
  • Copy link puts the page address on your clipboard. Share hands it to your device's share sheet. Neither sends anything to Auric Artisan.
  • The address can include the page's remembered state after #p=, such as values you typed into a tool. Check the link before you share it if that matters. See Shareable links.
