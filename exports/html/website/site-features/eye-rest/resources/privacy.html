Eye rest privacy

What eye rest stores and sends.

  • Your on or off choice and your interval are kept in this browser, as part of your settings. Turn these off is remembered in this browser too, and Not this session in this tab only.
  • Eye rest does not record when you rest, how often you skip, or anything about your activity, and it sends nothing over the network.
  • The cookie policy lists the eye rest preference under Functional storage.
