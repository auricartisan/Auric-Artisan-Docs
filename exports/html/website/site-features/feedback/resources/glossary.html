Feedback glossary

Terms used in the feedback guides.

TermMeaning
Automatic promptThe feedback panel the site shows by itself after 20 minutes of active use.
Active timeTime with the page visible on screen; time in a background tab does not count.
Quiet periodThe time after a prompt during which the site does not ask again.
Anonymous feedbackFeedback sent without a name or email.
Bug reportA description of something that went wrong, sent with details of the page.
SeverityHow badly a bug affects you: Minor, Normal or Blocking.
DraftText you have typed but not sent, kept on your device.
