Troubleshooting feedback

Fixes for common feedback problems.

The prompt never appears

Why: it needs 20 minutes of active time on one page, it does not appear on sign-in, checkout, payment or legal pages, it waits at least a week between prompts, and it stops for good after Don't ask again.

What to do: use the Feature Kit's Feedback panel whenever you want to give feedback.

I chose Don't ask again by mistake

Why: the choice is kept in this browser.

What to do: use the Feature Kit's Feedback panel, which is always available. Clearing the site's data in your browser also resets the choice.

Continue is greyed out

Why: it needs a star rating or a comment.

Send report is greyed out

Why: What happened? is required.

Pressing a number changed my rating

Why: the keys 1 to 5 set the rating when the comment box does not have focus.

What to do: click in the comment box before typing numbers.

I was offline when I sent feedback

It is kept on your device and sent automatically when you are back online.
