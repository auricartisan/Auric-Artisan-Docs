Screen recorder privacy

What the recorder captures, where recordings are kept, and what is shared.

  • Permission first. Nothing is captured until you start a recording and, for screen, window, tab and region sources, allow sharing in your browser's dialog. The microphone is used only if you switch it on and allow it.
  • What is captured. Exactly what you shared: a screen or window can include other apps, notifications and personal information. Check what is visible before you record.
  • Where recordings live. In your browser's storage for auricartisan.com, on this device only. They are not uploaded, not added to your account and not placed in your Library.
  • Sharing. A recording leaves your device only when you download it and send the file yourself.
  • Preferences. Your recorder settings and the dialog position are kept in this browser.
  • Deleting. Delete in My recordings removes a recording. Clearing site data for auricartisan.com removes them all.
