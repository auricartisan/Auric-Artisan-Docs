Settings panel shortcuts

Keyboard shortcuts that open the Settings panel, move around it, and change settings from anywhere on the site.

Anywhere on the site

KeysAction
Ctrl + , (Cmd + , on a Mac)Open or close the Settings panel
Alt + RTurn reading mode on or off
Alt + TSwitch theme: Light, then Dark, then System
Alt + +Make text one step larger
Alt + -Make text one step smaller
Alt + 0Return text to the default size
Alt + MTurn reduced motion on or off

The Alt shortcuts are ignored while you type in a text field.

Inside the panel

KeysAction
Left / Right arrow on the section listMove to the previous or next section
Tab / Shift + TabMove between controls
Space or Enter on a switch or buttonChange it
EscClose an open drop-down list first, then the panel
Esc in the Reset all confirmationCancel the reset
Tab in the Reset all confirmationStays inside the confirmation until you choose

For every shortcut on the site, see Keyboard shortcuts.
