Settings panel glossary

Plain-language definitions of the terms used in the Settings panel.

TermMeaning
AccentThe site's highlight colour for links, buttons, active tabs, focus rings, kit launchers and tooltip borders. Gold by default.
Contrast ratioThe WCAG measure of how different two colours are in lightness, from 1:1 (none) to 21:1 (black on white).
WCAGThe Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, the international standard for accessible web content.
AA, AAAWCAG contrast levels for body text: AA needs 4.5:1, the stricter AAA needs 7:1.
AA LargeA contrast of at least 3:1, accepted by WCAG for large text and interface parts but not body text.
EyedropperA browser tool that lets you click any pixel on your screen to read its colour.
Hex colourA colour written as # and six hexadecimal digits for red, green and blue, for example #D3AF37.
Local storageA small store inside your browser where a website keeps data on your device.
ModelessA panel that does not block the page behind it. You can keep using the page while it is open.
Reading modeA distraction-free layout with a narrow text column, larger type and no floating controls.
Saved colorsYour own palette of up to 18 accent colours, added only when you select the + button.
SectionOne of the groups in the panel's list: Appearance, Comfort, Highlights, Clock, Tooltips, Ads, Shortcuts.
System themeA theme setting that follows your device's light or dark preference and changes when it changes.
ToastA short message that appears briefly to confirm an action, such as "Settings exported".
TooltipThe small card that appears when you hover over or focus a control, explaining what it does.
