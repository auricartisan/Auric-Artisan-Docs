Tooltips and ads

Turn the site's tooltips off or change how much they show, and adjust ad preferences on paid plans.

Tooltips

Most buttons, links and cards on the site show a tooltip when you hover over them or move keyboard focus to them: a small card with a title, a description and sometimes a picture, tags, a keyboard shortcut and extra details.

The Tooltips section starts with a Live preview (how tooltips will look). It shows a sample tooltip for the Design System Generator with a picture, three tags, a description, the Ctrl + K shortcut and a details table, and it changes as you flip the switches below it.

SwitchWhat it doesDefault
Show tooltipsShows the rich hover and focus tooltips across the site. Off hides them; the preview reads "Tooltips are turned off".On
Detailed tooltipsExpands the collapsible details: preview image, metadata and tags. Off keeps tooltips compact: title, description and shortcut.On
Advanced tooltipsAlways expands the data section (category, route, read time). Off keeps it collapsed.Off

See more details in one tooltip

With Advanced tooltips off, a tooltip that has extra data shows an Alt — More details hint at its foot. While the tooltip is open, press Alt to expand the data section for that tooltip only. The next tooltip starts collapsed again.

Restore section defaults returns the three switches to their defaults.

Ads

The Ads section appears only while the site is showing ads. It opens with the note Ad preferences — "paid perk — free plan shows standard ads".

ControlWhat it doesValuesDefault
Show adsShows sponsored slots across the siteOn, offOn
Ad densityHow many in-content slots appear per pageStandard, ReducedStandard
Ads in panels & searchShows the compact sponsored strip inside kits, Spotlight and search resultsOn, offOn

Ad density is dimmed while Show ads is off.

Who can change these

Ad preferences are a paid perk. Every tool on the site stays free on every plan either way.

  • Signed out or on the free plan: the controls are shown but disabled. Selecting one opens an upgrade message: "Ad preferences are a paid perk — upgrade to hide ads or tune where they appear. Every tool stays free either way." Nothing changes until you upgrade.
  • On a plan that includes the ad-free perk: the controls work like any other setting.

Plans and the ad-free perk are explained in Account and billing.

Restore section defaults returns the three ad controls to their defaults.
