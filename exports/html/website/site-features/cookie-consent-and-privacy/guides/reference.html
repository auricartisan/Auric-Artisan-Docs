Cookie consent reference

The cookie categories, every listed cookie, the consent controls and their messages.

Categories

CategoryCan be turned offDescription on the Cookie Policy page (summary)
EssentialNo (Always Active)Core functions: authentication, security, session management and access control
FunctionalYesRemembers settings such as theme, language, layout choices and form data
AnalyticsYesHow visitors use the platform; aggregate or pseudonymous, never used for advertising profiles
MarketingYesReserved for campaign attribution if enabled later; no marketing pixels or cross-site profiles now

Listed cookies

CookieCategoryPurposeDurationType
aa_sidEssentialSigned-in session management and authenticationUp to 30 daysFirst-party
aa_csrfEssentialCross-site request forgery protectionUp to 30 daysFirst-party
aa_cookie_consentEssentialStores your cookie preferences1 yearFirst-party
aa_themeFunctionalStores light/dark mode preference1 yearFirst-party
aa_langFunctionalLanguage and locale preference1 yearFirst-party
aa_eye_restFunctionalEye rest feature interval and duration settings1 yearFirst-party
aa_layoutFunctionalGrid/list view and sidebar preferences6 monthsFirst-party
aa_recentFunctionalRecently viewed items for quick access30 daysFirst-party
aa_analytics_idAnalyticsAnonymous visitor identifier for usage metrics1 yearFirst-party
aa_perfAnalyticsPage load and interaction performance timingSessionFirst-party

Marketing lists no cookies.

Consent bar

ButtonEffect
Accept AllAll four categories on; saved
Reject AllEssential only; non-essential cookies deleted; saved
CustomiseOpens Manage Cookie Preferences on the Cookie Policy page

Preferences section (Cookie Policy page)

ControlEffect
Category switchesChoose each optional category
Show cookies / Hide cookiesShows or hides the category's cookie table
Accept AllSets every optional switch on (not saved until Save Preferences)
Reject AllSets every optional switch off (not saved until Save Preferences)
Save PreferencesSaves the switches; deletes cookies of switched-off categories
Clear All CookiesDeletes non-essential cookies and forgets your choice
Withdraw ConsentForgets your choice and deletes non-essential cookies
Consent recordedDate and time of your saved choice

Messages

MessageWhen
Cookie preferences saved successfully.After Save Preferences
All non-essential cookies have been cleared.After Clear All Cookies
Cookie consent withdrawn. Preferences reset.After Withdraw Consent

Defaults before any choice

Functional on; Analytics off; Marketing off. No analytics events are recorded until you give Analytics consent.
