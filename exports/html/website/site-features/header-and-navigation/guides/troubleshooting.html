Troubleshooting the header and navigation

Fixes for common problems with the header, the drawer, the page utilities bar and the footer.

The header shows a menu button instead of Company, Tools and Learn

Why: your window is narrower than 960 pixels, or your browser is zoomed in far enough to make the page that narrow.

What to do: use the drawer, or widen the window or zoom out (Ctrl + -) to see the full header.

A header menu closed before I could click an entry

Why: menus close when keyboard focus or a click moves outside them.

What to do: open the menu again and select the entry directly. On a touch screen, use the drawer, whose sections stay open until you close them.

I cannot see the bottom of a long menu

Why: tall menus scroll inside themselves.

What to do: scroll inside the menu. The SCROLL DOWN hint shows while more entries are below.

The page does not scroll while the drawer is open

Why: this is intended, so that swiping inside the drawer does not move the page behind it.

What to do: close the drawer with the menu button, Esc, or a tap on the dimmed area.

The floating buttons disappeared

Why: the page utilities button and other floating buttons are hidden while the mobile drawer, the screen saver or an eye rest is showing.

What to do: close the drawer, move the mouse or press a key to leave the screen saver, or finish or end the eye rest.

The theme buttons in the drawer do not match what I see

Why: the drawer shows the stored choice: Auto follows your device, so a dark page can show Auto selected.

What to do: select Light or Dark to fix the theme regardless of your device.

The Library button does nothing

Why: the library panel loads a moment after the page.

What to do: wait until the page has finished loading and select it again, or use Ctrl + Shift + L.
