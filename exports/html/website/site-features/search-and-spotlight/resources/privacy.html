Spotlight privacy

What Spotlight stores and what it sends over the network.

Stored in your browser

  • Your last six searches (the Recent list). Select Clear to delete them.
  • A small record of which results you have opened from search, used only to rank those pages a little higher for you next time.

Both stay in this browser and are not sent to Auric Artisan.

Uses the network

  • Site searches run inside your browser against the site's search index, which is downloaded from auricartisan.com. Your query is not sent anywhere.
  • Web searches send your query to the site's web search service to find matches in trusted outside sources.
  • Opening a web result opens that outside website in a new tab.
