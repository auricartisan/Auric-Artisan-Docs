Info bars and notices glossary

Terms used in the info bars and notices guides.

TermMeaning
Info barA slim strip under the header: welcome, announcement or quick links.
Clock stripA live clock under the header with the date and time zone.
Hero noticeA highlighted banner in the homepage's opening section.
Demo chipA small label marking a live or demo section.
Time zoneA region's standard time, such as Asia/Kolkata or Europe/London.
UTC offsetHow far a time zone is ahead of or behind Coordinated Universal Time.
ISO dateA date written year-month-day, for example 2026-09-24.
Status messageA short message at the edge of the screen about the connection or the app.
