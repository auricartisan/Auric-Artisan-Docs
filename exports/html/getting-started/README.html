Getting started with Auric Artisan

Where to begin with Auric Artisan — what it is, how its parts fit together, and the first things to set up.

This folder is the front door to the Auric Artisan documentation. It explains what Auric Artisan is, how the website, the extensions and the services fit together, and how to get from your first visit to useful work in about an hour. You do not need an account for most of it.

Read the pages in order if you are new, or jump to the one that answers your question.
