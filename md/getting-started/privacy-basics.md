# Privacy basics

This page is a short orientation. The detailed version is [Privacy overview](../help/privacy.md), and the official, authoritative text is the Privacy Policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/ and the Cookie Policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/.

## The short version

- Most tools run in your browser. Colour calculations, palettes, vision simulations and theme generation are done locally where possible.
- Your saved work and your preferences are stored in your browser on your device.
- An account stores the details you give it (email, names, profile fields) and the security records needed to keep it safe.
- Analytics is first-party, sets no cookies, and runs only if you consent to the Analytics category.
- Payments go to Razorpay. Auric Artisan never sees or stores your card, UPI or bank details.
- The Privacy Policy states that Auric Artisan does not sell personal data or use it for behavioural advertising.

## What stays in your browser

| Kept in your browser | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Saved work | Palettes, gradients, collections and exports in My Library |
| Drafts | Tool state and your Portfolio Builder draft |
| Preferences | Theme, language, accent colour, text size, eye-rest and other settings |
| Your cookie choice | The consent record from the cookie banner |

The Privacy Policy says many preferences stay on your device and are not sent to Auric Artisan's servers unless a tool needs them. Because this data lives in the browser, clearing your browser's site data removes it, and it does not follow you to another device. See [Clear data and start fresh](../help/troubleshooting.md#clear-site-data-and-start-fresh).

## What goes to a server

| Sent to Auric Artisan's servers | When |
| --- | --- |
| Account details | When you sign up, sign in, or edit your profile |
| Security records | Sessions, sign-in attempts and security events |
| Web analysis inputs | When a tool fetches or analyses a URL for you |
| Server-backed tool work | When you start a run that uses tokens |
| Messages | When you use the contact form |
| Order records | When you buy something (the item, amount and payment reference — not card details) |
| Analytics events | Only with your consent: an event name, the page path (never query strings), the referrer's origin and a few coarse properties such as device class |

## Cookies and consent

On your first visit a bar at the bottom of the page, **We value your privacy**, offers three choices:

- **Accept All** — turns on every optional category.
- **Reject All** — keeps only what is strictly necessary.
- **Customise** — opens the preferences section of the Cookie Policy page, where you choose each category.

Essential cookies (for example the signed-in session and its security companion) are always on because the site cannot work securely without them. You can change your choice at any time on https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/ or from **Settings**. The full walkthrough is in [Cookie consent and privacy](../website/site-features/cookie-consent-and-privacy/README.md).

Analytics also stays off when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, as the Privacy Policy states.

## Third parties

The Privacy Policy names three: Cloudflare (hosting and storage), Razorpay (payments) and Google Fonts (font files on some pages, which means Google receives the technical request, such as your IP address). The Security page adds Resend for email delivery.

## The browser extension

The extension has its own policy: it only reads a page when you use a tool on it, keeps everything in your browser's extension storage, and makes no network requests with your data. See [Extension privacy policy](../website/company-and-legal/docs/extension-privacy-policy.md).
