# Create an account (optional)

You do not need an account to use Auric Artisan's tools. The pricing page puts it plainly: every tool is free "with no login required to start". An account adds a few things that need to be tied to you rather than to one browser.

## What works without an account

- Every website tool, including LUT Lab, the ICC Parser, the Color Science Lab and the clinical vision simulations.
- The libraries, the Learning Hub, the documentation and the blog.
- Saving palettes, gradients and collections to **My Library** in your browser.
- Drafting a portfolio in the Portfolio Builder.
- The browser extension and the VS Code extensions.

## What an account adds

| With an account you can… | Why it needs one |
| --- | --- |
| Run server-backed Analyzer work, such as whole-site crawls | It is paid for with tokens, and tokens belong to an account. The free plan includes 100 starter tokens. |
| Buy a paid plan pass | A pass belongs to your account so it follows you to other devices. An account is required before any payment. |
| Publish your portfolio at your own address | The published page is linked to your username. |
| Create API keys for the REST API | Keys and credits are counted per account (API access is included from the Specialist plan). |
| See signed-in devices, turn on two-step verification and export your data | These are account security controls. |
| Approve the Auric command-line tool on a new computer | Device approval signs the tool in as you. |

## Sign up in short

1. Go to https://auricartisan.com/auth/ (or select **Create account** in the header).
2. Select the **Create Account** tab.
3. Fill in **Display Name**, **Username**, **Email**, **Password** and **Confirm Password**. Passwords need 12–128 characters with upper and lower case letters, a number and a symbol.
4. Tick **I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy**.
5. Select **Create Account** and complete the short **Auric human check** (repeat a sequence of three marks).
6. Open the verification email and confirm your address within 24 hours.

Or select **Google** or **GitHub** under **or sign up with** to use an existing account with one of those providers.

The full walkthrough, including every rule and error message, is in [Sign up](../website/account-and-billing/docs/sign-up.md).

> **Note:** Until you verify your email address, the account works as a limited workspace. The sign-in page says billing, promotions, API keys and device approval stay locked until the address is verified.

## After you sign up

- Your dashboard is at https://auricartisan.com/auth/dashboard/. See [The dashboard](../website/account-and-billing/docs/getting-started.md).
- You start on the free **Apprentice** plan. Nothing is charged, and no card is asked for.
- Consider turning on two-step verification: [Two-step verification](../website/account-and-billing/docs/two-step-verification.md).
