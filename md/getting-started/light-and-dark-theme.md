# Light and dark theme

The website has a light theme and a dark theme. By default it follows your device: if your operating system or browser is set to dark mode, the site opens dark.

## Choose a theme

| Where | Control | Choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Header | The theme button (label "Toggle dark mode") | Switches between light and dark |
| Mobile navigation menu | **Preferences** › **Theme** | **Auto**, **Light**, **Dark** |
| Settings (`Ctrl` + `,`) | **Appearance** › **Theme** | **Light**, **Dark**, **System** |
| Keyboard | `Alt` + `T` | Cycles light, then dark, then system |

**Auto** and **System** mean the same thing: follow your device's setting, and change when it changes.

## How the choice is remembered

- A choice you make is saved in this browser and applies to every page, including after a refresh.
- An explicit Light or Dark choice wins over your device setting until you pick **System** or **Auto** again.
- The choice is per browser. Another browser or device starts by following that device's setting.

## Accent colour and comfort options

The same **Settings** panel lets you pick an accent colour, change text size, reduce motion or transparency, boost contrast and more. See [Settings panel](../website/site-features/settings-panel/README.md) and [Accessibility of the site](../help/accessibility.md).
