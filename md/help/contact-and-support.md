# Contact and support

Every message is read and answered by a person — the contact page says there is "no ticket queue and no bot in front of it". The contact form is at https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/.

## Before you write

- Many questions are answered in the [FAQ](faq.md), the [FAQ page](https://auricartisan.com/company/faq/) on the site, or [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
- Inside the site, press `?` for the Guide or `Ctrl` + `K` to search.
- Each topic on the contact page shows links that might answer your question faster.

## Choose the right topic

The topic decides which inbox receives your message and how quickly you can expect a reply.

| Topic | Goes to | Use it for | Reference field asks for | Reply time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Using a tool** | hello@auricartisan.com | How to do something | Link to the tool or page | 2–3 days |
| **Billing** | hello@auricartisan.com | Payments, refunds, receipts, discounts | Order or invoice ID (starts with `order_`) | 1–2 days |
| **Something else** | hello@auricartisan.com | Anything not listed | Anything that helps place it | Up to a week |
| **A bug** | developer@auricartisan.com | Something broken, including accessibility barriers | URL where it happened | 2–4 days |
| **API or integration** | developer@auricartisan.com | The REST API, keys, SDKs | Endpoint, or a request ID | 2–4 days |
| **Security** | security@auricartisan.com | A vulnerability | Affected URL or endpoint | Within 48 hours |
| **Privacy or data** | security@auricartisan.com | Data access, correction or deletion | Account email, if different | Within 30 days |

Reply times are those shown on the contact page; the page notes replies can be late at weekends or during a release.

## Send a message

1. Open https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/.
2. Under **What is this about?**, select a topic. (With the keyboard, the topics are one stop: use the arrow keys.)
3. Fill in **Name** and **Email** (both required). Use the email address on your account for account or billing questions.
4. Fill in the reference field — its label changes with the topic.
5. Write your **Message** (up to 2,000 characters; a counter shows how many you have used). The toolbar offers **Bold**, **Italic**, **Inline code**, **Bulleted list**, **Code block** and **Link**; `Ctrl` + `B`, `Ctrl` + `I` and `Ctrl` + `K` work too. The message is sent as plain text, and pasted text loses its formatting.
6. Optional: add up to four screenshots (see below).
7. Select **Send**.

Result: "Thanks. Your message was sent." You receive a confirmation at the address you gave. The reply comes from the topic's inbox, in the same email thread. If your message leads to a change, you get the link when it ships.

If something is missing, the form says so: "Name is required.", "Enter a valid email address.", "Message cannot be empty." or "Message exceeds 2000 characters." If sending fails: "Something went wrong. Please try again or email directly."

## Attach screenshots

- Select **Add an image**, paste a screenshot with `Ctrl` + `V`, or drag an image onto the message.
- Up to four images; PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP; up to 12 MB each. Large images are shrunk before sending.
- Select an attached image to mark it up with a **Pen**, **Highlighter** or **Box** in red, gold, blue or black, with **Undo** and **Clear all marks**, then **Save marks**.
- Remove an image with its remove button.

## Write a message that gets a fast answer

| Topic | Include |
| --- | --- |
| A bug | The page address, your browser and version, what you did, what happened, what you expected, and a screenshot |
| API | The request (method, endpoint and body), the response, and what you expected. Never send a full API key. |
| Billing | The order reference, the date, the amount and what you want |
| Colour-science inaccuracy | The tool, the input values, the output, what you expected, and the standard or paper you are checking against |
| Accessibility barrier | The page, the task, your assistive technology, and what stopped you |

Never send passwords, backup codes, full card numbers or full API keys.

## Security reports

Email security@auricartisan.com, or use the **Security** topic (https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/?topic=security opens it directly). Send the location and the impact, not a working exploit; a way to send the rest will follow. The Security page aims to acknowledge within 24 hours and states that researchers acting in good faith will not face legal action. See [Security page summary](../website/company-and-legal/docs/security-page.md#responsible-disclosure).

## Open the form on a topic

Add `?topic=` and one of `support`, `billing`, `other`, `bug`, `api`, `security` or `privacy` to the contact address, for example https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/?topic=billing.
