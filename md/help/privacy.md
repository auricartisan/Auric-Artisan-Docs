# Privacy overview

This page brings together what the policies and the product say about your data. The authoritative documents are the Privacy Policy (https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/), the Cookie Policy (https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/), the Data Retention and Deletion Policy and, for the browser extension, the Extension Privacy Policy. Plain-language summaries of each are in [Company and legal](../website/company-and-legal/README.md).

## Stored in your browser

| What | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| My Library (palettes, gradients, collections, exports) | Stays on this device; not uploaded unless you publish or export it |
| Tool state and drafts, including the Portfolio Builder draft | Stays on this device |
| Settings: theme, language, accent colour, text size, comfort options, eye rest | The Privacy Policy says many preferences are not sent to the servers |
| Your cookie choice | Kept for up to a year |
| The offline cache, if you prepared one | The site's pages and data, for offline use |
| Your sign-in (session and CSRF cookies) | Only when you sign in; up to 30 days or until sign-out |

Anything in your browser is lost if you clear site data, and does not follow you to other devices. See [Clear site data and start fresh](troubleshooting.md#clear-site-data-and-start-fresh).

## Sent to Auric Artisan's servers

| What | When |
| --- | --- |
| Account details and profile fields | When you sign up, sign in or edit your profile |
| Security records: sessions, sign-in attempts, security events | While you use an account |
| Web analysis inputs: URLs, page metadata, screenshots, extracted colours, scan results | When a tool analyses a page for you. The FAQ page says no URL history or page content is kept after the analysis session. |
| Server-backed tool work | When you start a run that uses tokens |
| Contact messages and attachments | When you use the contact form |
| Order records: item, amount, payment reference | When you buy something |
| Analytics events | Only with consent (below) |

The Privacy Policy says colour calculations, palettes, vision simulations and theme generation run in your browser where possible and are not sent anywhere.

## Analytics and consent

- Analytics is first-party: events go to Auric Artisan's own servers. There are no third-party analytics scripts and no analytics cookies.
- Events are sent only if you accepted the **Analytics** category in the cookie bar or on the Cookie Policy page. No choice means no events.
- Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control in your browser turn analytics off regardless.
- An event contains the event name, the page path (never the query string), the referrer's origin, and a few coarse properties such as device class.

The dashboard's **Usage data** switch (in **Security & Privacy**) records your choice on your account; the cookie choice is what governs analytics on the website.

## Third parties

| Company | Role (as the policies describe it) |
| --- | --- |
| Cloudflare | Hosting, DNS, CDN, compute and storage for account, preference and order data |
| Razorpay | Payments. Card, UPI and bank details go to Razorpay directly and never reach Auric Artisan. |
| Google Fonts | Font files on some pages; Google receives the technical request, such as your IP address |
| Resend | Email delivery (named on the Security page) |

Free-plan pages can show sponsored slots. The Subscription and Billing Policy says ads shown to free users are limited and consent-gated, and that private data is not used for advertising profiles.

## What each product collects

| Product | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| Website, no account | Your browser keeps your work and settings; servers see page requests, tool inputs for server-backed work, and analytics only with consent |
| Website, with account | The above, plus account, security and order records |
| Browser extension | Reads a page only when you use a tool on it; stores everything in the browser's extension storage; no analytics, telemetry or network requests with your data |
| VS Code extensions | Described on https://auricartisan.com/vscode/ as needing no account and sending no telemetry |
| REST API | Your requests and usage, metered against your account |

## No sale, no tracking across sites

The Privacy Policy states that Auric Artisan does not sell, rent or trade personal data, does not take part in data-broker networks or cross-site tracking, and does not use personal data for behavioural advertising or automated decisions with legal effects.

## Your rights, in practice

| Right | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Access or a copy | Dashboard › **Security & Privacy** › **Export**; for more, contact topic **Privacy or data** |
| Correction | Dashboard › **Profile**; for your email address, contact support |
| Deletion | Dashboard danger zone, or contact topic **Privacy or data** |
| Withdraw consent | Cookie Policy page › **Withdraw Consent**, or change the switches |
| Object or complain | Contact topic **Privacy or data** |

**Privacy or data** messages go to security@auricartisan.com, with a stated reply time of within 30 days. The Cookie Policy also lists privacy@auricartisan.com for cookie questions.
