# Licences

Every icon on the CDN is free for commercial use, redistribution, embedding and modification, under the conditions of its set's own open-source licence. Auric Artisan does not claim ownership of any icon: each set remains the property of its authors and is redistributed under its original licence. The licence reference at https://icons.auricartisan.com/LICENSE.md gives the details and the full licence texts.

| Remix Icon License v1.0 | Yes | Yes | Yes | A permissive, Apache-2.0-style licence; see its text |

Lucide is a community fork of Feather; the original Feather icons it derives from remain under MIT.

## Brand icons

Some sets include logos of brands and companies. Those logos remain trademarks of their owners, and are provided for identification, not endorsement. Using a logo may need its owner's permission, whatever the set's licence says.

## Complying

1. When you ship icons in a product or a download, include the relevant licence notices, for example in your credits or a licence file.
2. If you change Material icon files, note your changes, as Apache 2.0 requires.
3. Check brand and company logos separately.
