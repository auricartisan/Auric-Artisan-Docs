# Privacy

This page describes what the account area stores and where. The authoritative documents are the Privacy Policy (https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/), the Cookie Policy (https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/) and the Data Retention and Deletion Policy (https://auricartisan.com/legal/data-retention-and-deletion-policy/).

## Stored with your account

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Email address, display name, username | To identify you and sign you in |
| Bio, website, location, profile photo | What you chose to put on your profile |
| Password (as a one-way hash only) | To check your password; it cannot be read back |
| Two-step secret (encrypted) and backup codes | To check your codes |
| Sessions and sign-in records | To list your devices and protect the account |
| Dashboard preferences, including the **Usage data** choice | So they follow you between browsers |
| Plan, orders and payments | To give you what you paid for and keep receipts |
| Token and API credit balances and their history | To meter and show your usage |
| API keys (as a hash and a short prefix only) | To recognise your keys; the full key is shown once |
| Published portfolio | To serve your public page |

## Kept in your browser, not the account

- **My Library** — palettes, gradients, collections and exports.
- **Portfolio draft** and its counters (views, downloads, shares).
- **Site preferences** — theme, language, accent colour, text size and other settings.
- **Your cookie choice.**
- **Dismissed dashboard suggestions** and the **Your account is nearly there** dismissal.
- **An API key pasted into Live usage** — kept in that browser tab only, and forgotten when it closes.

Clearing your browser's site data removes these. They do not follow you to another device.

## Never reaches Auric Artisan

- Card numbers, UPI IDs and bank credentials. They are entered into Razorpay's checkout and go to Razorpay directly. Auric Artisan keeps only order records: what was bought, the amount and the payment reference.
- Your password in readable form.

## Cookies the account uses

The Cookie Policy lists two essential cookies for signed-in use: a session cookie and a cross-site request forgery (CSRF) protection cookie, each lasting up to 30 days or until you sign out. They cannot be switched off, because the account cannot work securely without them.

## Exporting and deleting

- **Export** — **Security & Privacy** › **Export everything** downloads your account details, preferences and this browser's library and portfolio draft. See [Export your data](../docs/export-your-data.md).
- **Delete** — the danger zone deletes the account. Personal details are removed; payment records are kept in anonymised form for tax law. See [Delete your account](../docs/delete-your-account.md).
- **Other requests** — access, correction or a fuller copy: use the contact page, topic **Privacy or data**.
