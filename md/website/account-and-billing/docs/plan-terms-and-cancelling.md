# Plan terms, renewal and cancelling

Every paid plan sold on Auric Artisan is a one-time purchase of a fixed period — one month or twelve. This page explains what that means in practice.

## Nothing renews

- No recurring subscription is created when you buy a plan.
- No payment mandate, standing instruction or saved card is kept for a future charge.
- No second charge is ever scheduled.

So there is no renewal to be surprised by, and nothing to cancel. The dashboard's plan line says so: for example "Artisan — active until 12 Oct 2026. Does not renew automatically."

> **Note:** Some policy pages use the word "subscription". The Terms of Service explain that, on Auric Artisan, it means the plan record and its billing period — not a recurring charge.

## When a pass ends

The pass ends on its own at the end of the term you bought. You do not need to do anything.

- The account returns to the free **Apprentice** plan.
- Every tool keeps working.
- Your saved work is not touched. It lives in your browser, so it stays exactly where it is whatever your plan does.
- The paid conveniences stop: ad-free browsing, watermark-free exports, the monthly token allowance and any higher limits.
- The plan's included API credit allowance ends with it. Purchased API credits stay on the account (see below).

## Buying another term

To continue, buy another pass from the pricing page when the current one ends, the same way you bought the first. See [Buy a plan](buy-a-plan.md). An upgrade starts as soon as the payment clears.

## Stopping early

Because there is no future charge, stopping early means asking for a refund rather than cancelling:

- Within 7 days of purchase, a refund is unconditional.
- After 7 days, refunds are considered case by case.

On a paid pass, **Plan & Usage** shows a **Request a refund** button where a cancel button would otherwise be. It opens the contact page. See [Refunds](refunds.md).

## Tokens and API credits when a plan ends

| Balance | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Included monthly tokens | Expire at the end of the billing period, as they would anyway |
| Purchased tokens | Stay on the account (token packs are not on sale today) |
| Included API credits | End with the paid period |
| Purchased API credits | Stay on the account and do not expire. They become spendable again if the account returns to a plan with API access. |

Overage is prepaid, so a plan ending never leaves an outstanding bill for API usage.

## If recurring billing is ever introduced

The Subscription and Billing Policy and the Refund Policy both promise that, if a genuine recurring subscription is offered later, checkout will present it as one, with the renewal date and amount shown before you authorise payment and a working cancel control in your account — and that the policies will be updated before the first such charge. The dashboard already has a **Cancel Subscription** button for that case; it appears only on a plan that actually renews.
