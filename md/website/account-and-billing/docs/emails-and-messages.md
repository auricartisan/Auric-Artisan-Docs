# Emails and messages

Your Auric Artisan account has no message inbox of its own. Everything the site needs to tell you arrives by email, at the address on your account, and replies to anything you send through the contact form arrive by email too.

## Emails the account sends

| Email | When it arrives | What to do with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verification link | After you sign up with email and password, or when you ask for a fresh link | Open it within 24 hours and select **Confirm email ownership**. See [Verify your email](verify-email.md). |
| Welcome | Once your account is usable — straight away for Google or GitHub sign-ups, after verification for email sign-ups | Nothing; it lists places to start: the tools, the Learn library and your dashboard. Replying reaches a person. |
| Password reset link | When you use **Forgot password?** | Open it within one hour. See [Passwords](passwords.md). |
| Receipt | After a purchase | Keep it; it carries your order reference, which starts with `order_`. |
| Contact form confirmation | When you send a message through the contact page | Nothing; it confirms the message arrived. |
| Reply to your message | When Auric Artisan answers | Reply in the same thread. |

Email is delivered through Resend, named on the Security page as a third-party provider.

## Where replies come from

The contact page routes each topic to one of three addresses, and replies come from the same one:

| Address | Topics |
| --- | --- |
| hello@auricartisan.com | **Using a tool**, **Billing**, **Something else** |
| developer@auricartisan.com | **A bug**, **API or integration** |
| security@auricartisan.com | **Security**, **Privacy or data** |

Invoices name support@auricartisan.com for questions about an invoice.

See [Contact and support](../../../help/contact-and-support.md) for reply times and how to write a useful message.

## Beware of look-alikes

Auric Artisan asks you to pay only through the Razorpay checkout on auricartisan.com. If an email asks you to pay somewhere else, or asks for your password or backup codes, do not act on it; report it through the contact page.

## Change the email address on the account

The email address cannot be changed from the dashboard yet. Its field in **Profile** is read-only and says "Email changes are not supported yet — contact support." Write through the contact page from your current address.
