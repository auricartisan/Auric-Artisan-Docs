# Status Kit privacy

## Read from your browser, shown only to you

The **System status** panel reads your connection estimate, battery, device memory, processor threads, memory use, storage use, cache count, screen and window size, pixel ratio, colour depth and gamut, frame rate, page load time, service worker state, app mode, language, time zone and cookie setting.

These readings are displayed in the panel and not stored or sent anywhere. They stop updating when you close the kit.

## Kept in your browser

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your last 8 scans: the path, target page title and status, page count and time | For **Recent scans** |
| Which result categories you collapsed | To keep them collapsed |
| Position, expanded size, Focus mode and theme | Shared kit preferences |

## Fetched from auricartisan.com

- When the kit first opens on a page: the central list of page statuses, the site map, and the current page (to read its title).
- During a scan: the Menu Kit's page list and each page in the scanned section.

These are ordinary page requests to auricartisan.com, like visiting the pages.
