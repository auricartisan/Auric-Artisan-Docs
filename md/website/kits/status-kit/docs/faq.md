# Status Kit FAQ

## Can I scan another website?

No. The scanner lists pages of auricartisan.com only. If you paste another site's address, only its path is used, and it is looked up on auricartisan.com. To check other websites, use the [URL Analyzer](../../../tools/accessibility-and-vision/analyzer/README.md).

## Does the Status Kit send my device details anywhere?

No. The readings are taken in your browser and shown only to you. See [Privacy](../others/privacy.md).

## Is the Speed reading a speed test?

No. It is your browser's rough estimate of your connection, not a measurement.

## Why is there no keyboard shortcut?

The Status Kit has none. Open it from **Page utilities** > **Status**.

## Does P3 gamut mean my screen is colour-accurate?

No. It means your browser reports that the display can show roughly the P3 range of colours. It says nothing about calibration.

## Can I export a scan?

No. Scan results stay in the panel. Your last eight scan paths are remembered under **Recent scans**.

## What counts as an issue?

Pages whose status is **Alpha**, **Deprecated** or **Archived**.
