# Update Kit FAQ

## Why does a website need updating?

Your browser, and the site's offline support, keep copies of the site's files so pages load fast and work offline. After a release, those copies can be older than the site. Updating replaces them.

## Will updating delete my saved work?

No. Library items, notes, calculator history, settings and your sign-in are kept. Only unsaved work in the page you are on is lost when it reloads.

## Is the Update Kit a panel I can open?

No. It is the notice and the **Update website** command. There is no launcher for it in the page utilities bar.

## How do I know which release I am on?

The notice names the new version. The site's release history is at https://auricartisan.com/library/changelog/.

## Does updating need a connection?

Yes. Files are re-downloaded from auricartisan.com.

## Should I update the installed app the same way?

Yes. The installed app is the same site, so the notice and the command work there too. See [Download and offline use](../../../download-and-offline/README.md).

## Does the Update Kit update my browser extension or VS Code extensions?

No. It only refreshes the website in your browser. Extensions update through their own stores.
