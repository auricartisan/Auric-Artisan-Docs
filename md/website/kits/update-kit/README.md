# Update Kit

The Update Kit keeps the copy of Auric Artisan in your browser up to date. Browsers and the site's offline support keep copies of the site's files so pages load quickly and work offline. After a new release, those copies can be out of date. The Update Kit notices when a newer release is out and lets you refresh everything in one step.

It has no panel. You meet it in two places: an **Update available** notice that appears at the top of the page when a new release is detected, and the **Update website** command in the site's right-click menu, also available as `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `U`.

Updating clears the site's cached files, re-downloads the files the current page uses, and reloads the page. Your saved library items, notes, settings and account are not touched.

## Who it is for

- Anyone who sees the **Update available** notice.
- Anyone whose page looks broken or out of date after a release.
- People who use the site offline or as an installed app, whose cached copy is used most.

## Where to find it

- The notice appears by itself at the top of any page of https://auricartisan.com when an update is available.
- Right-click anywhere on a page (outside text boxes) and choose **Update website**.
- Press `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `U` on any page.

## Quick start

1. When **Update available** appears at the top of the page, read the version it names, such as **Update available v0.9.11**.
2. Save any work in progress in the page you are on.
3. Select **Update now**.
4. The page reloads on the new release.

Or, at any time:

1. Press `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `U`.
2. A message reads **Checking for updates…**, then the site refreshes its files and reloads the page.

## What you can do

- See when a new release of the site is available, and which version.
- Update to it with one button, or put the notice away with **x** and update later.
- Force a refresh of the site's cached files at any time, from the right-click menu or the keyboard.
