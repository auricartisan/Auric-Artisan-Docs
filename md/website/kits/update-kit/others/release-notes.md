# Update Kit release notes

The website's public release history is at https://auricartisan.com/library/changelog/ and https://auricartisan.com/company/timeline/. It does not list the Update Kit by name.

## Recorded releases

| Release | Date | What the public history says |
|---|---|---|
| v0.9.9 | 5 June 2026 | PWA (installable app) support was added, the offline caching that the Update Kit refreshes |
| v0.9.11 | 12 August 2026 | The newest release in the public changelog at the time of writing |

## The Update Kit as of 24 September 2026

- Checks for a newer release on each page load and shows an **Update available** notice with **Update now** and **x**.
- **Update website** in the right-click menu, with states for available, updating and failed, and a banner at the top of the menu when an update is ready.
- `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `U` from any page.
- Updates clear the site's caches, refresh the service worker and the current page's files, and keep your saved data.

Changes not recorded in the public history are not listed here.
