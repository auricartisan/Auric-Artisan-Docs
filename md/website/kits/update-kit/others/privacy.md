# Update Kit privacy

## Stored in your browser

The release number you last updated to, so the site can tell when a newer one is out. Nothing else.

## Fetched from auricartisan.com

- On each page load: the site's release information, to find the newest release number.
- When you update: the files the current page uses, downloaded again.

These are ordinary requests to auricartisan.com. No information about you or your device is sent by the Update Kit.

## What an update deletes

The site's cached files in your browser. Your saved library, notes, settings and sign-in are not deleted.
