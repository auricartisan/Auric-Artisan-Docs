# Update Kit glossary

**Release** — A published version of the website, such as v0.9.11, listed in the changelog.

**Update notice** — The **Update available** message at the top of the page.

**Cache** — Copies of the site's files kept by your browser so pages load faster and work offline.

**Service worker** — A background script from the site that manages its offline copies.

**Hard reload** — A browser reload that ignores cached copies of the page's files.

**Installed app** — The site installed from your browser and opened in its own window.

**Site storage** — Data the site keeps in your browser, such as library items and settings. An update does not clear it.
