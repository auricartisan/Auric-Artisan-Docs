# Feature Kit

The Feature Kit is a floating toolbox that opens on any page of auricartisan.com. It holds six tools behind one menu: a **Scientific Calculator** with graphing and history, a **Unit Converter** with 1,344 units in 70 categories, a **Feedback** form, a Markdown **Notes** scratchpad, a **Date & Time** workbench and a set of **Developer Tools**.

The calculator is more than a keypad. Its expression bar understands variables, functions you define yourself (such as `f(x) = x^2 + 1`), statistics, number theory, physical constants and numerical calculus: derivatives, integrals, equation solving, sums and products. Results show as exact fractions when a tidy one exists, and any function can be plotted on a graph you can pan and zoom.

Everything except sending feedback and loading the unit list runs in your browser. Calculator history and notes are kept in your browser between visits.

## Who it is for

- Designers and developers who need a quick calculation, conversion, timestamp or hash while working in a tool.
- Students checking maths, including derivatives, integrals and statistics.
- Anyone who wants a scratchpad or a date calculator that stays one click away on every page.
- Visitors who want to tell the Auric Artisan team what works and what does not.

## Where to find it

- On any page of https://auricartisan.com, select **Page utilities** in the bottom-left corner, then **Feature**.
- Or press `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `K` (`Cmd` + `Shift` + `K` on a Mac).

## Quick start

1. Press `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `K`. The Feature Kit opens on its menu with six cards.
2. Press `1` to open **Scientific Calculator**.
3. Select the expression bar (the long box with the calculator icon) and type `integral(sin(x), 0, pi)`.
4. Press `Enter`. The display shows `2`.
5. Type `f(x) = x^2 - 2` and press `Enter`. The display confirms `ƒ f(x) defined`.
6. Type `solve(f(x), 1)` and press `Enter`. The display shows `1.41421356237`, the square root of 2.
7. Select the **Graph** button in the title bar and type `f(x)` in the **y =** box to see the curve.
8. Press `Esc` to go back to the menu, and `Esc` again to close the kit.

## What you can do

- Calculate with a Basic, Scientific or Programmer keypad, or type full expressions.
- Use variables, `ans`, your own functions, constants such as `pi`, `c` and `NA`, and memory keys.
- Differentiate, integrate, solve equations, and add or multiply series numerically.
- Work out mean, median, standard deviation, greatest common divisor, primes and more.
- Plot a function, pan and zoom the graph.
- Reuse or delete past calculations from the history.
- Convert between 1,344 units in 70 categories, from length and temperature to radiation dose and typography.
- Keep several Markdown notes that save as you type, preview them, copy them or export them as `.md` files.
- Pick dates on a calendar, count days and business days between dates, add or subtract days, weeks, months or years, and watch clocks in eight time zones.
- Format and inspect JSON, encode Base64 and URLs, hash text, generate UUIDs, decode JWTs, convert timestamps, test regular expressions, explain cron schedules, change text case and encode HTML entities.
- Send quick or detailed feedback about the site.
