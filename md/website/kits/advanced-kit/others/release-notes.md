## The Advanced Kit as of 24 September 2026

- Four illustrated mode cards: **Basic** (Recommended), **Advanced**, **Everything** and **Custom**.
- A **Current mode** badge and an illustrated mode summary with switch buttons.
- A Custom checklist of the site's tools, grouped by category, with **Filter tools…**, **Enable all**, **Disable all** and **Reset**.
- Quick cards for **Analyze URLs**, **Image Color Picker** and **Generate Ishihara Plates**.
- Shared kit controls: Focus mode, per-kit theme, Resize, Minimize, Close, and a labelled **Advanced** launcher in the page utilities bar.

Changes not recorded in the public history are not listed here.
