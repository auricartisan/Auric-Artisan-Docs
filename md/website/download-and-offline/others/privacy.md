# Download and offline privacy

## Stored on your device

- The offline copy of the site's files, in your browser's storage for auricartisan.com.
- Your profile choice, the low-power switch, and whether you have asked for an offline copy (so an interrupted or out-of-date copy can be completed later).
- Files you open with the installed app, or text and links you share to it, are held on your device for the Workspace to pick up.

## What the Download page reads

The page reads your device's processor thread count, approximate memory, graphics processor name, connection type and storage estimate, as your browser reports them. It uses them to show the metrics and to choose the Auto profile. It does not send them to Auric Artisan.

## What uses the network

- Downloading the offline copy and updates, from auricartisan.com.
- Checking for updates.

## Removing it

Select **Reset cache** on the Download page, uninstall the app, or clear the site's data in your browser (see [Uninstall and remove data](../docs/uninstall.md)).
