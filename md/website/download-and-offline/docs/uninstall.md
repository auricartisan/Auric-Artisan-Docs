# Uninstall and remove data

## Remove the installed app

Uninstalling is done by your browser or system. The usual ways:

- **Chrome or Edge (Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS):** open the app, open its menu (⋮ or …) in the title bar, and choose **Uninstall Auric Artisan**. On Windows you can also use **Settings** › **Apps**.
- **Safari on macOS:** remove the app from the Dock and delete it from your Applications folder.
- **Android:** long-press the icon and choose **Uninstall** (or **Remove**), or use **App info**.
- **iOS and iPadOS:** long-press the icon, choose **Remove App**, then **Delete from Home Screen** (wording varies by version).

The exact wording depends on your browser and system version.

## Remove the offline copy

Uninstalling the app may leave the offline copy and your other site data in the browser. To remove the offline copy:

1. Open https://auricartisan.com/download/.
2. Select **Reset cache**. The offline store is deleted and only the small app shell is rebuilt.

## Remove everything

To remove all data the site keeps in this browser (the offline copy, your settings, your library, recordings, and preferences), clear the site data for auricartisan.com in your browser's settings, usually under **Privacy** or **Site settings**.

> **Important:** Clearing site data deletes your local library items and recordings. Export or download anything you want to keep first.
