# Cookie Policy

Official page: https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/ — **Cookie Policy & Preferences**, version 1.2, effective 27 April 2026, last updated 23 May 2026. **The official policy is authoritative.**

## Key points

- Limited cookies and browser storage keep the site working and remember preferences.
- Account cookies (session and CSRF) are essential when you are signed in.
- No cookies for cross-site tracking, behavioural profiling or invasive advertising.
- Web analysis and theme generation use temporary processing data, not marketing cookies.
- Preferences such as theme and eye-rest are stored per browser and device.
- You can manage non-essential cookies on this page or in your browser.

## Section by section

1. **Overview** — "We use limited browser storage to keep the site working, remember your settings, support interactive tools, and understand basic platform performance. We do not use cookies to follow you across the web."
2. **Cookies and local storage** — What each is; some data disappears when the session ends, some remains until it expires or you clear it.
3. **Current status** — Sign-in uses essential first-party cookies. Payment providers may set their own cookies inside their checkout.
4. **How cookies are used** — Three practical groups: essential, functional and privacy-friendly analytics. Essential ones cannot be turned off in the preferences panel.
5. **No cross-site tracking or marketing pixels** — No Facebook Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag, Google Ads remarketing cookies or similar trackers "on this page".
6. **Third-party services** — Infrastructure, security, font and analytics services may receive limited technical information; providers' cookies follow their own terms.
7. **Your legal rights** — Under the DPDP Act, GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA and similar laws: know what is used, accept or reject non-essential cookies, withdraw consent, clear storage, and ask questions.
8. **Manage cookie preferences** — The on-page panel (below).
9. **Current cookie and storage list** — The table (below).
10. **Browser instructions** — Where to manage cookies in Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge. Disabling essential cookies or clearing storage may reset preferences or stop features working.
11. **Changes** — The policy may be updated.

Questions: privacy@auricartisan.com, as listed on the page.

## The preferences panel (section 8)

The panel lists four categories, each with a switch and a **Show cookies** button that lists the items in it:

| Category | Description on the panel | Can be turned off? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Essential Cookies** | Authentication, security, sessions and access control | No — marked **Always Active** |
| **Functional Cookies** | Theme, language, layout choices and form data | Yes |
| **Analytics Cookies** | Aggregate or pseudonymous usage and performance data, never used for advertising profiles | Yes |
| **Marketing Cookies** | "Reserved for campaign attribution if enabled later"; no marketing pixels are used | Yes |

Buttons:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept All** | Turns every switch on (select **Save Preferences** to keep it) |
| **Reject All** | Turns every optional switch off (select **Save Preferences** to keep it) |
| **Save Preferences** | Saves your choices: "Cookie preferences saved successfully." |
| **Clear All Cookies** | Clears non-essential cookies and your saved choice: "All non-essential cookies have been cleared." |
| **Withdraw Consent** | Shown once you have chosen; resets your choice: "Cookie consent withdrawn. Preferences reset." |

Under the buttons, **Consent recorded:** shows when you last chose. Your choice is saved in your browser and applies to that device only.

The first-visit bar (**We value your privacy**) offers **Accept All**, **Reject All** and **Customise**; **Customise** opens this panel. The bar is not shown over the sign-in and verification pages. Full walkthrough: [Cookie consent and privacy](../../site-features/cookie-consent-and-privacy/README.md).

> **Note:** The panel's own tables list a slightly different set of item names and durations from section 9 (for example "1 year" for functional items). The purposes are the same. If an exact name or duration matters to you, ask through the contact page.
