# Pricing page

Official page: https://auricartisan.com/company/pricing/. The pricing page and the checkout are authoritative for prices. The full, figure-by-figure documentation is [Plans and pricing](../../account-and-billing/docs/plans-and-pricing.md); this page explains how the pricing page is laid out.

## The opening

- Headline: **Pricing** — "Every tool is free, forever".
- Four facts: free tools, all of them; renewal: none — one payment; guarantee: 7-day money-back; term: 1 month or 12 months.
- **↓ Compare plans** and **Talk to us**.

## Key points (as the page lists them)

- Every tool is free on every tier — no tool is locked behind a paywall.
- Paid plans sell conveniences and scale: batch, API access, higher limits and a monthly token allowance.
- The REST API is metered in credits — 1,000,000 a month on Specialist, 10,000,000 on Industrial Pro; overage is opt-in, prepaid and never invoiced after the fact.
- Start free with no credit card.
- Whole-site accessibility crawls, up to 24 pages a run, unlock on Artisan; branded WCAG reports arrive with Specialist.
- A paid plan is a one-time pass for the term you choose; it does not auto-renew, and carries a 7-day money-back window.
- An account is required before any payment.

## The sections

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Choose your plan** | The **Monthly** / **Annual** switch (**Save 17%**), **USD $** / **INR ₹**, four figures ("40+ pro tools, all free", "$0 to start — no card", "No auto-renewal, ever", "7-day money-back window") and the four plan cards |
| **Free on every plan** | The tools that used to be locked, now free |
| **Tokens — what each plan includes** | Tokens compared with API credits, the allowances, and the token estimator |
| **API credits** | Credit costs by endpoint type, what is included, going past the allowance, safety nets, and the Industrial Pro note for purchases before 12 August 2026 |
| **Optional add-ons** | Add-ons free for everyone, included from Specialist, on request, or planned |
| **Compare all features** | The full comparison table |
| **The ones people actually ask** | Seven questions: are the tools free; what am I paying for; switching plans; free trial; payment methods; discounts; billing and refunds |
| **Ready when you are** | **Open the tools — free** and **Create an account** |
