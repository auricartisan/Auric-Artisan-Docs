# Privacy Policy

Official page: https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/ — version 2.3, effective 27 April 2026, last updated 27 July 2026. **The official policy is authoritative.** This summary is for understanding only.

## Key points

- Only the minimum data needed to operate and improve the platform is collected.
- Sign-in, sessions and security features process authentication data where enabled.
- Payments go through Razorpay; raw card, UPI or bank details are not stored.
- Web analysis inputs can include URLs, page metadata, screenshots, extracted colours and scan results.
- Theme, eye-rest and interface preferences may be stored locally in your browser.
- Personal data is not sold or used for behavioural advertising.

## Section by section

1. **Scope** — Covers all Auric Artisan tools, pages and features. Using the platform means agreeing to these practices.
2. **Current platform status** — Accounts, the dashboard, API keys and one-time purchases are live; recurring subscriptions are not offered. Payment credentials go to Razorpay and never touch Auric Artisan's servers.
3. **Data collected**
   - *Usage data* — first-party analytics, only with consent: the event name, the page path (never query strings), the referrer's origin, and a few coarse properties such as device class. No cookies, no cross-site tracking; Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured. No consent means no events.
   - *Device data* — browser, operating system, screen size, language and technical environment.
   - *Tool input data* — what you enter into tools.
   - *Communication data* — what you send through contact forms, email or feedback.
   - *Account data* — email, display name, authentication details, sessions, security events and saved preferences.
   - Not intentionally collected: raw card details, government IDs, precise location, biometric identifiers or medical diagnostic data.
4. **Web analysis data** — URLs, titles, metadata, screenshots, page structure, text snippets, palettes, performance metrics, layout information and security headers. Submit only sites you own or are authorised to test; do not submit private dashboards, credentials or restricted systems.
5. **Local storage and preferences** — Browser storage and cookies keep the site usable and remember preferences (theme, language, layout, eye rest, recent tool state, consent). Many stay on your device.
6. **How data is used** — Running the tools; understanding anonymous usage and fixing problems; security; answering messages; supporting account features. No behavioural profiling or automated decisions with legal or similar effects.
7. **No sale of personal data** — Not sold, rented or traded; no data brokers or cross-site behavioural tracking.
8. **Advertising and payments** — Only order records are kept (item, amount, payment reference). Personal data is not used for targeted advertising; the policy says it will be updated before or alongside any new advertising features.
9. **Security and protection** — HTTPS/TLS in transit and safeguards appropriate to the platform's stage. Vision simulations and the Ishihara-style generator are not medical tools.
10. **Third-party services** — Cloudflare (hosting, DNS, CDN, compute and storage for account, preference and order data), Razorpay (payments) and Google Fonts (font files on some pages; Google receives the technical request, such as your IP address). Analytics is first-party.
11. **Data retention** — Kept only as long as needed. Temporary tool data briefly; web analysis data cached temporarily; local preferences until you clear them; contact messages as needed for support; anonymous analytics longer in aggregate. Details: [Data Retention and Deletion Policy](data-retention-and-deletion.md).
12. **Your rights** — Depending on where you live: access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection, withdrawing consent, grievance and appeal, and opting out of sale or targeted advertising (Auric Artisan does not sell data). Use the platform's controls, clear browser storage, adjust cookie settings, or contact Auric Artisan.
13. **Legal compliance** — Aims to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws where they apply.
14. **Changes** — May be updated; material changes communicated through the platform or other reasonable channels; continued use means acceptance.

## How to use your rights in practice

| Right | How |
| --- | --- |
| See or take a copy of your data | Dashboard **Security & Privacy** › **Export**; for more, contact page topic **Privacy or data** |
| Correct your data | Dashboard **Profile**; for the email address, contact support |
| Delete your data | Dashboard danger zone, or contact page topic **Privacy or data** |
| Withdraw consent for analytics | Cookie Policy page or **Settings**; or turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser |
| Clear local data | Your browser's site-data settings |

See [Export your data](../../account-and-billing/docs/export-your-data.md) and [Delete your account](../../account-and-billing/docs/delete-your-account.md).
