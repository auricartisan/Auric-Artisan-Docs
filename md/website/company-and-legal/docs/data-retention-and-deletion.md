# Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Official page: https://auricartisan.com/legal/data-retention-and-deletion-policy/ — version 2.2, effective 27 April 2026, last updated 23 May 2026. **The official policy is authoritative.**

## Key points

- Only data needed to operate, secure and improve the platform is kept.
- Account, session, billing and API data are kept only as needed.
- Tool inputs are processed only as needed; temporary processing data is deleted or anonymised when no longer required.
- Security logs and abuse-prevention records may be kept for a limited period.
- You can request deletion of applicable personal data through the contact channels.

## Section by section

1. **Retention principles** — Minimal retention: purpose limitation, storage limitation, accountability, and practices that follow what is actually deployed.
2. **Types of data kept**

   | Data | How long |
   | --- | --- |
   | Contact and support messages | As needed to respond and keep records |
   | Tool inputs (URLs, colours, images, palettes, settings) | Only where necessary |
   | Generated outputs (reports, palettes, themes, exports, previews) | May be temporarily stored, cached or delivered |
   | Usage analytics (aggregated or anonymised) | May be kept to improve the platform |
   | Security logs (IP addresses, request metadata, abuse signals, errors) | For security, debugging and abuse prevention |
   | Billing records | As required by tax, accounting, payment-provider and financial rules |

3. **Tool processing data** — What processing can involve. Avoid entering sensitive personal data, credentials, medical records, payment information or private tokens into exploratory tools unless a feature asks for it and explains why.
4. **Deletion requests** — Request account deletion through the account controls or support. A valid request is reviewed; eligible data in active systems is removed, minimised or anonymised within a reasonable period; backups and security logs may persist until their normal rotation. Save anything you need first — deleted data may not be recoverable.
5. **Automatic deletion** — Temporary processing data, cached reports and extracted assets may be removed after use; session tokens, cookies and local preferences expire according to browser settings or platform configuration; security logs are kept for a limited period.
6. **Legal and compliance retention** — Data may be kept longer when law, valid legal requests, disputes, investigations, abuse prevention, security or financial record-keeping require it, and then deleted, minimised or anonymised.
7. **Your rights** — Access, correction, export, restriction, objection, withdrawing consent, appeal and deletion, depending on applicable law (GDPR, India's DPDP Act, CCPA/CPRA and others). Use the account controls, the contact page or official support channels. Requests are answered within the time the law requires.
8. **Data security** — Access controls, secure transport, logging, monitoring and encryption where appropriate. See [Security Policy](security-policy.md).
9. **Changes** — May be updated; continued use means acceptance.

## How this applies to your account

- **Delete your account** from the dashboard's danger zone. The Security page explains that erasure anonymises personal data while keeping financial records, because tax law requires invoices to be kept. See [Delete your account](../../account-and-billing/docs/delete-your-account.md).
- The FAQ page gives approximate timings: access disabled at once, personal data scheduled for deletion within about 90 days unless the law requires it to be kept.
- **Data in your browser** is under your control: clear it in your browser's settings, or with **Erase this browser's workspace** in the dashboard.
- **Privacy or data** requests through the contact form have a stated reply time of within 30 days.
