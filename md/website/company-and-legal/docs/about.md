# About Auric Artisan

Official page: https://auricartisan.com/company/about/ (last updated 27 July 2026). The official page is authoritative; this is a summary to help you find your way.

## At a glance

- A platform for colour tools, colour-science resources, articles and structured learning material.
- Web analysis across security, performance, responsive design, palette extraction and related checks.
- Theme generation for colour systems, interfaces and web design.
- CIE colour science: XYZ, Lab, LCH, Oklab, sRGB, Display P3, colour-difference formulas, chromatic adaptation, gamut mapping and metamerism.
- More than 70 vision simulation models, based on Viénot 1999, Brettel 1997 and Machado 2009.
- An Ishihara-style plate generator for learning and demonstration only — not a medical device.
- Every tool free on every tier; optional paid plan passes are one-time purchases through Razorpay.
- Independent, and not affiliated with any government or institution.

## What the page covers, section by section

1. **About Auric Artisan** — The platform is built and written by Chirag Bansal, who also writes every Learn article and the documentation. The name joins "Auric" (gold, warmth, luminance) and "Artisan" (careful craft). The page calls the current release "a working foundation — functional, researched, and expanding."
2. **What Auric Artisan provides** — Four capability areas: colour science and measurement, vision simulation, web analysis, and theme and design-system generation.
3. **Colour science and spectral tools** — The colour spaces covered (CIE 1931 XYZ, CIELAB, CIE LCH, CIELUV, Oklab and Oklch, sRGB, Display P3, Adobe RGB 1998, HSL/HSV/HSB, HWB and CMYK approximation); colour-difference metrics (CIE76, CIE94 and its graphic-arts variant, CIEDE2000, CMC l:c); chromatic adaptation (Von Kries, Bradford, CIECAT02, CAT16) and illuminants (D50, D55, D65, A, F2, F11 and LED series); gamut mapping, metamerism, contrast ratio, colour harmonies and Munsell reference.
4. **Vision simulation models** — Congenital deficiencies (the three dichromacies, three anomalous trichromacies, complete and incomplete achromatopsia), acquired conditions (cataracts, macular degeneration, glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, optic neuritis) and low-vision models (blur, contrast loss, scotopic, mesopic and photophobia simulations). All are software models, not certified for clinical or safety-critical use.
5. **Web analysis and accessibility checking** — WCAG 2.1 and 2.2 contrast (AA 4.5:1 and 3:1; AAA 7:1 and 4.5:1), colour-blind-safe palette analysis, focus indicators, text alternatives, touch-target size; security headers; Core Web Vitals estimates and resource checks; palette extraction, dark-mode compatibility and responsive review.
6. **Theme generation and design systems** — Tonal scales in Oklab or Lab, semantic roles, automatic contrast pairing, dark-mode variants; exports to CSS custom properties, design-token JSON, Tailwind, SCSS, Figma-compatible styles, Swift/SwiftUI and Android/Jetpack Compose; palette import, comparison and a built-in accessibility audit.
7. **Articles and learning resources** — Topic areas from colour-space primers to design-token architecture, plus reference material.
8. **Ishihara-style generator** — For exploration and demonstration. The page's medical disclaimer: it is not a medical device, not a diagnostic test, and must not be used for clinical decisions, employment screening, certification or any official vision assessment.
9. **Who uses Auric Artisan** — Designers, developers, accessibility specialists, colour scientists, art directors, students and educators, print professionals, and game and film colourists.
10. **Data and privacy principles** — Collects only what is needed; does not sell personal information or build advertising profiles; preferences stay in the browser; colour inputs are processed locally where possible.
11. **Pricing, sign-ups and availability** — Accounts are live; paid access is sold as one-time passes; nothing auto-renews; features may change over time.
12. **Independence and neutrality** — Not a government entity or standards body; implementations follow published CIE standards, peer-reviewed research and W3C specifications.
13. **Our philosophy** — "Tools That Respect Intelligence": explain rather than obscure, and design with care for people with colour-vision deficiency, low vision or sensitivity to visual stimulation.
