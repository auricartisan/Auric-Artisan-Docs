# Company and legal

Auric Artisan publishes a set of company pages — About, Contact, FAQ, Timeline, Accessibility, the author page, Pricing and a Security page — and twelve legal documents, from the Terms of Service to the Transparency and Ethics Policy. This folder summarises each of them in plain language so you can find what matters to you quickly.

**The official pages are authoritative.** These summaries are written to help you understand the documents, not to replace them. They add no obligations and remove none. Where a summary and the official page differ, the official page wins; each summary links to its source, and names the version and date it was written from.

Some pages on the site describe the same thing slightly differently, because they were updated at different times. Where that matters to you — for example, which address to send a security report to — the summary points it out and names the more specific or more recent page.

## Who it is for

- Anyone deciding whether to use Auric Artisan, create an account or buy a plan.
- People who want to know what happens to their data.
- Researchers reporting a security issue.
- Anyone who needs to cite or check a policy.

## Quick start

1. Open https://auricartisan.com/legal/ to see every policy, with its effective date and version.
2. Select a policy. Each opens with **Key Points** and an **On this page** list.
3. Select a numbered section heading to expand or collapse it.
4. For a plain-language version, open the matching summary in this folder.
5. For anything the policies do not answer, use https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/.

## What you can do with this folder

- Find out, in a few minutes, what each policy says and who it applies to.
- See the refund, billing, privacy and deletion rules that affect your account.
- Learn how to contact the right inbox, report a vulnerability or request your data.
- Check what Auric Artisan says it does not claim — certifications, audits, medical use.
