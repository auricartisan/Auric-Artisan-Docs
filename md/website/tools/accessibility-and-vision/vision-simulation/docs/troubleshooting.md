# Troubleshooting the Vision Lab

### The Catalogue shows only 17 of 78 conditions

- **Why:** the **Level** filter is set to **Basic**, which shows only the per-colour conditions. It follows the site-wide Simple/Advanced preference.
- **What to do:** select **Filter**, then **Level** › **All**.

### "Nothing matches that."

- **Why:** the search and filters together exclude every condition.
- **What to do:** select **Clear the filters**, or clear the search.

### Opening a card shows an upgrade prompt

- **Why:** on the free plan you can open 25 simulations a day from the Catalogue, and today's are used.
- **What to do:** use the **Workbench**, which is not counted, or come back tomorrow. Paid plans have no daily cap.

### A panel I had open disappeared when I opened another

- **Why:** up to five panels can be open; a sixth replaces the oldest panel that is not pinned.
- **What to do:** select **Pin panel** on panels you want to keep, or close panels you no longer need.

### Nothing opens when I select a card

- **Why:** five panels are open and all five are pinned, or today's free simulations are used.
- **What to do:** unpin or close a panel, or check the daily tally.

### My image does not appear in the panels

- **Why:** **Auto-apply to new panels** may be off or paused, or the image was loaded before the panels opened.
- **What to do:** open **Source image** and select **Apply to all panels**.

### My image is processed slowly

- **Why:** **Quality** is set to Balanced or Full, which processes more pixels.
- **What to do:** choose **Fast · 1024px** while exploring.

### The model buttons change nothing

- **Why:** the model applies to the colour-vision conditions. Field conditions, such as glaucoma, are not colour transforms.
- **What to do:** choose a per-colour condition to compare models.

### The simulation does not match what someone with the condition sees

- **Why:** simulations model a typical case on an uncalibrated screen. Real vision varies from person to person, and your display, its settings and the room light all change how colours appear.
- **What to do:** treat the result as a guide, not a measurement. See [Limits and accuracy](../others/limits-and-accuracy.md).

### A shared link opened a different view

- **Why:** a `?vw=` link always opens the Workbench; a `#v=` link restores the Catalogue workspace.
- **What to do:** use **Copy link to this view** in the Workbench, **Copy link** in the Catalogue, or **Copy link to this sheet** in Compare, depending on what you want to share.

### My own image is missing from a shared link

- **Why:** your images never leave your device, so they cannot travel in a link. Built-in samples and test charts do.
- **What to do:** send the image separately, or share a built-in sample.

### My settings are gone

- **Why:** the lab remembers your work in this browser only. A different browser, a private window, or clearing site data starts afresh.
- **What to do:** share important views as links, and export what you need.
