# The 78 conditions

The Vision Lab simulates 78 conditions in 11 categories. This page lists them as the cards show them.

## How to read the tables

- **Severity** is an estimated visual-impact score from 1 to 10. It orders the collection; it is not a clinical grade and does not rank anyone's sight.
- **Kind** is **Per-colour** (a colour-in, colour-out transform: every colour has an exact answer) or **Field** (an effect on the visual field, such as blur, lost regions or glare). The 17 per-colour conditions are the Catalogue's **Basic** level; the 61 field conditions are **Advanced**.
- **Summary** and **Prevalence** are the short texts on each card, with abbreviations such as "/yr" and "HTN" spelled out here. Prevalence figures are approximate, and some cards say "Educational" or "N/A" for conditions that are teaching aids rather than diagnoses.

Palette audit checks every per-colour condition: Protanopia, Protanomaly, Deuteranopia, Deuteranomaly, Tritanopia, Tritanomaly, Achromatopsia, Color Inversion, Grayscale, the four cone-isolation conditions, Age-Related Shift, Chloropsia, Cyanopsia and Erythropsia.

> **Important:** These simulations are educational visualisations, not medical simulations or diagnostic tools. Real experience of any condition varies from person to person.
