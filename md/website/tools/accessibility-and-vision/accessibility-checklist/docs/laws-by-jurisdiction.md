# Laws by jurisdiction

The **Reference** tab is headed "What the levels mean, and what the law asks for". It explains the four levels used on the page and summarises the accessibility law in 15 jurisdictions, with links to the primary sources.

> **Important:** The page states this is "Orientation, not legal advice." Dates move: the page notes that two of its dates moved in the eighteen months before its last review. It was last reviewed on **11 September 2026**, covering 29 instruments in 15 jurisdictions. Check the primary source before you rely on any date. The summaries below repeat the page's content as of that review.

## The four levels on this page

| Level | Checks | What the page says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 22 | The floor. Nearly every instrument below names it, or something built on it. |
| AA | 13 | What the statutes and procurement documents actually cite. A + AA is the legal line. |
| AAA | 2 | Stricter than any law here requires. Worth doing; never demanded wholesale. |
| Practice | 13 | No criterion covers it, and every audit finds it anyway. |

The page's summary: WCAG 2.1 Level AA is the common denominator almost everywhere. The UK and Australia have already moved to WCAG 2.2 AA, and EN 301 549 V4 takes the European standard there too. Build to WCAG 2.2 AA and you are above every line on the page.

## Finding your way

A side rail headed **Jurisdictions** jumps to each card: India (5 instruments), United States (4), European Union (3), United Kingdom (2), Canada (3), Australia (2), Japan (2) and **Elsewhere** (8). On narrow screens it becomes a row of buttons you scroll sideways.

Each card shows the jurisdiction, its standard, a table of instrument, what it does and when, and who it binds. Instrument names link to their primary sources. Dates that are current are highlighted.

A second rail, **Also on this site**, links to the Learn library ("The science behind each rule"), the Analyzer ("What the analyzer checks") and WCAG 2.2 at the W3C.

## India

Standard: WCAG 2.1 AA via GIGW 3.0. Binds government, establishments and service providers, public and private.

| Instrument | When | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act (Act 49 of 2016, ss. 40, 42, 46) | 2016 | Empowers accessibility standards, requires accessible ICT, and places the duty on service providers |
| Rajive Raturi v. Union of India, Supreme Court of India | 8 Nov 2024 | Held merely recommendatory guidelines ultra vires; gave the Union three months to set a mandatory floor |
| GIGW 3.0, Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (MeitY / NIC) | 2023, revised 2025 | 88 mandatory checkpoints; names WCAG 2.1 Level AA; certified by STQC |
| IS 17802 (Parts 1 and 2), Bureau of Indian Standards | 2021 | India's own ICT accessibility standard, written to be citable in procurement |
| Draft ICT Accessibility Rules (consultation, after Raturi) | In progress | The mandatory floor the Court ordered; check its current status |

Non-compliance with the Act is an offence under section 89, according to the page.

## United States

Standard: WCAG 2.1 AA, with Section 508 still at WCAG 2.0 AA. Binds federal, state and local government, and private business through Title III litigation.

| Instrument | When | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ADA Title II, DOJ final rule (28 CFR Part 35, April 2024) | 2027 / 2028 | State and local government web content and apps to WCAG 2.1 AA |
| Section 508, Rehabilitation Act (the 2017 Refresh) | 2018 | Federal agencies and what they buy, to WCAG 2.0 AA by reference |
| Section 504 (HHS and Department of Education rules) | 2024 onwards | Recipients of federal funding, such as hospitals and universities, to WCAG 2.1 AA |
| ADA Title III (public accommodations) | 1990 | No technical standard; where most private litigation happens, with WCAG as the practical measure |

The page notes that in April 2026 the DOJ pushed both Title II deadlines back a year: large entities to April 2027, and smaller entities and special districts to April 2028. The underlying duty not to discriminate never paused.

## European Union

Standard: EN 301 549 V3.2.1, which points at WCAG 2.1 AA. Binds the public sector and, since 2025, much of the private sector across 27 member states.

| Instrument | When | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882) | 28 Jun 2025 | Reaches the private sector: e-commerce, banking, e-books, communications, ticketing, ATMs |
| Web Accessibility Directive (Directive (EU) 2016/2102) | 2019 / 2021 | Public sector sites and apps, with an accessibility statement and a feedback route |
| EN 301 549 (V3.2.1, March 2021) | V3.2.1 | The harmonised standard; clause 9 is WCAG 2.1 AA, and V4.1.1 moves it to WCAG 2.2 |

Service microenterprises are exempt from the European Accessibility Act.

## United Kingdom

Standard: WCAG 2.2 AA. Binds the public sector by regulation and everyone else by duty.

| Instrument | When | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public Sector Bodies Accessibility Regulations (SI 2018/952) | 2.2 since 2024 | Public sector to WCAG 2.2 AA, monitored against 2.2 since October 2024, plus a statement |
| Equality Act 2010 (s. 20, reasonable adjustments) | 2010 | Outcome-based duty with no named standard; applies to private service providers too |

## Canada

Standard: CAN/ASC-EN 301 549:2024. Binds federally regulated bodies; provinces add their own rules.

| Instrument | When | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accessible Canada Act (S.C. 2019, c. 10) and Regulations | 2019 | Federally regulated organisations publish accessibility plans, take feedback and report |
| CAN/ASC-EN 301 549:2024, Accessibility Standards Canada | 31 May 2024 | Adopts the European standard: WCAG 2.1 AA plus extra ICT requirements |
| AODA, Ontario Integrated Accessibility Standards (s. 14) | 2021 | WCAG 2.0 AA, without live captions and audio description, for large organisations and the public sector |

## Australia

Standard: WCAG 2.2 AA. Binds government directly and business through the Disability Discrimination Act.

| Instrument | When | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (and Maguire v SOCOG, 2000) | 1992 | Names no standard; the Sydney Olympics case is why WCAG is the measure |
| Government digital standards (with AS EN 301 549:2020 for procurement) | 2.2 AA | WCAG 2.2 AA formally adopted as the minimum, up from 2.0 |

## Japan

Standard: JIS X 8341-3:2016, which carries WCAG 2.0. Binds public bodies by standard and private business by duty since 2024.

| Instrument | When | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| JIS X 8341-3:2016, Japanese Industrial Standard | 2016 | The success criteria of WCAG 2.0, with its own conformance and testing procedure |
| Act on Eliminating Discrimination against Persons with Disabilities (2021 amendment) | Apr 2024 | Reasonable accommodation required of private business, not merely encouraged |
