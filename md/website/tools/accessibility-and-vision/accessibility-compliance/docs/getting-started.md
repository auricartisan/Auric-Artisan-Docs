# Getting started

## What happens when you open the address

1. You open https://auricartisan.com/accessibility-compliance/ (or `/compliance/`).
2. A very short page titled "Redirecting to Accessibility Checklist" loads, with the text "Redirecting to Accessibility Checklist..." and a link.
3. Your browser moves on at once to https://auricartisan.com/resources/accessibility-checklist/. The forward replaces the old address in your history, so your browser's Back button skips it.

If your browser blocks the automatic forward, select the **Accessibility Checklist** link on that page.

The result: you are on the Accessibility Checklist, with its **Checklist**, **Export** and **Reference** tabs.

## What to do next

| If you want to | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Know which law applies to you, and which WCAG level it cites | The **Reference** tab. See [Laws by jurisdiction](../../accessibility-checklist/docs/laws-by-jurisdiction.md). |
| Check your site against WCAG 2.2 by hand | The **Checklist** tab. See [Work through the checklist](../../accessibility-checklist/docs/work-through-the-checklist.md). |
| Find the problems a tool can find automatically | **Run the automated pass first →**, which opens the [Analyzer](../../analyzer/README.md) |
| Hand findings to your team | The **Export** tab. See [Export your results](../../accessibility-checklist/docs/export-your-results.md). |

## Update your links

If you link to the compliance page from your own site or documents, link to https://auricartisan.com/resources/accessibility-checklist/ directly. The old address still works, but the direct address saves a step.
