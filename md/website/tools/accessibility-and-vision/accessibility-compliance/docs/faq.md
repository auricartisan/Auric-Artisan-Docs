# Accessibility Compliance FAQ

## Why does /accessibility-compliance/ open the checklist?

The compliance resource was folded into the Accessibility Checklist, which now carries both the checks and the legal reference. The old address forwards there so existing links keep working.

## Is there still a separate compliance page?

No. Everything a visitor needs is on the checklist page: the 50 checks, evidence and export, and the **Reference** tab on the law in 15 jurisdictions.

## Does the forward keep anything I had on the old page?

The address has no content of its own to keep. Work you do on the checklist is saved in your browser, as described in the checklist's [Privacy](../../accessibility-checklist/others/privacy.md) page.

## Does the checklist make my site compliant?

No. It is a structured way to check and record your work. The page says a worked checklist is evidence of a process, not a conformance claim, and its legal summaries are orientation, not legal advice.

## Where is the menu entry?

In the site header, **Learn** › **Accessibility Compliance** ("WCAG checks & guidance"). It opens the checklist.
