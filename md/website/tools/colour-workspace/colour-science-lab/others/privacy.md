# Privacy

## Stays in your browser

All calculations run in the page. Colours you enter are not sent anywhere to be computed. Exports (PNG, JSON, CSV) are created in your browser.

## Remembered in your browser

In your browser's local storage on this device: the last view, the Workbench specimen colour, the Catalogue domain, favourites, the last ten tools opened, whether you dismissed the quick start note, and the level, which is shared with the site-wide complexity setting. Open panels are not stored.

## Uses the network

- Loading the page and its scripts.
- **Save to Library** stores presets in your Auric Artisan Library; see the [Library Kit](../../../../kits/library-kit/README.md).
- Site-wide analytics and advertising follow your cookie choices; see the [Help centre](../../../../../help/README.md).
