# Limits and accuracy

## Categories and keywords cover two sets

Only Tabler and Remix icons (6,783 of 12,896) have categories and keywords. For Phosphor, Lucide, Heroicons, Feather and Material, the category filter, the popular categories and keyword search find nothing, and you search by the words in the icon's name.

## Find your icon filters, it does not rank

**Find your icon** lists the first twelve icons that match your set, category and brief, in catalogue order. Its match figures follow the list order and do not measure fit.

## Names are the sets' own

Icon names come from each set, so the same idea can have different names in different sets (for example *trash*, *delete* or *bin*). Try more than one word.

## Previews and exports

- The grid and panel controls change the preview. Only the panel's colour and stroke carry into the copied code and the restyled **Download .svg**.
- The **Download .svg** button in the panel header, the ZIP and the sprite hold icons as they come from their sets, in the style the grid showed when you selected them.
- The data URI is sized to the grid's **Size**, not the panel's preview size.
- Stroke only affects outline icons.
- Material icons are a webfont: they cannot be downloaded as SVG, are left out of the sprite, and appear in the ZIP as text notes.

## Page limits

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Icons shown before scrolling loads more | 120 |
| Category chips per set | 40 |
| Find your icon results | 12 |
| Recently viewed | The last 12 icons |
| Panel preview size | 16 to 160 pixels |

## Known issues

At the time of writing, the **×** on a licence chip under **Applied** can switch the wrong licence when more than one is listed. Use the chip itself in the **License** group.
