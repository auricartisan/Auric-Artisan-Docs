# Font Library

The Font Library is the browsing and testing page for the Auric Artisan font catalogue: 2,213 families and 4,883 font files, 550 of them variable fonts, covering 60 scripts and Unicode blocks. Every card is set in its own typeface, so you judge fonts by looking at them. Type your own words and every card shows them.

Open a family and a panel docks beside the grid with eleven tabs: a specimen you can type into and style, variable-axis sliders and OpenType switches, computed suitability scores, suggested pairings, the full glyph set, every style and file, the licence and its permissions, the character coverage, developer exports (font stacks, Tailwind, SCSS, tokens and a modular type scale) and ready-to-paste embed code.

Star the families you like and they gather in **Your kit**, kept in your browser. From there you can compare up to four side by side, copy one combined embed snippet, or download a self-hosting kit: a ZIP with the font files, a stylesheet, a demo page and the licence. A **Recommend** panel suggests families for an industry, a brand style or even a colour palette.

The fonts are served by the Auric Artisan Font CDN, so previews and embeds make no request to any third party. The CDN itself (hosts, loader script, self-hosting and licences) is documented under [Font CDN](../../../../services/font-cdn/README.md).

## Who it is for

- Web designers and developers choosing type for a site or product.
- Brand designers looking for a typeface and a partner for it.
- Anyone who needs fonts with a clear open licence to self-host.
- People checking whether a font covers a language or has a feature such as tabular figures.

## Where to find it

- Page: https://auricartisan.com/tool/font/
- No sign-in is needed. Your selection is kept in your browser.

## Quick start

1. Open https://auricartisan.com/tool/font/.
2. Type a sentence in *Type to preview every family…* above the grid.
3. On the left, pick a category such as **Serif**, or type a name in *Family, designer or foundry*.
4. Select a card. The family opens in the panel on the right, on its **Specimen** tab.
5. Try the **Suitability** and **Pairings** tabs.
6. Select **＋ Select** to add the family to **Your kit**.
7. Open **Use & Embed** and select **Copy**, or select **Download kit** for a self-hosting ZIP.

## What you can do

- Search, sort and filter 2,213 families by category, variable or italic, weights, OpenType features, scripts, licence and source.
- Browse six curated collections, or ask the recommender for a shortlist.
- Test a family with your own text, sizes, weights, colours, variable axes and OpenType features.
- Read computed scores for accessibility, readability, UI, code and editorial use, and see suggested pairings.
- Copy any glyph, check character coverage, and read the licence and its permissions.
- Copy a font stack, Tailwind, SCSS, design-token or type-scale code, and embed code for one family or a whole selection.
- Compare two to four families side by side, and download self-hosting kits.
