# Privacy

## Stays in your browser

Your preview text, specimen edits, recommender choices and palettes are used only in the page. They are not sent anywhere. Download kits are assembled in your browser.

## Remembered in your browser

In your browser's local storage on this device:

- **Your kit**: the families you selected;
- which filter groups you folded;
- the width of the family panel;
- the last twelve families you opened (**Recently viewed**).

Like other pages on the site, the page also keeps the values of some fields, such as your preview text, in the page address after `#p=`, so a reload restores them. If you share the address, the other person sees those values. See [Shareable links](../../../../site-features/shareable-links/README.md).

## Uses the network

- Loading the page and its scripts.
- The catalogue, the specimens and every font file load from the Auric Artisan Font CDN at fonts.auricartisan.com. No third-party font service is involved.
- Downloads fetch the font files from the same CDN.
- Site-wide analytics and advertising follow your cookie choices. See the [Help centre](../../../../../help/README.md).

For what the CDN itself records, see [Font CDN privacy](../../../../../services/font-cdn/others/privacy.md).
