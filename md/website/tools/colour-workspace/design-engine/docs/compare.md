# Compare styles and industries

Because a system is generated almost instantly, the tool can build many at once. The **Compare** view keeps your brief and varies one thing, the style or the industry, so you can see every option side by side.

## Open Compare

Select **Compare every style →** at the bottom of **The brief**. **← Back to the system** returns to the tab you were on.

The heading says what is being compared, for example *Healthcare at AAA, through all ten styles.*, and the range of scores.

## The controls

| Control | Options | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Vary** | **Style**, **Industry** | Varies the style (ten cards) or the industry (one card per industry setting, including Auto). |
| **Order by** | **Score**, **Chroma**, **Listed** | Sorts by score, by how colourful the primary is, or in the list order. |

Compare uses your contrast target, density and other overrides, but ignores your seed colour so that each style or industry shows its own hue.

## Each card

- The name, **on screen** if it matches your current system, and the score (colour-coded).
- The primary ramp, and chips for primary, accent, success, warning and danger.
- The heading font drawn in its weight, with the body font, type ratio and icon family.
- A sample **Continue** button and a tile showing the corner and shadow.
- A footer with the corner style and harmony, **audit clean** or *N failing* (light and dark together), and **Use**.

When sorted by score, the highest-scoring card is highlighted.

## Adopt a result

Select **Use** on a card. Your brief switches to that style or industry, the system regenerates, and you return to your tab with a message such as **Using …**.

## Things to know

- The score measures contrast headroom, surviving chroma and text hierarchy, not taste; the page says as much under the cards.
- Compare is built only while the view is open, so it does not slow the rest of the tool.
