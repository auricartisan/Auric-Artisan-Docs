# Privacy

## Stays in your browser

Every sweep, score, token system and mockup is computed in the page. Your colours and your brief are not sent anywhere to generate results. The JSON download is created in your browser.

## Remembered in your browser

In your browser's local storage on this device: your pool, style chips, use case, brief, engine settings, weights and filters, the open tab, Simple or Advanced mode, and the Studio's palette choice, theme, density, **Test** and **Inspect** settings and overrides.

Results are not stored. Clearing your browser's site data resets the generator to its starting pool and defaults.

Like other pages on the site, the page may also keep the values of some on-page controls in the page address after `#p=`, so a reload or a shared link restores them. See [Shareable links](../../../../site-features/shareable-links/README.md).

## Uses the network

- Loading the page and its scripts.
- **Save** and **Save brand system** store items in your Auric Artisan Library. See the [Library Kit](../../../../kits/library-kit/README.md).
- The Public API, if you choose to use it, runs on Auric Artisan's servers. See the [Public API](../../../../../services/public-api/README.md).
- Site-wide analytics and advertising follow your cookie choices. See the [Help centre](../../../../../help/README.md).
