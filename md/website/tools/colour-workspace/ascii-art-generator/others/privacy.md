# Privacy

## Stays in your browser

Pictures you load, paste or drop are read and converted on your device. They are not uploaded. Text you type is drawn and converted the same way. Every download is built in your browser.

## Remembered

- In your browser's local storage: whether you dismissed the **Drop a picture · Pick a look · Take it away** strip.
- In the page address: like other pages on the site, the generator keeps the values of its controls in the address after `#p=`, so a reload restores them. Your picture is not part of the address. If you share the address, the other person gets your settings, not your picture. See [Shareable links](../../../../site-features/shareable-links/README.md).

## Uses the network

- Loading the page and its scripts.
- The Library's **Save to Library**, if you use it, stores the art and settings in your Auric Artisan Library. See the [Library Kit](../../../../kits/library-kit/README.md).
- Site-wide analytics and advertising follow your cookie choices. See the [Help centre](../../../../../help/README.md).
