# Frequently asked questions

## Are my pictures uploaded?

No. Pictures are read and converted in your browser and never leave your device. Downloads are built on your device too. See [Privacy](../others/privacy.md).

## Do I need an account?

No. The generator works without signing in.

## Is it really ASCII?

Only the **Standard**, **Detailed** and **Minimal** sets use plain ASCII characters. **Blocks**, **Symbols**, **Lines** and **Braille** use Unicode characters, which need a font that has them and may not display in every terminal or app.

## Which settings give the best result?

Start with a look: **Classic** for photos, **Line art** for logos and drawings, **Blocks** for small, bold art, **Fine** for large, detailed art. Then adjust **Detail** for the space you have. Pictures with a clear subject and strong contrast convert best.

## How wide should the art be?

It depends on where it goes. A README or chat message fits 60 to 100 columns; a terminal is often 80 to 120 columns wide; a poster or a PNG can go up to 300.

## Can I keep the colours?

Yes, with **From image**. Colour survives in the HTML, PNG (from the Canvas render), SVG and ANSI downloads. Plain text and **Copy** carry no colour; **Copy for terminal** does.

## Can I make animated ASCII art?

Not on this page. An animated GIF is converted as one still frame, and the webcam source cannot currently be used on the site.

## Can I convert text into big letters?

Yes. Choose **Text** as the source, type a word, and pick a font, size and weight. The word is drawn and then converted. **Batch** on the **Export** tab measures a whole list of words.

## Why does my text look wrong when I paste it?

The place you pasted into is probably using a proportional font. Put the art in a code block or set a monospaced font there.

## Can I load a JSON file back in?

No. The JSON records the settings and the art, but the page has no import. You can save the art and settings to your Library instead; see [Copy, export and batch](export.md).

## Does it work on a phone?

Yes. On narrow screens the art comes first and the controls follow. Pasting and dragging depend on the phone's browser; **Choose a file** always works.
