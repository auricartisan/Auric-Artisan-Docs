# Privacy

## Pictures from your device stay in your browser

- A picture you drop or choose is read by the page itself and drawn to a canvas in your browser. The tool does not upload it or send it anywhere.
- The extraction, the readouts, the charts and the batch analysis are all worked out on your device.
- Downloads are created in the browser and saved by your browser's normal download.
- The clipboard is written only when you select a copy button or press `H` or `J`, or when you click a swatch or pin.

## Pictures from a web address

When you use **Load URL**, your browser requests the image from the address you typed, exactly as it would when you open that address. The site that hosts the image sees that request. The Image Picker does not send the image anywhere afterwards.

## Nothing is kept after you leave

The picture, the palette and the pins are not stored. **Reset**, reloading or leaving the page clears them. The only thing remembered is whether you closed the **Automate this** line, which is kept in your browser.

## What an export contains

The JSON export contains the picture's file name (or the last part of its address), its sizes, your settings, the palette and your pins. It does not contain the picture itself.

## The page itself

The Image Picker is a normal page on auricartisan.com. Site-wide features such as sign-in, preferences and any consent-based services follow the site's own privacy policy and your cookie choices; see [Help](../../../../../help/README.md).
