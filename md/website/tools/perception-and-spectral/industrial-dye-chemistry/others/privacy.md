# Privacy

## What stays in your browser

- Every calculation: spectra, colours, the lights row and the tolerance sweep are computed in the page.
- Spectrum files you load are read from your disk by the page. They are not uploaded and are forgotten when you reload.

## What leaves the page, and only when you ask

- **Download the spectrum** and **Download the bath** create files through your browser's normal download. The bath file includes the name of any spectrum file you loaded.
- **Copy a link to this bath** writes a link to your clipboard. It contains the pH, path length, light and each dye's preset, pKa and concentration, never your spectrum files.

## The rest of the page

The tool page loads the site's usual pages, styles and scripts, and, depending on your cookie choices, advertising and consent-based analytics, like the rest of auricartisan.com. See the Privacy Policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/ and the Cookie Policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/.
