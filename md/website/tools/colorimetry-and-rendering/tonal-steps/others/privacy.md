# Tonal Steps privacy

- **Everything runs in your browser.** Ramps, sets, contrast figures and list analyses are computed on your device.
- **Exports are created locally** and saved through your browser's download, or copied to your clipboard when you choose.
- **The link** carries the base colour, engine, step count, gamma and mixing space. Anyone with the link sees them.
- **Colours you paste** into Analyse a list stay in the page.
- **Save to Library** stores a palette in your Auric Artisan Library.
- The page loads the site's shared features like every other page on auricartisan.com; see the site's privacy policy.
