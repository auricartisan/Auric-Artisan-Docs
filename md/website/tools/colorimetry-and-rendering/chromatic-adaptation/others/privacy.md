# Chromatic Adaptation privacy

- **Your image never leaves your device.** When you choose an image, the browser reads it locally, draws it onto the page and adapts it there. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Exports are created locally.** The PNG frame and JSON file are generated in the browser and saved through your normal download.
- **Share links carry settings only** — illuminants, transform, D, initial adaptation and time. They never include your image.
- **Clipboard.** Copy Link, Generate, Copy and the Copy as buttons write to your clipboard only when you use them; your browser may ask for permission.
- **Batch colours** you paste stay in the page and are gone when you close it.
- **Save to Library** stores a preset of your settings in your Auric Artisan Library; it does not store the image.
- The page loads the site's shared features in the same way as every other page on auricartisan.com; see the site's privacy policy.
