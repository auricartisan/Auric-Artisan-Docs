# Settings panel privacy

## What stays on your device

- Every setting in the panel, including your saved colours, is kept in your browser's local storage for auricartisan.com.
- The panel's position and size are kept the same way.
- Nothing is sent to Auric Artisan when you change a setting. The settings work signed out and are not attached to an account.
- **Export** creates a file on your own device. It contains only your settings.

## What uses the network

- Opening the **Ads** section checks your plan in the background, to decide whether the ad controls are unlocked. If you are signed in, this uses your existing session.
- Choosing **हिन्दी** downloads the Hindi dictionaries for the pages you visit (see [Language](../../language/README.md)).

## Links can carry settings

If you copy a page link from the site, it can include your appearance settings that differ from the defaults (for example your accent and text size), so the person you send it to sees the page as you do. See [Shareable links](../../shareable-links/README.md) for what a link contains and how to share a clean one.

## Removing your settings

- **Reset all** in the panel returns everything to the defaults (saved colours are kept until you remove them).
- Clearing site data for auricartisan.com in your browser removes all stored settings.

The site's full policies are on https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/ and https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/.
