# The reminder and the rest

## The reminder card

When the countdown ends, a card appears:

- **Time to look away**
- "You have been at this for 20 minutes. 30 seconds looking into the distance is enough." (with your own interval and rest length)
- **Rest now** and **Later**
- a quieter row: **Change**, **Not this session**, **Turn these off**
- a thin bar that empties over 60 seconds

If you do nothing, the card leaves by itself after 60 seconds (when the bar is empty), and the next countdown starts.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Rest now** | Starts the rest straight away |
| **Later** | Closes the card; the next reminder comes after a full interval |
| **Change** | Shows the timing options (below) |
| **Not this session** | No more reminders until you close this browser tab |
| **Turn these off** | Stops reminders (see the note in [Getting started](getting-started.md#turn-reminders-off)) |

## Change how often and how long

Select **Change**. The card becomes **How often, and how long**:

1. **Remind me every**: **20m**, **35m**, **45m** or **60m**. This changes the same setting as **Reminder interval** in Settings, and the card's sentence updates.
2. **Rest for**: **5s**, **10s**, **15s**, **20s**, **25s** or **30s**.
3. Select **Rest now** to start a rest with these timings, or **Later** or **Turn these off** below.

This view also leaves by itself after 60 seconds.

> **Note:** The rest length you pick here applies until you move to another page or reload. It then returns to 30 seconds.

## During the rest

- The screen fills with a dark sky and ground, divided by a horizon line.
- Above the line: **Look past the screen** — "Something about twenty feet away — a doorway, a window, the far wall. The screen will brighten when the rest is over."
- Below the line: the seconds remaining, counting down.
- The horizon line slowly shrinks towards the centre as the rest passes. With your device's reduced-motion setting on, it stays still.
- The floating buttons, kits, dock and info bars are hidden. The pointer stays visible.
- **End early** stops the rest. It does not turn reminders off; the next countdown starts.

## When the rest ends

The whole screen brightens to a warm off-white, a change you can notice from the corner of your eye. It shows **Rest complete**, **Welcome back** and "Next reminder in 20 minutes." (with your interval). After about 2.6 seconds the page returns and the next countdown starts.
