# Header and navigation privacy

- The header's menus and the drawer do not store anything themselves.
- The **Theme** and **Language** switches in the header and drawer store your choice in this browser, the same way the Settings panel does.
- The header checks whether you are signed in, so it can show your account menu instead of **Sign in**. For visitors who are not signed in, no account request is made.
- The page utilities bar does not store whether it was open.
- The footer's social links open other websites (Product Hunt, LinkedIn, GitHub, X) in a new tab only when you select them. **Explore our portfolio, LEACB** opens the studio's own portfolio site.
