# The footer

The footer closes every page. It has four parts.

## Introduction

- The Auric Artisan logo (links to the homepage) and the statement "Color intelligence. Crafted for everyone."
- The **Start a conversation** card: "Questions, collaborations, or support?" with an **Email our studio** link to `hello@auricartisan.com`, and **Explore our portfolio, LEACB**, which opens the studio's portfolio site in a new tab.

Right-click the Start a conversation card for a small menu: **Email us**, **Copy email**, **Copy link** and **LEACB**. See [Other right-click menus](../../context-menu/docs/other-right-click-menus.md#footer-contact-card).

## Directory

Under the heading "Tools, policies, and useful links", four numbered columns:

| Column | Links |
|---|---|
| **01 Product** | Tools, Learn, Blog, Portfolios, API, Search Console, Download |
| **02 Explore** | Privacy, Terms, Cookies, Refund Policy, License |
| **03 Policies** | Acceptable Use Policy, Content & Intellectual Property, Data Retention & Deletion, Transparency, Subscription & Billing |
| **04 Trust** | Trust Center, Security, Accessibility, Contact |

## Bottom row

- The copyright line and "Design and development by Auric Artisan", with the company's registered address.
- Social links, each opening in a new tab: Product Hunt, LinkedIn, GitHub and X.
- **Made with care · Built in India**, with a small 3D Indian flag. Double-click the flag to play a short celebration animation. On Indian festival days, a festival greeting appears beside it.

## Closing note

"Company registered in India. Product names, logos, and brands remain the property of their respective owners."
