# The mobile drawer

When the window is narrower than 960 pixels, for example on a phone, a tablet in portrait, or a narrow desktop window, the header menus and quick tools move into a **drawer**. The drawer is the same content as the desktop header, with every item labelled in words.

## The bar

The header bar on a narrow screen holds:

- the **Auric Artisan** name (to the homepage);
- a **Search** button. On screens 600 pixels wide or more it also shows the word "Search" and a `Ctrl K` hint; on phones it is an icon;
- your account button, once you are signed in;
- the menu button (three lines). Its label is **Open navigation**, and **Close navigation** while the drawer is open, when it shows an X.

## Open and close the drawer

1. Tap the menu button. The drawer opens:
   - on phones, as a full-width sheet from under the bar to the bottom of the screen;
   - on screens from 600 to 959 pixels wide, as a sheet 400 pixels wide on the right.
2. While it is open, the page behind it does not scroll, and the floating buttons are hidden.
3. To close it, tap the menu button again, tap the dimmed page outside the drawer, press `Esc`, or open any link in it. It also closes if you widen the window past 960 pixels or go back or forward in your history.

## What is in the drawer

From top to bottom:

### Search field

**Search tools, colours and pages**. Tap it to open Spotlight search.

### Browse

**Company**, **Tools** and **Learn**, each with a count of its entries. Tap one to open it in place, like an accordion. Opening one section does not close the drawer, and tapping a switch further down does not fold the section away.

In **Tools**, a row of **group chips** sits above the list: **Core tools**, **Creative utilities**, **Colour science**, **Gamut & rendering**, **Perception**, **Spectral** and **Utilities**, each with a count. Only the selected group's tools are listed, with **All Tools** always at the top. The drawer selects the group of the page you are on, or **Core tools** if you are not on a tool page. Tap another chip to switch group.

### Your work

- **Library** — Saved palettes and exports. Opens your library panel.
- **Guide** — Tours and shortcuts. Opens the page guide.

### Get Auric

- **The app** — Works offline in a browser tab. Opens the Download page.
- **Browser extension** — The Auric toolbar in your browser.
- **VS Code** — Icon collections and colour themes.

### Preferences

- **Theme**: **Auto**, **Light**, **Dark**. **Auto** follows your device, like **System** in the Settings panel.
- **Language**: **English**, **हिन्दी**. Each language is written in its own script, so you can find yours even if you cannot read the current one.

The highlighted buttons always show your current choice, including changes made elsewhere, such as in Settings.

### Account

Pinned to the foot of the drawer: **Sign in** and **Create account** when you are signed out, or your account once you are signed in.

## With the keyboard

The drawer works with `Tab`, `Enter` and `Space` like the rest of the page. Inside an open section, `Up`, `Down`, `Home` and `End` move between the visible entries. `Esc` closes an open section first, then the drawer, and returns focus to the menu button.
