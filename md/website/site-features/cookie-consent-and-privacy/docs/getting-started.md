# Get started with cookie consent

## When the bar appears

The consent bar appears at the bottom of the page on your first visit, once the short welcome animation has finished. It stays until you choose. It does not dim or block the page, so you can read the page before deciding.

It does not appear on the sign-in and verification pages, and it does not appear again once you have made a choice in this browser.

## What it says

**We value your privacy**

The bar explains that Auric Artisan uses cookies and local storage to keep the platform secure, remember your preferences (theme, accent colour, language and tool settings) and understand basic, anonymous usage. Essential cookies are always on; analytics and other optional cookies run only with your consent, and your data is never sold. It links to the full **Cookie Policy**.

## The three buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Accept All** | Turns on every category: Essential, Functional, Analytics and Marketing |
| **Reject All** | Keeps only Essential, turns the others off, and deletes the site's non-essential cookies |
| **Customise** | Opens the Cookie Policy page at **Manage Cookie Preferences**, where you choose each category |

After **Accept All** or **Reject All**, the bar slides away and your choice is recorded with the date and time.

> **Note:** The bar says you can change your choice "from Settings". The cookie controls are on the Cookie Policy page, not in the Settings panel. See [Manage your cookie preferences](manage-cookie-preferences.md).

## What your choice affects

- **Analytics:** with Analytics on, the site may record anonymous usage events, such as which pages and features are used. With it off, no usage events are recorded. See [Privacy](../others/privacy.md) for details, including Do Not Track.
- **Functional:** covers remembering preferences such as theme, language and layout.
- **Marketing:** reserved for campaign attribution if the site enables it later. The policy states that Auric Artisan does not currently use marketing pixels or cross-site advertising profiles.
- **Essential:** signing in, security and remembering your consent choice. Always on.
