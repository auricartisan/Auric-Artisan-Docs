# Cookie consent FAQ

## Does the site sell my data?

The consent notice states that your data is never sold. The full terms are in the Privacy Policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/.

## Are the cookies third-party?

All cookies listed on the Cookie Policy page are first-party (set by auricartisan.com).

## What happens if I ignore the bar?

Nothing optional is enabled. No analytics events are recorded until you accept Analytics. The bar stays until you choose.

## Does rejecting cookies stop the tools from working?

No. Essential functions and every tool keep working. Rejecting only turns off the optional categories.

## Does my browser's Do Not Track setting count?

Yes. With Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control turned on in your browser, the site's analytics stays off even if you accepted Analytics. See [Privacy](../others/privacy.md).

## Where are the full policies?

- Cookie Policy: https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/
- Privacy Policy: https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/

They are also summarised in [Company and legal](../../../company-and-legal/README.md).
