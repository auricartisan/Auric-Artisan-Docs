# Screen saver privacy

- Your on or off choice and delay are kept in this browser, as part of your settings.
- The screen saver watches for activity only to know when you are idle. It does not record or send what you do.
- The first time it starts, it downloads a small list of atlas colours from auricartisan.com. Nothing is sent in return.
