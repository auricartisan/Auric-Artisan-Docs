# Feedback FAQ

## Do I have to give my name or email?

No. **Send anonymously** is always available and sends your feedback without contact details.

## Will I get a reply?

Only if you add contact details and the team decides to follow up. A bug report is not a promise of a fix, but reports are reviewed.

## Can I attach a screenshot or recording?

Not in the feedback panel. You can describe the problem, or contact the team through https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/.

## Why does the site wait so long before asking?

So that it asks people who have actually used the site, and does not interrupt short visits.

## Does the prompt appear on every device?

The timing and your choices are kept per browser, so each browser counts separately.
