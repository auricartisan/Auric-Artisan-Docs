# Troubleshooting the navigation dock

## I cannot find the dock

**Why:** it collapses to a thin, faint line at the edge of the window a moment after the page loads. On phones it has no visible button on the narrowest screens.

**What to do:** look halfway down the left edge (or the right edge, if you moved it) and point at the line. On a phone, open the page utilities bar and select **Navigate**.

## The dock closes before I can use it

**Why:** it collapses half a second after the pointer leaves it.

**What to do:** keep the pointer over the dock, or tab into it with the keyboard; it stays open while a button in it has focus. The outline keeps it open too.

## Back and Forward are greyed out

**Why:** there is no page in that direction in this tab's history. If you opened the page in a new tab, there is nothing to go back to.

## Share only copied the link

**Why:** your browser or device has no share sheet available to web pages, so the dock copies the link instead and shows a tick.

**What to do:** paste the link into the app you want to share it with.

## The copied link is very long

**Why:** the address includes the page's remembered state after `#p=`, such as your scroll position and values you entered. See [Shareable links](../../shareable-links/README.md).

**What to do:** to share a clean link, scroll to the top of the page and clear any values you typed before copying, or delete the part from `#p=` onwards after pasting.

## The outline says "No sections on this page"

**Why:** the page's main content has no headings the outline can list.

**What to do:** use **Scroll to top** and **Scroll to bottom**, or the page guide (`?`).

## The dock covers part of the page

**What to do:** drag it up or down with the grip, or move it to the other side with **Flip dock side**. On a phone, tap outside it to close it.
