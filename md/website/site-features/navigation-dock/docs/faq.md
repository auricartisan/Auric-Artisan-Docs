# Navigation dock FAQ

## Can I turn the dock off?

There is no switch to remove it. It collapses to a thin line when you are not using it, and you can move it wherever it is least in the way.

## Can I keep the dock open all the time?

No. On desktop it opens when you point at it or tab into it, and collapses when you move away.

## Does the dock work in the installed app?

Yes. It is especially useful there, where the browser's own Back, Forward and Reload buttons may not be visible.

## Is the dock the same as the header menus?

No. The header takes you to other pages; the dock moves you around the page you are on and through your history.

## Why is there no dock inside split view?

Each pane of split view has its own Back, Forward and Reload buttons, so the dock stays out of the panes.
