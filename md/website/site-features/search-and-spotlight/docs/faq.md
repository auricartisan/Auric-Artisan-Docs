# Spotlight FAQ

## What is the difference between Spotlight and the search page?

Spotlight is the quick box that floats over any page and shows up to 8 results. The search page at https://auricartisan.com/search/ shows every result with filters, previews and answers. **View all results for "…"** moves your query from one to the other. The search page is documented in [Search](../../../search/README.md).

## Does Spotlight work offline?

Searching the site can work offline once you have prepared the offline version on the Download page, because the site's search index is part of it (see [Download and offline](../../../download-and-offline/README.md)). Opening a result offline works only for pages that were saved. The **Web** tab always needs a connection.

## Can I search in Hindi?

Commands can be found by their Hindi or English names. Page search matches the English text of pages, so English words give the best results.

## Why does a result I open often move up?

Spotlight gives a small boost to pages you have opened from search before, in this browser.

## Does Spotlight show ads?

It can show a compact sponsored strip while ads are running on the site. The **Ads in panels & search** setting controls it on plans that include ad preferences (see [Settings panel › Tooltips and ads](../../settings-panel/docs/tooltips-and-ads.md)).

## What does `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `K` do?

It opens the Feature Kit (calculator, converter and other small tools), which is separate from Spotlight. See [Kits](../../../kits/README.md).
