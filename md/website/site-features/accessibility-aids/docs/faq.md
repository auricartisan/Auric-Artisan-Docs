# Accessibility aids FAQ

## Do I need an account to use these aids?

No. All of them work signed out and are kept in your browser.

## Can I carry my setup to another computer?

Yes. Use **Export** in the Settings panel, then **Import** on the other computer.

## Is there a dyslexia-friendly font?

The site does not offer a font switch. Reading mode, larger text and underlined links can make long text easier to read.

## Where is the site's accessibility statement?

At https://auricartisan.com/company/accessibility/, also reachable from the **Company** menu and the footer.

## Are the colour tools themselves accessible?

The tools have their own documentation, which describes their keyboard and screen reader support where it differs from the site's.
