# Info bars and notices privacy

- Which bars and notices you show or hide, and your clock choices, are kept in this browser as part of your settings.
- The first-visit welcome remembers that it has played, in this browser.
- The clock uses your device's time and time zone. Nothing about them is sent to Auric Artisan.
- Nothing is recorded when you open, copy or hide a bar.
