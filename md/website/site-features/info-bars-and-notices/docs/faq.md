# Info bars and notices FAQ

## Why are the bars only on the homepage?

They are an introduction. On other pages they would push the content down, so by default they stay on the homepage. You can show them everywhere from Settings › Highlights.

## Can I see the first-visit welcome again?

It plays once per browser. It plays again only if you clear the site's data in your browser.

## Does the site send notifications?

No. All messages appear on the page itself.

## Can I show a world clock for another city?

Yes. Right-click the clock strip and choose that city's time zone. The strip shows one zone at a time.

## Does the clock strip use my Settings clock choices?

Yes. The strip and Settings › Clock share 24-hour time, seconds, date format and time zone.
