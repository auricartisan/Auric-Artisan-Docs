# Notices and status messages

## Hero notice

A highlighted notice banner in the homepage's opening section. Select its ✕ (**Dismiss (re-enable in Settings → Comfort)**) to hide it. Switch it back on with **Hero notice** in Settings › **Highlights**.

## Demo chips

Small labels with an information mark that sit above sections showing a live demo, marking them as "live" or "demo" content. Select the ✕ on any chip (**Hide demo chips (re-enable in Settings → Highlights)**) to hide every demo chip on the site. Switch them back on with **Live demo chips** in Settings › Highlights.

## First-visit welcome

The first time you open the site in a browser, a short animated welcome plays over the page for about a second (shorter if your device asks for reduced motion). Click it, or press `Esc`, `Enter` or `Space`, to skip it. It plays only once per browser. The cookie consent bar appears after it.

## Loading screen

While a page is loading, a brief screen with the Auric Artisan logo and the line "Preparing local color tools." may show, with a progress bar. It disappears when the page is ready.

## Connection messages

A small message appears at the edge of the screen when your connection changes:

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Offline mode** | Your device has lost its connection. The message stays while you are offline. Saved pages and tools keep working. |
| **Back online** | The connection has returned. |

## App messages

When the site is installed as an app or prepared for offline use, you may see:

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Update ready** — "Refresh to use the newest offline cache." (**Refresh** / **Later**) | A new version of the site is ready |
| **Saving for offline — N%** | An offline download you asked for is in progress |
| **Full offline app ready** | The offline download has finished |
| **Auric Artisan installed** | The app was installed |
| **N files ready** / **Could not open file** | Files opened with the installed app |

These are explained in [Download and offline](../../../download-and-offline/README.md). Website updates can also be announced by the Update Kit; see [Kits](../../../kits/README.md).

## Confirmation messages

Many actions show a short confirmation that fades after a couple of seconds, for example "Settings exported", "Page URL copied" or "Theme: Dark". They are announced to screen readers without moving focus. Each feature's reference page lists its messages.

## Notifications

The site does not send system or push notifications. Everything above appears on the page itself.
