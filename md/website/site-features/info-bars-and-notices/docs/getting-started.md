# Get started with info bars and notices

## What appears by default

| Item | Default |
|---|---|
| Welcome bar | Homepage only |
| Announcement bar | Homepage only |
| Quick-links bar | Homepage only (also at the foot of the Collections page) |
| Clock strip | Off |
| Hero notice | On (homepage) |
| Demo chips | On (where a page has them) |

On every page, the announcement bar sits directly under the header, followed by the clock strip, then the page content.

## Hide something

Select the ✕ at the end of a bar, on the hero notice, or on a demo chip. It disappears at once, and the matching switch in Settings › **Highlights** turns off. A demo chip's ✕ hides every demo chip on the site.

## Show something again, or everywhere

1. Press `Ctrl` + `,` and select **Highlights**.
2. Switch on the item you want.

For the three bars, switching on shows the bar on **every** page. To return to the homepage-only default, select **Restore section defaults**. Details are in [Settings panel › Highlights and clock](../../settings-panel/docs/highlights-and-clock.md).

## Changes apply everywhere at once

A change made with a ✕ or in Settings applies straight away to every page, including pages already open in other tabs.

## In reading mode and the screen saver

The bars and the clock strip are hidden in reading mode and while the screen saver is showing.
