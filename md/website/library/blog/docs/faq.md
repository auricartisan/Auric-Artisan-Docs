# Blog FAQ

## How often are posts published?

Occasionally. The page describes them as "occasional posts". There were three at the time of writing.

## What is the difference between the blog, the changelog and Learn?

The blog explains plans and reasoning. The [changelog](../../changelog/README.md) records what changed in each release. [Learn](../../learn/README.md) holds the long-form colour-science course.

## Can I comment?

Posts do not have comments. The welcome post invites questions and topic ideas by email or social media; see [Company and legal](../../../company-and-legal/README.md) for contact details.

## Is there a feed?

Yes: https://auricartisan.com/library/blog/rss.xml.
