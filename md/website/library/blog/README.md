# Blog

The Auric Artisan blog is where the site's author writes, in the page's words, "occasional posts about what is being built and why — roadmaps, product notes, and the odd essay on colour". It is small by design: there are three posts so far. Release-by-release detail lives in the changelog, and long-form colour science in Learn; the blog is for the reasoning and the plans in between.

The blog page shows the newest post large, with its opening paragraph and a **Read the post** link, and lists the earlier posts underneath with their dates, categories and one-line summaries.

## Who it is for

- Anyone who wants to know where Auric Artisan is heading and why.
- Existing users who want a readable summary of what has changed, beyond the changelog's lists.

## Where to find it

- The blog: https://auricartisan.com/library/blog/
- From the site header: **Learn** menu › **Blog** ("Articles, ideas & studio news").
- RSS feed: https://auricartisan.com/library/blog/rss.xml

## Quick start

1. Open https://auricartisan.com/library/blog/.
2. Read the opening of the newest post and select **Read the post**.
3. On the post, use the **Table of Contents** or open and close sections as you read.
4. Select **← Back to Blog** to return, or **Print** to print the post.
5. Add the RSS feed to your news reader to hear about new posts.

## What you can do

- Read every post, newest first.
- Follow new posts by RSS.
- Print a post.
