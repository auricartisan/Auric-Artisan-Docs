# Privacy

## What stays in your browser

| What | Why | How to remove it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saved colours | To fill the **Saved** tab and mark stars on tiles. | Unsave each colour, or clear this site's data. |
| A copy of each saved colour in your Library | So saved colours appear alongside your other saved items. | Unsave the colour, or remove it in the Library. |
| Whether you dismissed the **Automate this** note | So it stays hidden. | Clear this site's data. |

The colours you type into **Start from a colour you already have**, your searches, filters and chip choices are worked out in your browser and are not sent anywhere. They are not remembered after you leave the page.

## What uses the network

- When the page opens, it downloads the atlas from auricartisan.com.
- When you open a colour, it may download that colour's full record from auricartisan.com.
- Downloads you make (JSON, SVG, PNG, CSS and so on) are created in your browser and saved straight to your device.
- Copying writes to your device's clipboard only.

The page can show advertising, and the site's analytics run only with your consent. See [Company and legal](../../../../company-and-legal/README.md) for the privacy policy and your consent choices.
