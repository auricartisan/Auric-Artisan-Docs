# Troubleshooting

## "Could not load harmony database"

**Why:** The small description of the collection could not be downloaded.

**What to do:** Check your connection and any content blocker, and reload.

## The page is slow to open

**Why:** All 8,192 harmonies are calculated in your browser when the page opens, so that every filter looks at the whole collection. On an older device this can take a moment.

**What to do:** Wait for the grid to appear. After that, filtering is immediate.

## Choosing a Method does not change the grid

**Why:** On the current page, the **Method** filter does not narrow the results.

**What to do:** Type the method's name into the search box, for example `triadic` or `golden_ratio`. Use **Family** for broader groups.

## "No scheme uses N colours"

**Why:** The schemes have between two and seven colours. With eight or more colours there is nothing to compare.

**What to do:** Remove colours until you have seven or fewer.

## The identifier names several schemes

**Why:** Some schemes differ only in lightness or saturation, which hue matching cannot see: shades, tints and neutral share a shape with monochromatic 5; warm cool with complementary; triad shifted with triadic.

**What to do:** Choose between them by looking at your colours' lightness and saturation. See the scheme descriptions in the [Glossary](../others/glossary.md).

## My palette is "Not one of the 23"

**Why:** Its hues do not sit near any scheme's pattern. As the page says, most palettes in the world are not one of 23 schemes.

**What to do:** Nothing needs fixing. If you want a textbook scheme, the runners-up under **Also considered** show which is nearest.

## Few harmonies have text-safe pairs

**Why:** Harmonies are defined by hue. Unless their colours also differ strongly in lightness, no pair reaches 4.5:1. About a quarter of the collection has at least one AA pair.

**What to do:** Use the **Accessibility** tab to see which harmonies do, or pair a harmony with a neutral text colour from the [Accessibility Library](../../accessibility-library/README.md).

## A share link does not open the harmony

**Why:** The link was cut short or edited.

**What to do:** Ask for the id and search for it.

## Saved harmonies have gone

**Why:** Saves live in this browser only (up to 500).

**What to do:** Save them again, or keep their ids.
