# Continue your journey

Every Learn article ends with **Continue your journey**. Instead of a fixed list, the page suggests seven articles chosen for the one you are reading and for what you have already read in this browser.

## What you see

- A line above the cards: **Tailored to** the article's category, followed by **You've read N of 113 in the library**.
- A **More like this** button, when there are more suggestions to show.
- One featured card, full width — usually the natural next article.
- Six more cards.

Each card has an illustration coloured by category, a small badge giving the reason it was chosen, the title, the category, a tick if you have read it, and a pin button.

### The reason badges

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pinned** | You pinned this article. |
| **Up next in** a category | The next unread article in the same category, in course order. |
| **Shares** a topic | It shares one or more topic tags with the current article. |
| **More in** a category | It is in the same category. |
| **Related idea** | Its title or summary shares key words with the current article. |
| **Recommended** | A good general match. |

## How suggestions are chosen

The page scores every other article against the one you are reading:

- same category: a strong boost;
- each shared topic tag: a boost;
- shared key words in title and summary: a small boost;
- close to it in course order within the same category: a small boost;
- not yet read: a boost; already read: a penalty (read articles can still appear);
- the next unread article in the same category: an extra boost, so it usually takes the featured slot;
- pinned by you: always first.

To keep the list varied, no more than three suggestions from one category come first; the rest follow.

## Pin an article

1. Select the pin button on a card. Its label reads "Pin" and the title; its tooltip says **Pin to top**.
2. The suggestions are re-ranked, and the pinned article moves to the front with the **Pinned** badge.
3. To unpin, select the pin again (tooltip **Pinned — click to unpin**).

Pins are kept in this browser and apply on every article, so a pinned article stays at the front of the suggestions until you unpin it. These pins are separate from pins elsewhere on the site.

## See more

Select **More like this** to show the next seven suggestions. Select it again to keep rotating; it wraps back to the start.

## If the suggestions do not change

If the page cannot load the Learn catalogue (for example when offline), or the article is not in it, the author's original suggestions stay in place. They are still good links; they just are not personalised.

## Result

You always have a sensible next step: the next article in the course, the closest related ideas, and the articles you chose to keep.
