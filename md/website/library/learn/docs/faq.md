# Learn FAQ

## How many articles are there?

113, numbered in course order, taking about 64 hours in total. See the [Article catalogue](article-catalog.md).

## Do I have to read them in order?

No. The order is a suggested path from basics to specialist topics. Filter by category or search for what you need.

## Do I need an account?

No. Everything is free and open, and your progress is kept in your browser.

## Does my progress sync between devices?

No. Progress, ticks and pins stay in the browser where you read.

## Can I keep a copy of an article?

Yes. Use **Download** for Markdown or plain text, or **Print**.

## Are the demos accurate?

They are teaching models built for the article, computed in your browser. They illustrate the ideas faithfully but are not measurement instruments. See [Limits and accuracy](../others/limits-and-accuracy.md).

## Is there a feed?

Yes: https://auricartisan.com/library/learn/rss.xml.

## Why do titles say "color"?

Article titles keep the spelling they were published with. The site's own interface text uses British spelling.
