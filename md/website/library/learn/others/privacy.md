# Privacy

## What stays in your browser

| What | Why | How to remove it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How far you scrolled in each article, and when | To tick read articles, show your progress and offer **Pick up where you left off**. | Clear this site's data. |
| Articles you pinned in **Continue your journey** | To keep them at the front of the suggestions. | Unpin them, or clear this site's data. |

None of this is sent to Auric Artisan or linked to an account.

## What uses the network

- Pages, images and the Learn catalogue are downloaded from auricartisan.com.
- Demos run in your browser.
- **Download** builds the file in your browser.

Advertising and analytics follow your consent choices. See [Company and legal](../../../company-and-legal/README.md).
