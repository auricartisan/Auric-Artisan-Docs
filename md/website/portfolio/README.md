# Portfolio Builder and Portfolio Index

The **Portfolio Builder** at https://auricartisan.com/portfolio/ turns the work you make on Auric Artisan into a public page. You stack sections — headings, Markdown text, images, galleries, a timeline, stats, testimonials, your saved palettes and more, 25 kinds in all — and type straight into a live preview of the page as it builds beside you. You choose a typeface, a paper and a rhythm, and an accent colour taken from your own saved palettes and graded for contrast. A **Check** pane audits the rendered page for accessibility problems before anyone else sees it.

When it is ready, you publish it at your own address, `auricartisan.com/p/your-name`, or share it privately as a self-contained link. You can also download it as an HTML file or PDF, or export the document as JSON to keep or move. Publishing again updates the same address, and you can take it down at any time.

The **Portfolio Index** at https://auricartisan.com/portfolios/ lists portfolios in one place — those made with the builder and those people keep on their own sites — searchable by name, craft, place and colour. A builder portfolio joins it when you publish (unless you switch that off); a portfolio on your own site can be added through the Search Console.

## Who it is for

- Designers, illustrators, photographers and developers who want a portfolio without building a website.
- Anyone who wants to show palettes, gradients and scales they made on Auric Artisan as live, copyable values.
- People who want a CV-style page, a studio one-pager or a simple link page.
- Visitors looking for designers by craft, place or colour.

## Where to find it

- The builder: https://auricartisan.com/portfolio/ (also in the **Tools** menu and on the Collections page as **Portfolio Builder**).
- Published portfolios: `https://auricartisan.com/p/` followed by the owner's address.
- The directory: https://auricartisan.com/portfolios/
- You need an Auric Artisan account, signed in, to build and publish. Viewing portfolios and the directory needs no account.

## Quick start

1. Sign in and open https://auricartisan.com/portfolio/.
2. Select **Templates**, pick one (for example **Designer**) and select **Use this template**.
3. Fill in the **Header**: your name, headline and the details you want to show.
4. Click any text in the live preview and type over the examples.
5. Open **Style** and choose a typeface, paper, rhythm and accent.
6. Open **Check** and fix anything marked blocking.
7. Select **Publish**, choose **Public at an address**, type your address and select **Publish**.
8. Copy the address and share it.

## What you can do

- Build a page from 25 section types, and type directly into the preview.
- Start from eight templates: Designer, Developer, Photographer, Freelancer, Founder, Writer, Resume and Minimal.
- Write in Markdown, or paste your own HTML and CSS into a sanitised Raw HTML section.
- Show your saved palettes as live swatches visitors can copy as CSS or JSON.
- Style the page with four typefaces, five papers, three rhythms and a graded accent.
- Preview at fit, desktop, tablet and mobile widths, and full screen.
- Audit contrast, links, alt text, headings and target sizes before publishing.
- Undo and redo; your draft saves itself in the browser and, when signed in, to your account.
- Export HTML, PDF or JSON; import JSON; copy a snapshot link.
- Publish at your own address with a QR code, a share card, a view count and control over search, the index and PDF downloads.
- Find portfolios in the Portfolio Index, and list yours — including one you host elsewhere.
