# Getting started

## Sign in

Open https://auricartisan.com/portfolio/. If you are not signed in, the page shows **Sign in to build your portfolio** — "Sign in to add sections, write in Markdown or HTML, showcase your work, and publish or export your portfolio." — with a **Sign In** button. Sign in, and the builder opens. For accounts and signing in, see [Account and billing](../../account-and-billing/README.md).

## A tour of the screen

### The top bar

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Your portfolio** and an address | Your published address, for example `auricartisan.com/p/your-name`, and **Not published** until it is live. |
| Command field — "Add a section, change the look, publish…" (`Ctrl K`) | Opens a searchable list of everything the builder can do. Type a few letters and press `Enter`. |
| Section count and **Saved** | How many sections the page has, and whether your latest change is saved. |
| Undo and Redo | Step back and forward through changes. |
| **Templates** | Start from a ready-made layout. |
| **Preview** | Open the finished page in a new tab. |
| **Export** | Download HTML or PDF, export or import JSON, copy a snapshot link, and the credit-line switch. |
| **Publish** | Open the publish dialog. |

On narrower screens, **Templates**, **Preview** and **Export** may be tucked away; the command field still reaches them.

### The mode tabs

- **Content** — the Header panel and your sections.
- **Style** — typeface, paper, rhythm and accent.
- **Check** — an accessibility audit of the page. A dot appears on the tab when it has something to say.

### The Content column

- **Header** — the top of your portfolio: name, headline, email, location, website, avatar and accent. See [Header and avatar](header-and-avatar.md).
- Your sections, one card each, with controls to move, hide, duplicate and delete. See [Manage sections](manage-sections.md).
- **Add a section** — a searchable set of all 25 section types.

### The live preview

On the right (or below, on narrow screens), **Live preview** shows the page as visitors will see it. You can type straight into it; see [Edit in the preview](edit-in-the-preview.md). Its bar has width buttons (**Fit**, desktop, tablet, mobile), the current width and section count, and a full-screen button.

## Your first portfolio

1. Select **Templates**. The **Start from a template** dialog opens.
2. Choose **Designer** and select **Use this template**. The page fills with example sections.
3. In **Header**, type your **Name** and a **Headline / tagline**. Leave fields blank to use your profile details.
4. In the preview, click the **Selected Work** heading and any example text, and type your own.
5. Open **Style**, choose a **Typeface** and **Paper**, and pick an accent from your palettes.
6. Open **Check**. If it lists anything as **Blocking**, fix it; the **Publish** button stays disabled until you do.
7. Select **Publish**. Choose **Public at an address**, type an address in **Your address** and wait for it to be confirmed as available.
8. Select **Publish**. The dialog shows your live address with **Copy**, **Open** and **QR code**.

**Result:** your portfolio is live at `auricartisan.com/p/` plus your address, and by default it is listed in the [Portfolio Index](portfolio-index.md).
