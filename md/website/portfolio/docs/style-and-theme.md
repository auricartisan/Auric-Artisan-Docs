# Style and theme

Open the **Style** tab. A portfolio's look is four choices, each from a named, checked set rather than free colour pickers, so the page stays readable.

## Typeface

| Choice | Headings | Body text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fraunces** (default) | Fraunces (serif) | Manrope (sans-serif) |
| **Newsreader** | Newsreader | Newsreader |
| **Space Grotesk** | Space Grotesk | Space Grotesk |
| **Manrope** | Manrope | Manrope |

## Paper

The page's background and text colour.

| Choice | Background | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cream** (default) | `#FBFAF7` | Dark text. |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | Dark text. |
| **Sand** | `#F2EDE3` | Dark text. |
| **Ink** | `#14151A` | A dark page with light text. |
| **No background** | None — "Nothing behind the page" | No fill, padding or rounded corner. Text follows the viewer's own light or dark theme. For pages that draw their own background, such as a Raw HTML hero. |

All the greys the page uses for secondary text are chosen to clear AA contrast on each of the four papers.

## Rhythm

The spacing and roundness of the page.

| Choice | Feel |
| --- | --- |
| **Tight** | Close spacing, square corners. |
| **Even** (default) | Moderate spacing, softly rounded. |
| **Airy** | Generous spacing, well rounded. |

## Accent

The accent colours links, headings and buttons. Under **Accent, from your library**, the Style tab lists the palettes and scales you have saved in your Library, newest first, as swatches.

- Each swatch is graded against the paper you chose. Hover to see, for example, "#8A6D16 — 4.70:1 on Cream (AA)".
- Swatches below 4.5:1 are marked as weak ("below AA for text"). You can still pick one, for borders and rules.
- If no step of a palette clears 4.5:1, the pane says "Nothing in this palette clears 4.5:1 on" the paper, and suggests a darker step or a different paper.
- With no saved palettes, it says: **Save a palette from any colour tool and its steps show up here as accents.**

Under the swatches, a line grades your current accent: "Your accent clears AA on" the paper, "at any size", or "Below 4.5:1 on" the paper, with the advice that it is "Fine for a rule or a border, not for words".

You can also set the accent with the colour picker in the Header panel.

## Reset

**Reset to defaults** returns to Fraunces, Cream, Even and the default accent `#8A6D16`.

## From the command field

Every choice is also a command: type, for example, "paper" and choose **Paper — Ink**, or "typeface" for **Typeface — Newsreader**.

## Result

Your portfolio has a consistent, readable look, and the accent is one you made yourself and know passes contrast.
