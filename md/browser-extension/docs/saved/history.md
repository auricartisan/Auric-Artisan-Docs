# History

History keeps the last 50 colours you picked or chose in the Picker, newest first, so a colour is never lost to a stray click.

## What goes into history

- colours you pick with the screen eyedropper;
- colours you finish choosing in the Picker;
- colours you open in the Picker from elsewhere, such as Recent, Page palette or the colour of the day.

The same colour is kept once, whatever its letter case. The newest 50 are kept.

## Use it

Open Saved › **History** ("Your last 50 colours.").

- **Click** a colour to copy it in the format set in Settings › Tools › **Copy colours as**.
- **Double-click** a colour to open it in the Picker.

Your most recent colours also appear on Home under **Recent**, and in the **Recent** strip of the Picker, Contrast, Harmony, Scale, Gradient and Mix.

## Clear it

- **Clear** empties the history at once. It does not ask first.
- Settings › Data › **Clear history & library** clears history and the library together, after you confirm.

## Turn history off

Turn off Settings › Tools › **Keep a colour history**. New colours are then not recorded. Colours already in history stay until you clear them.
