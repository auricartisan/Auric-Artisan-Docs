==============================================================================
LIGHT AND DARK THEME
==============================================================================
How to choose Light, Dark or System theme on the Auric Artisan website, and
how the choice is remembered.
Area: Getting started   Updated: 2026-09-25

The website has a light theme and a dark theme. By default it follows your
device: if your operating system or browser is set to dark mode, the site
opens dark.

CHOOSE A THEME
==============

  Where:   Header
  Control: The theme button (label "Toggle dark mode")
  Choices: Switches between light and dark

  Where:   Mobile navigation menu
  Control: Preferences › Theme
  Choices: Auto, Light, Dark

  Where:   Settings (`Ctrl` + `,`)
  Control: Appearance › Theme
  Choices: Light, Dark, System

  Where:   Keyboard
  Control: `Alt` + `T`
  Choices: Cycles light, then dark, then system

Auto and System mean the same thing: follow your device's setting, and change
when it changes.

HOW THE CHOICE IS REMEMBERED
============================

  - A choice you make is saved in this browser and applies to every page,
    including after a refresh.
  - An explicit Light or Dark choice wins over your device setting until you
    pick System or Auto again.
  - The choice is per browser. Another browser or device starts by following
    that device's setting.

ACCENT COLOUR AND COMFORT OPTIONS
=================================

The same Settings panel lets you pick an accent colour, change text size,
reduce motion or transparency, boost contrast and more. See Settings panel
