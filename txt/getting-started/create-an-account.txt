==============================================================================
CREATE AN ACCOUNT (OPTIONAL)
==============================================================================
What works without an Auric Artisan account, what an account adds, and the
short version of signing up.
Area: Getting started   Updated: 2026-09-25

You do not need an account to use Auric Artisan's tools. The pricing page puts
it plainly: every tool is free "with no login required to start". An account
adds a few things that need to be tied to you rather than to one browser.

WHAT WORKS WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
=============================

  - Every website tool, including LUT Lab, the ICC Parser, the Color Science
    Lab and the clinical vision simulations.
  - The libraries, the Learning Hub, the documentation and the blog.
  - Saving palettes, gradients and collections to My Library in your browser.
  - Drafting a portfolio in the Portfolio Builder.
  - The browser extension and the VS Code extensions.

WHAT AN ACCOUNT ADDS
====================

  With an account you can…:Run server-backed Analyzer work, such as whole-site
                           crawls
  Why it needs one:        It is paid for with tokens, and tokens belong to an
                           account. The free plan includes 100 starter tokens.

  With an account you can…:Buy a paid plan pass
  Why it needs one:        A pass belongs to your account so it follows you to
                           other devices. An account is required before any
                           payment.

  With an account you can…:Publish your portfolio at your own address
  Why it needs one:        The published page is linked to your username.

  With an account you can…:Create API keys for the REST API
  Why it needs one:        Keys and credits are counted per account (API
                           access is included from the Specialist plan).

  With an account you can…:See signed-in devices, turn on two-step
                           verification and export your data
  Why it needs one:        These are account security controls.

  With an account you can…:Approve the Auric command-line tool on a new
                           computer
  Why it needs one:        Device approval signs the tool in as you.

SIGN UP IN SHORT
================

  1. Go to https://auricartisan.com/auth/ (or select Create account in the
     header).
  2. Select the Create Account tab.
  3. Fill in Display Name, Username, Email, Password and Confirm Password.
     Passwords need 12–128 characters with upper and lower case letters, a
     number and a symbol.
  4. Tick I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
  5. Select Create Account and complete the short Auric human check (repeat a
     sequence of three marks).
  6. Open the verification email and confirm your address within 24 hours.

Or select Google or GitHub under or sign up with to use an existing account
with one of those providers.

The full walkthrough, including every rule and error message, is in Sign up
[../website/account-and-billing/docs/sign-up.txt].

  | Note: Until you verify your email address, the account works as a limited
  | workspace. The sign-in page says billing, promotions, API keys and device
  | approval stay locked until the address is verified.

AFTER YOU SIGN UP
=================

  - Your dashboard is at https://auricartisan.com/auth/dashboard/. See The
    dashboard [../website/account-and-billing/docs/getting-started.txt].
  - You start on the free Apprentice plan. Nothing is charged, and no card is
    asked for.
  - Consider turning on two-step verification: Two-step verification
    [../website/account-and-billing/docs/two-step-verification.txt].
