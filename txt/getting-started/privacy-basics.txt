==============================================================================
PRIVACY BASICS
==============================================================================
What Auric Artisan keeps in your browser, what it sends to a server, how the
cookie choice works, and where to read the full policies.
Area: Getting started   Updated: 2026-09-25

This page is a short orientation. The detailed version is Privacy overview
[../help/privacy.txt], and the official, authoritative text is the Privacy
Policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/ and the Cookie Policy at
https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/.

THE SHORT VERSION
=================

  - Most tools run in your browser. Colour calculations, palettes, vision
    simulations and theme generation are done locally where possible.
  - Your saved work and your preferences are stored in your browser on your
    device.
  - An account stores the details you give it (email, names, profile fields)
    and the security records needed to keep it safe.
  - Analytics is first-party, sets no cookies, and runs only if you consent to
    the Analytics category.
  - Payments go to Razorpay. Auric Artisan never sees or stores your card, UPI
    or bank details.
  - The Privacy Policy states that Auric Artisan does not sell personal data
    or use it for behavioural advertising.

WHAT STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER
==========================

  Kept in your browser | Examples
  ---------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Saved work           | Palettes, gradients, collections and exports in My Library
  Drafts               | Tool state and your Portfolio Builder draft
  Preferences          | Theme, language, accent colour, text size, eye-rest and other settings
  Your cookie choice   | The consent record from the cookie banner

The Privacy Policy says many preferences stay on your device and are not sent
to Auric Artisan's servers unless a tool needs them. Because this data lives
in the browser, clearing your browser's site data removes it, and it does not
follow you to another device. See Clear data and start fresh
[../help/troubleshooting.txt].

WHAT GOES TO A SERVER
=====================

  Sent to Auric Artisan's servers:Account details
  When:                    When you sign up, sign in, or edit your profile

  Sent to Auric Artisan's servers:Security records
  When:                    Sessions, sign-in attempts and security events

  Sent to Auric Artisan's servers:Web analysis inputs
  When:                    When a tool fetches or analyses a URL for you

  Sent to Auric Artisan's servers:Server-backed tool work
  When:                    When you start a run that uses tokens

  Sent to Auric Artisan's servers:Messages
  When:                    When you use the contact form

  Sent to Auric Artisan's servers:Order records
  When:                    When you buy something (the item, amount and
                           payment reference — not card details)

  Sent to Auric Artisan's servers:Analytics events
  When:                    Only with your consent: an event name, the page
                           path (never query strings), the referrer's origin
                           and a few coarse properties such as device class

COOKIES AND CONSENT
===================

On your first visit a bar at the bottom of the page, We value your privacy,
offers three choices:

  - Accept All — turns on every optional category.
  - Reject All — keeps only what is strictly necessary.
  - Customise — opens the preferences section of the Cookie Policy page, where
    you choose each category.

Essential cookies (for example the signed-in session and its security
companion) are always on because the site cannot work securely without them.
You can change your choice at any time on
https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/ or from Settings. The full walkthrough
is in Cookie consent and privacy
[../website/site-features/cookie-consent-and-privacy/README.txt].

Analytics also stays off when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global
Privacy Control, as the Privacy Policy states.

THIRD PARTIES
=============

The Privacy Policy names three: Cloudflare (hosting and storage), Razorpay
(payments) and Google Fonts (font files on some pages, which means Google
receives the technical request, such as your IP address). The Security page
adds Resend for email delivery.

THE BROWSER EXTENSION
=====================

The extension has its own policy: it only reads a page when you use a tool on
it, keeps everything in your browser's extension storage, and makes no network
requests with your data. See Extension privacy policy
[../website/company-and-legal/docs/extension-privacy-policy.txt].
