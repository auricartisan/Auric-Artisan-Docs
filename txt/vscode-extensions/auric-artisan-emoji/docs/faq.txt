==============================================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
==============================================================================
Short answers to common questions about Auric Artisan Emoji.
Area: VS Code extensions › Auric Artisan Emoji   Updated: 2026-09-25

DOES IT NEED THE INTERNET?
==========================

No. The catalog and Studio are bundled. There is no telemetry and no account.

WHY DO EMOJI LOOK DIFFERENT ON ANOTHER COMPUTER?
================================================

Emoji are drawn by each system's emoji font. The characters are the same; the
artwork differs.

DOES CLEARING HISTORY REMOVE FAVORITES?
=======================================

No. Clear Emoji History removes recent emoji and last-emoji recall only.

CAN I CHANGE THE DEFAULT SKIN TONE?
===================================

Yes: the Skin tone filter, Make {tone} my default in the inspector, or
`auricEmoji.preferredSkinTone`.

WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN INSERT AND THE CLICK ACTION?
===========================================================

The card's Insert button always inserts. Selecting the emoji itself (or
pressing `Enter`) runs your click action, which can be Insert, Copy, or Insert
and copy.

IS THIS THE SAME AS THE EMOJI IN ICON STUDIO?
=============================================

Icon Studio also includes 1,914 emoji for browsing alongside icons. This
extension adds the full emoji workflow: sequences, completions, shortcode
replacement, code hovers, six copy formats and a Hindi interface.

ARE AURIC ICONS: PLAYFUL ICONS EMOJI?
=====================================

No. Playful is a library of emoji-style SVG artwork, not Unicode emoji
characters.
