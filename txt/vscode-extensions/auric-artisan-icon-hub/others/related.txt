ICON HUB OR ICON STUDIO?
========================

  You want to: Recolour, restroke and resize icons with a full colour picker
  Use:         Icon Hub

  You want to: Export an SVG sprite or a ZIP with licences
  Use:         Icon Hub

  You want to: Load icons on your site from the Auric CDN
  Use:         Icon Hub

  You want to: Browse Auric Modern, Drawn and Playful artwork and emoji too
  Use:         Icon Studio

  You want to: Work with no network access at all
  Use:         Icon Studio (Icon Hub also works offline if you turn off
               metadata checks and avoid CDN output)

The two keep separate settings, views, favorites and recents. Icon Hub keeps
its own view even when Icon Studio hosts the Auric collections.
