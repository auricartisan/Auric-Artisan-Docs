==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
==============================================================================
Symptoms, causes and fixes for common problems with Auric Artisan Theme and
Theme Studio.
Area: VS Code extensions › Auric Artisan Theme   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Symptom:        Dials and colour changes have no effect.
  Why it happens: A non-Auric colour theme is active. Customisations are
                  scoped to the Auric theme names.
  What to do:     Run Auric Theme: Switch Variant… and pick an Auric theme.

  Symptom:        A message says "customisations apply to the Auric Artisan
                  themes. Pick one first."
  Why it happens: Same as above.
  What to do:     Select Choose theme in the message.

  Symptom:        The status bar item is missing.
  Why it happens: It only shows while an Auric theme is active.
  What to do:     Switch to an Auric theme.

  Symptom:        My colour override is not exactly the colour I typed.
  Why it happens: The contrast guard adjusted it to meet your minimum
                  contrast.
  What to do:     Lower Minimum contrast or turn off Enforce contrast in the
                  Accessibility dials. Check the Audit tab afterwards.

  Symptom:        Overrides disappeared after switching theme.
  Why it happens: Colour overrides are stored per variant.
  What to do:     Switch back to the variant where you made them, or save a
                  preset and load it on the new variant.

  Symptom:        A warning keeps saying colour pairs no longer meet the
                  minimum.
  Why it happens: Audit on apply reports failures after each change.
  What to do:     Fix the failing pairs (see the Audit tab), or turn off Audit
                  on apply.

  Symptom:        "Auric Theme: could not save auricTheme.…"
  Why it happens: VS Code could not write your user settings, often because
                  `settings.json` has a syntax error or is read-only.
  What to do:     Open Preferences: Open User Settings (JSON), fix any error,
                  then try again.

  Symptom:        Import fails with "That is not valid JSON".
  Why it happens: The pasted text is incomplete or not JSON.
  What to do:     Copy the whole block again, including the braces.

  Symptom:        Import says the customisation "came from" another tool.
  Why it happens: The JSON was not produced by Auric Artisan Theme.
  What to do:     Only import files exported by this extension.

  Symptom:        Import says a newer version is needed.
  Why it happens: The JSON uses a newer format.
  What to do:     Update the extension.

  Symptom:        The sidebar's Export button seems to do nothing.
  Why it happens: It prepares customisation JSON for the Studio's Share tab;
                  it does not open a save dialog.
  What to do:     Use Export as Theme File… from the sidebar's … menu, or Copy
                  Customisation to Clipboard.

  Symptom:        Italics look slanted and blurry.
  Why it happens: Your editor font has no true italic, so it is synthesised.
  What to do:     Set Italics to Off, or run Auric Theme: Toggle Italics.

  Symptom:        Whitespace marks setting has no visible effect.
  Why it happens: Marks only show when VS Code draws whitespace.
  What to do:     Turn on `editor.renderWhitespace`.

  Symptom:        Title bar dial has no visible effect.
  Why it happens: The title bar colour only applies with VS Code's custom
                  title bar style.
  What to do:     Set `window.titleBarStyle` to `custom`.

  Symptom:        A preset I saved in an older version is missing.
  Why it happens: In versions before 0.2.0, presets with distinct Hindi or
                  punctuation-only names could overwrite each other.
  What to do:     This was fixed in 0.2.0, but presets already overwritten
                  cannot be recovered.

  Symptom:        Recent colours in the picker are gone.
  Why it happens: Recent colours last only for the current panel session.
  What to do:     Note important colours as presets or overrides.

  Symptom:        The Preview tab does not look like my editor.
  Why it happens: Preview is an illustrative specimen of the palette, not a
                  full editor.
  What to do:     Look at a real file to judge the result; edits are already
                  live.

  Symptom:        The Studio shows a banner listing problems.
  Why it happens: The theme engine reported something it could not apply.
  What to do:     Read the listed problem; resetting the affected dial or slot
                  usually clears it.

  Symptom:        Hindi labels do not appear in the Command Palette.
  Why it happens: Command titles follow VS Code's display language, not the
                  Studio switch.
  What to do:     Run Configure Display Language and choose Hindi, or keep the
                  Studio switch for the panels only.

START AGAIN FROM THE SHIPPED THEME
==================================

  1. Run Auric Theme: Reset Everything and confirm with Reset, or
  2. In Theme Studio, select Reset all, then Reset everything? within four
     seconds.

This clears every dial and the active variant's colour overrides. Presets are
not deleted.

STILL STUCK
===========

Collect your VS Code version, the extension version (0.2.0), the active theme
and the steps that reproduce the problem, then contact support at
https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/.
