FULL PACK PRIVACY

What the Full Pack and its members do with your code and data.
Area: VS Code extensions › Auric Artisan Studio › Full Pack   Updated: 2026-09-25

The pack contains no code, so it does nothing with your data by itself. VS
Code downloads it and its members from the Visual Studio Marketplace.

Its three members analyse your code entirely on your machine: no network
requests, no telemetry, no account. They write their results to a
`.auric-artisan` folder in your workspace and keep a few preferences in VS
Code's settings and storage.

For the details, see the Studio privacy page [../../others/privacy.txt].
