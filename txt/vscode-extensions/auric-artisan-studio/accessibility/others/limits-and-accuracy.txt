==============================================================================
AURIC ACCESSIBILITY LIMITS AND ACCURACY
==============================================================================
What Auric Accessibility's rules can judge, how their accuracy was measured,
and what needs a person.
Area: VS Code extensions › Auric Artisan Studio › Auric Accessibility   Updated: 2026-09-25

WHAT IT DOES WELL
=================

  - Reads markup with one shared tag reader that understands quoted values and
    JSX braces, so arrow functions and template expressions never cut a tag
    short, and matches attribute names whole.
  - Tells components (`<Input>`, `<Form.Label>`, `<mat-form-field>`) apart
    from native elements, and treats a file without `<html>` or `<body>` as a
    component or partial rather than a whole page.
  - Ignores comments, script and style contents, PHP blocks and strings that
    only look like markup, while keeping reported lines and columns exact.
  - Treats values supplied at runtime (`{{id}}`, `${id}`, `:attr`, `[attr.x]`,
    `attr={…}`) as unknown, not wrong.
  - Checks ARIA against the WAI-ARIA 1.2 role matrix, HTML-AAM implicit roles
    and the HTML autofill grammar.

MEASURED ACCURACY
=================

During development, the rules were checked against axe-core on rendered HTML.
On a regression suite of 264 cases, false positives fell from 132 to 0 and
false negatives from 21 to 0. Of 83 rendered cases, 80 agree with axe; the
three that differ are contested or best-practice cases.

WHAT IT CANNOT JUDGE
====================

  - Anything decided at runtime: markup generated by JavaScript, class-driven
    semantics, component state, computed styles and the full CSS cascade.
  - Relationships across files, such as a label defined in another component.
  - Quality and meaning: whether alt text is accurate, a link's purpose is
    clear, a heading describes its section, or an authentication flow is
    usable. These rules suggest a fix to review, never an automatic repair.
  - Most of WCAG's operable and understandable requirements in practice, such
    as focus order, keyboard traps and status announcements. See Manual
    evidence [../docs/manual-evidence.txt].

WHAT THE SCORE IS
=================

A prioritisation and progress measure over what static analysis can see. It is
not a certification, and a clean result does not mean the product meets WCAG.

LIMITS
======

  - Files larger than 1 MB are not checked.
  - The Inspect card sees only the current file's styles.
  - Suppressed findings are removed from results and scores; branding marks
    never hide structural findings.

For contrast and scanning limits, see the Studio limits and accuracy page
[../../others/limits-and-accuracy.txt].
