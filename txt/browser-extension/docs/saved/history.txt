==============================================================================
HISTORY
==============================================================================
The extension's colour history — your last 50 colours, how to copy or reopen
them, clear them, or turn history off.
Area: Browser extension › Saved   Updated: 2026-09-25

History keeps the last 50 colours you picked or chose in the Picker, newest
first, so a colour is never lost to a stray click.

WHAT GOES INTO HISTORY
======================

  - colours you pick with the screen eyedropper;
  - colours you finish choosing in the Picker;
  - colours you open in the Picker from elsewhere, such as Recent, Page
    palette or the colour of the day.

The same colour is kept once, whatever its letter case. The newest 50 are
kept.

USE IT
======

Open Saved › History ("Your last 50 colours.").

  - Click a colour to copy it in the format set in Settings › Tools › Copy
    colours as.
  - Double-click a colour to open it in the Picker.

Your most recent colours also appear on Home under Recent, and in the Recent
strip of the Picker, Contrast, Harmony, Scale, Gradient and Mix.

CLEAR IT
========

  - Clear empties the history at once. It does not ask first.
  - Settings › Data › Clear history & library clears history and the library
    together, after you confirm.

TURN HISTORY OFF
================

Turn off Settings › Tools › Keep a colour history. New colours are then not
recorded. Colours already in history stay until you clear them.
