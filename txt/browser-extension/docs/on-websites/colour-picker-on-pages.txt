==============================================================================
COLOUR PICKER ON PAGES
==============================================================================
The compact Auric colour picker that replaces a website's colour inputs — its
layout, formats, the five panes, opacity and theme.
Area: Browser extension › On websites   Updated: 2026-09-25

With the website pickers on and the Colour type switched on, every colour
input on a page opens the Auric colour picker, the same picker
auricartisan.com uses, in a compact 296 px popover. The page keeps a small
swatch in place of its colour input; everything else lives in an isolated
panel that the site's styles cannot affect.

LAYOUT
======

From top to bottom:

  1. The spectrum and the Hue slider, plus an Opacity slider when the page's
     input asks for one.
  2. The value field, with a Colour format menu. The menu lists the twelve
     formats (HEX, RGB, HSL, HSV, HWB, CMYK, LAB, LCH, OKLAB, OKLCH, XYZ and
     P3) and searches as you type: type "ok" for OKLAB and OKLCH.
  3. Five toggles: Shades, Harmony, Contrast, Vision and Page (or Recent).

THE FIVE PANES
==============

At most one pane is open at a time.

  - Shades: An 11-step OKLCH scale from 50 to 950 around the colour
  - Harmony: Partner colours, rotated in HSL or OKLCH
  - Contrast: How readable the colour is as text on white, black or the page's
    own background, with the closest passing colour when it fails AA
  - Vision: The colour as people with the four main colour-vision deficiencies
    see it
  - Page / Recent: Colours the page uses, or colours you picked recently in
    the extension

The pane you last opened comes back on every colour picker on that page, until
you leave the page.

BEHAVIOUR
=========

  - Near the bottom of the window the picker opens upward, and it stays
    anchored when a pane opens.
  - `Esc` closes it.
  - The page receives the new value through its normal events, as if you had
    used the browser's picker.
  - The picker never writes to the website's storage. Recent colours are kept
    by the extension, not by the site.

THEME
=====

On Dark or Light the picker uses that theme. On System it matches the page
behind it. Your accent colour tints it.
