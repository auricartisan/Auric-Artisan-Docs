BACK UP AND MOVE YOUR DATA
==============================================================================
Back up your settings, colour history, library and snippets to a file, restore
them, and move them to another browser or computer.
Area: Browser extension › Workflows   Updated: 2026-09-25

The extension keeps everything in your browser and does not sync it anywhere.
To keep your work safe, or to move it to another browser or computer, use a
backup file.

BACK UP
=======

  1. Open Settings › Data.
  2. Check the counts: History, Colors, Palettes and Snippets.
  3. Choose Download a backup. A file named `auric-artisan-backup-` followed
     by today's date is saved to your downloads.
  4. Keep the file somewhere safe. It contains your snippets and the addresses
     of the pages they came from.

RESTORE ON ANOTHER BROWSER OR COMPUTER
======================================

  1. Install the extension there; see Install [../install/README.txt].
  2. Copy the backup file to that computer.
  3. Open Settings › Data › Restore from a file and choose the file.
  4. The message "Backup restored" confirms it. Your theme, accent and other
     settings apply at once, and History, Library and Snippets fill in.

Restoring replaces what is stored now with what is in the file. Back up the
new browser first if it already has data you want.

MOVE ONLY YOUR SETTINGS
=======================

  1. On the first browser, choose Settings › Data › Copy settings.
  2. Send the copied text to the other browser, for example through a note.
  3. On the other browser, choose Paste settings and paste it.

This moves your theme, accent, switches and site lists, but not your colours
or snippets.

BEFORE YOU UNINSTALL
====================

Uninstalling deletes everything the extension stored. Download a backup first
if you might come back.

The result: your settings, colours and snippets, safe in a file you control.
