==============================================================================
CHECK AND FIX A BRAND COLOUR
==============================================================================
Test a brand colour as text and as a background in WCAG and APCA, see it
through colour-vision simulations, and find accessible variants that keep its
hue.
Area: Browser extension › Workflows   Updated: 2026-09-25

Brand colours are often too light for text on white, or too dark for white
text on top. This workflow tells you where a brand colour works and gives you
accessible variants that still look like the brand.

1. GET THE COLOUR
=================

Do one of these:

  - On the brand's site, choose Pick a colour on Home and click the colour
    (Chromium browsers).
  - Choose Colour › Page palette, Read the page's colours, and choose the
    colour.
  - Type its value into the Picker's value field.

2. READ ITS SPECS
=================

  1. In the Picker, open the Specs pane. Note its contrast on white and on
     black and its WCAG levels.
  2. Open the Contrast pane. Switch between White, Black and Page backgrounds.
     If it fails AA, the closest passing colour is offered.

3. TEST IT AS TEXT
==================

  1. Choose Check as text. Contrast opens with the brand colour as text.
  2. Set Background to the background you use, for example `#ffffff`.
  3. Read the verdict and the APCA readable sizes. A colour may pass for large
     headings (AA Large) but not for body text.
  4. Choose Fix text with the target you need. Note the new colour.

4. TEST IT AS A BACKGROUND
==========================

  1. Go back to the Picker and choose Check as background.
  2. Set Text to the text colour you plan to use on it, often white or
     near-black.
  3. Read the verdict. Choose Swap to test the reverse.
  4. Choose Fix background if it fails.

5. CHECK COLOUR VISION
======================

  1. In Contrast, read the Colour-vision check: the ratio for protan, deutan
     and tritan vision.
  2. In the Picker's Vision pane, see how the colour itself changes. A ΔE
     under about 2 means the colour barely changes.

6. BUILD ACCESSIBLE VARIANTS
============================

  1. From the Picker's Scale pane, choose Open the scale generator →.
  2. Keep Tailwind 50–950. The step nearest your brand colour is marked.
  3. Choose darker or lighter steps and use Contrast on each to find the ones
     that pass on your backgrounds.

The result: you know which uses of the brand colour pass, and you have tested
variants in the same hue for the ones that do not.
