==============================================================================
CONVERT
==============================================================================
Type a colour in any of ten formats and read it in all the others at once,
with random, invert and complement helpers.
Area: Browser extension › Colour   Updated: 2026-09-25

Convert (the Colour converter, "every format at once") shows one colour in ten
formats. Type into any field and the others follow. Open it from the Picker's
Specs pane (Open the converter →) or search for "Convert".

FIELDS
======

  Field | Example input
  ------+---------------------
  HEX   | `#d3af37`
  RGB   | `rgb(211,175,55)`
  HSL   | `hsl(43,65%,52%)`
  HSV   | `hsv(43,74%,83%)`
  CMYK  | `cmyk(0,17,74,17)`
  LAB   | `lab(72 5 60)`
  LCH   | `lch(72 60 85)`
  OKLCH | `oklch(76% 0.13 85)`
  HWB   | `hwb(43 22% 17%)`
  Name  | `goldenrod`

Commas or spaces both work as separators. A swatch shows the current colour.

HELPERS
=======

  Button     | What it does
  -----------+-------------------------------------
  Random     | A random colour
  Invert     | The colour's inverse
  Complement | The colour opposite on the hue wheel

Picker at the top returns to the Picker.

  | Note: CMYK here is a simple conversion from RGB, not a colour-managed print
  | profile. Use it as a starting point for print, not a proof.
