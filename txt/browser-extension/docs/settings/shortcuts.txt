==============================================================================
SHORTCUTS SETTINGS
==============================================================================
The keyboard map in Settings, how to change the browser-level shortcuts, and
how to replay the tour.
Area: Browser extension › Settings   Updated: 2026-09-25

Settings › Shortcuts lists the keys the extension answers to and links to
where you change them.

THE KEYBOARD MAP
================

  Action                                 | Keys
  ---------------------------------------+----------------------
  Open Auric Artisan                     | `Alt` + `Shift` + `A`
  Open the contrast checker              | `Alt` + `Shift` + `C`
  Read the page palette                  | `Alt` + `Shift` + `E`
  Toggle vision simulation               | `Alt` + `Shift` + `V`
  Search in the popup                    | `/` or `Ctrl` + `K`
  Inspector: pin · CSS · selector · JSON | `P`, `C`, `S`, `J`
  Close a picker, panel or menu          | `Esc`

The first four are browser-level shortcuts and work from any page. Inspect an
element on the page and Run a full accessibility audit are also available but
have no key until you assign one.

BUTTONS
=======

  - Change them in the browser: Opens your browser's extension shortcuts page:
    `chrome://extensions/shortcuts` in Chromium browsers, `about:addons` in
    Firefox (then the gear menu › Manage Extension Shortcuts)
  - Replay the tour: Closes Settings and starts the five-step tour

The complete list, including the inspector's and the right-click menu's keys,
is in Keyboard shortcuts [../../others/shortcuts.txt].
