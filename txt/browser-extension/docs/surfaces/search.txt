==============================================================================
SEARCH
==============================================================================
Find any tool, setting, action or auricartisan.com page by name from the
search field, and how results are ordered.
Area: Browser extension › Surfaces   Updated: 2026-09-25

The search field in the header finds every tool, many settings, a few actions
and pages on auricartisan.com. It is the quickest way to reach a tool whose
space you do not remember.

USE IT
======

  1. Press `/` or `Ctrl` + `K`, or click the field.
  2. Type. Every word you type must match; words can match the name, the
     description or hidden keywords (for example "right click", "backup" or
     "colour blind").
  3. Use `Up` and `Down` to move through the results, or hover them.
  4. Press `Enter`, or click a result, to run it.
  5. Press `Esc` to clear the search.

A Hindi interface can be searched in Hindi or English. The Language setting
can also be found with "भाषा".

HOW RESULTS ARE ORDERED
=======================

Results are grouped under headings:

  Group:         Best match
  What it holds: A result whose title is exactly what you typed, so typing
                 "Settings" and pressing `Enter` opens Settings

  Group:         Tools
  What it holds: The extension's tools; each result shows its space on the
                 right

  Group:         Settings
  What it holds: Individual settings; choosing one opens Settings at that
                 section and focuses the control

  Group:         Actions
  What it holds: Settings and Open in a Window

  Group:         On the web
  What it holds: Pages on auricartisan.com, opened in a new tab

Within a group, titles that start with your text come first. If nothing
matches, the list says so.
