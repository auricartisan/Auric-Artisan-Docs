Background material for the browser extension — glossary, shortcuts, limits
and accuracy, related tools, release notes and privacy.
Area: Browser extension   Updated: 2026-09-25

This folder holds the reference material that sits around the how-to pages:
the terms the extension uses, every keyboard shortcut, what its measurements
can and cannot tell you, where to go next, what changed in each version, and
what happens to your data.
