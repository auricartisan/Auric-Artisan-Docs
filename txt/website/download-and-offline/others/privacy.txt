==============================================================================
DOWNLOAD AND OFFLINE PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What the installed app and offline copy store on your device, and what the
Download page reads.
Area: Website › Download and offline   Updated: 2026-09-25

STORED ON YOUR DEVICE
=====================

  - The offline copy of the site's files, in your browser's storage for
    auricartisan.com.
  - Your profile choice, the low-power switch, and whether you have asked for
    an offline copy (so an interrupted or out-of-date copy can be completed
    later).
  - Files you open with the installed app, or text and links you share to it,
    are held on your device for the Workspace to pick up.

WHAT THE DOWNLOAD PAGE READS
============================

The page reads your device's processor thread count, approximate memory,
graphics processor name, connection type and storage estimate, as your browser
reports them. It uses them to show the metrics and to choose the Auto profile.
It does not send them to Auric Artisan.

WHAT USES THE NETWORK
=====================

  - Downloading the offline copy and updates, from auricartisan.com.
  - Checking for updates.

REMOVING IT
===========

Select Reset cache on the Download page, uninstall the app, or clear the
site's data in your browser (see Uninstall and remove data
[../docs/uninstall.txt]).
