==============================================================================
DOWNLOAD AND OFFLINE GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Terms used in the download and offline guides.
Area: Website › Download and offline   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    PWA
  Meaning: Progressive web app: a website a browser can install like an app
           and run offline.

  Term:    Install
  Meaning: Adding the site to your device as an app with its own window and
           icon.

  Term:    Standalone
  Meaning: Running in its own window without the browser's toolbar.

  Term:    Service worker
  Meaning: A part of the site that runs in your browser and serves stored
           files when you are offline.

  Term:    Cache
  Meaning: The browser's store of site files kept for offline use.

  Term:    App shell
  Meaning: The small core of pages, styles and scripts stored automatically on
           your first visit.

  Term:    Offline profile
  Meaning: How much of the site to store: Auto, Lite, Balanced or Full.

  Term:    Persistent storage
  Meaning: Browser storage that is not cleared automatically when space runs
           low.

  Term:    Best-effort storage
  Meaning: Browser storage that the browser may clear when space runs low.

  Term:    Quota
  Meaning: The amount of storage the browser allows the site to use.

  Term:    Audit
  Meaning: A check of which files of your profile are stored and which are
           missing.

  Term:    Background sync
  Meaning: A browser feature that finishes a task, such as a download, when
           you are next online.

  Term:    Share target
  Meaning: An app that appears in your device's share menu.
