==============================================================================
THE WEBSITE
==============================================================================
A map of everything on auricartisan.com — the site-wide features, the tools,
the kits, the library, the portfolio builder, search, your account and the
company pages.
Area: Website   Updated: 2026-09-25

https://auricartisan.com is the home of Auric Artisan. Its tools, libraries
and learning material work in any modern browser, most of them without an
account. You can install the site as an app and prepare it to work offline,
and a set of site-wide features — a settings panel, a right-click menu,
search, themes, English and Hindi, and comfort options such as eye-rest
reminders — follow you from page to page.

WHERE TO START
==============

  1. Open https://auricartisan.com and choose Tools in the header, or read the
     tools catalogue [tools/README.txt] to pick one by task.
  2. Most tools work straight away with no account. Your settings and saved
     items are kept in your browser.
  3. Open the settings panel to choose your theme, language and comfort
     options — see Site features [site-features/README.txt].
  4. Create an account when you want to sync, publish a portfolio or use the
     API — see Account and billing [account-and-billing/README.txt].
