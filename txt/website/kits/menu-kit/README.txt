==============================================================================
MENU KIT
==============================================================================
A floating start menu for auricartisan.com with tabbed page groups,
recommended and most-used tools, fuzzy search and quick actions.
Area: Website › Kits › Menu Kit   Updated: 2026-09-25

The Menu Kit is a start menu for the whole of auricartisan.com. It opens over
any page and lists the site's tools and pages as illustrated tiles, grouped
into seven tabs: Tools, Design, Analyze, Account, Library, Company and Trust.
Selecting a tile takes you to that page.

Type in its search box and the tiles give way to a ranked list of matching
pages and quick actions, such as Copy current URL or Open a random tool. The
search forgives typos in the middle of words: typing `clrlib` finds Color
Library. Pages you open often rise in the results and fill the Most Used
section.

The Menu Kit follows the site's tool visibility mode, set in the Advanced Kit
[../advanced-kit/README.txt]: in Basic mode it shows beginner-friendly tools,
in Advanced mode the scientific ones, and in Everything mode the lot.

WHO IT IS FOR
=============

  - New visitors who want an overview of everything the site offers.
  - Regular users who want to jump to a tool in a couple of keystrokes from
    any page.
  - Anyone looking for account, library, company or legal pages.

WHERE TO FIND IT
================

  - On any page of https://auricartisan.com, select Page utilities in the
    bottom-left corner, then Menu.
  - Or press `Ctrl` + `M`.

QUICK START
===========

  1. Press `Ctrl` + `M`. The Menu Kit opens with the cursor in Search Auric
     Artisan….
  2. Look at the Tools tab: it starts with Recommended and Most Used sections.
  3. Select another tab, for example Library, to see its pages.
  4. Select the search box and type `icon`. The tiles are replaced by a list
     of results.
  5. Press `Down arrow` to highlight a result and `Enter` to open it.
  6. On the new page, press `Ctrl` + `M` again. The Menu Kit remembers the tab
     you used last.
  7. Press `Esc` to close it.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
===============

  - Browse the site in seven tabs, with collapsible sections and a count of
    pages in each.
  - See at a glance which tile is the page you are on.
  - Start from eight recommended entry points and your own most-used pages.
  - Search pages by name, section or description, with forgiving matching and
    a result count.
  - Run quick actions: copy the page URL, scroll to top, go home, go back,
    reload, open a random tool, or switch the menu's theme.
  - Navigate entirely with the keyboard.
  - See the site's status (for example Beta) in the title bar.
