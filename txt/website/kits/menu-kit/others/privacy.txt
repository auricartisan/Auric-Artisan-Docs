What the Menu Kit keeps in your browser and what it downloads.

KEPT IN YOUR BROWSER
====================

  What: The last tab you used
  Why:  To open on it next time

  What: Which sections you collapsed
  Why:  To keep them collapsed

  What: How many times you opened each page from the Menu Kit
  Why:  To fill Most Used and rank search results

  What: Position, expanded size, Focus mode and theme
  Why:  Shared kit preferences

None of this is sent anywhere. It is removed when you clear this site's data
in your browser.

DOWNLOADED
==========

The first time you open the Menu Kit on a page, it downloads the list of pages
and the site status from auricartisan.com. Your searches are not sent:
matching happens in your browser.

The Copy current URL quick action writes the page address to your clipboard,
which your browser may ask permission for.
