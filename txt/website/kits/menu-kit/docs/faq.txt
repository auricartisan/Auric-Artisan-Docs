==============================================================================
MENU KIT FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers to common questions about the Menu Kit.
Area: Website › Kits › Menu Kit   Updated: 2026-09-25

HOW IS THE MENU KIT DIFFERENT FROM THE SITE HEADER MENU?
========================================================

The Menu Kit opens on any page, including tool pages where the header is
scrolled away, and holds the whole site in one searchable panel with tabs,
recommended pages and your most-used pages.

HOW IS IT DIFFERENT FROM THE SITE-WIDE SEARCH?
==============================================

The Menu Kit searches its own list of pages by name, section and description,
and also offers quick actions. The site-wide search (`Ctrl` + `K`, or
https://auricartisan.com/search/) searches the content of pages and can search
the web. See Search [../../../search/README.txt].

DOES THE MENU KIT LIST EVERY PAGE ON THE SITE?
==============================================

No. It lists the main tools and pages. Individual articles, library entries
and some tool sub-pages are not in it; use the site-wide search for those.

WHY DO I SEE FEWER TOOLS THAN A FRIEND?
=======================================

The Menu Kit follows the tool visibility mode chosen in the Advanced Kit
[../../advanced-kit/README.txt], which is stored in each browser. New visitors
start in Basic mode.

CAN I REORDER OR PIN TILES?
===========================

No. The order is fixed, apart from Most Used, which follows how often you open
pages from the Menu Kit.

CAN I RESET MOST USED?
======================

There is no reset button. Clearing this site's data in your browser resets it,
along with your other kit preferences.

DOES SEARCH UNDERSTAND TYPOS?
=============================

Partly. It matches letters in order with gaps, so `clrlib` finds Color
Library. It does not correct swapped or wrong letters.

IS ANYTHING I TYPE SENT ANYWHERE?
=================================

No. Searching and quick actions run in your browser. See Privacy
[../others/privacy.txt].
