Where the Library Kit keeps your saved work, and the only ways it can leave

WHERE YOUR LIBRARY IS KEPT
==========================

Every saved item, collection, draft and library setting is stored in your
browser's storage for auricartisan.com, on this device. It is not uploaded to
Auric Artisan and not linked to your account. Anyone who uses the same browser
profile can see it.

The Library Kit's own view preferences (tab, grouping, density, collapsed
groups, panel position, size, theme and Keep open) are stored the same way.

THE ONLY WAYS YOUR ITEMS LEAVE THE DEVICE
=========================================

  Action:                  Share a link…
  What leaves, and where it goes:A copy of the item, packed into the link. It
                           goes wherever you send the link. Anyone with the
                           link can open the copy

  Action:                  Export, Export JSON, downloads, reports
  What leaves, and where it goes:Files saved to your device by your browser

  Action:                  Sync
  What leaves, and where it goes:Your library (with or without full data,
                           drafts and previews, as you choose) sent from your
                           browser to the URLs you configure, with your bearer
                           token if you set one

Nothing is sent to Auric Artisan's servers by the Library Kit.

SENSITIVE SETTINGS
==================

Sync destinations, including any Auth bearer token, are stored in your browser
as part of the library settings. They are included in a full library backup
(`auric-library-backup.json`) and in full reports. Treat those files as
private if you use tokens, or remove destinations before exporting.

REMOVING YOUR DATA
==================

  - Delete items individually, empty the archive, or use Settings > Storage >
    Reset library to delete everything.
  - Clearing this site's data in your browser also deletes the library.
  - Deleted data cannot be recovered unless you exported it.
