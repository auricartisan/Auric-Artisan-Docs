==============================================================================
LIBRARY KIT FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers to common questions about the Library Kit and your saved work.
Area: Website › Kits › Library Kit   Updated: 2026-09-25

IS MY LIBRARY SAVED TO MY ACCOUNT?
==================================

No. It is stored in your browser on this device. Export it (Export in the
footer) to keep a backup or move it to another browser.

HOW MANY ITEMS CAN I KEEP?
==========================

Your plan sets the number of active saved items: 20 on Apprentice, 500 on
Artisan, 2,500 on Specialist and 10,000 on Industrial Pro. Archived items do
not count. Your browser's storage limit applies on top of that, especially for
images.

ARE THE TOOLS STILL FREE IF I REACH THE LIMIT?
==============================================

Yes. The limit only affects saving new items. See Account and billing
[../../../account-and-billing/README.txt].

WHAT HAPPENED TO THE LIBRARY WORKSPACE PAGE?
============================================

The library now opens as a floating panel on any page.
https://auricartisan.com/library/workspace/ still works and opens the panel.

CAN OTHER PEOPLE SEE MY LIBRARY?
================================

No. Only items you share as links, export as files or push to your own sync
destinations leave your browser.

WHAT DOES A SHARE LINK CONTAIN?
===============================

The item's data, packed into the address. Anyone with the link can open a
copy. Nothing is stored on a server.

WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN ARCHIVE AND DELETE?
==================================================

Archiving hides an item and keeps it on the Archive tab, where you can restore
it. Deleting removes it for good.

WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN A COLLECTION AND A PROJECT?
==========================================================

A collection is a folder that groups items; each item can be in one. A project
is a type of saved item; the Library Kit can create empty ones, and some tools
save their own. Use collections to group your items.

CAN I UNDO A DELETE?
====================

No. Deletes, Empty the archive and Reset library cannot be undone.

DOES THE LIBRARY WORK OFFLINE?
==============================

Browsing, searching, organising, comparing and exporting work offline once the
kit has loaded, because everything is in your browser. Sync needs a
connection, and opening an item in a tool needs that tool's page to load.

WHICH TOOLS CAN SAVE TO THE LIBRARY?
====================================

Tools with a Save to Library button. Saved items record which tool made them,
and many can also be opened in other tools that accept the same type of item.
