==============================================================================
COMPARE TWO ITEMS
==============================================================================
Compare two saved items in the Library Kit: palettes by colour difference and
contrast, images side by side or as a heatmap, settings field by field, and
audits by score.
Area: Website › Kits › Library Kit   Updated: 2026-09-25

The Compare tab puts two saved items side by side and shows exactly what
changed between them. What you see depends on the kind of items you compare.

OPEN A COMPARISON
=================

Any of these opens the Compare tab:

  - Select the Compare tab.
  - Right-click a tile and choose Compare with…. That item becomes Before.
  - In multi-select, select two items, then type `>` and choose Compare items.
    The first becomes Before and the second After.
  - Right-click empty space in the panel and choose Compare items….

The workspace is headed Comparison workspace — Compare saved assets, with the
line "Choose a before and after asset to inspect exactly what changed."

CHOOSE THE TWO ITEMS
====================

  1. In Before, choose an item from the list (— Choose an asset — means none).
     Each entry shows a short type label in brackets and the name, newest
     first. Archived items are included.
  2. In After, choose the second item.
  3. Select Swap sides to exchange them.

Under the pickers, each side shows its type, tool, name, description and
Updated date. Until both are chosen, the comparison area reads Pick assets on
both sides to see the diff.

PALETTES
========

When both items are palettes, or both carry colours, you see:

Summary figures

  Figure     | Meaning
  -----------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Swatches   | Number of colours before → after, and the change
  Avg ΔE2000 | Average colour difference between matched colours, with a verbal rating
  Max ΔE     | The largest difference between matched colours
  Shifted    | How many matched colours differ by 2 or more, and how many by 10 or more (distinct)

ΔE2000 is a standard measure of how different two colours look; about 1 is the
smallest difference most people notice. The verbal ratings are:

  ΔE2000         | Rating
  ---------------+--------------
  Under 1        | Imperceptible
  1 to under 2   | Very close
  2 to under 5   | Close
  5 to under 10  | Noticeable
  10 to under 20 | Distinct
  20 or more     | Far

Colour-by-colour table. Each row pairs a Left colour with its closest Right
colour and shows the ΔE2000 and the Shift: how the lightness and hue moved.

Unmatched colours. Colours present on only one side are listed as Removed
(only in left) and Added (only in right).

Weakest contrast pair. For each palette, the two colours with the lowest
contrast ratio between them, shown as swatches and a ratio such as `1.42:1`,
labelled Left palette and Right palette, plus the change (Δ worst-pair ratio).
A higher ratio means the weakest pair is easier to tell apart.

A gradient compared with a palette or another coloured item is treated the
same way. If neither side has colours, you see Neither asset has any colors to
compare.

IMAGES AND PLATES
=================

When both items are images or plates, choose a view mode:

  Mode:          Side
  What it shows: Before and After next to each other

  Mode:          Wipe
  What it shows: The two images stacked, with a slider that reveals more of
                 one or the other

  Mode:          ΔE heatmap
  What it shows: A map of where the images differ: differences under 2 show as
                 a faint blue, 2 to 10 as yellow, and over 10 as pink ("Pink =
                 where colors differ most (ΔE > 10)."). A line reports the
                 number of pixels compared and the average and maximum ΔE

The heatmap works on copies scaled down to at most 800 pixels on each side. If
the images are different sizes, the heatmap compares the overlapping top-left
area and says so. If one side has no image, it shows No image; if neither
does, you see Neither asset has image data. An image that cannot be loaded is
reported as "Couldn't load the left image" or "right image".

PRESETS AND CONFIGURATIONS
==========================

When both items are presets or configurations, the comparison lists
differences in two sections, Settings and Data fields, each with a count of
changed, added, removed and identical fields. Changed fields show before →
after; added fields are marked with + and removed ones with −. If there are
none, the section says No differences.

If either item came from the URL Analyzer, a strip at the top shows the URL of
each, the Score before → after and the Issues count before → after, coloured
by whether it improved.

URL ANALYZER AUDITS
===================

When both items are saved analyses, you see each audit's score in a colour for
its band (90 and above, 70 to 89, 50 to 69, below 50), its address, how many
versions and issues it holds, and a sentence such as "the second scores 6
higher". A What each holds table compares each section by name, marking
sections one side lacks as not stored, and Colours on each page shows up to 12
colours from each.

DIFFERENT KINDS OF ITEM
=======================

When the two items are different types and do not both carry colours, the
comparison explains that only shared signals are compared and shows Extracted
colors (up to 12 from each) and their Tags.
