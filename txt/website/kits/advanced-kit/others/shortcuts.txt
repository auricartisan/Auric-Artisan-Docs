==============================================================================
ADVANCED KIT SHORTCUTS
==============================================================================
Keyboard shortcuts for the Advanced Kit.
Area: Website › Kits › Advanced Kit   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Shortcut:     `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `A`
  Where:        Anywhere
  What it does: Opens or closes the Advanced Kit

  Shortcut:     `Esc`
  Where:        Kit open
  What it does: Closes the kit

  Shortcut:     `Tab`, `Shift` + `Tab`
  Where:        Kit open
  What it does: Move between the mode cards, buttons, filter box and
                checkboxes

  Shortcut:     `Enter` or `Space`
  Where:        A mode card
  What it does: Selects that mode

  Shortcut:     `Space`
  Where:        A tool checkbox
  What it does: Ticks or unticks the tool

When the kit opens, keyboard focus goes to the card of the current mode.

See Kit keyboard shortcuts [../../others/shortcuts.txt] for the other kits.
