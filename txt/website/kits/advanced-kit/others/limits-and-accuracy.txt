==============================================================================
ADVANCED KIT LIMITS
==============================================================================
What the tool visibility modes affect, what they do not, and how tools are
classified.
Area: Website › Kits › Advanced Kit   Updated: 2026-09-25

WHAT THE MODES AFFECT
=====================

  - The Menu Kit's tiles and search results.
  - Tool collection grids, such as https://auricartisan.com/collections/.
  - The Feature Kit calculator's keypad tabs.
  - Some wording on the home page and on the URL Analyzer, Vision Simulation,
    Colour Science Lab, Basic Colour Tools and Ishihara Plate Generation
    pages.

WHAT THEY DO NOT AFFECT
=======================

  - Whether a tool page opens: nothing is locked.
  - Browser tab titles and search-engine descriptions.
  - The site-wide search.
  - Your saved library items, settings and account.

HOW TOOLS ARE CLASSIFIED
========================

  - Tools listed in the Menu Kit carry a level of basic, advanced or both, set
    by the site. Where no level is set, the site decides from the tool's
    section: tools in the Tools tab count as basic, tools in the Design tab as
    advanced, and account, library, company and legal pages as both.
  - The classification is the site's, not yours. To show or hide a particular
    tool regardless of its level, use Custom mode.

CUSTOM MODE
===========

  - The checklist covers tool pages only (addresses starting with `/tool/`),
    not other pages.
  - Custom choices are stored as a list of tools you have turned off. New
    tools added to the site later start switched on.
  - Page wording follows the Basic set while Custom is active.

STORAGE
=======

  - The mode and your custom choices are stored in this browser only.
  - Some tools can change the mode when you use their own mode switch; see
    Troubleshooting [../docs/troubleshooting.txt].
