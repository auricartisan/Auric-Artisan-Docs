KEPT IN YOUR BROWSER
====================

  What                                          | Why
  ----------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------
  Your tool visibility mode                     | So every page shows the tools you chose
  The tools you unticked in Custom mode         | So your custom set applies site-wide
  Position, expanded size, Focus mode and theme | Shared kit preferences

Nothing is linked to your account or sent anywhere. Clearing this site's data
in your browser resets the mode to Basic and clears your custom choices.

DOWNLOADED
==========

When you first open the Custom checklist on a page, the kit downloads the
site's tool lists from auricartisan.com to build it.
