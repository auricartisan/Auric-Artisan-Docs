THE FEATURE KIT AS OF 24 SEPTEMBER 2026
=======================================

  - Six tools behind one menu: Scientific Calculator, Unit Converter,
    Feedback, Notes, Date & Time and Developer Tools.
  - A calculator with Basic, Scientific and Programmer keypads, an expression
    bar with variables, user-defined functions, statistics, number theory,
    physical constants and numerical calculus (`diff`, `diff2`, `integral`,
    `solve`, `sum`, `product`), exact-fraction hints, a function reference
    panel, a graph and a 100-entry history.
  - A Unit Converter with 70 categories and 1,344 units.
  - Notes with several Markdown notes, preview, copy and `.md` export.
  - Date & Time with Calendar, Difference, Add / Subtract and Timezones tabs.
  - Developer Tools with JSON, Base64, URL, Hash, UUID, JWT, Epoch, Regex,
    Cron, Text and HTML tabs.
  - Quick and detailed feedback that is kept on your device until it has been
    delivered.
  - Shared kit controls: Focus mode, per-kit theme, Resize, Minimize and
    Close, and a labelled Feature launcher in the page utilities bar.

Changes not recorded in the public history are not listed here.
