==============================================================================
GETTING STARTED WITH THE FEATURE KIT
==============================================================================
Open the Feature Kit, tour its menu and title bar, and run your first
calculation.
Area: Website › Kits › Feature Kit   Updated: 2026-09-25

The Feature Kit opens on a menu of six tools. This guide opens the kit,
explains what you see, and runs a first calculation.

OPEN THE KIT
============

Use either:

  - Page utilities (bottom-left corner) > Feature, or
  - `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `K` (`Cmd` + `Shift` + `K` on a Mac) from anywhere on
    the page.

The first time you open it on a page, the launcher dims for a moment while the
kit loads.

THE MENU
========

The kit opens on its menu: six cards, each with an illustration, a title, a
one-line description and a number key.

  Key | Card                  | Description on the card
  ----+-----------------------+-----------------------------------------------
  `1` | Scientific Calculator | Advanced math, graphing, history
  `2` | Unit Converter        | 1000+ conversions across 50+ categories
  `3` | Feedback              | Quiet, optional feedback in Feature Kit
  `4` | Notes                 | Markdown scratchpad with autosave
  `5` | Date & Time           | Date math, durations, time zones
  `6` | Developer Tools       | JSON, JWT, regex, cron, hash, UUID, text tools

Below the cards, a hint line reads 1 – 6 Open · Ctrl ⇧ K Toggle (with ⌘ on a
Mac).

To open a tool, select its card, press its number key, or use the arrow keys
to reach the card and press `Enter`.

THE TITLE BAR
=============

  Button:          Back (left arrow)
  When you see it: Inside any tool
  What it does:    Returns to the menu

  Button:          Title
  When you see it: Always
  What it does:    Reads Feature Kit on the menu and the tool's name inside a
                   tool. Drag it to move the kit.

  Button:          Graph (wave)
  When you see it: Calculator only
  What it does:    Shows or hides the graph

  Button:          History (clock)
  When you see it: Calculator only
  What it does:    Shows or hides past calculations

  Button:          Focus mode (target)
  When you see it: Always
  What it does:    Keeps the kit open across pages and other kits

  Button:          Theme (sun or moon)
  When you see it: Always
  What it does:    Switches the kit between light and dark

  Button:          Resize (four arrows)
  When you see it: Always
  What it does:    Switches between normal and full size

  Button:          Minimize (dash)
  When you see it: Always
  What it does:    Shrinks the kit into a small restore icon

  Button:          Close (cross)
  When you see it: Always
  What it does:    Closes the kit and turns Focus mode off

DIMMED PAGE AND FOCUS MODE
==========================

With Focus mode off, the page behind the Feature Kit is dimmed while the kit
is open, `Tab` moves only between the kit's own controls, and clicking the
dimmed page closes the kit. Select Focus mode to stop dimming the page and
keep the kit open while you work. See Panel controls
[../../docs/panel-controls.txt].

YOUR FIRST CALCULATION
======================

  1. Press `1` to open Scientific Calculator.
  2. Type `12*7` on your keyboard. The top line of the display shows the
     expression and the large line shows a live result, `84`.
  3. Press `Enter`. The result is stored as `ans`.
  4. Select the expression bar under the display, type `ans/8` and press
     `Enter`. The display shows `10.5 = 21/2`: the decimal and the exact
     fraction.
  5. Select the large result. A message reads Copied! and the result is on
     your clipboard.

You have used both ways of entering a calculation: the keypad (or your
keyboard) and the expression bar.

LEAVE THE KIT
=============

  - Press `Esc` once to go back to the menu from a tool, and again to close
    the kit.
  - Or select Close in the title bar.

The kit remembers your calculator history and notes for next time.
