==============================================================================
SEND FEEDBACK
==============================================================================
Send quick or detailed feedback about Auric Artisan from the Feature Kit, and
what happens when sending fails.
Area: Website › Kits › Feature Kit   Updated: 2026-09-25

The Feedback tool in the Feature Kit lets you tell the Auric Artisan team how
the site is working for you. A quick rating takes one click; a detailed form
takes about a minute. Both are optional.

OPEN THE FEEDBACK FORM
======================

Select the Feedback card on the Feature Kit menu, or press `3` on the menu.

You may also see a small prompt inside the Feature Kit that reads Feedback
when you're ready with the line "The full form lives in Feature Kit and stays
out of the way." Select Open form to go to the form, or Later to dismiss it.
The prompt hides by itself after 10 seconds.

SEND QUICK FEEDBACK
===================

  1. Read the question at the top. It depends on what you did last in the kit,
     for example How's your experience so far?, How was the calculator? or How
     was the unit converter?
  2. Choose one of five faces: Terrible, Bad, Okay, Good or Amazing.
  3. A comment box appears: Tell us more (optional).... Type a comment if you
     like.
  4. Select Submit. It reads Sending... while it sends.

A thank-you message appears, matched to your rating: Thank you!, We hear you.
or Thanks for sharing!. Select Done, or wait 5 seconds, to return to the
Feature Kit menu.

Submit stays disabled until you choose a face.

SEND DETAILED FEEDBACK
======================

  1. On the quick feedback screen, optionally choose a face, then select Give
     Detailed Feedback.
  2. Answer each question. A progress bar and a counter such as 2/8 show where
     you are.
  3. Use Next → to continue, Skip to leave a question unanswered, or ← Back to
     return to the previous question.
  4. On the last question, the forward button reads Submit with a tick. Select
     it to send.

The questions, in order:

  Question:       How easy was it to find what you needed?
  How you answer: A scale of 1 to 5, from Very hard to Very easy

  Question:       How would you rate the speed & performance?
  How you answer: A scale of 1 to 5, from Very slow to Lightning

  Question:       How do you feel about the design?
  How you answer: Five faces: Love it, Nice, Okay, Meh, Needs work

  Question:       Follow-up questions
  How you answer: Depend on your quick rating (see below)

  Question:       What features would you like to see?
  How you answer: Pick any of: Dark/Light themes, More tools, Keyboard
                  shortcuts, Mobile improvements, Accessibility,
                  Import/Export, Other

  Question:       Would you recommend this to a colleague?
  How you answer: A scale of 0 to 10, from Not likely to Very likely

  Question:       Anything else you'd like to share?
  How you answer: Free text

Follow-up questions:

  - After a 4 or 5 rating: "What do you like most?" (free text) and "Would you
    share this with others?" (Definitely!, Maybe, Probably not).
  - After a 1 or 2 rating, or no rating: "What frustrated you the most?",
    "What did you expect to happen?" (both free text) and "What should we fix
    first?" (Bugs, Performance, Design, Features, Usability).
  - After a 3 rating: "What one thing would make this better?" (free text).

NOT NOW
=======

Select Not now on the quick feedback screen to leave without sending. The kit
returns to its menu and the prompts give you more room before they appear
again.

IF SENDING FAILS
================

If your feedback cannot be sent, you see Could not send yet with one of these
messages:

  Message:       Your feedback is saved on this device. Check your connection
                 and try again.
  What it means: You are offline or the server did not answer. The feedback is
                 kept and will be sent later

  Message:       The server could not accept this feedback. You can try again
                 or return to the menu.
  What it means: The server refused this submission

  Message:       Your feedback could not be sent or saved on this device. Keep
                 this page open and try again.
  What it means: Your browser would not store it, for example in a private
                 window

  Message:       Your feedback is saved, but the current account could not be
                 confirmed yet. Check your connection and try again.
  What it means: You were signed in, and your sign-in could not be checked

  Message:       This feedback belongs to a different signed-in account.
                 Return to that account before retrying.
  What it means: You switched accounts after writing it

Select Try again (or Check account) to retry, or Done to return to the menu.
Feedback saved on your device is retried automatically, including when your
connection comes back.

WHEN THE KIT ASKS FOR FEEDBACK
==============================

The kit asks quietly and rarely:

  - only while the Feature Kit is open on its menu, never while you are in a
    tool;
  - after you have used the calculator or the converter a few times, or after
    about 15 minutes;
  - no more than twice in one visit, and no more than once an hour;
  - less often if you have dismissed several prompts.

If a prompt comes due while the kit is closed, it waits and appears the next
time you open the menu.

WHAT IS SENT
============

See Privacy [../others/privacy.txt]. In short: your rating, comment and
answers, the page you were on, and which tool you used last. If you are signed
in, the feedback is linked to your account.

For feedback outside the Feature Kit, see Site features
[../../../site-features/README.txt].
