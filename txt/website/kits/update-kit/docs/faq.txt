==============================================================================
UPDATE KIT FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers to common questions about updating auricartisan.com in your
browser.
Area: Website › Kits › Update Kit   Updated: 2026-09-25

WHY DOES A WEBSITE NEED UPDATING?
=================================

Your browser, and the site's offline support, keep copies of the site's files
so pages load fast and work offline. After a release, those copies can be
older than the site. Updating replaces them.

WILL UPDATING DELETE MY SAVED WORK?
===================================

No. Library items, notes, calculator history, settings and your sign-in are
kept. Only unsaved work in the page you are on is lost when it reloads.

IS THE UPDATE KIT A PANEL I CAN OPEN?
=====================================

No. It is the notice and the Update website command. There is no launcher for
it in the page utilities bar.

HOW DO I KNOW WHICH RELEASE I AM ON?
====================================

The notice names the new version. The site's release history is at
https://auricartisan.com/library/changelog/.

DOES UPDATING NEED A CONNECTION?
================================

Yes. Files are re-downloaded from auricartisan.com.

SHOULD I UPDATE THE INSTALLED APP THE SAME WAY?
===============================================

Yes. The installed app is the same site, so the notice and the command work
there too. See Download and offline use
[../../../download-and-offline/README.txt].

DOES THE UPDATE KIT UPDATE MY BROWSER EXTENSION OR VS CODE EXTENSIONS?
======================================================================

No. It only refreshes the website in your browser. Extensions update through
their own stores.
