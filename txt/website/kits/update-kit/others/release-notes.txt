THE UPDATE KIT AS OF 24 SEPTEMBER 2026
======================================

  - Checks for a newer release on each page load and shows an Update available
    notice with Update now and x.
  - Update website in the right-click menu, with states for available,
    updating and failed, and a banner at the top of the menu when an update is
    ready.
  - `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `U` from any page.
  - Updates clear the site's caches, refresh the service worker and the
    current page's files, and keep your saved data.

Changes not recorded in the public history are not listed here.
