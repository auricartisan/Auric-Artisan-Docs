==============================================================================
UPDATE KIT PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What the Update Kit stores in your browser and what it fetches.
Area: Website › Kits › Update Kit   Updated: 2026-09-25

STORED IN YOUR BROWSER
======================

The release number you last updated to, so the site can tell when a newer one
is out. Nothing else.

FETCHED FROM AURICARTISAN.COM
=============================

  - On each page load: the site's release information, to find the newest
    release number.
  - When you update: the files the current page uses, downloaded again.

These are ordinary requests to auricartisan.com. No information about you or
your device is sent by the Update Kit.

WHAT AN UPDATE DELETES
======================

The site's cached files in your browser. Your saved library, notes, settings
and sign-in are not deleted.
