==============================================================================
STATUS KIT
==============================================================================
See how finished the page you are on is, scan a whole section of the site for
page statuses, and watch live device, network and performance readings.
Area: Website › Kits › Status Kit   Updated: 2026-09-25

The Status Kit answers two questions: how finished is this part of Auric
Artisan, and how is my device coping? Its This page card shows the status of
the page you are on, such as Beta, Stable or Experimental. Its scanner lists
every page under a path, such as `/tool/` or `/library/`, with each page's
status, a summary and filters.

Above the scanner, a collapsible System status panel shows live readings from
your browser across five tabs: Network, Hardware, Memory, Display and Runtime,
including your connection type, battery level, storage use, screen resolution,
colour gamut and a live frame-rate graph.

All readings are taken in your browser and shown only to you. Scans read pages
of auricartisan.com.

WHO IT IS FOR
=============

  - Visitors who want to know whether a tool is stable or still experimental
    before relying on it.
  - Anyone reporting a problem who needs to describe their device, browser and
    connection.
  - Designers checking what their display supports, such as the P3 wide gamut.

WHERE TO FIND IT
================

On any page of https://auricartisan.com, select Page utilities in the
bottom-left corner, then Status. The Status Kit has no keyboard shortcut.

QUICK START
===========

  1. Select Page utilities > Status. The Status Kit opens with the cursor in
     the path box.
  2. Read the This page card: the page title and its status badge.
  3. Select the Hardware tab in System status to see your battery, device
     memory and CPU threads.
  4. Type `/library/` in the path box and press `Enter`.
  5. Read the summary cards (Pages, Tools, Stable, Beta, Issues) and the
     grouped list of pages.
  6. Select a filter chip such as Stable to narrow the list.
  7. Select a page in the list to open it in a new tab.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
===============

  - See the current page's status at a glance.
  - Scan any section of the site and count its pages by status.
  - Filter scan results by status and search them by title.
  - Re-run any of your last eight scans with one click.
  - Check your connection, battery, memory, storage, caches, display, frame
    rate, page load time, service worker, app mode, language, time zone and
    cookie setting.
