STATUS KIT FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers to common questions about the Status Kit.
Area: Website › Kits › Status Kit   Updated: 2026-09-25

CAN I SCAN ANOTHER WEBSITE?
===========================

No. The scanner lists pages of auricartisan.com only. If you paste another
site's address, only its path is used, and it is looked up on
auricartisan.com. To check other websites, use the URL Analyzer
[../../../tools/accessibility-and-vision/analyzer/README.txt].

DOES THE STATUS KIT SEND MY DEVICE DETAILS ANYWHERE?
====================================================

No. The readings are taken in your browser and shown only to you. See Privacy
[../others/privacy.txt].

IS THE SPEED READING A SPEED TEST?
==================================

No. It is your browser's rough estimate of your connection, not a measurement.

WHY IS THERE NO KEYBOARD SHORTCUT?
==================================

The Status Kit has none. Open it from Page utilities > Status.

DOES P3 GAMUT MEAN MY SCREEN IS COLOUR-ACCURATE?
================================================

No. It means your browser reports that the display can show roughly the P3
range of colours. It says nothing about calibration.

CAN I EXPORT A SCAN?
====================

No. Scan results stay in the panel. Your last eight scan paths are remembered
under Recent scans.

WHAT COUNTS AS AN ISSUE?
========================

Pages whose status is Alpha, Deprecated or Archived.
