READ FROM YOUR BROWSER, SHOWN ONLY TO YOU
=========================================

The System status panel reads your connection estimate, battery, device
memory, processor threads, memory use, storage use, cache count, screen and
window size, pixel ratio, colour depth and gamut, frame rate, page load time,
service worker state, app mode, language, time zone and cookie setting.

These readings are displayed in the panel and not stored or sent anywhere.
They stop updating when you close the kit.

KEPT IN YOUR BROWSER
====================

  What: Your last 8 scans: the path, target page title and status, page count
        and time
  Why:  For Recent scans

  What: Which result categories you collapsed
  Why:  To keep them collapsed

  What: Position, expanded size, Focus mode and theme
  Why:  Shared kit preferences

FETCHED FROM AURICARTISAN.COM
=============================

  - When the kit first opens on a page: the central list of page statuses, the
    site map, and the current page (to read its title).
  - During a scan: the Menu Kit's page list and each page in the scanned
    section.

These are ordinary page requests to auricartisan.com, like visiting the pages.
