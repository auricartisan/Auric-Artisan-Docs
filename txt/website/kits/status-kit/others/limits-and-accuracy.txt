==============================================================================
STATUS KIT LIMITS
==============================================================================
What the Status Kit's page statuses and system readings can and cannot tell
you.
Area: Website › Kits › Status Kit   Updated: 2026-09-25

PAGE STATUSES
=============

  - Statuses are the site's own labels, set centrally. They are not measured
    from the page.
  - A page without its own entry takes the status of its folder, or of the
    whole site (Beta).
  - The kit does not explain why a page has a given status.

SCANS
=====

  - Scans cover pages of auricartisan.com only, taken from the site map.
  - The home page and individual colour pages under `/color/` are never
    listed.
  - Titles come from fetching each page, so a scan of a large section makes
    many requests and takes longer.
  - Categories come from the Menu Kit where a page is listed there, and are
    otherwise guessed from words in the address, so a page can land in Other
    or in a category that does not quite fit.
  - Results are not saved; only your last eight scan paths are remembered.

SYSTEM READINGS
===============

  - Readings are what your browser chooses to report. Browsers round, cap or
    withhold some values to protect privacy, so the same device can show
    different figures in different browsers.
  - Speed and Latency are browser estimates, not a speed test.
  - Device memory is approximate and capped by the browser.
  - Storage shows the space the site uses against the space your browser
    allows it, which is not your disk's free space.
  - Color reports whether the display can show roughly the P3 range. It is not
    a calibration or a measurement of colour accuracy.
  - Resolution and Viewport are in CSS pixels; multiply by Pixel ratio for
    physical pixels.
  - Frame rate counts redraws in the last second while the kit is open. It is
    an indicator, not a benchmark.
  - Page load is the time the page took to finish loading on this visit.
