==============================================================================
KITS AND YOUR PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What each kit keeps in your browser, what it sends over the network, and how
to clear it.
Area: Website › Kits   Updated: 2026-09-25

The kits run in your browser. Almost everything they remember stays in your
browser's storage for auricartisan.com and is never sent anywhere. This page
lists what each kit keeps and the few cases where a kit uses the network.

WHAT EVERY KIT KEEPS IN YOUR BROWSER
====================================

For each kit separately:

  - the panel position after you drag it;
  - whether it is expanded;
  - whether Focus mode is on;
  - its own light or dark theme, once you have used its Theme button.

WHAT EACH KIT KEEPS, AND WHAT IT SENDS
======================================

  Kit:                  Feature Kit
  Kept in your browser: Calculator history (up to 100 entries), notes,
                        recently picked dates (up to 8), the tool you had open
                        while Focus mode was on, feedback waiting to be sent
  Uses the network for: Loading the unit list the first time you open the Unit
                        Converter; sending feedback you submit

  Kit:                  Menu Kit
  Kept in your browser: Last tab, collapsed sections, how often you open each
                        page (for Most Used)
  Uses the network for: Loading the list of pages and the site status

  Kit:                  Advanced Kit
  Kept in your browser: Your tool visibility mode and your Custom tool ticks
  Uses the network for: Loading the list of tools for the Custom checklist

  Kit:                  Status Kit
  Kept in your browser: Your last 8 scans, collapsed result groups
  Uses the network for: Reading the site map and the pages you scan, all from
                        auricartisan.com

  Kit:                  Library Kit
  Kept in your browser: Every saved item, collection and library setting,
                        including any sync destinations you add
  Uses the network for: Only the sync destinations you set up yourself

  Kit:                  Update Kit
  Kept in your browser: The site version you last updated to
  Uses the network for: Checking the latest release and re-downloading the
                        site's files when you update

THINGS THAT ARE NEVER STORED
============================

  - Calculator variables, functions you define and calculator memory last only
    until you leave or reload the page.
  - Text you type into the Developer Tools stays on the page and is not kept.
  - The Status Kit reads your connection, battery, memory and screen details
    in your browser to show them to you. It does not send them.

PRIVATE WINDOWS AND BLOCKED STORAGE
===================================

In a private window, or when your browser blocks storage for the site, the
kits still work but forget everything when you close the page.

CLEAR WHAT THE KITS HAVE STORED
===============================

Clear the stored data for auricartisan.com in your browser's settings. This
resets every kit and also deletes your Library Kit items, calculator history
and notes. Export anything you want to keep first: the Library Kit has Export,
and Notes has Export for each note.

For the site's privacy policy, see https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/.
