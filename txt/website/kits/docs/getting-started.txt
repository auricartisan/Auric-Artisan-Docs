==============================================================================
GETTING STARTED WITH KITS
==============================================================================
Open your first kit from the page utilities bar, learn the parts of a kit
panel, and close it again.
Area: Website › Kits   Updated: 2026-09-25

A kit is a floating panel that opens over the page you are on. This guide
opens one, walks through the parts every kit shares, and closes it again.

BEFORE YOU START
================

  - Kits work on desktop and on phones. On a narrow screen the launcher bar
    wraps into two rows.
  - Kits need JavaScript. Each kit loads the first time you open it, so the
    first open can take a moment on a slow connection. While it loads, its
    launcher looks dimmed and shows a busy pointer.
  - Nothing needs an account. Kits keep their preferences in your browser. See
    Privacy [../others/privacy.txt].

OPEN YOUR FIRST KIT
===================

  1. Go to any page, for example https://auricartisan.com.
  2. Find the round button in the bottom-left corner of the window. Its
     tooltip reads Page utilities.
  3. Select it. A bar slides open with labelled launchers: Close, Feature,
     Menu, Advanced, Status, Library, Navigate and Settings.
  4. Select Feature. The bar folds away and the Feature Kit opens in the
     bottom-left corner of the window, showing six cards.
  5. Select Scientific Calculator, type `2+3` with your keyboard and press
     `Enter`.

You see `5` in the calculator's display. You have opened a kit, switched to
one of its tools and used it.

THE PARTS OF A KIT PANEL
========================

Every kit panel has the same frame.

  - Title bar. The kit's name sits on the left. Drag the title bar to move the
    panel.
  - Title-bar buttons. On the right: Focus mode, Theme, Resize, Minimize and
    Close, in that order. The Library Kit puts all but Close in a Panel
    options menu.
  - Body. The kit's own content: cards, tabs, a search box, results. The body
    scrolls when there is more than fits.
  - Launcher. The labelled button in the page utilities bar that opens the
    kit. While a kit is open, its launcher shows a cross, and selecting it
    again closes the kit.

Panel controls [panel-controls.txt] explains each button in detail.

KEEP WORKING WHILE A KIT IS OPEN
================================

Kits do not block the page. While a kit is open you can scroll the page, click
links and type into tools. Clicking on the page does not close the kit.

The Feature Kit is the one exception: when Focus mode is off, it dims the page
behind it, and clicking the dimmed page closes it. Turn on Focus mode in the
Feature Kit to work on the page while it stays open.

OPEN MORE THAN ONE KIT
======================

Opening a kit normally closes whichever kit was already open. To keep two kits
open side by side:

  1. Open the first kit and select its Focus mode button. The button turns
     gold.
  2. Open the second kit from the page utilities bar.
  3. Drag the two panels apart by their title bars.

Both kits stay open. The Status Kit is an exception: it closes when another
kit opens, even with Focus mode on.

CLOSE A KIT
===========

Use any of these:

  - Select Close (the cross) in the kit's title bar.
  - Press `Esc`. In the Feature Kit, the first `Esc` goes back to the Feature
    Kit menu and the second closes the kit. In the Menu Kit and the Library
    Kit, the first `Esc` clears the search box if it has text in it.
  - Select the kit's launcher again. While the Feature Kit's Focus mode is on,
    its launcher moves keyboard focus into the kit instead of closing it.

If Focus mode is on, a closed kit can open again by itself on the next page
you visit. Turn Focus mode off first if you want it to stay closed. See
Troubleshooting [troubleshooting.txt].
