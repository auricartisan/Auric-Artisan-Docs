==============================================================================
GUIDES FOR ALL KITS
==============================================================================
Contents of the shared kit guides, which cover the launcher bar and the panel
controls every kit uses.
Area: Website › Kits   Updated: 2026-09-25

This folder covers what the kits have in common: how to open them, the page
utilities bar that launches them, and the title-bar controls that move,
resize, minimise, theme and close every panel. Guides for a single kit live in
that kit's own folder.

  File:           getting-started.md [getting-started.txt]
  What it covers: Open your first kit, learn the parts of a kit panel, and
                  close it again

  File:           the-kit-dock.md [the-kit-dock.txt]
  What it covers: The page utilities bar: its launchers, its phone layout and
                  how it behaves

  File:           panel-controls.md [panel-controls.txt]
  What it covers: Every title-bar control, what each kit remembers, and how
                  the kits differ

  File:           troubleshooting.md [troubleshooting.txt]
  What it covers: Problems that affect all kits, with causes and fixes

  File:           faq.md [faq.txt]
  What it covers: Short answers to common questions about kits
