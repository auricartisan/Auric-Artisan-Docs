==============================================================================
KITS FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers to common questions about the floating kits on auricartisan.com.
Area: Website › Kits   Updated: 2026-09-25

WHAT IS A KIT?
==============

A floating panel that opens over any page of the site and does one job:
calculations and utilities (Feature Kit), site navigation (Menu Kit), tool
visibility (Advanced Kit), page and device status (Status Kit), or your saved
work (Library Kit).

DO I NEED AN ACCOUNT TO USE THE KITS?
=====================================

No. Every kit works without signing in. The Library Kit keeps your saved items
in your browser, not in an account.

ARE THE KITS FREE?
==================

Yes. Every kit is available on every plan. Your plan only sets how many active
items the Library Kit can keep; see the Library Kit FAQ
[../library-kit/docs/faq.txt].

WHERE DO I FIND THE KITS?
=========================

Select the round Page utilities button in the bottom-left corner of any page.
See The page utilities bar [the-kit-dock.txt].

CAN I HAVE TWO KITS OPEN AT ONCE?
=================================

Yes. Turn on Focus mode in the first kit before opening the second. Without
Focus mode, opening a kit closes the one already open. The Status Kit always
closes when another kit opens.

WILL A KIT STAY OPEN WHEN I GO TO ANOTHER PAGE?
===============================================

Only with Focus mode on. Each page load starts fresh, and kits with Focus mode
on open again automatically.

DOES A KIT BLOCK THE PAGE?
==========================

No, with one exception. Kits float above the page and you can keep using the
page. The Feature Kit dims the page while its Focus mode is off; turn Focus
mode on to keep the page usable.

WHY DOES A KIT LOOK DIFFERENT FROM THE REST OF THE PAGE?
========================================================

Each kit can have its own light or dark theme, set with its Theme button. Once
you use that button, the kit keeps its own choice instead of following the
site.

CAN I MOVE A KIT BACK TO THE CORNER?
====================================

In the Library Kit, yes: Panel options > Reset position. For other kits, drag
the panel back by its title bar.

DO KITS WORK ON A PHONE?
========================

Yes. On a narrow screen the launcher bar wraps into two rows, and kits open
across the width of the screen.

CAN I USE KITS WITH THE KEYBOARD ONLY?
======================================

Yes. The round Page utilities button and every launcher can be reached with
`Tab`. The Feature, Menu, Advanced and Library Kits also have their own
shortcuts; see Keyboard shortcuts [../others/shortcuts.txt]. Moving a panel
with the arrow keys is possible only in the Library Kit.

WHAT IS THE "UX KIT" IN THE CHANGELOG?
======================================

It is not a panel. It is the part of the site that changes wording and shows
or hides sections when you pick a mode in the Advanced Kit. See What each mode
changes [../advanced-kit/docs/what-each-mode-changes.txt].

IS THE UPDATE KIT A PANEL?
==========================

No. It shows an Update available notice at the top of the page when a new
release is out, and it runs the Update website command. See Update Kit
[../update-kit/README.txt].

WHAT DO THE KITS STORE ABOUT ME?
================================

Only preferences and content in your own browser, such as positions, themes,
calculator history, notes and saved items. Feedback you send from the Feature
Kit is sent to Auric Artisan. See Privacy [../others/privacy.txt].
