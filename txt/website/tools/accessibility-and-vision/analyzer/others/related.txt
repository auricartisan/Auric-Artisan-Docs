STANDARDS
=========

  - WCAG 2.2 and the W3C Understanding documents, linked from every rule as
    How to meet this: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/

    https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/
