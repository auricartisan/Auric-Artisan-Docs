==============================================================================
ISHIHARA PLATE GENERATOR KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
==============================================================================
The keys the Ishihara Plate Generator responds to.
Area: Website › Tools › Accessibility and vision   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Keys               | Where           | Action
  -------------------+-----------------+------------------
  `Enter` or `Space` | A focused plate | Open it full size
  Double-click       | A plate         | Open it full size
  `Right`            | Full-size view  | Next plate
  `Left`             | Full-size view  | Previous plate
  `Esc`              | Full-size view  | Close it

All other controls are standard buttons and menus: move between them with
`Tab` and `Shift` + `Tab`, and use them with `Enter` or `Space`.

For shortcuts that work across the whole site, see Keyboard shortcuts
[../../../../site-features/keyboard-shortcuts/README.txt].
