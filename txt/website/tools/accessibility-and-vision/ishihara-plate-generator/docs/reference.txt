LAB: MAIN CONTROLS
==================

  Control: Plate Type
  Options: Number, Letter, Symbol, Number (Gradient), Shape, Multi-layer
           (Shape+Number), Hidden Path
  Default: Number

  Control: Color Palette
  Options: Auto (Random) or one of eight named palettes (see Generate plates
           [generate-plates.txt])
  Default: Auto (Random)

  Control: Difficulty
  Options: Easy, Medium, Hard, Extreme
  Default: Medium

  Control: + Generate
  Options: One plate
  Default: —

  Control: ×1, ×3, ×6
  Options: Several plates
  Default: —

  Control: Custom
  Options: Opens Create Custom Plate
  Default: —

  Control: Clear All
  Options: Removes every plate
  Default: —

  Control: ↓ Export All
  Options: ZIP of every plate
  Default: —

  Control: Plate Size
  Options: S (210 px), M (290 px), L (440 px), XL (900 px)
  Default: M

CREATE CUSTOM PLATE
===================

  Field             | Values
  ------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Plate Type        | As above
  Content           | A number, a letter, or a symbol or shape name
  Color Palette     | As above
  Difficulty        | As above
  Seed (optional)   | Any whole number; the same seed and settings give an identical plate
  Cancel / Generate | —

Symbol names: star, arrow, triangle, heart, diamond, circle, square, moon,
sun, check, x, target, squareDot, dot, leaf, wave, shield. Shape names:
pentagon, octagon, rounded-square, annulus, oval.

PLATE CARD
==========

  Item:    Heading
  Meaning: Plate number and content, for example "#1 - 45"

  Item:    Tags
  Meaning: Type, palette, difficulty, dot count, placement engine, dot style

  Item:    QA PASS and percentage
  Meaning: Pass or review, and the quality score

  Item:    Preview switch
  Meaning: Normal, Protan, Deutan, Tritan

  Item:    Normal ΔE
  Meaning: Figure against background for typical vision (OKLab)

  Item:    CVD min
  Meaning: The smallest figure-to-background distance through the three
           deficiencies

  Item:    Density
  Meaning: Share of the plate covered by dots

  Item:    Figure
  Meaning: Share of dots in the figure

  Item:    Seed
  Meaning: The number that rebuilds the plate

  Item:    Material
  Meaning: The card stock

  Item:    Copy seed, Download PNG, Download SVG
  Meaning: Actions

FULL-SIZE VIEW
==============

Opened by double-clicking a plate, or `Enter` or `Space` on it. Shows content,
type, palette, difficulty, dots, plate number, QA score, Separation ΔE,
Concealment ΔE, seed and position; the preview switch; `Left` / `Right` to
move; `Esc` to close.

THIS BATCH (LAB)
================

Plates, Rendered, Avg dots, Color space ("P3 HDR" or "sRGB"), Avg QA.

DIAGNOSTICS
===========

  Item:   Summary
  Detail: Plates, Passing, Mean score, Weakest

  Item:   Components
  Detail: Dot density (.24), Figure ratio (.20), Separation (.32), Concealment
          (.24)

  Item:   Through each deficiency
  Detail: ΔE from the background under Protanopia, Deuteranopia, Tritanopia

  Item:   Pass rule
  Detail: Density 0.08–0.95, figure 5–65% of dots, separation above 0.01,
          score 45 or more

EXPORT
======

  Action: Card Download PNG
  Output: `ishihara-plate-<number>-<content>.png`, 4,096 px

  Action: Card Download SVG
  Output: `ishihara-plate-<number>-<content>.svg`

  Action: Export every plate, Download every .png, ↓ Export All
  Output: `ishihara-plates.zip`: PNG (2,048 px), SVG and JSON per plate

  Action: Download every .svg
  Output: One SVG per plate

  Action: Download the seeds
  Output: `ishihara-seeds.txt`: content, seed, type, palette, difficulty,
          dots, quality
