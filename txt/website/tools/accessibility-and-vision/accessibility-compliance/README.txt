==============================================================================
ACCESSIBILITY COMPLIANCE
==============================================================================
What the Accessibility Compliance address offers a visitor today, and where it
takes you.
Area: Website › Tools › Accessibility and vision   Updated: 2026-09-25

https://auricartisan.com/accessibility-compliance/ is the address of Auric
Artisan's accessibility compliance resource. It no longer has a page of its
own: opening it forwards you straight to the Accessibility Checklist
[../accessibility-checklist/README.txt] at
https://auricartisan.com/resources/accessibility-checklist/, which is where
the compliance material now lives.

What a visitor gets, therefore, is the checklist: 50 WCAG 2.2 checks explained
and ready to mark, the tools to record evidence and export findings, and a
Reference tab on what the law asks for in 15 jurisdictions. The site header
still calls this destination Accessibility Compliance in its Learn menu.

WHO IT IS FOR
=============

Anyone following an old link, a bookmark, or the Accessibility Compliance menu
entry, who wants to know what WCAG and the law require and how to check a site
against them.

WHERE TO FIND IT
================

  Address:      https://auricartisan.com/accessibility-compliance/
  What happens: Forwards immediately to the Accessibility Checklist

  Address:      https://auricartisan.com/compliance/
  What happens: Redirects permanently to `/accessibility-compliance/`, which
                then forwards to the checklist

  Address:      https://auricartisan.com/compliance
  What happens: Shows the `/accessibility-compliance/` page, which forwards to
                the checklist

  Address:      Site header: Learn › Accessibility Compliance ("WCAG checks &
                guidance")
  What happens: Opens the checklist directly

QUICK START
===========

  1. Open https://auricartisan.com/accessibility-compliance/.
  2. Your browser moves to the Accessibility Checklist at once. If it does
     not, select the Accessibility Checklist link on the short "Redirecting"
     page.
  3. Select the Reference tab to read what the law asks for where you work.
  4. Select the Checklist tab to work through the checks.
  5. Select Run the automated pass first → to audit your page with the
     Analyzer.
  6. Use the Export tab to print or copy your findings.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
===============

Everything the Accessibility Checklist [../accessibility-checklist/README.txt]
offers:

  - read 50 WCAG 2.2 checks, each explained, with a test and a fix;
  - mark checks and attach annotated screenshots;
  - export your findings;
  - read the accessibility law for India, the United States, the European
    Union, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Japan and eight more
    countries.
