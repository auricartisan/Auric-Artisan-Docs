GETTING STARTED
==============================================================================
What happens when you open the Accessibility Compliance address, and how to
use the checklist it takes you to.
Area: Website › Tools › Accessibility and vision   Updated: 2026-09-25

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU OPEN THE ADDRESS
======================================

  1. You open https://auricartisan.com/accessibility-compliance/ (or
     `/compliance/`).
  2. A very short page titled "Redirecting to Accessibility Checklist" loads,
     with the text "Redirecting to Accessibility Checklist..." and a link.
  3. Your browser moves on at once to
     https://auricartisan.com/resources/accessibility-checklist/. The forward
     replaces the old address in your history, so your browser's Back button
     skips it.

If your browser blocks the automatic forward, select the Accessibility
Checklist link on that page.

The result: you are on the Accessibility Checklist, with its Checklist, Export
and Reference tabs.

WHAT TO DO NEXT
===============

  If you want to: Know which law applies to you, and which WCAG level it cites
  Go to:          The Reference tab. See Laws by jurisdiction
                  [../../accessibility-checklist/docs/laws-by-jurisdiction.txt].

  If you want to: Check your site against WCAG 2.2 by hand
  Go to:          The Checklist tab. See Work through the checklist
                  [../../accessibility-checklist/docs/work-through-the-checklist.txt].

  If you want to: Find the problems a tool can find automatically
  Go to:          Run the automated pass first →, which opens the Analyzer
                  [../../analyzer/README.txt]

  If you want to: Hand findings to your team
  Go to:          The Export tab. See Export your results
                  [../../accessibility-checklist/docs/export-your-results.txt].

UPDATE YOUR LINKS
=================

If you link to the compliance page from your own site or documents, link to
https://auricartisan.com/resources/accessibility-checklist/ directly. The old
address still works, but the direct address saves a step.
