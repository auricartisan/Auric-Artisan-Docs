==============================================================================
ACCESSIBILITY CHECKLIST PRIVACY
==============================================================================
Where the Accessibility Checklist keeps your marks and screenshots, and how to
delete them.
Area: Website › Tools › Accessibility and vision   Updated: 2026-09-25

The page puts it simply: "Your work stays on this device."

WHAT IS KEPT, AND WHERE
=======================

  Item:  Your marks on each check
  Where: Your browser's local storage

  Item:  Screenshots, the original image and its marks, and a marked-up copy
  Where: Your browser's database storage

  - There is no account, no sync and no server copy. The checklist sends none
    of your marks or screenshots anywhere.
  - Your work is per browser, per profile and per device.
  - In a private window, or when storage is blocked, the page still works but
    forgets when you close it. Without database storage, screenshots cannot be
    attached, but marks still work.

WHAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE
=======================

Only what you choose to take out:

  - Copy as Markdown puts the findings on your clipboard. Screenshots are
    never included.
  - Export the report prints the page, including small marked-up screenshot
    thumbnails.

REDACTION
=========

The Redact tool covers an area only in the marked-up copy used for thumbnails
and printing. The original screenshot stays stored in your browser,
unredacted. Crop or blur sensitive details before you attach a screenshot.

WORKING IN TWO TABS
===================

Each open tab keeps its own copy of your marks and saves it as a whole.
Working in two tabs at once may overwrite marks made in the other. Use one tab
at a time.

DELETE YOUR DATA
================

  - Export › Reset removes every mark and every screenshot at once, without
    asking.
  - Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes everything too.

The rest of the page loads the site's normal scripts, including advertising,
under the site-wide consent settings. See Company and legal
