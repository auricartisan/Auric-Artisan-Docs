==============================================================================
ACCESSIBILITY CHECKLIST FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers to common questions about the Accessibility Checklist.
Area: Website › Tools › Accessibility and vision   Updated: 2026-09-25

IS IT FREE? DO I NEED AN ACCOUNT?
=================================

It is free, and there is no account. Nothing to install either: it works in
any modern browser.

IS A FINISHED CHECKLIST A WCAG CERTIFICATION?
=============================================

No. The page says it plainly: "A worked checklist is evidence of a process,
not a conformance claim." A claim needs a full evaluation against the whole
standard, and the involvement of people who use assistive technology every
day.

DOES IT COVER ALL OF WCAG 2.2?
==============================

It covers the ground most audits hit, in 50 checks tied to 33 WCAG criteria,
plus 13 good-practice checks. It does not have a check of its own for every
criterion; for example, the WCAG 2.2 additions Dragging Movements (2.5.7),
Consistent Help (3.2.6), Redundant Entry (3.3.7) and Accessible Authentication
(3.3.8) have no dedicated check. The Analyzer [../../analyzer/README.txt] has
automated rules for three of those four.

WHAT DO AUTO, PARTLY AND BY HAND MEAN?
======================================

How much the Analyzer can decide: AUTO checks it decides on its own, PARTLY
checks it narrows down for a person to confirm, and BY HAND checks only a
person can judge. The checklist does not read Analyzer results; you mark every
check yourself.

WHICH CHECKS ARE REQUIRED?
==========================

Level A and AA checks, 35 in all, marked Required. They are what laws and
procurement documents cite. AAA and Practice checks are optional.

HOW DO I UNDO A MARK?
=====================

Select the same verdict again. The check goes back to Not checked yet.

WHERE IS MY WORK SAVED?
=======================

In this browser, on this device. Nothing is uploaded. It survives closing the
tab, but not clearing site data, and it does not follow you to another browser
or device.

CAN I MOVE MY PROGRESS TO ANOTHER COMPUTER, OR SHARE IT WITH A COLLEAGUE?
=========================================================================

Not as a file you can import. Share the result instead: Copy as Markdown for
your issue tracker, or Export the report and save it as a PDF.

CAN I ADD NOTES TO A CHECK?
===========================

There is no notes field. Use screenshots with numbered callouts, or add notes
in your tracker after copying the Markdown.

ARE SCREENSHOTS INCLUDED IN THE EXPORT?
=======================================

The printed report shows up to four small marked-up thumbnails per check. The
Markdown says how many screenshots are attached but does not include them.

IS THE LEGAL INFORMATION UP TO DATE?
====================================

It was last reviewed on 11 September 2026, and the page notes that dates move.
It is orientation, not legal advice; check the primary source linked from each
instrument.

WHY IS THE MENU ENTRY CALLED ACCESSIBILITY COMPLIANCE?
======================================================

The site header's Learn menu lists this page as Accessibility Compliance, and
the old `/accessibility-compliance/` address forwards here. The page itself is
titled The Accessibility Checklist.
