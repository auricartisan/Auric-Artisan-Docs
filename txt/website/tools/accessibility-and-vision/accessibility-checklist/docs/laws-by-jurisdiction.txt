THE FOUR LEVELS ON THIS PAGE
============================

  Level:              A
  Checks:             22
  What the page says: The floor. Nearly every instrument below names it, or
                      something built on it.

  Level:              AA
  Checks:             13
  What the page says: What the statutes and procurement documents actually
                      cite. A + AA is the legal line.

  Level:              AAA
  Checks:             2
  What the page says: Stricter than any law here requires. Worth doing; never
                      demanded wholesale.

  Level:              Practice
  Checks:             13
  What the page says: No criterion covers it, and every audit finds it anyway.

The page's summary: WCAG 2.1 Level AA is the common denominator almost
everywhere. The UK and Australia have already moved to WCAG 2.2 AA, and EN 301
549 V4 takes the European standard there too. Build to WCAG 2.2 AA and you are
above every line on the page.

FINDING YOUR WAY
================

A side rail headed Jurisdictions jumps to each card: India (5 instruments),

Each card shows the jurisdiction, its standard, a table of instrument, what it

A second rail, Also on this site, links to the Learn library ("The science

INDIA
=====

Standard: WCAG 2.1 AA via GIGW 3.0. Binds government, establishments and
service providers, public and private.

  Instrument:   Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act (Act 49 of 2016, ss.
                40, 42, 46)
  When:         2016
  What it does: Empowers accessibility standards, requires accessible ICT, and
                places the duty on service providers

  Instrument:   Rajive Raturi v. Union of India, Supreme Court of India
  When:         8 Nov 2024
  What it does: Held merely recommendatory guidelines ultra vires; gave the
                Union three months to set a mandatory floor

  Instrument:   GIGW 3.0, Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (MeitY /
                NIC)
  When:         2023, revised 2025
  What it does: 88 mandatory checkpoints; names WCAG 2.1 Level AA; certified
                by STQC

  Instrument:   IS 17802 (Parts 1 and 2), Bureau of Indian Standards
  When:         2021
  What it does: India's own ICT accessibility standard, written to be citable
                in procurement

  Instrument:   Draft ICT Accessibility Rules (consultation, after Raturi)
  When:         In progress
  What it does: The mandatory floor the Court ordered; check its current
                status

Non-compliance with the Act is an offence under section 89, according to the
page.

UNITED STATES
=============

Standard: WCAG 2.1 AA, with Section 508 still at WCAG 2.0 AA. Binds federal,
state and local government, and private business through Title III litigation.

  Instrument:   ADA Title II, DOJ final rule (28 CFR Part 35, April 2024)
  When:         2027 / 2028
  What it does: State and local government web content and apps to WCAG 2.1 AA

  Instrument:   Section 508, Rehabilitation Act (the 2017 Refresh)
  When:         2018
  What it does: Federal agencies and what they buy, to WCAG 2.0 AA by
                reference

  Instrument:   Section 504 (HHS and Department of Education rules)
  When:         2024 onwards
  What it does: Recipients of federal funding, such as hospitals and
                universities, to WCAG 2.1 AA

  Instrument:   ADA Title III (public accommodations)
  When:         1990
  What it does: No technical standard; where most private litigation happens,
                with WCAG as the practical measure

The page notes that in April 2026 the DOJ pushed both Title II deadlines back
a year: large entities to April 2027, and smaller entities and special
districts to April 2028. The underlying duty not to discriminate never paused.

EUROPEAN UNION
==============

Standard: EN 301 549 V3.2.1, which points at WCAG 2.1 AA. Binds the public
sector and, since 2025, much of the private sector across 27 member states.

  Instrument:   European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882)
  When:         28 Jun 2025
  What it does: Reaches the private sector: e-commerce, banking, e-books,
                communications, ticketing, ATMs

  Instrument:   Web Accessibility Directive (Directive (EU) 2016/2102)
  When:         2019 / 2021
  What it does: Public sector sites and apps, with an accessibility statement
                and a feedback route

  Instrument:   EN 301 549 (V3.2.1, March 2021)
  When:         V3.2.1
  What it does: The harmonised standard; clause 9 is WCAG 2.1 AA, and V4.1.1
                moves it to WCAG 2.2

Service microenterprises are exempt from the European Accessibility Act.

UNITED KINGDOM
==============

Standard: WCAG 2.2 AA. Binds the public sector by regulation and everyone else
by duty.

  Instrument:   Public Sector Bodies Accessibility Regulations (SI 2018/952)
  When:         2.2 since 2024
  What it does: Public sector to WCAG 2.2 AA, monitored against 2.2 since
                October 2024, plus a statement

  Instrument:   Equality Act 2010 (s. 20, reasonable adjustments)
  When:         2010
  What it does: Outcome-based duty with no named standard; applies to private
                service providers too

CANADA
======

Standard: CAN/ASC-EN 301 549:2024. Binds federally regulated bodies; provinces
add their own rules.

  Instrument:   Accessible Canada Act (S.C. 2019, c. 10) and Regulations
  When:         2019
  What it does: Federally regulated organisations publish accessibility plans,
                take feedback and report

  Instrument:   CAN/ASC-EN 301 549:2024, Accessibility Standards Canada
  When:         31 May 2024
  What it does: Adopts the European standard: WCAG 2.1 AA plus extra ICT
                requirements

  Instrument:   AODA, Ontario Integrated Accessibility Standards (s. 14)
  When:         2021
  What it does: WCAG 2.0 AA, without live captions and audio description, for
                large organisations and the public sector

AUSTRALIA
=========

Standard: WCAG 2.2 AA. Binds government directly and business through the
Disability Discrimination Act.

  Instrument:   Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (and Maguire v SOCOG, 2000)
  When:         1992
  What it does: Names no standard; the Sydney Olympics case is why WCAG is the
                measure

  Instrument:   Government digital standards (with AS EN 301 549:2020 for
                procurement)
  When:         2.2 AA
  What it does: WCAG 2.2 AA formally adopted as the minimum, up from 2.0

JAPAN
=====

Standard: JIS X 8341-3:2016, which carries WCAG 2.0. Binds public bodies by
standard and private business by duty since 2024.

  Instrument:   JIS X 8341-3:2016, Japanese Industrial Standard
  When:         2016
  What it does: The success criteria of WCAG 2.0, with its own conformance and
                testing procedure

  Instrument:   Act on Eliminating Discrimination against Persons with
                Disabilities (2021 amendment)
  When:         Apr 2024
  What it does: Reasonable accommodation required of private business, not
                merely encouraged
