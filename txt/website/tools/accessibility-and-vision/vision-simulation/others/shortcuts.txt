==============================================================================
VISION LAB KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
==============================================================================
Every key the Vision Lab responds to.
Area: Website › Tools › Accessibility and vision   Updated: 2026-09-25

The lab's single-key shortcuts work anywhere on the page except while you are
typing in a field, and not with `Ctrl`, `Alt` or `Cmd` held. Press `?` to see
them on screen.

NAVIGATION
==========

  Key   | Action
  ------+--------------------------------------------
  `/`   | Focus the search field
  `Esc` | Clear the search (while it has focus)
  `?`   | Show or hide the keyboard shortcuts overlay
  `F`   | Favourites
  `A`   | Analytics
  `C`   | Compare

PANELS
======

  Keys                                          | Action
  ----------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------
  `R`                                           | Open a random simulation
  `T`                                           | Tile all panels
  `1` to `5`                                    | Bring panel 1 to 5 forward
  `Shift` + click, or `Ctrl` + click, on a card | Duplicate a panel that is already open

CARDS AND TILES
===============

  Keys               | Where                    | Action
  -------------------+--------------------------+----------------------------------
  `Enter` or `Space` | A focused Catalogue card | Open its panel
  `Left` / `Up`      | A Compare tile's grip    | Move the tile one place earlier
  `Right` / `Down`   | A Compare tile's grip    | Move the tile one place later
  `Home` / `End`     | A Compare tile's grip    | Move the tile to the start or end

For shortcuts that work across the whole site, see Keyboard shortcuts
[../../../../site-features/keyboard-shortcuts/README.txt].
