==============================================================================
VISION LAB PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What the Vision Lab does with your colours and images, and what it keeps in
your browser.
Area: Website › Tools › Accessibility and vision   Updated: 2026-09-25

YOUR IMAGES STAY ON YOUR DEVICE
===============================

The page says it plainly: "Whatever you drop stays on your machine — the
pipeline runs in this tab and nothing is uploaded." Every simulation,
measurement and audit runs in your browser.

WHAT IS KEPT IN YOUR BROWSER
============================

  - the view you last used, and the Workbench's condition, severity, model,
    specimen and base colour;
  - the Catalogue category, your favourites, the Compare sheet and the Palette
    audit palette;
  - the panels you had open, their snapshots, and the position of the Vision
    Control dock.

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.

THE DAILY COUNT
===============

On the free plan, the lab counts how many simulations you open from the
Catalogue each day, and the count is also checked by Auric Artisan. Only the
count is involved; your colours and images are not sent.

LINKS
=====

A workspace link (`#v=`) and a Workbench link (`?vw=`) carry your settings in
the address, readable by anyone who has the link. They never contain your own
images.
