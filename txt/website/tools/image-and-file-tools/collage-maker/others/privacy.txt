What the Collage Maker does with your pictures, and what stays in your
browser.
Area: Website › Tools › Image and file tools › Collage Maker   Updated: 2026-09-25

YOUR PICTURES STAY IN YOUR BROWSER
==================================

  - Pictures are read by the page itself and drawn straight to a canvas in
    your browser. The tool does not upload them or send them anywhere.
  - The measurements, the arrangement comparison and the exports are all
    worked out on your device.
  - Exported files (PNG, JPEG, session and reading) are created in the browser
    and saved by your browser's normal download.
  - The clipboard is used only when you select Copy to the clipboard, and only
    to place the PNG there.

NOTHING IS KEPT AFTER YOU LEAVE
===============================

The tool does not store your pictures, your layout or your history. Reloading
or leaving the page clears them. To keep a layout, save a session file; to
keep the picture, save a PNG or JPEG.

WHAT A SESSION FILE CONTAINS
============================

A session file contains the canvas settings and, for each picture, its file
name, position, size, scale, rotation, opacity, flips and visibility. It
contains no image data. If you share a session file, the recipient sees your
picture file names.

THE PAGE ITSELF
===============

The Collage Maker is a normal page on auricartisan.com. Site-wide features
such as sign-in, preferences and any consent-based services follow the site's
own privacy policy and your cookie choices; they are covered in the site's
help pages at Help [../../../../../help/README.txt].
