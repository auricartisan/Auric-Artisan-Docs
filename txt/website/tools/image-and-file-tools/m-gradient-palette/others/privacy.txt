What the M-Gradient Palette does with your colours and settings.
Area: Website › Tools › Image and file tools › M-Gradient Palette   Updated: 2026-09-25

EVERYTHING RUNS IN YOUR BROWSER
===============================

  - The ramp, the measurements, the route comparison, the contrast checks and
    the colour-vision views are all worked out on your device.
  - The tool sends nothing to a server.
  - Save the SVG and Save the reading create their files in the browser and
    save them through your browser's normal download.
  - The clipboard is written only when you select a copy button or a step of
    the ramp.

NOTHING IS KEPT AFTER YOU LEAVE
===============================

Your colours and settings are not stored. Reloading or leaving the page
returns to the default ramp. To keep a ramp, copy the custom properties or
save the reading.

THE PAGE ITSELF
===============

The M-Gradient Palette is a normal page on auricartisan.com. Site-wide
features such as sign-in, preferences and any consent-based services follow
the site's own privacy policy and your cookie choices; see Help
