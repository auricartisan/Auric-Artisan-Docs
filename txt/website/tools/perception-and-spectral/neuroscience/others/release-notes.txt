User-facing history of the Visual Pathway tool.
Area: Website › Tools › Perception and spectral › Neuroscience   Updated: 2026-09-25

The site-wide history is at https://auricartisan.com/library/changelog/. The
entries below cover what changed for users of this tool.

SEPTEMBER 2026 — REBUILT AS THE VISUAL PATHWAY
==============================================

The page was rebuilt with six tabs (Lab, Pipeline, Cells, Data, Export,
Reference). The Pipeline and Cells tabs can show values from the earlier
version beside the current ones.

Added:

  - Viewing distance and Pixel pitch controls; every frequency is now in
    cycles per degree, including the V1 Gabor frequency (previously cycles per
    image).
  - The Nyquist limit of the grid readout and a warning when the grating is
    finer than the screen can draw.
  - Contrast achieved and a note when the requested contrast does not fit in
    sRGB.
  - A working scale bar under every map, including a hue wheel for the
    orientation map and a "flat" label for maps with no variation.
  - The Test card as a stimulus source.
  - The cone figure with the published peak wavelengths marked, the L − M peak
    gap, Published gap and Neutral off-axis readouts, and a cone-table
    installer on the Data tab.
  - The Data register with a status for every dataset and model.

Changed:

  - Cone signals are now computed from CIE XYZ, so greys sit at zero on both
    chromatic channels. This changes all LGN and V4 values and the Export
    table.
  - The colour-vision simulation is now applied in cone space and credited to
    Viénot, Brettel and Mollon (1999).
  - The opponent axes are no longer called DKL, and the sensitivity curve is
    no longer credited to Watson and Ahumada; it now peaks at 4 c/deg
    regardless of pupil size.
  - Maps are computed in the background, so sliders respond faster.

Removed:

  - The Detection percentage, the spike raster panel and its Poisson option,
    the dominant-wavelength readout, and two checkboxes that switched nothing.
