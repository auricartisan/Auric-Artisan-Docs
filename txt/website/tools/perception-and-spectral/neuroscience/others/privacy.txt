WHAT STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER
==========================

  - Every calculation: the stimulus, the five stages, the readouts and the
    colour list are computed in the page.
  - A cone table you install is read from your disk by the page. It is not
    uploaded and is forgotten when you reload.
  - The colours you type in the Export tab stay in the page.

WHAT LEAVES THE PAGE, AND ONLY WHEN YOU ASK
===========================================

  - Download the CSV, Save the map and Download the settings create files
    through your browser's normal download.
  - Copy to clipboard and Copy a link to this view write to your clipboard.
  - A copied link contains your settings in its address (for example the base
    colour and viewing distance). Anyone you send it to can read them.
