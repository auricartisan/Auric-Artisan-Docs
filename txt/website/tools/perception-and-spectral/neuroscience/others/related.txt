TOOLS IN THIS GROUP
===================

  - Perception and Vision [../../perception-and-vision/README.txt] — the same
    dichromacy simulation, plus night vision, lens ageing, cataract, central
    loss and acuity, over your own images and with a severity control.
  - Psychophysical Experiment Engine
    [../../psychophysical-experiment-engine/README.txt] — measure a real
    contrast threshold for a Gabor patch, using the same viewing-geometry
    idea.
  - Material Lighting [../../material-lighting/README.txt] and Industrial Dye
    Chemistry [../../industrial-dye-chemistry/README.txt] — where the colour a
    cone receives comes from: light and matter, wavelength by wavelength.
