THE SPECTRUM CSV
================

The file starts with `#` header lines:

  Line:     `sampling`
  Contents: 380–780 nm at 5 nm, 81 samples

  Line:     `observer`
  Contents: CIE 1931 2°, published at 10 nm, interpolated to 5

  Line:     `illuminant`
  Contents: The light and its definition

  Line:     `measured_cct`
  Contents: The measured colour temperature and its error band

  Line:     `reference`
  Contents: The reference light's kind, temperature and the reason

  Line:     `material`, `reflectance`
  Contents: The material, and whether its curve is MEASURED (with the file
            name) or SYNTHESISED

  Line:     `surface`
  Contents: Conductor, or dielectric with its refractive index; the lobe; the
            roughness

  Line:     `geometry`
  Contents: Light, view and azimuth angles

  Line:     `exposure`
  Contents: The scale that makes a white diffuser read Y = 1

  Line:     `swatch_white`, `lab_white`
  Contents: That the swatch is un-adapted and L*a*b* uses the light's own
            white

  Line:     `rendering_shift`
  Contents: ΔE₀₀ for this sample between the source and its reference

  Line:     `NOT_A_RENDERING_INDEX`
  Contents: That this is one sample, and that CRI and TM-30 are withdrawn

  Line:     `sensitivity`
  Contents: The sweep's median and 95th percentile, if you ran one

Then 81 rows:

  Column:  `wavelength_nm`
  Meaning: 380 to 780 in steps of 5

  Column:  `illuminant_relative`
  Meaning: The illuminant's power, scaled to peak at 1

  Column:  `reflectance`
  Meaning: The material's reflectance

  Column:  `radiance_relative`
  Meaning: The light leaving the surface at your geometry, scaled by the
           exposure

  Column:  `observer_xbar`, `observer_ybar`, `observer_zbar`
  Meaning: The CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions

THE SCENE JSON
==============

  Field:    `tool`, `generated`
  Contents: The tool name and time

  Field:    `sampling`, `observer`
  Contents: The grid, and the observer's published and used steps with a note
            on interpolation

  Field:    `illuminant`, `measured_cct`, `reference`
  Contents: The light, its measured temperature and band, and the reference
            chosen

  Field:    `material`
  Contents: Its id, reflectance status and source, conductor flag and
            refractive index

  Field:    `surface`, `geometry`, `exposure`
  Contents: Lobe and roughness; the three angles; the exposure scale

  Field:    `colour`
  Contents: sRGB hex (un-adapted), L*a*b*, the white used, JzAzBz, gamut flag

  Field:    `rendering_shift`
  Contents: ΔE₀₀, marked `is_rendering_index: false`

  Field:    `withdrawn`
  Contents: CRI and TM-30, each with why and what it would take

  Field:    `sensitivity`
  Contents: The sweep result, or `null`

  Field:    `spectrum`
  Contents: Wavelength, illuminant, reflectance and radiance arrays

  Field:    `register`
  Contents: Every Data register entry with its source and status

LINKS
=====

A link rebuilds the scene with these parameters: `illuminant` (`d65`, `d50`,
`d55`, `d75`, `a`, `e`, `planck`, `daylight`), `cct`, `material` (for example
`gold`), `ior`, `roughness`, `thetaL`, `thetaV`, `phi`, `lobe` (`ggx`,
`beckmann`, `ward`, `ashikhmin-shirley`, `oren-nayar`) and `metal` (`1` for
conductor, `0` for dielectric). For example:

    https://auricartisan.com/tool/general/spectral-tools/material-lighting/?illuminant=a&cct=2700&material=skin&ior=1.5&roughness=0.4&thetaL=30&thetaV=20&phi=0&lobe=ggx&metal=0

A link does not carry a measured reflectance file or the sweep settings.

You should now have a file that records the light, the material's provenance
and the geometry along with the numbers.
