WHAT THE TOOL IS
================

A spectral appearance bench for learning and exploration: one light, one flat
material, one geometry, computed wavelength by wavelength. It is not a
production renderer, a lighting-design certification tool or a
colour-rendering meter.

THE DATA REGISTER, IN PLAIN TERMS
=================================

  Entry:                 CIE 1931 2° standard observer
  Status:                Verbatim
  What it means for you: The published 10 nm table, unaltered; every other 5
                         nm sample is interpolated

  Entry:                 CIE D-series eigenvector basis
  Status:                Verbatim
  What it means for you: D50, D55, D65, D75 and chosen daylights land within
                         0.0003 of their published chromaticities

  Entry:                 sRGB primaries and transfer curve
  Status:                Verbatim
  What it means for you: The swatch uses the published sRGB definition,
                         un-adapted

  Entry:                 CIEDE2000 colour difference
  Status:                Verbatim
  What it means for you: Every ΔE₀₀ uses the full formula, against the light's
                         own white

  Entry:                 JzAzBz
  Status:                Verbatim
  What it means for you: The published constants

  Entry:                 Planck's radiation law
  Status:                Verbatim
  What it means for you: Every blackbody is computed at the 81 samples

  Entry:                 GGX microfacet distribution
  Status:                Verbatim
  What it means for you: The published form, including Smith masking

  Entry:                 The other four reflectance lobes
  Status:                Verbatim
  What it means for you: Beckmann, Ward, Ashikhmin-Shirley and Oren-Nayar in
                         their published forms

  Entry:                 Correlated colour temperature
  Status:                Computed
  What it means for you: McCamy's formula, with error bands measured for the
                         whole chain (±11 to ±18 K below 7000 K)

  Entry:                 The reference illuminant for a source
  Status:                Computed
  What it means for you: The CIE 13.3 selection rule only, not the index built
                         on it

  Entry:                 Rendering shift
  Status:                Computed
  What it means for you: A colour difference for one material; never a
                         property of the lamp

  Entry:                 Exposure normalisation
  Status:                Computed
  What it means for you: A white diffuser in the same light and geometry reads
                         Y = 1

  Entry:                 Illumination and view geometry
  Status:                Computed
  What it means for you: From the three angle controls, with the normal fixed
                         vertical

  Entry:                 The twenty pigment reflectances
  Status:                Stand-in
  What it means for you: Invented curves; loading a measured file makes this
                         Verbatim

  Entry:                 The sensitivity sweep
  Status:                Stand-in
  What it means for you: A perturbation the tool chooses, not repeat
                         measurements

  Entry:                 CIE colour rendering index
  Status:                Absent
  What it means for you: Withdrawn; needs the fourteen measured test colours
                         and the specified colour space

  Entry:                 TM-30 Rf and Rg
  Status:                Absent
  What it means for you: Withdrawn; needs 99 samples, CAM02-UCS and a
                         gamut-area calculation

PRECISION
=========

  - 81 samples, 380–780 nm at 5 nm. Narrow emission lines (such as fluorescent
    or LED spikes) can fall between samples, and light outside 380–780 nm is
    ignored.
  - The observer is interpolated from 10 nm to 5 nm.
  - The CCT error bands are those in Compare with the reference light
    [../docs/compare-with-the-reference.txt].
  - Clipped colours are flagged; L*a*b*, Jz and ΔE₀₀ are computed before
    clipping.
  - Your own screen is assumed to be sRGB.

MODEL LIMITS
============

  - One light, one surface, no shadows, inter-reflections, subsurface depth,
    transparency or fluorescence.
  - Refractive index is a single number for all wavelengths, and a conductor's
    Fresnel term is fixed at full reflectance, with its colour coming from the
    reflectance curve.
  - The Ashikhmin-Shirley lobe is used in its isotropic form; Oren-Nayar in
    its qualitative approximation.
  - The sphere is a preview seen head-on; the view angles apply to the
    readouts, not to the sphere.
  - There are no fluorescent or LED illuminants.

WHAT THE TOOL DOES NOT DO
=========================

  - It does not score light sources (no CRI, no TM-30).
  - It does not compare two materials side by side for metamerism.
  - It does not measure real materials; it uses invented curves unless you
    load a measured file.
