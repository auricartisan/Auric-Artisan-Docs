What Material Under Light keeps in your browser and what uses the network.
Area: Website › Tools › Perception and spectral › Material Lighting   Updated: 2026-09-25

WHAT STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER
==========================

  - Every calculation: spectra, the sphere, the readouts, the reference
    comparison and the sweep are computed in the page.
  - Reflectance files you load are read from your disk by the page. They are
    not uploaded and are forgotten when you reload.

WHAT LEAVES THE PAGE, AND ONLY WHEN YOU ASK
===========================================

  - Save the spectrum, Download the spectrum and Download the scene create
    files through your browser's normal download. They include the name of any
    reflectance file you loaded.
  - Copy a link to this scene writes a link to your clipboard. It contains
    your scene settings, never your files.

THE REST OF THE PAGE
====================

The tool page loads the site's usual pages, styles and scripts, and, depending
on your cookie choices, advertising and consent-based analytics, like the rest
of auricartisan.com. See the Privacy Policy at
https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/ and the Cookie Policy at
https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/.
