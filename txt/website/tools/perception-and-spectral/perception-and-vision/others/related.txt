TOOLS IN THIS GROUP
===================

  - Neuroscience [../../neuroscience/README.txt] — the same cone and
    dichromacy maths, followed through the retina, LGN, V1, V2 and V4.
  - Psychophysical Experiment Engine
    [../../psychophysical-experiment-engine/README.txt] — measure a contrast
    threshold, the quantity a contrast sensitivity simulation would need.
  - Material Lighting [../../material-lighting/README.txt] — how an illuminant
    changes a material's colour, spectrally rather than by adaptation.

OTHER TOOLS ON THE SITE
