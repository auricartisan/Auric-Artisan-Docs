What the Psychophysics Bench keeps in your browser and what uses the network.

WHAT STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER
==========================

  - Your trials, answers and response times are held by the page only for the
    current run. They are cleared when you start another run or leave the
    page.
  - All stimulus drawing and every calculation happen in the page.

WHAT LEAVES THE PAGE, AND ONLY WHEN YOU ASK
===========================================

  - Download the trials and Download the session create files through your
    browser's normal download. They contain your answers and response times,
    so treat them as personal data if you collect them from other people.
  - Copy a link to this setup writes a link to your clipboard. It contains
    settings only, never results.

THE REST OF THE PAGE
====================

The tool page loads the site's usual pages, styles and scripts, and, depending
on your cookie choices, advertising and consent-based analytics, like the rest
of auricartisan.com. See the Privacy Policy at
https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/ and the Cookie Policy at
