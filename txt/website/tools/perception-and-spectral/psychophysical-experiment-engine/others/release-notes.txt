SEPTEMBER 2026 — REBUILT AS THE PSYCHOPHYSICS BENCH
===================================================

Changed:

  - Contrast is now Michelson contrast in relative luminance about a stated
    background, through the sRGB curve. Earlier versions built the grating in
    screen code values, so a requested 0.02 was drawn at about 0.051. Sessions
    recorded before this change cannot be converted to the new axis, because
    their exports did not record the background.
  - Spatial frequency is now in cycles per degree, from a viewing distance and
    pixel pitch you enter. Earlier versions used cycles across the patch.
  - Staircase steps are ratios in decibels instead of a fixed 0.05, and
    reversals are averaged geometrically in an even number.
  - The Bayesian and accelerated procedures are named as the tool's own rather
    than QUEST and PEST.
  - The stage shows the stimulus at the starting contrast between runs.

Added: Background, Viewing distance, Pixel pitch, Envelope σ in degrees,
Delivered / asked, At what percentage, the Nyquist and contrast-cap warnings,
and the header lines in the trials CSV.

Removed: the m-alternative forced choice, same/different and odd-one-out
tasks, which could not score a trial correctly.
