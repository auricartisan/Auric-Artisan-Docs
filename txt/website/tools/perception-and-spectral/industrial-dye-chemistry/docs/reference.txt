TABS
====

  Tab:      Lab
  Contents: Bath, light and component controls; the spectrum plot; colour,
            absorbance and wavelength readouts; the lights row

  Tab:      Method
  Contents: Four explanation views and the tolerance sweep

  Tab:      Safety
  Contents: The withdrawn hazard gate and what stays

  Tab:      Data
  Contents: The register of 18 tables and formulas, filterable by status; the
            tab label shows the count; measured-spectrum loader

  Tab:      Export
  Contents: Spectrum CSV, bath JSON and link

  Tab:      Reference
  Contents: Thirteen sources, filterable

Links in the page text (for example Method or Safety in the note strip) switch
to that tab.

LAB TAB — THE BATH
==================

  Control           | Range and step                                                     | Default
  ------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+--------
  pH                | 0–14, step 0.05                                                    | 3.50
  Path length       | 0.1–10 cm, step 0.1                                                | 1.0 cm
  Run the titration | Steps pH from 0 to 14 by 0.2 every 90 ms; reads Stop while running | —

LAB TAB — THE LIGHT
===================

  Control: Illuminant
  Values:  D65 — daylight, 6504 K, D50 — daylight, 5003 K, A — a blackbody at
           2856 K, E — equal energy
  Default: D65

LAB TAB — THE COMPONENTS AND THE SELECTED DYE
=============================================

  Control:         Component buttons
  What it does:    Select a dye; show its swatch, pKa, concentration, band
                   peaks and Stand-in or Measured
  Values or range: 1 to 4 dyes
  Default:         Weak-acid indicator

  Control:         Dye menu
  What it does:    The dye Add will add
  Values or range: Six presets
  Default:         Weak-acid indicator

  Control:         Add
  What it does:    Adds the dye in the menu and selects it
  Values or range: Up to 4 components
  Default:         —

  Control:         Mute
  What it does:    Toggles the selected dye out of and back into the bath
  Values or range: —
  Default:         —

  Control:         Remove
  What it does:    Deletes the selected dye
  Values or range: At least 1 remains
  Default:         —

  Control:         pKa
  What it does:    The selected dye's pKa
  Values or range: 0–14, step 0.1
  Default:         The preset's

  Control:         Concentration
  What it does:    The selected dye's concentration
  Values or range: 0.5–200 µM, step 0.5
  Default:         The preset's

  Control:         Reset the bath
  What it does:    Default bath, clears sweep and measured spectra
  Values or range: —
  Default:         —

LAB TAB — SPECTRUM PLOT
=======================

  Element          | Description
  -----------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Acid form `HA`   | The selected dye's acid-form molar absorptivity, dashed
  Base form `A⁻`   | The base-form molar absorptivity, dashed
  What you measure | Their weighted sum at the current pH, filled
  Isosbestic       | Green circle and label where the forms cross, when the crossing is clear
  Axes             | 380–780 nm; molar absorptivity in thousands (k)
  Drop and click   | Drop a spectrum file on the plot, or click it to choose one

LAB TAB — READOUTS
==================

  Readout: sRGB
  Meaning: Un-adapted colour under the chosen light
  Format:  Hex

  Readout: CIE L*
  Meaning: Lightness against the light's own white
  Format:  One decimal

  Readout: a*, b*
  Meaning: Opponent coordinates against the light's own white
  Format:  One decimal each

  Readout: Base fraction
  Meaning: Share of the selected dye in the base form
  Format:  Three decimals

  Readout: Peak absorbance
  Meaning: Highest bath absorbance
  Format:  Three decimals

  Readout: λmax
  Meaning: Wavelength of that peak
  Format:  nm

  Readout: Beer–Lambert holds
  Meaning: yes, A x.xx or no — A x.xx is past 1 (highlighted)
  Format:  —

  Readout: Isosbestic point
  Meaning: N nm, N nm (weak) or none in range (last two highlighted)
  Format:  —

  Readout: Dominant λ
  Meaning: Dominant wavelength, or complementary with a `c` prefix
  Format:  nm

  Readout: Excitation purity
  Meaning: Distance from white towards the spectral edge
  Format:  %, one decimal, or —

  Readout: Inside sRGB
  Meaning: yes or no (highlighted)
  Format:  —

  Readout: Observer
  Meaning: Fixed
  Format:  CIE 1931 2°

LAB TAB — THE SAME BATH UNDER EVERY LIGHT
=========================================

Swatches for D65, D50, A and E; the current light is labelled — this bath with
its hex, the others show ΔE₀₀ from it. An F11 card reads not held.

METHOD TAB
==========

  Control:         Method
  Values or range: The titration, Beer–Lambert, The light, What was resampled
  Default:         The titration

  Control:         Concentration (sweep)
  Values or range: ±0–20%, step 0.5
  Default:         ±2.0%

  Control:         Path length (sweep)
  Values or range: ±0–2 mm, step 0.01
  Default:         ±0.10 mm

  Control:         Molar absorptivity (sweep)
  Values or range: ±0–30%, step 0.5
  Default:         ±5.0%

  Control:         Draws
  Values or range: 100–2000, step 100
  Default:         500

  Control:         Run the sweep
  Values or range: Runs the sweep; shows Median, 95th percentile and Worst
                   draw in ΔE₀₀
  Default:         —

SAFETY TAB
==========

Shows GHS hazard assessment and PPE compliance gate, marked Withdrawn, with a
table of what an earlier version did and what GHS classification actually is,
and What stays, because it is true and needs no calculation: gloves, eye
protection and a lab coat for any dye work; the supplier's safety data sheet
(section 2 for the GHS classification, section 8 for exposure controls and
PPE); and disposing of dye liquor through your institution's waste stream.

DATA TAB
========

  Control:      Status
  What it does: All (18), Verbatim (6), Computed (5), Stand-in (4), Absent (3)

  Control:      Register row
  What it does: Select, or press `Enter` or `Space`, to expand its note and
                what it would take

  Control:      Form menu
  What it does: Into the acid form or Into the base form

  Control:      Drop a spectrum
  What it does: Loads a measured spectrum; click or `Enter`/`Space` opens a
                file chooser

  Control:      Back to the drawn bands
  What it does: Shown after loading; restores drawn bands

Entries: CIE 1931 2° standard observer, CIE D-series eigenvector basis, sRGB
primaries and transfer curve, CIEDE2000 colour difference, The Beer–Lambert
law, Henderson–Hasselbalch (Verbatim); The two-species absorbance, The
isosbestic point, Dominant wavelength and excitation purity, Colour
inconstancy index, Special metamerism index, change of illuminant (Computed);
The absorption band shape, The six dye presets, The default sweep tolerances,
Kubelka-Munk on an opaque substrate (Stand-in); The CIE fluorescent
illuminants (F-series), Colourfastness and content testing, GHS classification
and PPE compliance (Absent). The metamerism index and Kubelka–Munk entries
describe methods that have no control in the current interface.

MESSAGES
========

  Message: Titration complete
  Meaning: The titration reached pH 14

  Message: Four components is the ceiling here
  Meaning: A fifth dye was refused

  Message: The bath needs at least one dye
  Meaning: The last dye cannot be removed

  Message: Bath reset
  Meaning: Defaults restored

  Message: Swept N draws in M ms
  Meaning: The sweep finished

  Message: N rows into the acid form — peak N nm, ε N
  Meaning: A spectrum was loaded

  Message: No two-column numeric rows in that file
  Meaning: The file had no usable rows

  Message: Not installed: fewer than eight rows / fewer than eight usable rows
  Meaning: Too few rows

  Message: Not installed: covers X to Y nm; at least 420 to 680 is needed …
  Meaning: The file does not span 420–680 nm

  Message: Back to the drawn bands
  Meaning: Measured spectra cleared

  Message: Link copied
  Meaning: The link is on the clipboard

FILE FORMATS
============

  File:     `dye-in-solution-spectrum.csv`
  Format:   CSV with `#` header lines
  Contents: `wavelength_nm,absorbance,transmittance,eps_acid,eps_base,observer_x,observer_y,observer_z`

  File:     `dye-in-solution-bath.json`
  Format:   JSON
  Contents: See Export and share [export-and-share.txt]

  File:     Measured spectrum (input)
  Format:   `.csv`, `.tsv` or `.txt`
  Contents: Two columns: wavelength (nm), molar absorptivity (M⁻¹ cm⁻¹); at
            least 8 rows spanning 420–680 nm

LINK PARAMETERS
===============

`ph`, `path` (cm), `ill` (`d65`, `d50`, `a`, `e`), `dyes` (`id:pKa:µM`,
comma-separated; ids `weak-acid`, `mid-range`, `high-range`, `azo-red`,
`anthraquinone-blue`, `yellow`).
