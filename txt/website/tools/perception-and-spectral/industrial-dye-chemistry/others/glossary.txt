==============================================================================
DYE IN SOLUTION GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Chemistry and colour terms used in the Dye in Solution tool, each explained
briefly.
Area: Website › Tools › Perception and spectral › Industrial Dye Chemistry   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Absorbance (A)
  Meaning: −log₁₀ of the fraction of light transmitted; A = 1 lets 10% through

  Term:    Acid form (HA)
  Meaning: The protonated form of a dye, holding an extra hydrogen ion

  Term:    Base form (A⁻)
  Meaning: The deprotonated form of a dye

  Term:    Base fraction (f)
  Meaning: The share of a dye in its base form at a given pH, from
           Henderson–Hasselbalch

  Term:    Beer–Lambert law
  Meaning: A = ε × c × l: absorbance is proportional to molar absorptivity,
           concentration and path length

  Term:    CIE 1931 2° observer
  Meaning: The standard set of colour-matching functions describing average
           human colour vision for small fields

  Term:    CIE L*a*b*
  Meaning: A colour space with lightness L* and opponent axes a* (red–green)
           and b* (yellow–blue)

  Term:    CIEDE2000 (ΔE₀₀)
  Meaning: The CIE's recommended formula for how different two colours look

  Term:    Colour inconstancy
  Meaning: How far one sample's colour changes between two lights

  Term:    Complementary wavelength
  Meaning: For purples, the wavelength on the opposite side of the white
           point, given instead of a dominant wavelength

  Term:    Concentration (c)
  Meaning: Amount of dye per volume; shown in micromoles per litre (µM)

  Term:    Dominant wavelength
  Meaning: The spectral colour that, mixed with the light's white, matches the
           sample

  Term:    Excitation purity
  Meaning: How far a colour lies from the white point towards the spectral
           edge, as a percentage

  Term:    Gaussian band
  Meaning: A bell-shaped absorption curve; the tool draws preset bands this
           way

  Term:    GHS
  Meaning: The Globally Harmonized System for classifying and labelling
           chemical hazards, per substance

  Term:    Henderson–Hasselbalch
  Meaning: The relation f = 1 ÷ (1 + 10^(pKa − pH)) between pH, pKa and the
           base fraction

  Term:    Illuminant
  Meaning: A standard light defined by its spectral power: D65, D50
           (daylights), A (tungsten), E (equal energy)

  Term:    Isosbestic point
  Meaning: The wavelength where both forms of a dye absorb equally, so
           absorbance there does not change with pH

  Term:    Kubelka–Munk
  Meaning: A model of colour in opaque, light-scattering layers such as dyed
           cloth; not offered in the current interface

  Term:    λmax
  Meaning: The wavelength at which a band absorbs most

  Term:    Metamerism
  Meaning: Two samples with different spectra that match under one light but
           not under another

  Term:    Molar absorptivity (ε)
  Meaning: How strongly one mole of dye absorbs at a wavelength, in M⁻¹ cm⁻¹

  Term:    Path length (l)
  Meaning: The distance light travels through the solution, in cm

  Term:    pKa
  Meaning: The pH at which half of a dye is in each form

  Term:    Safety data sheet (SDS)
  Meaning: The supplier's document for a substance, giving its hazards
           (section 2) and controls (section 8)

  Term:    sRGB
  Meaning: The standard colour space of screens and the web

  Term:    Transmittance (T)
  Meaning: The fraction of light at a wavelength that passes through the
           solution

  Term:    UV–Vis spectrum
  Meaning: A measurement of absorbance across ultraviolet and visible
           wavelengths
