WHAT STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER
==========================

  - Every calculation: spectra, colours, the lights row and the tolerance
    sweep are computed in the page.
  - Spectrum files you load are read from your disk by the page. They are not
    uploaded and are forgotten when you reload.

WHAT LEAVES THE PAGE, AND ONLY WHEN YOU ASK
===========================================

  - Download the spectrum and Download the bath create files through your
    browser's normal download. The bath file includes the name of any spectrum
    file you loaded.
  - Copy a link to this bath writes a link to your clipboard. It contains the
    pH, path length, light and each dye's preset, pKa and concentration, never
    your spectrum files.
