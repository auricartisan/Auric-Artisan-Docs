==============================================================================
GAMUT LAB LIMITS AND ACCURACY
==============================================================================
How far the Gamut Lab's diagrams, curves, areas and volumes can be trusted.
Area: Website › Tools › Colorimetry and rendering   Updated: 2026-09-25

EXACT
=====

  - Primaries and white points of the 13 spaces are the published
    chromaticities.
  - Matrices are derived from the primaries and white by the standard
    construction.
  - Transfer curves run as their standards define them, except HLG (see
    below). The BT.2020, ROMM and Adobe RGB curves were corrected in the
    current version and are marked so on the Transfer tab.
  - Areas are computed by the shoelace formula in the named plane.

COMPUTED WITH STATED LIMITS
===========================

  - Spectral locus — built from the CIE 1931 2° colour matching functions at 5
    nm and four decimals, from 380 to 715 nm. Beyond 715 nm the published
    values are too small to give a reliable chromaticity.
  - Share of the locus — divided by the lab's own locus area in the same
    plane. Published shares divided by other locus areas differ (for Rec.
    2020: 63.4% of the xy locus here against the often-quoted 75.8%).
  - Volume — Halton quasi-random sampling in a box fitted to each space. The ±
    is the spread against a half-size run, not a confidence interval.
  - Overlap — sampled in u′v′ with point-in-triangle tests.

STAND-INS
=========

  - HLG — the lab draws the inverse OETF only. The full HLG EOTF needs a
    display peak luminance and a surround the lab does not ask for.
  - MacAdam ellipses — 24 hand-rounded ellipses approximating the published
    figure, drawn at a fixed exaggeration. MacAdam published 25 as coefficient
    triples; those are not held. Do not read thresholds from the overlay.

NOT DONE
========

  - The CIE 1964 10° observer (the option has no effect).
  - The CIE 170-1 cone-fundamental chromaticity diagram.
  - Colour conversion or gamut mapping between spaces.
  - Loading tables through the Data tab's install area.

CURRENT-VERSION CAVEATS
=======================

  - The OKLab slice stays at L = 0.5 whatever the Level slider shows.
  - The vertex readout treats clicks as CIE 1931 x, y, so its colour and
    membership are only correct in the xy diagram mode.
  - The 3-D wireframe is a fixed projection.
  - The WebGL fill's colours are an approximate sRGB rendering of each
    chromaticity.
