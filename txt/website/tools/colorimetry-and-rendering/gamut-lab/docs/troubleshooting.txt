==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING THE GAMUT LAB
==============================================================================
Fixes for common problems in the Gamut Lab.
Area: Website › Tools › Colorimetry and rendering   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Symptom:        A space I picked earlier disappeared from the diagram
  Why it happens: The diagram draws up to five spaces; adding a sixth drops
                  the oldest
  What to do:     Tick Show all library spaces, or pick the space again

  Symptom:        Choosing Custom shows nothing
  Why it happens: The custom space does not exist until you solve it
  What to do:     Enter primaries and white, then select Solve from primaries

  Symptom:        My custom space vanished after reloading
  Why it happens: A solved Custom space is not saved
  What to do:     Export it (This space, JSON) and import it with Add to the
                  library

  Symptom:        Import says "Invalid JSON"
  Why it happens: The pasted object has no `name` or no `primaries`
  What to do:     Use the format in Define a custom colour space
                  [define-a-custom-space.txt]

  Symptom:        Import says "Parse error: …"
  Why it happens: The text is not valid JSON
  What to do:     Check quotes, commas and brackets

  Symptom:        Choosing the 10° observer changes nothing
  Why it happens: Only the CIE 1931 2° observer is used
  What to do:     Expected

  Symptom:        The OKLab slice does not move with Level
  Why it happens: In the current version the OKLab slice is fixed at L = 0.5;
                  only the label changes
  What to do:     Use Constant L* (CIELAB) to move through lightness

  Symptom:        In the u′v′ diagram, the vertex readout colour and
                  membership look wrong
  Why it happens: The readout treats the clicked coordinates as CIE 1931 x, y
  What to do:     Switch Diagram mode to CIE 1931 xy before probing

  Symptom:        The vertex readout always shows L* 100
  Why it happens: The probe takes the chromaticity at Y = 1, the brightest
                  colour of that chromaticity
  What to do:     Expected; it describes chromaticity, not a particular
                  lightness

  Symptom:        The WebGL fill does nothing
  Why it happens: The browser or device does not support WebGL 2, or it is
                  disabled
  What to do:     Leave the fill off; the diagram works without it

  Symptom:        The wireframe cannot be rotated
  Why it happens: It is drawn at a fixed viewing angle
  What to do:     Expected

  Symptom:        A share of "> 100%" appears
  Why it happens: The space's primaries lie outside the spectral locus
  What to do:     Expected for ACES AP0 and ProPhoto RGB; read the volume
                  instead

  Symptom:        Percentages differ from other tools
  Why it happens: Other tools often divide by a different locus area, and
                  often in xy
  What to do:     See Compare colour spaces [compare-colour-spaces.txt]

  Symptom:        Volume figures change between runs
  Why it happens: They are sampled estimates
  What to do:     Raise Samples on the Compare tab; the ± spread shrinks

  Symptom:        The equation box shows an odd power-law form for Rec. 2020,
                  Adobe RGB or ProPhoto
  Why it happens: The box simplifies those curves
  What to do:     Use the Transfer tab or Reference › Formulas

  Symptom:        The comparison export has fewer rows than spaces
  Why it happens: It covers only the spaces drawn on the diagram
  What to do:     Draw the spaces you want first

  Symptom:        A long link warning appears
  Why it happens: Links over about 1,800 characters may be cut off by some
                  apps
  What to do:     Send the link as a file or shorten the view (fewer spaces)

  Symptom:        Copy to clipboard is missing
  Why it happens: It is hidden for PNG and link choices
  What to do:     Use Download, or Copy the link for links
