==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING TONAL STEPS
==============================================================================
Fixes for common problems in Tonal Steps, Tints and Shades.
Area: Website › Tools › Colorimetry and rendering   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Symptom:        My base colour is not in the ramp
  Why it happens: The engines step lightness or luminance evenly from white to
                  black; the base rarely lands exactly on a step. The base
                  note says "not a step"
  What to do:     Expected. Use the key sets, where 500 is always your colour,
                  or tints and shades, which start from it

  Symptom:        Changing Mix in does not change the ramp
  Why it happens: The mixing space applies to tints, shades, tones and key
                  sets, not to the main ramp
  What to do:     Tick a set under Also show to see the effect

  Symptom:        sRGB linear gives the same result as OKLab
  Why it happens: In the current version the sRGB linear option mixes in OKLab
  What to do:     Use CIELAB or HSL for a visibly different mix

  Symptom:        Easing γ does nothing
  Why it happens: Easing applies only to Equal luminance and the neutral
                  ladder
  What to do:     Switch the engine to Equal luminance

  Symptom:        The Equal contrast engine is missing
  Why it happens: It produces the same ramp as Equal luminance, so it is not
                  offered on the Lab tab
  What to do:     Use Equal luminance; the Ramps tab shows both

  Symptom:        Pressing `3` shows a ramp that looks like Equal luminance
  Why it happens: `3` selects the Equal contrast engine, which gives the same
                  colours
  What to do:     Expected

  Symptom:        The base colour field ignores my value
  Why it happens: It needs a six-digit HEX with `#`
  What to do:     Type a value such as `#D3AF37`

  Symptom:        The Metric choice on Contrast changes nothing
  Why it happens: Both WCAG and APCA figures are always shown
  What to do:     Read the column you need

  Symptom:        The contrast export ignores my Against choice
  Why it happens: Exports list contrast against both white and black
  What to do:     Use the Contrast tab for the base colour

  Symptom:        Download the result did not save a file
  Why it happens: It copies the analysis as CSV to your clipboard
  What to do:     Paste it where you need it

  Symptom:        "No valid hex colours found."
  Why it happens: The list has no six-digit HEX values
  What to do:     Put one value per line, for example `2563eb`

  Symptom:        Copying fails
  Why it happens: The browser blocked clipboard access
  What to do:     Allow clipboard access, or select the preview and copy
                  manually

  Symptom:        `1`, `2`, `3` or `R` do nothing
  Why it happens: The focus is in a field or slider
  What to do:     Click an empty part of the page first

  Symptom:        Colours differ from the Tailwind or Material palettes
  Why it happens: The key sets borrow the key names only
  What to do:     Expected; they are this tool's own ladders

  Symptom:        OKLab L values differ from an older export
  Why it happens: Earlier versions fed OKLab gamma-encoded values; OKLab now
                  receives linear sRGB
  What to do:     Re-export; new files note `oklab_input=linear_srgb`

  Symptom:        APCA values differ from an older export
  Why it happens: APCA-W3 0.1.9 now runs as published
  What to do:     Turn on Show the difference to compare
