==============================================================================
TONE MAPPING RELEASE NOTES
==============================================================================
User-facing changes to the Tone Mapping lab.
Area: Website › Tools › Colorimetry and rendering   Updated: 2026-09-25

CURRENT VERSION (2026 WORKBENCH REBUILD)
========================================

  - Tabs are now Lab, Operators, Scopes, Data, Export and Reference.
  - The Lab offers seven tone curves instead of nine. The two labelled BT.2446
    Method A and C were renamed Gamma-domain scale and Scaled Reinhard and
    moved to the Operators tab, because neither implements BT.2446.
  - The "ACES" operator is named Narkowicz fit.
  - Display peak and source peak are now two separate controls.
  - The grade mixes towards the working space's own luminance, and the hue
    control rotates hue.
  - Mark out-of-gamut pixels now paints an overlay.
  - The false-colour scale was rebuilt with a key, including the 203-nit HDR
    diffuse white.
  - Scene reference white, Highlight stress and the zebra thresholds were
    removed; the Scopes tab lists them as controls that did nothing.
  - Export now offers a 1D curve `.cube` or CSV with a stated domain, and a 3D
    `.cube` that runs the whole pipeline, with a preview and a list of the
    steps each file carries.
