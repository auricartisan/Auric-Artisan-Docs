THE FIVE COLOUR SPACES
======================

  Space          | White | Transfer curve used to decode HEX
  ---------------+-------+-------------------------------------
  sRGB / Rec.709 | D65   | sRGB piecewise curve (IEC 61966-2-1)
  Display P3     | D65   | sRGB piecewise curve
  Rec. 2020      | D65   | BT.2020 curve
  Adobe RGB      | D65   | Power law 563/256 (about 2.2)
  ProPhoto RGB   | D50   | ROMM: power 1.8 with a linear toe

The slice is drawn on the source space's white. A space with a different white
is adapted onto it with the Bradford transform before its boundary is tested.

LAB TAB
=======

Controls
--------

  Control:         Base colour picker and HEX field
  What it does:    The colour to analyse, interpreted in the source space
  Values or range: Any six-digit HEX
  Default:         #247DEB

  Control:         Random
  What it does:    Picks a random base colour
  Values or range: Button
  Default:         —

  Control:         Lightness Slice L*
  What it does:    The lightness of the ab slice
  Values or range: 5 to 95, step 1
  Default:         65

  Control:         Source
  What it does:    The space colours come from
  Values or range: The five spaces
  Default:         sRGB / Rec.709

  Control:         Target
  What it does:    The space colours go to
  Values or range: The five spaces
  Default:         Display P3

  Control:         Swap Source & Target
  What it does:    Exchanges the two
  Values or range: Button
  Default:         —

  Control:         Method
  What it does:    The mapping method
  Values or range: Chroma compression, Clip in target RGB, Uniform RGB scale,
                   Adapt white, then clip
  Default:         Chroma compression

  Control:         Compression Strength
  What it does:    How far chroma compression goes (used by chroma compression
                   only)
  Values or range: 0 to 1, step 0.01
  Default:         0.90

  Control:         Resolution
  What it does:    Sample grid for the slices
  Values or range: 60 to 512, step 20
  Default:         120 × 120

  Control:         Sampler
  What it does:    How sample points are placed
  Values or range: Uniform grid; Stratified (2×2), randomly jittered; Halton
                   (base 2/3), a low-discrepancy sequence
  Default:         Uniform grid

  Control:         Adaptive edge refinement
  What it does:    Shown but not applied in the current version
  Values or range: On or off
  Default:         Off

  Control:         Mark out-of-gamut in source
  What it does:    Draws the target's boundary on the source slice
  Values or range: On or off
  Default:         On

  Control:         Show base colour on slice
  What it does:    Rings the base colour on the source slice
  Values or range: On or off
  Default:         On

  Control:         Hue tick lines
  What it does:    Draws radial lines every 30° of hue
  Values or range: On or off
  Default:         Off

  Control:         Gamut boundary outlines
  What it does:    Outlines the source boundary on the source slice and the
                   target boundary on the mapped slice
  Values or range: On or off
  Default:         On

  Control:         Axes & labels
  What it does:    Shown but not applied; axes are always drawn
  Values or range: On or off
  Default:         On

  Control:         ΔE Playground A
  What it does:    First colour (picker and HEX field)
  Values or range: Six-digit HEX
  Default:         #735244

  Control:         ΔE Playground B
  What it does:    Second colour
  Values or range: Six-digit HEX
  Default:         #3BB273

  Control:         ΔE formula menu
  What it does:    Shown but does not change the readout; all three formulas
                   are always listed
  Values or range: ΔE₀₀, ΔE₉₄, ΔE₇₆
  Default:         ΔE₀₀

Readouts
--------

  Readout:       Base Colour Info
  What it shows: L*, a*, b*, C*, h°, and whether the colour is in the source
                 and in the target (Yes or OOG)

  Readout:       Source Slice
  What it shows: Colours inside the source gamut at the chosen L*, painted in
                 sRGB, a* and b* from −128 to +128. Click to probe

  Readout:       Mapped to Target
  What it shows: The source-gamut colours mapped into the target. In the
                 current version this slice always uses chroma compression to
                 the boundary, whatever the method and strength

  Readout:       Statistics line
  What it shows: L*, the source and target slice areas (in ab units), their
                 ratio as a percentage, the method and the sampler with its
                 resolution

  Readout:       Provenance line
  What it shows: The transfer curve, the white the Lab values are referenced
                 to, and the method

  Readout:       Probe Point
  What it shows: L*, a*, b*, C*, h°, sRGB, HEX, and whether the point is in
                 the source and the target

  Readout:       Before / After (12 Test Colours)
  What it shows: #FF0000, #00FF00, #0000FF, #FFFF00, #FF00FF, #00FFFF,
                 #FF8800, #88FF00, #0088FF, #FF0088, #8800FF and #00FF88,
                 before (left) and after (right) mapping with your method and
                 strength

  Readout:       ΔE Playground result
  What it shows: ΔE₀₀ with its verbal band, ΔE94 and ΔE76

ΔE₀₀ verbal bands: below 1 Imperceptible; below 2 Barely perceptible; below
3.5 Noticeable; below 5 Significant; 5 and above Very different.

BOUNDARY TAB
============

  Control or readout: Spaces on the plot
  What it does:       Toggles each space on the boundary tiles
  Values:             Five chips
  Default:            sRGB and Display P3 on

  Control or readout: Boundary tiles
  What it does:       ab boundaries at L* 20, 40, 65, 80 and 90 on one shared
                      scale
  Values:             —
  Default:            —

  Control or readout: Volume samples (n)
  What it does:       Monte Carlo samples per space
  Values:             5,000 to 200,000, step 5,000
  Default:            20k

  Control or readout: Measure the five
  What it does:       Estimates every space's CIELAB volume
  Values:             Button
  Default:            —

  Control or readout: Volume table
  What it does:       Space, White, Lab volume ± 1 SE, vs sRGB, Sample box
  Values:             —
  Default:            —

METHODS TAB
===========

  Control or readout: Compare on
  What it does:       The colours the figures are computed on
  Values:             The base colour; 12 probe colours
  Default:            The base colour

  Control or readout: Method rows
  What it does:       Name, what it does, tag "this tool", explanation, and
                      mean ΔE₀₀, max ΔE₀₀ and out-of-gamut count
  Values:             —
  Default:            The Lab's method open

  Control or readout: Provenance
  What it does:       Count of published intents (0) and this tool's methods
                      (4)
  Values:             —
  Default:            —

DATA TAB
========

  Dataset                              | Status
  -------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------
  Primaries, 5 RGB spaces              | Verbatim
  White points D65 and D50             | Verbatim
  Transfer functions, 5 spaces         | Computed
  Bradford adaptation matrix           | Verbatim
  Gamut boundary, ab slice             | Computed (360 hues per L*, 24 bisection steps)
  ΔE₀₀, ΔE94, ΔE76                     | Computed
  Gamut volume in CIELAB               | Computed (uniform Monte Carlo in a fitted box)
  ICC perceptual and saturation tables | Absent
  Reference gamut-mapping results      | Absent

Status chips filter the list (All, Verbatim, Computed, Absent). The Install a
table area ("Drop a CSV, JSON or ICC profile") does not load files in the
current version.

EXPORT TAB
==========

  Control: Source slice, PNG
  Output:  `gamut-map-gm-canvas-src.png`

  Control: Mapped slice, PNG
  Output:  `gamut-map-gm-canvas-dst.png`

  Control: Everything as JSON
  Output:  `gamut-map-config.json` with `sourceSpace`, `targetSpace`,
           `intent`, `strength`, `lStar`, `resolution`, `sampler`, `timestamp`

  Control: Link field, Link, Copy
  Output:  A URL with `src`, `dst`, `int` (method), `l` (L*), `res`, `sam`,
           `str` and `hex`

  Control: Map a list of colours at once, Run the list
  Output:  Table of Input, Output, OOG, ΔE₀₀ and Lab for up to 50 colours,
           with a summary

Method values in the link: `compress`, `clip`, `scale`, `adapt-clip`. Sampler
values: `grid`, `stratified`, `halton`. Space values: `sRGB`, `P3`, `Rec2020`,
`AdobeRGB`, `ProPhoto`.

REFERENCE TAB
=============

  Control                                        | What it does
  -----------------------------------------------+------------------------------------
  Standards, Formulas, Citations, Research notes | Switch sections; each shows a count
  Filter                                         | Searches all four; `Esc` clears it

Standards: IEC 61966-2-1 (sRGB), SMPTE ST 432-1 (Display P3), ITU-R BT.2020-2,
Adobe RGB (1998), ProPhoto RGB (ROMM), ICC.1:2022 rendering intents, ISO
11664-6 (CIEDE2000) and CIE 15:2018, each with a badge read from the data
register. Formulas: hard clip, chroma compression, uniform scaling, Bradford
adaptation plus clip, boundary bisection, RGB–XYZ matrix derivation, ΔE
formulas, and the Halton and stratified samplers. Some research notes use the
older intent names and mention area comparisons that have moved to the
Boundary tab; the Methods tab reflects the current methods.
