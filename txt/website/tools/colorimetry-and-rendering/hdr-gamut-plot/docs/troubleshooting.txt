==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING THE HDR GAMUT PLOT
==============================================================================
Fixes for common problems in the HDR Gamut Plot.
Area: Website › Tools › Colorimetry and rendering   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Symptom:        Changing Working colour space changes nothing
  Why it happens: The control is not applied in the current version
  What to do:     Use Overlay gamuts to choose which spaces are drawn

  Symptom:        Choosing the 2015 10° observer changes nothing
  Why it happens: The locus is always the CIE 1931 2° observer's
  What to do:     Expected

  Symptom:        ICtCp marker draws nothing
  Why it happens: Not applied in the current version
  What to do:     No workaround

  Symptom:        The Tone mapping operator menu changes nothing
  Why it happens: Not applied in the current version
  What to do:     Use the Tone Mapping [../../tone-mapping/README.txt] lab

  Symptom:        PQ does not change when I move Peak brightness
  Why it happens: PQ is absolute; the peak does not scale it
  What to do:     Expected. Choose HLG or a gamma curve to see the peak's
                  effect

  Symptom:        The HLG reading is marked Adjusted
  Why it happens: Dim or bright surround applies this tool's own factor
  What to do:     Choose Reference dark for BT.2100's value

  Symptom:        The EOTF comparison chart does not follow my settings
  Why it happens: It is drawn once when the page loads
  What to do:     Use Tone curve output for your settings

  Symptom:        My image does not appear on the diagram
  Why it happens: The current version reports the image's occupancy as text
                  under the drop area; it does not scatter the pixels on the
                  diagram
  What to do:     Read the results under Plot an image on it

  Symptom:        The image readout shows no nits
  Why it happens: A PNG or JPEG does not record absolute luminance
  What to do:     Expected; the tool will not invent one

  Symptom:        Every image shows 100% of pixels inside all three gamuts
  Why it happens: Untagged images are read as sRGB, which sits inside P3 and
                  Rec.2020
  What to do:     Read the occupancy figures, which do vary

  Symptom:        Large images look squashed in the analysis
  Why it happens: The image is reduced to at most 1,024 pixels on each side,
                  which can change its proportions
  What to do:     Proportions do not affect chromaticity occupancy

  Symptom:        Convert to ICtCp says "No valid hex colours found."
  Why it happens: Only six-digit HEX values are read
  What to do:     Use values such as `#FF0000`

  Symptom:        Download the result did not download a file
  Why it happens: It copies the CSV to your clipboard
  What to do:     Paste it into a spreadsheet or text file

  Symptom:        Copying failed
  Why it happens: The browser blocked clipboard access
  What to do:     Allow clipboard access, or select the table and copy
                  manually

  Symptom:        Pressing 1–4 or A does nothing
  Why it happens: The focus is in a text box, slider or list
  What to do:     Click an empty area of the page first

  Symptom:        My shared link opens with different overlays
  Why it happens: Links carry axes, peak, curve and plane only
  What to do:     Set the other controls again

  Symptom:        Shares differ from published figures
  Why it happens: The tool divides by its own drawn locus area
  What to do:     See Compare broadcast gamuts [compare-broadcast-gamuts.txt]
