HDR GAMUT PLOT REFERENCE

Every control, readout, option and export in the HDR Gamut Plot.
Area: Website › Tools › Colorimetry and rendering   Updated: 2026-09-25

Address:

LAB TAB
=======

  Control:      Working colour space
  What it does: Shown on the page, but in the current version it does not
                change the diagram or figures
  Values:       sRGB / Rec.709 (BT.709); Display-P3 (D65); Rec.2020 (BT.2020)
  Default:      Rec.2020

  Control:      Overlay gamuts
  What it does: Shows or hides each triangle (also used by the Planes tab)
  Values:       Rec.709 / sRGB, Display-P3, Rec.2020
  Default:      All on

  Control:      Spectral locus
  What it does: Draws the locus
  Values:       On or off
  Default:      On

  Control:      Line of purples
  What it does: Draws the straight edge of the locus
  Values:       On or off
  Default:      On

  Control:      ICtCp marker
  What it does: Shown, but draws nothing in the current version
  Values:       On or off
  Default:      Off

  Control:      Chromaticity axes
  What it does: The diagram's plane
  Values:       CIE 1931 xy; CIE 1976 u′v′
  Default:      CIE 1931 xy

  Control:      Standard observer
  What it does: Shown, but the locus is always the CIE 1931 2° observer's
  Values:       CIE 1931 2° (Standard); CIE 2015 10° (Revised)
  Default:      CIE 1931 2°

  Control:      Drop a PNG or JPEG
  What it does: Opens a file picker and measures the image's gamut occupancy
  Values:       Image files; reduced to at most 1,024 pixels a side
  Default:      —

  Readout:       Gamut chromaticity diagram
  What it shows: The triangles, locus, purples line and D65 marker; the axis
                 name is shown over it

  Readout:       Hover line
  What it shows: Coordinates, XYZ at Y = 0.35, L*, and I, Ct, Cp at the PQ
                 luminance of the Signal level

  Readout:       Gamut metrics
  What it shows: Space, Primaries, White, Share of locus, Area, in the plane
                 on screen

  Readout:       Image results
  What it shows: Size analysed, pixels sampled, distinct chromaticities,
                 occupancy of each gamut, and a note

CURVES TAB
==========

  Control:      Peak brightness
  What it does: The display peak L_W: scales display-referred curves and sets
                HLG's system gamma; does not scale PQ
  Values:       100 to 10,000 nits, step 50
  Default:      1000

  Control:      Tone curve
  What it does: The curve drawn and used for the Signal level
  Values:       Gamma 2.2 (SDR); Gamma 2.4 (BT.1886, zero black); PQ
                (ST.2084); HLG (BT.2100)
  Default:      PQ

  Control:      HLG environment
  What it does: Surround factor for HLG's system gamma
  Values:       Reference dark (×1); Dim surround (×0.95); Bright surround
                (×0.90)
  Default:      Reference dark

  Control:      Signal level
  What it does: A signal value to convert to absolute luminance
  Values:       0 to 1, step 0.01
  Default:      0.5

  Readout:       γsys
  What it shows: HLG system gamma at the current peak and surround

  Readout:       Surround note
  What it shows: Whether the HLG reading is BT.2100's own or adjusted

  Readout:       I =
  What it shows: The ICtCp intensity of the signal's absolute luminance

  Readout:       Tone curve output
  What it shows: Signal against cd/m² (log), with a peak label

  Readout:       Curve rows
  What it shows: One per curve, tagged Absolute, Display-referred or Adjusted,
                 with its defining document and details

  Readout:       EOTF Comparison — PQ vs HLG vs sRGB
  What it shows: All three on one log-nits axis, drawn at page load

PLANES TAB
==========

  Control:      Perceptual plane
  What it does: The space for the plane and rings
  Values:       ICtCp (Ct–Cp); JzAzBz (Az–Bz)
  Default:      ICtCp

  Control:      Tone mapping operator
  What it does: Shown, but does not change any plot in the current version
  Values:       Reinhard; Hable (Filmic); ACES Filmic; Uchimura (Gran Turismo)
  Default:      Reinhard

  Control:      Boundary rings
  What it does: Key to the eight ring levels
  Values:       0.1, 1, 10, 100, 500, 1k, 4k, 10k nits
  Default:      —

  Readout:       Plane
  What it shows: Gamut boundaries at the Signal level's absolute luminance

  Readout:       Boundary rings by luminance
  What it shows: Boundaries at the eight levels for each gamut switched on in
                 the Lab

DATA TAB
========

  Dataset                                      | Status
  ---------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------
  Primaries and white, 3 spaces                | Verbatim
  Spectral locus (65 xy pairs, 380–700 nm)     | Verbatim
  PQ transfer (ST 2084 constants as rationals) | Verbatim
  ICtCp matrices (BT.2100)                     | Verbatim
  HLG transfer (with the OOTF)                 | Computed
  JzAzBz (Safdar et al. 2017, own quantiser)   | Computed
  Gamut area and share                         | Computed
  Tone-mapping operators (four curves)         | Computed
  HLG surround factor                          | Synthesised (this tool's own)
  Image luminance                              | Absent: 8-bit PNG and JPEG carry none

Status chips filter the list. Install a table ("Drop a CSV or JSON") does not
load files in the current version.

EXPORT TAB
==========

  Control:      What to take
  What it does: The measurements, The curve, The diagram, The curve plot, The
                plane, A link to this view

  Control:      As
  What it does: Only the formats that fit (below)

  Control:      Download (names the file)
  What it does: Saves it; for the link, copies it

  Control:      Copy to clipboard
  What it does: Copies text payloads

  Control:      Exactly what you will get
  What it does: Preview of the payload and its size

  Control:      Convert a list, Convert to ICtCp, Download the result
  What it does: HEX conversion; the last button copies the result as CSV

  Control:      The three spaces, side by side
  What it does: Bar chart and table of Area, Share of locus and White

  Choice: The measurements
  Format: CSV
  File:   `hdr-gamut.csv`: Space, Plane, Red x, Red y, Green x, Green y, Blue
          x, Blue y, White x, White y, Area, Locus share %, plus a footer
          naming the locus area, plane and observer

  Choice: The measurements
  Format: JSON
  File:   `hdr-gamut.json`: per space label, primaries, white point, plane,
          area and locus share, and a `_meta` block (axes, peak, curve, locus
          area, observer, locus points)

  Choice: The measurements
  Format: CSS
  File:   `hdr-gamut.css`: custom properties such as
          `--gamut-rec2020-locus-share`, `--gamut-rec2020-area`, the R, G and
          B primaries, `--gamut-plane`, `--gamut-locus-area`,
          `--hdr-peak-nits` and `--hdr-curve-type`

  Choice: The curve
  Format: CSV
  File:   `hdr-curve-<curve>.csv`: 257 rows of signal and nits, with a footer

  Choice: The diagram
  Format: PNG
  File:   `hdr-gamut-diagram.png`

  Choice: The curve plot
  Format: PNG
  File:   `hdr-tone-curve.png`

  Choice: The plane
  Format: PNG
  File:   `hdr-perceptual-plane.png`

  Choice: A link to this view
  Format: Link
  File:   A URL with `axes`, `peak`, `curve` and `mode` (the perceptual plane)

REFERENCE TAB
=============

  Control                                        | What it does
  -----------------------------------------------+-----------------------------------
  Standards, Formulas, Citations, Research notes | Switch sections
  Filter                                         | Searches all four; `Esc` clears it

Standards: ITU-R BT.2020, BT.2100, SMPTE ST 2084, ARIB STD-B67, ICtCp, Jzazbz,
BT.709 and sRGB, and Display P3. Formulas: PQ, HLG OETF and OOTF, ICtCp,
Jzazbz, gamut area and locus share, four tone-mapping operators, and xy/u′v′
conversion. Citations: 17 references. Research notes explain why a file has no
nits, occupancy against coverage, the single locus denominator, absolute
against display-referred curves, the surround factor, the two quantisers, and
what "ACES" means here.
