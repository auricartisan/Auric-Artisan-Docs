==============================================================================
GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Plain-language definitions of the terms used in the Design System Generator
documentation.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    APCA / Lc
  Meaning: Accessible Perceptual Contrast Algorithm, a contrast method
           proposed for WCAG 3; Lc is its lightness-contrast value, shown here
           without its sign.

  Term:    Brief
  Meaning: The set of inputs (industry, style, target and overrides) a system
           is generated from.

  Term:    Chroma
  Meaning: How colourful a colour is; in OKLCH, the C value.

  Term:    Colour mode
  Meaning: Whether the system builds light roles, dark roles or both.

  Term:    Contrast target
  Meaning: The minimum WCAG contrast body text must reach: 3:1, 4.5:1 or 7:1.

  Term:    Density
  Meaning: How compact the type and spacing are.

  Term:    Design system
  Meaning: A shared set of design decisions (colours, type, spacing and more)
           and the components built from them.

  Term:    Design token
  Meaning: One named design decision stored as data, such as `--space-4:
           16px`.

  Term:    Deterministic
  Meaning: Always producing the same output from the same inputs.

  Term:    Gamut mapping
  Meaning: Reducing a colour's chroma until it can be shown in sRGB.

  Term:    Harmony
  Meaning: The positions of the secondary and accent hues relative to the
           primary.

  Term:    Headroom
  Meaning: How far body text's contrast clears the target.

  Term:    Hierarchy
  Meaning: Body, muted and subtle text reading as three distinct levels.

  Term:    Modular scale
  Meaning: Font sizes that grow by a fixed ratio, such as 1.25.

  Term:    Neutral ramp
  Meaning: A ramp of greys, here tinted slightly towards the brand hue.

  Term:    OKLCH
  Meaning: A perceptual colour space with lightness (L), chroma (C) and hue
           (H), where equal steps look roughly equal.

  Term:    Ramp
  Meaning: A series of steps of one hue from light to dark, such as 50 to 950.

  Term:    Ramp shape
  Meaning: How far a ramp travels in lightness (span) and where its chroma
           peaks (peak).

  Term:    Role
  Meaning: A token named for its job, such as `text-muted` or `surface`,
           rather than its colour.

  Term:    Seed
  Meaning: The brand colour a system's hue and chroma come from.

  Term:    Semantic colour
  Meaning: A colour with a fixed meaning: success, warning, danger or info.

  Term:    Token naming
  Meaning: The prefix and case (kebab, camel, snake, Pascal) applied to every
           token name.

  Term:    W3C Design Tokens
  Meaning: A standard JSON format for tokens, readable by Figma Variables and
           Tokens Studio.

  Term:    WCAG contrast ratio
  Meaning: The luminance ratio between two colours, from 1:1 to 21:1, used by
           WCAG 2.x.
