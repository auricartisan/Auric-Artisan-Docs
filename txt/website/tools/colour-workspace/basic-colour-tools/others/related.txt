OTHER TOOL GROUPS
=================

  - Accessibility and vision [../../../accessibility-and-vision/README.txt]:
    the Contrast Checker, Vision Simulation, the URL Analyzer and more, for
    full accessibility checks beyond colour pairs.
  - Image and file tools [../../../image-and-file-tools/README.txt]: the full
    Collage Maker page, Image Picker (palettes from images), SVG Recolour, LUT
    Lab and more.
  - Colorimetry and rendering [../../../colorimetry-and-rendering/README.txt]:

  - Perception and spectral [../../../perception-and-spectral/README.txt]:
    spectral, dye and material tools.
