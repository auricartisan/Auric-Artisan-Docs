What Basic Color Tools and the Colour Tools keep on your device, what they
store in your browser, and what uses the network.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

Basic Color Tools and the Colour Tools do their work in your browser. This
page lists what stays on your device, what is remembered, and what reaches the
network.

STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
====================

  - Colours you enter or generate. Palettes, gradients, conversions and scores
    are calculated in the page.
  - Images. Images you choose for Palette Studio's From Image or for Collage
    Maker are read by your browser from your device. They are not uploaded.
  - Images and videos in the Colour Tools (for example CMYK Soft-Proof or
    Animation Contrast) are also processed in the browser.
  - The eyedropper. Color Picker's Pick button uses your browser's eyedropper.
    It samples a single pixel only when you choose one, and the page receives
    only that colour.
  - Clipboard. The tools write to your clipboard only when you select a copy
    button. They do not read your clipboard.
  - Downloads. PNG and SVG files are created in the browser and saved by your
    browser's download feature.

REMEMBERED IN YOUR BROWSER
==========================

These preferences are stored in your browser's local storage on this device.
They are not sent anywhere by the tools, and clearing the site's data in your
browser removes them.

  What                                                    | Where it is used
  --------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------
  The view you used last (Workspace, Catalogue or Panels) | Basic Color Tools
  Level (Basic, Advanced or Both) and category            | Basic Color Tools Catalogue
  Favourite tools                                         | Basic Color Tools Catalogue
  Whether you dismissed the quick start note              | Basic Color Tools Catalogue
  Each Colour Tool's panel size, position and pin state   | Colour Tools
  The position of the Colour Tools control bar            | Colour Tools

Open panels and their contents are not stored. They are gone when you reload
or leave the page.

USES THE NETWORK
================

  - Loading the page and tools. The page, its scripts and each Colour Tool are
    downloaded from auricartisan.com when you open them.
  - Colour Tools data. Some Colour Tools download reference data from the site
    the first time you open a tab that needs it, such as the Color Inspector's
    Names and Psychology tabs.
  - Save to Library. Items you save go to your Auric Artisan Library. How the
    Library stores and syncs items is described in the Library Kit
    [../../../../kits/library-kit/README.txt] documentation.
  - The rest of the site. Site-wide features such as analytics and advertising
    follow your cookie choices. See the Help centre
    [../../../../../help/README.txt] and Company and legal
    [../../../../company-and-legal/README.txt].
