The glossary, shortcuts, limits, related links and privacy notes for Basic

This folder holds the reference material that supports the Basic Color Tools
guides: the terms they use, every shortcut, what the numbers mean and do not
mean, where to go next, and what happens to your data.

  File:           glossary.md [glossary.txt]
  What it covers: Colour, contrast and workspace terms used in these docs.

  File:           shortcuts.md [shortcuts.txt]

                  and the Colour Tools.

  File:           limits-and-accuracy.md [limits-and-accuracy.txt]
  What it covers: How precise the conversions, contrast checks and simulations
                  are, and what they are not.

  File:           related.md [related.txt]
  What it covers: Related tools, colour libraries, kits and Learn articles.

  File:           privacy.md [privacy.txt]
  What it covers: What stays on your device, what is stored in your browser,
                  and what uses the network.
