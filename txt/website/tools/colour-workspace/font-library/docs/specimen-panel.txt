==============================================================================
THE SPECIMEN PANEL
==============================================================================
How the family panel docks, resizes and closes, and how to use the Specimen
tab's editor, variable axes, named instances, OpenType switches and presets.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

Selecting a card opens that family in a panel. It is where you test a family
and find everything about it.

WHERE THE PANEL SITS
====================

  - On a wide screen (wider than about 1,180 pixels), the panel docks to the
    right edge as a column of the page. The page moves aside, nothing is
    covered, and you can keep selecting cards: the panel simply shows the next
    family.
    - Drag the grip on the panel's left edge to make it wider or narrower (380
      to 720 pixels; 460 at first). Double-click the grip to go back to 460.
    - With the grip focused, `←` and `→` resize it by 16 pixels, or 48 with
      `Shift`.
    - The width you choose is remembered.
  - On a narrower screen, the panel is a sheet from the bottom, covering about
    62% of the screen so the card you tapped stays visible.
    - Drag the grip up to open it to 94%, or down to close it.
    - With the grip focused, `↑` and `↓` move it in steps.
    - Tapping the dimmed page above closes it.

Close the panel with ✕ or `Esc`. Focus returns to the card you opened it from.

THE PANEL HEADER
================

  - The family name, and a line with its category, by designer, number of
    styles, ⚡ number of variable axes (for variable fonts) and number of
    glyphs.
  - ＋ Select adds the family to Your kit; it then reads ✓ Selected, and
    selecting it again removes the family.
  - Download kit downloads a self-hosting ZIP for this family. For families
    that are listed but not served it reads Installed only and is disabled.
    See Your kit, compare and download [kit-compare-and-download.txt].

Eleven tabs follow: Specimen, Suitability, Pairings, Glyphs, Features, Styles,
About, License, Charset, Develop and Use & Embed. The Specimen tab is
described here; the others are in Family details [family-details.txt] and
Develop and embed [develop-and-embed.txt].

THE SPECIMEN TAB
================

A large editable specimen, set at 64 pixels in the family's regular weight. It
starts with your preview text, or The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.
Click into it and type anything; the placeholder reads Type here to test the
typeface… when it is empty.

The toolbar
-----------

  Control:      B, I, U, S
  What it does: Bold, italic, underline and strikethrough for the selected
                text.

  Control:      Align left, centre, right
  What it does: Aligns the specimen.

  Control:      Weight menu
  What it does: The family's weights, such as 400 Regular or 700 Bold.

  Control:      Size
  What it does: 8 to 400 pixels (64 at first).

  Control:      Line height
  What it does: 0.6 to 3 (1.2 at first).

  Control:      Letter spacing
  What it does: −10 to 40 pixels (0 at first).

  Control:      Text colour
  What it does: Colours the selected text, or the whole specimen if nothing is
                selected.

  Control:      ↺
  What it does: Resets text, size, weight, spacing and axes.

Bold and italic only look right if the family has those styles; otherwise the
browser imitates them.

Variable axes
-------------

For a variable font, a slider appears for each axis under the specimen,
labelled with its name, tag and range, for example Weight · wght · 100–900.
Moving a slider changes the specimen at once. Choosing a weight from the menu
also moves the weight axis.

If the font has named instances (such as Light or Condensed Bold), they appear
as chips; select one to set every axis to that instance.

OpenType features
-----------------

If the family has features you can switch, an OpenType features box lists
them, grouped as Ligatures, Letterforms, Figures & numbers, Spacing, Stylistic
sets and Character variants, and says how many there are. Select a chip to
switch a feature on in the specimen; select it again to switch it off. The
Features tab shows each one before and after.

Presets
-------

Chips under the specimen replace the text with a standard sample:

  Preset    | Text
  ----------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Pangram   | The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog
  Paragraph | A paragraph about typography, for testing body text
  Alphabet  | a to z
  Uppercase | A to Z
  Numbers   | Digits, currency signs, fractions and other number signs
  Symbols   | Ampersand, at sign, copyright and other punctuation
  Code      | A short program with operators, look-alike characters and a hexadecimal number

Code also caps the size at 24 pixels, keeps the line breaks, and switches on
contextual alternates and ligatures if the family has them, so you can see
coding ligatures such as `=>` and `!=`.
