==============================================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
==============================================================================
Short answers to common questions about the Font Library.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

DO I NEED AN ACCOUNT?
=====================

No. Browsing, testing, embed code and downloads all work without signing in.
Your kit is kept in your browser.

ARE THE FONTS FREE TO USE?
==========================

Most families are under the SIL Open Font License, and others under Apache
2.0, MIT, Creative Commons or other terms; 191 families have no licence the
catalogue could identify. Check each family's License tab and its full licence
text before you use it, especially commercially. The Font CDN licences
[../../../../../services/font-cdn/others/licences.txt] page explains the
licence types.

WHAT DOES INSTALLED MEAN?
=========================

The family is listed for reference but not served: it came from fonts
installed on a computer, whose licences do not allow redistribution. The page
can show it only if the same font is installed on your device, and it cannot
be downloaded or embedded from the CDN.

DOES EMBEDDING A FONT SEND MY VISITORS' DATA TO A THIRD PARTY?
==============================================================

The embed code points at the Auric Artisan Font CDN, not at a third-party font
service. To avoid any external request, download the kit and self-host the
files.

HOW ARE THE SUITABILITY SCORES WORKED OUT?
==========================================

They are computed from each font's own outlines and metrics, such as x-height,
how distinct look-alike characters are (I, l, 1; 0, O), proportions, weight
range and how well it renders on screens. They are not editorial ratings. See
Limits and accuracy [../others/limits-and-accuracy.txt].

WHY DOES THE RECOMMENDER KEEP SUGGESTING WELL-KNOWN FONTS?
==========================================================

It ranks within a shortlist of about 130 versatile, widely used families, so
its suggestions are dependable. To explore beyond them, use the filters, the
sort and the collections.

CAN I TEST VARIABLE FONTS?
==========================

Yes. The Specimen tab has a slider for each axis and chips for the font's
named instances. Filter for them with ⚡ Variable under Properties, or sort by
Variable first.

CAN I SEE A FONT IN MY OWN LAYOUT?
==================================

The specimen editor lets you type, style and colour text. For your own pages,
copy the embed code or download the kit, which includes a demo page.

CAN I UPLOAD MY OWN FONTS?
==========================

No. The library shows the Auric Artisan catalogue only.

IS MY KIT SHARED BETWEEN DEVICES?
=================================

No. It is kept in the browser on the device where you made it.
