==============================================================================
SHORTCUTS
==============================================================================
Keyboard shortcuts and the command palette in the Font Library.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

Shortcuts work when you are not typing in a field. They do not use `Ctrl`,
`Cmd` or `Alt`: `Ctrl` + `K` belongs to the site-wide search.

  Key   | What it does
  ------+-----------------------------------------------
  `?`   | Opens the library command palette
  `/`   | Focuses the search box
  `S`   | Shuffles the results into a random order
  `G`   | Grid view
  `L`   | List view
  `T`   | Opens the Your kit tray (close it with Hide)
  `[`   | Makes card specimens smaller
  `]`   | Makes card specimens larger
  `C`   | Compares your kit (two or more families)
  `Esc` | Closes the command palette or the family panel
  `F`   | Meant to show or hide filters; see below
  `R`   | Meant to open the recommender; see below

At the time of writing, `F` and `R` switch their panel twice, so nothing
appears to change. Use the Filters and Recommend buttons, or the command
palette.

THE COMMAND PALETTE
===================

Press `?` to open a search box of commands. Type to narrow the list (for
example shuffle, filters or size), move with `↑` and `↓`, run a command with
`Enter`, and close with `Esc`.

Commands include Focus search, Shuffle results, Grid view, List view, Toggle
filters, Find your font, Open selected tray, Download selected, Increase
preview size, Decrease preview size, Scroll to top, Toggle light / dark theme
and Focus preview text, plus a Category · command for each category. Each
shows its key, if it has one.

IN THE FAMILY PANEL
===================

  Key:          `Enter`
  Where:        A focused card
  What it does: Opens the family

  Key:          `←`, `→`
  Where:        The panel grip, wide screens
  What it does: Makes the panel wider or narrower; add `Shift` for bigger
                steps

  Key:          `↑`, `↓`
  Where:        The panel grip, narrow screens
  What it does: Raises or lowers the sheet

  Key:          `Tab`, then `Enter` or `Space`
  Where:        Filter chips
  What it does: Switches a chip
