PRIVACY

What the ASCII Art Generator keeps in your browser and what uses the network.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER
=====================

Pictures you load, paste or drop are read and converted on your device. They
are not uploaded. Text you type is drawn and converted the same way. Every
download is built in your browser.

REMEMBERED
==========

  - In your browser's local storage: whether you dismissed the Drop a picture
    · Pick a look · Take it away strip.
  - In the page address: like other pages on the site, the generator keeps the
    values of its controls in the address after `#p=`, so a reload restores
    them. Your picture is not part of the address. If you share the address,
    the other person gets your settings, not your picture. See Shareable links
    [../../../../site-features/shareable-links/README.txt].

USES THE NETWORK
================

  - Loading the page and its scripts.
  - The Library's Save to Library, if you use it, stores the art and settings
    in your Auric Artisan Library. See the Library Kit
    [../../../../kits/library-kit/README.txt].
  - Site-wide analytics and advertising follow your cookie choices. See the
    Help centre [../../../../../help/README.txt].
