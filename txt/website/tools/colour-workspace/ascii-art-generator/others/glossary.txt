==============================================================================
GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Terms used in the ASCII Art Generator and its documentation.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    ANSI colour codes
  Meaning: Escape sequences that terminals read as colour instructions. The
           ANSI download uses 24-bit (true colour) codes.

  Term:    Area average
  Meaning: A sampling method that averages every pixel under a character cell.
           The page labels it SAT, after the summed-area table that makes it
           fast.

  Term:    Bayer matrix
  Meaning: A fixed grid of thresholds used for ordered dithering, here 4 × 4
           or 8 × 8.

  Term:    Bilinear sampling
  Meaning: Reads a cell's value by blending the four nearest pixels.

  Term:    Braille pattern
  Meaning: One of the 256 characters made of up to eight raised dots, used
           here as fine texture.

  Term:    Cell aspect
  Meaning: A character cell's width divided by its height. Used to keep the
           art in proportion.

  Term:    Character set, ramp
  Meaning: The characters the art is drawn with, ordered from least ink to
           most.

  Term:    Density
  Meaning: How much of a character's cell is covered by ink, from 0 to 1.

  Term:    Dithering
  Meaning: Spreading the rounding error between cells so that smooth tones
           appear as texture rather than bands.

  Term:    Edge-aware selection
  Meaning: Swapping in a thin line character where the picture has a strong
           edge.

  Term:    Floyd–Steinberg
  Meaning: An error-diffusion dithering method that passes each cell's error
           to its neighbours.

  Term:    Gamma
  Meaning: A curve applied to brightness before conversion. Here, above 1
           lightens the mid-tones.

  Term:    Glyph
  Meaning: A single character as drawn by a font.

  Term:    Luminosity (Rec.601)
  Meaning: A brightness formula that weights red, green and blue as the eye
           does, green most.

  Term:    Monospaced font
  Meaning: A font in which every character has the same width, so columns line
           up.

  Term:    Monotonic
  Meaning: Of a character set: each character has at least as much ink as the
           one before it.

  Term:    Point sampling
  Meaning: Reads a cell's value from a single pixel. Fastest, but can shimmer
           on fine detail.

  Term:    Sobel operator
  Meaning: A standard way to measure how quickly brightness changes, used to
           find edges.

  Term:    True colour
  Meaning: 24-bit colour, about 16.7 million colours.
