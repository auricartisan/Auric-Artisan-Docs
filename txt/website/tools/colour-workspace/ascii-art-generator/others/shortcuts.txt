==============================================================================
SHORTCUTS
==============================================================================
Keyboard and mouse shortcuts in the ASCII Art Generator.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Shortcut:     `Ctrl` + `V` (`Cmd` + `V` on a Mac)
  Where:        Anywhere on the page, when not typing in a field
  What it does: Loads an image from the clipboard.

  Shortcut:     Drag a file onto the tool
  Where:        Lab
  What it does: Loads the image.

  Shortcut:     `Enter` or `Space`
  Where:        On a group heading in the rail
  What it does: Folds or opens the group.

  Shortcut:     Double-click a chart
  Where:        Analysis
  What it does: Opens the chart full screen.

  Shortcut:     `Esc`
  Where:        Full-screen chart
  What it does: Closes it.

The full-screen view of the art closes with × only.
