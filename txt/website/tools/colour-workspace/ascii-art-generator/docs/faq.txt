==============================================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
==============================================================================
Short answers to common questions about the ASCII Art Generator.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

ARE MY PICTURES UPLOADED?
=========================

No. Pictures are read and converted in your browser and never leave your
device. Downloads are built on your device too. See Privacy
[../others/privacy.txt].

DO I NEED AN ACCOUNT?
=====================

No. The generator works without signing in.

IS IT REALLY ASCII?
===================

Only the Standard, Detailed and Minimal sets use plain ASCII characters.
Blocks, Symbols, Lines and Braille use Unicode characters, which need a font
that has them and may not display in every terminal or app.

WHICH SETTINGS GIVE THE BEST RESULT?
====================================

Start with a look: Classic for photos, Line art for logos and drawings, Blocks
for small, bold art, Fine for large, detailed art. Then adjust Detail for the
space you have. Pictures with a clear subject and strong contrast convert
best.

HOW WIDE SHOULD THE ART BE?
===========================

It depends on where it goes. A README or chat message fits 60 to 100 columns;
a terminal is often 80 to 120 columns wide; a poster or a PNG can go up to
300.

CAN I KEEP THE COLOURS?
=======================

Yes, with From image. Colour survives in the HTML, PNG (from the Canvas
render), SVG and ANSI downloads. Plain text and Copy carry no colour; Copy for
terminal does.

CAN I MAKE ANIMATED ASCII ART?
==============================

Not on this page. An animated GIF is converted as one still frame, and the
webcam source cannot currently be used on the site.

CAN I CONVERT TEXT INTO BIG LETTERS?
====================================

Yes. Choose Text as the source, type a word, and pick a font, size and weight.
The word is drawn and then converted. Batch on the Export tab measures a whole
list of words.

WHY DOES MY TEXT LOOK WRONG WHEN I PASTE IT?
============================================

The place you pasted into is probably using a proportional font. Put the art
in a code block or set a monospaced font there.

CAN I LOAD A JSON FILE BACK IN?
===============================

No. The JSON records the settings and the art, but the page has no import. You
can save the art and settings to your Library instead; see Copy, export and
batch [export.txt].

DOES IT WORK ON A PHONE?
========================

Yes. On narrow screens the art comes first and the controls follow. Pasting
and dragging depend on the phone's browser; Choose a file always works.
