==============================================================================
SHORTCUTS
==============================================================================
Keyboard shortcuts and the command palette in the Icon Library.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

Shortcuts work when you are not typing in a field. They do not use `Ctrl`,
`Cmd` or `Alt`: `Ctrl` + `K` belongs to the site-wide search.

  Key   | What it does
  ------+---------------------------------------------
  `?`   | Opens the library command palette
  `/`   | Focuses the search box
  `S`   | Shuffles the icons into a random order
  `F`   | Shows or hides the filter chips
  `R`   | Opens or closes Find your icon
  `T`   | Opens or closes the Your kit tray
  `[`   | Makes the icons smaller
  `]`   | Makes the icons larger
  `Esc` | Closes the command palette or the icon panel

THE COMMAND PALETTE
===================

Press `?` to open a search box of commands. Type to narrow the list, move with
`↑` and `↓`, run a command with `Enter`, and close with `Esc`.

Commands include Focus search, Shuffle results, Toggle filters, Find your
icon, Open selected tray, Download selected, Increase preview size, Decrease
preview size, Scroll to top, Toggle light / dark theme, Pick icon color,
Thicker stroke and Thinner stroke, plus a Category · command for each set.
Each shows its key, if it has one.

IN THE ICON PANEL
=================

  Key:          `←`, `→`
  Where:        The panel grip, wide screens
  What it does: Makes the panel wider or narrower; add `Shift` for bigger
                steps

  Key:          `↑`, `↓`
  Where:        The panel grip, narrow screens
  What it does: Raises or lowers the sheet
