PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What the Icon Library keeps in your browser and what uses the network.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER
=====================

Your searches, colour and stroke choices and copied code are used only in the
page. ZIP files and sprites are assembled in your browser.

REMEMBERED IN YOUR BROWSER
==========================

In your browser's local storage on this device:

  - Your kit: the icons you selected;
  - the chosen set, style, sort, size, stroke and colour;
  - which filter groups you folded;
  - the width of the icon panel;
  - the last twelve icons you opened (Recently viewed).

Like other pages on the site, the page address may also record how far you
have scrolled, after `#p=`, so a reload returns you there. See Shareable links
[../../../../site-features/shareable-links/README.txt].

USES THE NETWORK
================

  - Loading the page and its scripts.
  - The catalogue and the icon drawings load from the Auric Artisan Icon CDN
    at icons.auricartisan.com. No third-party icon service is involved.
  - Site-wide analytics and advertising follow your cookie choices. See the
    Help centre [../../../../../help/README.txt].

For what the CDN itself records, see Icon CDN privacy
[../../../../../services/icon-cdn/others/privacy.txt].
