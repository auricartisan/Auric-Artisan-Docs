==============================================================================
THE 45 TOOLS
==============================================================================
Every Color Science Lab panel tool by domain, with its level, what it is for
and its default model.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

Level F means Basic / Foundation (shown when the level is Basic or Both); R
means Advanced / Research (shown when the level is Advanced or Both). Every
tool opens the same panel described in The Catalogue and its panels
[catalogue-and-panels.txt]; the default model, space and theory differ.

COLORIMETRY (5)
===============

  Tool:           CIE XYZ and Lab Workbench
  Level:          F
  What it is for: Convert samples between sRGB, CIE XYZ, xyY and CIE Lab with
                  live white-point context.

  Tool:           Chromaticity Diagram
  Level:          F
  What it is for: Plot samples, white points and approximate sRGB gamut
                  positions on the CIE xy diagram.

  Tool:           Delta E Comparator
  Level:          F
  What it is for: Compare two colours by Lab distance, luma drift, chroma
                  drift and hue direction.

  Tool:           Standard Observer Lab
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Compare 2° and 10° observers, xy chromaticity and u'v'
                  uniform chromaticity. Default model: CIE 1931 observer.

  Tool:           Color Difference Suite
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Audit Delta E 76 distance, chroma drift, luma drift and
                  perceptual proxy spaces side by side. Default space: DIN99.

CHROMATIC ADAPTATION (6)
========================

  Tool:           Bradford Adaptation
  Level:          F
  What it is for: Move a colour from one reference white to another with a
                  Bradford transform.

  Tool:           CAT02 and CAT16 Bench
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Compare modern adaptation transforms with source and target
                  whites.

  Tool:           Illuminant Match Bench
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Study a colour under A, D50, D65, D75 and equal-energy
                  whites.

  Tool:           Von Kries Adaptation Lab
  Level:          F
  What it is for: Compare classic von Kries scaling with Bradford-style
                  transforms.

  Tool:           CAT Matrix Comparison
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Switch between XYZ scaling, von Kries, Bradford, CMCCAT,
                  Sharp, CAT02, CAT16 and Bianco-Schettini. Default: CAT16.

  Tool:           White Point Reference Atlas
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Inspect A, B, C, D-series, equal-energy, fluorescent, DCI
                  and ACES D60 whites.

SPECTRAL ANALYSIS (6)
=====================

  Tool:           Visible Spectrum Explorer
  Level:          F
  What it is for: Sweep wavelengths from violet to red and inspect approximate
                  sRGB mapping.

  Tool:           Blackbody Radiation
  Level:          F
  What it is for: Explore colour temperature from incandescent to daylight and
                  blue sky.

  Tool:           Emission Line Mixer
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Mix narrow spectral lines and compare against a broadband
                  reference.

  Tool:           Metamerism Workspace
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Compare similar colours under several illuminants and
                  estimate metameric instability.

  Tool:           Daylight and Planckian Locus
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Compare CIE daylight and Planckian blackbody behaviour and
                  chromaticity drift.

  Tool:           Observer Sensitivity Curves
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Explore wavelength bands through cone-like, CIE-like and
                  opponent sensitivity proxies.

APPEARANCE MODELS (6)
=====================

  Tool:           CIECAM16 Sandbox
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Adjust luminance, surround and adaptation strength to see
                  appearance behaviour.

  Tool:           HDR Appearance Lab
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Push samples through high-dynamic-range viewing assumptions.

  Tool:           Surround Adaptation Study
  Level:          F
  What it is for: Place a sample on dark, dim and average surrounds.

  Tool:           Fairchild Appearance Lab
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Fairchild-style appearance ideas: media white, adapting
                  luminance, colourfulness, context.

  Tool:           Hunt and Nayatani Models
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Compare the Hunt and Nayatani models with modern CAM-style
                  controls.

  Tool:           CIECAM02 and CAM16 Comparison
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Compare CIECAM02, CAM16, CAT02 and CAT16 assumptions.

PERCEPTUAL SPACES (6)
=====================

  Tool:           Jzazbz Slice Visualizer
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Inspect a perceptual slice for HDR and high-chroma
                  relationships.

  Tool:           CAM02-UCS Distance
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Compare uniformity estimates across Lab-like and CAM-derived
                  coordinates.

  Tool:           Uniformity Stress Test
  Level:          F
  What it is for: Generate hue and chroma ramps to see where equal numeric
                  steps stop looking equal.

  Tool:           Oklab and Oklch Workbench
  Level:          F
  What it is for: Oklab and Oklch coordinates for UI gradients, gamut checks
                  and hue paths.

  Tool:           CIELAB, CIELUV, and LCh Suite
  Level:          F
  What it is for: Compare Lab, Luv, LCh(ab), LCh(uv), Hunter Lab, DIN99 and
                  related coordinates.

  Tool:           HDR Uniform Space Bench
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Compare Jzazbz, JzCzhz, ICtCp, IPT, CAM02-UCS and CAM16-UCS.

COLOR SPACES (7)
================

  Tool:           40+ Color Space Atlas
  Level:          F
  What it is for: Browse RGB, CIE, perceptual, print, video, HDR and
                  colour-order spaces from one panel.

  Tool:           RGB Working Space Matrix Lab
  Level:          F
  What it is for: Compare sRGB, linear sRGB, Display P3, Adobe RGB, ProPhoto
                  RGB, Rec.2020, ACES and classic RGB profiles.

  Tool:           Wide Gamut and HDR Space Lab
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Rec.2020, ACES2065-1, ACEScg, ICtCp, Jzazbz, PQ, HLG and
                  scene-linear workflows.

  Tool:           Video Component Space Lab
  Level:          F
  What it is for: YCbCr, YPbPr, YUV, YIQ, Rec.601, Rec.709, Rec.2020 and HDR
                  encodings.

  Tool:           Artist Cylindrical Space Lab
  Level:          F
  What it is for: HSL, HSV, HSI, HWB, HSLuv, HPLuv, LCh, Oklch and
                  Munsell-like hue systems.

  Tool:           Print and Process Space Lab
  Level:          F
  What it is for: CMY, CMYK, ECI RGB v2, ColorMatch RGB, Best RGB, Beta RGB,
                  Don RGB and D50 print workflows.

  Tool:           CIE and Perceptual Space Map
  Level:          R
  What it is for: XYZ, xyY, u'v'Y, LMS, Lab, Luv, Hunter Lab, DIN99, Oklab,
                  IPT, CAM02-UCS and CAM16-UCS.

THEORY AND HISTORY (4)
======================

  Tool:           Newton Color Theory Studio
  Level:          F
  What it is for: Newton's prism spectrum, colour circle and hue ordering,
                  linked to modern wavelength and RGB views.

  Tool:           Classic Theory Comparison
  Level:          F
  What it is for: Compare Newton, Goethe, Young-Helmholtz, Hering, Maxwell,
                  Munsell, Ostwald and Grassmann.

  Tool:           Opponent and Color Constancy Lab
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Hering opponent channels, von Kries adaptation, Retinex, NCS
                  notation and colour constancy.

  Tool:           Fairchild and CAM Theory Lab
  Level:          R
  What it is for: Trace Fairchild, Hunt, Nayatani, RLAB, LLAB, CIECAM02,
                  CAM16, iCAM, IPT and Oklab ideas.

THE COLOUR-SPACE REGISTRY
=========================

Color space / standard lists 63 spaces: 30 RGB working spaces (sRGB, linear
sRGB, scRGB, Display P3, DCI-P3, P3-D65, Adobe RGB (1998), ProPhoto RGB /
ROMM, Rec.2020, Rec.2100 PQ and HLG, Rec.709, Rec.601 525 and 625, NTSC
(1953), SMPTE-240M, Apple RGB, ColorMatch RGB, Adobe Wide Gamut RGB, CIE RGB,
Best RGB, Beta RGB, Bruce RGB, Don RGB 4, ECI RGB v2, Ekta Space PS5,
ACES2065-1, ACEScg, ACEScc, ACEScct), the CIE spaces (XYZ, xyY, u'v'Y), LMS,
perceptual spaces (CIELAB, CIELCh(ab), CIELUV, CIELCh(uv), Hunter Lab, DIN99,
DIN99o, Oklab, Oklch, HSLuv, HPLuv, IPT), HDR spaces (ICtCp, Jzazbz, JzCzhz),
appearance spaces (CAM16-UCS, CAM02-UCS), cylindrical RGB (HSL, HSV / HSB,
HSI, HWB), print (CMY, CMYK), video (Y'CbCr (709), Y'PbPr, Y'UV, Y'IQ) and
colour-order systems (Munsell HVC, Natural Color System).
