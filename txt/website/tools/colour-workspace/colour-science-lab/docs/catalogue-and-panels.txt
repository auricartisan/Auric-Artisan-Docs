==============================================================================
THE CATALOGUE AND ITS PANELS
==============================================================================
Find tools in the Catalogue, and use every control and tab of a Color Science
Lab tool panel.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

The Catalogue view lists the lab's 45 tools as cards. Selecting a card opens
that tool as a floating panel over the page. All 45 panels share one layout,
so once you know one, you know them all; the tool you choose sets the defaults
(model, colour space and theory) and what the canvas draws.

THE FILTER BAR
==============

  Control:      Color Lab
  What it does: Reloads the page.

  Control:      Search box
  What it does: Search tools, models, spaces, illuminants.... Matches tool
                names, summaries, tags, domains, default models and spaces,
                and, for colour-space and theory tools, the names and details
                of every space and theory. Results update a moment after you
                stop typing.

  Control:      Clear
  What it does: Empties the search.

  Control:      Domain list
  What it does: All, the eight domains, Favorites, Compare, Analytics and Open
                Panels.

  Control:      Level
  What it does: Basic (20 Foundation tools), Advanced (25 Research tools) or
                Both (all 45, the default).

  Control:      Count
  What it does: Cards shown out of the total, for example 20 of 45.

  Control:      Copy link
  What it does: Copies the page address.

  Control:      Quick start button
  What it does: Shows or hides the quick start note.

The Level setting is shared with the site-wide complexity mode (the Advanced
Kit), so changing it in one place changes it in the other.

The domains are Colorimetry, Chromatic Adaptation, Spectral Analysis,
Appearance Models, Perceptual Spaces, Color Spaces, Theory and History and
Gamut and Rendering. Compare and Analytics are described in Catalogue Compare
and Analytics [catalogue-compare-and-analytics.txt]; Open Panels in Arrange,
link, save and export [arrange-link-save-export.txt].

TOOL CARDS
==========

Each card shows the domain, the tool name, a strip of colours computed for
that domain, a summary, and tags: the domain, Basic / Foundation or Advanced /
Research, up to three topic tags, and ● Open when it has a panel.

  - Select a card to open the tool, or to bring its panel forward.
  - Hold `Shift`, `Ctrl` or `Cmd` while selecting to open another copy.
  - Select the star to add or remove a favourite.

The full list is in The 45 tools [tool-list.txt].

THE PANEL
=========

Controls (left column)
----------------------

  Control:         Sample color
  What it does:    The colour under study: colour well, HEX box, Random.
  Values or range: Any sRGB colour
  Default:         #D3AF37

  Control:         Compare color
  What it does:    A second colour for difference metrics.
  Values or range: Any sRGB colour
  Default:         #2FA7D8

  Control:         Model
  What it does:    The model for the tool's domain.
  Values or range: Depends on the domain (see below)
  Default:         The tool's default

  Control:         Color space / standard
  What it does:    The space used for coordinate readouts and the dossier.
  Values or range: 63 colour spaces
  Default:         The tool's default

  Control:         Theory / observer
  What it does:    The theory or observer framework.
  Values or range: 24 theories and models
  Default:         The tool's default

  Control:         White points: Source
  What it does:    Reference white for the sample.
  Values or range: A, B, C, D50, D55, D60 / ACES, D65, D75, DCI, E, F2, F7,
                   F11
  Default:         D65

  Control:         White points: Target
  What it does:    Reference white for the result.
  Values or range: Same list
  Default:         D50

  Control:         Experiment strength
  What it does:    How strongly the model is applied to the result preview.
  Values or range: 0 to 100 %
  Default:         72 %

  Control:         Color temperature
  What it does:    A light temperature used by spectral tools and the
                   temperature metric.
  Values or range: 1800 to 12000 K, step 100
  Default:         6500 K

  Control:         Show reference guides
  What it does:    Draws guides on the canvases.
  Values or range: On or off
  Default:         On

  Control:         Apply adaptation preview
  What it does:    Turns the result preview on or off (off shows the sample
                   unchanged).
  Values or range: On or off
  Default:         On

The HEX boxes accept a partial value while you type and apply it when it is
complete, when you press `Enter`, or when you leave the box.

Models by domain
----------------

  Domain: Colorimetry
  Models: XYZ + Lab, xyY, u'v'Y, Delta E, CIE 1931 observer, CIE 1964 observer

  Domain: Chromatic adaptation
  Models: XYZ scaling, Von Kries, Bradford, CAT02, CAT16, CMCCAT97,

  Domain: Appearance

  Domain: Perceptual

  Models: Newton prism and wheel, Goethe, Young-Helmholtz, Hering opponent,

Tabs (right column)
-------------------

  Tab:           Experiment
  What it shows: The tool's main canvas, and three swatches: Reference (the
                 sample), Result (the model's output) and Compare, each with
                 HEX, RGB, xy and its white point.

  Tab:           Metrics
  What it shows: Twelve cards: dE76 to compare, dE76 after model, Luma drift,
                 Chroma drift, Lab result, CCT color dE, Source Lab, Active
                 space, Sample coordinates (with an in-gamut or out-of-gamut
                 note), Result coordinates, Theory model and Target white.

  Tab:           Chart
  What it shows: A channel and Lab chart for the sample, result, compare and
                 temperature colours.

  Tab:           Graph
  What it shows: A response graph for the domain, such as an adaptation
                 response, spectral sweep, appearance curve, uniformity check,
                 space preview, theory transform or rendering compression.

  Tab:           Diagram
  What it shows: The model path: input sample, reference white, and the
                 domain's steps through to the output.

  Tab:           Audit
  What it shows: The tool summary; a dossier of the selected colour space; the
                 current model, space, theory, whites and catalogue size; and
                 tables of related spaces and theories.

The colour-space dossier
------------------------

On the Audit tab, the dossier describes the space chosen in Color space /
standard for the current sample: its family and category, a summary, the
sample's coordinates in that space, an In gamut or Out of … gamut pill, key
facts (primaries, white, transfer function, and for many spaces the year,
standard, typical encoding and gamut area relative to sRGB), the Conversion
chain, typical uses and notes. This sample across 21 spaces expands to a table
of the sample's coordinates in 21 representative spaces.

TASKS
=====

Adapt a colour from D65 to D50
------------------------------

  1. Open Bradford Adaptation (Chromatic Adaptation).
  2. Set Sample color. Leave Source on D65 and set Target to D50.
  3. Set Experiment strength to 100 %.
  4. Read the Result swatch and, on Metrics, Lab result and dE76 after model.

Result: the adapted colour and how far it moved.

Read a colour in an unfamiliar space
------------------------------------

  1. Open 40+ Color Space Atlas (Color Spaces).
  2. Set Color space / standard, for example to ICtCp or Munsell HVC.
  3. Open Audit and read the dossier and the conversion chain.

Result: the sample's coordinates in that space, with what the space is for.
