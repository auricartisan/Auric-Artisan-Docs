What the Color Science Lab keeps in your browser and what uses the network.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER
=====================

All calculations run in the page. Colours you enter are not sent anywhere to
be computed. Exports (PNG, JSON, CSV) are created in your browser.

REMEMBERED IN YOUR BROWSER
==========================

In your browser's local storage on this device: the last view, the Workbench
specimen colour, the Catalogue domain, favourites, the last ten tools opened,
whether you dismissed the quick start note, and the level, which is shared
with the site-wide complexity setting. Open panels are not stored.

USES THE NETWORK
================

  - Loading the page and its scripts.
  - Save to Library stores presets in your Auric Artisan Library; see the
    Library Kit [../../../../kits/library-kit/README.txt].
  - Site-wide analytics and advertising follow your cookie choices; see the
    Help centre [../../../../../help/README.txt].
