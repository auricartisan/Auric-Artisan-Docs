==============================================================================
GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Plain-language definitions of the colour-science terms used in the Color
Science Lab documentation.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    ACES
  Meaning: Academy Color Encoding System, a family of colour spaces for film
           and visual effects (AP0, AP1, ACEScg).

  Term:    Appearance model
  Meaning: A model that predicts how a colour looks under given viewing
           conditions, such as CIECAM02 or CAM16.

  Term:    Bradford transform
  Meaning: A widely used chromatic adaptation transform from the von Kries
           family.

  Term:    CAT02, CAT16
  Meaning: Chromatic adaptation transforms from the CIECAM02 and CAM16
           appearance models.

  Term:    CCT
  Meaning: Correlated colour temperature, in kelvin.

  Term:    Chromatic adaptation
  Meaning: The eye's adjustment to the colour of the light, and the formulas
           that predict it.

  Term:    Chromaticity (x, y)
  Meaning: The colour of a stimulus apart from its brightness, plotted on the
           CIE 1931 diagram.

  Term:    CIEDE2000 (ΔE 2000)
  Meaning: The CIE's current formula for perceived colour difference.

  Term:    CIE XYZ
  Meaning: The tristimulus values that other colour spaces are defined from.

  Term:    CIELAB / LCh
  Meaning: A perceptual space (lightness, a, b) and its polar form (lightness,
           chroma, hue).

  Term:    Dossier
  Meaning: The panel's detailed description of a colour space for the current
           sample.

  Term:    Gamut
  Meaning: The range of colours a space or device can hold.

  Term:    Headroom (margin)
  Meaning: How far a colour sits from the edge of a gamut.

  Term:    Illuminant
  Meaning: A standard light defined by its spectrum or white point, such as
           D65 or A.

  Term:    Linked panels
  Meaning: Panels that copy each other's control changes.

  Term:    Metamerism
  Meaning: Two colours that match under one light and differ under another.

  Term:    Oklab / Oklch
  Meaning: A modern perceptual space and its polar form, used in CSS colour.

  Term:    Planckian locus
  Meaning: The path of blackbody colours on the chromaticity diagram as
           temperature changes.

  Term:    Standard observer
  Meaning: The CIE model of average human colour matching: 2° (1931) or 10°
           (1964).

  Term:    Von Kries
  Meaning: The principle of scaling each cone response independently to model
           adaptation.

  Term:    White point
  Meaning: The colour treated as white in a space or under a light.
