==============================================================================
SHORTCUTS
==============================================================================
Keyboard shortcuts and mouse gestures in the Color Science Lab.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

The single-key shortcuts work anywhere on the page when the cursor is not in a
text box, list or text area, and when `Ctrl`, `Cmd` and `Alt` are not held.
The letter shortcuts act on the Catalogue, even if another view is showing.

  Keys:         `/`
  What it does: Focus the Catalogue search.

  Keys:         `Esc`
  What it does: In the search box: clear the search. Also closes the shortcuts
                overlay.

  Keys:         `?`
  What it does: Show or hide the keyboard shortcuts overlay.

  Keys:         `F`
  What it does: Show Favorites in the Catalogue.

  Keys:         `A`
  What it does: Show Analytics in the Catalogue.

  Keys:         `C`
  What it does: Show Compare in the Catalogue.

  Keys:         `R`
  What it does: Open a random tool from those visible.

  Keys:         `T`
  What it does: Tile all panels.

  Keys:         `1` to `5`
  What it does: Bring panel 1 to 5 (in opening order) to the front.

  Keys:         `Shift` or `Ctrl` + select a card
  What it does: Open another copy of a tool.

  Keys:         `Esc` inside a panel
  What it does: Restore a maximised panel; otherwise close the panel.

  Keys:         Double-click a title bar
  What it does: Maximise or restore.

  Keys:         `Enter` in a panel HEX box
  What it does: Apply the typed colour.

  Keys:         `Arrow Down`, `Enter` or `Space` on a panel list
  What it does: Open the list; `Esc` closes it.
