==============================================================================
BRAND STUDIO
==============================================================================
Paint any generated palette across sixteen live mockups, switch theme and
density, inspect tokens, swap colours live and save the brand system.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

The Studio tab applies one palette to sixteen live mockups at once, so you can
judge it on real layouts rather than as swatches. It opens on the leader of
the last sweep; Open in Studio on any palette card, or a row in Recent
palettes on Overview, opens that palette instead.

HOW THE PALETTE IS TURNED INTO A BRAND SYSTEM
=============================================

From the palette the Studio picks:

  - a background and text pair: the most readable pair in the palette;
  - an accent: the most colourful remaining colour, and an accent 2, the next
    most colourful;
  - a surface: the background mixed a little towards the accent;
  - quieter tones mixed between background and text for secondary surfaces,
    muted text and borders;
  - success, warning, danger and info colours: a palette colour in the green,
    amber, red or blue range where there is one, otherwise the accent or
    accent 2;
  - a type system chosen from your use case and style chips.

CHOOSE THE PALETTE
==================

Available Palettes lists up to 24 of the kept palettes with their names and
scores. Select one to apply it to every mockup. Shuffle in the toolbar picks
another at random from the same 24.

Choosing a palette clears any colours you swapped in with Test.

THE TOOLBAR
===========

  Control:      Auto, Light, Dark
  What it does: Auto uses the palette's readable pair as it is. Light swaps
                background and text if the background is dark; Dark swaps them
                if the background is light.

  Control:      Cozy, Compact
  What it does: Compact tightens the grid and makes the mockup text slightly
                smaller.

  Control:      Test
  What it does: Opens the test panel, below.

  Control:      Inspect
  What it does: Outlines the parts of each mockup that use a token, and shows
                the token name and HEX when you hover one.

  Control:      Shuffle
  What it does: Opens a random palette from the first 24.

  Control:      Save brand system
  What it does: Saves the current palette and its brand system to your
                Library.

The mockups are pictures of interfaces built from the tokens. Their buttons
and fields are for looks only and do nothing. The text in them is sample copy.

TEST: SWAP COLOURS LIVE
=======================

Tick Test to open Test · live swap. It lists five tokens, Background, Text,
Accent, Accent 2 and Surface, each with its current value and a row of the
palette's colours. Select a colour in a row to assign it to that token; every
mockup updates straight away, and the token shows override.

The panel header shows the current BG/FG contrast ratio, so you can see at
once whether a swap keeps text readable. Reset removes all overrides.

Overrides stay while you work, but choosing another palette or selecting
Shuffle clears them.

SAVE THE BRAND SYSTEM
=====================

Save brand system saves an item named after the palette, for example Balanced
Analogous · Brand system, to your Library. It holds the palette, its score,
the derived brand system, your overrides, and the theme and density you chose.
See Export, save and share [export-and-save.txt].

TIPS
====

  - Use Dark and Light to check one palette works both ways before choosing
    it.
  - If text is hard to read in several mockups, open Test and watch the BG/FG
    figure while you try other background and text colours.
  - The Studio uses the same font stacks as Typography, so faces you do not
    have installed fall back to others. See Limits and accuracy
    [../others/limits-and-accuracy.txt].
