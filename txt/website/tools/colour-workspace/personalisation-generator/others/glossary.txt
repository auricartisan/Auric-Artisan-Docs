==============================================================================
GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Terms used in the Personalization Generator and its documentation.
Area: Website › Tools › Colour workspace   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Accent
  Meaning: The most colourful colour in a palette once the background and text
           are chosen. Accent 2 is the next most colourful.

  Term:    APCA Lc
  Meaning: A contrast value from the Accessible Perceptual Contrast Algorithm.
           Higher means easier to read. The generator shows its size without
           the sign.

  Term:    Brief
  Meaning: The text box at the top of the left column; the same setting as
           Preference note in Tune. Its words are matched against reference
           palette names.

  Term:    Candidate
  Meaning: One palette generated during a sweep, before it is scored.

  Term:    CIEDE2000
  Meaning: A formula for the perceived difference between two colours.

  Term:    CVD
  Meaning: Colour vision deficiency. The generator simulates protanopia,
           deuteranopia and tritanopia.

  Term:    Endless
  Meaning: A mode that runs sweep after sweep and merges the results until
           stopped.

  Term:    Leader
  Meaning: The highest-scoring palette of the last sweep. It feeds the Tokens
           tab and opens first in the Studio.

  Term:    OKLCH
  Meaning: A colour model of lightness, chroma and hue in which equal steps
           look roughly equal.

  Term:    Pool
  Meaning: Your own set of colours, up to 240. Palettes are scored on how
           close they stay to it.

  Term:    Pool target
  Meaning: The size Build fills the pool to.

  Term:    Preset
  Meaning: A starting point of five colours, two style chips and a use case.

  Term:    Reference palette
  Meaning: One of about 80 built-in named palettes the generator draws on,
           such as Clay Garden.

  Term:    Relationship
  Meaning: The hue pattern a palette is built on, such as Analogous or
           Triadic.

  Term:    Semantic role
  Meaning: A named job for a colour: primary, secondary, accent, neutral,
           success, warning, danger or info.

  Term:    Style chip
  Meaning: A mood word, such as Calm or Neon, with preferred ranges of
           saturation, lightness, chroma, warmth and contrast.

  Term:    Sweep
  Meaning: One run: sample candidates, score them, keep the best.

  Term:    Tonal ramp
  Meaning: One colour from very light (50) to very dark (950) in eleven steps.

  Term:    Use case
  Meaning: What the system is for: Ui, Brand, Poster, Editorial or Dashboard.

  Term:    WCAG contrast ratio
  Meaning: The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines contrast measure, from 1:1
           to 21:1. Body text needs 4.5:1 for AA and 7:1 for AAA; large text
           3:1 for AA.
