==============================================================================
LEARN
==============================================================================
What the Learn library is, who it is for, where to find it, and how to start
reading.
Area: Website › Library › Learn   Updated: 2026-09-25

Learn is Auric Artisan's library of long-form articles about colour: 113
articles that run, in the page's words, "from what light is to how a press
holds a colour". The Learn page presents them as a course, in the order they
were written to be read, numbered 1 to 113, with about 64 hours of reading in
total. You can start at article 1 and work through, or filter by one of seven
categories — Foundations, Physics, Vision, Colorimetry, Digital, Design and
Print — and take the part you need.

Most articles are interactive. They include live demos (the site calls them
labs) that you can open in a panel, adjust and explore — spectra you can
reshape, contrast you can test, gamuts you can rotate — and end with a short
quiz and a "Continue your journey" section that suggests what to read next
based on the article, the course order and what you have already read.

The Learn page remembers how far you have got, in your browser. It shows a
tick next to articles you have read, a progress bar, and a link to pick up
where you left off.

WHO IT IS FOR
=============

  - Students and self-learners who want a structured course in colour science.
  - Designers and developers who want the reasoning behind contrast, colour
    spaces, gradients and colour management.
  - Accessibility specialists who need the background to colour vision
    deficiency and contrast standards.
  - Anyone curious about why the sky is blue or why hot things glow.

WHERE TO FIND IT
================

  - The Learn page: https://auricartisan.com/library/learn/
  - From any page: the site header's Learn menu, Learning Hub ("Practical
    color science lessons").
  - Articles live at https://auricartisan.com/library/learn/articles/ followed
    by the article's name.
  - An RSS feed is available at
    https://auricartisan.com/library/learn/rss.xml.

No account is needed.

QUICK START
===========

  1. Open https://auricartisan.com/library/learn/.
  2. Read the line under the heading: how many articles there are and how long
     the course takes.
  3. Select a category chip, such as Vision, or type a topic into Search
     titles and topics.
  4. Select an article title to open it.
  5. Use the table of contents to move around, and open a Live demo to try the
     idea for yourself.
  6. Answer the quiz at the end, then choose a suggestion under Continue your
     journey.
  7. Come back to the Learn page later: it shows Pick up where you left off or
     Next in the course.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
===============

  - Read 113 articles in course order or by category, with reading times.
  - Search titles, summaries and categories.
  - Track your progress in this browser, with ticks, a progress bar and a
    resume link.
  - Open interactive demos in a panel that can be docked or made full screen.
  - Test yourself with a quiz at the end of each article.
  - Get personalised "Continue your journey" suggestions, pin articles to the
    top of them, and rotate for more.
  - Print an article, or download it as Markdown or plain text.
