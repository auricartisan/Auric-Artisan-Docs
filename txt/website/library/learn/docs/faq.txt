Short answers to common questions about the Learn library.

HOW MANY ARTICLES ARE THERE?
============================

113, numbered in course order, taking about 64 hours in total. See the Article
catalogue [article-catalog.txt].

DO I HAVE TO READ THEM IN ORDER?
================================

No. The order is a suggested path from basics to specialist topics. Filter by
category or search for what you need.

DO I NEED AN ACCOUNT?
=====================

No. Everything is free and open, and your progress is kept in your browser.

DOES MY PROGRESS SYNC BETWEEN DEVICES?
======================================

No. Progress, ticks and pins stay in the browser where you read.

CAN I KEEP A COPY OF AN ARTICLE?
================================

Yes. Use Download for Markdown or plain text, or Print.

ARE THE DEMOS ACCURATE?
=======================

They are teaching models built for the article, computed in your browser. They
illustrate the ideas faithfully but are not measurement instruments. See
Limits and accuracy [../others/limits-and-accuracy.txt].

IS THERE A FEED?
================

Yes: https://auricartisan.com/library/learn/rss.xml.

WHY DO TITLES SAY "COLOR"?
==========================

Article titles keep the spelling they were published with. The site's own
interface text uses British spelling.
