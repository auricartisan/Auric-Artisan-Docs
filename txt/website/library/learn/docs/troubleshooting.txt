==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
==============================================================================
Symptoms, causes and fixes for problems on the Learn page and in Learn
articles.
Area: Website › Library › Learn   Updated: 2026-09-25

THE LEARN PAGE SHOWS A PLAIN LIST, WITH NO SEARCH OR CATEGORIES
===============================================================

Why: The page could not load the Learn catalogue, so it shows the built-in
list of all articles instead.

What to do: Check your connection and reload. Every link in the plain list
still works.

MY PROGRESS AND TICKS HAVE GONE
===============================

Why: Progress is kept in this browser only. It does not follow you to another
browser or device, and clearing this site's data, or using a private window,
removes it.

What to do: Nothing can restore it. Reading past 55% of an article marks it as
read again.

AN ARTICLE I FINISHED IS NOT TICKED
===================================

Why: An article counts as read when you have scrolled at least 55% of the way
through it. Jumping straight to the end with the table of contents still
counts; reading only the top half does not.

What to do: Scroll through the article.

"CONTINUE YOUR JOURNEY" IS THE SAME ON EVERY VISIT
==================================================

Why: If the Learn catalogue cannot load, or the article is not in it, the
author's original suggestions stay.

What to do: Reload with a working connection. Also note that pinned articles
always come first.

A LIVE DEMO WILL NOT OPEN, OR LOOKS EMPTY
=========================================

Why: Demos need JavaScript and a modern browser. Some content blockers or very
old browsers stop them.

What to do: Allow scripts for auricartisan.com and use a current browser. Try
Full screen if the demo is cramped.

THE DOWNLOADED FILE HAS NO DEMOS OR PICTURES
============================================

Why: Download saves the article's text only. Demos, figures and the quiz are
left out by design.

What to do: Use Print (or print to PDF) to keep the page as it looks.

SEARCH FINDS NOTHING
====================

Why: Every word you type must appear in an article's number, title, summary or
category. Articles use US spelling in titles ("color").

What to do: Use fewer words, or try the other spelling. Select Show all
articles to reset.
