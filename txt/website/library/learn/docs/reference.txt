THE LEARN PAGE (HTTPS://AURICARTISAN.COM/LIBRARY/LEARN/)
========================================================

  Element:               Heading line
  What it does or shows: Number of articles and total reading time.

  Element:               Pick up where you left off / Next in the course
  What it does or shows: Your most recent unfinished article, or the first
                         unread one; with number, title, category, minutes.
                         Shown only after you have opened an article.

  Element:               Progress bar
  What it does or shows: N of 113 read · time left.

  Element:               Search titles and topics
  What it does or shows: Filters as you type; every word must match the
                         number, title, summary or category.

  Element:               Category chips
  What it does or shows: All, Foundations, Physics, Vision, Colorimetry,
                         Digital, Design, Print, each with a count. Select
                         again to clear.

  Element:               Count line
  What it does or shows: N of 113 · time, shown only when filtered.

  Element:               Course row
  What it does or shows: Number, title (link), category, minutes, tick when
                         read, summary.

  Element:               Nothing in the course matches that.
  What it does or shows: No results; Show all articles clears the search and
                         category.

  Element:               Foot
  What it does or shows: Links to the blog and changelog.

If the course list cannot be built (for example, offline), the page shows a
plain list of All articles instead.

Reading times
-------------

Shown in minutes per article; totals under 90 minutes are given in minutes,
longer totals as "about N hours".

AN ARTICLE PAGE
===============

  Element:      Auric Artisan · Learn
  What it does: Label above the title.

  Element:      Facts row
  What it does: Category and number, demos, reading time, subject.

  Element:      Download › Markdown / Plain text
  What it does: Saves the article text as `.md` or `.txt`.

  Element:      Print
  What it does: Prints the article.

  Element:      ← All Articles
  What it does: Back to the Learn page.

  Element:      Table of Contents
  What it does: Jump to a section.

  Element:      Reading-progress bar
  What it does: Fills as you scroll.

  Element:      Live demo button (Expand)
  What it does: Opens the demo panel.

  Element:      Demo panel
  What it does: Chips Live, Article, Dockable; Dock panel, Full screen, Close.

  Element:      Quick check
  What it does: Multiple-choice quiz; Question N of 6; Next →.

  Element:      Continue your journey
  What it does: Seven personalised suggestions; pin buttons; More like this.

  Element:      Article N of 113
  What it does: Place in the course.

KEYS
====

  Key      | Where         | Effect
  ---------+---------------+----------------------------------
  `Escape` | Demo panel    | Closes it.
  `Escape` | Download menu | Closes it.
  `Tab`    | Anywhere      | Moves between links and controls.
