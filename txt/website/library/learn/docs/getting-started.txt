==============================================================================
GETTING STARTED
==============================================================================
Use the Learn page to browse the course, filter by category, search, and pick
up where you left off.
Area: Website › Library › Learn   Updated: 2026-09-25

OPEN THE LEARN PAGE
===================

Go to https://auricartisan.com/library/learn/, or choose Learning Hub from the
Learn menu in the site header.

THE PAGE, TOP TO BOTTOM
=======================

The heading
-----------

Learn, with a line such as: "Every article on this site about colour, in the
order it was written to be read — 113 of them, about 64 hours end to end, from
what light is to how a press holds a colour. Start at one, or take the part
you need."

Where you are
-------------

Once you have opened at least one article, a panel appears under the heading:

  - Pick up where you left off — the article you opened most recently and did
    not finish; or
  - Next in the course — the first article, in course order, that you have not
    read.

It shows the article's number, title, category and reading time, and a
progress bar with a line such as 12 of 113 read · about 58 hours to go.

Search and categories
---------------------

  - Search titles and topics — type one or more words. An article is shown
    when every word appears in its number, title, summary or category. The
    list updates as you type.
  - Category chips — All and the seven categories, in the order the course
    introduces them: Foundations, Physics, Vision, Colorimetry, Digital,
    Design, Print. Each chip shows how many articles it holds. Select a chip
    to show only that category; select it again (or All) to show everything.

When you have narrowed the list, a line shows how much is left, for example 17
of 113 · about 11 hours.

The course list
---------------

One row per article, in course order:

  - the article's number;
  - its title (select it to open the article);
  - its category and reading time, for example Vision and 55 min;
  - a tick if you have read it;
  - a one-sentence summary.

The article marked as next for you is highlighted.

If nothing matches your search, the page says Nothing in the course matches
that. with a Show all articles button that clears the search and the category.

The foot of the page
--------------------

A note points to the blog [../../blog/README.txt] for shorter notes on what is
being built, and the changelog [../../changelog/README.txt] for release
detail.

HOW "READ" IS DECIDED
=====================

An article counts as read once you have scrolled at least 55% of the way
through it. The page remembers the furthest point you reached in each article,
in this browser. See Privacy [../others/privacy.txt].

YOUR FIRST TASK: START THE COURSE
=================================

  1. Open the Learn page.
  2. Select article 1, What Color Is and How Humans See It.
  3. Read it, trying the live demos as you go.
  4. Scroll to the end and answer the quiz.
  5. Return to the Learn page. Article 1 now shows a tick, and Next in the
     course points to article 2.

Result: you have started the course, and the Learn page will keep your place
in this browser.

For the full list of articles, see the Article catalogue
[article-catalog.txt].
