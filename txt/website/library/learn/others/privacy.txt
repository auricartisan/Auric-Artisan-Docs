WHAT STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER
==========================

  What:             How far you scrolled in each article, and when
  Why:              To tick read articles, show your progress and offer Pick
                    up where you left off.
  How to remove it: Clear this site's data.

  What:             Articles you pinned in Continue your journey
  Why:              To keep them at the front of the suggestions.
  How to remove it: Unpin them, or clear this site's data.

None of this is sent to Auric Artisan or linked to an account.

WHAT USES THE NETWORK
=====================

  - Pages, images and the Learn catalogue are downloaded from
    auricartisan.com.
  - Demos run in your browser.
  - Download builds the file in your browser.

Advertising and analytics follow your consent choices. See Company and legal
