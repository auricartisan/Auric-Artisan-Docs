==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
==============================================================================
Symptoms, causes and fixes for problems in the Accessibility Library.
Area: Website › Library › Colour libraries › Accessibility Library   Updated: 2026-09-25

FEWER THAN TWELVE PICKS APPEAR
==============================

Why: Some hues cannot reach the target on this ground at any lightness, or
their pick would land just under the target. Those hues are left out rather
than showing a failing colour.

What to do: Lower the target, or choose a ground further from mid-grey (very
dark or very light).

"UNREACHABLE AT THIS HUE"
=========================

Why: On a mid-tone ground, some hues never reach the target however light or
dark they are.

What to do: Move to a different hue, change the ground, or use Swap to explore
from the other side.

TERRITORY IS VERY SMALL
=======================

Why: Mid-tone grounds leave little room: text must be much lighter or much
darker, and at some hues neither is possible.

What to do: Use a darker or lighter ground if the design allows. Territory
tells you how much room each ground leaves.

PASTED COLOURS DO NOT APPEAR
============================

Why: Only hex codes that start with `#` are read, with 3 to 6 digits. Names,
`rgb()` and hex codes without `#` are ignored. Only the first 24 colours are
placed.

What to do: Paste hex codes with `#`, for example `#6B7280`.

A COLOUR PASSES HERE BUT FAILS IN AN AUDIT TOOL
===============================================

Why: This page measures against the ground you chose, with the WCAG 2 formula.
An audit tool may use the actual background behind the text (including images
or transparency), text size, or a different method such as APCA.

What to do: Check that the ground matches the real background, and read the
APCA figure as well as the ratio.

"COPY FAILED"
=============

Why: Your browser blocked clipboard access.

What to do: Click inside the page and try again, or allow clipboard access for
auricartisan.com.

THE BROWSE GRID SHOWS FEW CARDS
===============================

Why: With Pairs that pass, most random pairs are skipped; at 7:1 even fewer
qualify.

What to do: Keep scrolling — the page keeps scanning — or choose Everything.

THE INDEX LOOKUP SAYS "NOT A RECORD ID"
=======================================

Why: Record ids look like `acc_` followed by digits and letters, for example
`acc_91`.

What to do: Type the id exactly, or type the record's number.
