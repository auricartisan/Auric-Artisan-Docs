==============================================================================
SHADE LIBRARY GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Definitions of the terms used in the Shade Library.
Area: Website › Library › Colour libraries › Shade Library   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Auto text
  Meaning: The better of near-black (`#111111`) or white text for a token:
           near-black on tokens with luminance above 0.5, white otherwise.

  Term:    Base
  Meaning: The colour a scale is built around, usually near its middle.

  Term:    Contrast ratio
  Meaning: The WCAG 2 lightness-difference measure, 1:1 to 21:1. 4.5:1 is AA
           for body text; 3:1 for large text.

  Term:    Even steps
  Meaning: A scale in which no step between neighbouring tokens is much larger
           than the average step.

  Term:    Min adjacent contrast
  Meaning: The contrast between the two most similar neighbouring tokens.

  Term:    OKLCH
  Meaning: A perceptual colour space: lightness (0 to 1), chroma
           (colourfulness) and hue angle.

  Term:    Profile
  Meaning: The Shade summary badge: Neutral, Hue Shift, Light, Deep or
           Balanced.

  Term:    Scale (shade system, ramp)
  Meaning: One colour taken through lightness in named steps.

  Term:    Shade
  Meaning: A darker version of a colour, towards black.

  Term:    Tint
  Meaning: A lighter version of a colour, towards white.

  Term:    Token
  Meaning: A named step in a scale, such as `500`.
