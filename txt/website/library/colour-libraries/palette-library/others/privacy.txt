WHAT STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER
==========================

  What:             Saved palette ids (up to 500)
  Why:              To fill the Saved tab.
  How to remove it: Unsave each palette, or clear this site's data.

  What:             A copy of each saved palette in your Library
  Why:              So it appears with your other saved items.
  How to remove it: Unsave it, or remove it in the Library.

  What:             Whether you dismissed the Automate this note
  Why:              So it stays hidden.
  How to remove it: Clear this site's data.

Your searches, filters and sort choices are handled in your browser and are
not remembered after you leave.

WHAT USES THE NETWORK
=====================

  - When the page opens, it downloads a short description of the collection
    from auricartisan.com. Palettes themselves are worked out in your browser.
  - Open in Coolors opens coolors.co in a new tab and passes it the palette's
    five hex codes in the address. Coolors is a separate service with its own
    privacy policy.
  - Download all in the design tokens dialog may download a small helper from
    auricartisan.com to build the ZIP file.
  - Share links contain the palette's id and colours in the link itself;
    nothing is uploaded when you create one.

Advertising and analytics on the site follow your consent choices. See Company
and legal [../../../../company-and-legal/README.txt].
