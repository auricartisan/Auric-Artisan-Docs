WHAT STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER
==========================

  What:             Saved colours
  Why:              To fill the Saved tab and mark stars on tiles.
  How to remove it: Unsave each colour, or clear this site's data.

  What:             A copy of each saved colour in your Library
  Why:              So saved colours appear alongside your other saved items.
  How to remove it: Unsave the colour, or remove it in the Library.

  What:             Whether you dismissed the Automate this note
  Why:              So it stays hidden.
  How to remove it: Clear this site's data.

The colours you type into Start from a colour you already have, your searches,
filters and chip choices are worked out in your browser and are not sent
anywhere. They are not remembered after you leave the page.

WHAT USES THE NETWORK
=====================

  - When the page opens, it downloads the atlas from auricartisan.com.
  - When you open a colour, it may download that colour's full record from
    auricartisan.com.
  - Downloads you make (JSON, SVG, PNG, CSS and so on) are created in your
    browser and saved straight to your device.
  - Copying writes to your device's clipboard only.

The page can show advertising, and the site's analytics run only with your
consent. See Company and legal [../../../../company-and-legal/README.txt] for
the privacy policy and your consent choices.
