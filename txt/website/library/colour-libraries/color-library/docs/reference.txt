START FROM A COLOUR YOU ALREADY HAVE
====================================

  Control:      Colour field
  What it does: Takes a colour in any CSS format; results update as you type.
  Values:       Hex (3, 4, 6, 8 digits), `rgb()`, `hsl()`, `hwb()`, `lab()`,
                `lch()`, `oklab()`, `oklch()`, `color()` (`srgb`,
                `srgb-linear`, `display-p3`), 148 CSS names, three bare
                numbers
  Default:      `#D3AF37`

  Control:      Swatch
  What it does: Shows the colour the field was read as.
  Values:       —
  Default:      —

  Control:      Find its neighbours
  What it does: Runs the match now.
  Values:       —
  Default:      —

  Control:      Result cells
  What it does: Your colour, then the five nearest atlas colours with ΔE and
                contrast. Select a neighbour to open it.
  Values:       —
  Default:      —

  Control:      Neighbourhood sentence
  What it does: One of three verdicts on whether nearby colours carry text.
  Values:       —
  Default:      —

ΔE words
--------

  ΔE (CIE76)     | Word
  ---------------+-------------------
  Under 2.3      | indistinguishable
  2.3 to under 5 | a close match
  5 to under 10  | visibly different
  10 or more     | a different colour

OR FROM WHAT IT HAS TO DO
=========================

  Chip              | Test                            | Count alone
  ------------------+---------------------------------+------------
  Readable on white | Contrast on white 4.5:1 or more | 2,998
  Readable on black | Contrast on black 4.5:1 or more | 5,344
  Vivid             | OKLCH chroma 0.18 or more       | 2,707
  Muted             | OKLCH chroma below 0.06         | 695
  Calm              | Emotion label `calm`            | 843
  For typography    | Design usage `typography`       | 819
  As an accent      | Design tag `accent`             | 822

Chips combine with AND. Each chip's number is what would remain if you toggled
it. The line under the chips reports the matching count and its percentage of
8,192.

BROWSE THE ATLAS
================

  Control:      Search by hex (#a6e223) or id…
  What it does: Every word must match the hex, id, emotion, art movement, mood
                or design usage. Part-words match.
  Default:      Empty

  Control:      Shuffle
  What it does: Sets the sort to Random.
  Default:      —

  Control:      Open a random colour
  What it does: Opens any colour from the whole atlas.
  Default:      —

  Control:      Filter inside results box
  What it does: A second search, combined with the first.
  Default:      Empty

  Control:      Clear
  What it does: Empties the Filter inside results box.
  Default:      —

FILTERS (LIBRARY TAB)
=====================

  Filter:  Hue
  Options: All hues
  Test:    No test.

  Filter:  -
  Options: Red
  Test:    HSL hue 340° to under 20°

  Filter:  -
  Options: Orange
  Test:    20° to under 50°

  Filter:  -
  Options: Yellow
  Test:    50° to under 80°

  Filter:  -
  Options: Green
  Test:    80° to under 160°

  Filter:  -
  Options: Cyan
  Test:    160° to under 200°

  Filter:  -
  Options: Blue
  Test:    200° to under 260°

  Filter:  -
  Options: Purple
  Test:    260° to under 300°

  Filter:  -
  Options: Pink
  Test:    300° to under 340°

  Filter:  -
  Options: Neutral / Gray
  Test:    OKLCH chroma below 0.04 (the hue options above need 0.04 or more)

  Filter:  Lightness
  Options: Any lightness, Dark (L < 35)**, **Mid (35–70)**, **Light (> 70)
  Test:    CIE L*

  Filter:  Chroma
  Options: Any chroma, Muted (below 0.07), Balanced (0.07 to 0.15), Vivid
           (above 0.15)
  Test:    OKLCH chroma

  Filter:  Emotion
  Options: All emotions, calm, cheerful, cool, elegant, energetic, melancholy,
           mysterious, playful, romantic, warm
  Test:    Emotion label

  Filter:  Art movement
  Options: All movements, Art Deco, Bauhaus, De Stijl, Fauvism, Impressionism,
           Minimalism, Pop Art, Romanticism
  Test:    Art movement label

  Filter:  AI mood
  Options: All moods, dark, dreamy, futuristic, industrial, intense, joyful,
           luxurious, nostalgic, organic, serene
  Test:    Mood label

  Filter:  Design usage
  Options: All usages, data-viz, gradient-end, gradient-start, icon-fill,
           illustration, packaging, shadow, typography, ui-surface, web-hero
  Test:    Design usage label

  Filter:  WCAG
  Options: Any WCAG, AA on white, AAA on white, AA on black, AAA on black
  Test:    AA: 4.5:1 or more; AAA: 7:1 or more

The design tag (CTA, accent, background, earth-tone, jewel-tone, muted,
neutral, pastel, primary, vibrant) has no filter of its own; the As an accent
chip tests one of its values.

SORT BY
=======

  Option         | Order
  ---------------+-----------------------------------
  Sort: original | By atlas id (default).
  Hue (rainbow)  | By HSL hue, ascending.
  Lightness ↑    | By OKLCH lightness, darkest first.
  Lightness ↓    | Lightest first.
  Chroma ↓       | Highest chroma first.
  Luminance ↓    | Highest relative luminance first.
  Random         | Shuffled.

LIBRARY TAB BUTTONS AND GRID
============================

  Control:      Reset
  What it does: Empties both searches, resets every filter and sets Sort:
                original. Chips are kept.

  Control:      Export visible
  What it does: Opens the Export tab.

  Control:      Tile
  What it does: Shows the colour and hex; select it, or press `Enter` or
                `Space` on it, to open the colour.

  Control:      Tile footer
  What it does: Hex code, emotion label, save star (☆ to save, ★ to unsave).

  Control:      Range line
  What it does: For example 1–48 of 8,192 colours; Nothing to show when empty.

  Control:      Back to top
  What it does: Scrolls the grid to its first colour.

THE COLOUR TAB
==============

  Block:    Identity
  Contents: Swatch, hex, Atlas id and labels; Copy and Save buttons.

  Block:    What it can carry
  Contents: Text specimens on white and black with ratios; a one-sentence
            verdict.

  Block:    Under other eyes
  Contents: Normal vision, Protanopia (red-blind), Deuteranopia (green-blind),
            Tritanopia (blue-blind), each with a hex code.

  Block:    Every space, and the metrics
  Contents: RGB, HSL, CIE L*a*b*, OKLab, OKLCH, CMYK, Relative luminance,
            Chroma, Vividness, Perceptual uniformity.

  Block:    Nearest in the atlas
  Contents: Five nearest colours with ΔE and a word.

MESSAGES
========

  Message: Loading color atlas…
  When:    While the atlas loads.

  Message: Could not load color atlas
  When:    The atlas failed to load; the reason follows.

  Message: No colors match these filters
  When:    Nothing matches; with the hint Try clearing the search or selecting
           "Any" on filters.

  Message: Not a colour this reads.
  When:    The colour field could not read your text.

  Message: No colour open
  When:    The colour tab before any colour is opened.

  Message: No color selected.
  When:    Export before any colour is opened.

  Message: No colors
  When:    Stats with nothing matching.

  Message: No saved colors yet
  When:    Saved with nothing saved.

  Message: HEX copied, RGB copied, HSL copied, Lab copied, OKLCH copied, CSS
           variables copied
  When:    After a copy.

  Message: Downloaded and a file name
  When:    After a bulk download.

  Message: PNG downloaded
  When:    After a PNG download.

  Message: Could not encode PNG
  When:    The browser could not make the PNG.

  Message: Nothing to export
  When:    A bulk export with no matching colours.

KEYBOARD
========

  Key                | Action
  -------------------+--------------------------------------------------------
  `/`                | Focus the main search box (when not typing in a field).
  `Enter` or `Space` | Open the focused tile.
  `Tab`              | Move between controls and tiles.
