HOW OFTEN ARE POSTS PUBLISHED?
==============================

Occasionally. The page describes them as "occasional posts". There were three
at the time of writing.

WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE BLOG, THE CHANGELOG AND LEARN?
=================================================================

The blog explains plans and reasoning. The changelog
[../../changelog/README.txt] records what changed in each release. Learn
[../../learn/README.txt] holds the long-form colour-science course.

CAN I COMMENT?
==============

Posts do not have comments. The welcome post invites questions and topic ideas
by email or social media; see Company and legal
[../../../company-and-legal/README.txt] for contact details.
