==============================================================================
GETTING STARTED
==============================================================================
Read the Auric Artisan blog page and its posts, print a post, and follow new
posts by RSS.
Area: Website › Library › Blog   Updated: 2026-09-25

THE BLOG PAGE
=============

Open https://auricartisan.com/library/blog/.

  - The heading Blog and the line "Occasional posts about what is being built
    and why — roadmaps, product notes, and the odd essay on colour", followed
    by a count such as Three so far.
  - The newest post, shown large: its date (for example 27 July 2026), its
    category, its title, its opening paragraph, an illustration and Read the
    post.
  - Earlier — the other posts, newest first, each with date, category, title
    and a one-line summary.
  - A footer that points to the changelog [../../changelog/README.txt] for
    release detail and Learn [../../learn/README.txt] for longer
    colour-science writing, and an RSS link.

If the list of posts cannot be loaded, the page shows The blog could not be
loaded. and a plain list of All posts still links to every post.

READING A POST
==============

Each post has:

  Part:         Date, Author, Category
  What it does: When it was written, by whom, and what kind of post it is.

  Part:         Print
  What it does: Prints the post.

  Part:         ← Back to Blog
  What it does: Returns to the blog page.

  Part:         Overview
  What it does: A short summary of the post.

  Part:         Table of Contents
  What it does: Links to each section.

  Part:         Sections
  What it does: The post itself. Each section heading can be selected to fold
                the section away or open it again.

Some older posts open with a dated update note, for example to say that much
of a roadmap has since shipped.

FOLLOW NEW POSTS
================

Add https://auricartisan.com/library/blog/rss.xml to your news reader. New
posts appear there automatically.

RESULT
======

You can read every post, keep it on paper, and hear about the next one.
