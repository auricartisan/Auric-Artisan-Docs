==============================================================================
COLLECTIONS GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Definitions of the terms used on the Collections page.
Area: Website › Library › Collections   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Card
  Meaning: One tool's tile in the grid: artwork, category, name, description
           and a pin button.

  Term:    Catalogue order
  Meaning: The fixed order in which tools are listed in the catalogue. The
           Reference lists all 30 tools in this order.

  Term:    Category
  Meaning: The broad group a tool belongs to, such as Colorimetry or
           Utilities. Each tool has exactly one.

  Term:    Card menu
  Meaning: The menu that opens when you right-click a card, with card actions
           and grid settings.

  Term:    Columns
  Meaning: How many cards sit side by side in grid view. Auto fits the screen;
           2, 3 or 4 force a number.

  Term:    Density
  Meaning: The spacing between cards. Cozy is normal; Compact is tighter.

  Term:    Everything
  Meaning: The tool-visibility setting that shows all 30 tools.

  Term:    Frecency
  Meaning: A mix of frequency and recency: how often you opened a tool,
           weighted towards recent opens. The Most used order is based on it.

  Term:    Mode
  Meaning: A tool's level: Basic (everyday), Advanced (scientific or
           power-user) or Both (shown in every setting).

  Term:    Open
  Meaning: Selecting a card to go to the tool. Each open is counted towards
           Most used.

  Term:    Pin
  Meaning: A mark you put on a tool so it is grouped under Pinned and comes
           first in Start here and Pinned first.

  Term:    Reading mode
  Meaning: A site setting that makes pages calmer and easier to read. The card
           menu's Reading switch turns it on and off.

  Term:    Start here
  Meaning: The slider of three featured tools near the top of the page.

  Term:    Tool visibility
  Meaning: The site-wide setting, chosen in the Advanced Kit, that decides
           which tools appear: Basic, Advanced, Everything or Custom.

  Term:    Topic
  Meaning: A short tag on a tool, such as `palette` or `hdr`. A tool can have
           several. The Topics filter lists up to 14 of the most common.
