==============================================================================
COLLECTIONS
==============================================================================
What the Collections page is, who it is for, and how to find, pin and open any
Auric Artisan tool from it.
Area: Website › Library › Collections   Updated: 2026-09-25

Collections is the catalogue of Auric Artisan's tools. It lists every tool,
engine and generator on the site — from the URL Analyzer and Vision Simulation
to the Font Library, LUT Lab and the Portfolio Builder — as a grid of cards
you can search, filter, sort and pin. Each card says what the tool does in one
sentence and opens the tool with one click.

The page learns a little from how you use it. Tools you pin stay easy to
reach, tools you open often can rise to the top of the Most used order, and
the Recommended order leans on both. All of this is remembered only in the
browser you are using; nothing about your pins or opens is sent to an account.

Every tool in the collection is free to use on every plan. The lower part of
the page shows the plans, which add conveniences such as cloud sync and
watermark-free exports rather than unlocking tools.

WHO IT IS FOR
=============

  - Anyone new to Auric Artisan who wants to see everything the site offers.
  - Regular users who want a fast way back to the tools they use most.
  - Designers, developers, accessibility specialists and colour-science
    students who want to browse by category (Accessibility, Colorimetry, Gamut
    & Rendering, Perception, Spectral and more) or by topic.

WHERE TO FIND IT
================

  - Address: https://auricartisan.com/collections/
  - From any page: open the Tools menu in the site header and choose All
    Tools.
  - It works in any modern browser. You do not need an account.

QUICK START
===========

  1. Open https://auricartisan.com/collections/.
  2. Look at Start here for a short list of three featured tools, or scroll to
     All tools.
  3. Type a word such as `gradient` or `contrast` into the Search tools... box
     above the grid.
  4. Tick a box under Category or Topics in the Filter and sort panel to
     narrow the list.
  5. Select the pin button on a card to pin that tool; it now appears under
     the Pinned category.
  6. Right-click a card to open the card menu: switch View, Density, Columns
     or Sort, or copy the tool's link.
  7. Select a card to open the tool.

  | Note: By default the site shows its Basic tool set, so Collections starts with
  | the beginner-friendly tools. To see all 30 tools, choose Everything in the
  | Advanced Kit. See Show basic or advanced tools
  | [docs/show-basic-or-advanced-tools.txt].

WHAT YOU CAN DO
===============

  - Browse all 30 tools as cards, in a grid or a list.
  - Search by name, description, category, address or topic.
  - Filter by one or more categories and topics at once.
  - Sort by title, category, recommendation, how often you use a tool, pinned
    first, or most detailed description.
  - Pin tools so they stay together under Pinned and lead the Start here
    shortlist.
  - Right-click a card for quick actions and layout settings (view, density,
    columns, sort, theme, reading mode).
  - Choose whether the catalogue shows basic tools, advanced tools,
    everything, or your own custom set.
  - Compare the plans and switch prices between monthly and annual billing,
    and between US dollars and Indian rupees.
