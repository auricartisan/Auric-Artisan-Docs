==============================================================================
COLLECTIONS FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers to common questions about the Collections tool catalogue.
Area: Website › Library › Collections   Updated: 2026-09-25

HOW MANY TOOLS ARE THERE?
=========================

Thirty. With the default Basic tool-visibility setting, Collections shows 14
of them; choose Everything in the Advanced Kit to see all 30. See Show basic
or advanced tools [show-basic-or-advanced-tools.txt].

ARE THE TOOLS FREE?
===================

Yes. Every card says Free to use, and the plans section states "Every tool
free on every plan". Paid plans add conveniences such as cloud sync, version
history, batch power, API access and watermark-free exports.

DO I NEED AN ACCOUNT TO PIN TOOLS?
==================================

No. Pins work without signing in. They are stored in the browser you are
using.

WILL MY PINS APPEAR ON MY PHONE TOO?
====================================

No. Pins and usage history stay in the browser where you made them. Signing in
does not copy them to other devices.

WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN PINNED, MOST USED AND RECOMMENDED?
=================================================================

  - Pinned is the tools you chose yourself.
  - Most used is the tools you open most often and most recently from this
    page.
  - Recommended combines your pins and opens with how central a tool is to the
    site's main subjects and how fully it is described.

See Pin tools and use recommendations [pin-and-recommendations.txt].

DOES AURIC ARTISAN SEE WHICH TOOLS I PIN OR OPEN HERE?
======================================================

The Collections page keeps pins and opens in your browser and does not send
them to Auric Artisan. The site's own analytics, which run only with your
consent, are covered by the privacy policy. See Privacy
[../others/privacy.txt].

HOW DO I RESET THE PAGE TO HOW IT WAS?
======================================

Right-click a card and select Reset to restore the layout (grid view, cozy
density, automatic columns, Title A to Z). To clear pins, unpin each tool. To
clear usage history as well, clear this site's data in your browser.

CAN I SHARE A LINK TO A TOOL?
=============================

Yes. Right-click its card and choose Copy link, then paste the address
wherever you like.

WHY DOES THE RIGHT-CLICK MENU LOOK DIFFERENT ON CARDS?
======================================================

Cards have their own menu with card actions and grid settings. Everywhere else
on the page, right-clicking opens the site's usual menu.

WHY ARE SOME CATEGORIES SPELLED "COLOR"?
========================================

Official names keep their published spelling, such as "Color Science" and
"Colorimetry". Descriptions on the site use British spelling ("colour").

WHERE DO I FIND GUIDES FOR EACH TOOL?
=====================================

Each tool has a user guide in the Documentation library. See Documentation
[../../documentation/README.txt].
