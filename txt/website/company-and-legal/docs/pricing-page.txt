PRICING PAGE
==============================================================================
How Auric Artisan's pricing page is organised and what each section says, with
a pointer to the full plan-by-plan documentation.
Area: Website › Company and legal   Updated: 2026-09-25

Official page: https://auricartisan.com/company/pricing/. The pricing page and
the checkout are authoritative for prices. The full, figure-by-figure
documentation is Plans and pricing
[../../account-and-billing/docs/plans-and-pricing.txt]; this page explains how
the pricing page is laid out.

THE OPENING
===========

  - Headline: Pricing — "Every tool is free, forever".
  - Four facts: free tools, all of them; renewal: none — one payment;
    guarantee: 7-day money-back; term: 1 month or 12 months.
  - ↓ Compare plans and Talk to us.

KEY POINTS (AS THE PAGE LISTS THEM)
===================================

  - Every tool is free on every tier — no tool is locked behind a paywall.
  - Paid plans sell conveniences and scale: batch, API access, higher limits
    and a monthly token allowance.
  - The REST API is metered in credits — 1,000,000 a month on Specialist,
    10,000,000 on Industrial Pro; overage is opt-in, prepaid and never
    invoiced after the fact.
  - Start free with no credit card.
  - Whole-site accessibility crawls, up to 24 pages a run, unlock on Artisan;
    branded WCAG reports arrive with Specialist.
  - A paid plan is a one-time pass for the term you choose; it does not
    auto-renew, and carries a 7-day money-back window.
  - An account is required before any payment.

THE SECTIONS
============

  Section:       Choose your plan
  What it holds: The Monthly / Annual switch (Save 17%), USD $ / INR ₹, four
                 figures ("40+ pro tools, all free", "$0 to start — no card",
                 "No auto-renewal, ever", "7-day money-back window") and the
                 four plan cards

  Section:       Free on every plan
  What it holds: The tools that used to be locked, now free

  Section:       Tokens — what each plan includes
  What it holds: Tokens compared with API credits, the allowances, and the
                 token estimator

  Section:       API credits
  What it holds: Credit costs by endpoint type, what is included, going past
                 the allowance, safety nets, and the Industrial Pro note for
                 purchases before 12 August 2026

  Section:       Optional add-ons
  What it holds: Add-ons free for everyone, included from Specialist, on
                 request, or planned

  Section:       Compare all features
  What it holds: The full comparison table

  Section:       The ones people actually ask
  What it holds: Seven questions: are the tools free; what am I paying for;
                 switching plans; free trial; payment methods; discounts;
                 billing and refunds

  Section:       Ready when you are
  What it holds: Open the tools — free and Create an account
