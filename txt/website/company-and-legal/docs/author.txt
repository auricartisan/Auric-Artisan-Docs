WHO WRITES AURIC ARTISAN
========================

Chirag Bansal is the founder and engineer of Auric Artisan and, in the page's
words, "the author of everything published here". He builds and maintains the
platform end to end — the colour engine, the tools, the site and the writing —
works in India, and publishes the browser extension and the VS Code
extensions.

WHAT THE WORK COVERS
====================

  - Colour science — the engine for CIE XYZ, L*a*b*, LCH, Oklab, sRGB and
    Display P3, colour-difference formulas (CIE76, CIE94, CIEDE2000, CMC),
    chromatic adaptation, gamut mapping with selectable rendering intents, and
    metamerism.
  - Colour vision — simulation models following Viénot, Brettel and Mollon
    (1999), Brettel, Viénot and Mollon (1997), and Machado, Oliveira and
    Fernandes (2009). The Ishihara-style generator is a teaching tool, not a
    medical device.
  - Accessibility — the rule engine behind the Analyzer: WCAG 2.2 and APCA
    contrast, ARIA 1.2 validation, accessible-name computation, screen-reader
    transcript modelling, colour-vision-deficiency checks and VPAT-style
    reporting.
  - Tooling — the cross-browser extension and the VS Code extensions.

HOW THE ARTICLES ARE WRITTEN
============================

  - The articles and documentation are written by hand, not commissioned or
    syndicated.
  - Where an article states a formula or threshold, it is the one the code
    uses.
  - Articles name the published models they rest on. Most carry no formal
    bibliography; the page says a references section is "something the library
    is still missing".
  - Interactive figures in the Learn library compute their results live with
    the same engine the tools use, so you can check a result yourself.
  - Corrections are welcome and made in place, with the revision date updated.
