==============================================================================
DATA RETENTION AND DELETION POLICY
==============================================================================
A plain-language summary of how long Auric Artisan keeps different kinds of
data, how deletion requests are handled, and what may be kept for legal
reasons.
Area: Website › Company and legal   Updated: 2026-09-25

Official page:
https://auricartisan.com/legal/data-retention-and-deletion-policy/ — version
2.2, effective 27 April 2026, last updated 23 May 2026. The official policy is
authoritative.

KEY POINTS
==========

  - Only data needed to operate, secure and improve the platform is kept.
  - Account, session, billing and API data are kept only as needed.
  - Tool inputs are processed only as needed; temporary processing data is
    deleted or anonymised when no longer required.
  - Security logs and abuse-prevention records may be kept for a limited
    period.
  - You can request deletion of applicable personal data through the contact
    channels.

SECTION BY SECTION
==================

  1. Retention principles — Minimal retention: purpose limitation, storage
     limitation, accountability, and practices that follow what is actually
     deployed.
  2. Types of data kept
  Data:     Contact and support messages
  How long: As needed to respond and keep records

  Data:     Tool inputs (URLs, colours, images, palettes, settings)
  How long: Only where necessary

  Data:     Generated outputs (reports, palettes, themes, exports, previews)
  How long: May be temporarily stored, cached or delivered

  Data:     Usage analytics (aggregated or anonymised)
  How long: May be kept to improve the platform

  Data:     Security logs (IP addresses, request metadata, abuse signals,
            errors)
  How long: For security, debugging and abuse prevention

  Data:     Billing records
  How long: As required by tax, accounting, payment-provider and financial
            rules
  3. Tool processing data — What processing can involve. Avoid entering
     sensitive personal data, credentials, medical records, payment
     information or private tokens into exploratory tools unless a feature
     asks for it and explains why.
  4. Deletion requests — Request account deletion through the account controls
     or support. A valid request is reviewed; eligible data in active systems
     is removed, minimised or anonymised within a reasonable period; backups
     and security logs may persist until their normal rotation. Save anything
     you need first — deleted data may not be recoverable.
  5. Automatic deletion — Temporary processing data, cached reports and
     extracted assets may be removed after use; session tokens, cookies and
     local preferences expire according to browser settings or platform
     configuration; security logs are kept for a limited period.
  6. Legal and compliance retention — Data may be kept longer when law, valid
     legal requests, disputes, investigations, abuse prevention, security or
     financial record-keeping require it, and then deleted, minimised or
     anonymised.
  7. Your rights — Access, correction, export, restriction, objection,
     withdrawing consent, appeal and deletion, depending on applicable law
     (GDPR, India's DPDP Act, CCPA/CPRA and others). Use the account controls,
     the contact page or official support channels. Requests are answered
     within the time the law requires.
  8. Data security — Access controls, secure transport, logging, monitoring
     and encryption where appropriate. See Security Policy
     [security-policy.txt].
  9. Changes — May be updated; continued use means acceptance.

HOW THIS APPLIES TO YOUR ACCOUNT
================================

  - Delete your account from the dashboard's danger zone. The Security page
    explains that erasure anonymises personal data while keeping financial
    records, because tax law requires invoices to be kept. See Delete your
    account [../../account-and-billing/docs/delete-your-account.txt].
  - The FAQ page gives approximate timings: access disabled at once, personal
    data scheduled for deletion within about 90 days unless the law requires
    it to be kept.
  - Data in your browser is under your control: clear it in your browser's
    settings, or with Erase this browser's workspace in the dashboard.
  - Privacy or data requests through the contact form have a stated reply time
    of within 30 days.
