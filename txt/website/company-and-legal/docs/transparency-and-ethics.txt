==============================================================================
TRANSPARENCY AND ETHICS POLICY
==============================================================================
A plain-language summary of Auric Artisan's Transparency and Ethics Policy —
its philosophy, what it commits to document, accuracy limits, automation,
advertising, pricing fairness and independence.
Area: Website › Company and legal   Updated: 2026-09-25

Official page: https://auricartisan.com/legal/transparency/ — Transparency &
Ethics Policy, version 2.2, effective 27 April 2026, last updated 23 May 2026.
The official policy is authoritative.

KEY POINTS
==========

  - Auric Artisan explains what its tools can and cannot do.
  - Web analysis outputs are guidance, not certified security, accessibility
    or performance audits.
  - Vision simulations and the Ishihara-style generator are exploratory, not
    medical diagnostics.
  - Pricing, account, checkout and paid features apply only where clearly
    enabled.
  - The platform stays independent and documents limitations, changes and data
    practices.

SECTION BY SECTION
==================

  1. Philosophy — "Tools should respect intelligence": help people understand
     what they are doing instead of hiding it behind vague output; avoid
     inflated claims; make limits visible.
  2. Operational transparency — A commitment to document what data is
     collected and why; what the tools can and cannot do; when outputs are
     estimates, heuristics or models; how submitted material is processed;
     what is still in development; and changes to terms, pricing, access,
     payments or availability.
  3. Accuracy and limitations — Results can depend on browser behaviour,
     rendering, source quality, network conditions and implementation. Web
     analysis results are not certified audits, official Lighthouse reports,
     legal compliance reviews or guaranteed findings. Vision models may not
     match every viewer, display or lighting condition. The Ishihara-style
     generator is not a medical device.
  4. Automation and AI — Where automated systems, machine learning or
     AI-assisted tools are used, the aim is to say so in the interface or
     documentation; outputs should be reviewed; none are presented as
     professional certification or advice.
  5. Advertising and commercial status — No behavioural profiling for
     advertising, no use of private data for ad targeting, no hidden
     sponsorship mixed into tool outputs; sponsored content will be clearly
     distinguishable.
  6. Pricing status and fairness — No hidden fees or surprise charges; costs
     shown before purchase; a clear line between free, preview and paid;
     advance notice where practical for material changes.
  7. Neutrality and independence — Not a government entity, medical provider,
     certification body, standards authority or testing laboratory. No
     partnerships that compromise privacy, data integrity, honest labelling or
     editorial independence.
  8. User responsibility — Verify outputs and use your own judgement: analyse
     only what you may; do not treat analysis as a professional audit or
     simulations as diagnosis; review generated material before production;
     respect others' rights.
  9. Ongoing evaluation — Tool labels, model limits, explanations and privacy
     notices are reviewed as features are added. Feedback about unclear
     pricing, data handling or limits is welcome through the contact page.
  10. Changes — May be updated; continued use means acceptance.

WHERE THE COMMITMENTS SHOW UP IN PRACTICE
=========================================

  - The Security page's What we do not claim section lists missing
    certifications, the lack of an external penetration test, and that the
    platform is operated by one person. See Security page [security-page.txt].
  - The pricing page marks unbuilt features Coming soon or Planned, and
    explains manual processes (discounts, proration) rather than showing
    controls that do not exist. See Plans and pricing
    [../../account-and-billing/docs/plans-and-pricing.txt].
