QUICK ANSWERS (FROM THE TOP OF THE PAGE)
========================================

  - Auric Artisan is a colour-science platform: tools, simulations, web
    analysis and learning resources.
  - More than 70 vision simulation models based on the Viénot, Brettel and
    Machado algorithms, for education and design, not clinical use.
  - Web analysis covers WCAG contrast, security headers, performance signals
    and palette extraction.
  - Theme generation exports CSS custom properties, design tokens, Tailwind
    configuration, SCSS and Figma-compatible output.
  - Many tools work without an account.
  - You own the content and exports you create.
  - Only data needed to operate is collected; personal information is never
    sold.
  - Simulation outputs are software models, not certified for clinical,
    regulatory or safety-critical use.
  - Every tool is free on every tier; paid plans are one-time purchases
    through Razorpay.

ANSWERS WORTH KNOWING
=====================

  - Is it free? Yes, every tool on every tier. Paid plans add conveniences.
  - Do you store the URLs I analyse? The FAQ says no URL history or page
    content is retained after the analysis session.
  - Can I use generated themes commercially? Yes. Auric Artisan claims no
    rights over tool outputs.
  - Can I quote the articles? Brief quotation with attribution and a link is
    acceptable; full reproduction needs written permission
    (hello@auricartisan.com).
  - Multiple accounts? Technically permitted, but using them to get round
    usage limits may lead to suspension of all of them.
  - Minimum age? No specific age group; parents and guardians should review
    the Terms before letting minors create accounts.
  - Refunds? A 7-day money-back window on plan purchases; approved refunds
    return in 5–7 business days.
  - Is traffic encrypted? Yes, HTTPS with HSTS; passwords are hashed.

WHERE OTHER PAGES GIVE NEWER OR MORE SPECIFIC DETAIL
====================================================

The FAQ was last updated before some later changes. These notes point you to
the page that is more specific.

  | Note: Account creation. The FAQ says accounts "may be available where the auth
  | system is enabled". Accounts are live: the Terms of Service (updated 12 August
  | 2026) list registration, sign-in and the dashboard as live.

  | Note: Managing sessions. The FAQ's tip mentions a "Sessions page in your
  | account settings". Signed-in devices are now under Security & Privacy in the
  | dashboard. See Security & Privacy section
  | [../../account-and-billing/docs/security-and-privacy.txt].

  | Note: Deleting an account. The FAQ says access is disabled immediately and
  | personal data is scheduled for deletion within about 90 days unless the law
  | requires it to be kept. The dashboard describes deletion as immediate and
  | permanent, and the Security page explains that payment records are kept in
  | anonymised form for tax law. See Delete your account
  | [../../account-and-billing/docs/delete-your-account.txt].

  | Note: Reporting a vulnerability. The FAQ says to email hello@auricartisan.com
  | with "Security" in the subject. The Security page and the contact form route
  | security reports to security@auricartisan.com, with acknowledgement within 24
  | hours. Use security@auricartisan.com. See Security page [security-page.txt].

  | Note: Currency. The FAQ describes payments in INR. The pricing page charges US
  | dollars outside India and rupees within it.
