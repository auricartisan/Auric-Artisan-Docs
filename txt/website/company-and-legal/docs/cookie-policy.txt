==============================================================================
COOKIE POLICY
==============================================================================
A plain-language summary of Auric Artisan's Cookie Policy — cookie categories,
the storage list, the preferences panel and how to change or withdraw consent.
Area: Website › Company and legal   Updated: 2026-09-25

Official page: https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/ — Cookie Policy &
Preferences, version 1.2, effective 27 April 2026, last updated 23 May 2026.
The official policy is authoritative.

KEY POINTS
==========

  - Limited cookies and browser storage keep the site working and remember
    preferences.
  - Account cookies (session and CSRF) are essential when you are signed in.
  - No cookies for cross-site tracking, behavioural profiling or invasive
    advertising.
  - Web analysis and theme generation use temporary processing data, not
    marketing cookies.
  - Preferences such as theme and eye-rest are stored per browser and device.
  - You can manage non-essential cookies on this page or in your browser.

SECTION BY SECTION
==================

  1. Overview — "We use limited browser storage to keep the site working,
     remember your settings, support interactive tools, and understand basic
     platform performance. We do not use cookies to follow you across the
     web."
  2. Cookies and local storage — What each is; some data disappears when the
     session ends, some remains until it expires or you clear it.
  3. Current status — Sign-in uses essential first-party cookies. Payment
     providers may set their own cookies inside their checkout.
  4. How cookies are used — Three practical groups: essential, functional and
     privacy-friendly analytics. Essential ones cannot be turned off in the
     preferences panel.
  5. No cross-site tracking or marketing pixels — No Facebook Pixel, LinkedIn
     Insight Tag, Google Ads remarketing cookies or similar trackers "on this
     page".
  6. Third-party services — Infrastructure, security, font and analytics
     services may receive limited technical information; providers' cookies
     follow their own terms.
  7. Your legal rights — Under the DPDP Act, GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA and similar
     laws: know what is used, accept or reject non-essential cookies, withdraw
     consent, clear storage, and ask questions.
  8. Manage cookie preferences — The on-page panel (below).
  9. Current cookie and storage list — The table (below).
  10. Browser instructions — Where to manage cookies in Chrome, Firefox,
      Safari and Edge. Disabling essential cookies or clearing storage may
      reset preferences or stop features working.
  11. Changes — The policy may be updated.

Questions: privacy@auricartisan.com, as listed on the page.

THE PREFERENCES PANEL (SECTION 8)
=================================

The panel lists four categories, each with a switch and a Show cookies button
that lists the items in it:

  Category:                Essential Cookies
  Description on the panel:Authentication, security, sessions and access
                           control
  Can be turned off?:      No — marked Always Active

  Category:                Functional Cookies
  Description on the panel:Theme, language, layout choices and form data
  Can be turned off?:      Yes

  Category:                Analytics Cookies
  Description on the panel:Aggregate or pseudonymous usage and performance
                           data, never used for advertising profiles
  Can be turned off?:      Yes

  Category:                Marketing Cookies
  Description on the panel:"Reserved for campaign attribution if enabled
                           later"; no marketing pixels are used
  Can be turned off?:      Yes

Buttons:

  Button:       Accept All
  What it does: Turns every switch on (select Save Preferences to keep it)

  Button:       Reject All
  What it does: Turns every optional switch off (select Save Preferences to
                keep it)

  Button:       Save Preferences
  What it does: Saves your choices: "Cookie preferences saved successfully."

  Button:       Clear All Cookies
  What it does: Clears non-essential cookies and your saved choice: "All
                non-essential cookies have been cleared."

  Button:       Withdraw Consent
  What it does: Shown once you have chosen; resets your choice: "Cookie
                consent withdrawn. Preferences reset."

Under the buttons, Consent recorded: shows when you last chose. Your choice is
saved in your browser and applies to that device only.

The first-visit bar (We value your privacy) offers Accept All, Reject All and
Customise; Customise opens this panel. The bar is not shown over the sign-in
and verification pages. Full walkthrough: Cookie consent and privacy
[../../site-features/cookie-consent-and-privacy/README.txt].

  | Note: The panel's own tables list a slightly different set of item names and
  | durations from section 9 (for example "1 year" for functional items). The
  | purposes are the same. If an exact name or duration matters to you, ask
  | through the contact page.
