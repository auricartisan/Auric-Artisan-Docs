==============================================================================
ADVERTISE ON AURIC ARTISAN
==============================================================================
What advertising on Auric Artisan offers, who it is for, and how to request
your first campaign with the AdsSense campaign builder.
Area: Website › Advertise   Updated: 2026-09-25

AdsSense is Auric Artisan's own advertising system. It puts your product in
front of the designers, developers and colour scientists who use the site,
next to the tools and articles they came for. The page at
https://auricartisan.com/advertise/ has everything in one place: the formats
and their prices, a live example of each placement, and a six-step campaign
builder that shows the full price before you send anything.

Every ad is clearly labelled Sponsored. Ads are placed by page context, not by
following people across sites: you choose where your campaign runs, and the
audience groups you pick guide the creative review only. Every creative that
runs is listed publicly in the Ad Library at
https://auricartisan.com/advertise/library/.

Campaigns are booked by request. When you send a campaign from the builder,
nothing is booked and nothing is charged. Auric Artisan confirms the campaign
and then sends you a Razorpay link to pay the price you were shown. Your
campaign runs after payment and creative approval.

WHO IT IS FOR
=============

  - Companies and makers of design, development and colour products.
  - Studios, educators and communities that want to reach people who work with
    colour.
  - Anyone launching a product to a design-minded audience.

WHERE TO FIND IT
================

  - The campaign page: https://auricartisan.com/advertise/
  - The Ad Library: https://auricartisan.com/advertise/library/
  - Questions about custom flights, bulk buys or studio-made creatives:
    Contact us at https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/
  - No account is needed to build and send a campaign request.

QUICK START
===========

  1. Open https://auricartisan.com/advertise/ and go to Create your campaign.
  2. Basics: name the campaign, add your brand and the address clicks should
     go to, and choose a goal.
  3. Format: choose one of the four formats. The goal you chose marks one as
     Recommended.
  4. Creative: design the ad in the built-in studio, or upload finished
     artwork.
  5. Audience: choose where it runs and how often one visitor can see it.
  6. Budget: choose the flight length, start date, share of voice and
     currency, and add a promo code if you have one.
  7. Review: check the summary, add a billing email, accept the policies and
     select Request campaign.
  8. Wait for Auric Artisan to confirm and send a Razorpay payment link.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
===============

  - Choose from four formats — Leaderboard, Medium Rectangle, Native In-Feed
    Card, and Panels & Search Strip — and five zones, from the homepage to the
    whole site.
  - See a live quote and a delivery estimate that update with every choice.
  - Build the final click address with UTM auto-tagging, custom parameters and
    tracking macros.
  - Design an accessible creative with a live contrast check, or upload PNG,
    JPG, WebP or SVG artwork.
  - Pay in Indian rupees or US dollars, with lower daily rates for longer
    flights.
  - Receive a flight report at the end of the campaign.
  - Browse every ad that runs on Auric Artisan in the Ad Library, and report
    one that breaks the rules.
