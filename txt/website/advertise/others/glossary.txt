==============================================================================
ADVERTISE GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Advertising terms used on the Auric Artisan advertise page and in the Ad
Library.
Area: Website › Advertise   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Ad Library
  Meaning: The public list of every ad that runs on Auric Artisan.

  Term:    ads.txt
  Meaning: A public file that declares who may sell a site's ad space.

  Term:    AdsSense
  Meaning: Auric Artisan's own advertising system.

  Term:    Auto-tagging
  Meaning: Adding UTM parameters to the click address automatically.

  Term:    Booking request
  Meaning: A campaign sent from the builder. It books and charges nothing
           until confirmed and paid.

  Term:    Call to action
  Meaning: The button text on an ad, such as "LEARN MORE".

  Term:    Campaign
  Meaning: One ad in one format and zone, for one flight.

  Term:    Creative
  Meaning: The ad itself: the artwork people see.

  Term:    CTR (click-through rate)
  Meaning: Clicks divided by impressions.

  Term:    Destination URL
  Meaning: The page a click on the ad opens.

  Term:    Display link
  Meaning: The short form of the destination shown with the ad.

  Term:    eff. CPM
  Meaning: Effective cost per thousand impressions: the price divided by
           estimated impressions, times 1,000.

  Term:    Final URL
  Meaning: The destination with tracking parameters added.

  Term:    Flight
  Meaning: The period a campaign runs, from its start date.

  Term:    Flight report
  Meaning: The report of delivered impressions, viewable impressions, clicks
           and effective CPM at the end of a campaign.

  Term:    Format
  Meaning: The shape of an ad: Leaderboard, Medium Rectangle, Native In-Feed
           Card, or Panels & Search Strip.

  Term:    Frequency cap
  Meaning: The most times one visitor can see your ad in a browsing session.

  Term:    House ad
  Meaning: An ad for Auric Artisan's own products, marked "(house)" in the Ad
           Library.

  Term:    Impression
  Meaning: One showing of an ad.

  Term:    Macro
  Meaning: A placeholder in the click address, such as `{zone}`, filled in
           each time the ad is shown.

  Term:    MRC-viewable
  Meaning: Counted as seen: at least 50% of the ad visible for one second.

  Term:    Share of voice
  Meaning: The fraction of eligible ad renders in a zone reserved for your
           campaign.

  Term:    Sponsored
  Meaning: The label every ad on Auric Artisan carries.

  Term:    Takeover
  Meaning: A 100% share of voice.

  Term:    UTM parameters
  Meaning: Standard tags such as `utm_source` that analytics tools use to
           attribute visits.

  Term:    Zone
  Meaning: The part of the site a campaign runs on, such as the Homepage or
           Run of site.
