==============================================================================
ADVERTISE FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers to common questions about advertising on Auric Artisan.
Area: Website › Advertise   Updated: 2026-09-25

DO I PAY WHEN I SEND THE CAMPAIGN?
==================================

No. Sending books nothing and charges nothing. Auric Artisan confirms the
campaign and sends a Razorpay link for the price you were shown.

HOW SOON CAN MY CAMPAIGN START?
===============================

The earliest start date is tomorrow. A campaign starts at 00:00 IST on its
start date, after payment and creative approval. The confirmation and payment
link arrive within one business day of your request.

DO I NEED AN ACCOUNT?
=====================

No. You need a billing email for receipts and campaign updates.

WHICH CURRENCIES CAN I PAY IN?
==============================

Indian rupees or US dollars. Choose in the Budget step.

IS TAX INCLUDED?
================

The quote shows a Tax line when tax applies. The Total is the price you are
asked to pay.

CAN I TARGET INDIVIDUAL PEOPLE?
===============================

No. Campaigns are placed by page context: you choose the zone. The audience
groups you choose guide the creative review only; they do not identify or
follow visitors.

DO MY ADS TRACK VISITORS?
=========================

No. Ads never track visitors across sites. Third-party ad technology runs only
for visitors who accept the marketing cookie category.

HOW IS MY CAMPAIGN MEASURED?
============================

Impressions, MRC-viewable impressions (at least half the ad visible for one
second), clicks and effective CPM. You receive them in a flight report at the
end of the campaign.

WHO SEES MY AD?
===============

Visitors to the zone you chose, up to your frequency cap per session. Paid
Auric Artisan subscribers browse without ads.

WHAT IS SHARE OF VOICE?
=======================

The fraction of eligible ad renders in your zone that are reserved for your
campaign: 25%, 50% or 100%.

CAN I CHANGE THE FORMAT AFTER DESIGNING THE CREATIVE?
=====================================================

Yes. A studio design is redrawn at the new size. An uploaded file may be
cropped to the new shape; upload artwork at the new size for the sharpest
result.

CAN AURIC ARTISAN MAKE THE CREATIVE FOR ME?
===========================================

Ask through Contact us at https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/, which the
advertise page offers for studio-made creatives, custom flights and bulk buys.

CAN I GET A REFUND?
===================

Yes, in full, at any time before the campaign start date. After it starts,
refunds for the undelivered part are handled case by case. See
https://auricartisan.com/legal/refund/.

IS MY AD LISTED PUBLICLY?
=========================

Yes. Every ad that runs is listed in the Ad Library [ad-library.txt] for its
full run and at least 90 days after.

WHAT GETS AN AD REJECTED?
=========================

Ads that imitate the site's interface, such as fake buttons or system dialogs,
that auto-play sound, or that track visitors across sites. Every ad must be
labelled Sponsored. See Design your creative [design-your-creative.txt].
