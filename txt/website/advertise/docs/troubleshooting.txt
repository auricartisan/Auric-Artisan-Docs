==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
==============================================================================
Symptoms, causes and fixes for problems with the AdsSense campaign builder,
booking requests and the Ad Library.
Area: Website › Advertise   Updated: 2026-09-25

CONTINUE DOES NOTHING
=====================

Why: Something in the current step is missing or not valid. The message under
the buttons says what.

What to do: Read the message and fix that field. In the first step, fields
with a problem are also marked. See the messages in the Reference
[reference.txt].

I CANNOT OPEN A LATER STEP FROM THE STEP CHIPS
==============================================

Why: The chips open only steps you have already reached with Continue.

What to do: Work through the steps in order once. After that you can jump
between them.

THE DESTINATION ADDRESS IS REJECTED
===================================

Why: It is incomplete, uses a scheme other than http or https, contains a user
name or password, or its site name is not a full domain.

What to do: Paste the full address from your browser's address bar, starting
`https://`. Use the final address, not a link shortener.

MY UPLOADED FILE IS REJECTED
============================

Why: The file is not PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG, is over 300 KB, or cannot be read
as an image.

What to do: Export the artwork again in one of the four types and compress it
under 300 KB. The Creative specs section suggests under 150 KB for the
Leaderboard and Medium Rectangle.

THE PREVIEW CROPS MY ARTWORK
============================

Why: The artwork's shape does not match the format.

What to do: Upload artwork at the recommended size for your format, shown
under the upload area: 728×90, 300×250, 320×100, or 720×600 for the in-feed
card.

THE STUDIO SAYS "BELOW WCAG AA"
===============================

Why: The headline colour and background have less than 4.5:1 contrast.

What to do: Choose a darker Text colour or a lighter Background, or the other
way round. You can continue anyway, but a readable ad performs better and is
fairer to readers.

THE START DATE WILL NOT GO EARLIER
==================================

Why: The earliest start date is tomorrow.

What to do: Choose tomorrow or later. The campaign starts at 00:00 IST on that
day, after creative approval.

MY PROMO CODE SHOWS "UNKNOWN CODE"
==================================

Why: The code is not recognised.

What to do: Check the spelling; codes are not case-sensitive. The quote shows
a Promo line only when a code is valid.

THE REQUEST BUTTON STAYS ON "COMPLETE CAMPAIGN TO REQUEST BOOKING"
==================================================================

Why: One of the steps is incomplete: usually the billing email or the policy
checkbox in Review.

What to do: Go to the Review step. Enter a valid Billing email, tick the
policy box, and check the earlier steps: each needs a campaign name, brand,
valid destination, creative and start date.

"COULD NOT SEND THE REQUEST"
============================

Why: The request did not reach Auric Artisan.

What to do: Try again. If it still fails, email ads@auricartisan.com with your
campaign details, as the message says. Your draft stays in this browser until
a request is sent.

THE CONFIRMATION SAYS "PAYMENT VERIFIED", BUT I HAVE NOT PAID
=============================================================

Why: The Campaign submitted panel uses the same wording for every campaign.
For a booking request, nothing has been charged. The reference beginning REQ-
identifies your request, and the Payment line stays empty.

What to do: Keep the reference and wait for the Razorpay payment link at your
billing email, within one business day.

I HAVE NOT RECEIVED THE PAYMENT LINK
====================================

Why: Confirmation can take up to one business day, or the email may have been
filtered.

What to do: Check your spam folder for the billing email you entered. After
one business day, use Contact us at https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/
and quote your REQ- reference.

MY DRAFT HAS GONE
=================

Why: Drafts are saved in the browser you used. Another browser or device, a
private window, or clearing site data starts a new draft. A draft is also
cleared once its request is sent.

What to do: Build the campaign again. Keep your artwork file to hand.

MY CAMPAIGN IS NOT IN THE AD LIBRARY
====================================

Why: Campaigns appear in the Ad Library while they run, after confirmation,
payment and creative approval.

What to do: Check again after your start date.
