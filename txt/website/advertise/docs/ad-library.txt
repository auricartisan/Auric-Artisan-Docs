==============================================================================
THE AD LIBRARY
==============================================================================
How to browse the Auric Artisan Ad Library, read an ad's details, and report
an ad that breaks the creative policy.
Area: Website › Advertise   Updated: 2026-09-25

The Ad Library at https://auricartisan.com/advertise/library/ lists every ad
that runs on Auric Artisan: Every ad, in the open. Each entry shows the
creative, the advertiser, the format, where it runs, its flight dates and its
landing address. There are no hidden ads and no ads shown only to some people.

An ad is listed for its full run and for at least 90 days after it ends.
Anyone can open the library; no account is needed.

BROWSE THE LIBRARY
==================

At the top of the page:

  - Search advertiser or text… — filters the ads by advertiser, ad text or
    landing address as you type.
  - Format chips — All formats, Leaderboard, Rectangle, In-feed and Strip.
  - Status chips — All, Active and Ended.

The count beside the filters says how many ads match, for example 12 ads. If
none match, the page says No ads match those filters.

READ AN AD
==========

Each card shows:

  Item:    Creative
  Meaning: The ad exactly as it is served.

  Item:    Advertiser
  Meaning: Who runs the ad. Auric Artisan's own promotions are marked (house).

  Item:    Status badge
  Meaning: Active or Ended.

  Item:    Format
  Meaning: For example Leaderboard · 728×90.

  Item:    Runs on
  Meaning: The zones where it appears, such as Homepage or Learn library (113
           articles).

  Item:    Flight
  Meaning: since and the start date, or the start and end dates.

  Item:    Landing
  Meaning: Where a click goes. Addresses on Auric Artisan open in the same
           tab; other sites open in a new tab.

  Item:    Report this ad
  Meaning: Opens the contact page.

REPORT AN AD
============

If an ad breaks the creative policy — for example it imitates a button or
system dialog, is not labelled Sponsored, or misleads — select Report this ad
on its card, or Report it in About this library. Both open
https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/. Auric Artisan pulls violating
creatives within one business day.

WHO MAY SELL THESE ADS
======================

The file https://auricartisan.com/ads.txt declares every authorised seller of
Auric Artisan's ad space.
