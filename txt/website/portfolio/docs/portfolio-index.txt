==============================================================================
THE PORTFOLIO INDEX
==============================================================================
Browse, search and filter the Portfolio Index by name, craft, place and
colour, and share a search.
Area: Website › Portfolio   Updated: 2026-09-25

The Portfolio Index at https://auricartisan.com/portfolios/ is "Every
portfolio, in one place": portfolios made with the Portfolio Builder and
portfolios people keep on their own sites, "searched together by name, craft,
place and colour". Anyone can browse it; no account is needed.

THE PAGE
========

  - The heading, with Publish yours (opens the Portfolio Builder) and Add one
    you host (opens the Search Console's Portfolios tab).
  - Four counts: In the index, Made here, From elsewhere, Verified.
  - The search box.
  - Source buttons, Filters, the results, and How a portfolio gets in.

SEARCH
======

Type into Search by name, craft, place or a colour like #d3af37, or press `/`
anywhere on the page to jump to it. Results update as you type; `Escape`
clears the box.

Words are matched against names, headlines, crafts, places and descriptions,
with allowance for typing mistakes and word beginnings. You can also use:

  You type: `"exact words"`
  It means: Those words together, in that order.

  You type: `-word`
  It means: Leave out results with that word.

  You type: A hex colour such as `#d3af37`
  It means: Portfolios whose colours are nearest to it come first (colours are
            compared perceptually, in OKLab).

  You type: `tag:branding` or `tag:"brand identity"`
  It means: A craft.

  You type: `in:lisbon`
  It means: A place.

  You type: `made:here` / `made:elsewhere`
  It means: Builder portfolios / portfolios on other sites.

  You type: `is:verified` or `verified`
  It means: Verified portfolios only.

Under the box, Try offers example tokens — such as `made:here`, `is:verified`
and a colour — built from what is in the index; select one to put it in the
box.

FILTER
======

  Control        | Options
  ---------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Source buttons | All, Made here, Elsewhere, each with a count.
  Filters        | Opens the filter row; a number shows how many are on.
  Verified only  | Only portfolios whose owner proved the site is theirs.
  Colour         | Colour family: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Teal, Blue, Violet, Pink, Neutral.
  Craft          | The crafts in the index (· none yet when there are none).
  Location       | The places in the index.

Every count reflects your other filters, so you can see what each choice would
leave. Active filters are shown as removable chips.

SORT AND VIEW
=============

  Sort             | Order
  -----------------+-------------------------------------------------------
  Best match       | By relevance to your search.
  Recently updated | Featured portfolios first, then most recently updated.
  Newest           | Newest first.
  Name, A to Z     | Alphabetical.
  Most viewed      | Most viewed first.

Grid and List switch the layout. In the grid, a featured portfolio at the top
gets a larger spotlight card.

THE CARDS
=========

Each card shows the portfolio's colours as a cover, its source (Made here or
Elsewhere), a Verified mark ("The owner proved it is theirs") or Reviewed for
a checked external entry, an avatar or initials, the name and place, the
headline, up to four crafts, where it lives, when it was updated, and Open
portfolio. External portfolios open in a new tab. The list view shows the same
facts in a row.

Results come 24 at a time; Show N more of M loads the next batch.

MESSAGES
========

  Message: Loading the index…
  Meaning: The index is loading.

  Message: "N portfolios" (and "for" your search)
  Meaning: The number of results.

  Message: Nothing matches your search / No portfolios match these filters
  Meaning: Loosen the search or filters.

  Message: The index is empty so far.
  Meaning: No portfolios have been listed yet.

  Message: The index is new. The first few get the front row.
  Meaning: Only a few portfolios are listed.

  Message: The portfolio index could not be reached
  Meaning: The page could not load the index; try again later.

SHARE A SEARCH
==============

The address of the page updates with your search, filters, sort and view, so
you can copy it to share exactly what you are looking at, and the browser's
Back button steps back through your changes.

HOW A PORTFOLIO GETS IN
=======================

The page explains three steps:

  1. Publish, or add an address — "Builder portfolios are copied in within
     fifteen minutes of publishing. Portfolios on other sites are added in the
     Search Console."
  2. Proof lists it at once — an address on a site you have verified goes in
     straight away, marked Verified; anything else waits for someone on the
     Auric Artisan team to review it.
  3. Found by what it holds — name, craft, place and colour.

"Leave whenever you like: switch off “List it in the Portfolio Index” the next
time you publish." See List your portfolio [list-your-portfolio.txt].

The index is searched in your browser and needs JavaScript. Without it, every
builder portfolio is still at its own address.
