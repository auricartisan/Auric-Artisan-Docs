==============================================================================
PORTFOLIO GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Terms used in the Portfolio Builder and the Portfolio Index.
Area: Website › Portfolio   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Accent
  Meaning: The colour used for links, headings and buttons, picked from your
           saved palettes.

  Term:    Address (handle)
  Meaning: The name after `auricartisan.com/p/` in your published address.

  Term:    Alt text
  Meaning: A written description of an image for people using screen readers.

  Term:    Blocking finding
  Meaning: A Check result that must be fixed before you can publish.

  Term:    Credit line
  Meaning: The small "Made with Auric Artisan" line on exported HTML and PDF
           files.

  Term:    Draft
  Meaning: Your portfolio as it is in the builder, saved automatically; not
           what visitors see until you publish.

  Term:    External portfolio
  Meaning: A portfolio on your own website, added to the Portfolio Index
           through the Search Console.

  Term:    Markdown
  Meaning: A plain-text way of writing formatted text, such as `**bold**` and
           `# Heading`.

  Term:    Paper
  Meaning: The page's background and text colour: Cream, White, Sand, Ink or
           No background.

  Term:    Placeholder
  Meaning: Grey example text in an empty field; it is not published.

  Term:    Portfolio Index
  Meaning: The directory of portfolios at
           https://auricartisan.com/portfolios/.

  Term:    Publish
  Meaning: Put the current draft online at your address.

  Term:    Raw HTML
  Meaning: A section that accepts your own HTML and CSS, cleaned for safety.

  Term:    Rhythm
  Meaning: The page's spacing and corner roundness: Tight, Even or Airy.

  Term:    Reviewed
  Meaning: An external portfolio a person has checked and approved.

  Term:    Search Console
  Meaning: Auric Artisan's tool for site owners at
           https://auricartisan.com/search/console/, where external portfolios
           are added.

  Term:    Section
  Meaning: One building block of the page, such as a Heading or a Timeline.

  Term:    Share card
  Meaning: The preview (name, headline, avatar) shown when your address is
           shared in apps.

  Term:    Snapshot link
  Meaning: A link that contains the whole portfolio in the address itself.

  Term:    Verified
  Meaning: An external portfolio on a site whose owner proved it is theirs.

  Term:    WCAG
  Meaning: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines; the Check pane tests against
           WCAG 2.2 AA.
