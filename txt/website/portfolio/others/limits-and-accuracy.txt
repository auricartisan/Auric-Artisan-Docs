==============================================================================
LIMITS AND ACCURACY
==============================================================================
Size limits, what the Check pane can and cannot tell you, and other limits of
the Portfolio Builder and the Portfolio Index.
Area: Website › Portfolio   Updated: 2026-09-25

SIZE
====

  - A published portfolio can be up to half a megabyte. Uploaded images and
    pictures are stored inside it and use that space quickly; linking to
    images hosted elsewhere does not.
  - Snapshot links carry the whole portfolio in the address. With several
    embedded images they become very long, and some apps and sites will not
    accept them. Large avatars are left out of snapshot links.

THE CHECK PANE
==============

  - It checks the rendered page for text contrast (WCAG 2.2 AA), link text,
    image alt text, heading order and target size. It is a strong pre-flight,
    not a full accessibility audit: it cannot judge whether your alt text is
    accurate, whether your content is clear, or how the page behaves with
    every assistive technology.
  - Text in colour formats it cannot measure is reported as unknown rather
    than passed.
  - Contrast is measured against the page's own paper. Text over images is not
    measured.

STYLE
=====

  - Accents offered in Style come from palettes saved in your Library in this
    browser.
  - The No background paper takes its text colour from the visitor's light or
    dark theme, so the page can look different for different visitors.

DRAFTS AND SYNC
===============

  - Drafts sync to your account when you are signed in. If both devices change
    the draft, the newer copy wins; the builder will not overwrite a draft you
    are in the middle of editing.
  - Images in the Work / Projects section come from your Library in the
    browser you publish from.

THE PORTFOLIO INDEX
===================

  - Builder portfolios are copied into the index within fifteen minutes of
    publishing.
  - External portfolios that are not on a verified site wait for a person to
    review them.
  - The index honours your search-engine setting: with Let search engines list
    it off, the portfolio is not listed.
  - Colour search compares colours perceptually (OKLab); results are ordered
    by closeness, not by exact match.

PUBLISHED PAGES
===============

  - The view count is a single number. It does not identify visitors.
  - Embeds are limited to trusted hosts; Raw HTML is cleaned, so some markup
    is removed by design.
