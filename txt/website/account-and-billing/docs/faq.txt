==============================================================================
FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers to common questions about Auric Artisan accounts, plans,
payments, tokens, API keys and deleting an account.
Area: Website › Account and billing   Updated: 2026-09-25

ACCOUNTS
========

Do I need an account to use the tools?
--------------------------------------

No. Every tool is free with no login required. An account is needed for
server-backed tool work (tokens), buying a plan, API keys, publishing a
portfolio, and account security. See Create an account (optional)
[../../../getting-started/create-an-account.txt].

Can I sign in with my username?
-------------------------------

Yes. The first field on the sign-in page accepts your email address or your
username.

Can I change my email address?
------------------------------

Not from the dashboard yet. The field is read-only: "Email changes are not
supported yet — contact support."

How often can I change my username?
-----------------------------------

Once every 30 days. A username that someone gives up is held for 30 days
before anyone else can claim it.

Why was I signed out after changing my password?
------------------------------------------------

Changing a password signs out every device, including the one you are using,
so that anyone holding an old session loses it. Sign in with the new password.

Can I have more than one account?
---------------------------------

The FAQ page says multiple accounts are technically permitted, but using them
to get around usage limits may lead to all of them being suspended.

Is there an inbox in my account?
--------------------------------

No. Everything arrives by email. See Emails and messages
[emails-and-messages.txt].

PLANS AND PAYMENTS
==================

Are the tools really free?
--------------------------

Yes, every tool on every plan. Paid plans add conveniences such as ad-free
browsing, watermark-free exports, a monthly token allowance and higher limits.
See Plans and pricing [plans-and-pricing.txt].

Which plans can I buy?
----------------------

Artisan, today. Specialist and Industrial Pro are marked Coming soon on the
pricing page.

Will I be charged again automatically?
--------------------------------------

No. A plan is a one-time pass for one month or twelve months. Nothing renews,
no card is stored, and there is nothing to cancel.

How do I cancel?
----------------

There is nothing to cancel. The pass ends by itself and the account returns to
the free plan. To stop early, ask for a refund: Refunds [refunds.txt].

Is there a free trial?
----------------------

No. The free Apprentice plan is the trial, and paid plans have a 7-day
money-back window.

Which payment methods can I use?
--------------------------------

Everything Razorpay offers at checkout: cards, UPI, netbanking and wallets.
Auric Artisan never sees your card, UPI or bank details.

Can I pay from outside India?
-----------------------------

Yes, in US dollars. Non-Indian cards are accepted; American Express needs a
separate permission and may not go through. If a card is declined at the last
step, nothing is charged; tell Auric Artisan through the contact page.

Is GST included? Can I get a GST invoice?
-----------------------------------------

The price you see is the total. Auric Artisan is not currently registered for
GST, so no GST is added and receipts are payment receipts, not GST invoices.

Do you offer student or nonprofit discounts?
--------------------------------------------

Yes, arranged by hand before you buy. Write through the contact page with
proof of eligibility. There is no discount code field.

Can I upgrade or downgrade in the middle of a pass?
---------------------------------------------------

An upgrade starts as soon as the payment clears. There is no downgrade to
arrange: a pass runs its term and stops. Proration is not automatic; contact
Auric Artisan if a change leaves a real difference in your favour.

What happens to my work when my plan ends?
------------------------------------------

Nothing. Your saved work lives in your browser and stays where it is. Every
tool keeps working.

TOKENS AND CREDITS
==================

What is the difference between tokens and API credits?
------------------------------------------------------

Tokens pay for server-backed work you start in a website tool, such as
whole-site crawls. API credits pay for calls your own code makes to the REST
API. They are separate and never convert. See Tokens [tokens.txt].

Do tokens expire?
-----------------

Included monthly tokens expire at the end of the billing period. The free
plan's 100 starter tokens do not expire but do not renew.

Can I buy more tokens?
----------------------

Not today. Token packs are defined but not on sale; tokens come with a plan.

Can my API usage produce a surprise bill?
-----------------------------------------

No. Overage is off by default and prepaid: it can only spend credits you
already bought, and nothing is ever invoiced afterwards. You can also set a
monthly spend cap. See API credits and spend controls
[api-credits-and-spend.txt].

SECURITY AND DATA
=================

Should I turn on two-step verification?
---------------------------------------

Yes. It is free and takes a minute. See Two-step verification
[two-step-verification.txt].

How do I see where I am signed in?
----------------------------------

Security & Privacy › Signed-in devices. You can revoke any device, or sign out
everywhere else.

What does the export include?
-----------------------------

Your account details, dashboard preferences, and the portfolio draft and
library saved in the browser you export from. No passwords, two-step material
or API keys. See Export your data [export-your-data.txt].

What happens when I delete my account?
--------------------------------------

It closes at once and you are signed out everywhere. Personal details are
removed; payment records are kept in anonymised form because tax law requires
invoices to be retained. Work in your browser is not affected. See Delete your
account [delete-your-account.txt].
