==============================================================================
EXPORT YOUR DATA
==============================================================================
Download your Auric Artisan account details, preferences, portfolio draft and
saved library as one JSON file from the dashboard.
Area: Website › Account and billing   Updated: 2026-09-25

The dashboard can put your account details and the work saved in this browser
into one JSON file (a plain-text data format that most tools can read).
Nothing secret is included.

DOWNLOAD THE EXPORT
===================

  1. Open the dashboard and select Security & Privacy.
  2. Find the row Export everything.
  3. Select Export.

Result: "Export downloaded." and a file named like
`auric-artisan-export-2026-09-25.json` (the date of the export) is saved by
your browser.

WHAT THE FILE CONTAINS
======================

  Section:  `exported_at`
  Contents: When you made the export

  Section:  `profile`
  Contents: Your account details as the site holds them: display name,
            username, email address, bio, website, location, photo and related
            account fields

  Section:  `preferences`
  Contents: Your dashboard preferences

  Section:  `portfolio`
  Contents: Your Portfolio Builder draft as saved in this browser

  Section:  `portfolio_stats`
  Contents: The view, download and share counters kept in this browser

  Section:  `library`
  Contents: Everything in My Library in this browser — palettes, gradients,
            collections and other saved items

WHAT IT DOES NOT CONTAIN
========================

  - Secrets. Passwords, two-step verification material and API keys are never
    included.
  - Work saved in other browsers. The library and portfolio sections come from
    the browser you are using. Export from each browser you use.
  - Receipts. The on-screen description mentions receipts, but your invoices
    are not written into this file. View, print or save them as PDF from Plan
    & Usage › Invoices & Payment History — see Invoices and receipts
    [invoices-and-receipts.txt].

A COMPLETE COPY OF WHAT AURIC ARTISAN HOLDS
===========================================

The Privacy Policy lists a right to portability — "a portable copy of
applicable data where technically feasible" — and a right of access. For a
copy of data held on Auric Artisan's servers beyond this export (for example
order and payment records, or contact messages), write through the contact
page with the topic Privacy or data (reply time: within 30 days). See Contact
and support [../../../help/contact-and-support.txt].

KEEP THE FILE SAFE
==================

The export holds your email address and profile details. Store it somewhere
private.
