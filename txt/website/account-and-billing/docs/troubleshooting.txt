==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
==============================================================================
Fixes for problems with signing in, verification emails, passwords, two-step
codes, payments, invoices, API keys and the Auric Artisan dashboard.
Area: Website › Account and billing   Updated: 2026-09-25

Each entry gives the symptom, the likely cause and what to do. For problems
with the website in general — browser support, cookies, offline use — see
Help: troubleshooting [../../../help/troubleshooting.txt].

I CANNOT SIGN IN
================

  Symptom:    "Enter a valid email address." although you typed a username
  Why:        The field contains an @, so it is checked as an email address
  What to do: Type the username without @, or your full email address

  Symptom:    The sign-in is refused with a credentials message
  Why:        Wrong email, username or password
  What to do: Check for typing errors, then use Forgot password?

  Symptom:    Refused after several attempts
  Why:        Sign-in attempts are rate-limited; repeated failures can lock
              sign-in for a while
  What to do: Wait, then try again, or reset your password

  Symptom:    "Complete the Auric human check before continuing."
  Why:        A human check is on screen and not finished
  What to do: Repeat the three marks and select Verify sequence, then Sign In

  Symptom:    The human check keeps expiring
  Why:        Each check lasts a few minutes and is used once
  What to do: Select New sequence, answer, and sign in straight away

  Symptom:    "Enter your email or username before verifying the sequence."
  Why:        The check is tied to the identifier in the form
  What to do: Fill in Email or username first

  Symptom:    You signed up with Google or GitHub and have no password
  Why:        Provider accounts have no Auric Artisan password
  What to do: Use the same provider button, or set a password with Forgot
              password?

  Symptom:    "That sign-in request expired. Start again."
  Why:        The 10-minute second-factor window ran out
  What to do: Sign in again and enter the code promptly

  Symptom:    You are signed in but keep being sent to the sign-in page
  Why:        Your session ended (password change, revoked device, or it
              expired)
  What to do: Sign in again

GOOGLE OR GITHUB SIGN-IN ERRORS
===============================

After returning from the provider, one of these may appear at the top of the
sign-in page:

  Message:    "OAuth response was incomplete."
  What to do: Try again from the Auric Artisan sign-in page.

  Message:    "OAuth session expired. Try signing in again."
  What to do: You took too long, or opened the provider page in another tab.
              Start again.

  Message:    "Could not complete sign-in with that provider."
  What to do: Try again; if it persists, use email and password.

  Message:    "Your provider account has no verified email address."
  What to do: Verify your email with Google or GitHub, or sign up with email
              and password.

  Message:    "This email already has an account. Sign in with the method
              already connected to it."
  What to do: Sign in with email and password (or the other provider) you used
              first.

  Message:    "Could not link that account. Try signing in again."
  What to do: Try again.

  Message:    "Signed in, but the session could not be started."
  What to do: Check that your browser allows cookies for auricartisan.com,
              then try again.

  Message:    "OAuth sign-in failed."
  What to do: Try again, or use email and password.

THE VERIFICATION EMAIL DID NOT ARRIVE
=====================================

  1. Wait a minute and check your spam or junk folder.
  2. Check the address shown (partly masked) on the Confirm your inbox.
     screen.
  3. Select Resend verification email once the wait timer ends. Each new link
     replaces the old one; use the newest.
  4. From the dashboard, use Send it again in the Your account is nearly there
     panel.
  5. If the address is wrong, it cannot be changed from the dashboard; contact
     support.

If the link says Verification link unavailable., it has expired (after 24
hours), been used, or been replaced. Request a fresh one.

THE RESET EMAIL DID NOT ARRIVE
==============================

  - The reset form never says whether an address has an account. If nothing
    arrives, check that you typed the email address on the account (not your
    username) and look in spam.
  - Reset requests are rate-limited; wait a few minutes before asking again.
  - Links last one hour and work once. Request a new one if yours has expired.
  - Accounts created with Google or GitHub receive a reset link too; that is
    how you add a password.

MY AUTHENTICATOR CODE IS REFUSED
================================

  Symptom:    "Code is invalid. Try again." during setup
  What to do: Enter the code currently on screen. Make sure your phone's date
              and time are set automatically.

  Symptom:    "2FA setup expired — start again."
  What to do: Select Enable 2FA again and scan the new QR code.

  Symptom:    Codes are refused at sign-in
  What to do: Check the device clock, and that you are using the Auric Artisan
              entry in your app. Use a backup code if needed.

  Symptom:    No app and no backup codes
  What to do: Contact support through the contact page (topic Security) from
              the account's email address.

THE DASHBOARD
=============

  Symptom:    "Could not load subscription details."
  Why:        The plan could not be read
  What to do: Refresh. If it persists, sign out and in again.

  Symptom:    Panels stay empty or show "—"
  Why:        Figures appear only when there is something true to show
  What to do: This is expected for a new account; see Overview section
              [dashboard-overview.txt]

  Symptom:    Usage meters differ between browsers
  Why:        Meters include counts recorded in each browser
  What to do: Expected; they converge as the account's own figures update

  Symptom:    Your work shows nothing although you saved work
  Why:        The library lives in the browser where you saved it
  What to do: Open the dashboard in that browser

  Symptom:    The Change plan cards show rupees
  Why:        These cards show prices in rupees
  What to do: Use the pricing page to see US dollars

  Symptom:    "Changed here, but not saved to your account" on Usage data
  Why:        The choice could not be saved
  What to do: Try the switch again later

  Symptom:    "This list does not say which device you are reading on…"
  Why:        The device list could not mark your device
  What to do: Be careful: every row, including yours, can be revoked

  Symptom:    Sign out everywhere did nothing
  Why:        It needs a second press within about six seconds
  What to do: Press Sign out every other device? to confirm

THE DANGER ZONE WILL NOT UNLOCK
===============================

  Message:    "Not yet — it has to match word for word."
  What to do: Retype the sentence exactly as displayed.

  Message:    "That password is not right."
  What to do: Retype your password.

  Message:    "Set an account password before using these actions."
  What to do: Add a password first: Passwords [passwords.txt].

  Message:    "This page has lost its session token, so it cannot send this
              safely. Reload the page and try again — your password is fine."
  What to do: Reload. This happens if you signed out in another tab, or opened
              the site over an insecure address.

  Message:    "This page could not prove it is still your session…"
  What to do: Reload the page.

  Message:    "The danger zone is not available on this server yet. Nothing is
              wrong with what you typed."
  What to do: The zone shows Unavailable. Contact support to delete the
              account.

  Message:    "Too many attempts. Try again in a few minutes."
  What to do: Wait before trying again.

PAYMENTS AND CHECKOUT
=====================

  Symptom:    Selecting Get Artisan shows "Sign in first…"
  Why:        An account is required before payment
  What to do: Select Sign in or create a free account; you return to the
              pricing page

  Symptom:    The payment is refused because the email is unverified
  Why:        Billing is locked until you verify
  What to do: Verify your email, then try again

  Symptom:    A foreign card is declined at the last step
  Why:        Non-Indian cards and American Express depend on permissions on
              Auric Artisan's payment account
  What to do: Nothing is charged. Try UPI or another card, or tell Auric
              Artisan through the contact page so they can arrange it

  Symptom:    "Checkout is briefly unavailable, so nothing was charged."
  Why:        The checkout could not start
  What to do: Try again in a moment, or contact support

  Symptom:    "Checkout cancelled."
  Why:        The checkout window was closed
  What to do: Nothing was charged; start again if you meant to pay

  Symptom:    The confirmation page stays on Confirming your payment
  Why:        Razorpay's confirmation is delayed
  What to do: Wait; do not pay again. Check Billing history; contact support
              with the order reference if it does not update

  Symptom:    Payment not completed but money left your account
  Why:        A bank hold or a delayed confirmation
  What to do: Holds are released automatically. If the money does not return,
              contact support with the order reference

  Symptom:    The plan does not show after paying
  Why:        The page loaded before the update
  What to do: Refresh the dashboard. Quote the order reference if it still
              does not show

  Symptom:    You were charged twice
  Why:        —
  What to do: Contact support with both order references

  Symptom:    Current Plan on the pricing page does not match
  Why:        You are signed in to another account
  What to do: Check the email in the header account menu

INVOICES
========

  Symptom:    Invoice not found
  What to do: Sign in to the account that made the payment and open the
              invoice from Invoices & Payment History.

  Symptom:    You need a GST tax invoice
  What to do: Auric Artisan is not currently registered for GST; receipts are
              payment receipts. See Invoices and receipts
              [invoices-and-receipts.txt].

API KEYS AND CREDITS
====================

  Symptom:    No create button in Your API Keys
  Why:        Your plan does not include the API
  What to do: API access starts at Specialist

  Symptom:    "You can hold at most 10 active keys."
  Why:        The account limit
  What to do: Revoke a key you do not need

  Symptom:    You lost a key
  Why:        Keys are shown once
  What to do: Create a new key and revoke the old one

  Symptom:    Calls return 402 with `PLAN_UPGRADE_REQUIRED`
  Why:        The account's plan does not include the API
  What to do: Move to a plan with API access

  Symptom:    Calls return 429
  Why:        The monthly allowance is used up, the rate limit was hit, or
              overage cannot continue (no prepaid balance, spend cap or
              ceiling reached)
  What to do: Wait for the reset, slow down, add credits, or raise your cap

  Symptom:    Usage & cost says "That key was refused."
  Why:        The pasted key was revoked or mistyped
  What to do: Paste another key

  Symptom:    The pack picker has nothing to buy
  Why:        Credits belong to plans with API access
  What to do: See API credits and spend controls [api-credits-and-spend.txt]

  Symptom:    "Enter a cap between $0 and $10,000."
  Why:        The cap is out of range
  What to do: Enter a value in range, or 0 for no cap of your own
