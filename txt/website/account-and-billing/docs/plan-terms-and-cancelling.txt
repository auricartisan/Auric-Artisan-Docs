==============================================================================
PLAN TERMS, RENEWAL AND CANCELLING
==============================================================================
Why Auric Artisan plan passes never renew, what happens when a pass ends, how
to stop early, and what happens to tokens and credits.
Area: Website › Account and billing   Updated: 2026-09-25

Every paid plan sold on Auric Artisan is a one-time purchase of a fixed period
— one month or twelve. This page explains what that means in practice.

NOTHING RENEWS
==============

  - No recurring subscription is created when you buy a plan.
  - No payment mandate, standing instruction or saved card is kept for a
    future charge.
  - No second charge is ever scheduled.

So there is no renewal to be surprised by, and nothing to cancel. The
dashboard's plan line says so: for example "Artisan — active until 12 Oct
2026. Does not renew automatically."

  | Note: Some policy pages use the word "subscription". The Terms of Service
  | explain that, on Auric Artisan, it means the plan record and its billing
  | period — not a recurring charge.

WHEN A PASS ENDS
================

The pass ends on its own at the end of the term you bought. You do not need to
do anything.

  - The account returns to the free Apprentice plan.
  - Every tool keeps working.
  - Your saved work is not touched. It lives in your browser, so it stays
    exactly where it is whatever your plan does.
  - The paid conveniences stop: ad-free browsing, watermark-free exports, the
    monthly token allowance and any higher limits.
  - The plan's included API credit allowance ends with it. Purchased API
    credits stay on the account (see below).

BUYING ANOTHER TERM
===================

To continue, buy another pass from the pricing page when the current one ends,
the same way you bought the first. See Buy a plan [buy-a-plan.txt]. An upgrade
starts as soon as the payment clears.

STOPPING EARLY
==============

Because there is no future charge, stopping early means asking for a refund
rather than cancelling:

  - Within 7 days of purchase, a refund is unconditional.
  - After 7 days, refunds are considered case by case.

On a paid pass, Plan & Usage shows a Request a refund button where a cancel
button would otherwise be. It opens the contact page. See Refunds
[refunds.txt].

TOKENS AND API CREDITS WHEN A PLAN ENDS
=======================================

  Balance:      Included monthly tokens
  What happens: Expire at the end of the billing period, as they would anyway

  Balance:      Purchased tokens
  What happens: Stay on the account (token packs are not on sale today)

  Balance:      Included API credits
  What happens: End with the paid period

  Balance:      Purchased API credits
  What happens: Stay on the account and do not expire. They become spendable
                again if the account returns to a plan with API access.

Overage is prepaid, so a plan ending never leaves an outstanding bill for API
usage.

IF RECURRING BILLING IS EVER INTRODUCED
=======================================

The Subscription and Billing Policy and the Refund Policy both promise that,
if a genuine recurring subscription is offered later, checkout will present it
as one, with the renewal date and amount shown before you authorise payment
and a working cancel control in your account — and that the policies will be
updated before the first such charge. The dashboard already has a Cancel
Subscription button for that case; it appears only on a plan that actually
renews.
