==============================================================================
SIGN UP
==============================================================================
Create an Auric Artisan account with an email address and password, or with
Google or GitHub, including every field rule and the human check.
Area: Website › Account and billing   Updated: 2026-09-25

You can create an account in two ways: with an email address and a password,
or with an existing Google or GitHub account. Both are free and neither asks
for a card.

The sign-up form is at https://auricartisan.com/auth/#register. You can also
open https://auricartisan.com/auth/ and select the Create Account tab, or
select Create account in the site header.

SIGN UP WITH EMAIL AND PASSWORD
===============================

  1. Open https://auricartisan.com/auth/ and select Create Account. The panel
     is headed Create your identity.
  2. Fill in the fields:
  Field:         Display Name
  What to enter: The name shown on anything you publish
  Rules:         Required, 2–64 characters

  Field:         Username
  What to enter: Your public handle, typed after the @
  Rules:         Required, 3–30 characters: letters, numbers and underscores
                 only. You can sign in with it instead of your email.

  Field:         Email
  What to enter: Your email address
  Rules:         Required, a valid address

  Field:         Password
  What to enter: Your password
  Rules:         12–128 characters, upper and lower case letters, at least one
                 number and one symbol, no character repeated four or more
                 times in a row, not a common password

  Field:         Confirm Password
  What to enter: The same password again
  Rules:         Must match
  3. As you type the username, a tick means it is available and a cross means
     it is not. Hover over the mark, or read the message under the field, for
     the reason.
  4. As you type the password, a checklist shows which of the six rules are
     met, and a label reads Password strength: Weak, Fair, Good or Strong.
     Select the eye button to show or hide what you typed.
  5. Tick I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Both names are
     links to the full documents.
  6. Select Create Account.
  7. The Auric human check appears the first time you submit, with the message
     "One quick check that you are human, then we will sign you in." Complete
     it (see below), then select Create Account again.

Result: the panel changes to Confirm your inbox. It shows your email address
partly masked, a 24-hour Verification window countdown, a Resend button
(available after 60 seconds) and Continue to limited workspace. Continue with
Verify your email [verify-email.txt].

  | Note: If the email address already has an account, the same confirmation
  | screen appears but offers Return to sign in instead of Continue to limited
  | workspace. No second account is created. Sign in with the existing account, or
  | use Forgot password? if you do not remember its password.

THE AURIC HUMAN CHECK
=====================

The human check replaces a traditional CAPTCHA. It proves a person is at the
keyboard for this one attempt.

  1. A sequence of three marks is shown, with a countdown.
  2. Below it, select the same marks in the same order from the choices. Each
     choice you select fills the next answer slot.
  3. To undo a choice, select its slot and it is removed.
  4. When all three slots are filled, select Verify sequence.
  5. If it matches, the status reads "Human signal verified. This proof is
     single use."

  Message:       "Enter your email or username before verifying the sequence."
  What it means: The check is tied to the address or username in the form.
                 Fill that in first.

  Message:       "Take a moment, then verify the sequence again."
  What it means: You answered faster than a person usually can. Wait a second
                 and select Verify sequence again.

  Message:       "That did not match. Repeat the displayed sequence."
  What it means: The order was wrong. The slots are cleared; try again.

  Message:       "This check expired. Request a new sequence."
  What it means: The countdown ran out. Select New sequence.

  Message:       "A fresh human check is required for another attempt."
  What it means: Each proof works once. After a failed or completed attempt,
                 complete a new check.

A proof belongs to the email or username you typed when you verified. If you
change that field afterwards, the proof no longer applies and you need a new
check.

SIGN UP WITH GOOGLE OR GITHUB
=============================

  1. On the Create Account tab, under or sign up with, select Google or
     GitHub.
  2. Sign in to that provider if asked, and allow Auric Artisan to use your
     account.
  3. You return to Auric Artisan signed in.

Result: your account is created with the provider's email address, which
counts as verified, so there is no verification email to wait for. A welcome
email confirms that you signed in with Google or GitHub, "so there's no
password to remember."

Things to know about provider accounts
--------------------------------------

  - An account created this way has no Auric Artisan password. Actions that
    ask for your password — such as unlocking the danger zone to delete the
    account — ask you to set one first. To set one, use Forgot password? on
    the sign-in page with the account's email address. See Passwords
    [passwords.txt].
  - If an account already exists for the provider's email address, you see
    "This email already has an account. Sign in with the method already
    connected to it." Use that method instead.
  - If the provider account has no verified email address, you see "Your
    provider account has no verified email address." Verify it with the
    provider, or sign up with email and password.

Other messages you might see after returning from a provider are listed in
Troubleshooting [troubleshooting.txt].

AFTER YOU SIGN UP
=================

  - You are on the free Apprentice plan.
  - If you have no username (possible for some older accounts), the dashboard
    asks for one.
  - Next: Verify your email [verify-email.txt], then Getting started with the
    dashboard [getting-started.txt].
