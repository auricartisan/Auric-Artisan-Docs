==============================================================================
PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What the Auric Artisan account stores on its servers, what stays in your
browser, what never reaches Auric Artisan, and how to export or delete it.
Area: Website › Account and billing   Updated: 2026-09-25

This page describes what the account area stores and where. The authoritative
documents are the Privacy Policy (https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/),
the Cookie Policy (https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/) and the Data
Retention and Deletion Policy
(https://auricartisan.com/legal/data-retention-and-deletion-policy/).

STORED WITH YOUR ACCOUNT
========================

  Data: Email address, display name, username
  Why:  To identify you and sign you in

  Data: Bio, website, location, profile photo
  Why:  What you chose to put on your profile

  Data: Password (as a one-way hash only)
  Why:  To check your password; it cannot be read back

  Data: Two-step secret (encrypted) and backup codes
  Why:  To check your codes

  Data: Sessions and sign-in records
  Why:  To list your devices and protect the account

  Data: Dashboard preferences, including the Usage data choice
  Why:  So they follow you between browsers

  Data: Plan, orders and payments
  Why:  To give you what you paid for and keep receipts

  Data: Token and API credit balances and their history
  Why:  To meter and show your usage

  Data: API keys (as a hash and a short prefix only)
  Why:  To recognise your keys; the full key is shown once

  Data: Published portfolio
  Why:  To serve your public page

KEPT IN YOUR BROWSER, NOT THE ACCOUNT
=====================================

  - My Library — palettes, gradients, collections and exports.
  - Portfolio draft and its counters (views, downloads, shares).
  - Site preferences — theme, language, accent colour, text size and other
    settings.
  - Your cookie choice.
  - Dismissed dashboard suggestions and the Your account is nearly there
    dismissal.
  - An API key pasted into Live usage — kept in that browser tab only, and
    forgotten when it closes.

Clearing your browser's site data removes these. They do not follow you to
another device.

NEVER REACHES AURIC ARTISAN
===========================

  - Card numbers, UPI IDs and bank credentials. They are entered into
    Razorpay's checkout and go to Razorpay directly. Auric Artisan keeps only
    order records: what was bought, the amount and the payment reference.
  - Your password in readable form.

COOKIES THE ACCOUNT USES
========================

The Cookie Policy lists two essential cookies for signed-in use: a session
cookie and a cross-site request forgery (CSRF) protection cookie, each lasting
up to 30 days or until you sign out. They cannot be switched off, because the
account cannot work securely without them.

EXPORTING AND DELETING
======================

  - Export — Security & Privacy › Export everything downloads your account
    details, preferences and this browser's library and portfolio draft. See
    Export your data [../docs/export-your-data.txt].
  - Delete — the danger zone deletes the account. Personal details are
    removed; payment records are kept in anonymised form for tax law. See
    Delete your account [../docs/delete-your-account.txt].
  - Other requests — access, correction or a fuller copy: use the contact
    page, topic Privacy or data.
