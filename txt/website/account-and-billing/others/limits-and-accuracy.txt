==============================================================================
LIMITS
==============================================================================
Every rule, size limit, time window and count in the Auric Artisan account
area, and what the dashboard's figures do and do not measure.
Area: Website › Account and billing   Updated: 2026-09-25

All the numbers in the account area in one place. Plan prices and allowances
are on Plans and pricing [../docs/plans-and-pricing.txt]; the pricing page at
https://auricartisan.com/company/pricing/ is authoritative for them.

IDENTITY
========

  Item:  Display name
  Limit: 2–64 characters at sign-up; up to 64 in the profile

  Item:  Username
  Limit: 3–30 characters; letters, numbers and underscores; reserved words and
         near-duplicates refused

  Item:  Username changes
  Limit: Once every 30 days

  Item:  Released usernames
  Limit: Held for 30 days before anyone else can claim them

  Item:  Bio
  Limit: Up to 500 characters

  Item:  Website
  Limit: Up to 200 characters; an `http://` or `https://` address

  Item:  Location
  Limit: Up to 120 characters

  Item:  Profile photo
  Limit: PNG, JPG or GIF, up to 2 MB before upload; cropped to a square and
         made smaller before it is saved

  Item:  Email address
  Limit: Cannot be changed from the dashboard

PASSWORDS AND SIGN-IN
=====================

  Item:  Password length
  Limit: 12–128 characters

  Item:  Password content
  Limit: Upper and lower case, a number, a symbol; no character four times in
         a row; not a common password

  Item:  Session with Remember me
  Limit: Up to 30 days; ends after seven days without use

  Item:  Session without Remember me
  Limit: Until the browser closes; 24 hours at most

  Item:  Second-factor step
  Limit: 10 minutes to enter the code

  Item:  Human check proof
  Limit: Single use, tied to the email or username typed; expires after a
         short countdown

  Item:  Password reset link
  Limit: One hour, single use

  Item:  Verification link
  Limit: 24 hours, single use; a new one replaces the old

  Item:  Resend wait
  Limit: 60 seconds by default between verification emails

TWO-STEP VERIFICATION
=====================

  Item         | Limit
  -------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------
  Code         | Six digits, changing every 30 seconds
  Backup codes | Ten, each single use; replaced with a new ten when you set up again

WHAT THE FIGURES MEAN
=====================

  - Usage meters combine the account's own figures with counts recorded in the
    browser you are using, and show whichever is larger. Daily meters reset
    each day and monthly meters each calendar month, both in UTC.
  - Projects, Your work and the library counts come from the library saved in
    the browser you are using, not from the account.
  - Usage & cost in the API console measures the current calendar month (UTC)
    and needs one of your API keys to read the figures.
  - Projected by period end is a straight-line projection of your pace so far,
    not a forecast.
  - Where in the device list is the network address a sign-in came from, not a
    precise location.
  - Browser and system names in the device list are read from what each
    browser reports, and can be wrong for unusual browsers.
  - The token and credit balances shown are the account's records; purchased
    credits can take a few seconds to appear after payment.
