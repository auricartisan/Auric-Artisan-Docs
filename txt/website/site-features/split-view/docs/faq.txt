==============================================================================
SPLIT VIEW FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers about split view.
Area: Website › Site features › Split view   Updated: 2026-09-25

IS THE LIVE PANE A SCREENSHOT?
==============================

No. It is the real page, fully working, in its own window area. You can click,
type and scroll in it.

DOES SPLIT VIEW CHANGE MY PAGE?
===============================

No. Your original page keeps running untouched behind split view.

CAN I SEE A PAGE AT PHONE WIDTH?
================================

Yes. Set the pane's Viewport to phone (400 pixels) in its More menu. The page
reflows as it would on a phone.

CAN I COMPARE LIGHT AND DARK?
=============================

Yes. Use Mirror so both panes show the same page, then set one pane's Theme to
Light and the other to Dark. Compare then highlights every difference.

CAN I HAVE MORE THAN TWO PANES?
===============================

No. Split view shows two panes.

DOES SPLIT VIEW WORK ON A PHONE?
================================

It opens, but two pages on a small screen are cramped. It is designed for
larger windows.
