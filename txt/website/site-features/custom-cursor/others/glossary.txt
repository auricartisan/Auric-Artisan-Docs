==============================================================================
CUSTOM CURSOR GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Terms used in the custom cursor guides.
Area: Website › Site features › Custom cursor   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term            | Meaning
  ----------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------
  System pointer  | The mouse pointer drawn by your operating system.
  Ring            | The outer part of the custom cursor that changes shape.
  Dot             | The small inner point of the custom cursor, where clicks land.
  Hover           | Resting the pointer over something without clicking.
  Reduced motion  | A device setting that asks apps and websites to limit animation.
  Adaptive colour | The ring's colour, chosen from the surface under it so it stays visible.
