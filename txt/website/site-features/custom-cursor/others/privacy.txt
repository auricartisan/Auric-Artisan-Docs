CUSTOM CURSOR PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What the custom cursor reads and stores.
Area: Website › Site features › Custom cursor   Updated: 2026-09-25

  - The cursor reads the colour of the page under the pointer, inside your
    browser, to choose its own colour. It does not record where you point or
    send pointer movements anywhere.
  - Your on or off choice is kept in this browser as part of your settings.
