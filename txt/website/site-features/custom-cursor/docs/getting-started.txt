==============================================================================
GET STARTED WITH THE CUSTOM CURSOR
==============================================================================
How the custom cursor behaves, when it is hidden, and how to turn it off.
Area: Website › Site features › Custom cursor   Updated: 2026-09-25

THE TWO PARTS
=============

  - The dot is where you are pointing. Clicks happen at the dot.
  - The ring trails slightly behind and changes shape to show what the element
    under it does.

HOW IT REACTS
=============

  - Hover: over links, buttons and other clickable things, the ring grows and
    softly glows (the "magnetic" look). Over text it becomes an I-beam.
  - Click: the ring shrinks briefly.
  - Fast movement: the ring stretches in the direction you move.
  - Rest: when you stop moving for a moment, or the pointer leaves the window,
    the ring fades to half strength.
  - Loading: while a page or tool is busy, the ring turns dashed and spins.
  - Colour: the ring's colour follows the surface under it, and the dot
    switches between dark and light so it is always visible.

The full list of shapes is in the Reference [reference.txt].

WHEN THE CUSTOM CURSOR IS NOT SHOWN
===================================

Your system pointer is used instead when:

  - you switched off Custom cursor in Settings › Comfort;
  - your device uses touch, or has no pointer that can hover (such as most
    phones and tablets);
  - your device's reduced-motion setting is on;
  - reading mode is on.

When the custom cursor is off, the site still shows the right system pointer:
a hand over links and buttons, an I-beam over text fields, and a left-right
arrow over sliders.

TURN IT OFF OR ON
=================

  1. Press `Ctrl` + `,` and select Comfort.
  2. Switch Custom cursor ("The ring and dot that follow your pointer. Turn
     off for your system cursor.") off or on.

The change is immediate, with no reload, and applies to every page and open
tab.
