==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING THE CUSTOM CURSOR
==============================================================================
Fixes for common custom cursor problems.
Area: Website › Site features › Custom cursor   Updated: 2026-09-25

THE CURSOR FEELS SLOW OR LAGS BEHIND
====================================

Why: the ring follows the pointer smoothly on purpose, and on a busy page or a
slower computer it can fall further behind.

What to do: aim with the dot, which is exact. If the lag bothers you, switch
off Custom cursor in Settings › Comfort.

I CANNOT SEE THE CURSOR
=======================

Possible causes and fixes:

  - The ring fades when you stop moving. Move the mouse.
  - The page is still loading. Wait a moment.
  - Switch off Custom cursor in Settings to use your system pointer.

MY LARGE OR HIGH-CONTRAST SYSTEM POINTER IS REPLACED
====================================================

Why: the custom cursor hides the system pointer.

What to do: switch off Custom cursor in Settings › Comfort. Your system
pointer, with its size and colour, returns everywhere on the site.

THE CUSTOM CURSOR DOES NOT APPEAR AT ALL
========================================

Why: it is off in Settings, your device has no hovering pointer, your device
asks for reduced motion, or reading mode is on. This is intended.

SCREEN RECORDINGS SHOW A RING AND DOT
=====================================

Why: the custom cursor is part of the page, so recordings of the page include
it.

What to do: switch it off before recording, or use the recorder's Highlight
the cursor option with the custom cursor off.
