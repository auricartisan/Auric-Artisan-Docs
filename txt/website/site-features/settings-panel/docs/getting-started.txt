==============================================================================
GET STARTED WITH THE SETTINGS PANEL
==============================================================================
Open the Settings panel, learn its parts, and make your first change.
Area: Website › Site features › Settings panel   Updated: 2026-09-25

OPEN THE PANEL
==============

Use whichever way is closest:

  Where you are: Anywhere, with a keyboard
  What to do:    Press `Ctrl` + `,` (`Cmd` + `,` on a Mac). Press it again to
                 close the panel.

  Where you are: Anywhere, with a mouse or touch
  What to do:    Select the page utilities button at the bottom-left of the
                 screen, then Settings.

  Where you are: Right-clicking the page
  What to do:    Choose Site, then Settings. Choose Keyboard shortcuts to open
                 the Shortcuts section directly.

  Where you are: In Spotlight search
  What to do:    Press `Ctrl` + `K`, type `>`, and choose Open settings.

  Where you are: On the clock strip
  What to do:    Right-click the strip and select All settings. The panel
                 opens on the Clock section.

  Where you are: On an info bar
  What to do:    Right-click the bar and select Settings. The panel opens on
                 the Comfort section.

The page utilities bar is the launcher row that also holds the kits. It is
described in Header and navigation
[../../header-and-navigation/docs/page-utilities-bar.txt] and, in more depth,
in Kits [../../../kits/README.txt].

A TOUR OF THE PANEL
===================

The panel has four parts.

  1. The title bar. It reads Your Settings. Drag it to move the panel; the
     position is remembered. On the right are four buttons:
     - Focus mode — the pin-style button shared with the kits. Its tooltip
       reads "Keep Settings open through navigation and while opening other
       kits."
     - Resize — switches between the normal size and a wider size (up to 980
       pixels, or the width of your window).
     - Minimize — collapses the panel to its title bar. Select the title bar,
       or the same button (now Restore), to bring it back.
     - Close settings — the X.
  2. The section list. Appearance, Comfort, Highlights, Clock, Tooltips, Ads
     and Shortcuts. Each shows how many controls it holds. With a section
     selected, press the `Left` and `Right` arrow keys to move between
     sections.
  3. The section body. The controls for the selected section. Most sections
     end with Restore section defaults.
  4. The footer. Export and Import on the left; Reset all and a reminder of
     the `Ctrl` + `,` shortcut on the right.

The panel does not dim or block the page. You can scroll and click the page
while it is open.

  | Note: The Ads section appears only while the site is showing ads. If ads are
  | switched off site-wide, the section is not shown at all.

MAKE YOUR FIRST CHANGE
======================

  1. Open the panel with `Ctrl` + `,`.
  2. In Appearance, look at the Preview card at the top. It shows a heading,
     an accent chip, a link, a line of text and a button in your current look.
  3. Under Accent color, select Sapphire. The preview, the panel and the whole
     page change to blue at once.
  4. Under Text size, select the largest A (X-Large). The text across the site
     grows.
  5. Select Comfort in the section list and switch on Underline links.
  6. Close the panel with `Esc`.

Result: every page you open now uses the sapphire accent, extra-large text and
underlined links in articles. To undo the three changes, open the panel again
and use Restore section defaults in Appearance and in Comfort, or select Reset
all.

CLOSE THE PANEL
===============

  - Press `Esc`. If a drop-down list inside the panel is open, the first `Esc`
    closes the list and the second closes the panel.
  - Press `Ctrl` + `,` again.
  - Select the X in the title bar.

When the panel closes, keyboard focus returns to whatever you used to open it.
