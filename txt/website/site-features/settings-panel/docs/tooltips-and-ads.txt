TOOLTIPS
========

Most buttons, links and cards on the site show a tooltip when you hover over
them or move keyboard focus to them: a small card with a title, a description
and sometimes a picture, tags, a keyboard shortcut and extra details.

The Tooltips section starts with a Live preview (how tooltips will look). It
shows a sample tooltip for the Design System Generator with a picture, three
tags, a description, the `Ctrl` + `K` shortcut and a details table, and it
changes as you flip the switches below it.

  Switch:       Show tooltips
  What it does: Shows the rich hover and focus tooltips across the site. Off
                hides them; the preview reads "Tooltips are turned off".
  Default:      On

  Switch:       Detailed tooltips
  What it does: Expands the collapsible details: preview image, metadata and
                tags. Off keeps tooltips compact: title, description and
                shortcut.
  Default:      On

  Switch:       Advanced tooltips
  What it does: Always expands the data section (category, route, read time).
                Off keeps it collapsed.
  Default:      Off

See more details in one tooltip
-------------------------------

With Advanced tooltips off, a tooltip that has extra data shows an Alt — More
details hint at its foot. While the tooltip is open, press `Alt` to expand the
data section for that tooltip only. The next tooltip starts collapsed again.

Restore section defaults returns the three switches to their defaults.

ADS
===

The Ads section appears only while the site is showing ads. It opens with the
note Ad preferences — "paid perk — free plan shows standard ads".

  Control:      Show ads
  What it does: Shows sponsored slots across the site
  Values:       On, off
  Default:      On

  Control:      Ad density
  What it does: How many in-content slots appear per page
  Values:       Standard, Reduced
  Default:      Standard

  Control:      Ads in panels & search
  What it does: Shows the compact sponsored strip inside kits, Spotlight and
                search results
  Values:       On, off
  Default:      On

Ad density is dimmed while Show ads is off.

Who can change these
--------------------

Ad preferences are a paid perk. Every tool on the site stays free on every
plan either way.

  - Signed out or on the free plan: the controls are shown but disabled.
    Selecting one opens an upgrade message: "Ad preferences are a paid perk —
    upgrade to hide ads or tune where they appear. Every tool stays free
    either way." Nothing changes until you upgrade.
  - On a plan that includes the ad-free perk: the controls work like any other
    setting.

Plans and the ad-free perk are explained in Account and billing
[../../../account-and-billing/README.txt].

Restore section defaults returns the three ad controls to their defaults.
