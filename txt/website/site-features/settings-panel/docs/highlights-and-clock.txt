HIGHLIGHTS: BARS, NOTICES AND CHIPS
===================================

The Highlights section decides which extra strips and labels appear around
page content. The bars themselves are described in Info bars and notices
[../../info-bars-and-notices/README.txt].

  Switch:        Live demo chips
  What it shows: The highlighted "live / demo" chips above demo sections
  Default:       On

  Switch:        Welcome bar
  What it shows: The rotating welcome bar above the page
  Default:       Homepage only

  Switch:        Announcement bar
  What it shows: The site announcements bar
  Default:       Homepage only

  Switch:        Quick links bar
  What it shows: The quick-links bar (documentation, changelog, timeline,
                 blog, learn)
  Default:       Homepage only

  Switch:        Clock strip
  What it shows: A live clock, date and time zone above every page
  Default:       Off

  Switch:        Hero notice
  What it shows: The highlighted notice banner on the homepage hero
  Default:       On

How the "homepage only" switches behave
---------------------------------------

The Welcome, Announcement and Quick links bars start in a third state: shown
on the homepage only. In that state the switch appears on.

  - Switch it off to hide that bar everywhere, including the homepage.
  - Switch it on again to show that bar above every page, not only the
    homepage.
  - To get back to "homepage only", select Restore section defaults.

The Quick links bar also shows by default at the foot of the Collections page,
where it is part of the layout.

Hide a bar or chip from the page itself
---------------------------------------

Each bar, the hero notice and each demo chip has its own ✕ button. Selecting
it has the same effect as switching the matching setting off, so the switch in
Highlights moves to off as well. For demo chips, one ✕ hides every demo chip
on the site.

  | Note: The tooltip on a bar's ✕ says you can turn it back on in "Settings →
  | Comfort". The switches are in the Highlights section.

Restore the section
-------------------

Restore section defaults returns all six switches to the defaults in the table
above. A message confirms "Highlights restored to defaults".

CLOCK: TIME AND DATE DISPLAY
============================

The Clock section controls the live clock strip at the top of pages (when
Clock strip is on in Highlights). The 24-hour choice is also used by the clock
on the screen saver.

At the top, Live preview (your clock right now) shows the time and date
exactly as your settings will draw them, updating every second.

  Control:      24-hour time
  What it does: Shows clocks in 24-hour format instead of AM / PM
  Values:       On, off
  Default:      Off

  Control:      Show seconds
  What it does: Includes seconds on the live clock
  Values:       On, off
  Default:      On

  Control:      Show date
  What it does: Shows the calendar date next to the clock
  Values:       On, off
  Default:      On

  Control:      Timezone
  What it does: The time zone the clock shows (defaults to your location)
  Values:       Auto-detect, or any time zone your browser lists
  Default:      Auto-detect

  Control:      Date format
  What it does: How the date is written
  Values:       Long, DD/MM/YYYY, MM/DD/YYYY
  Default:      Long

The Auto-detect entry names the zone your device reports, for example
"Auto-detect (Asia/Kolkata)". Long writes the weekday, month, day and year in
words, for example "Thursday, September 24, 2026".

Set a fixed time zone
---------------------

  1. Open the panel and select Clock.
  2. Open the Timezone list and choose a zone, for example Europe/London.
  3. The preview switches to that zone at once.

To follow your device again, choose Auto-detect at the top of the list.

Quicker clock controls
----------------------

Right-click the clock strip itself for a small Clock settings menu with a few
extra choices (an ISO date format, and switches for the weekday and the zone
label). It is described in Info bars and notices
[../../info-bars-and-notices/docs/clock-strip.txt]. Its All settings link
opens this Clock section.

Restore the section
-------------------

Restore section defaults returns all five clock settings to their defaults. A
message confirms "Clock restored to defaults".
