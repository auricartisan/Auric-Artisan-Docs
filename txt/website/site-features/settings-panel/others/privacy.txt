==============================================================================
SETTINGS PANEL PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What the Settings panel stores, where, and what it sends over the network.
Area: Website › Site features › Settings panel   Updated: 2026-09-25

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
=========================

  - Every setting in the panel, including your saved colours, is kept in your
    browser's local storage for auricartisan.com.
  - The panel's position and size are kept the same way.
  - Nothing is sent to Auric Artisan when you change a setting. The settings
    work signed out and are not attached to an account.
  - Export creates a file on your own device. It contains only your settings.

WHAT USES THE NETWORK
=====================

  - Opening the Ads section checks your plan in the background, to decide
    whether the ad controls are unlocked. If you are signed in, this uses your
    existing session.
  - Choosing हिन्दी downloads the Hindi dictionaries for the pages you visit
    (see Language [../../language/README.txt]).

LINKS CAN CARRY SETTINGS
========================

If you copy a page link from the site, it can include your appearance settings
that differ from the defaults (for example your accent and text size), so the
person you send it to sees the page as you do. See Shareable links
[../../shareable-links/README.txt] for what a link contains and how to share a
clean one.

REMOVING YOUR SETTINGS
======================

  - Reset all in the panel returns everything to the defaults (saved colours
    are kept until you remove them).
  - Clearing site data for auricartisan.com in your browser removes all stored
    settings.

The site's full policies are on https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/ and
https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/.
