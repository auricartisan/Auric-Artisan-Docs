==============================================================================
SETTINGS PANEL SHORTCUTS
==============================================================================
Keyboard shortcuts that open the Settings panel, move around it, and change
settings from anywhere on the site.
Area: Website › Site features › Settings panel   Updated: 2026-09-25

ANYWHERE ON THE SITE
====================

  Keys                                | Action
  ------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------
  `Ctrl` + `,` (`Cmd` + `,` on a Mac) | Open or close the Settings panel
  `Alt` + `R`                         | Turn reading mode on or off
  `Alt` + `T`                         | Switch theme: Light, then Dark, then System
  `Alt` + `+`                         | Make text one step larger
  `Alt` + `-`                         | Make text one step smaller
  `Alt` + `0`                         | Return text to the default size
  `Alt` + `M`                         | Turn reduced motion on or off

The `Alt` shortcuts are ignored while you type in a text field.

INSIDE THE PANEL
================

  Keys                                       | Action
  -------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------
  `Left` / `Right` arrow on the section list | Move to the previous or next section
  `Tab` / `Shift` + `Tab`                    | Move between controls
  `Space` or `Enter` on a switch or button   | Change it
  `Esc`                                      | Close an open drop-down list first, then the panel
  `Esc` in the Reset all confirmation        | Cancel the reset
  `Tab` in the Reset all confirmation        | Stays inside the confirmation until you choose

For every shortcut on the site, see Keyboard shortcuts
[../../keyboard-shortcuts/README.txt].
