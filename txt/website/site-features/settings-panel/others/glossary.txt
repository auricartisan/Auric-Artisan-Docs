==============================================================================
SETTINGS PANEL GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Plain-language definitions of the terms used in the Settings panel.
Area: Website › Site features › Settings panel   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Accent
  Meaning: The site's highlight colour for links, buttons, active tabs, focus
           rings, kit launchers and tooltip borders. Gold by default.

  Term:    Contrast ratio
  Meaning: The WCAG measure of how different two colours are in lightness,
           from 1:1 (none) to 21:1 (black on white).

  Term:    WCAG
  Meaning: The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, the international
           standard for accessible web content.

  Term:    AA, AAA
  Meaning: WCAG contrast levels for body text: AA needs 4.5:1, the stricter
           AAA needs 7:1.

  Term:    AA Large
  Meaning: A contrast of at least 3:1, accepted by WCAG for large text and
           interface parts but not body text.

  Term:    Eyedropper
  Meaning: A browser tool that lets you click any pixel on your screen to read
           its colour.

  Term:    Hex colour
  Meaning: A colour written as `#` and six hexadecimal digits for red, green
           and blue, for example `#D3AF37`.

  Term:    Local storage
  Meaning: A small store inside your browser where a website keeps data on
           your device.

  Term:    Modeless
  Meaning: A panel that does not block the page behind it. You can keep using
           the page while it is open.

  Term:    Reading mode
  Meaning: A distraction-free layout with a narrow text column, larger type
           and no floating controls.

  Term:    Saved colors
  Meaning: Your own palette of up to 18 accent colours, added only when you
           select the + button.

  Term:    Section
  Meaning: One of the groups in the panel's list: Appearance, Comfort,
           Highlights, Clock, Tooltips, Ads, Shortcuts.

  Term:    System theme
  Meaning: A theme setting that follows your device's light or dark preference
           and changes when it changes.

  Term:    Toast
  Meaning: A short message that appears briefly to confirm an action, such as
           "Settings exported".

  Term:    Tooltip
  Meaning: The small card that appears when you hover over or focus a control,
           explaining what it does.
