ITEMS AND SWITCHES
==================

  Item:                    Welcome bar
  Settings › Highlights switch:Welcome bar
  Default:                 Homepage only
  Hide from the page:      ✕ or right-click › Hide bar

  Item:                    Announcement bar
  Settings › Highlights switch:Announcement bar
  Default:                 Homepage only
  Hide from the page:      ✕ or right-click › Hide bar

  Item:                    Quick-links bar
  Settings › Highlights switch:Quick links bar
  Default:                 Homepage only; also Collections
  Hide from the page:      ✕ or right-click › Hide bar

  Item:                    Clock strip
  Settings › Highlights switch:Clock strip
  Default:                 Off
  Hide from the page:      Switch off in Settings

  Item:                    Hero notice
  Settings › Highlights switch:Hero notice
  Default:                 On
  Hide from the page:      ✕

  Item:                    Demo chips
  Settings › Highlights switch:Live demo chips
  Default:                 On
  Hide from the page:      ✕ on any chip (hides all)

BAR BEHAVIOUR
=============

  Behaviour: Message rotation (welcome, announcement)
  Value:     Every 6 seconds, cross-fade

  Behaviour: Pause
  Value:     While the pointer is over the bar, and while the tab is hidden

  Behaviour: Reduced motion
  Value:     Messages swap without fading

  Behaviour: Quick-links bar
  Value:     Continuous scrolling row of cards

  Behaviour: Order under the header
  Value:     Announcement bar, then clock strip, then page content

BAR MENU (RIGHT-CLICK)
======================

  Heading                                            | Buttons
  ---------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------
  Welcome messages / Site announcements / Site tools | Open, Copy link, Hide bar, Settings

CLOCK SETTINGS MENU (RIGHT-CLICK THE CLOCK STRIP)
=================================================

  Control        | Options                   | Default
  ---------------+---------------------------+------------
  Format         | 12h, 24h                  | 12h
  Date           | Long, D/M/Y, M/D/Y, ISO   | Long
  Show › Seconds | On, off                   | On
  Show › Weekday | On, off                   | On
  Show › Zone    | On, off                   | On
  Time zone      | Auto-detect, or any zone  | Auto-detect
  Reset          | Restores all of the above | —
  All settings → | Opens Settings › Clock    | —
