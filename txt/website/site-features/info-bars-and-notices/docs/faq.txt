==============================================================================
INFO BARS AND NOTICES FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers about the info bars, clock strip and notices.
Area: Website › Site features › Info bars and notices   Updated: 2026-09-25

WHY ARE THE BARS ONLY ON THE HOMEPAGE?
======================================

They are an introduction. On other pages they would push the content down, so
by default they stay on the homepage. You can show them everywhere from
Settings › Highlights.

CAN I SEE THE FIRST-VISIT WELCOME AGAIN?
========================================

It plays once per browser. It plays again only if you clear the site's data in
your browser.

DOES THE SITE SEND NOTIFICATIONS?
=================================

No. All messages appear on the page itself.

CAN I SHOW A WORLD CLOCK FOR ANOTHER CITY?
==========================================

Yes. Right-click the clock strip and choose that city's time zone. The strip
shows one zone at a time.

DOES THE CLOCK STRIP USE MY SETTINGS CLOCK CHOICES?
===================================================

Yes. The strip and Settings › Clock share 24-hour time, seconds, date format
and time zone.
