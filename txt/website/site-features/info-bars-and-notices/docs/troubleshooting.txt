==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING INFO BARS AND NOTICES
==============================================================================
Fixes for common problems with the info bars, clock strip and notices.
Area: Website › Site features › Info bars and notices   Updated: 2026-09-25

A BAR I HID IS BACK
===================

Why: someone switched it on in Settings › Highlights, or you restored the
section's defaults, which brings the bars back on the homepage.

What to do: select its ✕ again.

A BAR NOW SHOWS ON EVERY PAGE, NOT JUST THE HOMEPAGE
====================================================

Why: switching a bar on in Settings shows it everywhere.

What to do: select Restore section defaults in Settings › Highlights to go
back to homepage only.

THE ✕ TOOLTIP SAYS COMFORT, BUT I CANNOT FIND THE SWITCH THERE
==============================================================

Why: the switches are in the Highlights section of Settings, not Comfort.

THE CLOCK SHOWS THE WRONG TIME ZONE
===================================

Why: a fixed zone was chosen in the clock menu or in Settings › Clock, or your
device reports a zone the site could not match exactly (the label then shows
"(default)").

What to do: right-click the strip and choose Auto-detect, or pick your zone
from the list.

THE CLOCK STRIP IS MISSING
==========================

Why: it is off by default, and it is hidden in reading mode.

What to do: switch on Clock strip in Settings › Highlights.

"OFFLINE MODE" WILL NOT GO AWAY
===============================

Why: your device reports that it has no connection. The message stays until
the connection returns.

What to do: check your connection. Saved pages keep working meanwhile.
