==============================================================================
GET STARTED WITH INFO BARS AND NOTICES
==============================================================================
What bars and notices appear where, and how to show or hide each one.
Area: Website › Site features › Info bars and notices   Updated: 2026-09-25

WHAT APPEARS BY DEFAULT
=======================

  Item             | Default
  -----------------+---------------------------------------------------------
  Welcome bar      | Homepage only
  Announcement bar | Homepage only
  Quick-links bar  | Homepage only (also at the foot of the Collections page)
  Clock strip      | Off
  Hero notice      | On (homepage)
  Demo chips       | On (where a page has them)

On every page, the announcement bar sits directly under the header, followed
by the clock strip, then the page content.

HIDE SOMETHING
==============

Select the ✕ at the end of a bar, on the hero notice, or on a demo chip. It
disappears at once, and the matching switch in Settings › Highlights turns
off. A demo chip's ✕ hides every demo chip on the site.

SHOW SOMETHING AGAIN, OR EVERYWHERE
===================================

  1. Press `Ctrl` + `,` and select Highlights.
  2. Switch on the item you want.

For the three bars, switching on shows the bar on every page. To return to the
homepage-only default, select Restore section defaults. Details are in
Settings panel › Highlights and clock
[../../settings-panel/docs/highlights-and-clock.txt].

CHANGES APPLY EVERYWHERE AT ONCE
================================

A change made with a ✕ or in Settings applies straight away to every page,
including pages already open in other tabs.

IN READING MODE AND THE SCREEN SAVER
====================================

The bars and the clock strip are hidden in reading mode and while the screen
saver is showing.
