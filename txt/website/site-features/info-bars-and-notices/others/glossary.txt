==============================================================================
INFO BARS AND NOTICES GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Terms used in the info bars and notices guides.
Area: Website › Site features › Info bars and notices   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term           | Meaning
  ---------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Info bar       | A slim strip under the header: welcome, announcement or quick links.
  Clock strip    | A live clock under the header with the date and time zone.
  Hero notice    | A highlighted banner in the homepage's opening section.
  Demo chip      | A small label marking a live or demo section.
  Time zone      | A region's standard time, such as Asia/Kolkata or Europe/London.
  UTC offset     | How far a time zone is ahead of or behind Coordinated Universal Time.
  ISO date       | A date written year-month-day, for example 2026-09-24.
  Status message | A short message at the edge of the screen about the connection or the app.
