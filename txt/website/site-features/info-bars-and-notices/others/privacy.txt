INFO BARS AND NOTICES PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What the info bars, clock strip and notices store.
Area: Website › Site features › Info bars and notices   Updated: 2026-09-25

  - Which bars and notices you show or hide, and your clock choices, are kept
    in this browser as part of your settings.
  - The first-visit welcome remembers that it has played, in this browser.
  - The clock uses your device's time and time zone. Nothing about them is
    sent to Auric Artisan.
  - Nothing is recorded when you open, copy or hide a bar.
