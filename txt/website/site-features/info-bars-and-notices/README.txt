==============================================================================
INFO BARS AND NOTICES
==============================================================================
The welcome, announcement and quick-links bars, the clock strip, hero notice,
demo chips, the first-visit welcome and the site's status messages.
Area: Website › Site features › Info bars and notices   Updated: 2026-09-25

Around the content of a page, the site can show a few slim strips and small
notices:

  - the welcome bar, which cycles through short introductions to the platform;
  - the announcement bar, with the latest news, such as roadmap updates and
    offline support;
  - the quick-links bar, a scrolling row of cards for Documentation,
    Changelog, Timeline, Blog and Learn;
  - the clock strip, a live clock with the date and your time zone;
  - the hero notice on the homepage, and demo chips that mark live or demo
    sections.

By default the three bars appear on the homepage only, and the clock strip is
off, so ordinary pages start with their own content. Every bar and notice has
a ✕ to hide it, and the Settings panel's Highlights section switches each one
on or off, for the homepage or for every page.

The site also shows short status messages: a brief welcome animation on your
first visit, "Offline mode" and "Back online" when your connection changes,
and prompts when the installed app has an update.

WHO IT IS FOR
=============

Everyone. Keep the bars if you like to see news and shortcuts; hide them for a
quieter page.

WHERE TO FIND IT
================

  - The bars and the clock strip appear under the header.
  - Their switches are in Settings (`Ctrl` + `,`) › Highlights.
  - Right-click a bar or the clock strip for its own small menu.

QUICK START
===========

  1. Open the homepage. The announcement bar sits under the header, cycling
     its messages.
  2. Point at the bar: it pauses. Select a message to open it.
  3. Select the ✕ at the end of the bar. It disappears from every page.
  4. Press `Ctrl` + `,`, select Highlights, and switch Announcement bar back
     on. It now shows on every page.
  5. Switch on Clock strip. A live clock appears under the header.
  6. Right-click the clock and choose 24h.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
===============

  - Show or hide each bar on the homepage only, on every page, or nowhere.
  - Pause a bar by pointing at it; open, copy or hide it from its right-click
    menu.
  - Show a live clock and set its format, date style, weekday, zone label and
    time zone.
  - Hide the hero notice and the demo chips.
