Short answers about eye-rest reminders.

WHY ARE REMINDERS OFF BY DEFAULT?
=================================

They interrupt what you are doing, so the site leaves the choice to you.

WHY TWENTY FEET?
================

Looking into the distance gives your eyes a break from focusing close up. The
reminder's wording follows the common "20-20-20" habit: every 20 minutes, look
at something about 20 feet (6 metres) away for about 20 seconds. The site's
default interval is 20 minutes and its default rest is 30 seconds.

DOES EYE REST WORK WHEN THE TAB IS IN THE BACKGROUND?
=====================================================

The countdown keeps running, so the reminder can be waiting when you return.
It is shown on the page, not as a system notification.

IS EYE REST THE SAME AS THE SCREEN SAVER?
=========================================

No. Eye rest reminds you on a schedule while you work. The screen saver
appears after you stop using the page for a while. See Screen saver
[../../screen-saver/README.txt].

CAN I TRIGGER A REST WITHOUT WAITING?
=====================================

Not directly. Choose the shortest interval, 20m, or wait for the next
reminder.
