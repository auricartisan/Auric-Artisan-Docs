==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING EYE REST
==============================================================================
Fixes for common eye-rest problems.
Area: Website › Site features › Eye rest   Updated: 2026-09-25

I SWITCHED REMINDERS ON BUT NONE APPEAR
=======================================

Possible causes and fixes:

  - The countdown restarts on every page load. If you move between pages
    often, a 20-minute reminder may never be reached. Stay on one page, or
    choose a shorter interval.
  - You selected Not this session earlier in this tab. Open the site in a new
    tab.
  - You selected Turn these off earlier. Switch Eye-rest reminders off and on
    again in Settings › Comfort.
  - A few pages do not include the eye-rest reminder. Try another page.

REMINDERS CAME BACK AFTER I TURNED THEM OFF
===========================================

Why: Turn these off on the card does not change the switch in Settings.
Changing any setting while that switch shows on brings the reminders back.

What to do: switch off Eye-rest reminders in Settings › Comfort.

THE REST ALWAYS LASTS 30 SECONDS
================================

Why: the Rest for choice lasts only until you move to another page or reload.

What to do: choose it again under Change on the card.

THE HORIZON LINE DOES NOT MOVE
==============================

Why: your device's reduced-motion setting is on, so the line stays still. The
countdown number still shows the time left, and the screen still brightens at
the end.

THE SCREEN WENT WHITE AT THE END OF A REST
==========================================

That is the end signal. It lasts about 2.6 seconds, then the page returns.
