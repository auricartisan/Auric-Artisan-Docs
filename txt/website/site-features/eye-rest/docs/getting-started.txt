==============================================================================
GET STARTED WITH EYE REST
==============================================================================
Turn eye-rest reminders on, choose the interval, and turn them off again.
Area: Website › Site features › Eye rest   Updated: 2026-09-25

TURN REMINDERS ON
=================

  1. Press `Ctrl` + `,` to open Settings, then select Comfort.
  2. Switch on Eye-rest reminders ("Gentle reminders to rest your eyes on a
     schedule.").
  3. Under Reminder interval (minutes between reminders), select 20m, 35m, 45m
     or 60m.

The countdown starts at once. Changing the interval later restarts it.

HOW THE COUNTDOWN WORKS
=======================

  - The countdown runs while a page is open. It starts again whenever you load
    a page, including moving to another page or reloading.
  - It keeps running while the tab is in the background, so a reminder can be
    waiting when you come back.
  - After a reminder, the next countdown starts when you finish, end or put
    off the rest.

TURN REMINDERS OFF
==================

Any of these:

  - Settings › Comfort › switch off Eye-rest reminders. This is the most
    reliable way.
  - On a reminder card, select Turn these off.
  - For this session only, select Not this session on a reminder card.

  | Note: Turn these off on the card stops the reminders but does not move the
  | switch in Settings. If you later change any setting while that switch still
  | shows on, the reminders come back. Switch it off in Settings as well to keep
  | them off.
