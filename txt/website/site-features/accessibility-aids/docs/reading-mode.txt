==============================================================================
READING MODE
==============================================================================
Turn reading mode on and off, and understand what it changes on the page.
Area: Website › Site features › Accessibility aids   Updated: 2026-09-25

Reading mode is a distraction-free layout with comfortable typography, for
articles, documentation and other long pages.

TURN IT ON OR OFF
=================

  - Press `Alt` + `R` (not while typing in a field).
  - Or Settings › Comfort › Reading mode.
  - Or right-click the page, choose View, then Reading mode (Leave reading
    mode when it is on).
  - Or, on card pages, right-click a card and select the Reading pill.

To leave it, you can also select the Reading mode ✕ chip at the bottom centre
of the screen.

Reading mode stays on as you move between pages until you turn it off.

WHAT CHANGES
============

  Area:            Text column
  In reading mode: Centred and limited to a comfortable line length (about 68
                   to 76 characters)

  Area:            Body text
  In reading mode: Larger, with generous line spacing

  Area:            Headings
  In reading mode: Clear sizes and spacing, kept to a readable width

  Area:            Links
  In reading mode: Underlined for scanning

  Area:            Images and media
  In reading mode: Kept within the column

  Area:            Header
  In reading mode: Scrolls away with the page instead of staying pinned

  Area:            Floating controls
  In reading mode: Kit launchers, the library launcher and the right-edge menu
                   line are hidden

  Area:            Info bars and clock strip
  In reading mode: Hidden

  Area:            Pointer
  In reading mode: Your system pointer replaces the custom cursor

  Area:            Decorative animation
  In reading mode: Stopped

  Area:            Progress
  In reading mode: A thin bar across the top of the window fills as you scroll

  Area:            Exit
  In reading mode: Reading mode ✕ chip at the bottom of the screen

WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE
====================

Tools keep working normally. The Settings panel, Spotlight (`Ctrl` + `K`), the
guide (`?`) and the right-click menu remain available.
