ACCESSIBILITY AIDS FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers about the site's accessibility aids.
Area: Website › Site features › Accessibility aids   Updated: 2026-09-25

DO I NEED AN ACCOUNT TO USE THESE AIDS?
=======================================

No. All of them work signed out and are kept in your browser.

CAN I CARRY MY SETUP TO ANOTHER COMPUTER?
=========================================

Yes. Use Export in the Settings panel, then Import on the other computer.

IS THERE A DYSLEXIA-FRIENDLY FONT?
==================================

The site does not offer a font switch. Reading mode, larger text and
underlined links can make long text easier to read.

WHERE IS THE SITE'S ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT?
============================================

At https://auricartisan.com/company/accessibility/, also reachable from the
Company menu and the footer.

ARE THE COLOUR TOOLS THEMSELVES ACCESSIBLE?
===========================================

The tools have their own documentation, which describes their keyboard and
screen reader support where it differs from the site's.
