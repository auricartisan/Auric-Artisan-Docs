==============================================================================
GET STARTED WITH THE ACCESSIBILITY AIDS
==============================================================================
Set up the site's text size, contrast, motion, transparency, link and pointer
options for your needs.
Area: Website › Site features › Accessibility aids   Updated: 2026-09-25

All of these apply to every page at once and are remembered in this browser.

MAKE TEXT LARGER
================

  - Press `Alt` + `+` (one step larger) or `Alt` + `-` (one step smaller).
    `Alt` + `0` returns to the default.
  - Or open Settings › Appearance › Text size and choose Small (90%), Default,
    Large (112%) or X-Large (125%).

This scales the site's text. Your browser's own zoom (`Ctrl` + `+`) also works
and scales everything, including layout.

STRONGER CONTRAST
=================

Settings › Comfort › Boost contrast draws secondary text in the stronger text
colour and makes borders darker, in both themes. You can also choose the theme
that suits you in Appearance › Theme.

LESS MOTION
===========

  - Settings › Comfort › Reduce motion (or `Alt` + `M`) makes animations and
    transitions finish instantly and turns smooth scrolling into jumps.
  - If your device's reduced-motion setting is on, the site also hides the
    custom cursor, stops the eye-rest horizon from animating, shortens the
    first-visit welcome, and swaps info-bar messages without fading.

CLEARER SURFACES
================

Settings › Comfort › Reduce transparency replaces blurred, see-through panels
with solid ones.

EASIER-TO-SPOT LINKS
====================

Settings › Comfort › Underline links always underlines links in articles and
other text.

YOUR SYSTEM POINTER
===================

Settings › Comfort › Custom cursor off restores your own pointer, including
any large or high-contrast pointer you set in your operating system.

FEWER DISTRACTIONS
==================

  - Reading mode (`Alt` + `R`) for long pages. See Reading mode
    [reading-mode.txt].
  - Hide the info bars, hero notice and demo chips with their ✕ or in Settings
    › Highlights.
  - Turn off tooltips, or make them shorter, in Settings › Tooltips.

LANGUAGE
========

The whole interface is available in Hindi. See Language
[../../language/README.txt].

KEEP YOUR SETUP
===============

Export your settings from the Settings panel (Export) and import them on
another browser. See Settings panel › Back up, restore and reset
[../../settings-panel/docs/back-up-restore-and-reset.txt].
