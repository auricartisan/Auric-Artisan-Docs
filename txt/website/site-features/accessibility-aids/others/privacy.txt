What the accessibility aids store.
Area: Website › Site features › Accessibility aids   Updated: 2026-09-25

  - Your choices are kept in this browser as part of your settings. They are
    not sent to Auric Artisan or attached to an account.
  - The site reads your device's colour-scheme and reduced-motion preferences
    only to adjust the page; it does not record them.
  - A page link you copy can carry appearance settings that differ from the
    defaults, such as text size (see Shareable links
    [../../shareable-links/README.txt]).
