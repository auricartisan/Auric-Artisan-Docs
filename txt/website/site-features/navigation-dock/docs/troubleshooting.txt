==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING THE NAVIGATION DOCK
==============================================================================
Fixes for common problems with the navigation dock.
Area: Website › Site features › Navigation dock   Updated: 2026-09-25

I CANNOT FIND THE DOCK
======================

Why: it collapses to a thin, faint line at the edge of the window a moment
after the page loads. On phones it has no visible button on the narrowest
screens.

What to do: look halfway down the left edge (or the right edge, if you moved
it) and point at the line. On a phone, open the page utilities bar and select
Navigate.

THE DOCK CLOSES BEFORE I CAN USE IT
===================================

Why: it collapses half a second after the pointer leaves it.

What to do: keep the pointer over the dock, or tab into it with the keyboard;
it stays open while a button in it has focus. The outline keeps it open too.

BACK AND FORWARD ARE GREYED OUT
===============================

Why: there is no page in that direction in this tab's history. If you opened
the page in a new tab, there is nothing to go back to.

SHARE ONLY COPIED THE LINK
==========================

Why: your browser or device has no share sheet available to web pages, so the
dock copies the link instead and shows a tick.

What to do: paste the link into the app you want to share it with.

THE COPIED LINK IS VERY LONG
============================

Why: the address includes the page's remembered state after `#p=`, such as
your scroll position and values you entered. See Shareable links
[../../shareable-links/README.txt].

What to do: to share a clean link, scroll to the top of the page and clear any
values you typed before copying, or delete the part from `#p=` onwards after
pasting.

THE OUTLINE SAYS "NO SECTIONS ON THIS PAGE"
===========================================

Why: the page's main content has no headings the outline can list.

What to do: use Scroll to top and Scroll to bottom, or the page guide (`?`).

THE DOCK COVERS PART OF THE PAGE
================================

What to do: drag it up or down with the grip, or move it to the other side
with Flip dock side. On a phone, tap outside it to close it.
