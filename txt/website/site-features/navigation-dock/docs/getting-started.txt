==============================================================================
GET STARTED WITH THE NAVIGATION DOCK
==============================================================================
Open the navigation dock and learn what each of its buttons does.
Area: Website › Site features › Navigation dock   Updated: 2026-09-25

OPEN THE DOCK
=============

When a page loads on a computer, the dock shows fully for a moment and then
tucks itself away into a thin gold line at the edge of the window. To bring it
back:

  - point at the line; or
  - click it; or
  - move keyboard focus into it with `Tab`.

It tucks away again about half a second after your pointer leaves, unless the
page outline is open or a button in the dock has keyboard focus.

On a phone, open the page utilities bar at the bottom-left and select
Navigate.

THE BUTTONS
===========

From top to bottom (left to right on a phone):

  Button:       Grip (six dots)
  What it does: Drag to move the dock. Tooltip: "Drag to move". Not shown on
                phones.

  Button:       Back
  What it does: Goes back one page. Greyed out when there is nothing to go
                back to.

  Button:       Forward
  What it does: Goes forward one page. Greyed out when there is nothing ahead.

  Button:       Reload
  What it does: Reloads the page.

  Button:       Page outline
  What it does: Opens the On this page list of headings.

  Button:       Open split view
  What it does: Opens split view with this page (see Split view
                [../../split-view/README.txt]).

  Button:       Record screen (Alt+Shift+R)
  What it does: Opens the screen recorder (see Screen recorder
                [../../screen-recorder/README.txt]).

  Button:       Copy link
  What it does: Copies the page's address. The icon briefly becomes a tick.

  Button:       Share
  What it does: Opens your device's share sheet; without one, copies the link
                and shows a tick.

  Button:       Scroll to top
  What it does: Scrolls smoothly to the top. The ring around it shows how far
                down you are.

  Button:       Scroll to bottom
  What it does: Scrolls smoothly to the end of the page.

  Button:       Flip dock side
  What it does: Moves the dock to the other edge. Not shown on phones.

Hover or focus a button to see its name in a tooltip.

  | Note: Copy link and Share copy the address exactly as it is in the address
  | bar, which can include the page's remembered state, such as your scroll
  | position and the values you typed into a tool. See Shareable links
  | [../../shareable-links/README.txt].

TRY IT
======

  1. Open a long page, such as https://auricartisan.com/company/about/.
  2. Scroll halfway down. Point at the dock: the ring around Scroll to top is
     half full.
  3. Select Page outline. The heading of the section you are reading is
     highlighted.
  4. Select the first heading. The page scrolls up to it.
  5. Select Copy link and paste the address somewhere. Opening it later
     returns you to the same place on the page.
