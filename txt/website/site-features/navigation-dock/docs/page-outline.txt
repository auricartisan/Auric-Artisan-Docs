==============================================================================
JUMP AROUND WITH THE PAGE OUTLINE
==============================================================================
Use the dock's page outline to see the page's sections, find where you are,
and jump to any heading.
Area: Website › Site features › Navigation dock   Updated: 2026-09-25

The Page outline button opens a panel titled On this page beside the dock. It
lists the headings of the page you are on and highlights the one you are
reading.

OPEN THE OUTLINE AND JUMP TO A SECTION
======================================

  1. Point at the dock and select Page outline (the list icon).
  2. The On this page panel opens. Main headings are listed normally;
     sub-headings are indented and slightly smaller.
  3. The heading of the section you are reading is highlighted in the accent
     colour. As you scroll, the highlight moves with you.
  4. Select a heading.

Result: the page scrolls smoothly to that heading, leaving room for the site
header above it, and the panel closes.

CLOSE THE OUTLINE
=================

Select Page outline again, press `Esc`, or click anywhere outside the panel
and the dock.

WHAT THE OUTLINE LISTS
======================

  - The main headings and their sub-headings (heading levels 1, 2 and 3) in
    the page's main content.
  - Headings in the header, footer and navigation areas are left out, as are
    hidden headings and headings longer than 90 characters.
  - The list is rebuilt every time you open it, so headings that appear later
    (for example in a tool's results) are included.
  - On a page without headings, the panel reads "No sections on this page".

OTHER OUTLINES ON THE SITE
==========================

Long company, pricing, legal and security pages also have a section rail
pinned under the header. It is described in Guide and page outline
[../../guide-and-page-outline/docs/section-rail.txt]. The page guide (`?`)
lists the page's tour stops. See Guide and page outline
[../../guide-and-page-outline/README.txt].
