==============================================================================
NAVIGATION DOCK GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Terms used in the navigation dock guides.
Area: Website › Site features › Navigation dock   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Dock
  Meaning: The floating rail of navigation buttons on the edge of the window.

  Term:    Collapsed
  Meaning: The dock's resting state: a thin line at the edge (desktop) or a
           small round button (phone).

  Term:    Grip
  Meaning: The six-dot handle at the top of the dock, used to drag it.

  Term:    Page outline
  Meaning: The On this page panel listing the page's headings.

  Term:    Progress ring
  Meaning: The ring around Scroll to top that fills as you scroll down the
           page.

  Term:    Share sheet
  Meaning: Your device's own panel for sending a link to another app or
           person.

  Term:    Page state
  Meaning: Details the site keeps in the address after `#p=`, such as your
           scroll position.
