==============================================================================
NAVIGATION DOCK
==============================================================================
The floating navigation rail with Back, Forward, Reload, page outline, split
view, recorder, copy, share and scroll controls.
Area: Website › Site features › Navigation dock   Updated: 2026-09-25

The navigation dock is a slim floating rail on the edge of every page. It
gathers the controls you reach for while reading or working: Back, Forward and
Reload; a Page outline that lists the page's headings and jumps to them; Open
split view; Record screen; Copy link and Share; and Scroll to top (with a ring
that fills as you read) and Scroll to bottom.

The dock is independent of the header, so it stays with you as you scroll.
When you are not using it, it tucks away to a thin gold line at the edge of
the window and slides out when you point at it. You can drag it up or down,
and move it to the other side of the screen; it remembers where you put it.

On phones it becomes a horizontal bar at the bottom of the screen, opened from
the page utilities bar.

WHO IT IS FOR
=============

  - Readers of long pages who want to jump between sections or back to the
    top.
  - Anyone using the site as an installed app, where the browser's own Back
    and Reload buttons may be hidden.
  - People who share pages often.

WHERE TO FIND IT
================

  - Desktop and tablets: a thin gold line halfway down the left edge of the
    window (or the right edge, if you moved it). Point at it to open the dock.
  - Phones: open the page utilities bar at the bottom-left and select
    Navigate.

QUICK START
===========

  1. Move your pointer to the thin line at the left edge of the window. The
     dock slides out.
  2. Select Page outline (the list icon). A panel titled On this page lists
     the page's headings.
  3. Select a heading. The page scrolls to it and the panel closes.
  4. Select Scroll to top. The ring around it shows how far down the page you
     were.
  5. Drag the dotted grip at the top of the dock to the right-hand half of the
     screen. The dock moves to the right edge and stays there on every page.
  6. Move your pointer away. After a moment the dock tucks back into a thin
     line.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
===============

  - Go back, go forward and reload, even when the browser's own buttons are
    hidden.
  - Jump to any heading on the page and see which section you are in.
  - Open split view or the screen recorder.
  - Copy the page link, including what you have changed on the page, or share
    it.
  - Scroll to the top or bottom, and see your reading progress.
  - Move the dock and keep it where you like.
