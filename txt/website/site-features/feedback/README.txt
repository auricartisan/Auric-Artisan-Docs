==============================================================================
FEEDBACK
==============================================================================
The occasional feedback prompt, the quick star rating, the six-question survey
and bug reports, and how often the site asks.
Area: Website › Site features › Feedback   Updated: 2026-09-25

Auric Artisan asks for feedback rarely and quietly. After you have spent
twenty minutes of active time on a page, a small Feedback panel may appear:
"You have been here a little while — how is it going?" You can give a star
rating and a comment, open a six-question survey, or report a bug with details
of what happened. You can also put it off with Maybe later, or tell the site
never to ask again with Don't ask again.

Contact details are always optional. You can send any feedback anonymously,
and adding your name or email is a separate, equal choice.

The site does not ask on pages where a prompt would get in the way: signing
in, checking out, paying, or reading legal pages. After any prompt, it waits
at least a week before asking again on this device.

To give feedback whenever you like, use the Feedback panel in the Feature Kit
(see Kits [../../kits/README.txt]).

WHO IT IS FOR
=============

Anyone with something to say: praise, a complaint, a missing feature, or a
bug.

WHERE TO FIND IT
================

  - The automatic prompt, occasionally, after twenty minutes of active use.
  - The Feature Kit's Feedback panel, at any time.

QUICK START
===========

  1. When the Feedback panel appears, select a number of stars (or press `1`
     to `5`).
  2. The question below changes to suit your rating. Type an answer if you
     like.
  3. Select Continue.
  4. Choose Send anonymously, or Add contact details to give a name or email.
  5. A thank-you message confirms that your feedback has been received.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
===============

  - Rate your experience from one to five stars, with an optional comment.
  - Answer a short, optional six-question survey.
  - Report a bug with what happened, what you expected, how to reproduce it,
    and how badly it affects you.
  - Choose whether to be contacted.
  - Put the prompt off, or stop it for good.
