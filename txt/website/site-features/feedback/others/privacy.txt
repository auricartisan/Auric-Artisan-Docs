==============================================================================
FEEDBACK PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What feedback sends to Auric Artisan and what it keeps on your device.
Area: Website › Site features › Feedback   Updated: 2026-09-25

SENT WHEN YOU SUBMIT
====================

  - Rating and comment: your stars and what you typed, and the page you were
    on.
  - Survey: your answers.
  - Bug report: what you typed, the severity, and page details: the page
    address, window and screen size, browser and platform, language, theme,
    and recent page errors where available.
  - Contact details: only if you choose Add contact details and fill them in.

Nothing is sent until you select a send button.

KEPT ON THIS DEVICE
===================

  - When you were last asked and how you answered (to respect the quiet
    periods and Don't ask again).
  - Unsent bug-report drafts.
  - Feedback waiting to be sent while you are offline.

To remove these, clear the site's data in your browser.
