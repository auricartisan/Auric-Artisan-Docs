WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN SPOTLIGHT AND THE SEARCH PAGE?
=============================================================

Spotlight is the quick box that floats over any page and shows up to 8
results. The search page at https://auricartisan.com/search/ shows every
result with filters, previews and answers. View all results for "…" moves your
query from one to the other. The search page is documented in Search
[../../../search/README.txt].

DOES SPOTLIGHT WORK OFFLINE?
============================

Searching the site can work offline once you have prepared the offline version
on the Download page, because the site's search index is part of it (see
Download and offline [../../../download-and-offline/README.txt]). Opening a
result offline works only for pages that were saved. The Web tab always needs
a connection.

CAN I SEARCH IN HINDI?
======================

Commands can be found by their Hindi or English names. Page search matches the
English text of pages, so English words give the best results.

WHY DOES A RESULT I OPEN OFTEN MOVE UP?
=======================================

Spotlight gives a small boost to pages you have opened from search before, in
this browser.

DOES SPOTLIGHT SHOW ADS?
========================

It can show a compact sponsored strip while ads are running on the site. The
Ads in panels & search setting controls it on plans that include ad
preferences (see Settings panel › Tooltips and ads
[../../settings-panel/docs/tooltips-and-ads.txt]).

WHAT DOES `CTRL` + `SHIFT` + `K` DO?
====================================

It opens the Feature Kit (calculator, converter and other small tools), which
is separate from Spotlight. See Kits [../../../kits/README.txt].
