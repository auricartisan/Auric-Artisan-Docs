==============================================================================
SPOTLIGHT PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What Spotlight stores and what it sends over the network.
Area: Website › Site features › Search and Spotlight   Updated: 2026-09-25

STORED IN YOUR BROWSER
======================

  - Your last six searches (the Recent list). Select Clear to delete them.
  - A small record of which results you have opened from search, used only to
    rank those pages a little higher for you next time.

Both stay in this browser and are not sent to Auric Artisan.

USES THE NETWORK
================

  - Site searches run inside your browser against the site's search index,
    which is downloaded from auricartisan.com. Your query is not sent
    anywhere.
  - Web searches send your query to the site's web search service to find
    matches in trusted outside sources.
  - Opening a web result opens that outside website in a new tab.
