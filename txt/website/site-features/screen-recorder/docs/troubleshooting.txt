==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING THE SCREEN RECORDER
==============================================================================
Fixes for common screen recorder problems.
Area: Website › Site features › Screen recorder   Updated: 2026-09-25

THE SCREEN SOURCES ARE GREYED OUT
=================================

Why: "This browser cannot capture the screen." Most mobile browsers, and some
older desktop browsers, do not let web pages capture the screen.

What to do: use a current desktop browser such as Chrome, Edge or Firefox, or
use Tool canvas on a tool page.

TOOL CANVAS IS GREYED OUT
=========================

Why: "No recordable canvas on this page." The page has no visible canvas
larger than 80 by 80 pixels.

What to do: open a tool that draws on a canvas, or choose another source.

NOTHING HAPPENED AFTER START RECORDING
======================================

Why: you cancelled or closed your browser's sharing dialog, or denied
permission.

What to do: select Start recording again and allow sharing. If you denied it
permanently, allow screen sharing for auricartisan.com in your browser's site
settings.

THE RECORDING BAR IS IN MY VIDEO
================================

Why: with This tab, the bar is part of the page, so it is recorded.

What to do: collapse it to a dot, record a Region of this page that leaves it
out, or use Screen or window and pick another window.

THERE IS NO SOUND
=================

Possible causes:

  - Tab audio was off, or the page made no sound.
  - In your browser's sharing dialog, the option to share audio was not ticked
    (some browsers only offer audio for tabs).
  - The microphone was refused ("Microphone unavailable — recording without
    it.").

THE RECORDING STOPPED BY ITSELF
===============================

Why: it reached 30 minutes, you pressed your browser's Stop sharing, or the
shared window was closed.

THE RECORDING WAS DOWNLOADED INSTEAD OF SAVED
=============================================

Why: the browser refused to store it, usually because storage is full or the
browser is in a private mode.

What to do: delete old recordings in My recordings, or keep the downloaded
file.

EXPORTING A GIF IS SLOW OR THE GIF IS LARGE
===========================================

Why: GIFs are made in your browser and hold every frame as an image.

What to do: trim the recording first, record at a lower resolution, or
download the video instead.

TRIMMING TAKES A LONG TIME
==========================

Why: trimming re-encodes the clip in real time, so it takes as long as the
part you keep.

MY RECORDINGS DISAPPEARED
=========================

Why: they are stored in this browser. Clearing site data, using a private
window, or a different browser or profile means they are not there.

What to do: download recordings you want to keep.
