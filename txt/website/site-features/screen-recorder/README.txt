==============================================================================
SCREEN RECORDER
==============================================================================
Record your screen, a window, this tab, a region of the page or a tool's
canvas, and export video, animated GIF or PNG images.
Area: Website › Site features › Screen recorder   Updated: 2026-09-25

Every page on auricartisan.com has a built-in recorder. Press `Alt` + `Shift`
+ `R`, choose what to record, and start. You can capture:

  - Screen or window — any screen, window or tab you pick in your browser's
    sharing dialog;
  - This tab — the page you are on;
  - Region of this page — a rectangle you draw, or a part of the page you
    click;
  - Tool canvas — a tool's drawing surface, captured pixel for pixel.

While you record, a small bar shows the time and file size, with pause, stop
and discard buttons. When you stop, the recording is kept on your device under
My recordings, where you can play it, rename it, trim it, and export it as a
video file, an animated GIF, a ZIP of PNG frames or a single PNG still.

Recordings are made and stored entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

WHO IT IS FOR
=============

  - Designers and developers showing a tool, a palette or a bug to someone
    else.
  - Teachers and writers making short clips or GIFs of colour tools.
  - Anyone filing a bug report who wants to show what happened.

WHERE TO FIND IT
================

  - Press `Alt` + `Shift` + `R` on any page.
  - Select Record screen in the navigation dock.

The recorder works in desktop browsers that let web pages capture the screen,
such as Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Mobile browsers generally cannot capture the
screen; the Tool canvas source can still work where a page has a canvas.

QUICK START
===========

  1. Press `Alt` + `Shift` + `R`. The Your Recorder dialog opens.
  2. Under Recording source, choose This tab.
  3. Leave the capture settings as they are and select Start recording.
  4. Your browser asks to share this tab. Allow it.
  5. After a 3-second countdown, recording starts and the recording bar
     appears.
  6. Do what you want to show, then select Stop and save on the bar (or press
     `Alt` + `Shift` + `R`).
  7. My recordings opens with your new clip at the top. Select GIF to export
     it as an animated GIF.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
===============

  - Record a screen, window, tab, page region or tool canvas.
  - Choose frame rate, quality, maximum resolution and a countdown.
  - Add microphone narration and the page's own sound; highlight the pointer.
  - Pause, resume, stop or discard a recording.
  - Play, rename, trim, download and delete recordings.
  - Export an animated GIF, a ZIP of PNG frames or a PNG still.
