==============================================================================
SCREEN RECORDER SHORTCUTS
==============================================================================
Keyboard shortcuts for the screen recorder.
Area: Website › Site features › Screen recorder   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Keys:   `Alt` + `Shift` + `R`
  Action: Open the recorder; while recording, stop and save

  Keys:   `Alt` + `Shift` + `P`
  Action: Pause or resume a recording

  Keys:   `Esc`
  Action: Close the dialog, recordings list or player; cancel the region
          picker

  Keys:   Arrow keys on the dialog grip
  Action: Move the dialog; hold `Shift` for finer steps

`Alt` + `Shift` was chosen to avoid browser shortcuts: `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `R`
is your browser's hard reload. The full list is in Keyboard shortcuts
[../../keyboard-shortcuts/README.txt].
