==============================================================================
GUIDED TOURS
==============================================================================
Take a guided tour of any page, move through its stops, leave, resume, replay
and skip.
Area: Website › Site features › Guide and page outline   Updated: 2026-09-25

A guided tour walks through the important areas of the page you are on. Each
stop highlights one area and explains it in a small card beside it.

START A TOUR
============

  1. Press `?` to open the guide.
  2. The tour card at the top reads Take the guided tour, with the number of
     stops, for example "11 stops · leave at any point".
  3. Select it (its action reads Start the tour).

To start at a particular stop, select that stop under On this page or Getting
around.

THE TOUR CARD
=============

  What it shows: Take the guided tour — "N stops · leave at any point"
  When:          You have not taken this page's tour

  What it shows: Pick up where you left off — "You stopped at X of N · leave
                 at any point", action Resume at stop X
  When:          You left this page's tour part-way

  What it shows: Replay the tour — "N stops on this page · you have finished
                 this one"
  When:          You finished this page's tour

  What it shows: Start over from stop 1 (below the card)
  When:          You left part-way and want the whole tour again

A progress bar on the card shows how much of the tour you have seen. Progress
is kept for each page separately.

If a page has nothing to tour, the guide says "This page has no tourable
sections yet, but the paths below will still help you move around."

MOVE THROUGH A TOUR
===================

The highlighted area is outlined on the page, which scrolls to bring it into
view. The card beside it shows:

  - a rail of dots, one per stop;
  - the position, for example "3 of 11", and whether the stop is about this
    page or the whole site;
  - the stop's title and explanation;
  - sometimes a note;
  - What to look for: a list of details you can open.

Controls:

  Control:      Next
  Key:          `Right`
  What it does: Next stop

  Control:      Back
  Key:          `Left`
  What it does: Previous stop (unavailable on the first stop)

  Control:      Finish
  Key:          —
  What it does: On the last stop, ends the tour and marks it finished

  Control:      X (Leave the tour)
  Key:          `Esc`
  What it does: Leaves the tour; your place is remembered

  Control:      Skip to this page’s N stops
  Key:          —
  What it does: Shown on the site-wide stops at the start, to jump to the
                stops about this page

While the tour is running, `Tab` stays inside the tour card.

THE STOPS EVERY PAGE SHARES
===========================

Every tour includes a few stops about the site itself, the same everywhere:

  - Start from the top — the header;
  - Search when the path is unclear — search and `Ctrl` + `K`;
  - Keep useful work close — your library and workspace;
  - Match the viewing mode — the theme button;
  - Use footer links for trust and support — the footer.

Once you have seen them, the guide folds them away under Getting around, and
Skip to this page’s N stops lets you pass them.

WHEN AN EYE REST APPEARS
========================

A tour is hidden while an eye rest is on screen, and reappears afterwards.
