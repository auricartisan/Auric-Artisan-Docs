==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING THE GUIDE AND SECTION RAIL
==============================================================================
Fixes for common problems with the guide, tours and section rail.
Area: Website › Site features › Guide and page outline   Updated: 2026-09-25

PRESSING `?` DOES NOTHING
=========================

Possible causes and fixes:

  - The cursor is in a text field. Click an empty part of the page first.
  - A modifier key is held, or your keyboard layout needs `Shift` for `?`
    (that is fine) but also another key such as `Alt Gr` (which is not). Use
    the header's Guide button instead.
  - The page is still loading. Wait a moment.
  - A tour or the guide is already open.

THE TOUR HIGHLIGHTS NOTHING, OR THE WRONG PLACE
===============================================

Why: a stop points at an area the page has not shown yet, for example one that
loads late, is hidden on small screens or is folded away.

What to do: select Next to move on, or widen the window and try again.

THE TOUR CARD SAYS I FINISHED, BUT I WANT TO SEE IT AGAIN
=========================================================

Select Replay the tour.

THE TOUR STARTS IN THE MIDDLE
=============================

Why: you left part-way through last time. The card offers Pick up where you
left off.

What to do: select Start over from stop 1.

THE SECTION RAIL COVERS PART OF A HEADING AFTER JUMPING
=======================================================

Why: on some pages the header scrolls away during the jump, leaving the
heading slightly lower than expected.

What to do: scroll a little; the correct section stays highlighted.

A LEGAL SECTION STAYED FOLDED
=============================

What to do: select its heading to open it. Jumping from the rail or a direct
link normally opens it for you.
