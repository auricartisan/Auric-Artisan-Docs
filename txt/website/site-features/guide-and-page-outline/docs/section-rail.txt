==============================================================================
THE SECTION RAIL
==============================================================================
Use the "On this page" section rail on long company, pricing, legal and trust
pages.
Area: Website › Site features › Guide and page outline   Updated: 2026-09-25

Long pages about the company, pricing, policies and security have a section
rail: a row of links to the page's sections, labelled "On this page", near the
top of the page.

WHERE IT APPEARS
================

  - Company pages: About, Pricing, Accessibility and FAQ.
  - Every legal page, such as Privacy, Terms, Cookies, Refund Policy,
    Licenses, Security, Transparency, Acceptable Use Policy, Content &
    Intellectual Property, Data Retention & Deletion, Subscription & Billing
    and the Extension privacy policy.
  - The Trust Center at https://auricartisan.com/security/.

HOW IT BEHAVES
==============

  - Pinned. As you scroll down, the rail sticks just below the site header, so
    it is always in reach. It gains a shadow once it is pinned.
  - Shows where you are. The link for the section you are reading is
    highlighted. A section counts as "current" once its heading has passed a
    line a little below the rail.
  - Jumps. Select a link to scroll to that section. On legal pages, whose
    sections can be folded, jumping to a folded section opens it.
  - Narrow screens. The rail scrolls sideways, and keeps the current section's
    link in view.
  - At the very end of the page, the last section is highlighted.

The links are ordinary page links, so they also work with the keyboard (`Tab`
and `Enter`), and you can copy one to share a link straight to a section.
Opening such a link on a legal page opens that section.
