COOKIE CONSENT AND PRIVACY CHOICES
==============================================================================
The cookie notice, the four cookie categories, and how to accept, reject,
customise or withdraw your choice.
Area: Website › Site features › Cookie consent and privacy   Updated: 2026-09-25

On your first visit, after the short welcome animation, a bar appears at the
bottom of the page: We value your privacy. It explains that Auric Artisan uses
cookies and local storage to keep the site secure, remember your preferences
and understand basic, anonymous usage, and it offers three choices: Accept
All, Reject All and Customise. The page stays readable and usable while the
bar is showing.

Your choice covers four categories: Essential (always on), Functional,
Analytics and Marketing. You can change it at any time on the Cookie Policy
page, which lists every cookie in each category with its purpose and how long
it lasts, and where you can also clear the site's non-essential cookies or
withdraw your consent.

WHO IT IS FOR
=============

Every visitor. The choice decides whether the site may measure how pages are
used, and which optional cookies it may set.

WHERE TO FIND IT
================

  - The consent bar on your first visit.
  - The Cookie Policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/, section
    Manage Cookie Preferences.
  - The Cookies link in the footer.

QUICK START
===========

  1. On your first visit, read the bar at the bottom of the page.
  2. Select Customise. The Cookie Policy page opens at Manage Cookie
     Preferences.
  3. Switch Analytics Cookies on or off as you prefer. Essential Cookies are
     always on.
  4. Select Save Preferences. A message confirms "Cookie preferences saved
     successfully."
  5. The section now shows Consent recorded with the date and time.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
===============

  - Accept all optional cookies, reject them, or choose by category.
  - See every cookie the site lists, with its purpose, duration and type.
  - Change your choice at any time.
  - Clear all non-essential cookies, or withdraw your consent.
