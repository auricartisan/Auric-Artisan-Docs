COOKIE CONSENT FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers about cookies and privacy choices on Auric Artisan.
Area: Website › Site features › Cookie consent and privacy   Updated: 2026-09-25

DOES THE SITE SELL MY DATA?
===========================

The consent notice states that your data is never sold. The full terms are in
the Privacy Policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/.

ARE THE COOKIES THIRD-PARTY?
============================

All cookies listed on the Cookie Policy page are first-party (set by
auricartisan.com).

WHAT HAPPENS IF I IGNORE THE BAR?
=================================

Nothing optional is enabled. No analytics events are recorded until you accept
Analytics. The bar stays until you choose.

DOES REJECTING COOKIES STOP THE TOOLS FROM WORKING?
===================================================

No. Essential functions and every tool keep working. Rejecting only turns off
the optional categories.

DOES MY BROWSER'S DO NOT TRACK SETTING COUNT?
=============================================

Yes. With Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control turned on in your browser,
the site's analytics stays off even if you accepted Analytics. See Privacy
[../others/privacy.txt].

============================

  - Privacy Policy: https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/

They are also summarised in Company and legal
