==============================================================================
MANAGE YOUR COOKIE PREFERENCES
==============================================================================
Change, save, clear or withdraw your cookie choices on the Cookie Policy page.
Area: Website › Site features › Cookie consent and privacy   Updated: 2026-09-25

All cookie controls are in the Manage Cookie Preferences section of the Cookie
Policy page.

OPEN THE PREFERENCES
====================

  - Select Customise on the consent bar; or
  - open https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/#cookies-preferences; or
  - select Cookies in the footer, then Manage Cookie Preferences in the page's
    section rail.

The section opens and scrolls into view.

CHOOSE BY CATEGORY
==================

Each category is a card with a title, a count of listed cookies, a description
and a switch:

  - Essential Cookies — marked Always Active; the switch cannot be turned off.
  - Functional Cookies
  - Analytics Cookies
  - Marketing Cookies

Select Show cookies under a card to see a table of that category's cookies
(Cookie, Purpose, Duration, Type), and Hide cookies to fold it away.

To save a choice:

  1. Turn the switches on or off.
  2. Select Save Preferences.

Result: "Cookie preferences saved successfully." Cookies from any category you
switched off are deleted, and the Consent recorded line shows the new date and
time.

  | Important: Accept All and Reject All on this page only set the switches.
  | Select Save Preferences afterwards to apply them.

If you have never made a choice, the switches start with Functional on and
Analytics and Marketing off.

CLEAR ALL NON-ESSENTIAL COOKIES
===============================

Select Clear All Cookies. The site deletes its non-essential cookies, forgets
your consent choice and turns off the optional switches: "All non-essential
cookies have been cleared." The consent bar will appear again on your next
page.

WITHDRAW CONSENT
================

Once you have made a choice, a Consent recorded line appears with the date,
beside Withdraw Consent. Select it to forget your choice and delete
non-essential cookies: "Cookie consent withdrawn. Preferences reset." The
consent bar appears again on your next page, so you can choose afresh.

WHERE YOUR CHOICE APPLIES
=========================

Your choice is kept in this browser. Another browser or device asks again.
Clearing your browser's data for auricartisan.com also removes it.
