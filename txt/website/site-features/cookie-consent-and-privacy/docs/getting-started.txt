GET STARTED WITH COOKIE CONSENT
==============================================================================
What the cookie consent bar says, and what each of its buttons does.
Area: Website › Site features › Cookie consent and privacy   Updated: 2026-09-25

WHEN THE BAR APPEARS
====================

The consent bar appears at the bottom of the page on your first visit, once
the short welcome animation has finished. It stays until you choose. It does
not dim or block the page, so you can read the page before deciding.

It does not appear on the sign-in and verification pages, and it does not
appear again once you have made a choice in this browser.

WHAT IT SAYS
============

We value your privacy

The bar explains that Auric Artisan uses cookies and local storage to keep the
platform secure, remember your preferences (theme, accent colour, language and
tool settings) and understand basic, anonymous usage. Essential cookies are
always on; analytics and other optional cookies run only with your consent,
and your data is never sold. It links to the full Cookie Policy.

THE THREE BUTTONS
=================

  Button:       Accept All
  What it does: Turns on every category: Essential, Functional, Analytics and
                Marketing

  Button:       Reject All
  What it does: Keeps only Essential, turns the others off, and deletes the
                site's non-essential cookies

  Button:       Customise
  What it does: Opens the Cookie Policy page at Manage Cookie Preferences,
                where you choose each category

After Accept All or Reject All, the bar slides away and your choice is
recorded with the date and time.

  | Note: The bar says you can change your choice "from Settings". The cookie
  | controls are on the Cookie Policy page, not in the Settings panel. See Manage
  | your cookie preferences [manage-cookie-preferences.txt].

WHAT YOUR CHOICE AFFECTS
========================

  - Analytics: with Analytics on, the site may record anonymous usage events,
    such as which pages and features are used. With it off, no usage events
    are recorded. See Privacy [../others/privacy.txt] for details, including
    Do Not Track.
  - Functional: covers remembering preferences such as theme, language and
    layout.
  - Marketing: reserved for campaign attribution if the site enables it later.
    The policy states that Auric Artisan does not currently use marketing
    pixels or cross-site advertising profiles.
  - Essential: signing in, security and remembering your consent choice.
    Always on.
