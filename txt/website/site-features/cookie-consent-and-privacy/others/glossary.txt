==============================================================================
COOKIE CONSENT GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Terms used in the cookie consent guides.
Area: Website › Site features › Cookie consent and privacy   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Cookie
  Meaning: A small piece of data a website stores in your browser and receives
           back on later visits.

  Term:    Local storage
  Meaning: Browser storage that a website's pages can read, but which is not
           sent to the website automatically.

  Term:    First-party
  Meaning: Set by the website you are visiting (here, auricartisan.com), not
           by another company.

  Term:    Consent
  Meaning: Your recorded choice about which optional categories the site may
           use.

  Term:    Essential
  Meaning: Needed for the site to work securely; cannot be turned off.

  Term:    Functional
  Meaning: Remembers your preferences.

  Term:    Analytics
  Meaning: Measures how the site is used, in aggregate or pseudonymously.

  Term:    Marketing
  Meaning: Campaign attribution and advertising measurement; not currently
           used.

  Term:    Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control
  Meaning: Browser settings that ask websites not to track you.

  Term:    Pseudonymous
  Meaning: Linked to a random identifier rather than to your name or account.
