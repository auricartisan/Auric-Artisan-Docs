==============================================================================
THEME
==============================================================================
The site's light, dark and system themes, and every place you can switch
between them.
Area: Website › Site features › Theme   Updated: 2026-09-25

Auric Artisan has a light theme and a dark theme. A third choice, System
(called Auto in the mobile drawer), follows your device: when your operating
system switches between light and dark, the site switches with it, even on a
page that is already open. System is the default.

You can change the theme from several places: the sun and moon button in the
header, the Settings panel, the mobile drawer, a keyboard shortcut, the
right-click menu, Spotlight's commands and the card menu. They all change the
same setting, which applies to every page in this browser and to every open
tab. The site also applies your theme before the page is drawn, so a page does
not flash the wrong theme as it loads.

Some panels can override the theme for themselves: each kit has its own light
and dark button, and each pane of split view can be forced light or dark.
These do not change the site's theme.

WHO IT IS FOR
=============

Everyone. Dark suits low light and long sessions; light suits bright rooms,
printing and checking colours against white. When you compare colours or
contrast, it can help to check them in both.

WHERE TO FIND IT
================

  - The sun or moon button at the right of the header.
  - Settings › Appearance › Theme (`Ctrl` + `,`).
  - The mobile drawer › Preferences › Theme.
  - `Alt` + `T` anywhere on the site.

QUICK START
===========

  1. Select the moon in the header. The page turns dark and the button now
     shows a sun.
  2. Select the sun. The page turns light.
  3. Press `Alt` + `T` three times and watch the message: "Theme: Light",
     "Theme: Dark", "Theme: System" (the order depends on where you start).
  4. Open Settings with `Ctrl` + `,` and select System under Theme.
  5. Change your device between light and dark mode. The page follows.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
===============

  - Choose Light, Dark or System.
  - Switch quickly from the header, the keyboard, the right-click menu,
    Spotlight or the card menu.
  - Keep a kit, or a split-view pane, in a different theme from the page.
