==============================================================================
THEME PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What is stored about your theme choice.
Area: Website › Site features › Theme   Updated: 2026-09-25

  - Your theme mode is kept in this browser's local storage for
    auricartisan.com, along with the last light or dark choice made with a
    toggle. The cookie policy lists this under Functional storage.
  - Each kit's own theme choice is kept the same way.
  - Nothing about your theme is sent to Auric Artisan when you change it.
  - A page link copied from the site can include your theme mode (see
    Shareable links [../../shareable-links/README.txt]).
