==============================================================================
GET STARTED WITH THEMES
==============================================================================
Understand the light, dark and system theme modes and choose one.
Area: Website › Site features › Theme   Updated: 2026-09-25

THE THREE MODES
===============

  Mode:         Light
  What you see: Light pages with dark text, always

  Mode:         Dark
  What you see: Dark pages with light text, always

  Mode:         System (Auto in the drawer)
  What you see: Light or dark to match your device's setting, changing when it
                changes

System is the default for a new visitor.

CHOOSE A MODE
=============

  1. Press `Ctrl` + `,` to open Settings. Appearance is selected.
  2. Under Theme, select Light, Dark or System.

The page changes at once, and every other page and open tab follows.

HOW SYSTEM MODE FOLLOWS YOUR DEVICE
===================================

With System selected, the site asks your browser whether your device is set to
dark mode, and uses dark if it is. When the device switches (for example on a
schedule at sunset), the site switches too, without a reload.

Where to change your device's setting:

  - Windows: Settings › Personalisation › Colours › Choose your mode.
  - macOS: System Settings › Appearance.
  - Android: Settings › Display › Dark theme.
  - iOS and iPadOS: Settings › Display & Brightness.

WHAT THE HEADER BUTTON DOES TO SYSTEM MODE
==========================================

The header's sun and moon button always switches to the opposite of what you
see now, and makes that choice fixed. If you were on System and the page was
dark, selecting the sun sets the theme to Light, and the Settings panel moves
from System to Light. To follow your device again, choose System in Settings
or Auto in the drawer.

WHAT CHANGES WITH THE THEME
===========================

  - The page background, text, borders and panels.
  - The default gold accent is drawn slightly darker in the light theme to
    stay readable. A custom accent is not changed.

Tool results, images and colour swatches are not recoloured.
