==============================================================================
RIGHT-CLICK MENU PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What the right-click menu stores and which of its actions use the network.
Area: Website › Site features › Right-click menu   Updated: 2026-09-25

STORED IN YOUR BROWSER
======================

  - Your quick-bar pins, layout, row height and right-click choice, in local
    storage for auricartisan.com.
  - Nothing about what you right-click or copy is stored or sent.

ACTIONS THAT LEAVE THE SITE
===========================

These open another website in a new tab and pass it the text you chose:

  - Search the web › Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia or YouTube — sends
    the selected text as the search query.
  - Translate — sends the selected text to Google Translate.
  - Open in new tab, Open in new window and Open image in new tab — open the
    address you right-clicked.

ACTIONS THAT STAY ON YOUR DEVICE
================================

  - The copy actions put text or an image on your clipboard.
  - Read aloud uses your device's own speech voice.
  - Share… hands the link to your device's share sheet; what happens next
    depends on the app you pick.
  - Copy its palette reads the image's colours inside your browser.

ACTIONS THAT USE THE SITE
=========================

  - Search this site opens the site's search page.
  - Analyze this page opens the URL Analyzer with the address of the page you
    are on, ready to audit it.
  - Update website checks the site for a newer version.
