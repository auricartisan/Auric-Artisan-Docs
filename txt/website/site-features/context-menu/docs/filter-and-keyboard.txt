FIND AN ACTION BY TYPING
========================

The filter field below the quick bar has keyboard focus as soon as the menu
opens, so you can start typing straight away.

  1. Right-click the page.
  2. Type part of an action's name, for example `mark`.
  3. The list is replaced by matching actions from every group, submenus
     included, each tagged with the group it belongs to (for example Page or
     Link). The field shows how many matched, for example "2 of 27".
  4. The first match is highlighted. Press `Enter` to run it, or use the arrow
     keys to choose another.

How matches are found:

  - The filter matches the action's name, the name of its group, and a few
    extra words for some actions. For example, `dark` or `light` finds Switch
    theme, `audit` or `seo` finds Analyze this page, and `colors` finds Copy
    its palette.
  - Names that start with what you typed are listed first, then names that
    contain it, then matches on the extra words.
  - Up to 12 results are shown.
  - If nothing matches, the list reads "No actions match" followed by your
    text.

Typing works from anywhere in the menu: if a row has focus and you type a
letter, it goes into the filter. Press `Esc` once to clear the filter.

KEYBOARD REFERENCE
==================

  Keys:         Letters and digits
  What they do: Type into the filter

  Keys:         `Down` from the filter
  What they do: Move to the highlighted result or the first action

  Keys:         `Up` / `Down`
  What they do: Move between rows and quick-bar buttons; the list wraps round

  Keys:         `Up` from a quick-bar button
  What they do: Return to the filter field

  Keys:         `Right` on a group row
  What they do: Open its submenu and move into it

  Keys:         `Right` on an action row
  What they do: Move to that row's pin star

  Keys:         `Left` on a pin star
  What they do: Move back to its row

  Keys:         `Left` or `Esc` in a submenu
  What they do: Close the submenu and return to its group row

  Keys:         `Enter` or `Space`
  What they do: Run the focused action, open a focused group, or toggle a
                focused star

  Keys:         `Enter` in the filter
  What they do: Run the highlighted result

  Keys:         `Esc`
  What they do: Close a submenu; otherwise clear the filter; otherwise close
                the menu

  Keys:         `Esc` in the preferences view
  What they do: Return to the action list

When the menu closes, keyboard focus goes back to where it was before you
opened it.

MOUSE BEHAVIOUR
===============

  - Hovering a group row opens its submenu after a short pause. Moving to
    another row closes it.
  - Submenus open to the right of the menu, or to the left when there is no
    room.
  - Selecting a group row also opens its submenu.
