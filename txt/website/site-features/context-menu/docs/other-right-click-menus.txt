==============================================================================
OTHER RIGHT-CLICK MENUS ON THE SITE
==============================================================================
The smaller right-click menus that some parts of the site show instead of the
main menu.
Area: Website › Site features › Right-click menu   Updated: 2026-09-25

A few parts of the site have a small right-click menu of their own, with
actions for that one thing. Right-clicking them opens that menu instead of the
main one. They share a look: a heading, labelled groups of buttons, and
sometimes a Reset button and an All settings link.

All of these close with `Esc`, a click elsewhere, a scroll or a window resize.

CLOCK STRIP
===========

Right-click the clock strip at the top of a page (when it is switched on) to
open Clock settings: a live preview, Format (12h, 24h), Date (Long, D/M/Y,
M/D/Y, ISO), Show switches (Seconds, Weekday, Zone), a searchable Time zone
list, Reset and All settings →. Details are in Info bars and notices › Clock
strip [../../info-bars-and-notices/docs/clock-strip.txt].

INFO BARS
=========

Right-click the welcome, announcement or quick-links bar to open a menu headed
with the bar's name ("Welcome messages", "Site announcements" or "Site
tools"). Under This bar:

  Button    | What it does
  ----------+------------------------------------------------------------
  Open      | Goes to the link you right-clicked, or the bar's first link
  Copy link | Copies that link's address
  Hide bar  | Hides this bar everywhere (same as its ✕)
  Settings  | Opens the Settings panel on the Comfort section

CARDS ON BROWSING PAGES
=======================

On pages with a grid of cards (the homepage, Collections, Blog, Changelog,
Documentation and Learn), right-clicking a card opens a Card settings menu
headed with the card's title:

  - Card: Pin (or Unpin), Open, New tab, Copy link;
  - View: Grid or List;
  - Density: Cozy or Compact;
  - Columns: Auto, 2, 3 or 4;
  - Sort: the sort orders the page offers;
  - System: Theme and Reading switches;
  - Reset and All settings →.

This menu is part of the card libraries and is described fully in Collections
› Card menu [../../../library/collections/docs/card-menu.txt].

FOOTER CONTACT CARD
===================

Right-click the "Start a conversation" card in the footer to open a menu
headed Start a conversation. Under Contact:

  Button     | What it does
  -----------+-------------------------------------------------------------
  Email us   | Opens your email app with a message to the studio address
  Copy email | Copies the email address (the button briefly reads "Copied")
  Copy link  | Copies the address of the page you are on
  LEACB      | Opens the studio's portfolio site in a new tab

INSIDE THE SETTINGS PANEL
=========================

Right-clicking inside the Settings panel opens a menu with Turn on or Turn off
for the switch you clicked, and Export, Import, Reset section and Reset all.
See Settings panel › Back up, restore and reset
[../../settings-panel/docs/back-up-restore-and-reset.txt].

INSIDE THE LIBRARY KIT
======================

The Library Kit's panel has its own right-click menu for your saved items. See
Kits [../../../kits/README.txt].

PLACES THAT KEEP THE BROWSER'S MENU
===================================

The main menu never opens, and your browser's menu appears instead, in:

  - text fields, text areas, drop-down lists and other editable areas;
  - the accessibility checklist's evidence drawing tool;
  - any spot a page has marked as needing the browser's own menu.
