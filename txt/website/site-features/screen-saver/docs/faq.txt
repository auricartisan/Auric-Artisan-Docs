SCREEN SAVER FAQ

Short answers about the screen saver.

DOES THE SCREEN SAVER STOP MY COMPUTER FROM SLEEPING?
=====================================================

No. It is part of the web page. Your computer's own power and sleep settings
still apply.

CAN I CHOOSE WHICH COLOURS IT SHOWS?
====================================

No. It picks a random starting colour from the atlas and moves on every twelve
seconds.

WHERE DO THE COLOURS COME FROM?
===============================

From the site's colour atlas of 8,192 named colours, each with a mood and an
art movement associated with it.

DOES IT USE MUCH POWER?
=======================

It is drawn with plain page elements rather than animation-heavy graphics, and
it pauses while its tab is hidden.

IS THE SCREEN SAVER THE SAME AS EYE REST?
=========================================

No. Eye rest reminds you to look away while you are working; the screen saver
appears when you have stopped. See Eye rest [../../eye-rest/README.txt].
