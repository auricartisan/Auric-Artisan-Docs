==============================================================================
THE FOOTER
==============================================================================
Every section and link of the footer at the bottom of each page.
Area: Website › Site features › Header and navigation   Updated: 2026-09-25

The footer closes every page. It has four parts.

INTRODUCTION
============

  - The Auric Artisan logo (links to the homepage) and the statement "Color
    intelligence. Crafted for everyone."
  - The Start a conversation card: "Questions, collaborations, or support?"
    with an Email our studio link to `hello@auricartisan.com`, and Explore our
    portfolio, LEACB, which opens the studio's portfolio site in a new tab.

Right-click the Start a conversation card for a small menu: Email us, Copy
email, Copy link and LEACB. See Other right-click menus
[../../context-menu/docs/other-right-click-menus.txt].

DIRECTORY
=========

Under the heading "Tools, policies, and useful links", four numbered columns:

  Column: 01 Product
  Links:  Tools, Learn, Blog, Portfolios, API, Search Console, Download

  Column: 02 Explore
  Links:  Privacy, Terms, Cookies, Refund Policy, License

  Column: 03 Policies
  Links:  Acceptable Use Policy, Content & Intellectual Property, Data
          Retention & Deletion, Transparency, Subscription & Billing

  Column: 04 Trust
  Links:  Trust Center, Security, Accessibility, Contact

BOTTOM ROW
==========

  - The copyright line and "Design and development by Auric Artisan", with the
    company's registered address.
  - Social links, each opening in a new tab: Product Hunt, LinkedIn, GitHub
    and X.
  - Made with care · Built in India, with a small 3D Indian flag. Double-click
    the flag to play a short celebration animation. On Indian festival days, a
    festival greeting appears beside it.

CLOSING NOTE
============

"Company registered in India. Product names, logos, and brands remain the
property of their respective owners."
