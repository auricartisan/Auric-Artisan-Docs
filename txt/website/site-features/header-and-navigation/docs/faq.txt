==============================================================================
HEADER AND NAVIGATION FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers about the header, drawer, page utilities bar and footer.
Area: Website › Site features › Header and navigation   Updated: 2026-09-25

IS THERE A PAGE THAT LISTS EVERY TOOL?
======================================

Yes. All Tools at the top of the Tools menu opens
https://auricartisan.com/collections/, which shows every tool with filters.

DO THE MENUS OPEN WHEN I HOVER OVER THEM?
=========================================

No. They open when you click or tap their button, or press `Enter`, `Space` or
`Down` on it, and they stay open until you choose an entry or move away.

CAN I HIDE THE PAGE UTILITIES BAR?
==================================

The bar collapses to a single button when you close it, and that button cannot
be removed. Reading mode hides the kit launchers.

WHERE IS THE NAVIGATION DOCK ON MY PHONE?
=========================================

On phones the dock has no button of its own. Open the page utilities bar and
select Navigate.

WHAT DOES THE FLAG IN THE FOOTER DO?
====================================

Double-click it to play a short celebration animation. It does nothing else.

HOW DO I CONTACT AURIC ARTISAN?
===============================

Use Email our studio in the footer (`hello@auricartisan.com`), or the Contact
page in the Company menu.
