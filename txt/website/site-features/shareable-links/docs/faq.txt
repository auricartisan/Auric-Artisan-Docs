==============================================================================
SHAREABLE LINKS FAQ
==============================================================================
Short answers about page links and their saved state.
Area: Website › Site features › Shareable links   Updated: 2026-09-25

IS THE `#P=` PART SENT TO AURIC ARTISAN?
========================================

No. Browsers do not send the part of an address after `#` to the website. It
is read by the page in the browser that opens it.

IS IT ENCRYPTED?
================

No. It is the page's state written compactly. Anyone who has the link can read
what it contains.

CAN I TURN IT OFF?
==================

There is no switch for it. It only affects the address; it does not store
anything elsewhere. Share a clean link if you prefer.

DOES IT WORK OFFLINE?
=====================

Yes, for pages that are saved on your device: reloading restores the state
without a connection.

DOES IT INCLUDE WHAT I SAVED IN MY LIBRARY?
===========================================

No. It includes what is on the page, not your saved library items.
