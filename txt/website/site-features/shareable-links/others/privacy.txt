==============================================================================
SHAREABLE LINKS PRIVACY
==============================================================================
What a copied page link can reveal, and how to share one safely.
Area: Website › Site features › Shareable links   Updated: 2026-09-25

  - Stays in the address. The page state is kept only in the address bar of
    your browser tab. It is not stored elsewhere and, because it follows `#`,
    it is not sent to Auric Artisan when the page loads.
  - Travels with the link. When you copy and share the address, the state goes
    with it: what you typed into the page's fields (except passwords and
    search boxes), your selections, open panels, your scroll position, and
    your appearance settings that differ from the defaults.
  - Readable by anyone with the link. The state is written compactly, not
    encrypted.
  - Browser history. The latest state of each page can appear in your browser
    history and bookmarks, like any address.

To share safely, share a clean link: scroll to the top and undo changes, or
delete everything from `#p=` onwards.
