==============================================================================
SHAREABLE LINKS GLOSSARY
==============================================================================
Terms used in the shareable links guides.
Area: Website › Site features › Shareable links   Updated: 2026-09-25

  Term:    Address (URL)
  Meaning: The web address shown in the browser's address bar.

  Term:    Fragment
  Meaning: The part of an address after `#`. Browsers keep it on your device
           and do not send it to the site.

  Term:    Query parameter
  Meaning: A part of an address after `?`, such as `?lang=hi`, which the page
           reads.

  Term:    Page state
  Meaning: The scroll position, inputs, selections and open panels of a page.

  Term:    `#p=`
  Meaning: The fragment in which the site keeps a page's state.

  Term:    Restore
  Meaning: Putting a page back into the state its address describes.

  Term:    Clean link
  Meaning: An address without the `#p=` part.
