BRAND ICONS
===========

Some sets include logos of brands and companies. Those logos remain trademarks
of their owners, and are provided for identification, not endorsement. Using a
logo may need its owner's permission, whatever the set's licence says.

COMPLYING
=========

  1. When you ship icons in a product or a download, include the relevant
     licence notices, for example in your credits or a licence file.
  2. If you change Material icon files, note your changes, as Apache 2.0
     requires.
  3. Check brand and company logos separately.
