==============================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
==============================================================================
Fixes for general Auric Artisan problems — browser support, pages that look
out of date, cookies and consent, sign-in, payments, offline use, language and
theme, and clearing site data.
Area: Help   Updated: 2026-09-25

Each entry gives the symptom, the likely cause and what to do. Problems
specific to one tool are covered in that tool's own troubleshooting page;
account and payment problems in detail are in Account and billing
troubleshooting [../website/account-and-billing/docs/troubleshooting.txt].

BROWSER SUPPORT
===============

The website is built for current, modern browsers. The download page says the
same package runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS, Android, iOS and iPadOS,
and that offline caching works "anywhere with a service worker and a secure
origin".

  Symptom:    A tool does not start, or a panel stays blank
  Why:        An old browser version, or a browser feature the tool needs
              (such as canvas or WebGL graphics) is turned off
  What to do: Update your browser; try another current browser

  Symptom:    Copy buttons do nothing
  Why:        The browser blocked clipboard access
  What to do: Allow clipboard access for auricartisan.com, or select and copy
              the text yourself

  Symptom:    The eyedropper in Settings is missing
  Why:        Not every browser offers a screen eyedropper
  What to do: Use the colour picker beside it

  Symptom:    A download or export window does not open
  Why:        A pop-up blocker stopped it
  What to do: Allow pop-ups for auricartisan.com

  Symptom:    The site behaves oddly in one browser only
  Why:        An extension (ad blocker, script blocker) interferes
  What to do: Try a private window with extensions off

The download page's Check local hardware and Check readiness buttons report
what your browser supports.

A PAGE LOOKS OUT OF DATE OR BROKEN
==================================

  Symptom:    A page shows old content after an update
  What to do: Reload the page. If you installed the app, open
              https://auricartisan.com/download/ and select Update app when it
              appears.

  Symptom:    Layout or styling is broken
  What to do: Reload; if it persists, open the download page and use Audit
              cache, then Repair cache.

  Symptom:    Nothing helps
  What to do: Use Reset cache on the download page, or see Clear site data and
              start fresh.

COOKIES AND CONSENT
===================

  Symptom:    The cookie bar appears on every visit
  Why:        Your choice is stored in the browser; private windows and
              cleared site data forget it
  What to do: Use a normal window, or allow site data for auricartisan.com

  Symptom:    The cookie bar does not appear on the sign-in page
  Why:        It is deliberately not shown over sign-in and verification pages
  What to do: Choose later on any other page, or at
              https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/

  Symptom:    You want to change your choice
  Why:        —
  What to do: Open https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/ › Manage Cookie
              Preferences, change the switches and select Save Preferences

  Symptom:    You want to start over
  Why:        —
  What to do: On the same page select Withdraw Consent or Clear All Cookies

Essential cookies cannot be turned off; without them you cannot stay signed
in. See Cookie Policy summary
[../website/company-and-legal/docs/cookie-policy.txt].

SIGN-IN PROBLEMS
================

  Symptom:    You sign in but appear signed out on the next page
  Why:        The browser blocked cookies for auricartisan.com
  What to do: Allow cookies for the site; its sign-in cookies are first-party

  Symptom:    Actions fail with a message about the page's session token
  Why:        You opened the site over an insecure address, or signed out in
              another tab
  What to do: Use https://auricartisan.com and reload the page

  Symptom:    You are signed out unexpectedly
  Why:        Your session ended: a password change, a revoked device, seven
              days without use, or closing the browser without Remember me
  What to do: Sign in again

  Symptom:    Human check, verification, reset or two-step problems
  Why:        —
  What to do: See Account and billing troubleshooting
              [../website/account-and-billing/docs/troubleshooting.txt]

PAYMENTS
========

  Symptom:    Checkout will not open
  What to do: Allow pop-ups and scripts for auricartisan.com and Razorpay,
              then try again

  Symptom:    A foreign card is declined
  What to do: Nothing is charged. Try another method, or tell Auric Artisan
              through the contact page

  Symptom:    Paid but the plan is not showing
  What to do: Refresh the dashboard; quote the order reference to support if
              it persists

  Symptom:    Anything else
  What to do: See Payments and checkout
              [../website/account-and-billing/docs/troubleshooting.txt]

Never pay anywhere other than the Razorpay checkout on auricartisan.com.

OFFLINE AND THE INSTALLED APP
=============================

  Symptom:    No Install app button
  Why:        Whether to offer installation is the browser's decision
  What to do: On iPhone and iPad use Add to Home Screen in Safari; on desktop
              try Chrome or Edge

  Symptom:    A tool will not open offline
  Why:        It was not cached, or the Lite profile keeps only core tools
  What to do: Go online, open the tool once, or choose Balanced or Full and
              select Prepare offline version

  Symptom:    The offline copy disappeared
  Why:        The browser cleared the cache to free space
  What to do: Grant persistent storage when asked, then prepare the offline
              version again

  Symptom:    Sign-in, the dashboard or checkout fail offline
  Why:        They always need a connection
  What to do: Reconnect

  Symptom:    Offline copy is out of date
  Why:        —
  What to do: Go online and select Update app on the download page

See Offline use [../getting-started/offline-use.txt] and Download and offline
[../website/download-and-offline/README.txt].

LANGUAGE AND THEME
==================

  Symptom:    A few words stay in English in Hindi
  What to do: Technical names (colour spaces, models, fonts) and some
              third-party names stay in Latin script by design

  Symptom:    Search finds nothing in Hindi
  What to do: Search in English

  Symptom:    Switching back to English reloads the page
  What to do: Expected: the original text is loaded again

  Symptom:    The theme keeps changing
  What to do: You chose System or Auto, which follows your device; pick Light
              or Dark

  Symptom:    Theme or language is not remembered
  What to do: They are stored per browser; private windows and cleared site
              data reset them

CLEAR SITE DATA AND START FRESH
===============================

Clearing Auric Artisan's site data in your browser removes everything the site
keeps in that browser:

  - your library of saved palettes, gradients, collections and exports;
  - your portfolio draft;
  - your settings (theme, language, accent colour, text size and so on) and
    your cookie choice;
  - the offline cache;
  - your sign-in on that browser.

It does not touch your account, your plan, your published portfolio or
anything on other devices.

  | Important: Export anything you want to keep first — see Export your data
  | [../website/account-and-billing/docs/export-your-data.txt] — because the
  | library exists only in your browser.

Gentler options first
---------------------

  - To clear only your saved work and preferences but stay signed in:
    dashboard Security & Privacy › danger zone › Erase this browser's
    workspace.
  - To clear only the offline cache: https://auricartisan.com/download/ ›
    Reset cache.
  - To reset only settings: Settings › Reset all.

Clear everything in your browser
--------------------------------

The Cookie Policy lists where to find the controls:

  Browser | Where
  --------+--------------------------------------------------------------------
  Chrome  | Settings › Privacy and security › Cookies and other site data
  Firefox | Settings › Privacy & Security › Cookies and Site Data
  Safari  | Settings › Privacy › Manage Website Data
  Edge    | Settings › Cookies and site permissions › Manage and delete cookies

Find auricartisan.com and remove its data, then reload the site.

STILL STUCK
===========

Report it through https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/ with the topic A
