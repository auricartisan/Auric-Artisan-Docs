General help for Auric Artisan — frequently asked questions, a glossary,
troubleshooting, accessibility, privacy, and how to get support.
Area: Help   Updated: 2026-09-25

This folder answers questions that apply across Auric Artisan rather than to
one tool: what things mean, why something is not working, how the site works
with assistive technology, what happens to your data, and how to reach a
person. Tool-specific help lives in each tool's own troubleshooting page under
Website tools [../website/tools/README.txt].

  What it covers: Browser support, cookies and consent, sign-in, payments,
                  offline use, and clearing data

  Page:           Accessibility of the site [accessibility.txt]
  What it covers: Keyboard use, screen readers, comfort settings, and
                  reporting barriers

  Page:           Privacy overview [privacy.txt]
  What it covers: What is stored locally, what is sent, analytics consent,
                  third parties and your rights

  Page:           Contact and support [contact-and-support.txt]
  What it covers: Which inbox to write to, reply times, and how to write a
                  message that gets a fast answer
