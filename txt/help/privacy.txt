==============================================================================
PRIVACY OVERVIEW
==============================================================================
What Auric Artisan stores in your browser, what it sends to its servers and
third parties, how analytics consent works, what each product collects, and
how to use your privacy rights.
Area: Help   Updated: 2026-09-25

This page brings together what the policies and the product say about your
data. The authoritative documents are the Privacy Policy
(https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/), the Cookie Policy
(https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/), the Data Retention and Deletion
Policy and, for the browser extension, the Extension Privacy Policy.
Plain-language summaries of each are in Company and legal
[../website/company-and-legal/README.txt].

STORED IN YOUR BROWSER
======================

  What:  My Library (palettes, gradients, collections, exports)
  Notes: Stays on this device; not uploaded unless you publish or export it

  What:  Tool state and drafts, including the Portfolio Builder draft
  Notes: Stays on this device

  What:  Settings: theme, language, accent colour, text size, comfort options,
         eye rest
  Notes: The Privacy Policy says many preferences are not sent to the servers

  What:  Your cookie choice
  Notes: Kept for up to a year

  What:  The offline cache, if you prepared one
  Notes: The site's pages and data, for offline use

  What:  Your sign-in (session and CSRF cookies)
  Notes: Only when you sign in; up to 30 days or until sign-out

Anything in your browser is lost if you clear site data, and does not follow
you to other devices. See Clear site data and start fresh
[troubleshooting.txt].

SENT TO AURIC ARTISAN'S SERVERS
===============================

  What: Account details and profile fields
  When: When you sign up, sign in or edit your profile

  What: Security records: sessions, sign-in attempts, security events
  When: While you use an account

  What: Web analysis inputs: URLs, page metadata, screenshots, extracted
        colours, scan results
  When: When a tool analyses a page for you. The FAQ page says no URL history
        or page content is kept after the analysis session.

  What: Server-backed tool work
  When: When you start a run that uses tokens

  What: Contact messages and attachments
  When: When you use the contact form

  What: Order records: item, amount, payment reference
  When: When you buy something

  What: Analytics events
  When: Only with consent (below)

The Privacy Policy says colour calculations, palettes, vision simulations and
theme generation run in your browser where possible and are not sent anywhere.

ANALYTICS AND CONSENT
=====================

  - Analytics is first-party: events go to Auric Artisan's own servers. There
    are no third-party analytics scripts and no analytics cookies.
  - Events are sent only if you accepted the Analytics category in the cookie
    bar or on the Cookie Policy page. No choice means no events.
  - Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control in your browser turn analytics off
    regardless.
  - An event contains the event name, the page path (never the query string),
    the referrer's origin, and a few coarse properties such as device class.

The dashboard's Usage data switch (in Security & Privacy) records your choice
on your account; the cookie choice is what governs analytics on the website.

THIRD PARTIES
=============

  Company:                 Cloudflare
  Role (as the policies describe it):Hosting, DNS, CDN, compute and storage
                           for account, preference and order data

  Company:                 Razorpay
  Role (as the policies describe it):Payments. Card, UPI and bank details go
                           to Razorpay directly and never reach Auric Artisan.

  Company:                 Google Fonts
  Role (as the policies describe it):Font files on some pages; Google receives
                           the technical request, such as your IP address

  Company:                 Resend
  Role (as the policies describe it):Email delivery (named on the Security
                           page)

Free-plan pages can show sponsored slots. The Subscription and Billing Policy
says ads shown to free users are limited and consent-gated, and that private
data is not used for advertising profiles.

WHAT EACH PRODUCT COLLECTS
==========================

  Product: Website, no account
  Summary: Your browser keeps your work and settings; servers see page
           requests, tool inputs for server-backed work, and analytics only
           with consent

  Product: Website, with account
  Summary: The above, plus account, security and order records

  Product: Browser extension
  Summary: Reads a page only when you use a tool on it; stores everything in
           the browser's extension storage; no analytics, telemetry or network
           requests with your data

  Product: VS Code extensions
  Summary: Described on https://auricartisan.com/vscode/ as needing no account
           and sending no telemetry

  Product: REST API
  Summary: Your requests and usage, metered against your account

NO SALE, NO TRACKING ACROSS SITES
=================================

The Privacy Policy states that Auric Artisan does not sell, rent or trade
personal data, does not take part in data-broker networks or cross-site
tracking, and does not use personal data for behavioural advertising or
automated decisions with legal effects.

YOUR RIGHTS, IN PRACTICE
========================

  Right:         Access or a copy
  How to use it: Dashboard › Security & Privacy › Export; for more, contact
                 topic Privacy or data

  Right:         Correction
  How to use it: Dashboard › Profile; for your email address, contact support

  Right:         Deletion
  How to use it: Dashboard danger zone, or contact topic Privacy or data

  Right:         Withdraw consent
  How to use it: Cookie Policy page › Withdraw Consent, or change the switches

  Right:         Object or complain
  How to use it: Contact topic Privacy or data

Privacy or data messages go to security@auricartisan.com, with a stated reply
time of within 30 days. The Cookie Policy also lists privacy@auricartisan.com
for cookie questions.
