FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Short answers to common questions about Auric Artisan — the platform, the

Area: Help   Updated: 2026-09-25

THE PLATFORM
============

What is Auric Artisan?
----------------------

A colour-science and accessibility platform: tools, libraries and learning
material on the website, plus a browser extension, VS Code extensions and a
REST API. See What Auric Artisan is
[../getting-started/what-is-auric-artisan.txt].

Who makes it?
-------------

One independent developer, Chirag Bansal, in India. The Security page notes
the platform is operated by one person. See Author page
[../website/company-and-legal/docs/author.txt].

Is it free?
-----------

Every tool is free on every plan, with no login needed to start. Paid plans
add conveniences such as ad-free browsing, watermark-free exports, a monthly
token allowance and higher limits. See The free-tools model
[../getting-started/free-tools-model.txt].

Why do some pages show ads?
---------------------------

The free plan's card on the pricing page says "Sponsored slots (ads) support
the free tools". Paid plans include ad-free browsing. The Subscription and
Billing Policy says ads shown to free users are limited and consent-gated, and
that private data is not used for advertising profiles.

Is Auric Artisan an official or government body?
------------------------------------------------

No. It is an independent private platform. References to CIE, W3C or ISO
standards are informational and do not imply affiliation.

THE TOOLS
=========

Can I rely on the results professionally?
-----------------------------------------

For design, accessibility evaluation, palette work and web work, the outputs
follow published standards. They are guidance, not certification. For
colour-critical industrial work use calibrated instruments; for accessibility
compliance have a qualified person review; for vision, see an eye-care
professional.

Is the Ishihara-style generator a real colour-vision test?
----------------------------------------------------------

No. It is for exploration and teaching only. It is not a medical device and
must not be used for screening, diagnosis, employment or licensing.

Can I analyse any website?
--------------------------

Only sites and pages you own, manage or are authorised to test. The Terms and
the Acceptable Use Policy require it.

Who owns what I make?
---------------------

You do. Palettes, themes, token files and reports you generate are yours to
use, including commercially. The platform's code, design and articles remain
Auric Artisan's.

Where is my saved work?
-----------------------

In My Library, stored in the browser you saved it in. It does not follow you
to another device; cloud sync is listed as coming soon. Export or back up
anything important.

ACCOUNTS AND PAYMENTS
=====================

Do I need an account?
---------------------

No, not for the tools. You need one for server-backed runs (tokens), buying a
plan, API keys, publishing a portfolio and account security. See Create an
account (optional) [../getting-started/create-an-account.txt].

How do I pay, and will it renew?
--------------------------------

Through Razorpay's checkout on auricartisan.com: cards, UPI, netbanking and
wallets. A plan is a one-time pass for one or twelve months and never renews.
See Buy a plan [../website/account-and-billing/docs/buy-a-plan.txt].

Can I get a refund?
-------------------

Yes, within 7 days of buying a plan, for any reason. See Refunds
[../website/account-and-billing/docs/refunds.txt].

What are tokens and API credits?
--------------------------------

Tokens pay for server-backed work in website tools; API credits pay for REST
API calls. They are separate. See Tokens
[../website/account-and-billing/docs/tokens.txt].

PRIVACY
=======

What data does the site collect?
--------------------------------

The minimum needed: account details if you have an account, security records,
the inputs to server-backed tools, messages you send, order records, and
analytics only if you consent. See Privacy overview [privacy.txt].

Does the site sell my data or track me across the web?
------------------------------------------------------

The Privacy Policy states it does not sell personal data, does no cross-site
tracking and does not use personal data for behavioural advertising.

How do I change my cookie choice?
---------------------------------

On https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/ in Manage Cookie Preferences, or
from Settings. See Cookie Policy summary
[../website/company-and-legal/docs/cookie-policy.txt].

LANGUAGE, THEME AND OFFLINE
===========================

Is the site available in other languages?
-----------------------------------------

English and Hindi. See Language: English and Hindi
[../getting-started/language.txt].

How do I switch to dark mode?
-----------------------------

Use the theme button in the header, Settings › Appearance › Theme, or `Alt` +
`T`. See Light and dark theme [../getting-started/light-and-dark-theme.txt].

Does it work offline?
---------------------

Yes, once you prepare it: install the site as an app and cache it from
https://auricartisan.com/download/. Account features and server-backed runs
still need a connection. See Offline use [../getting-started/offline-use.txt].

OTHER PRODUCTS
==============

Where is the browser extension?
-------------------------------

On the Chrome Web Store (also used by Edge, Opera, Brave and Vivaldi) and
Firefox Add-ons, linked from https://auricartisan.com/extension.html. See
Browser extension [../browser-extension/README.txt].

Where are the VS Code extensions?
---------------------------------

On the Visual Studio Marketplace under `auric-artisan`, listed at
https://auricartisan.com/vscode/. See VS Code extensions
[../vscode-extensions/README.txt].

Do the extensions need an account or send data?
-----------------------------------------------

No. The site describes the browser extension and the VS Code extensions as
needing no account and sending no telemetry. The browser extension makes no
network requests with your data.
