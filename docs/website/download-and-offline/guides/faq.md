# Download and offline FAQ

## Is there a desktop app to download?

The app is installed from your browser, not downloaded as a separate program. It then runs in its own window. The browser extension and the VS Code extensions are separate products.

## Is the app free?

Yes. Installing and using it offline costs nothing. Every tool is free on every plan.

## Does the installed app need an account?

No. An account is needed only for account features, which also need a connection.

## Do my saved palettes work offline?

Yes. Your library is kept on your device and works offline.

## Does the app update itself?

Yes, from the website. When a new version is ready you are asked to refresh; see [Updates](updates.md).

## Can I install it on more than one device?

Yes. Install and prepare it on each device. Your library and settings stay on each device separately, unless you use account features that sync them.

## How much space does it need?

It depends on the profile. Lite is small; Full can take more than 100 MB. The **Storage used** figure on the Download page shows your actual use.

## Which browsers work best?

For installing: Chrome and Edge on desktop and Android, and Safari on iPhone and iPad (Add to Home Screen) and on macOS (Add to Dock). For offline use: any current browser with service workers on a secure connection.
