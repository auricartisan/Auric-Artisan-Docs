# Library Kit FAQ

## Is my library saved to my account?

No. It is stored in your browser on this device. Export it (**Export** in the footer) to keep a backup or move it to another browser.

## How many items can I keep?

Your plan sets the number of active saved items: 20 on Apprentice, 500 on Artisan, 2,500 on Specialist and 10,000 on Industrial Pro. Archived items do not count. Your browser's storage limit applies on top of that, especially for images.

## Are the tools still free if I reach the limit?

Yes. The limit only affects saving new items. See [Account and billing](../../../account-and-billing/README.md).

## What happened to the Library Workspace page?

The library now opens as a floating panel on any page. https://auricartisan.com/library/workspace/ still works and opens the panel.

## Can other people see my library?

No. Only items you share as links, export as files or push to your own sync destinations leave your browser.

## What does a share link contain?

The item's data, packed into the address. Anyone with the link can open a copy. Nothing is stored on a server.

## What is the difference between archive and delete?

Archiving hides an item and keeps it on the **Archive** tab, where you can restore it. Deleting removes it for good.

## What is the difference between a collection and a project?

A collection is a folder that groups items; each item can be in one. A project is a type of saved item; the Library Kit can create empty ones, and some tools save their own. Use collections to group your items.

## Can I undo a delete?

No. Deletes, **Empty the archive** and **Reset library** cannot be undone.

## Does the library work offline?

Browsing, searching, organising, comparing and exporting work offline once the kit has loaded, because everything is in your browser. Sync needs a connection, and opening an item in a tool needs that tool's page to load.

## Which tools can save to the library?

Tools with a **Save to Library** button. Saved items record which tool made them, and many can also be opened in other tools that accept the same type of item.
