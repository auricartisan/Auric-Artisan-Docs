# The published page

## At your address

A published portfolio lives at `https://auricartisan.com/p/` followed by your address. It opens quickly: the portfolio appears first, and the site's header, footer, cursor and right-click menu arrive a moment later.

Visitors see:

- your header and sections, in your chosen typeface, paper, rhythm and accent;
- your saved palettes in the Work / Projects section as swatches they can select, and copy as **CSS** or **JSON**;
- **Copy link** and, if you allowed it, **PDF**;
- the view count, if you chose to show it.

### The right-click menu on a portfolio

On a portfolio page, the site's right-click menu offers actions for readers:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy link to this portfolio** | Copies the address. |
| **Save this portfolio as PDF** | Opens the print dialog. |
| **Email** and the owner's first name | Starts an email, when the portfolio shows an email address. |
| **Reading mode** (`Alt` + `R`) | Switches the site's reading mode. |
| **Make your own portfolio** | Opens the Portfolio Builder. |

## At a snapshot link

A snapshot link opens https://auricartisan.com/portfolio/public/ with the portfolio carried in the address. The page shows **Opening portfolio…**, then the portfolio with **Copy link** and **PDF**, and a footer: "Built with Auric Artisan — Colour intelligence, accessibility tools, and a portfolio builder." with **Make your own**.

Messages a visitor may see:

| Message | Why |
| --- | --- |
| "This page shows a shared portfolio, and this link is missing its data. Ask for a fresh link from the portfolio builder." | The link was cut short. |
| "This browser cannot decompress the link. Try a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari." | An old browser. |
| "This portfolio could not be opened. It may have been built by a newer version of the editor." | The link could not be read. |

## Result

Visitors get a fast, readable page that works on any device, and a way to copy, save or contact you.
