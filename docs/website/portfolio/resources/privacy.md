# Privacy

## While you build

| What | Where | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your draft | This browser, and your account when signed in | Syncing to your account lets it follow you between devices. |
| Your preview width | This browser | A convenience setting. |

Your draft is not public. Nothing a visitor sees changes until you publish.

## When you publish

What becomes public at your address:

- everything in your visible sections (hidden sections are left out);
- the header details you chose to show — your name and headline always; email, location, website and avatar only if their **Show** switches are on;
- a copy of the Library items in your Work / Projects section, taken at the moment you publish.

Your sign-in email is not published unless you type it into the header and switch on **Show**.

Settings under your control:

- **Let search engines list it** — off keeps the page out of search engines.
- **List it in the Portfolio Index** — off keeps it out of the directory.
- **Allow a PDF download** and **Show the view count**.

The view count is "Counted as a number and nothing else — no visitor records, no addresses." Uploaded avatars are re-encoded, which removes hidden photo information such as camera and location details.

## Snapshot links

A snapshot link contains the portfolio itself. Nothing is stored on the site, but anyone who has the link can open it, and it cannot be withdrawn: it keeps working even if you later change or delete your portfolio. Share it only with people you trust.

## The Portfolio Index

A listed portfolio shows its name, headline, place, crafts, colours, avatar, address and when it was updated. External entries show what you entered in the Search Console.

## Remove it

- **Unpublish** takes the page down at once; the address stops working.
- Switch off **List it in the Portfolio Index** and publish the change to leave the directory.
- **Take it out** removes an external entry.
