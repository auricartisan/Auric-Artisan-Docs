# Privacy

## What stays in your browser

The Collections page remembers a few things in your browser's local storage. None of it is sent to Auric Artisan or saved to your account.

| What | Why | How to remove it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pinned tools | To show **Pinned**, **Pinned first** and your **Start here** picks. | Unpin each tool, or clear this site's data. |
| Tool opens (count and last time) | To build **Most used** and part of **Recommended**. | Clear this site's data. |
| Grid or list view | To keep your chosen layout. | Choose the other view, or clear this site's data. |
| Density and columns | To keep your card-menu layout choices. | Select **Reset** in the card menu. |
| Tool-visibility setting | To show the tool set you chose in the Advanced Kit. | Change it in the Advanced Kit. |

Because these are stored per browser, a private window starts with none of them and forgets them when it closes.

## What uses the network

- The page downloads the tool catalogue and card artwork from auricartisan.com when it loads.
- **Copy link** writes to your device's clipboard only; nothing is sent.
- The page can show advertising. Advertising and analytics on the site follow your cookie and consent choices.

For the site's full privacy policy, consent choices and cookie settings, see [Company and legal](../../../company-and-legal/README.md).
