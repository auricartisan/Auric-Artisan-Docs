# Pin tools and use recommendations

Collections can put the tools that matter to you first. It uses two signals, both kept only in the browser you are using:

- **Pins** — tools you have marked yourself.
- **Opens** — how often, and how recently, you have opened each tool from the Collections page.

From these the page builds three groupings: **Pinned**, **Most used** and **Recommended**.

## Pin a tool

1. Find the tool's card in **All tools**.
2. Select the pin button in the card's corner. Its label reads "Pin" followed by the tool's name.
3. The button fills in to show the tool is pinned, and the card is marked as pinned.

You can also right-click the card and choose **Pin** in the card menu (see [Use the card menu](card-menu.md)).

To unpin, select the same button again (its label now reads "Unpin" and the tool's name), or choose **Unpin** in the card menu.

### Where pinned tools appear

- **The Pinned category.** A **Pinned** tick box appears at the top of **Category** in the **Filter and sort** panel, with a count. Tick it to see only your pinned tools. When you unpin your last tool, the box disappears and the filter switches itself off.
- **Pinned first.** Choose **Pinned first** in the sort list to bring pinned tools to the top while keeping everything else below them.
- **Start here.** The three-card **Start here** shortlist shows pinned tools before any others. Pin three tools and the shortlist becomes your own.
- **Recommended.** Pinned tools get a strong boost in the **Recommended** order.

## Most used

Each time you open a tool by selecting its card on the Collections page — in **All tools** or in **Start here** — the page counts that open. Selecting the pin button does not count as an open.

Choose **Most used** in the sort list to order tools by how much you use them. The order weighs both how many times you opened a tool and how recently:

| Last opened | Weight given to each open |
| --- | --- |
| Within the last day | Highest |
| Within 3 days | High |
| Within a week | Medium |
| Within 30 days | Low |
| Longer ago | Lowest |

So a tool you opened five times this week can rank above one you opened ten times last year. Tools you have never opened from this page follow, in an order based on your pins, core subjects and description depth.

> **Note:** Only opens from the Collections page count. Visiting a tool from the header menu, a bookmark or a search result does not change **Most used** here.

## Recommended

Choose **Recommended** in the sort list for the order the page thinks is most useful to you. It adds up several things:

- **Your pins.** A pinned tool gets a large boost.
- **Your opens.** Tools you open often and recently rise, up to a limit, so one busy afternoon does not bury everything else.
- **Core subjects.** Tools whose details mention central subjects on the site — among them accessibility, WCAG, APCA, contrast, gamut, HDR, ICC, tokens, performance and workflow — rise a little for each one.
- **Description depth.** Tools with fuller descriptions and more tags rise slightly.

With no pins and no history, **Recommended** relies on the last two signals only, so a first-time visitor sees a stable order. Your search words and ticked filters decide which tools are shown, not their order.

## Reset your pins and usage

There is no single reset button for pins and usage on the page. You can clear them in these ways:

- **Clear pins one by one.** Tick **Pinned** under **Category** to list your pinned tools, then select each pin button to unpin it.
- **Clear everything the site remembers.** Clear this site's data (sometimes called "cookies and site data") in your browser's settings. This removes your pins, your usage history and your layout choices on this page, along with other preferences the site keeps in your browser.

The **Reset** button in the card menu does not remove pins or usage. It only returns the layout to its defaults: grid view, cozy density, automatic columns and the first sort order (**Title A to Z**).

> **Important:** The site's own settings panel also has a **Reset all** button, but that resets site preferences such as theme and text size. It does not clear your Collections pins or usage.

## Result

Your pinned tools are grouped under **Pinned**, lead the **Start here** shortlist and top the **Pinned first** order, and the **Most used** and **Recommended** orders reflect how you actually use the site in this browser.
