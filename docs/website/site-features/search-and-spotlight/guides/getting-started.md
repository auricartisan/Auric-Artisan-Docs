# Get started with Spotlight

## Open and close Spotlight

- **Open:** press `Ctrl` + `K` (`Cmd` + `K` on a Mac), or select **Search** in the header.
- **Close:** press `Esc` (the first press clears what you typed, the second closes), press `Ctrl` + `K` again, or click the dimmed page around Spotlight.

While Spotlight is open, the page behind it does not scroll.

## The parts of Spotlight

1. **Search box.** A magnifier, an optional mode chip (for example **Colour** or **Command**), the text box, an `ESC` hint and an X to clear the box. The box's placeholder names the current scope, for example "Search Auric Artisan…".
2. **Scope tabs.** **All**, **Pages**, **Tools**, **Docs**, **Learn** and **Web**.
3. **Results list** on the left.
4. **Preview pane** on the right. It shows details of the highlighted result, or the answer when you type a colour.
5. **Footer.** Key hints (`↑` `↓` navigate, `↵` open, `TAB` filter, `ESC` close) and, on the right, a count such as "12 results · 3ms".

## Your first search

1. Open Spotlight. Before you type, it shows:
   - **Recent**: up to six of your recent searches, with a **Clear** button;
   - **Quick Actions**: shortcuts to Full Search, Collections, URL Analyzer, Color Science Lab, Documentation, Learn Color, Vision Simulations, Install the PWA and Install Extension.
2. Type at least two characters, for example `contrast`.
3. Results appear under **Results**. Each shows an icon for its kind, the title with your words highlighted, a short excerpt and the page address.
4. Move through them with `Up` and `Down`, or point at them. The preview pane shows the highlighted page's type, title, address, description and a **Why it ranks here** chart.
5. Press `Enter` to open the highlighted result. Hold `Ctrl` (`Cmd` on a Mac) as you press `Enter` to open it in a new tab.

Result: the page opens and Spotlight closes. Your search is added to **Recent**.

The first time you open Spotlight on a page, it prepares the site index in the background. For a moment the list may read "Search engine is loading…"; results then appear as you type.

## Go further

- At the foot of the results, **View all results for "…"** opens the full search page with filters and previews.
- Press `Tab` to move to the next scope tab. See [Scopes and results](scopes-and-results.md).
- Type a hex colour or two. See [Colour answers and filters](colour-answers-and-filters.md).
- Type `>` for commands. See [Commands](commands.md).
