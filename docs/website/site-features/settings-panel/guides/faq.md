# Settings panel FAQ

## Do I need an account to use Settings?

No. Every setting works signed out and is kept in this browser. The only exception is the Ads section, whose controls need a paid plan.

## Do my settings follow me to another device?

Not automatically. Export them with **Export** and load the file on the other device with **Import**.

## Does the accent colour change the colours inside the tools?

No. The accent recolours the site's interface: links, buttons, focus rings, tabs, kit launchers and tooltip borders. Colours you are working with in a tool, and colours shown in libraries, are not changed.

## Why is the default gold darker in the light theme?

Plain gold on a near-white page has too little contrast to read comfortably, so the light theme uses a darker gold for the default accent. Colours you pick yourself are used as chosen. The **Accent accessibility** readout shows how your accent performs.

## What is the difference between Restore section defaults and Reset all?

**Restore section defaults** resets only the section you are in. **Reset all** resets every section after asking you to confirm. Neither removes your saved colours.

## Is language included in an exported settings file?

No. The language is kept separately. Choose it again on the new browser, or open any page with `?lang=hi` added to its address (see [Language](../../language/README.md)).

## Can I open the panel on a specific section?

Yes, from a few places: the right-click menu's **Keyboard shortcuts** opens Shortcuts, the clock strip's **All settings** opens Clock, and an info bar's **Settings** opens Comfort.

## Does Reduce motion affect the custom cursor?

No. The custom cursor follows your operating system's reduced-motion preference, not this switch. To remove it, switch off **Custom cursor** in Comfort.

## Why does the panel look different on my phone?

On screens narrower than about 900 pixels, the section list becomes a strip across the top of the panel that you scroll sideways, and the control counts are hidden. All the same settings are there.
