# Related to the Settings panel

## Features the panel controls

- [Theme](../../theme/README.md) — light, dark and system themes and every other way to switch them.
- [Language](../../language/README.md) — English and Hindi.
- [Eye rest](../../eye-rest/README.md) — the reminders scheduled in Comfort.
- [Screen saver](../../screen-saver/README.md) — the idle screen scheduled in Comfort.
- [Custom cursor](../../custom-cursor/README.md) — the pointer switched in Comfort.
- [Info bars and notices](../../info-bars-and-notices/README.md) — the bars, notices, chips and clock strip switched in Highlights.
- [Accessibility aids](../../accessibility-aids/README.md) — reading mode and the other comfort settings, alongside the site's other aids.

## Other ways in

- [Context menu](../../context-menu/README.md) — **Site** › **Settings** and **Keyboard shortcuts**.
- [Search and Spotlight](../../search-and-spotlight/README.md) — the `>` command **Open settings**.
- [Header and navigation](../../header-and-navigation/README.md) — the page utilities bar and the mobile drawer's theme and language switches.
- [Kits](../../../kits/README.md) — the floating kits share the panel's title-bar controls and follow your accent colour.

- [Contrast Checker](../../../tools/accessibility-and-vision/contrast-checker/README.md) — check any two colours.
