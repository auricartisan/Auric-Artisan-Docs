# Troubleshooting languages

## The page stays in English after I choose Hindi

**Why:** the Hindi text is downloaded when you switch. On a slow or broken connection it may not arrive.

**What to do:** check your connection and reload the page; the site remembers Hindi and translates again on load. In the installed app offline, Hindi is available only if you prepared the offline version while the site was in Hindi.

## Some words are still in English

**Why:** some names and terms are kept in English on purpose (see [What is translated](what-is-translated.md)), and a few messages made up of several live values are not yet translated.

## Switching to English reloaded the page and I lost what I typed

**Why:** returning to English needs a reload.

**What to do:** before switching back, finish or copy your text. Many tool pages keep their inputs in the page address, so a reload restores them (see [Shareable links](../../shareable-links/README.md)).

## Devanagari text looks like boxes or a plain font

**Why:** your device has no Devanagari font, or not one of those the site asks for.

**What to do:** install a Devanagari font such as Noto Sans Devanagari, or add the Hindi language pack in your operating system's language settings.

## The site opened in Hindi although I never chose it

**Why:** you followed a link that ended in `?lang=hi`, which switches the site to Hindi and remembers it.

**What to do:** select the globe button, or choose **English** in Settings.

## Search does not find anything when I type in Hindi

**Why:** page search matches the English text of pages.

**What to do:** search with English words. Spotlight commands can be found in either language.
