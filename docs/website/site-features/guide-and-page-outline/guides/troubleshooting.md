# Troubleshooting the guide and section rail

## Pressing `?` does nothing

**Possible causes and fixes:**

- The cursor is in a text field. Click an empty part of the page first.
- A modifier key is held, or your keyboard layout needs `Shift` for `?` (that is fine) but also another key such as `Alt Gr` (which is not). Use the header's **Guide** button instead.
- The page is still loading. Wait a moment.
- A tour or the guide is already open.

## The tour highlights nothing, or the wrong place

**Why:** a stop points at an area the page has not shown yet, for example one that loads late, is hidden on small screens or is folded away.

**What to do:** select **Next** to move on, or widen the window and try again.

## The tour card says I finished, but I want to see it again

Select **Replay the tour**.

## The tour starts in the middle

**Why:** you left part-way through last time. The card offers **Pick up where you left off**.

**What to do:** select **Start over from stop 1**.

## The section rail covers part of a heading after jumping

**Why:** on some pages the header scrolls away during the jump, leaving the heading slightly lower than expected.

**What to do:** scroll a little; the correct section stays highlighted.

## A legal section stayed folded

**What to do:** select its heading to open it. Jumping from the rail or a direct link normally opens it for you.
