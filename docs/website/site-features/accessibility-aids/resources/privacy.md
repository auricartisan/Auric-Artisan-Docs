# Accessibility aids privacy

- Your choices are kept in this browser as part of your settings. They are not sent to Auric Artisan or attached to an account.
- The site reads your device's colour-scheme and reduced-motion preferences only to adjust the page; it does not record them.
- A page link you copy can carry appearance settings that differ from the defaults, such as text size (see [Shareable links](../../shareable-links/README.md)).
