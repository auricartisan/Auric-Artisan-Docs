# Cookie consent reference

## Categories

| Category | Can be turned off | Description on the Cookie Policy page (summary) |
|---|---|---|
| **Essential** | No (Always Active) | Core functions: authentication, security, session management and access control |
| **Functional** | Yes | Remembers settings such as theme, language, layout choices and form data |
| **Analytics** | Yes | How visitors use the platform; aggregate or pseudonymous, never used for advertising profiles |
| **Marketing** | Yes | Reserved for campaign attribution if enabled later; no marketing pixels or cross-site profiles now |

## Listed cookies

| Cookie | Category | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `aa_sid` | Essential | Signed-in session management and authentication | Up to 30 days | First-party |
| `aa_csrf` | Essential | Cross-site request forgery protection | Up to 30 days | First-party |
| `aa_cookie_consent` | Essential | Stores your cookie preferences | 1 year | First-party |
| `aa_theme` | Functional | Stores light/dark mode preference | 1 year | First-party |
| `aa_lang` | Functional | Language and locale preference | 1 year | First-party |
| `aa_eye_rest` | Functional | Eye rest feature interval and duration settings | 1 year | First-party |
| `aa_layout` | Functional | Grid/list view and sidebar preferences | 6 months | First-party |
| `aa_recent` | Functional | Recently viewed items for quick access | 30 days | First-party |
| `aa_analytics_id` | Analytics | Anonymous visitor identifier for usage metrics | 1 year | First-party |
| `aa_perf` | Analytics | Page load and interaction performance timing | Session | First-party |

Marketing lists no cookies.

## Consent bar

| Button | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Accept All** | All four categories on; saved |
| **Reject All** | Essential only; non-essential cookies deleted; saved |
| **Customise** | Opens **Manage Cookie Preferences** on the Cookie Policy page |

## Preferences section (Cookie Policy page)

| Control | Effect |
|---|---|
| Category switches | Choose each optional category |
| **Show cookies** / **Hide cookies** | Shows or hides the category's cookie table |
| **Accept All** | Sets every optional switch on (not saved until **Save Preferences**) |
| **Reject All** | Sets every optional switch off (not saved until **Save Preferences**) |
| **Save Preferences** | Saves the switches; deletes cookies of switched-off categories |
| **Clear All Cookies** | Deletes non-essential cookies and forgets your choice |
| **Withdraw Consent** | Forgets your choice and deletes non-essential cookies |
| **Consent recorded** | Date and time of your saved choice |

## Messages

| Message | When |
|---|---|
| Cookie preferences saved successfully. | After **Save Preferences** |
| All non-essential cookies have been cleared. | After **Clear All Cookies** |
| Cookie consent withdrawn. Preferences reset. | After **Withdraw Consent** |

## Defaults before any choice

Functional on; Analytics off; Marketing off. No analytics events are recorded until you give Analytics consent.
