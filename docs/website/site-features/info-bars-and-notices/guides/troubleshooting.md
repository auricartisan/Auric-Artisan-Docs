# Troubleshooting info bars and notices

## A bar I hid is back

**Why:** someone switched it on in Settings › Highlights, or you restored the section's defaults, which brings the bars back on the homepage.

**What to do:** select its ✕ again.

## A bar now shows on every page, not just the homepage

**Why:** switching a bar on in Settings shows it everywhere.

**What to do:** select **Restore section defaults** in Settings › Highlights to go back to homepage only.

## The ✕ tooltip says Comfort, but I cannot find the switch there

**Why:** the switches are in the **Highlights** section of Settings, not Comfort.

## The clock shows the wrong time zone

**Why:** a fixed zone was chosen in the clock menu or in Settings › Clock, or your device reports a zone the site could not match exactly (the label then shows "(default)").

**What to do:** right-click the strip and choose **Auto-detect**, or pick your zone from the list.

## The clock strip is missing

**Why:** it is off by default, and it is hidden in reading mode.

**What to do:** switch on **Clock strip** in Settings › Highlights.

## "Offline mode" will not go away

**Why:** your device reports that it has no connection. The message stays until the connection returns.

**What to do:** check your connection. Saved pages keep working meanwhile.
