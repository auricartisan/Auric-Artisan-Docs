# Screen recorder FAQ

## Are my recordings uploaded?

No. They are recorded and stored in your browser on this device. They leave it only when you download them.

## Are recordings saved in my Library?

No. Recordings have their own list, **My recordings**, separate from the library of palettes and assets.

## Can I record other websites or apps?

Yes, with **Screen or window**: your browser lets you pick any screen, window or tab to share.

## Why MP4 in one browser and WebM in another?

The recorder uses MP4 where the browser can record it, because it plays almost everywhere. Otherwise it records WebM. The dialog tells you which.

## Can I keep recording while I move to another page on the site?

No. Leaving or reloading the page ends the recording; the browser asks you to confirm first. Record with **Screen or window** if you need to move between pages, and pick the browser window.

## Does the custom cursor appear in recordings?

Yes, when you record the page itself. Switch it off in Settings › Comfort for a clean system pointer, and use **Highlight the cursor** if you want the pointer emphasised.
