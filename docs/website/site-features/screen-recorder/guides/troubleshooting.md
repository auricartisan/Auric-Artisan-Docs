# Troubleshooting the screen recorder

## The screen sources are greyed out

**Why:** "This browser cannot capture the screen." Most mobile browsers, and some older desktop browsers, do not let web pages capture the screen.

**What to do:** use a current desktop browser such as Chrome, Edge or Firefox, or use **Tool canvas** on a tool page.

## Tool canvas is greyed out

**Why:** "No recordable canvas on this page." The page has no visible canvas larger than 80 by 80 pixels.

**What to do:** open a tool that draws on a canvas, or choose another source.

## Nothing happened after Start recording

**Why:** you cancelled or closed your browser's sharing dialog, or denied permission.

**What to do:** select **Start recording** again and allow sharing. If you denied it permanently, allow screen sharing for auricartisan.com in your browser's site settings.

## The recording bar is in my video

**Why:** with **This tab**, the bar is part of the page, so it is recorded.

**What to do:** collapse it to a dot, record a **Region of this page** that leaves it out, or use **Screen or window** and pick another window.

## There is no sound

**Possible causes:**

- **Tab audio** was off, or the page made no sound.
- In your browser's sharing dialog, the option to share audio was not ticked (some browsers only offer audio for tabs).
- The microphone was refused ("Microphone unavailable — recording without it.").

## The recording stopped by itself

**Why:** it reached 30 minutes, you pressed your browser's **Stop sharing**, or the shared window was closed.

## The recording was downloaded instead of saved

**Why:** the browser refused to store it, usually because storage is full or the browser is in a private mode.

**What to do:** delete old recordings in **My recordings**, or keep the downloaded file.

## Exporting a GIF is slow or the GIF is large

**Why:** GIFs are made in your browser and hold every frame as an image.

**What to do:** trim the recording first, record at a lower resolution, or download the video instead.

## Trimming takes a long time

**Why:** trimming re-encodes the clip in real time, so it takes as long as the part you keep.

## My recordings disappeared

**Why:** they are stored in this browser. Clearing site data, using a private window, or a different browser or profile means they are not there.

**What to do:** download recordings you want to keep.
