# Feedback privacy

## Sent when you submit

- **Rating and comment:** your stars and what you typed, and the page you were on.
- **Survey:** your answers.
- **Bug report:** what you typed, the severity, and page details: the page address, window and screen size, browser and platform, language, theme, and recent page errors where available.
- **Contact details:** only if you choose **Add contact details** and fill them in.

Nothing is sent until you select a send button.

## Kept on this device

- When you were last asked and how you answered (to respect the quiet periods and **Don't ask again**).
- Unsent bug-report drafts.
- Feedback waiting to be sent while you are offline.

To remove these, clear the site's data in your browser.
