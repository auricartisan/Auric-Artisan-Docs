# Getting started with the dashboard

The account dashboard is one page with four sections. It shows what the account holds — your work, the public page that shows it, what you are paying for, and who is signed in — and it is where you change all of it.

## Open the dashboard

1. Sign in at https://auricartisan.com/auth/ (see [Sign in and sign out](sign-in.md)).
2. Open https://auricartisan.com/auth/dashboard/, or select your account chip in the header and choose **Dashboard**.

If you are not signed in, the dashboard sends you to the sign-in page and brings you back afterwards.

> **Tip:** After you sign in, the site takes you to the dashboard automatically if your account has no username yet, so you can claim one. Otherwise it returns you to the page you came from, or to the home page.

## The screen, top to bottom

### The masthead

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Auric Artisan · Account** | The page kicker |
| **@yourhandle** | Your username. It reads **@unclaimed** until you choose one, with a **Claim your handle** link beneath. |
| Profile photo | Your photo, or your initial, or an empty outline. Double-click it to open the photo viewer. |
| **Plan** | Your plan name, for example **Apprentice**. A status is added when it is not simply active, for example "Artisan · Past due". |
| Email address | The address you sign in with |
| Join date | "Joined today", or "Member since" and the month and year |
| **Open Studio** | Opens your saved work in the Library |
| **Upgrade** | Opens the pricing page. On a paid plan it reads **Manage** and opens **Plan & Usage** instead. It is hidden on Industrial Pro, the highest tier. |

### The section rail

Four tabs switch the sections without leaving the page:

| Tab | What it holds | Badge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overview** | A statement of the account, your saved work, suggested next steps and quick links to tools | — |
| **Profile** | Your name, username, bio, contact details, photo, and the doorway to your public portfolio | — |
| **Plan & Usage** | Your plan, usage meters, invoices, and (on plans with API access) keys and credits | Number of API keys |
| **Security & Privacy** | Password, two-step verification, cookies, signed-in devices, data export and the danger zone | Number of signed-in devices |

A badge turns green while its count is zero. With the rail focused, the `Left` and `Right` arrow keys move between tabs, and `Home` and `End` jump to the first and last.

Each section has its own address, so you can bookmark it: `#overview`, `#identity` (Profile), `#plan` and `#security`. Older addresses still work and land in the right place:

| Old address | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| `#profile`, `#portfolio`, https://auricartisan.com/auth/profile/ | **Profile** |
| `#billing`, `#api-keys`, https://auricartisan.com/auth/billing/, https://auricartisan.com/auth/api-keys/ | **Plan & Usage** |
| `#settings`, `#sessions`, https://auricartisan.com/auth/settings/, https://auricartisan.com/auth/sessions/ | **Security & Privacy** |
| https://auricartisan.com/auth/portfolio/ | The Portfolio Builder at https://auricartisan.com/portfolio/ |

### The "Finish setting up" panel

If your account is missing something the site needs — usually a username, sometimes a display name — a floating panel titled **Your account is nearly there** appears. It is not a blocking dialog: you can keep using the page, press `Esc`, or select **Later**. It returns after a week if the detail is still missing. It stays out of the way while the **Profile** section is open, because that section holds the same fields.

In the panel:

1. Type a username after the **@** (it is pre-filled with a suggestion when possible). A tick and **Available**, or a cross and the reason, appears as you type.
2. Type **Your name** if it is asked for.
3. If your email is not verified, select **Send it again** to get a new verification link.
4. Select **Save**.

## Your first five tasks

1. **Claim your username.** Select **Claim your handle** in the masthead, or open **Profile**, type it in **Username** and select **Save changes**. It becomes the address of your public portfolio and can be used to sign in. See [Profile section](profile.md).
2. **Verify your email.** Until you do, billing, API keys and device approval stay locked. See [Verify your email](verify-email.md).
3. **Add a name and photo.** The **Finish setting up** card in the Overview side column tracks these; it disappears when all four items are done.
4. **Turn on two-step verification.** Open **Security & Privacy**, select **Enable 2FA**, scan the code, and save your backup codes. See [Two-step verification](two-step-verification.md).
5. **Check your plan.** Open **Plan & Usage** to see your plan and usage. New accounts are on the free **Apprentice** plan with 100 starter tokens. See [Plans and pricing](plans-and-pricing.md).
