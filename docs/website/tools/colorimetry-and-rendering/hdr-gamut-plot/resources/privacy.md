# HDR Gamut Plot privacy

- **Images stay on your device.** An image you drop in is read and analysed by your browser; it is not uploaded, and the tool keeps only the resulting figures on the page.
- **All curves, planes and measurements are computed in your browser.**
- **Exports are created locally** and saved through your browser's download, or copied to your clipboard when you choose.
- **The link** carries the axes, peak brightness, tone curve and perceptual plane. Anyone with the link sees those settings.
- **Save to Library** stores a snapshot in your Auric Artisan Library.
- The page loads the site's shared features like every other page on auricartisan.com; see the site's privacy policy.
