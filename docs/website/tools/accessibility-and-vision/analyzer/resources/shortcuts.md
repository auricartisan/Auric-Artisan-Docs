# Analyzer keyboard shortcuts

The Analyzer can be used entirely from the keyboard. `Tab` and `Shift` + `Tab` move between controls as usual; the keys below are the Analyzer's own.

## Anywhere on the Analyzer page

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| `Ctrl` + `P` | Open the Sections picker, in Simple or Advanced; replaces the print shortcut on this page |

## In the URL field

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| `Enter` | Start the audit, the same as **Analyze** |

## In the Sections picker

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Type any text | Filter the sections by name |
| `Up` / `Down` | Move to the row above or below, as the rows are laid out |
| `Left` / `Right` | Move across columns, when the filter field is empty |
| `Enter` | Open the highlighted section (or scroll to it) and close the picker |
| `Space` | Open or close the highlighted section and keep the picker open |
| `Esc` | Close the picker |

## On a panel

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| `Left` / `Right` on the panel grip | Move the panel one place earlier or later in the workspace |
| `Enter` or `Space` on **Width**, **Focus**, **Collapse**, **Close** | Use that button |

## On the token meter

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| `Enter` or `Space` | Open the token sheet |

## Disclosures and cards

Folding cards and disclosures, such as **Why this score?**, **Cause chain** and the A11y+ cards, open and close with `Enter` or `Space`.

For shortcuts that work across the whole site, see [Keyboard shortcuts](../../../../site-features/keyboard-shortcuts/README.md).
