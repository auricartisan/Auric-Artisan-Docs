# Vision Lab privacy

## Your images stay on your device

The page says it plainly: "Whatever you drop stays on your machine — the pipeline runs in this tab and nothing is uploaded." Every simulation, measurement and audit runs in your browser.

## What is kept in your browser

- the view you last used, and the Workbench's condition, severity, model, specimen and base colour;
- the Catalogue category, your favourites, the Compare sheet and the Palette audit palette;
- the panels you had open, their snapshots, and the position of the Vision Control dock.

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.

## The daily count

On the free plan, the lab counts how many simulations you open from the Catalogue each day, and the count is also checked by Auric Artisan. Only the count is involved; your colours and images are not sent.

## Links

A workspace link (`#v=`) and a Workbench link (`?vw=`) carry your settings in the address, readable by anyone who has the link. They never contain your own images.

The site-wide privacy policy is covered in [Company and legal](../../../../company-and-legal/README.md).
