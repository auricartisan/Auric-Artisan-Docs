# Accessibility Checklist glossary

**APG** — The W3C ARIA Authoring Practices Guide, which sets out the expected keyboard behaviour for widgets such as tabs and menus.

**Area** — One of the nine numbered groups of checks, 01 to 09.

**AUTO, PARTLY, BY HAND** — How much the Analyzer can decide a check: on its own, by narrowing it down for a person, or not at all.

**Check** — One of the 50 items on the checklist.

**Decided** — Marked. On the Export tab's level bars this means Passed or Flagged; in the Markdown export it also includes N/A.

**Evidence** — Screenshots attached to a check, with their annotations.

**Flagged** — A check marked **Fail**. The button says Fail, the state word says Failed, and the meter and exports say flagged.

**Inspector** — The two-column working view: the index of checks beside the selected check in full.

**Index** — The list of checks on the left of the Inspector.

**Level A, AA, AAA** — WCAG conformance levels. A and AA are what laws cite; AAA is stricter and optional.

**Meter** — The counter beside the tabs: passed, flagged and left, out of 50.

**One-check view** — The phone layout that shows one check at a time.

**Pane** — The selected check in full, on the right of the Inspector.

**Pen** — The screenshot evidence dialog and its annotation tools.

**Practice** — A good-practice check with no WCAG success criterion of its own.

**Required** — A check at Level A or AA.

**Scope chip** — The label beside the meter that names the active filter and how many rows it shows.

**Success criterion** — One testable requirement in WCAG, such as 2.4.7 Focus Visible.

**Untouched** — A check that is not marked. Also shown as "Not checked yet", "Never checked" or "left". An untouched check is not a pass.

**Verdict** — A mark on a check: Pass, Fail or N/A.

**VPAT** — Voluntary Product Accessibility Template, the report format procurement often asks for.

**WCAG** — The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines published by the W3C. The checklist is written against WCAG 2.2.
