# Accessibility Checklist privacy

The page puts it simply: "Your work stays on this device."

## What is kept, and where

| Item | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Your marks on each check | Your browser's local storage |
| Screenshots, the original image and its marks, and a marked-up copy | Your browser's database storage |

- There is no account, no sync and no server copy. The checklist sends none of your marks or screenshots anywhere.
- Your work is per browser, per profile and per device.
- In a private window, or when storage is blocked, the page still works but forgets when you close it. Without database storage, screenshots cannot be attached, but marks still work.

## What leaves your device

Only what you choose to take out:

- **Copy as Markdown** puts the findings on your clipboard. Screenshots are never included.
- **Export the report** prints the page, including small marked-up screenshot thumbnails.

## Redaction

The **Redact** tool covers an area only in the marked-up copy used for thumbnails and printing. The original screenshot stays stored in your browser, unredacted. Crop or blur sensitive details before you attach a screenshot.

## Working in two tabs

Each open tab keeps its own copy of your marks and saves it as a whole. Working in two tabs at once may overwrite marks made in the other. Use one tab at a time.

## Delete your data

- **Export** › **Reset** removes every mark and every screenshot at once, without asking.
- Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes everything too.
