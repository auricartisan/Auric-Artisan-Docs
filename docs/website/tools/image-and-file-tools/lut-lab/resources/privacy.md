# Privacy

## Your files stay in your browser

- A `.cube` file you load is read as text by the page itself, in your browser. It is not uploaded.
- A picture you load is read in the page and scaled to fit 480 × 270 for the analysis. It is not uploaded.
- The looks, the measurements and the exports are all worked out on your device.
- Exported files are created in the browser and saved by your browser's normal download.
- The clipboard is written only when you select **Copy it** or **Copy a link to this setting**.

## Nothing is kept after you leave

LUT Lab does not store your `.cube`, your picture or your settings. Reloading or leaving the page clears them.

## What leaves in an export or link

- An exported `.cube` contains the transform's name; for a loaded file, that is the file's title or name.
- The JSON report contains your picture's file name, if you used one, and the figures.
- A share link contains only the scene, look, strength, exposure, size and kind. It does not contain your file or picture.

## The page itself

LUT Lab is a normal page on auricartisan.com. Site-wide features such as sign-in, preferences and any consent-based services follow the site's own privacy policy and your cookie choices; see [Help](../../../../../help/README.md).
