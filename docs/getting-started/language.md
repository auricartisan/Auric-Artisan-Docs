# Language: English and Hindi

The Auric Artisan website is available in English and Hindi (हिन्दी). The interface, the tool pages and the articles are translated. English is the default.

## Switch language

Use any of these; they all change the same setting.

- **Header button** — select the language button in the header (its label is "Switch language between English and Hindi"). Each press switches between the two.
- **Mobile menu** — open the navigation menu and, under **Preferences** › **Language**, select **English** or **हिन्दी**.
- **Settings** — press `Ctrl` + `,` (or use the floating settings button), open **Appearance**, and under **Language** select **English** or **हिन्दी**.

### What you see

- Switching to Hindi translates the page in place, without a reload.
- Switching back to English reloads the page, because the original English text has to be loaded again.
- Dates and numbers follow the chosen language.

## How the choice is remembered

Your language is saved in this browser and applies to every page you open afterwards, until you change it. It is not tied to your account, so a new browser or device starts in English.

## Share a page in a chosen language

Add `?lang=hi` to a page address to open it in Hindi, or `?lang=en` for English. The person who opens the link also has that language remembered in their browser. For example:

```text
https://auricartisan.com/company/pricing/?lang=hi
```

## Things to know about Hindi

- The site transliterates its own brand and product names into Devanagari. Third-party names that people type or search for, such as Google, GitHub, VS Code, Figma and Razorpay, stay in Latin script.
- Technical terms with no common Hindi equivalent — colour-space names like CIELAB, model names, font names and header names — stay in Latin script.
- Search queries are matched against English text, so search in English if a Hindi query finds nothing.
- Where a page asks you to type a confirmation sentence (for example, unlocking the danger zone in the dashboard), type the sentence exactly as it is displayed in your language.
- The sign-in and other account pages are translated too.
