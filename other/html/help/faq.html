Frequently asked questions

Short answers to common questions about Auric Artisan — the platform, the tools, accounts, payments, privacy, languages, offline use and the other products.

The platform

What is Auric Artisan?

A colour-science and accessibility platform: tools, libraries and learning material on the website, plus a browser extension, VS Code extensions and a REST API. See What Auric Artisan is.

Who makes it?

One independent developer, Chirag Bansal, in India. The Security page notes the platform is operated by one person. See Author page.

Is it free?

Every tool is free on every plan, with no login needed to start. Paid plans add conveniences such as ad-free browsing, watermark-free exports, a monthly token allowance and higher limits. See The free-tools model.

Why do some pages show ads?

The free plan's card on the pricing page says "Sponsored slots (ads) support the free tools". Paid plans include ad-free browsing. The Subscription and Billing Policy says ads shown to free users are limited and consent-gated, and that private data is not used for advertising profiles.

Is Auric Artisan an official or government body?

No. It is an independent private platform. References to CIE, W3C or ISO standards are informational and do not imply affiliation.

The tools

Can I rely on the results professionally?

For design, accessibility evaluation, palette work and web work, the outputs follow published standards. They are guidance, not certification. For colour-critical industrial work use calibrated instruments; for accessibility compliance have a qualified person review; for vision, see an eye-care professional.

Is the Ishihara-style generator a real colour-vision test?

No. It is for exploration and teaching only. It is not a medical device and must not be used for screening, diagnosis, employment or licensing.

Can I analyse any website?

Only sites and pages you own, manage or are authorised to test. The Terms and the Acceptable Use Policy require it.

Who owns what I make?

You do. Palettes, themes, token files and reports you generate are yours to use, including commercially. The platform's code, design and articles remain Auric Artisan's.

Where is my saved work?

In My Library, stored in the browser you saved it in. It does not follow you to another device; cloud sync is listed as coming soon. Export or back up anything important.

Accounts and payments

Do I need an account?

No, not for the tools. You need one for server-backed runs (tokens), buying a plan, API keys, publishing a portfolio and account security. See Create an account (optional).

How do I pay, and will it renew?

Through Razorpay's checkout on auricartisan.com: cards, UPI, netbanking and wallets. A plan is a one-time pass for one or twelve months and never renews. See Buy a plan.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 7 days of buying a plan, for any reason. See Refunds.

What are tokens and API credits?

Tokens pay for server-backed work in website tools; API credits pay for REST API calls. They are separate. See Tokens.

Privacy

What data does the site collect?

The minimum needed: account details if you have an account, security records, the inputs to server-backed tools, messages you send, order records, and analytics only if you consent. See Privacy overview.

Does the site sell my data or track me across the web?

The Privacy Policy states it does not sell personal data, does no cross-site tracking and does not use personal data for behavioural advertising.

How do I change my cookie choice?

On https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/ in Manage Cookie Preferences, or from Settings. See Cookie Policy summary.

Language, theme and offline

Is the site available in other languages?

English and Hindi. See Language: English and Hindi.

How do I switch to dark mode?

Use the theme button in the header, Settings › Appearance › Theme, or Alt + T. See Light and dark theme.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once you prepare it: install the site as an app and cache it from https://auricartisan.com/download/. Account features and server-backed runs still need a connection. See Offline use.

Other products

Where is the browser extension?

On the Chrome Web Store (also used by Edge, Opera, Brave and Vivaldi) and Firefox Add-ons, linked from https://auricartisan.com/extension.html. See Browser extension.

Where are the VS Code extensions?

On the Visual Studio Marketplace under auric-artisan, listed at https://auricartisan.com/vscode/. See VS Code extensions.

Do the extensions need an account or send data?

No. The site describes the browser extension and the VS Code extensions as needing no account and sending no telemetry. The browser extension makes no network requests with your data.

Getting help

How do I contact someone?

Use https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/ and pick the topic; a person answers. See Contact and support.

How do I report a security problem?

Email security@auricartisan.com or use the contact form's Security topic. See Security page summary.
