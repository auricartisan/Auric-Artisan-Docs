Privacy overview

What Auric Artisan stores in your browser, what it sends to its servers and third parties, how analytics consent works, what each product collects, and how to use your privacy rights.

This page brings together what the policies and the product say about your data. The authoritative documents are the Privacy Policy (https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/), the Cookie Policy (https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/), the Data Retention and Deletion Policy and, for the browser extension, the Extension Privacy Policy. Plain-language summaries of each are in Company and legal.

Stored in your browser

WhatNotes
My Library (palettes, gradients, collections, exports)Stays on this device; not uploaded unless you publish or export it
Tool state and drafts, including the Portfolio Builder draftStays on this device
Settings: theme, language, accent colour, text size, comfort options, eye restThe Privacy Policy says many preferences are not sent to the servers
Your cookie choiceKept for up to a year
The offline cache, if you prepared oneThe site's pages and data, for offline use
Your sign-in (session and CSRF cookies)Only when you sign in; up to 30 days or until sign-out

Anything in your browser is lost if you clear site data, and does not follow you to other devices. See Clear site data and start fresh.

Sent to Auric Artisan's servers

WhatWhen
Account details and profile fieldsWhen you sign up, sign in or edit your profile
Security records: sessions, sign-in attempts, security eventsWhile you use an account
Web analysis inputs: URLs, page metadata, screenshots, extracted colours, scan resultsWhen a tool analyses a page for you. The FAQ page says no URL history or page content is kept after the analysis session.
Server-backed tool workWhen you start a run that uses tokens
Contact messages and attachmentsWhen you use the contact form
Order records: item, amount, payment referenceWhen you buy something
Analytics eventsOnly with consent (below)

The Privacy Policy says colour calculations, palettes, vision simulations and theme generation run in your browser where possible and are not sent anywhere.

Analytics and consent

  • Analytics is first-party: events go to Auric Artisan's own servers. There are no third-party analytics scripts and no analytics cookies.
  • Events are sent only if you accepted the Analytics category in the cookie bar or on the Cookie Policy page. No choice means no events.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control in your browser turn analytics off regardless.
  • An event contains the event name, the page path (never the query string), the referrer's origin, and a few coarse properties such as device class.

The dashboard's Usage data switch (in Security & Privacy) records your choice on your account; the cookie choice is what governs analytics on the website.

Third parties

CompanyRole (as the policies describe it)
CloudflareHosting, DNS, CDN, compute and storage for account, preference and order data
RazorpayPayments. Card, UPI and bank details go to Razorpay directly and never reach Auric Artisan.
Google FontsFont files on some pages; Google receives the technical request, such as your IP address
ResendEmail delivery (named on the Security page)

Free-plan pages can show sponsored slots. The Subscription and Billing Policy says ads shown to free users are limited and consent-gated, and that private data is not used for advertising profiles.

What each product collects

ProductSummary
Website, no accountYour browser keeps your work and settings; servers see page requests, tool inputs for server-backed work, and analytics only with consent
Website, with accountThe above, plus account, security and order records
Browser extensionReads a page only when you use a tool on it; stores everything in the browser's extension storage; no analytics, telemetry or network requests with your data
VS Code extensionsDescribed on https://auricartisan.com/vscode/ as needing no account and sending no telemetry
REST APIYour requests and usage, metered against your account

No sale, no tracking across sites

The Privacy Policy states that Auric Artisan does not sell, rent or trade personal data, does not take part in data-broker networks or cross-site tracking, and does not use personal data for behavioural advertising or automated decisions with legal effects.

Your rights, in practice

RightHow to use it
Access or a copyDashboard › Security & Privacy › Export; for more, contact topic Privacy or data
CorrectionDashboard › Profile; for your email address, contact support
DeletionDashboard danger zone, or contact topic Privacy or data
Withdraw consentCookie Policy page › Withdraw Consent, or change the switches
Object or complainContact topic Privacy or data

Privacy or data messages go to security@auricartisan.com, with a stated reply time of within 30 days. The Cookie Policy also lists privacy@auricartisan.com for cookie questions.
