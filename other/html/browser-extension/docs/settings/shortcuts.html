Shortcuts settings

The keyboard map in Settings, how to change the browser-level shortcuts, and how to replay the tour.

Settings › Shortcuts lists the keys the extension answers to and links to where you change them.

The keyboard map

ActionKeys
Open Auric ArtisanAlt + Shift + A
Open the contrast checkerAlt + Shift + C
Read the page paletteAlt + Shift + E
Toggle vision simulationAlt + Shift + V
Search in the popup/ or Ctrl + K
Inspector: pin · CSS · selector · JSONP, C, S, J
Close a picker, panel or menuEsc

The first four are browser-level shortcuts and work from any page. Inspect an element on the page and Run a full accessibility audit are also available but have no key until you assign one.

Buttons

  • Change them in the browser: Opens your browser's extension shortcuts page: chrome://extensions/shortcuts in Chromium browsers, about:addons in Firefox (then the gear menu › Manage Extension Shortcuts)
  • Replay the tour: Closes Settings and starts the five-step tour

The complete list, including the inspector's and the right-click menu's keys, is in Keyboard shortcuts.
