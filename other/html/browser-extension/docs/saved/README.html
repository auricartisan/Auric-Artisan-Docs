Saved

The Saved space — your colour library and palettes, your colour history, and links to the full-size tools on auricartisan.com.

The Saved space keeps what you want to come back to. Library holds colours and palettes you saved on purpose, History holds the colours you picked recently, and On the web links to the full-size tools on auricartisan.com. Everything is stored in your browser only.
