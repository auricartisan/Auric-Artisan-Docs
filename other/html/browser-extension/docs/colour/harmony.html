Harmony

Build a colour harmony from a base colour in nine schemes on a wheel you can drag, then copy, send or save it.

Harmony (the Harmony builder) finds colours that go with a base colour, using the standard colour-wheel schemes. Open it from the Picker's Harmony pane (Open the full harmony builder →) or search for "Harmony".

Use it

  1. Set the base colour with its swatch or type a HEX value.
  2. Choose a scheme (Triadic is the default).
  3. Drag on the wheel to change the base colour's hue and saturation; its lightness stays the same.

The wheel marks each colour in the harmony and draws lines between them. A strip below shows the colours.

Schemes

SchemeWhat it gives
ComplementaryThe base and the colour opposite it
Split complementThe base and the two neighbours of its opposite
AnalogousThe base and its neighbours on the wheel
TriadicThree colours evenly spaced around the wheel
TetradicFour colours forming a rectangle on the wheel
SquareFour colours evenly spaced
MonochromaticVariations of the base hue
Double splitSplit complements on both sides
CompoundA mix of analogous and complementary colours

Buttons

ButtonWhat it does
CopyCopies the colours as a comma-separated HEX list
SendLoads the colours into Page palette, where every export is available
SaveSaves them to Saved › Library as a palette labelled with the scheme

Picker at the top returns to the Picker.
