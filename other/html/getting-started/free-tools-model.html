Getting started

The free-tools model

Why every Auric Artisan tool is free on every plan, what the paid plans add instead, and how plan passes are sold.

Every Auric Artisan tool is free, on every plan, for everyone. The pricing page (https://auricartisan.com/company/pricing/) opens with "Every tool is free, forever" and lists tools that used to sit behind a paywall — LUT Lab, the ICC Profile Parser, the Color Science Lab, the clinical vision simulations and the industrial suite — as free on every tier.

Paid plans do not unlock tools. They sell conveniences and scale.

What stays free

  • Every tool, with no login required to start.
  • Local saves in your browser.
  • The libraries, the Learning Hub and the documentation.
  • The browser extension and the VS Code extensions.
  • Two-step verification and account activity logging (the pricing page lists "2FA free for everyone").
  • Everything that runs in your browser is free and unmetered — for example, the pricing page notes that vision simulations run in your browser and are not metered at all.

What the paid plans add

The paid plans on the pricing page add, depending on the tier:

  • ad-free browsing and ad controls;
  • watermark-free Pro exports and more export formats;
  • a monthly allowance of tokens for server-backed work, such as whole-site accessibility crawls of up to 24 pages a run;
  • higher limits (saved projects, URL analyses);
  • the REST API, with a monthly allowance of API credits (Specialist and above);
  • branded WCAG accessibility reports (Specialist and above);
  • email or priority support.

Some listed perks — cloud sync across devices, version history, batch processing, team seats, white-label embedding and SSO — are marked coming soon or planned on the pricing page and are not live yet. The full plan-by-plan table is in Plans and pricing.

How paid plans are sold

  • One payment, fixed term. A paid plan is a one-time purchase of a pass for one month or twelve months. It does not auto-renew, no card is stored for a future charge, and there is no subscription to cancel.
  • When the term ends, the account returns to the free Apprentice plan on its own. Every tool keeps working and nothing you made is deleted.
  • Money-back window. Plan purchases carry a 7-day money-back window.
  • Account first. You must be signed in before you pay, so the pass belongs to your account rather than to one browser.
  • On sale today. The pricing page shows Artisan as available now; Specialist and Industrial Pro are marked Coming soon.

Tokens, in one paragraph

Tokens meter work that runs on Auric Artisan's servers. The free Apprentice plan includes 100 starter tokens once, which do not renew; paid plans include an allowance that refreshes each billing month (250 on Artisan). Unused included tokens expire at the end of the billing period. See Tokens.
