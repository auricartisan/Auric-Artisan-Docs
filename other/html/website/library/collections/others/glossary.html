Collections glossary

Definitions of the terms used on the Collections page.

TermMeaning
CardOne tool's tile in the grid: artwork, category, name, description and a pin button.
Catalogue orderThe fixed order in which tools are listed in the catalogue. The Reference lists all 30 tools in this order.
CategoryThe broad group a tool belongs to, such as Colorimetry or Utilities. Each tool has exactly one.
Card menuThe menu that opens when you right-click a card, with card actions and grid settings.
ColumnsHow many cards sit side by side in grid view. Auto fits the screen; 2, 3 or 4 force a number.
DensityThe spacing between cards. Cozy is normal; Compact is tighter.
EverythingThe tool-visibility setting that shows all 30 tools.
FrecencyA mix of frequency and recency: how often you opened a tool, weighted towards recent opens. The Most used order is based on it.
ModeA tool's level: Basic (everyday), Advanced (scientific or power-user) or Both (shown in every setting).
OpenSelecting a card to go to the tool. Each open is counted towards Most used.
PinA mark you put on a tool so it is grouped under Pinned and comes first in Start here and Pinned first.
Reading modeA site setting that makes pages calmer and easier to read. The card menu's Reading switch turns it on and off.
Start hereThe slider of three featured tools near the top of the page.
Tool visibilityThe site-wide setting, chosen in the Advanced Kit, that decides which tools appear: Basic, Advanced, Everything or Custom.
TopicA short tag on a tool, such as palette or hdr. A tool can have several. The Topics filter lists up to 14 of the most common.
