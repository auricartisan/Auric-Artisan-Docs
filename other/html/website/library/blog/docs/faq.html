Blog FAQ

Short answers about the Auric Artisan blog.

How often are posts published?

Occasionally. The page describes them as "occasional posts". There were three at the time of writing.

What is the difference between the blog, the changelog and Learn?

The blog explains plans and reasoning. The changelog records what changed in each release. Learn holds the long-form colour-science course.

Can I comment?

Posts do not have comments. The welcome post invites questions and topic ideas by email or social media; see Company and legal for contact details.

Is there a feed?

Yes: https://auricartisan.com/library/blog/rss.xml.
