What Shipped Since April

  • Date: 27 July 2026
  • Category: Product
  • Link: https://auricartisan.com/library/blog/articles/2026-07-27-what-shipped-since-april

An honest account of what came out of the April roadmap by late July 2026, with the reasoning, and a list of what was not yet done:

  • One browser extension instead of four. The colour, accessibility, developer-tools and suite extensions were merged into one product, which gained a side panel, pickers that work on any page, a custom right-click menu and a searchable settings view. Its accessibility checks grew from 20 to 97.
  • The Analyzer's accessibility checks were rebuilt on a 112-rule engine covering ARIA 1.2, accessible names and APCA contrast, reporting an accessibility tree, a screen-reader transcript, colour-vision simulation and a VPAT export. The rules load only when you open the tab.
  • Payments went live for one-time purchases, with prices and what you receive both decided by the site rather than the browser. First-party, consent-based analytics arrived at the same time.
  • The VS Code extension reached 0.13.2, joining the plugins for Figma, Sketch, Penpot, Raycast and Alfred — all free.
  • Not yet done (as of the post): token top-ups were not on sale; accounts were single-user; cloud sync covered account settings but not saved palettes and projects; there was no single sign-on, white-label embedding or uptime guarantee.

Auric Artisan Roadmap — Next Few Months

  • Date: 21 April 2026
  • Category: Roadmap
  • Link: https://auricartisan.com/library/blog/articles/2026-04-21-auric-artisan-roadmap

A phased plan for the following months, summarised as Organize → Explain → Simplify → Optimize → Expand: a clearer start menu with essentials first and advanced tools hidden; a Status Kit showing each tool's stability; a casual versus advanced mode with plain-language tooltips; faster, non-freezing heavy tools; and more customisation, better split-screen work and documentation. It closes with a month-by-month plan for May, June and July 2026. The post now opens with an update noting that much of the roadmap has shipped, with a link to the changelog.

Welcome to the Auric Artisan Blog

  • Date: 21 April 2026
  • Category: Blog
  • Link: https://auricartisan.com/library/blog/articles/2026-04-21-welcome-to-auric-artisan-blog

Chirag Bansal introduces the blog as a place to share the process behind Auric Artisan, not just finished results. It promises deep dives into colour theory and workflows, behind-the-scenes posts, interactive demos and code, and lessons learned, and invites questions and topic ideas by email or social media, with RSS for new posts.
