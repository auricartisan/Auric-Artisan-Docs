Learn glossary

Definitions of the terms used on the Learn page and in Learn articles' page furniture.

Each article also ends with its own glossary, The terms, defined, for the colour-science terms it teaches. This page covers the terms of the Learn library itself.

TermMeaning
Article numberAn article's place in the course, from 1 to 113.
CategoryOne of seven groups used to filter the course: Foundations, Physics, Vision, Colorimetry, Digital, Design, Print.
Course orderThe order the articles were written to be read in, from what light is to printing.
Continue your journeyThe personalised suggestions at the end of each article.
DockedA demo panel placed beside the article so both can be seen.
Lab, live demoAn interactive demonstration inside an article, opened with Live demo.
PinA mark that keeps an article at the front of Continue your journey.
Quick checkThe short quiz at the end of an article.
ReadAn article you have scrolled at least 55% of the way through, in this browser.
RSSA feed format that lets a news reader show new articles automatically.
