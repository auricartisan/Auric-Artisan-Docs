Troubleshooting

Symptoms, causes and fixes for problems on the Learn page and in Learn articles.

The Learn page shows a plain list, with no search or categories

Why: The page could not load the Learn catalogue, so it shows the built-in list of all articles instead.

What to do: Check your connection and reload. Every link in the plain list still works.

My progress and ticks have gone

Why: Progress is kept in this browser only. It does not follow you to another browser or device, and clearing this site's data, or using a private window, removes it.

What to do: Nothing can restore it. Reading past 55% of an article marks it as read again.

An article I finished is not ticked

Why: An article counts as read when you have scrolled at least 55% of the way through it. Jumping straight to the end with the table of contents still counts; reading only the top half does not.

What to do: Scroll through the article.

"Continue your journey" is the same on every visit

Why: If the Learn catalogue cannot load, or the article is not in it, the author's original suggestions stay.

What to do: Reload with a working connection. Also note that pinned articles always come first.

A live demo will not open, or looks empty

Why: Demos need JavaScript and a modern browser. Some content blockers or very old browsers stop them.

What to do: Allow scripts for auricartisan.com and use a current browser. Try Full screen if the demo is cramped.

The downloaded file has no demos or pictures

Why: Download saves the article's text only. Demos, figures and the quiz are left out by design.

What to do: Use Print (or print to PDF) to keep the page as it looks.

Search finds nothing

Why: Every word you type must appear in an article's number, title, summary or category. Articles use US spelling in titles ("color").

What to do: Use fewer words, or try the other spelling. Select Show all articles to reset.
