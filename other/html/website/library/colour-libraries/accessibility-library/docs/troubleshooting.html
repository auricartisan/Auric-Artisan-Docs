Troubleshooting

Symptoms, causes and fixes for problems in the Accessibility Library.

Fewer than twelve picks appear

Why: Some hues cannot reach the target on this ground at any lightness, or their pick would land just under the target. Those hues are left out rather than showing a failing colour.

What to do: Lower the target, or choose a ground further from mid-grey (very dark or very light).

"unreachable at this hue"

Why: On a mid-tone ground, some hues never reach the target however light or dark they are.

What to do: Move to a different hue, change the ground, or use Swap to explore from the other side.

Territory is very small

Why: Mid-tone grounds leave little room: text must be much lighter or much darker, and at some hues neither is possible.

What to do: Use a darker or lighter ground if the design allows. Territory tells you how much room each ground leaves.

Pasted colours do not appear

Why: Only hex codes that start with # are read, with 3 to 6 digits. Names, rgb() and hex codes without # are ignored. Only the first 24 colours are placed.

What to do: Paste hex codes with #, for example #6B7280.

A colour passes here but fails in an audit tool

Why: This page measures against the ground you chose, with the WCAG 2 formula. An audit tool may use the actual background behind the text (including images or transparency), text size, or a different method such as APCA.

What to do: Check that the ground matches the real background, and read the APCA figure as well as the ratio.

"Copy failed"

Why: Your browser blocked clipboard access.

What to do: Click inside the page and try again, or allow clipboard access for auricartisan.com.

The browse grid shows few cards

Why: With Pairs that pass, most random pairs are skipped; at 7:1 even fewer qualify.

What to do: Keep scrolling — the page keeps scanning — or choose Everything.

The index lookup says "Not a record id"

Why: Record ids look like acc_ followed by digits and letters, for example acc_91.

What to do: Type the id exactly, or type the record's number.
