Accessibility Library glossary

Definitions of the terms used in the Accessibility Library.

TermMeaning
AA, AAAWCAG conformance levels. For normal text, AA needs 4.5:1 and AAA needs 7:1.
APCA (Lc)Advanced Perceptual Contrast Algorithm; its lightness contrast value Lc runs from about −108 to 106. Larger absolute values are more readable.
Auric SDAuric Artisan's combined 0–100 score for a pair (WCAG, APCA, vision separation, lightness gap, hue gap), graded S, A, B, C, D or F.
CaretThe marker on the map showing the current foreground.
Contrast ratioThe WCAG 2 measure of the luminance difference between two colours, from 1:1 to 21:1.
CorpusThe five million scored pairs behind the index.
ForegroundThe text or icon colour.
GamutThe range of colours a colour space or screen can show. The map shows the sRGB gamut.
GroundThe background colour you are testing against.
Large textAt least 18 point (24 CSS pixels), or 14 point (about 18.7 CSS pixels) bold.
OKLCHA perceptual colour space: lightness (0 to 1), chroma and hue.
PickOne of up to twelve suggested passing foregrounds.
Record idacc_ followed by a record's number in base 36.
SeedThe fixed number (9001) from which every record is derived.
TargetThe contrast threshold you are testing: 3:1, 4.5:1 or 7:1.
TerritoryThe share of the map that clears the target on the current ground.
Vision marginWorst-case separation of the two colours under ten simulated vision conditions, 0 to 1.
WCAGWeb Content Accessibility Guidelines.
