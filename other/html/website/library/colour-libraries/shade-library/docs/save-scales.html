Save scales

Save shade scales, find them on the Saved tab, and reach them from your Library.

Save

Select Save on a card, or Save to library under Quick export on Inspect. The page confirms Shade saved, and the Saved tab's badge goes up.

Find saved scales

Open the Saved tab. Scales appear as cards, most recently saved first, with a note that they are Also saved to your Library workspace with image previews. With nothing saved: No saved shade systems yet.

Unsave

Select Saved on the card, or Unsave on Inspect. The page confirms Shade removed, and the scale leaves your Library too.

In your Library

Each saved scale is added to your Library under its name (for example Material Like #FF724C #00001), described by method, base colour and token count. Open your Library with Ctrl + Shift + L. See the Library Kit docs.

Limits

Up to 500 scales are kept; saving more drops the oldest. Saves are kept in this browser only.
