Privacy

What the Shade Library keeps in your browser and what it fetches over the network.

What stays in your browser

WhatHow to remove it
Saved scale ids (up to 500)Unsave each one, or clear this site's data.
A copy of each saved scale in your LibraryUnsave it, or remove it in the Library.
Whether you dismissed the Automate this noteClear this site's data.

Searches and filters are handled in your browser and are not remembered after you leave.

What uses the network

  • On opening, the page downloads the collection from auricartisan.com.
  • Downloads are created in your browser. Download all in the design tokens dialog may fetch a small helper from auricartisan.com.
  • Share links carry the scale in the link; nothing is uploaded.

Advertising and analytics follow your consent choices. See Company and legal.
