Privacy

What the Palette Library keeps in your browser and what it fetches over the network.

What stays in your browser

WhatWhyHow to remove it
Saved palette ids (up to 500)To fill the Saved tab.Unsave each palette, or clear this site's data.
A copy of each saved palette in your LibrarySo it appears with your other saved items.Unsave it, or remove it in the Library.
Whether you dismissed the Automate this noteSo it stays hidden.Clear this site's data.

Your searches, filters and sort choices are handled in your browser and are not remembered after you leave.

What uses the network

  • When the page opens, it downloads a short description of the collection from auricartisan.com. Palettes themselves are worked out in your browser.
  • Open in Coolors opens coolors.co in a new tab and passes it the palette's five hex codes in the address. Coolors is a separate service with its own privacy policy.
  • Download all in the design tokens dialog may download a small helper from auricartisan.com to build the ZIP file.
  • Share links contain the palette's id and colours in the link itself; nothing is uploaded when you create one.

Advertising and analytics on the site follow your consent choices. See Company and legal.
