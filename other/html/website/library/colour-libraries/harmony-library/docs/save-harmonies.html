Save harmonies

Save harmonies, find them on the Saved tab, and reach them from your Library.

Save

Select Save on a card (it becomes Saved), or Save to library under Quick export on Inspect (the page confirms Saved). The Saved tab's badge goes up.

Find your saved harmonies

Open the Saved tab. Harmonies appear as cards, most recently saved first, with a note that they are Also saved to your Library workspace with image previews. With nothing saved, the tab says No saved harmonies yet.

Unsave

Select Saved on the card, or Unsave on Inspect (Removed). The harmony also leaves your Library.

In your Library

Each saved harmony is added to your Library as a palette named, for example, Harmony har_5k, described by its method, family and number of colours. Open your Library from any page with Ctrl + Shift + L. See the Library Kit docs.

Limits

  • Up to 500 harmonies are kept; saving more drops the oldest.
  • Saves are kept in this browser only. Clearing site data removes them. Noting a harmony's id is a permanent way to find it again.
