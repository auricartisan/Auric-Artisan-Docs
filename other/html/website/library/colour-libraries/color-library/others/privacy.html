Privacy

What the Color Library keeps in your browser and what it fetches over the network.

What stays in your browser

WhatWhyHow to remove it
Saved coloursTo fill the Saved tab and mark stars on tiles.Unsave each colour, or clear this site's data.
A copy of each saved colour in your LibrarySo saved colours appear alongside your other saved items.Unsave the colour, or remove it in the Library.
Whether you dismissed the Automate this noteSo it stays hidden.Clear this site's data.

The colours you type into Start from a colour you already have, your searches, filters and chip choices are worked out in your browser and are not sent anywhere. They are not remembered after you leave the page.

What uses the network

  • When the page opens, it downloads the atlas from auricartisan.com.
  • When you open a colour, it may download that colour's full record from auricartisan.com.
  • Downloads you make (JSON, SVG, PNG, CSS and so on) are created in your browser and saved straight to your device.
  • Copying writes to your device's clipboard only.

The page can show advertising, and the site's analytics run only with your consent. See Company and legal for the privacy policy and your consent choices.
