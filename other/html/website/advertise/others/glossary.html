Advertise glossary

Advertising terms used on the Auric Artisan advertise page and in the Ad Library.

TermMeaning
Ad LibraryThe public list of every ad that runs on Auric Artisan.
ads.txtA public file that declares who may sell a site's ad space.
AdsSenseAuric Artisan's own advertising system.
Auto-taggingAdding UTM parameters to the click address automatically.
Booking requestA campaign sent from the builder. It books and charges nothing until confirmed and paid.
Call to actionThe button text on an ad, such as "LEARN MORE".
CampaignOne ad in one format and zone, for one flight.
CreativeThe ad itself: the artwork people see.
CTR (click-through rate)Clicks divided by impressions.
Destination URLThe page a click on the ad opens.
Display linkThe short form of the destination shown with the ad.
eff. CPMEffective cost per thousand impressions: the price divided by estimated impressions, times 1,000.
Final URLThe destination with tracking parameters added.
FlightThe period a campaign runs, from its start date.
Flight reportThe report of delivered impressions, viewable impressions, clicks and effective CPM at the end of a campaign.
FormatThe shape of an ad: Leaderboard, Medium Rectangle, Native In-Feed Card, or Panels & Search Strip.
Frequency capThe most times one visitor can see your ad in a browsing session.
House adAn ad for Auric Artisan's own products, marked "(house)" in the Ad Library.
ImpressionOne showing of an ad.
MacroA placeholder in the click address, such as {zone}, filled in each time the ad is shown.
MRC-viewableCounted as seen: at least 50% of the ad visible for one second.
Share of voiceThe fraction of eligible ad renders in a zone reserved for your campaign.
SponsoredThe label every ad on Auric Artisan carries.
TakeoverA 100% share of voice.
UTM parametersStandard tags such as utm_source that analytics tools use to attribute visits.
ZoneThe part of the site a campaign runs on, such as the Homepage or Run of site.
