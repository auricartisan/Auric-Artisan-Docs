Advertise FAQ

Short answers to common questions about advertising on Auric Artisan.

Do I pay when I send the campaign?

No. Sending books nothing and charges nothing. Auric Artisan confirms the campaign and sends a Razorpay link for the price you were shown.

How soon can my campaign start?

The earliest start date is tomorrow. A campaign starts at 00:00 IST on its start date, after payment and creative approval. The confirmation and payment link arrive within one business day of your request.

Do I need an account?

No. You need a billing email for receipts and campaign updates.

Which currencies can I pay in?

Indian rupees or US dollars. Choose in the Budget step.

Is tax included?

The quote shows a Tax line when tax applies. The Total is the price you are asked to pay.

Can I target individual people?

No. Campaigns are placed by page context: you choose the zone. The audience groups you choose guide the creative review only; they do not identify or follow visitors.

Do my ads track visitors?

No. Ads never track visitors across sites. Third-party ad technology runs only for visitors who accept the marketing cookie category.

How is my campaign measured?

Impressions, MRC-viewable impressions (at least half the ad visible for one second), clicks and effective CPM. You receive them in a flight report at the end of the campaign.

Who sees my ad?

Visitors to the zone you chose, up to your frequency cap per session. Paid Auric Artisan subscribers browse without ads.

What is share of voice?

The fraction of eligible ad renders in your zone that are reserved for your campaign: 25%, 50% or 100%.

Can I change the format after designing the creative?

Yes. A studio design is redrawn at the new size. An uploaded file may be cropped to the new shape; upload artwork at the new size for the sharpest result.

Can Auric Artisan make the creative for me?

Ask through Contact us at https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/, which the advertise page offers for studio-made creatives, custom flights and bulk buys.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in full, at any time before the campaign start date. After it starts, refunds for the undelivered part are handled case by case. See https://auricartisan.com/legal/refund/.

Is my ad listed publicly?

Yes. Every ad that runs is listed in the Ad Library for its full run and at least 90 days after.

What gets an ad rejected?

Ads that imitate the site's interface, such as fake buttons or system dialogs, that auto-play sound, or that track visitors across sites. Every ad must be labelled Sponsored. See Design your creative.
