Pricing and booking

How the AdsSense live quote is worked out, the discounts and promo codes, currencies, what happens after you send a request, payment, refunds and the flight report.

The live quote

Beside the campaign builder, the Live estimate panel prices your campaign as you build it. Every choice updates it at once, and the price shown is the price you are asked to pay.

Your campaign shows:

LineMeaning
SummaryFormat, zone, days and share, for example Leaderboard · Run of site (everywhere) · 30 days @ 50%.
Base runThe price before discounts.
Flight discountThe saving for a longer flight. Shown only when there is one.
PromoThe promo code saving, with the code. Shown only when there is one.
TaxShown only when tax applies.
TotalWhat you pay.

Estimated delivery shows:

FigureMeaning
impressionsHow many times the ad is expected to be shown: the zone's daily views, times the days, times your share of voice.
est. uniquesRoughly how many different people see it.
est. clicksImpressions times the format's typical click-through rate.
eff. CPMThe effective price per thousand impressions, before tax.

These are estimates. Your flight report shows what was actually delivered.

What changes the price

  • Format: each has a daily rate, from ₹299 ($4) to ₹649 ($8) a day. See Formats and placements.
  • Zone: busier zones cost more a day. Run of site (everywhere) costs the most and reaches the most views.
  • Flight length: longer flights have a lower daily rate. 1 week is the standard rate; 2 weeks save 5%, 1 month saves 15% and 3 months save 28%.
  • Share of voice: 25% share, 50% share or 100% takeover of the eligible ad renders in your zone. A larger share costs more and delivers more impressions.
  • Promo code: a valid code takes a percentage off the total after the flight discount.
  • Currency: INR ₹ or USD $. Each currency has its own rates.

Send the request

The button at the foot of the quote panel reads Request campaign with the total and (pay on approval) once every step is complete. The note under it reads Nothing is charged here · we confirm the campaign, then send a Razorpay link for the price shown.

Sending the request books nothing and charges nothing.

What happens next

  1. Confirmation. Auric Artisan confirms availability and sends a Razorpay payment link to your billing email within one business day.
  2. Payment. You pay the price shown in your quote through the Razorpay link.
  3. Creative review. Before launch, your creative and destination are reviewed for policy compliance.
  4. Launch. The campaign starts at 00:00 IST on your start date, after creative approval, and runs for the flight you chose.
  5. Transparency. While it runs, the campaign is listed in the Ad Library.
  6. Flight report. At the end, you receive a report of impressions, MRC-viewable impressions, clicks and effective CPM, measured by the ad system rather than estimated.

The request carries your campaign details as text: the campaign and brand names, format, zone, dates, share of voice, goal, frequency cap, audiences, final destination address, quoted total and estimated impressions. Your artwork is not attached to the request, so keep your file, or your studio settings, ready for when Auric Artisan replies.

Refunds

From the refund policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/refund/:

  • An advertising booking is refundable in full at any time before the campaign start date.
  • Once a campaign has started, refunds for the remaining, undelivered part are handled case by case, based on the booking terms shown at purchase.
