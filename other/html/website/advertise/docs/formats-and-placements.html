Formats and placements

The four ad formats on Auric Artisan, the five zones a campaign can run in, their sizes, benchmarks and starting prices.

A campaign has one format and runs in one zone. The format is the shape of the ad; the zone is the part of the site it appears on.

In US dollars the starting prices are $5, $6, $8 and $4 a day. Select USD $ in the builder to see every price in dollars.

The click-through rates are house averages measured by the ad system. Your flight report shows your own numbers.

Leaderboard

A wide banner in the content of articles, tools and hub pages. The page calls it the workhorse unit.

Medium Rectangle

A 300×250 unit inline with content, and the page's highest-engagement display unit.

Native In-Feed Card

A sponsored card that sits inside the tool and article grids, with a native look. Card artwork is 720×600.

Panels & Search Strip

A compact 320×100 strip inside the site's kits, in Spotlight (Ctrl+K) and in search results.

The zone changes both the price and the delivery estimate. See Pricing and booking.

Goals and recommendations

The first step asks What should this campaign do? Each goal recommends a format, a zone and a share of voice. Choosing a goal applies them, and you can change any of them afterwards.

GoalDescriptionRecommends
Brand awarenessBe seen everywhere color work happens.Leaderboard, Run of site, 50% share
Site trafficNative cards that earn real clicks.Native In-Feed Card, Learn library, 50% share
Product launchOwn the homepage for launch week.Medium Rectangle, Homepage, 100% takeover

In the Format step, the recommended format carries a Recommended badge. You can change format later without losing the rest of your draft.

How ads are shown

  • Every format is clearly labelled Sponsored.
  • Ad units render only as they come near the part of the page on screen.
  • Viewability is measured to the MRC standard: at least 50% of the ad visible for one second.
  • A frequency cap limits how often one visitor sees your ad in a browsing session.
  • A slot with no ad to fill it disappears from the page.
  • Paid Auric Artisan subscribers browse without ads, so they do not see your campaign.
  • No third-party ad technology runs unless a visitor accepts the marketing cookie category.

The See a live placement section of https://auricartisan.com/advertise/ shows real units served this way.
