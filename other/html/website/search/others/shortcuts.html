Search shortcuts

Keyboard shortcuts on the Auric Artisan search page and in the Search Console.

Getting to search

Keys or actionWhat it does
Ctrl+KOpens Spotlight from any page. Choose View all results for your query to open the search page.
Right-click selected text, Search this siteSearches the site for the selected text in a new tab.

In the search field

KeysWhat it does
↓ / ↑Moves through the suggestions, or through the results when no suggestions are open. Wraps round at the ends.
Home / EndJumps to the first or last suggestion or result, once one is highlighted. Otherwise they move the text cursor.
EnterOpens the highlighted suggestion or result. With nothing highlighted, runs the search and saves it as recent.
EscapeCloses the suggestions. Pressed again, clears the query.

Outside the search field

KeysWhat it does
↓ / ↑Selects the next or previous result and shows why it ranks there.
EnterOpens the selected result.
EndGoes to the bottom of the page once every result is shown.

These keys do nothing while you are typing in another field, or when Ctrl, Alt or a command key is held.

Search Console

KeysWhat it does
↓ / → and ↑ / ←Move between sections in the side rail when it has focus.
Home / EndThe first or last section in the side rail.
EnterAdds the site in Domain or URL prefix, or submits the address in Add a page or a sitemap.
EscapeCloses the inspection panel or the remove dialog.
