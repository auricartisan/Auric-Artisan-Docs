Limits and accuracy

What each Auric Artisan search index covers, how exact the answers and console figures are, and the limits that apply.

Site search

  • What is covered. The public pages of Auric Artisan: tools, Learn articles, documentation, blog, changelog, company, legal and similar pages, plus portfolios listed in the Portfolio Index. The start screen shows the current total as pages in the index.
  • What is not covered. The contents of the libraries themselves. Individual colours, palettes, gradients and scales are not pages in the index; search inside each library for those.
  • Freshness. Your browser downloads the index when you open the page. A page published after that appears when you reload.
  • Result cap. Up to 100 results are listed for a query.
  • Words. Words under two letters and very common words are ignored. Typo tolerance applies only to words of three letters or more that match nothing as typed.
  • Newest. Sorting by date uses a date in the page's address; most pages have none.

Answer cards

  • Contrast ratios use the WCAG 2.1 relative luminance formula on sRGB colours. They match the standard reference values: 21:1 for white on black, and 4.54:1 for #767676 on white.
  • Grades are decided on the exact ratio. The ratio shown is rounded to two decimals, so a pair shown as 4.50:1 can still be just under the threshold.
  • OKLCH values are converted from sRGB and rounded: lightness to one decimal of a percent, chroma to three decimals, hue to one decimal of a degree.
  • The cards check contrast only. They do not judge font size, weight or the rest of WCAG. For a full check, use the Contrast Checker or the URL Analyzer.

Web search

  • The web index is Auric Artisan's own crawl, very much smaller than Google's or Bing's.
  • The crawler reads HTML only and does not run JavaScript.
  • Counts marked about are estimates.
  • When the index has nothing, the live fallback searches a limited number of pages and takes longer.

Search Console

  • Impressions, clicks and positions come only from Auric Artisan's own web search.
  • The period can be 7, 28 or 90 days, compared with the same length of time before it.
  • Pages shows the most recently crawled addresses on a large site, and says so.
  • Links reports are not collected yet; only the site's rank is shown.
  • Crawl speed per site follows its Crawl-delay, between 1 and 60 seconds, with 1 second when none is set.
  • There is no limit on the number of sites per account.

Sponsored units

The search page may show units clearly labelled Sponsored. They are not search results and are not ranked with them.
