Search and the Search Console

What the Auric Artisan search page and the Search Console are, who they are for, and how to run your first search.

The search page at https://auricartisan.com/search/ finds tools, Learn articles, documentation, blog posts, changelog entries, portfolios and every other page on Auric Artisan. The search runs in your browser: the page downloads a small index of the site once, and everything you type is matched on your device. Results appear as you type, sorted by relevance, and you can narrow them to one section, sort them, and use a few simple operators such as "a phrase", -word and type:learn.

Because this is a colour site, the search also answers colour questions directly. Type a hex code such as #d3af37 and an answer card shows the colour with its contrast against black and white. Type two hex codes and it judges them as a text and background pair against WCAG 2.1. Type the name of a common tool, such as contrast, and a card takes you straight to it.

The globe button switches to a second index: Auric Artisan's own crawl of the web, an independent index much smaller than the big search engines. Site owners can put their own site into that index, and see how it is found, with the free Search Console at https://auricartisan.com/search/console/.

Who it is for

  • Anyone looking for a tool, article or guide on Auric Artisan.
  • Designers and developers who want a quick colour or contrast answer without opening a tool.
  • People searching the wider web for colour and design resources.
  • Site owners who want their site in Auric Artisan's web index and want to know why a page is or is not included.

Where to find it

  • The search page: https://auricartisan.com/search/ (also in the site menu under Search).
  • From anywhere on the site: open Spotlight with Ctrl+K, type, and choose View all results for your query.
  • Select text on any page, right-click and choose Search this site.
  • The Search Console: https://auricartisan.com/search/console/ (in the site menu under Search, and linked from the bottom of the search page as Put your site in it).
  • Searching needs no account. The Search Console needs an Auric Artisan account.

Quick start

  1. Open https://auricartisan.com/search/.
  2. Type at least two characters, for example oklch gradient. Results appear as you type.
  3. Select a section tab, such as Learn, to see only that kind of page.
  4. Hover a result, or press the down arrow, to see why it ranked where it did.
  5. Press Enter or select a result to open it.
  6. Type a hex code such as #1d2a3e to see the colour and its contrast.
  7. To search the web instead, select the globe button beside the field and press Enter.

What you can do

  • Search every page on the site, on your device, with typo correction and related-word matching.
  • Narrow results by section: All, Tools, Learn, Docs, Blog, Portfolios or Pages.
  • Sort by Relevance, Newest or A–Z.
  • Use phrases, exclusions and field filters such as tag: and title:.
  • Get instant colour, contrast-pair and tool answers.
  • See the score and matched fields behind every result.
  • Search Auric Artisan's own web index, with suggestions, spelling correction and paging.
  • In the Search Console, prove you own a site, request crawls, and see coverage, searches, clicks and positions for it.
