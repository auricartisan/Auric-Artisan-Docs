Settings panel

What the Settings panel is, how to open it, and what you can change with it on every page of Auric Artisan.

The Settings panel, titled Your Settings, is a floating panel that changes how the whole site looks and behaves for you. It holds your accent colour, theme, language and text size; comfort options such as reading mode, reduced motion and eye-rest reminders; which info bars appear; how clocks show the time; how tooltips behave; ad preferences; and a list of keyboard shortcuts.

Every change applies at once, on the page you are on and on every page you open afterwards. The panel keeps your choices in this browser, so they work without an account. If you have the site open in several tabs, a change in one tab is picked up by the others. You can also save all your settings to a file and load them again on another browser or device.

The panel is modeless: it floats over the page without dimming it, so you can keep reading and clicking the page while it is open. You can drag it, widen it, collapse it to its title bar and close it.

Who it is for

  • Everyone who uses the site: the defaults suit most people, and every choice can be undone.
  • People who need larger text, stronger contrast, underlined links or less motion.
  • People who prefer the site in Hindi, a 24-hour clock or a particular time zone.
  • Anyone who wants to move their setup to another browser.

Where to find it

On any page of https://auricartisan.com:

  • press Ctrl + , (or Cmd + , on a Mac);
  • open the page utilities bar at the bottom-left of the screen and select Settings;
  • right-click the page and choose Site, then Settings (or Keyboard shortcuts to open the Shortcuts section);
  • press Ctrl + K, type > and choose Open settings;
  • right-click the clock strip and choose All settings, which opens the Clock section.

Quick start

  1. Press Ctrl + , to open Your Settings.
  2. In Appearance, select an accent swatch, for example Emerald. The whole site recolours.
  3. Under Theme, select Light, Dark or System.
  4. Under Text size, select one of the four A buttons to make type smaller or larger across the site.
  5. Select Comfort in the section list and switch on anything you need, such as Reduce motion or Underline links.
  6. Select Export at the bottom of the panel to keep a copy of your settings as auric-settings.json.
  7. Press Esc or select the close button to put the panel away. Your choices stay in place on every page.

What you can do

  • Recolour the site with 13 preset accents, any custom colour, or a shade from the live accent ramp, and keep up to 18 saved colours.
  • Check that your accent is readable with a live WCAG contrast readout.
  • Choose light, dark or system theme, English or Hindi, and one of four text sizes.
  • Turn on reading mode, reduced motion, reduced transparency, boosted contrast and underlined links; turn the custom cursor off.
  • Schedule eye-rest reminders and set up the idle screen saver.
  • Choose which info bars, notices and demo chips appear, and whether the clock strip is shown.
  • Set 12 or 24-hour time, seconds, date display, time zone and date format.
  • Turn tooltips off, or make them more or less detailed.
  • Adjust ad preferences (a paid perk).
  • Restore one section, or everything, to its defaults; export and import your settings as a file.
