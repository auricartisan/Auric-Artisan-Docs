Highlights and clock

Choose which info bars, notices and demo chips appear, and set how the site's clocks show the time and date.

Highlights: bars, notices and chips

The Highlights section decides which extra strips and labels appear around page content. The bars themselves are described in Info bars and notices.

SwitchWhat it showsDefault
Live demo chipsThe highlighted "live / demo" chips above demo sectionsOn
Welcome barThe rotating welcome bar above the pageHomepage only
Announcement barThe site announcements barHomepage only
Quick links barThe quick-links bar (documentation, changelog, timeline, blog, learn)Homepage only
Clock stripA live clock, date and time zone above every pageOff
Hero noticeThe highlighted notice banner on the homepage heroOn

How the "homepage only" switches behave

The Welcome, Announcement and Quick links bars start in a third state: shown on the homepage only. In that state the switch appears on.

  • Switch it off to hide that bar everywhere, including the homepage.
  • Switch it on again to show that bar above every page, not only the homepage.
  • To get back to "homepage only", select Restore section defaults.

The Quick links bar also shows by default at the foot of the Collections page, where it is part of the layout.

Hide a bar or chip from the page itself

Each bar, the hero notice and each demo chip has its own ✕ button. Selecting it has the same effect as switching the matching setting off, so the switch in Highlights moves to off as well. For demo chips, one ✕ hides every demo chip on the site.

Note: The tooltip on a bar's ✕ says you can turn it back on in "Settings → Comfort". The switches are in the Highlights section.

Restore the section

Restore section defaults returns all six switches to the defaults in the table above. A message confirms "Highlights restored to defaults".

Clock: time and date display

The Clock section controls the live clock strip at the top of pages (when Clock strip is on in Highlights). The 24-hour choice is also used by the clock on the screen saver.

At the top, Live preview (your clock right now) shows the time and date exactly as your settings will draw them, updating every second.

ControlWhat it doesValuesDefault
24-hour timeShows clocks in 24-hour format instead of AM / PMOn, offOff
Show secondsIncludes seconds on the live clockOn, offOn
Show dateShows the calendar date next to the clockOn, offOn
TimezoneThe time zone the clock shows (defaults to your location)Auto-detect, or any time zone your browser listsAuto-detect
Date formatHow the date is writtenLong, DD/MM/YYYY, MM/DD/YYYYLong

The Auto-detect entry names the zone your device reports, for example "Auto-detect (Asia/Kolkata)". Long writes the weekday, month, day and year in words, for example "Thursday, September 24, 2026".

Set a fixed time zone

  1. Open the panel and select Clock.
  2. Open the Timezone list and choose a zone, for example Europe/London.
  3. The preview switches to that zone at once.

To follow your device again, choose Auto-detect at the top of the list.

Quicker clock controls

Right-click the clock strip itself for a small Clock settings menu with a few extra choices (an ISO date format, and switches for the weekday and the zone label). It is described in Info bars and notices. Its All settings link opens this Clock section.

Restore the section

Restore section defaults returns all five clock settings to their defaults. A message confirms "Clock restored to defaults".
