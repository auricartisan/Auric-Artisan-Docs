Get started with the Settings panel

Open the Settings panel, learn its parts, and make your first change.

Open the panel

Use whichever way is closest:

Where you areWhat to do
Anywhere, with a keyboardPress Ctrl + , (Cmd + , on a Mac). Press it again to close the panel.
Anywhere, with a mouse or touchSelect the page utilities button at the bottom-left of the screen, then Settings.
Right-clicking the pageChoose Site, then Settings. Choose Keyboard shortcuts to open the Shortcuts section directly.
In Spotlight searchPress Ctrl + K, type >, and choose Open settings.
On the clock stripRight-click the strip and select All settings. The panel opens on the Clock section.
On an info barRight-click the bar and select Settings. The panel opens on the Comfort section.

The page utilities bar is the launcher row that also holds the kits. It is described in Header and navigation and, in more depth, in Kits.

A tour of the panel

The panel has four parts.

  1. The title bar. It reads Your Settings. Drag it to move the panel; the position is remembered. On the right are four buttons:
    • Focus mode — the pin-style button shared with the kits. Its tooltip reads "Keep Settings open through navigation and while opening other kits."
    • Resize — switches between the normal size and a wider size (up to 980 pixels, or the width of your window).
    • Minimize — collapses the panel to its title bar. Select the title bar, or the same button (now Restore), to bring it back.
    • Close settings — the X.
  2. The section list. Appearance, Comfort, Highlights, Clock, Tooltips, Ads and Shortcuts. Each shows how many controls it holds. With a section selected, press the Left and Right arrow keys to move between sections.
  3. The section body. The controls for the selected section. Most sections end with Restore section defaults.
  4. The footer. Export and Import on the left; Reset all and a reminder of the Ctrl + , shortcut on the right.

The panel does not dim or block the page. You can scroll and click the page while it is open.

Note: The Ads section appears only while the site is showing ads. If ads are switched off site-wide, the section is not shown at all.

Make your first change

  1. Open the panel with Ctrl + ,.
  2. In Appearance, look at the Preview card at the top. It shows a heading, an accent chip, a link, a line of text and a button in your current look.
  3. Under Accent color, select Sapphire. The preview, the panel and the whole page change to blue at once.
  4. Under Text size, select the largest A (X-Large). The text across the site grows.
  5. Select Comfort in the section list and switch on Underline links.
  6. Close the panel with Esc.

Result: every page you open now uses the sapphire accent, extra-large text and underlined links in articles. To undo the three changes, open the panel again and use Restore section defaults in Appearance and in Comfort, or select Reset all.

Close the panel

  • Press Esc. If a drop-down list inside the panel is open, the first Esc closes the list and the second closes the panel.
  • Press Ctrl + , again.
  • Select the X in the title bar.

When the panel closes, keyboard focus returns to whatever you used to open it.
