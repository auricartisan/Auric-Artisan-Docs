Navigation dock glossary

Terms used in the navigation dock guides.

TermMeaning
DockThe floating rail of navigation buttons on the edge of the window.
CollapsedThe dock's resting state: a thin line at the edge (desktop) or a small round button (phone).
GripThe six-dot handle at the top of the dock, used to drag it.
Page outlineThe On this page panel listing the page's headings.
Progress ringThe ring around Scroll to top that fills as you scroll down the page.
Share sheetYour device's own panel for sending a link to another app or person.
Page stateDetails the site keeps in the address after #p=, such as your scroll position.
