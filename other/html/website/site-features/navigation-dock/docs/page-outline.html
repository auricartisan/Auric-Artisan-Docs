Jump around with the page outline

Use the dock's page outline to see the page's sections, find where you are, and jump to any heading.

The Page outline button opens a panel titled On this page beside the dock. It lists the headings of the page you are on and highlights the one you are reading.

Open the outline and jump to a section

  1. Point at the dock and select Page outline (the list icon).
  2. The On this page panel opens. Main headings are listed normally; sub-headings are indented and slightly smaller.
  3. The heading of the section you are reading is highlighted in the accent colour. As you scroll, the highlight moves with you.
  4. Select a heading.

Result: the page scrolls smoothly to that heading, leaving room for the site header above it, and the panel closes.

Close the outline

Select Page outline again, press Esc, or click anywhere outside the panel and the dock.

What the outline lists

  • The main headings and their sub-headings (heading levels 1, 2 and 3) in the page's main content.
  • Headings in the header, footer and navigation areas are left out, as are hidden headings and headings longer than 90 characters.
  • The list is rebuilt every time you open it, so headings that appear later (for example in a tool's results) are included.
  • On a page without headings, the panel reads "No sections on this page".

Other outlines on the site

Long company, pricing, legal and security pages also have a section rail pinned under the header. It is described in Guide and page outline. The page guide (?) lists the page's tour stops. See Guide and page outline.
