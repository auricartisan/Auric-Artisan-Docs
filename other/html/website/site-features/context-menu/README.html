Right-click menu

What the site's own right-click menu does, how it adapts to what you clicked, and how to switch back to your browser's menu.

When you right-click a page on auricartisan.com, the site opens its own menu instead of your browser's. It is built around what you clicked: the top of the menu names the thing under your pointer (the page, a link, an image, a selection of text or a colour) and its own actions come first. Everything else is grouped into a few rows that open submenus, so the whole menu fits on one screen.

A quick bar of one-click buttons sits across the top. It starts with Back, Forward, Reload, Home and Copy URL, and you can pin any action to it with the star on that action's row. A filter field lets you type part of an action's name to find it anywhere in the menu, submenus included. A gear button opens the menu's own preferences: a longer "Everything" layout, a compact row height, or handing right-click back to your browser for good.

Your browser's menu is always one keystroke away: hold Shift while you right-click.

Who it is for

  • Anyone who wants quick actions on the thing they are looking at: copy a colour as HEX, RGB, HSL or OKLCH; copy a link as Markdown; copy an image's palette; search the web for a selection.
  • Keyboard users: the menu can be driven entirely from the keyboard and searched by typing.
  • People who prefer their browser's menu: one setting makes the browser's menu the default.

Where to find it

On any page of https://auricartisan.com:

  • right-click anywhere that is not a text field;
  • or rest your pointer on the thin gold line at the middle of the right edge of the window for a moment;
  • or press the menu key on your keyboard (Shift + F10 on many keyboards), which your browser treats as a right-click.

Quick start

  1. Right-click an empty part of any page. The heading shows Page and the page's title.
  2. Select Copy URL in the quick bar. A message confirms "Page URL copied".
  3. Select some text on the page and right-click on it. The heading now reads Selection with a word and character count.
  4. Choose Search the web, then DuckDuckGo. A new tab opens with your selection searched.
  5. Right-click again and start typing dark. The list filters to Switch theme. Press Enter to run it.
  6. Right-click again, find Split view under View, and select the star on its row. Split view now appears in the quick bar.
  7. To use your browser's menu once, hold Shift and right-click.

What you can do

  • Copy the page URL, title or a Markdown link; share or print the page; open the URL Analyzer on it.
  • Copy, search, translate or read aloud selected text.
  • Open, copy or share links, including as Markdown.
  • Open, copy, save and extract the palette of images.
  • Copy any colour on the site in four formats and send it to the colour tools.
  • Reach View actions (scroll, reading mode, split view, full screen, theme) and Site actions (home, search, settings, shortcuts, website update).
  • Pin up to six actions to the quick bar.
  • Choose a Focused or Everything layout and a Standard or Compact row height.
  • Make the browser's own menu the default.
