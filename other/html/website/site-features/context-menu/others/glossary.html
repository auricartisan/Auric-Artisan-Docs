Right-click menu glossary

Terms used by the right-click menu and its guides.

TermMeaning
ContextThe thing you right-clicked: a page, link, image, selection or colour.
Context menuA menu that opens on right-click and offers actions for what is under the pointer.
Quick barThe row of one-click buttons at the top of the menu. Holds up to six pinned actions.
PinTo add an action to the quick bar with the star on its row.
GroupA set of related actions (Page, Colour tools, View, Site) shown as one row that opens a submenu.
SubmenuThe small panel that opens beside a group row, listing that group's actions.
FilterThe field that narrows the menu to actions whose names match what you type.
Focused layoutThe default layout: your context's actions, then one row per group.
Everything layoutA layout that lists every group's actions in full, with no submenus.
Markdown linkText written as [label](address), which many editors turn into a link.
HEX, RGB, HSLCommon ways of writing a colour: a hex code, red-green-blue values, or hue-saturation-lightness.
OKLCHA way of writing colour as lightness, chroma and hue that follows human perception closely.
Alt textThe text alternative of an image, read by screen readers and shown when the image cannot load.
Share sheetYour device's own panel for sending a link to an app or contact.
