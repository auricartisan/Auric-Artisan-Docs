Menu preferences and turning it off

Change the right-click menu's layout and row height, and make your browser's own menu the default.

The gear button at the bottom right of the menu, Customise this menu, replaces the action list with the menu's preferences. Every change is saved as soon as you make it; the footer confirms "Saved". Select Done, the gear again, or press Esc to return to the actions. Typing in the filter field also returns to the actions.

Quick bar

Shows your pinned actions as chips, each with an X to unpin it, and a Reset chip that restores the default five. See Quick bar and pinning.

Layout

OptionDescription on screenWhat it looks like

Choose Everything if you prefer to see all actions at once and do not mind a longer menu.

Right-click on this site

This choice decides which menu a plain right-click opens.

OptionDescription on screen
This menu (default)Hold Shift for the browser’s menu
The browser’s menuHold Shift for this one

Make your browser's menu the default

  1. Right-click the page and select the gear.
  2. Under Right-click on this site, select The browser’s menu.
  3. The footer confirms "Right-click now opens the browser’s menu".

From now on a plain right-click opens your browser's menu everywhere on the site. To reach the site menu, hold Shift while you right-click. You can also rest your pointer on the thin line at the right edge of the window.

Switch the site menu back on

  1. Hold Shift and right-click the page. The site menu opens.
  2. Select the gear, then This menu under Right-click on this site.

One-off use of the browser's menu

Without changing any preference:

  • hold Shift while you right-click; or
  • choose Browser’s own menu at the foot of the site menu, then right-click again.

Right-clicking in a text field, drop-down or editable area always opens the browser's menu.

Where preferences are kept

Your layout, row height, right-click choice and quick-bar pins are kept in this browser and apply on every page of the site. They are not part of the Settings panel's export file.
