Other right-click menus on the site

The smaller right-click menus that some parts of the site show instead of the main menu.

A few parts of the site have a small right-click menu of their own, with actions for that one thing. Right-clicking them opens that menu instead of the main one. They share a look: a heading, labelled groups of buttons, and sometimes a Reset button and an All settings link.

All of these close with Esc, a click elsewhere, a scroll or a window resize.

Clock strip

Right-click the clock strip at the top of a page (when it is switched on) to open Clock settings: a live preview, Format (12h, 24h), Date (Long, D/M/Y, M/D/Y, ISO), Show switches (Seconds, Weekday, Zone), a searchable Time zone list, Reset and All settings →. Details are in Info bars and notices › Clock strip.

Info bars

Right-click the welcome, announcement or quick-links bar to open a menu headed with the bar's name ("Welcome messages", "Site announcements" or "Site tools"). Under This bar:

ButtonWhat it does
OpenGoes to the link you right-clicked, or the bar's first link
Copy linkCopies that link's address
Hide barHides this bar everywhere (same as its ✕)
SettingsOpens the Settings panel on the Comfort section

Cards on browsing pages

On pages with a grid of cards (the homepage, Collections, Blog, Changelog, Documentation and Learn), right-clicking a card opens a Card settings menu headed with the card's title:

  • Card: Pin (or Unpin), Open, New tab, Copy link;
  • View: Grid or List;
  • Density: Cozy or Compact;
  • Columns: Auto, 2, 3 or 4;
  • Sort: the sort orders the page offers;
  • System: Theme and Reading switches;
  • Reset and All settings →.

This menu is part of the card libraries and is described fully in Collections › Card menu.

Footer contact card

Right-click the "Start a conversation" card in the footer to open a menu headed Start a conversation. Under Contact:

ButtonWhat it does
Email usOpens your email app with a message to the studio address
Copy emailCopies the email address (the button briefly reads "Copied")
Copy linkCopies the address of the page you are on
LEACBOpens the studio's portfolio site in a new tab

Inside the Settings panel

Right-clicking inside the Settings panel opens a menu with Turn on or Turn off for the switch you clicked, and Export, Import, Reset section and Reset all. See Settings panel › Back up, restore and reset.

Inside the Library Kit

The Library Kit's panel has its own right-click menu for your saved items. See Kits.

Places that keep the browser's menu

The main menu never opens, and your browser's menu appears instead, in:

  • text fields, text areas, drop-down lists and other editable areas;
  • the accessibility checklist's evidence drawing tool;
  • any spot a page has marked as needing the browser's own menu.
