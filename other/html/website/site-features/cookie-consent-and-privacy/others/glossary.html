Cookie consent glossary

Terms used in the cookie consent guides.

TermMeaning
CookieA small piece of data a website stores in your browser and receives back on later visits.
Local storageBrowser storage that a website's pages can read, but which is not sent to the website automatically.
First-partySet by the website you are visiting (here, auricartisan.com), not by another company.
ConsentYour recorded choice about which optional categories the site may use.
EssentialNeeded for the site to work securely; cannot be turned off.
FunctionalRemembers your preferences.
AnalyticsMeasures how the site is used, in aggregate or pseudonymously.
MarketingCampaign attribution and advertising measurement; not currently used.
Do Not Track, Global Privacy ControlBrowser settings that ask websites not to track you.
PseudonymousLinked to a random identifier rather than to your name or account.
