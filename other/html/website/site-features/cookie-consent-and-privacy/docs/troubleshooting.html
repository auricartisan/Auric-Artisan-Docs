Troubleshooting cookie consent

Fixes for common problems with the cookie notice and preferences.

The consent bar keeps coming back

Why: your choice is kept in this browser's storage. It is lost if the browser clears site data on exit, if you use a private window, or if storage is blocked for the site.

What to do: allow auricartisan.com to keep site data, or accept that a private window asks each time.

I cannot find the cookie settings in the Settings panel

Why: the consent bar mentions Settings, but the controls are on the Cookie Policy page.

What to do: open https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/#cookies-preferences.

I pressed Accept All or Reject All on the Cookie Policy page and nothing changed

Why: on that page, those buttons only set the switches.

What to do: select Save Preferences.

My settings survived Reject All

Why: your preferences (theme, accent, language and the Settings panel's choices) are kept in your browser's local storage so that the features you use keep working. Rejecting optional cookies does not reset them.

What to do: use Reset all in the Settings panel, or clear the site's data in your browser, to remove them.

The bar does not appear on the sign-in page

Why: the notice is not shown over sign-in and verification pages. It appears on the next ordinary page.
