Get started with the accessibility aids

Set up the site's text size, contrast, motion, transparency, link and pointer options for your needs.

All of these apply to every page at once and are remembered in this browser.

Make text larger

  • Press Alt + + (one step larger) or Alt + - (one step smaller). Alt + 0 returns to the default.
  • Or open Settings › Appearance › Text size and choose Small (90%), Default, Large (112%) or X-Large (125%).

This scales the site's text. Your browser's own zoom (Ctrl + +) also works and scales everything, including layout.

Stronger contrast

Settings › Comfort › Boost contrast draws secondary text in the stronger text colour and makes borders darker, in both themes. You can also choose the theme that suits you in Appearance › Theme.

Less motion

  • Settings › Comfort › Reduce motion (or Alt + M) makes animations and transitions finish instantly and turns smooth scrolling into jumps.
  • If your device's reduced-motion setting is on, the site also hides the custom cursor, stops the eye-rest horizon from animating, shortens the first-visit welcome, and swaps info-bar messages without fading.

Clearer surfaces

Settings › Comfort › Reduce transparency replaces blurred, see-through panels with solid ones.

Easier-to-spot links

Settings › Comfort › Underline links always underlines links in articles and other text.

Your system pointer

Settings › Comfort › Custom cursor off restores your own pointer, including any large or high-contrast pointer you set in your operating system.

Fewer distractions

  • Reading mode (Alt + R) for long pages. See Reading mode.
  • Hide the info bars, hero notice and demo chips with their ✕ or in Settings › Highlights.
  • Turn off tooltips, or make them shorter, in Settings › Tooltips.

Language

The whole interface is available in Hindi. See Language.

Keep your setup

Export your settings from the Settings panel (Export) and import them on another browser. See Settings panel › Back up, restore and reset.
