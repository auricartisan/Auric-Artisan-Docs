Guide privacy

What the guide remembers.

  • The guide keeps, in this browser, whether you have opened it and how far you got in each page's tour. This is what lets it offer Pick up where you left off.
  • Nothing about your use of the guide or tours is sent to Auric Artisan.
  • The section rail stores nothing.
