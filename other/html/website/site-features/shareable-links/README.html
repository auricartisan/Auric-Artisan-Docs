Shareable links

What the address bar remembers about the page you are on, how to share an exact view, and the other link options the site understands.

As you use a page on auricartisan.com, the site quietly keeps the page's state in the address bar, after #p=: how far you have scrolled, what you have typed or chosen in the page's fields, which tabs and toggles are selected, which panels are open, and your appearance settings where they differ from the defaults. Reload the page and it comes back as you left it. Copy the address and send it, and the other person sees the same view.

The site also understands a few other additions to an address: ?lang=hi opens a page in Hindi, ?color= opens a colour inspector over any page, and #split= restores a split view.

Who it is for

  • Anyone who wants a reload to keep their place and their inputs.
  • People sharing a specific tool setup, a scroll position in a long article or a filled-in form.
  • Teachers and support staff pointing someone to an exact view.

Where to find it

It works on its own on every page. To copy the current address, use your browser's address bar, Copy link in the navigation dock, or Copy URL in the right-click menu.

Quick start

  1. Open a tool, for example https://auricartisan.com/tool/basic-tools/, and change a few inputs.
  2. Scroll down a little. After a moment the address gains a #p= part.
  3. Reload the page. Your inputs and scroll position come back.
  4. Point at the navigation dock and select Copy link.
  5. Paste the link into a message. Whoever opens it sees the same inputs and position.

What you can do

  • Keep your place and inputs through a reload.
  • Share an exact view of a page.
  • Open any page in Hindi or English with ?lang=.
  • Inspect any colour over any page with ?color=.
  • Share a split view with #split=.
