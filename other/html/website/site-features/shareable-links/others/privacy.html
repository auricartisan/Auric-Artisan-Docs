Shareable links privacy

What a copied page link can reveal, and how to share one safely.

  • Stays in the address. The page state is kept only in the address bar of your browser tab. It is not stored elsewhere and, because it follows #, it is not sent to Auric Artisan when the page loads.
  • Travels with the link. When you copy and share the address, the state goes with it: what you typed into the page's fields (except passwords and search boxes), your selections, open panels, your scroll position, and your appearance settings that differ from the defaults.
  • Readable by anyone with the link. The state is written compactly, not encrypted.
  • Browser history. The latest state of each page can appear in your browser history and bookmarks, like any address.

To share safely, share a clean link: scroll to the top and undo changes, or delete everything from #p= onwards.
