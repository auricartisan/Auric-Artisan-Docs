Shareable links glossary

Terms used in the shareable links guides.

TermMeaning
Address (URL)The web address shown in the browser's address bar.
FragmentThe part of an address after #. Browsers keep it on your device and do not send it to the site.
Query parameterA part of an address after ?, such as ?lang=hi, which the page reads.
Page stateThe scroll position, inputs, selections and open panels of a page.
#p=The fragment in which the site keeps a page's state.
RestorePutting a page back into the state its address describes.
Clean linkAn address without the #p= part.
