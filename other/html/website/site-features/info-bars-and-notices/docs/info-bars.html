The info bars

Use the welcome, announcement and quick-links bars, pause them, open their links and hide them.

The welcome bar

A single-line bar that shows one message at a time and cross-fades to the next every six seconds. Its seven messages introduce the platform: colour science, colour-space tooling, accessibility, vision simulation, palettes, research tools such as Ishihara plates, and what comes next. Each message links to the welcome article on the blog. Hover a message for a short tooltip.

The announcement bar

Also one message at a time, every six seconds. It carries the latest platform news, for example:

  • "New library updates and workflow improvements are in progress — read the roadmap." (to the roadmap article)
  • "Every tool works offline — install Auric Artisan as an app from the Download page." (to the Download page)
  • "Early access: accounts, pricing, and sync are rolling out in stages." (to the roadmap article)

Highlighted messages are featured news.

The quick-links bar

A row of cards that scrolls continuously across the page:

Pausing and motion

  • Point at the welcome or announcement bar to pause it on the current message. It resumes when you move away.
  • Rotation also pauses while the tab is hidden.
  • With Reduce motion on (Settings › Comfort) or your device's reduced-motion setting, messages change instantly instead of fading.

Hide a bar

Select the ✕ at the end of the bar (Hide this bar (re-enable in Settings)). The bar is hidden on every page, and its switch in Settings › Highlights turns off.

Note: The tooltip on the ✕ says "Turn it back on in Settings → Comfort". The switches are in the Highlights section.

The bar's right-click menu

Right-click a bar, or a message in it, to open a small menu headed with the bar's name ("Welcome messages", "Site announcements" or "Site tools"). Under This bar:

ButtonWhat it does
OpenGoes to the message you right-clicked, or the bar's first link
Copy linkCopies that link's address
Hide barHides this bar everywhere
SettingsOpens the Settings panel (on the Comfort section)

Esc, a click elsewhere, a scroll or resizing the window closes the menu.
