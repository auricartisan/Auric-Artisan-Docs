Eye rest glossary

Terms used in the eye rest guides.

TermMeaning
Reminder intervalHow long a page is open before a reminder appears: 20, 35, 45 or 60 minutes.
RestThe few seconds (5 to 30) during which the screen dims and invites you to look away.
Reminder cardThe small card that appears when the interval ends: "Time to look away".
HorizonThe line across the rest screen that shrinks to show how much of the rest is left.
DawnThe brightening of the whole screen that marks the end of a rest.
SessionThe life of a browser tab; Not this session lasts until the tab is closed.
20-20-20A common screen habit: every 20 minutes, look about 20 feet away for about 20 seconds.
