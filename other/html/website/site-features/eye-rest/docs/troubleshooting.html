Troubleshooting eye rest

Fixes for common eye-rest problems.

I switched reminders on but none appear

Possible causes and fixes:

  • The countdown restarts on every page load. If you move between pages often, a 20-minute reminder may never be reached. Stay on one page, or choose a shorter interval.
  • You selected Not this session earlier in this tab. Open the site in a new tab.
  • You selected Turn these off earlier. Switch Eye-rest reminders off and on again in Settings › Comfort.
  • A few pages do not include the eye-rest reminder. Try another page.

Reminders came back after I turned them off

Why: Turn these off on the card does not change the switch in Settings. Changing any setting while that switch shows on brings the reminders back.

What to do: switch off Eye-rest reminders in Settings › Comfort.

The rest always lasts 30 seconds

Why: the Rest for choice lasts only until you move to another page or reload.

What to do: choose it again under Change on the card.

The horizon line does not move

Why: your device's reduced-motion setting is on, so the line stays still. The countdown number still shows the time left, and the screen still brightens at the end.

The screen went white at the end of a rest

That is the end signal. It lasts about 2.6 seconds, then the page returns.
