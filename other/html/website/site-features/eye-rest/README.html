Eye rest

Optional reminders that invite you to look away from the screen for a few seconds, on a schedule you choose.

Eye rest is an optional reminder to look away from the screen. When it is on, a small card appears after a set time on a page, "Time to look away", and invites you to rest your eyes for a few seconds. Select Rest now and the screen dims to a quiet horizon with one instruction: look at something about twenty feet away. A line on the horizon shrinks as the seconds pass, and when the rest is over the whole screen brightens, so you can tell it has finished without looking back at it.

Reminders are off by default. You choose how often they come (every 20, 35, 45 or 60 minutes) and how long each rest lasts (5 to 30 seconds). Every reminder can be put off with Later, silenced for the session, or turned off for good.

Who it is for

People who spend long stretches looking at colours, contrast checks or code on the site and want a gentle prompt to look away now and then.

Where to find it

Settings (Ctrl + ,) › Comfort › Eye-rest reminders.

Quick start

  1. Press Ctrl + , and select Comfort.
  2. Switch on Eye-rest reminders.
  3. Under Reminder interval, select 20m.
  4. Keep working on a page. After 20 minutes the card "Time to look away" appears.
  5. Select Rest now. The screen dims; look at something far away until it brightens.
  6. The page returns and the next reminder is scheduled.

What you can do

  • Choose how often reminders come and how long each rest lasts.
  • Start a rest straight from the reminder, or put it off with Later.
  • End a rest early.
  • Silence reminders for the rest of the session, or turn them off.
