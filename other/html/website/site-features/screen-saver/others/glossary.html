Screen saver glossary

Terms used in the screen saver guides.

TermMeaning
IdleNo mouse, keyboard, scroll, wheel or touch activity on the page.
Colour atlasThe site's collection of 8,192 named colours with mood and movement notes.
OKLCHA way of writing colour as lightness (L), chroma (C) and hue (H) that follows human perception.
Lightness stepOne of the seven bands, each a fixed OKLCH lightness from 0.22 to 0.94.
ChromaHow colourful a colour is, from grey (0) upwards.
Contrast on blackThe WCAG contrast ratio between the colour and pure black.
