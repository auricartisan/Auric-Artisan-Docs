Screen saver FAQ

Short answers about the screen saver.

Does the screen saver stop my computer from sleeping?

No. It is part of the web page. Your computer's own power and sleep settings still apply.

Can I choose which colours it shows?

No. It picks a random starting colour from the atlas and moves on every twelve seconds.

Where do the colours come from?

From the site's colour atlas of 8,192 named colours, each with a mood and an art movement associated with it.

Does it use much power?

It is drawn with plain page elements rather than animation-heavy graphics, and it pauses while its tab is hidden.

Is the screen saver the same as eye rest?

No. Eye rest reminds you to look away while you are working; the screen saver appears when you have stopped. See Eye rest.
