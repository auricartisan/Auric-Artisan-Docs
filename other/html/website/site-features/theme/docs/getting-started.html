Get started with themes

Understand the light, dark and system theme modes and choose one.

The three modes

ModeWhat you see
LightLight pages with dark text, always
DarkDark pages with light text, always
System (Auto in the drawer)Light or dark to match your device's setting, changing when it changes

System is the default for a new visitor.

Choose a mode

  1. Press Ctrl + , to open Settings. Appearance is selected.
  2. Under Theme, select Light, Dark or System.

The page changes at once, and every other page and open tab follows.

How System mode follows your device

With System selected, the site asks your browser whether your device is set to dark mode, and uses dark if it is. When the device switches (for example on a schedule at sunset), the site switches too, without a reload.

Where to change your device's setting:

  • Windows: Settings › Personalisation › Colours › Choose your mode.
  • macOS: System Settings › Appearance.
  • Android: Settings › Display › Dark theme.
  • iOS and iPadOS: Settings › Display & Brightness.

What the header button does to System mode

The header's sun and moon button always switches to the opposite of what you see now, and makes that choice fixed. If you were on System and the page was dark, selecting the sun sets the theme to Light, and the Settings panel moves from System to Light. To follow your device again, choose System in Settings or Auto in the drawer.

What changes with the theme

  • The page background, text, borders and panels.
  • The default gold accent is drawn slightly darker in the light theme to stay readable. A custom accent is not changed.

Tool results, images and colour swatches are not recoloured.
