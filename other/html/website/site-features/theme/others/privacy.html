Theme privacy

What is stored about your theme choice.

  • Your theme mode is kept in this browser's local storage for auricartisan.com, along with the last light or dark choice made with a toggle. The cookie policy lists this under Functional storage.
  • Each kit's own theme choice is kept the same way.
  • Nothing about your theme is sent to Auric Artisan when you change it.
  • A page link copied from the site can include your theme mode (see Shareable links).
