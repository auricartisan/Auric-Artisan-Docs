Theme glossary

Terms used in the theme guides.

TermMeaning
ThemeThe overall colour scheme of the site's interface: light or dark.
Light themeLight backgrounds with dark text.
Dark themeDark backgrounds with light text.
System modeA setting that follows your device's light or dark preference. Called Auto in the drawer.
ToggleA control that switches between two states, here light and dark.
OverrideA theme set for one panel (a kit or a split-view pane) that differs from the site's theme.
