Split view FAQ

Short answers about split view.

Is the live pane a screenshot?

No. It is the real page, fully working, in its own window area. You can click, type and scroll in it.

Does split view change my page?

No. Your original page keeps running untouched behind split view.

Can I see a page at phone width?

Yes. Set the pane's Viewport to phone (400 pixels) in its More menu. The page reflows as it would on a phone.

Can I compare light and dark?

Yes. Use Mirror so both panes show the same page, then set one pane's Theme to Light and the other to Dark. Compare then highlights every difference.

Can I have more than two panes?

No. Split view shows two panes.

Does split view work on a phone?

It opens, but two pages on a small screen are cramped. It is designed for larger windows.
