Split view

Show two pages of Auric Artisan side by side, each fully working and responsive, with per-pane theme, viewport and zoom.

Split view shows two pages side by side (or one above the other) in the same browser tab. The left pane, the live pane, is a working copy of the page you were on, scrolled to where you were. The right pane, the companion, can show any other page: a tool next to its documentation, two palettes, or the same page in light and dark. Both panes are complete, working pages, and each one behaves like a real window: make a pane narrow and the page inside it switches to its phone layout.

Each pane has its own Back, Forward, Reload and address bar, and its own theme, device width and zoom. A toolbar across the top lets you change the layout, swap the panes, overlay them in Compare mode to see differences, sync their scrolling, send links from one pane to the other, and watch live performance numbers. The address of the page changes as you work, so you can copy it and share the exact split.

Your original page keeps running untouched behind split view. When you close it, you are back where you were, or on the page the live pane moved to.

Who it is for

  • Designers checking a page or palette in light and dark, or at desktop and phone width, at the same time.
  • Developers comparing two versions of a page.
  • Learners reading an article next to the tool it describes.

Where to find it

  • Press Ctrl + Shift + D on any page.
  • Select Open split view in the navigation dock.
  • Right-click the page and choose View, then Split view.
  • Hold Alt and click a link to another page on the site: split view opens with that page in the companion pane.

Quick start

  1. Open https://auricartisan.com/tool/basic-tools/ and press Ctrl + Shift + D.
  2. The live pane shows Basic Tools; the companion shows your last companion page or the homepage.
  3. Select the companion's address bar, type docs, and choose Documentation from the list.
  4. Open the companion's More menu (three dots) and select Theme until it reads dark.
  5. Drag the divider between the panes to give the tool more room. A badge shows the split, for example "62 | 38".
  6. Press Alt + V to stack the panes, and Alt + V again to put them side by side.
  7. Press Esc to close split view.

What you can do

  • Show two site pages at once, each fully interactive and responsive.
  • Give each pane its own theme, device width (desktop, tablet, phone) and zoom.
  • Stack or place panes side by side, swap them, resize them, maximise one.
  • Compare two panes visually, with differences highlighted.
  • Sync scrolling, mirror the live page into the companion, and route links into the companion.
  • Watch frame rate, CPU and memory while you work.
  • Share the whole split as a link.
