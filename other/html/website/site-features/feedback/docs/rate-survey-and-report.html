Rate, survey and report a bug

Give a quick rating, choose whether to be contacted, answer the survey, or report a bug.

Quick rating

The panel asks "You have been here a little while — how is it going?" (or "How is Auric Artisan working for you?").

  1. Select one to five stars, or press 1 to 5 (when you are not typing in the comment box).

  2. The comment question changes with your rating:

    StarsQuestionHint
    1What went wrong? We would like to fix it.The more specific, the better
    2What let you down?What would have made this work for you?
    3What would move this from fine to good?Optional
    4Anything that would have made it a five?Optional
    5What worked well? It helps us keep it that way.Optional

    Before you rate, it reads "Anything you would like to add?".

  3. With one or two stars, the Report a bug button is highlighted and reads Something broke?, in case a bug report fits better.

  4. Select Continue. It becomes available once you have chosen a rating or typed a comment.

Choose whether to be contacted

The next screen asks "Would you like us to contact you about this feedback?" and notes that contact information is completely optional.

  • Send anonymously sends your rating and comment straight away, without asking again.

  • Add contact details shows two optional fields:

    • Name — optional ("How should we address you?", up to 120 characters);
    • Email — optional (you@example.com, up to 254 characters).

    Select Send feedback, or Back to return. If the email does not look valid, the message reads "That email does not look right. Correct it, clear it, or go back to send anonymously."

Either way, a thank-you screen confirms "Thank you for your feedback" and closes by itself.

The survey

Select Full survey in the Feedback panel. A few questions: "Six questions, about a minute. Every answer is optional."

QuestionAnswer type
What brings you here most often?One of: Design work, Development, Accessibility testing, Learning, Something else
How easy was it to find what you needed?1 to 5
How would you rate the speed and performance?1 to 5
How does the design feel to use?1 to 5
Is anything missing that you expected to find?Text ("A tool, an export format, a colour space…")
Anything you particularly liked?Text (optional)

A counter shows how many you have answered, for example "3 of 6 answered". Select Send answers, or Not now to close. The thank-you screen reads "Your responses have been received."

Report a bug

Select Report a bug (or Something broke?) in the Feedback panel.

  1. What happened? (required) — for example "Export HTML did nothing when I pressed it".
  2. What did you expect instead? — for example "A file should download".
  3. How can we reproduce it? — numbered steps.
  4. How badly does it affect you? — Minor, Normal (default) or Blocking.
  5. Select Send report. It becomes available once What happened? has text. Cancel closes the form.

What you type is saved as a draft on this device as you go. If you close the form and open it again, it reads "Restored what you had written earlier."

The report is sent with details of the page, so the problem can be reproduced: the page address, the window and screen size, your browser and platform, your language, the theme and, where available, a few recent error messages from the page. The thank-you screen reads "Your report has been received, with the page details attached. We review reports as part of improving the site."

When you are offline

Feedback you send while offline is kept on your device and sent when the connection returns.
