Custom cursor privacy

What the custom cursor reads and stores.

  • The cursor reads the colour of the page under the pointer, inside your browser, to choose its own colour. It does not record where you point or send pointer movements anywhere.
  • Your on or off choice is kept in this browser as part of your settings.
