Custom cursor

The ring-and-dot pointer that follows your mouse on Auric Artisan, the shapes it takes, and how to turn it off.

On a computer with a mouse or trackpad, Auric Artisan replaces your system pointer with its own: a thin ring that follows your pointer smoothly, and a small dot at the exact point you are aiming. The ring changes shape to tell you what the thing under it does: it grows over links and buttons, becomes an I-beam over text, a double arrow over a resize handle, a crosshair over precise controls, and so on.

The ring also picks its colour from what is under it. It keeps the hue of the surface but pushes its lightness away from it, so the ring stays visible on a dark photo, a light page or a colourful canvas. Over grey or neutral surfaces it uses your accent colour.

The custom cursor is on by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings. It is never shown on touch screens, on devices without a hovering pointer, or when your device asks for reduced motion.

Who it is for

Mouse and trackpad users. If you prefer your system pointer, for example because you use a large or high-contrast system cursor, turn the custom cursor off.

Where to find it

Settings (Ctrl + ,) › Comfort › Custom cursor.

Quick start

  1. Move your mouse over a page. The ring and dot follow it.
  2. Point at a link. The ring grows slightly.
  3. Point at a paragraph of text. The ring becomes a tall, narrow I-beam.
  4. Press Ctrl + ,, select Comfort, and switch off Custom cursor. Your system pointer returns at once.
  5. Switch it back on to see the ring again.

What you can do

  • Turn the custom cursor on or off instantly.
  • Read what a control does from the cursor's shape.
