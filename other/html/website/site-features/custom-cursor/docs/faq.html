Custom cursor FAQ

Short answers about the custom cursor.

Does the custom cursor change where I click?

No. Clicks happen at the dot, exactly where your system pointer would be.

Does Reduce motion in Settings turn it off?

No. The custom cursor follows your device's reduced-motion setting, not the site's Reduce motion switch. Use the Custom cursor switch to turn it off.

Why does the ring change colour?

It reads the colour of the surface under it and picks a version of that colour that stands out, so it stays visible on any background.

Is it shown on my phone?

No. Touch screens use the device's normal behaviour.

Does it work in the installed app?

Yes, on computers with a mouse or trackpad, the same as in the browser.
