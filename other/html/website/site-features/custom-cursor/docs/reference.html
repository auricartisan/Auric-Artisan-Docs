Custom cursor reference

Every shape and effect of the custom cursor, and where each appears.

Shapes

ShapeLooks likeAppears over
DefaultRound ring with a dotOrdinary page areas
PointerLarger ring with a soft glowLinks, buttons and other clickable items
TextTall, narrow I-beamText and text fields
GrabRounded squareThings you can drag
GrabbingSmaller rounded squareWhile dragging
PrecisionSmall ringSliders and vector graphics such as charts and diagrams
CanvasSmall dashed, spinning ringDrawing and graphics areas in tools
ResizeDouble-headed arrow, turned to the directionResize handles and split-view dividers (left-right, up-down or diagonal)
MoveFour-way arrowThings you can move in any direction
Zoom in / Zoom outPlus or minus in the ringImages and areas you can zoom
HelpQuestion mark in the ringAbbreviations and help items
CopySmall plus at the ring's cornerItems that copy when clicked
CrosshairHairline cross in a tight ringPickers and crosshair areas
Not allowedRed ring with a slashActions that are not allowed
DisabledFaint, grey ringDisabled controls
LoadingDashed, spinning ringWhile the page or a tool is busy

Effects

EffectWhen
Magnetic glowOver clickable items and draggable items
ShrinkOn click
StretchDuring fast movement
Fade to halfWhen idle, or when the pointer leaves the window
Adaptive colourAlways: the ring takes the hue of the surface under it, lightened or darkened to stand out
Accent colourOver grey and neutral surfaces
Dark or light dotChosen from the brightness of the surface under it

Setting

ControlValuesDefault
