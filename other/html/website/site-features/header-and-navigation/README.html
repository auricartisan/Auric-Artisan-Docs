Header and navigation

The site header, its menus and quick tools, the mobile drawer, the page utilities bar and the footer.

Every page on auricartisan.com opens with the same header. On a computer it shows the Auric Artisan name, three menus (Company, Tools and Learn), a row of quick tools (the app, the browser extension, the VS Code extensions, search, the guide, your library, language and theme) and a Sign in button. On a phone or a narrow window, the menus and tools fold into a drawer that opens from the menu button, with every item labelled.

Two more pieces of navigation float over every page. The page utilities bar sits at the bottom-left: one button that opens a row of launchers for the kits, the navigation dock and Settings. The navigation dock is a slim rail on the left or right edge with Back, Forward, Reload and scrolling controls; it has its own guide in Navigation dock.

At the bottom of every page, the footer gathers product links, policies, trust pages, contact details and social links.

Who it is for

Everyone who visits the site. The header is the quickest way to find a tool, the documentation or your saved work, and to switch theme or language.

Where to find it

  • The header is at the top of every page. It stays pinned to the top as you scroll (except in reading mode).
  • The page utilities bar button is at the bottom-left corner of the window.
  • The footer is at the end of every page.

Quick start

  1. Select Tools in the header. A menu opens listing every tool, grouped under headings such as Core tools and Colour science.
  2. Select a tool, for example Vision Simulation, to open it. The menu closes.
  3. Select the magnifier (Search) to open Spotlight search, or press Ctrl + K.
  4. Select the sun or moon button to switch between light and dark.
  5. On a phone, tap the three-line menu button. The drawer opens with Browse, Your work, Get Auric and Preferences.
  6. Select the page utilities button at the bottom-left, then Settings, to open the Settings panel.

What you can do

  • Reach every company page, every tool and every learning and library page from three menus.
  • Open search, the guide and your library from any page.
  • Download the app, the browser extension or the VS Code extensions.
  • Switch language and theme in one click.
  • Sign in, or reach your account once signed in.
  • Use a labelled drawer on small screens, with tools grouped behind chips.
  • Launch the kits, the navigation dock and Settings from the page utilities bar.
