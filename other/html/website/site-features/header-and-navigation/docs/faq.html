Header and navigation FAQ

Short answers about the header, drawer, page utilities bar and footer.

Is there a page that lists every tool?

Yes. All Tools at the top of the Tools menu opens https://auricartisan.com/collections/, which shows every tool with filters.

Do the menus open when I hover over them?

No. They open when you click or tap their button, or press Enter, Space or Down on it, and they stay open until you choose an entry or move away.

Can I hide the page utilities bar?

The bar collapses to a single button when you close it, and that button cannot be removed. Reading mode hides the kit launchers.

Where is the navigation dock on my phone?

On phones the dock has no button of its own. Open the page utilities bar and select Navigate.

What does the flag in the footer do?

Double-click it to play a short celebration animation. It does nothing else.

How do I contact Auric Artisan?

Use Email our studio in the footer (hello@auricartisan.com), or the Contact page in the Company menu.
