Language

Switch Auric Artisan between English and Hindi, and learn what is and is not translated.

Auric Artisan is available in English and Hindi (हिन्दी). English is the default. When you switch to Hindi, the page you are on is translated in place, without a reload, and every page you open afterwards appears in Hindi too, until you switch back.

The translation covers the site's interface and its pages: menus, buttons, labels, tooltips, messages, settings, kits and the text of articles and tool pages. The site's own brand and product names are written in Devanagari (for example ऑरिक आर्टिज़न for Auric Artisan), while names of other companies and products, code, hex values and keyboard keys stay as they are. Hindi text uses Devanagari fonts chosen for each platform, with slightly taller line spacing for comfortable reading.

Switching back to English reloads the page in English.

Who it is for

Hindi readers, and anyone who prefers to use the site in Hindi. The language choice also sets how dates and times are written (Indian Hindi conventions).

Where to find it

  • The globe button in the header (tooltip Language / भाषा). It switches between the two languages.
  • Settings › Appearance › Language: English or हिन्दी.
  • The mobile drawer › Preferences › Language.
  • Spotlight: press Ctrl + K, type >, and choose Switch language.
  • A link ending in ?lang=hi (or ?lang=en).

Quick start

  1. Select the globe button in the header.
  2. The page switches to Hindi. Menus, buttons and the page text now read in Devanagari.
  3. Open any other page: it is in Hindi too.
  4. Select the globe again. The page reloads in English.

What you can do

  • Use the whole site in Hindi, including the header, footer, kits, Settings, Spotlight, the right-click menu and page content.
  • Share a link that opens in Hindi by adding ?lang=hi.
  • Keep Hindi available offline in the installed app.
