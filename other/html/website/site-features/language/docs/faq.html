Language FAQ

Short answers about English and Hindi on Auric Artisan.

Which languages are available?

English and Hindi.

Is the Hindi version a separate website?

No. Every page has one address, and the same page is shown in the language you chose.

Does switching language change my other settings?

No. Theme, accent, text size and your other settings stay as they are.

Does my account remember the language?

The language is kept in your browser, not in your account. Choose it once on each browser you use, or open a link with ?lang=hi.

Why are some brand names written in Devanagari?

The site writes its own brand and product names in Devanagari so that Hindi pages read in one script. Names of other companies and tools that you would type into a search engine stay in Latin letters.

Can I read an article in Hindi and a tool in English?

Not at the same time. The language applies to the whole site in this browser.
