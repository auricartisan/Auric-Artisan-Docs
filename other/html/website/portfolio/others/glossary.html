Portfolio glossary

Terms used in the Portfolio Builder and the Portfolio Index.

TermMeaning
AccentThe colour used for links, headings and buttons, picked from your saved palettes.
Address (handle)The name after auricartisan.com/p/ in your published address.
Alt textA written description of an image for people using screen readers.
Blocking findingA Check result that must be fixed before you can publish.
Credit lineThe small "Made with Auric Artisan" line on exported HTML and PDF files.
DraftYour portfolio as it is in the builder, saved automatically; not what visitors see until you publish.
External portfolioA portfolio on your own website, added to the Portfolio Index through the Search Console.
MarkdownA plain-text way of writing formatted text, such as **bold** and # Heading.
PaperThe page's background and text colour: Cream, White, Sand, Ink or No background.
PlaceholderGrey example text in an empty field; it is not published.
Portfolio IndexThe directory of portfolios at https://auricartisan.com/portfolios/.
PublishPut the current draft online at your address.
Raw HTMLA section that accepts your own HTML and CSS, cleaned for safety.
RhythmThe page's spacing and corner roundness: Tight, Even or Airy.
ReviewedAn external portfolio a person has checked and approved.
Search ConsoleAuric Artisan's tool for site owners at https://auricartisan.com/search/console/, where external portfolios are added.
SectionOne building block of the page, such as a Heading or a Timeline.
Share cardThe preview (name, headline, avatar) shown when your address is shared in apps.
Snapshot linkA link that contains the whole portfolio in the address itself.
VerifiedAn external portfolio on a site whose owner proved it is theirs.
WCAGWeb Content Accessibility Guidelines; the Check pane tests against WCAG 2.2 AA.
