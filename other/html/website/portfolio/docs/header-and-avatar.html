Header and avatar

Fill in the portfolio header, follow your profile or override it, choose what to show, and set an avatar.

The Header panel at the top of the Content column controls the top of your portfolio: "The top of your portfolio. Leave a field blank to follow your profile."

The fields

FieldLimitShow switchNotes
Name80 charactersAlways shownBlank follows your profile's display name.
Headline / tagline120 charactersAlways shownAlso used on your share card.
Email120 charactersShow — off by defaultThe address you publish can differ from the one you sign in with.
Location80 charactersShow — on by defaultPlaceholder "City, Country".
Website200 charactersShow — on by default—
Avatar (image address)500 charactersShow — on by defaultOr pick a picture (below).
AccentA colour picker—The page's accent colour, also set from Style.

Following your profile

A blank field is not "no value" — it means "use my profile". The profile's value appears as grey placeholder text in the empty field, so you can see what the page will say. Type into the field to use a different value just for your portfolio; clear it to go back to following your profile. Changes to your profile then reach the portfolio automatically.

A Show switch that is off keeps that detail off the page, off exports and out of anything you publish or share, even if the field has a value.

Use the built-in header

The Use the built-in header switch turns the whole header block (name, avatar, headline and details) on or off. Turn it off when your page has its own opening — for example a Raw HTML hero — so it is not preceded by a second one. While it is off, the Avatar, Location and Email switches are greyed out.

Choose an avatar

  1. Double-click the avatar circle in the Header panel, or double-click your picture in the preview ("Double-click to choose a picture").
  2. Pick an image file.
  3. The builder resizes it to a small square, stores it inside your portfolio and confirms Avatar updated.

Because the picture is stored in the portfolio, it travels with every export, snapshot link and publish. Resizing also removes hidden photo information (such as camera and location data) from the file.

Messages you may see:

  • Turn the built-in header on first. — the header is switched off.
  • That picture is too large even shrunk. Try a smaller one.
  • That file could not be read as a picture.

You can also paste an image address into Avatar instead. With no avatar, the page shows your initial.

Note: A snapshot link has little room, so a large avatar is left out of snapshot links (the initial is shown instead). Published pages keep it.

Result

The top of your portfolio says exactly who you are, with only the details you chose to show.
