Send feedback

Send quick or detailed feedback about Auric Artisan from the Feature Kit, and what happens when sending fails.

The Feedback tool in the Feature Kit lets you tell the Auric Artisan team how the site is working for you. A quick rating takes one click; a detailed form takes about a minute. Both are optional.

Open the feedback form

Select the Feedback card on the Feature Kit menu, or press 3 on the menu.

You may also see a small prompt inside the Feature Kit that reads Feedback when you're ready with the line "The full form lives in Feature Kit and stays out of the way." Select Open form to go to the form, or Later to dismiss it. The prompt hides by itself after 10 seconds.

Send quick feedback

  1. Read the question at the top. It depends on what you did last in the kit, for example How's your experience so far?, How was the calculator? or How was the unit converter?
  2. Choose one of five faces: Terrible, Bad, Okay, Good or Amazing.
  3. A comment box appears: Tell us more (optional).... Type a comment if you like.
  4. Select Submit. It reads Sending... while it sends.

A thank-you message appears, matched to your rating: Thank you!, We hear you. or Thanks for sharing!. Select Done, or wait 5 seconds, to return to the Feature Kit menu.

Submit stays disabled until you choose a face.

Send detailed feedback

  1. On the quick feedback screen, optionally choose a face, then select Give Detailed Feedback.
  2. Answer each question. A progress bar and a counter such as 2/8 show where you are.
  3. Use Next → to continue, Skip to leave a question unanswered, or ← Back to return to the previous question.
  4. On the last question, the forward button reads Submit with a tick. Select it to send.

The questions, in order:

QuestionHow you answer
How easy was it to find what you needed?A scale of 1 to 5, from Very hard to Very easy
How would you rate the speed & performance?A scale of 1 to 5, from Very slow to Lightning
How do you feel about the design?Five faces: Love it, Nice, Okay, Meh, Needs work
Follow-up questionsDepend on your quick rating (see below)
What features would you like to see?Pick any of: Dark/Light themes, More tools, Keyboard shortcuts, Mobile improvements, Accessibility, Import/Export, Other
Would you recommend this to a colleague?A scale of 0 to 10, from Not likely to Very likely
Anything else you'd like to share?Free text

Follow-up questions:

  • After a 4 or 5 rating: "What do you like most?" (free text) and "Would you share this with others?" (Definitely!, Maybe, Probably not).
  • After a 1 or 2 rating, or no rating: "What frustrated you the most?", "What did you expect to happen?" (both free text) and "What should we fix first?" (Bugs, Performance, Design, Features, Usability).
  • After a 3 rating: "What one thing would make this better?" (free text).

Not now

Select Not now on the quick feedback screen to leave without sending. The kit returns to its menu and the prompts give you more room before they appear again.

If sending fails

If your feedback cannot be sent, you see Could not send yet with one of these messages:

MessageWhat it means
Your feedback is saved on this device. Check your connection and try again.You are offline or the server did not answer. The feedback is kept and will be sent later
The server could not accept this feedback. You can try again or return to the menu.The server refused this submission
Your feedback could not be sent or saved on this device. Keep this page open and try again.Your browser would not store it, for example in a private window
Your feedback is saved, but the current account could not be confirmed yet. Check your connection and try again.You were signed in, and your sign-in could not be checked
This feedback belongs to a different signed-in account. Return to that account before retrying.You switched accounts after writing it

Select Try again (or Check account) to retry, or Done to return to the menu. Feedback saved on your device is retried automatically, including when your connection comes back.

When the kit asks for feedback

The kit asks quietly and rarely:

  • only while the Feature Kit is open on its menu, never while you are in a tool;
  • after you have used the calculator or the converter a few times, or after about 15 minutes;
  • no more than twice in one visit, and no more than once an hour;
  • less often if you have dismissed several prompts.

If a prompt comes due while the kit is closed, it waits and appears the next time you open the menu.

What is sent

See Privacy. In short: your rating, comment and answers, the page you were on, and which tool you used last. If you are signed in, the feedback is linked to your account.

For feedback outside the Feature Kit, see Site features.
