Advanced Kit privacy

What the Advanced Kit keeps in your browser and what it downloads.

Kept in your browser

WhatWhy
Your tool visibility modeSo every page shows the tools you chose
The tools you unticked in Custom modeSo your custom set applies site-wide
Position, expanded size, Focus mode and themeShared kit preferences

Nothing is linked to your account or sent anywhere. Clearing this site's data in your browser resets the mode to Basic and clears your custom choices.

Downloaded

When you first open the Custom checklist on a page, the kit downloads the site's tool lists from auricartisan.com to build it.
