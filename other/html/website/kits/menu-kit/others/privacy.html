Menu Kit privacy

What the Menu Kit keeps in your browser and what it downloads.

Kept in your browser

WhatWhy
The last tab you usedTo open on it next time
Which sections you collapsedTo keep them collapsed
How many times you opened each page from the Menu KitTo fill Most Used and rank search results
Position, expanded size, Focus mode and themeShared kit preferences

None of this is sent anywhere. It is removed when you clear this site's data in your browser.

Downloaded

The first time you open the Menu Kit on a page, it downloads the list of pages and the site status from auricartisan.com. Your searches are not sent: matching happens in your browser.

The Copy current URL quick action writes the page address to your clipboard, which your browser may ask permission for.
