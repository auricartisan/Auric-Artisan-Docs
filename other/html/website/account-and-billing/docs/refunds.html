Refunds

The 7-day money-back window on Auric Artisan purchases, how to ask for a refund, how long it takes, and what a refund removes.

This page summarises the Refund Policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/refund/. The policy page is authoritative; this page explains how to use it.

What you can get back

PurchaseRefund terms
Plan pass (one month or twelve)Within 7 days of purchase, refunded for any reason. After 7 days, considered case by case.
Prepaid API creditsWithin 7 days of that purchase, to the extent they are unused. Credits already spent on API requests are not refundable; a partly used balance may be refunded pro rata at Auric Artisan's discretion.
Token packsWithin 7 days if unused; partly used packs may be refunded pro rata at Auric Artisan's discretion. Token packs are not on sale today.
Included allowancesNot refundable separately. The tokens and API credits that come with a plan are part of the plan price, and refunding the plan refunds them with it.
Advertising bookingsIn full at any time before the campaign starts; after that, case by case.

After the 7-day window, the policy says refunds are considered against the purchase terms shown at checkout, payment-provider records, account status, usage and applicable law.

Ask for a refund

From the dashboard

  1. Open the dashboard and select Plan & Usage.
  2. In Current Plan, select Request a refund. (This button appears on a paid pass.)
  3. The contact page opens. Choose the Billing topic.
  4. Fill in your name and the email address on the account.
  5. In the reference field (Order or invoice ID), paste your order reference. It is in your receipt email and on the confirmation page, and starts with order_.
  6. In the message, give the purchase date and what you bought, and say you want a refund.
  7. Select Send.

From the contact page directly

Go to https://auricartisan.com/company/contact/ and follow steps 3–7. The policy says both routes "reach the same place; the button simply saves you finding the page". Use the email address associated with the purchase.

Result: you get a confirmation email, and a reply from Auric Artisan in the same thread. The Billing topic lists a reply time of 1–2 days.

How long it takes

Approved refunds are issued through Razorpay to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of approval. Your bank or card issuer may take longer to show it on your statement.

What a refund removes

  • A refunded plan ends at once: plan benefits are removed and the account returns to the free Apprentice plan.
  • Refunded tokens or API credits are deducted from the balance they were granted to.
  • Your work stays yours. Content and projects you created are not removed.

The confirmation page for a refunded order says "The payment for this order has been refunded in full" and that anything the order activated has been removed. The payment appears as Refunded in Invoices & Payment History.

Charges you do not recognise

If you see a charge that appears to be from Auric Artisan and you did not make it, or you were asked to pay outside the official checkout, contact Auric Artisan through the contact page with the date, amount, payment provider and any transaction reference. Do not send full card numbers, passwords or other secrets.
