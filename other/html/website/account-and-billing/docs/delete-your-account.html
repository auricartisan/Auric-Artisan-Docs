Delete your account

Unlock the Auric Artisan danger zone, erase this browser's workspace, or permanently delete your account, and what deletion does and does not remove.

The Danger zone, at the bottom of Security & Privacy, holds the two actions that cannot be undone:

  • Erase this browser's workspace — clears the library, collections, drafts and preferences stored in this browser. Anything saved to the account stays.
  • Delete this account — permanently deletes your profile, portfolio, keys, sessions and billing history. There is no undo and no recovery.

The zone is locked until you prove it is you, and each action asks for its own confirmation word on top of that.

Before you delete

  • Export anything you want to keep. See Export your data. Download invoices you need from Plan & Usage.
  • Revoke or note your API keys. They stop working when the account is deleted.
  • Consider a refund first if you bought a plan in the last 7 days. See Refunds.
  • Accounts created with Google or GitHub need a password to unlock the danger zone. If you never set one, the zone says "Set an account password before using these actions." Set one with Forgot password? first — see Passwords.

Unlock the danger zone

  1. Open the dashboard and select Security & Privacy. Scroll to Danger zone. Its state reads Locked.
  2. The list Behind this door names the two actions it contains.
  3. Under First, type this sentence, type the sentence shown exactly: "I understand these actions cannot be undone". A hint says "That matches." when it does, or "Not yet — it has to match word for word."
  4. Under Then your account password, type your password.
  5. Select Unlock for 5 minutes.

Result: the state reads Unlocked and a timer shows "Unlocked — relocks in 5:00". The actions appear. When the timer reaches zero the zone locks itself again; select Lock now to lock it sooner. Reloading the page does not lock it early.

Tip: If you read the site in Hindi, type the sentence as it is displayed, in Hindi.

Erase this browser's workspace

  1. Unlock the danger zone.
  2. Next to Erase this browser's workspace, select Erase.
  3. Type ERASE in Type ERASE to confirm.
  4. Select Erase again (it becomes available once the word matches). Select Cancel to stop.

Result: "Cleared N stored items from this browser. Reload to see it." — or "There was nothing stored in this browser." Only this browser's library, portfolio draft, dashboard preferences and collection preferences are cleared. Your cookie choice and theme are kept, and your account, plan, keys and anything published are untouched.

Delete this account

  1. Unlock the danger zone.
  2. Next to Delete this account, select Delete account….
  3. Read the warning: "This immediately and permanently erases your profile, portfolio, API keys, sessions, and billing history. There is no undo and no recovery."
  4. Type DELETE in Type DELETE to confirm.
  5. Type your password in Confirm your password.
  6. Select Permanently delete account (available once both are filled in). Select Cancel to stop.

Result: "Permanently deleting your account…", then "Account deleted. Redirecting…", and the home page opens. You are signed out on every device.

If it fails: "Could not delete your account — check your password and try again."

What deletion does

  • The account is closed immediately. You are signed out everywhere and cannot sign in to it again.
  • Your personal details are removed from the account: the name, email address, password and two-step secret no longer belong to it.
  • API keys stop working, because they only work for an active account.
  • The dashboard describes the result as erasing "your profile, portfolio, API keys, sessions, and billing history". The next section explains how payment records are handled.

What is kept, and why

The Security page (https://auricartisan.com/security/) explains that "Account erasure anonymises personal data in place rather than destroying financial records. Tax law requires invoices to be retained, and an invoice naming nobody is not a valid record — so the transaction remains and your identity does not."

The Data Retention and Deletion Policy adds that backup copies and security logs may persist until their normal rotation completes, and that records required by law, disputes or security may be kept longer. The FAQ says personal data is scheduled for deletion within about 90 days unless the law requires it to be kept.

What deletion does not touch

  • Your browser. Work saved in this browser (the library, drafts, preferences) is not on the account and is not removed by deleting it. Use Erase this browser's workspace first, or clear your browser's site data.
  • Copies you made. Exports, downloaded files and anything you published elsewhere.
  • The browser and VS Code extensions. They keep their own local data; uninstall them to remove it.

Ask for deletion instead

If you cannot sign in, you can ask for deletion through the contact page with the topic Privacy or data, from the email address on the account. See Contact and support.
