Emails and messages

The emails your Auric Artisan account sends, where replies to your messages arrive, and why there is no in-account inbox.

Your Auric Artisan account has no message inbox of its own. Everything the site needs to tell you arrives by email, at the address on your account, and replies to anything you send through the contact form arrive by email too.

Emails the account sends

EmailWhen it arrivesWhat to do with it
Verification linkAfter you sign up with email and password, or when you ask for a fresh linkOpen it within 24 hours and select Confirm email ownership. See Verify your email.
WelcomeOnce your account is usable — straight away for Google or GitHub sign-ups, after verification for email sign-upsNothing; it lists places to start: the tools, the Learn library and your dashboard. Replying reaches a person.
Password reset linkWhen you use Forgot password?Open it within one hour. See Passwords.
ReceiptAfter a purchaseKeep it; it carries your order reference, which starts with order_.
Contact form confirmationWhen you send a message through the contact pageNothing; it confirms the message arrived.
Reply to your messageWhen Auric Artisan answersReply in the same thread.

Email is delivered through Resend, named on the Security page as a third-party provider.

Where replies come from

The contact page routes each topic to one of three addresses, and replies come from the same one:

AddressTopics
hello@auricartisan.comUsing a tool, Billing, Something else
developer@auricartisan.comA bug, API or integration
security@auricartisan.comSecurity, Privacy or data

Invoices name support@auricartisan.com for questions about an invoice.

See Contact and support for reply times and how to write a useful message.

Beware of look-alikes

Auric Artisan asks you to pay only through the Razorpay checkout on auricartisan.com. If an email asks you to pay somewhere else, or asks for your password or backup codes, do not act on it; report it through the contact page.

Change the email address on the account

The email address cannot be changed from the dashboard yet. Its field in Profile is read-only and says "Email changes are not supported yet — contact support." Write through the contact page from your current address.
