Sign up

Create an Auric Artisan account with an email address and password, or with Google or GitHub, including every field rule and the human check.

You can create an account in two ways: with an email address and a password, or with an existing Google or GitHub account. Both are free and neither asks for a card.

The sign-up form is at https://auricartisan.com/auth/#register. You can also open https://auricartisan.com/auth/ and select the Create Account tab, or select Create account in the site header.

Sign up with email and password

  1. Open https://auricartisan.com/auth/ and select Create Account. The panel is headed Create your identity.

  2. Fill in the fields:

    FieldWhat to enterRules
    Display NameThe name shown on anything you publishRequired, 2–64 characters
    UsernameYour public handle, typed after the @Required, 3–30 characters: letters, numbers and underscores only. You can sign in with it instead of your email.
    EmailYour email addressRequired, a valid address
    PasswordYour password12–128 characters, upper and lower case letters, at least one number and one symbol, no character repeated four or more times in a row, not a common password
    Confirm PasswordThe same password againMust match

  3. As you type the username, a tick means it is available and a cross means it is not. Hover over the mark, or read the message under the field, for the reason.

  4. As you type the password, a checklist shows which of the six rules are met, and a label reads Password strength: Weak, Fair, Good or Strong. Select the eye button to show or hide what you typed.

  5. Tick I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Both names are links to the full documents.

  6. Select Create Account.

  7. The Auric human check appears the first time you submit, with the message "One quick check that you are human, then we will sign you in." Complete it (see below), then select Create Account again.

Result: the panel changes to Confirm your inbox. It shows your email address partly masked, a 24-hour Verification window countdown, a Resend button (available after 60 seconds) and Continue to limited workspace. Continue with Verify your email.

Note: If the email address already has an account, the same confirmation screen appears but offers Return to sign in instead of Continue to limited workspace. No second account is created. Sign in with the existing account, or use Forgot password? if you do not remember its password.

The Auric human check

The human check replaces a traditional CAPTCHA. It proves a person is at the keyboard for this one attempt.

  1. A sequence of three marks is shown, with a countdown.
  2. Below it, select the same marks in the same order from the choices. Each choice you select fills the next answer slot.
  3. To undo a choice, select its slot and it is removed.
  4. When all three slots are filled, select Verify sequence.
  5. If it matches, the status reads "Human signal verified. This proof is single use."
MessageWhat it means
"Enter your email or username before verifying the sequence."The check is tied to the address or username in the form. Fill that in first.
"Take a moment, then verify the sequence again."You answered faster than a person usually can. Wait a second and select Verify sequence again.
"That did not match. Repeat the displayed sequence."The order was wrong. The slots are cleared; try again.
"This check expired. Request a new sequence."The countdown ran out. Select New sequence.
"A fresh human check is required for another attempt."Each proof works once. After a failed or completed attempt, complete a new check.

A proof belongs to the email or username you typed when you verified. If you change that field afterwards, the proof no longer applies and you need a new check.

Sign up with Google or GitHub

  1. On the Create Account tab, under or sign up with, select Google or GitHub.
  2. Sign in to that provider if asked, and allow Auric Artisan to use your account.
  3. You return to Auric Artisan signed in.

Result: your account is created with the provider's email address, which counts as verified, so there is no verification email to wait for. A welcome email confirms that you signed in with Google or GitHub, "so there's no password to remember."

Things to know about provider accounts

  • An account created this way has no Auric Artisan password. Actions that ask for your password — such as unlocking the danger zone to delete the account — ask you to set one first. To set one, use Forgot password? on the sign-in page with the account's email address. See Passwords.
  • If an account already exists for the provider's email address, you see "This email already has an account. Sign in with the method already connected to it." Use that method instead.
  • If the provider account has no verified email address, you see "Your provider account has no verified email address." Verify it with the provider, or sign up with email and password.

Other messages you might see after returning from a provider are listed in Troubleshooting.

After you sign up

  • You are on the free Apprentice plan.
  • If you have no username (possible for some older accounts), the dashboard asks for one.
  • Next: Verify your email, then Getting started with the dashboard.
