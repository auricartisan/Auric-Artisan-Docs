Plan & Usage section

Your Auric Artisan plan, usage meters, the Change plan cards, invoices and payment history, and the API console for plans with API access.

The Plan & Usage section (https://auricartisan.com/auth/dashboard/#plan) shows what you pay for and what you use: your plan, your usage meters, your invoices, and — on plans that include the REST API — your API keys, credits and spend controls. The header menu items Billing & plan and API keys both open this section, as do the older addresses #billing and #api-keys.

What you see depends on your plan:

  • On Apprentice and Artisan, the section shows your plan and receipts, and one card explaining that the API is not part of your plan.
  • On Specialist and Industrial Pro, it also shows the full API console.
  • If your account holds API keys from an earlier plan, the API console stays visible whatever you pay now, so you can still see and revoke those keys.

Current Plan

The Current Plan panel names your plan and its state in one line:

LineWhen
"You're on the free Apprentice plan."Free plan
"Artisan — active until 12 Oct 2026. Does not renew automatically." (plan and date vary)A paid pass, which ends on that date
"Your Artisan subscription has ended."A plan that has ended
"Payment for your Artisan plan failed — please update your payment method."A failed payment
"Artisan — no active subscription on this account."The plan is named but has no active purchase behind it

Buttons:

ButtonWhat it does
Change PlanOpens the pricing page
Request a refundShown on a paid pass. Its tooltip explains: "This pass does not renew, so there is nothing to cancel — you will not be charged again. Use this to ask for a refund instead." It opens the contact page. See Refunds.

Note: A Cancel Subscription button exists for a genuine recurring subscription, which would say when it renews. Auric Artisan does not sell recurring subscriptions today, so you will normally see Request a refund instead. See Plan terms, renewal and cancelling.

Usage meters

Under the plan line is a list of meters. Each shows how much you have used against your plan's figure (∞ means unlimited), with a bar that changes colour when you pass two-thirds of the figure and again when you reach it.

MeterCountsApprentice figure
Vision sims todayVision simulations run today25
URL analyses this monthPage analyses in the Analyzer this month20
SVG recolors this monthSVG downloads from SVG Recolor this month15
Experiment runsPsychophysics Engine runs25
ProjectsSaved items in your library20
Team seatsSeats on the account (every account is single-user today)1

Paid plans show their own, higher or unlimited, figures. A meter with nothing to count on your plan is hidden. If nothing has been used yet, the list says "No usage to report yet — your meters will appear as you use the tools."

Things to know about the meters:

  • Daily meters reset each day and monthly meters each calendar month, both in UTC.
  • Counts include use recorded in this browser, so a meter can differ between browsers until the account's own figure catches up.
  • Projects is counted from the library saved in this browser.
  • Every tool stays usable. The pricing page describes vision simulations as unmetered, because they run in your browser.

Change plan

Change plan is a folded panel. Its line reads "You are on Apprentice. Compare what each tier includes." (or "…the highest tier…" on Industrial Pro). Open it to see one card per plan:

Card partShows
Name and priceThe plan and its monthly price, or Free / forever
DescriptionWhat the tier adds
ButtonCurrent plan, Get this plan, Switch plan, Switch to Free, or Coming soon
  • Cards marked Coming soon cannot be selected; those tiers are not on sale.
  • Selecting a paid plan opens the pricing page, where you buy it. See Buy a plan.
  • Switch to Free returns you to the dashboard. A paid pass cannot be cut short from here; it ends on its own date, or you can ask for a refund.
  • Prices on these cards are shown in Indian rupees. The pricing page lets you choose US dollars or rupees.
  • See the full comparison opens the pricing page's comparison table.

If you have not bought anything: "No payments yet. Receipts appear here after your first purchase." See Invoices and receipts.

On plans without the API

Apprentice and Artisan see one card instead: Credits, keys and the live feed — Not part of Apprentice (or your plan's name). "They start at Specialist, which includes 1,000,000 API credits a billing month." with a Compare plans button.
