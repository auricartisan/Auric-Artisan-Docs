Invoices and receipts

Find your Auric Artisan payment history, open an invoice, print it or save it as PDF, and understand what the receipt is for tax purposes.

Every payment, refund and chargeback on your account is listed in the dashboard, and each one has its own printable invoice page.

Find your payment history

  1. Open the dashboard and select Plan & Usage (or choose Billing & plan in the header account menu).
  2. Scroll to Invoices & Payment History.

The table lists Date, Description (Payment, Refund or Chargeback, with the plan and term), Amount, Status (Paid, Failed or Refunded) and a View link. It shows 25 rows at a time; use <- Newer** and **Older -> to page through more.

With no purchases yet it reads "No payments yet. Receipts appear here after your first purchase."

Open an invoice

  1. Select View on a row.
  2. The invoice page opens at https://auricartisan.com/auth/billing/invoice/ with that invoice's reference in the address.

The invoice shows:

PartContents
HeaderAuric Artisan — "Color science platform & creative tools", the website and the support email address
Invoice #, Date, StatusThe invoice reference, the date and Paid, Failed or Refunded
FromAuric Artisan, "Internet · Worldwide", support@auricartisan.com
Billed toYour name and email address
Line itemsWhat was bought (the plan and its term) and the amount, then the Total
Payment note"Payment processed via" the provider (Razorpay), and the payment reference
Footer"Auric Artisan is operated under Indian law. Questions? Email support@auricartisan.com and quote the invoice number above."

Print or save as PDF

  1. Open the invoice.
  2. Select Print / Save as PDF.
  3. In your browser's print dialog, choose a printer, or choose Save as PDF (the wording depends on your browser).

Share or keep the link

Select Copy invoice URL to copy the invoice's address; the button briefly reads Copied. Only you can open it: the page needs you to be signed in to the account that made the payment.

Select ← Back to billing to return to the dashboard.

Receipts and GST

The Subscription and Billing Policy explains that Auric Artisan is not currently registered for GST. That means:

  • no GST or other tax is added at checkout — the price you see is the total you pay;
  • the receipts issued are payment receipts, not GST tax invoices: they carry no GSTIN and no tax component;
  • they cannot be used to claim input tax credit.

If you need a GST invoice for a business purchase, the policy asks you to wait until registration is in place rather than buying on the assumption that one will follow.

Note: The invoice page has a section for GST details ("This is a tax invoice. GST is inclusive in the total.") that appears only when an invoice actually carries tax. Under the current policy you should not expect to see it.

Your receipt email and order reference

The contact page's Billing topic says your order or invoice ID "is in your receipt email. It starts with order_." Quote it whenever you contact Auric Artisan about a payment. The confirmation page after checkout shows the same Order reference with a copy button.

Messages on the invoice page

MessageWhat to do
Invoice not found — "We couldn't load this invoice. It may have been removed, or you may need to sign in."Sign in to the account that paid, then open the invoice from your payment history.
"Missing invoice id."Open the invoice from the View link rather than a shortened address.
"That invoice could not be found on your account."The invoice belongs to another account, or the reference is wrong.
"We could not load this invoice. Try again later."A temporary problem. Try again.
"Network error loading the invoice."Check your connection.
