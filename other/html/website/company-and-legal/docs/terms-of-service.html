Terms of Service

A plain-language summary of Auric Artisan's Terms of Service, section by section, with the official page as the authority.

Official page: https://auricartisan.com/legal/terms/ — version 2.4, effective 27 April 2026, last updated 12 August 2026. The official Terms are authoritative. This summary is for understanding only; it does not change or add to them.

Key points

  • Auric Artisan provides colour tools, colour-science resources, web analysis, theme generation and visual simulations, including more than 70 vision simulation models.
  • Web analysis is for sites and assets you own or are authorised to test.
  • Accounts are live. One-time purchases go through Razorpay; recurring subscriptions are not offered.
  • API access is metered in API credits against a published monthly allowance; overage is opt-in, prepaid and never invoiced afterwards.
  • API credits and tokens are separate balances, and neither pays for the other.
  • Outputs are exploratory and informational — not certified professional, medical, legal, security or accessibility advice.
  • The Ishihara-style generator does not replace medical-grade Ishihara plates.

Section by section

  1. Introduction and agreement — Using the platform means agreeing to the Terms and the policies they reference. The plain-language note: use the tools responsibly, respect ownership and safety limits, and understand the platform is still evolving.
  2. What Auric Artisan provides — Colour tools and models, articles, simulations, web analysis (security-oriented checks, Lighthouse-like performance review, responsive review, palette extraction, structure and visual analysis) and theme generation.
  3. Current platform status — Registration, sign-in and the dashboard are live. Plan passes and advertising bookings are sold as one-time purchases through Razorpay; token packs are not on sale. Nothing renews and no mandate is stored; "subscription" in the Terms means the plan record and its period, not a recurring charge. Every tool is free on every tier. API access is included from Specialist and metered against a finite allowance for purchases on or after 12 August 2026. Features may change. Pay only through the Razorpay checkout on auricartisan.com.
  4. Eligibility and access — You are responsible for complying with the laws, permissions and consent rules where you are. Some parts need registration; access can vary by tool, region and capacity.
  5. Accounts and security — You are responsible for keeping your credentials secure, keeping recovery details current, and for all activity through your account.
  6. Acceptable use — No unlawful activity, harassment, abuse, spam, unauthorised access or disruption. No scraping, reverse engineering, overloading, copying, reselling or redistributing the platform without written permission. Full rules: Acceptable Use Policy.
  7. Web analysis and authorised testing — Analyse only what you own, manage or are explicitly authorised to test. Do not use findings to attack or gain access to anything. Security observations are informational and are not a certified audit; other observations are guidance. You are responsible for having permission before submitting a site.
  8. User content and ownership — You keep ownership of what you create, upload or submit, subject to third-party rights. Auric Artisan receives only the limited permission needed to process it and run the platform. You are responsible for not infringing others' rights. See Content and IP Policy.
  9. Simulations and disclaimers — Outputs are informational and "as is", and must not replace certified medical, legal, governmental, accessibility, security, industrial, regulatory or safety-critical review. The Ishihara-style generator is not a clinical tool and does not diagnose.
  10. Pricing, payments and billing — Auric Artisan sells one-time plan passes, token packs and advertising bookings through Razorpay; price, duration and inclusions are shown at checkout. Tokens and API credits are separate. API usage is metered against a finite allowance; exhausted allowances are refused, not billed. Overage is optional, off by default and prepaid, limited by your spend cap and a ceiling of ten times the allowance, and never invoiced afterwards. Industrial Pro purchases before 12 August 2026 keep their unlimited API allowance. Billing, cancellation, tax and refunds follow the Subscription and Billing Policy and Refund Policy in force at purchase, including the 7-day money-back window. Report any other payment request as fraudulent.
  11. Third-party services and availability — The platform relies on third parties for hosting, analytics, infrastructure and security, and is not responsible for their outages or behaviour. Tools and services may be changed, paused or removed.
  12. Liability, governing law and changes — To the extent the law allows, no liability for indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary or punitive damages from use, reliance on outputs, interruption, data loss or third-party issues. The Terms are governed by the laws of India, and aim to align data practices with laws including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The Terms may be updated; continued use means acceptance. Together with the referenced policies, they form the agreement between you and Auric Artisan.

Note: Section 10 mentions payments "in INR" and lists token packs among things sold, while section 3 says token packs are not on sale. The pricing page charges US dollars outside India and rupees within it, and marks token top-ups "not on sale". Follow the pricing page and checkout for what is actually offered.

What this means for you

  • You can use every tool without paying, for sites and material you have the right to use.
  • If you create an account, keep your password and backup codes safe; you are responsible for what happens under it.
  • You own your work.
  • Treat every result as guidance, not certification.
  • Pay only through the official checkout.
