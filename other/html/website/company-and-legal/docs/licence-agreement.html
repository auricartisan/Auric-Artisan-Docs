Licence Agreement

A plain-language summary of Auric Artisan's License Agreement — the licence to use the platform, its restrictions, your rights in outputs, open-source parts and termination.

Official page: https://auricartisan.com/legal/licenses/ — License Agreement, version 2.2, effective 27 April 2026, last updated 23 May 2026. The official agreement is authoritative.

Key points

  • Using the platform is a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence.
  • Outputs you create — palettes, themes, reports, exports and simulations — remain yours.
  • Web analysis is only for sites and assets you own or are authorised to test.
  • Ishihara-style outputs do not replace medical-grade plates.
  • Account, API, export and paid features are licensed only for legitimate use.
  • The platform's code, branding, design systems and models remain Auric Artisan's.

Section by section

  1. Platform licence — A limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable licence to access and use the platform. Ownership of the software, design, brand, models and documentation does not transfer.
  2. Current access status — Features vary by rollout, region and readiness. Pricing pages alone are not a licence to bypass access controls.
  3. Restrictions — You may not copy, redistribute or resell the platform; reverse engineer or extract models or source code; scrape or crawl it without permission; sublicense or rent access; remove notices or branding; build a directly competing product by copying its interface, workflow, data or logic; analyse sites you are not authorised to test; or present simulation outputs as medical, legal or certified results.
  4. Tool-specific output rights — Colour-science results, generated themes and tokens, web analysis reports and simulation outputs may be used by their creator. Auric Artisan's own articles and documentation remain protected unless stated otherwise.
  5. Web analysis use — Only for sites, code or assets you own, manage or are authorised to test.
  6. User-generated content — Stays your intellectual property, subject to third-party rights. The licence you grant for processing is in the Content and IP Policy.
  7. Exports — Your responsibility once they leave the platform. Results depend on rendering, source material, models and devices; verify them before relying on them professionally.
  8. Open-source software — Governed by the components' own licences (MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD and others), which do not cover Auric Artisan's proprietary parts.
  9. Termination — The licence can end at once on breach of this agreement, the Terms or the Acceptable Use Policy; misuse of web analysis; copying, scraping or reselling; or discontinuation of the platform. You must then stop using it; content you already exported stays yours.
  10. Changes — May be updated; continued use means acceptance.
