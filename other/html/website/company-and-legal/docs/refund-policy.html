Refund Policy

A plain-language summary of Auric Artisan's Refund Policy — what is sold, the 7-day money-back window, how to ask, how refunds are paid, and why there is nothing to cancel.

Official page: https://auricartisan.com/legal/refund/ — version 4.0, effective and last updated 16 August 2026. The official policy is authoritative. The step-by-step guide is Refunds.

Key points

  • Sold through Razorpay checkout: one-time plan passes and prepaid API credit packs. Token packs are defined but not on sale; advertising bookings are arranged by hand.
  • Nothing renews automatically; a plan pass covers its term and stops. There is no subscription to cancel.
  • Every tool is free on every tier.
  • One-time plan purchases have a 7-day money-back window from the purchase date.
  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
  • A refund ends the plan benefits, or removes the tokens or API credits the purchase granted.
  • Tokens and API credits are separate, and each is refunded on its own terms.
  • Unused prepaid API credits are refundable within the same 7-day window; spent credits are not.
  • Advertising bookings are refundable in full before the campaign starts.

Section by section

  1. What is sold today — Plan passes (one month or twelve months) and prepaid API credit packs. Advertising bookings are paid through a link sent after an enquiry. All payments go through Razorpay on auricartisan.com (cards, UPI, netbanking); Auric Artisan never sees your credentials. An account is required before any payment.
  2. Pricing model — Every tool is free; paid plans add conveniences. Prices, durations and inclusions are shown at checkout.
  3. 7-day refund window
    • Plans — refunded for any reason within 7 days of purchase.
    • Token packs — within 7 days if unused; partly used packs may be refunded pro rata at Auric Artisan's discretion.
    • Prepaid API credits — within 7 days to the extent unused; spent credits are not refundable; partly used balances may be refunded pro rata. The credit ledger on your account records every grant and debit.
    • Included allowances — part of the plan price; refunded with the plan, not separately.
    • After 7 days — case by case, against the checkout terms, provider records, account status, usage and law.
  4. How to request — Use Request a refund in the Billing tab of your dashboard (the Plan & Usage section), or write through the contact page from the email address used for the purchase. Include the purchase date, what you bought and the order or payment reference.
  5. Processing and effect — Refunds go through Razorpay to the original method within 5–7 business days of approval; your bank may take longer. The entitlement ends: plan benefits are removed and the account returns to the free tier; refunded tokens or credits are deducted. Your content and projects stay yours.
  6. Advertising bookings — Full refund before the start date; after that, the undelivered portion case by case.
  7. Accidental or unauthorised charges — Contact Auric Artisan with the date, amount, reference and any receipts. Do not send full card numbers, passwords or private keys.
  8. Ending a plan, renewals and cancellation — Nothing renews, so there is nothing to cancel. A plan ends by itself and the account returns to Apprentice; every tool stays free and your work stays yours. Stopping sooner means asking for a refund. If recurring billing is ever introduced, it will be presented as such at checkout with the renewal date and amount, with a working cancel control, and the policy will be rewritten before the first such charge. Purchased tokens and API credits are not confiscated when a plan ends. Overage is prepaid, so no usage bill can follow.
  9. Contact — Through the contact page.
