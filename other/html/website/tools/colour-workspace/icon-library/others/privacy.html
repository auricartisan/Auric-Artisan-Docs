Privacy

What the Icon Library keeps in your browser and what uses the network.

Stays in your browser

Your searches, colour and stroke choices and copied code are used only in the page. ZIP files and sprites are assembled in your browser.

Remembered in your browser

In your browser's local storage on this device:

  • Your kit: the icons you selected;
  • the chosen set, style, sort, size, stroke and colour;
  • which filter groups you folded;
  • the width of the icon panel;
  • the last twelve icons you opened (Recently viewed).

Like other pages on the site, the page address may also record how far you have scrolled, after #p=, so a reload returns you there. See Shareable links.

Uses the network

  • Loading the page and its scripts.
  • The catalogue and the icon drawings load from the Auric Artisan Icon CDN at icons.auricartisan.com. No third-party icon service is involved.
  • Site-wide analytics and advertising follow your cookie choices. See the Help centre.

For what the CDN itself records, see Icon CDN privacy.
