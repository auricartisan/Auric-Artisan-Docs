Shortcuts

Keyboard shortcuts and mouse gestures in the Color Science Lab.

The single-key shortcuts work anywhere on the page when the cursor is not in a text box, list or text area, and when Ctrl, Cmd and Alt are not held. The letter shortcuts act on the Catalogue, even if another view is showing.

KeysWhat it does
/Focus the Catalogue search.
EscIn the search box: clear the search. Also closes the shortcuts overlay.
?Show or hide the keyboard shortcuts overlay.
FShow Favorites in the Catalogue.
AShow Analytics in the Catalogue.
CShow Compare in the Catalogue.
ROpen a random tool from those visible.
TTile all panels.
1 to 5Bring panel 1 to 5 (in opening order) to the front.
Shift or Ctrl + select a cardOpen another copy of a tool.
Esc inside a panelRestore a maximised panel; otherwise close the panel.
Double-click a title barMaximise or restore.
Enter in a panel HEX boxApply the typed colour.
Arrow Down, Enter or Space on a panel listOpen the list; Esc closes it.
