Glossary

Plain-language definitions of the colour-science terms used in the Color Science Lab documentation.

TermMeaning
ACESAcademy Color Encoding System, a family of colour spaces for film and visual effects (AP0, AP1, ACEScg).
Appearance modelA model that predicts how a colour looks under given viewing conditions, such as CIECAM02 or CAM16.
Bradford transformA widely used chromatic adaptation transform from the von Kries family.
CAT02, CAT16Chromatic adaptation transforms from the CIECAM02 and CAM16 appearance models.
CCTCorrelated colour temperature, in kelvin.
Chromatic adaptationThe eye's adjustment to the colour of the light, and the formulas that predict it.
Chromaticity (x, y)The colour of a stimulus apart from its brightness, plotted on the CIE 1931 diagram.
CIEDE2000 (ΔE 2000)The CIE's current formula for perceived colour difference.
CIE XYZThe tristimulus values that other colour spaces are defined from.
CIELAB / LChA perceptual space (lightness, a, b) and its polar form (lightness, chroma, hue).
DossierThe panel's detailed description of a colour space for the current sample.
GamutThe range of colours a space or device can hold.
Headroom (margin)How far a colour sits from the edge of a gamut.
IlluminantA standard light defined by its spectrum or white point, such as D65 or A.
Linked panelsPanels that copy each other's control changes.
MetamerismTwo colours that match under one light and differ under another.
Oklab / OklchA modern perceptual space and its polar form, used in CSS colour.
Planckian locusThe path of blackbody colours on the chromaticity diagram as temperature changes.
Standard observerThe CIE model of average human colour matching: 2° (1931) or 10° (1964).
Von KriesThe principle of scaling each cone response independently to model adaptation.
White pointThe colour treated as white in a space or under a light.
