Shortcuts

Keyboard and mouse shortcuts in the ASCII Art Generator.

ShortcutWhereWhat it does
Ctrl + V (Cmd + V on a Mac)Anywhere on the page, when not typing in a fieldLoads an image from the clipboard.
Drag a file onto the toolLabLoads the image.
Enter or SpaceOn a group heading in the railFolds or opens the group.
Double-click a chartAnalysisOpens the chart full screen.
EscFull-screen chartCloses it.

The full-screen view of the art closes with × only.
