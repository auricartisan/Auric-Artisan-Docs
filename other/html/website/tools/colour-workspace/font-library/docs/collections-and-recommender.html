Collections and the recommender

The six curated collections, the Find your font recommender, and the Recently viewed strip.

Three shortcuts past the alphabet: curated collections, a recommender that ranks families for a brief, and a strip of families you opened recently.

Collections

Open the Collections view. Under Featured collections (curated starting points, ranked by our computed scores) there are six rows of up to 14 families each:

CollectionDescription on the page
Popular FontsWidely-used, battle-tested families
New FontsMost recently published in the collection
Best UI FontsInterface & product type — clean, high-legibility sans
Best Accessibility FontsHighest legibility & character distinction
Best Developer FontsMonospaced, distinct, code-ready
Best Editorial FontsFor headlines & long-form reading

Each mini card shows the family in its own face with its accessibility score. Select one to open the family.

The collections are hidden while a search, a filter or the recommender is active on the Specimens view. If the Collections view looks empty, clear those first; see Troubleshooting.

Find your font: the recommender

Select Recommend in the rail (or choose Find your font from the command palette) to open ✨ Find your font above the grid: Describe the brand or drop a colour palette — we rank the catalogue by computed fit.

  1. Industry: choose one of 13, or Any / not sure. The options are SaaS / Software, Developer / DevTools, Startup / Tech, E-commerce / Retail, Luxury, Fashion / Beauty, Editorial / Publishing, Finance / Legal, Healthcare, Education / Kids, Food / Hospitality, Corporate / B2B and Creative / Agency.
  2. Brand style: select any of Modern, Classic, Elegant, Playful, Bold, Minimal, Technical and Friendly.
  3. Accessibility priority: Essential, Balanced (default) or Any. The higher the priority, the more the accessibility score counts.
  4. Colour palette (optional): paste HEX colours such as #0F172A #F8FAFC #3B82F6. Swatches appear as you type.
  5. Select Recommend fonts.

The results are twelve ranked cards, each set in its own face, with a short reason (the family's two strongest style traits, and its accessibility score if it is 80 or more) and a match score. Select a card to open the family.

A note beside the buttons explains the ranking. With a palette it reads, for example, Palette reads cool, high-contrast → leaning modern / minimal.; without one, 12 matches ranked by fit.

Reset clears your choices; ✕ closes the panel.

How it ranks

  • Each industry, brand style and palette mood adds to a target style fingerprint across eight traits: modern, classic, elegant, playful, technical, friendly, bold and minimal.
  • A family scores on how closely its own fingerprint matches, how strong it is in the role the industry needs (UI, code, editorial, readability or accessibility), and its accessibility score, weighted by your priority.
  • Families outside the categories that suit the industry are marked down.
  • The palette is read for warmth, saturation, lightness and contrast. A cool, high-contrast palette leans modern and minimal; warm, vivid colours lean playful and friendly; dark, muted colours lean elegant and classic.

The recommender ranks within a shortlist of about 130 well-known, versatile families, not the whole catalogue, so its suggestions are families you can rely on. The match score shows how well a family fits your brief compared with a perfect fit, from 40 to 99.

Recently viewed

Every family you open is added to Recently viewed, which appears under the three views; it keeps the last twelve. Selecting a name types it into the search box, so the family's card comes up in the grid. Clear empties the list.
