Privacy

What Basic Color Tools and the Colour Tools keep on your device, what they store in your browser, and what uses the network.

Basic Color Tools and the Colour Tools do their work in your browser. This page lists what stays on your device, what is remembered, and what reaches the network.

Stays on your device

  • Colours you enter or generate. Palettes, gradients, conversions and scores are calculated in the page.
  • Images. Images you choose for Palette Studio's From Image or for Collage Maker are read by your browser from your device. They are not uploaded.
  • Images and videos in the Colour Tools (for example CMYK Soft-Proof or Animation Contrast) are also processed in the browser.
  • The eyedropper. Color Picker's Pick button uses your browser's eyedropper. It samples a single pixel only when you choose one, and the page receives only that colour.
  • Clipboard. The tools write to your clipboard only when you select a copy button. They do not read your clipboard.
  • Downloads. PNG and SVG files are created in the browser and saved by your browser's download feature.

Remembered in your browser

These preferences are stored in your browser's local storage on this device. They are not sent anywhere by the tools, and clearing the site's data in your browser removes them.

Open panels and their contents are not stored. They are gone when you reload or leave the page.

Uses the network

  • Loading the page and tools. The page, its scripts and each Colour Tool are downloaded from auricartisan.com when you open them.
  • Colour Tools data. Some Colour Tools download reference data from the site the first time you open a tab that needs it, such as the Color Inspector's Names and Psychology tabs.
  • Save to Library. Items you save go to your Auric Artisan Library. How the Library stores and syncs items is described in the Library Kit documentation.
  • The rest of the site. Site-wide features such as analytics and advertising follow your cookie choices. See the Help centre and Company and legal.
