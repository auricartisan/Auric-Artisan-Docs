Workspace and panels

How the Workspace, Catalogue and Panels views work, how to find tools, and how to move, resize, dock, duplicate and close panels.

Basic Color Tools is a panel workspace. You open tools as floating windows called panels, keep several on screen, and arrange them as you like. This guide explains the three views of the page, the catalogue's filters, and every way to handle a panel.

A panel is a movable, resizable window inside the page. It holds one tool. Panels are not browser windows or tabs, so they disappear when you reload or leave the page.

The three views

The row of buttons under the page title switches between three views. The page remembers the view you used last, in your browser's local storage, and opens on it next time. The first visit opens on Workspace.

ViewUse it to
WorkspaceOpen tools from a short list and work with them on a stage.
CatalogueBrowse tool cards, search, filter by category and level, and mark favourites.
PanelsSee which panels are open and in what state, and read what each title-bar button does.

Open panels are never closed by switching views. In the Workspace view they sit inside the stage. In the Catalogue and Panels views they float over the whole page. When you return to Workspace they move back into the stage.

The Workspace view

Open a tool

The Open a tool list on the left shows the tools, grouped under their category names. The number next to the heading is how many tools the list shows.

  • Each row shows a small colour band that hints at what the tool makes, the tool's name, and an Advanced label for the three advanced tools.
  • A row reads open when that tool already has a panel.
  • Select a row to open the tool. If the tool is already open, its panel comes to the front instead, and a docked panel is restored.

The list mirrors what the Catalogue currently shows. If you searched or filtered the Catalogue (for example by level or by Favorites), the Workspace list shows the same subset. Clear the Catalogue filters to see all seven tools again.

Panels and capacity

The Panels section under the list shows how many panels are open, out of a maximum of five, as a number and a bar.

  • While there is room it reads, for example, Room for 3 more.
  • At five it reads That is the ceiling. Close or dock one before opening another.
  • Each open panel is listed with its state: Floating, Docked or Maximised. Select an entry to bring that panel back to the front, restoring it if it is docked.

The stage

The stage is the large surface on the right. Panels open inside it and cannot be dragged outside it. Maximize makes a panel fill the stage. When nothing is open the stage shows Nothing open yet. with a short explanation.

On screens narrower than about 1,100 pixels the launcher column moves above the stage.

The Catalogue view

The Catalogue is a grid of tool cards under a single filter bar.

The quick start note says: pick any tool card to open it as a movable floating panel; each panel has a controls sidebar on the left and an interactive workspace on the right; open up to five at once, drag to reposition, resize from the corner, maximize to full screen, or close with the × button. The × on the note hides it and the page remembers that choice.

With All Tools or Favorites selected, cards are grouped under category headings, each with a count such as 3 tools. The page remembers the category you chose.

Tool cards

Each card shows the category, the tool name, a colour band, a one-line summary and tags: the category, Basic or Advanced, up to three topic tags, and ● Open when the tool has a panel.

  • Select a card to open the tool, or to bring its existing panel forward.
  • Hold Shift, Ctrl or Cmd while you select a card to open another instance even if one is already open.
  • Select the star on a card to add it to Favorites or remove it. Favourites are saved in your browser's local storage.

If no card matches your search, the grid shows No basic tools match. If Favorites is empty it shows No favorites yet. with a reminder to select the star on a card.

The Open Panels category

Choose Open Panels in the category list to see a card for each open panel. A card reads Floating workspace is active or Minimized in dock, and shows Maximized when the panel is maximised. Select a card to restore and focus that panel. With nothing open it shows No panels open.

The Panels view

The Panels view has two cards.

  • Open panels lists every panel with its name, category and state (floating, docked or maximised) and a count such as 2 of 5. With nothing open it says No panels open. The Workspace tab is where you start one.
  • What the bar does explains the four title-bar buttons: Duplicate makes a second copy with its own state; Minimise parks the panel in the dock and keeps its work; Maximise gives it the whole page and back; Close frees one of the five slots.

This view is for reading. To bring a panel back, use the Workspace Panels list or the Catalogue's Open Panels category.

Working with panels

Parts of a panel

  • Title bar: a grip, the tool's name and category, and four buttons.
  • Controls sidebar on the left and main area on the right. When the panel is narrow they stack, and the panel body scrolls.
  • Resize handle in the bottom-right corner.
  • Tabs across the top of the main area in most tools. Select a tab to switch what the main area shows.

Title-bar buttons

ButtonTooltipWhat it does
DuplicateOpen another instanceOpens a second panel of the same tool with its own, separate state.
−MinimizeHides the panel and docks it. Its work is kept. The Workspace Panels list shows it as docked.
□Maximize or restoreFills the stage (Workspace view) or the browser window (other views). Select again to return to the previous size.
×CloseCloses the panel and discards its work.

Move, resize and focus

  1. Drag the title bar to move a panel. Panels cannot be dragged while maximised.
  2. Drag the bottom-right handle to resize. The smallest size is 480 by 380 pixels, or the available space if that is smaller.
  3. Double-click the title bar to maximise or restore.
  4. Select anywhere on a panel to bring it to the front. The front panel is the focused panel: keyboard shortcuts and Save to Library act on it.

Panels always stay inside the stage or the window with a small margin. If you make the browser window smaller, open panels are pulled back inside it.

The Escape key

When the keyboard focus is inside a panel, Escape restores a maximised panel to its previous size. If the panel is not maximised, Escape closes it and its work is lost. Select outside the panel first if you only want to leave a text box.

Opening the same tool twice

Selecting a tool that is already open brings the existing panel forward. To get a second copy, select the title bar's Open another instance button, or hold Shift, Ctrl or Cmd while you select the tool's card in the Catalogue. Each copy keeps its own colours and settings.

The five-panel limit

Up to five panels can be open at once. If you open a sixth, the panel that was opened earliest is closed automatically to make room, and its work is lost. Save anything you want to keep to your Library first, or close a panel yourself.

What is kept and what is not

  • Kept in your browser's local storage: your last view, level, category, favourites, and whether you dismissed the quick start note.
  • Not kept: open panels and their contents. Reloading or leaving the page closes them. To keep a result, copy or download it, or use Save to Library. See Import, export and the Library.
