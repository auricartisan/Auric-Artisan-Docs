Brand Studio

Paint any generated palette across sixteen live mockups, switch theme and density, inspect tokens, swap colours live and save the brand system.

The Studio tab applies one palette to sixteen live mockups at once, so you can judge it on real layouts rather than as swatches. It opens on the leader of the last sweep; Open in Studio on any palette card, or a row in Recent palettes on Overview, opens that palette instead.

How the palette is turned into a brand system

From the palette the Studio picks:

  • a background and text pair: the most readable pair in the palette;
  • an accent: the most colourful remaining colour, and an accent 2, the next most colourful;
  • a surface: the background mixed a little towards the accent;
  • quieter tones mixed between background and text for secondary surfaces, muted text and borders;
  • success, warning, danger and info colours: a palette colour in the green, amber, red or blue range where there is one, otherwise the accent or accent 2;
  • a type system chosen from your use case and style chips.

Choose the palette

Available Palettes lists up to 24 of the kept palettes with their names and scores. Select one to apply it to every mockup. Shuffle in the toolbar picks another at random from the same 24.

Choosing a palette clears any colours you swapped in with Test.

Test: swap colours live

Tick Test to open Test · live swap. It lists five tokens, Background, Text, Accent, Accent 2 and Surface, each with its current value and a row of the palette's colours. Select a colour in a row to assign it to that token; every mockup updates straight away, and the token shows override.

The panel header shows the current BG/FG contrast ratio, so you can see at once whether a swap keeps text readable. Reset removes all overrides.

Overrides stay while you work, but choosing another palette or selecting Shuffle clears them.

Save the brand system

Save brand system saves an item named after the palette, for example Balanced Analogous · Brand system, to your Library. It holds the palette, its score, the derived brand system, your overrides, and the theme and density you chose. See Export, save and share.

Tips

  • Use Dark and Light to check one palette works both ways before choosing it.
  • If text is hard to read in several mockups, open Test and watch the BG/FG figure while you try other background and text colours.
  • The Studio uses the same font stacks as Typography, so faces you do not have installed fall back to others. See Limits and accuracy.
