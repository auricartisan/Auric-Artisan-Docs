Glossary

Terms used in the Personalization Generator and its documentation.

TermMeaning
AccentThe most colourful colour in a palette once the background and text are chosen. Accent 2 is the next most colourful.
APCA LcA contrast value from the Accessible Perceptual Contrast Algorithm. Higher means easier to read. The generator shows its size without the sign.
BriefThe text box at the top of the left column; the same setting as Preference note in Tune. Its words are matched against reference palette names.
CandidateOne palette generated during a sweep, before it is scored.
CIEDE2000A formula for the perceived difference between two colours.
CVDColour vision deficiency. The generator simulates protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia.
EndlessA mode that runs sweep after sweep and merges the results until stopped.
LeaderThe highest-scoring palette of the last sweep. It feeds the Tokens tab and opens first in the Studio.
OKLCHA colour model of lightness, chroma and hue in which equal steps look roughly equal.
PoolYour own set of colours, up to 240. Palettes are scored on how close they stay to it.
Pool targetThe size Build fills the pool to.
PresetA starting point of five colours, two style chips and a use case.
Reference paletteOne of about 80 built-in named palettes the generator draws on, such as Clay Garden.
RelationshipThe hue pattern a palette is built on, such as Analogous or Triadic.
Semantic roleA named job for a colour: primary, secondary, accent, neutral, success, warning, danger or info.
Style chipA mood word, such as Calm or Neon, with preferred ranges of saturation, lightness, chroma, warmth and contrast.
SweepOne run: sample candidates, score them, keep the best.
Tonal rampOne colour from very light (50) to very dark (950) in eleven steps.
Use caseWhat the system is for: Ui, Brand, Poster, Editorial or Dashboard.
WCAG contrast ratioThe Web Content Accessibility Guidelines contrast measure, from 1:1 to 21:1. Body text needs 4.5:1 for AA and 7:1 for AAA; large text 3:1 for AA.
