Material Under Light glossary

Colour, light and reflectance terms used in the Material Under Light tool, each explained briefly.

TermMeaning
AzimuthThe angle between the light and the eye measured around the surface normal (Between them)
Blackbody (Planckian radiator)An ideal hot object whose light depends only on its temperature, given by Planck's law
BRDF (lobe)A model of how a surface spreads reflected light over directions
CIE 1931 2° observerThe standard colour-matching functions describing average human colour vision
CIE L*a*b*A colour space with lightness L* and opponent axes a* (red–green) and b* (yellow–blue)
CIEDE2000 (ΔE₀₀)The CIE's recommended formula for how different two colours look
ConductorA metal; its colour comes entirely from specular reflection tinted by its reflectance
Correlated colour temperature (CCT)The temperature of the blackbody whose colour is closest to a light's, in kelvin
CRI (colour rendering index)A CIE score of how faithfully a light renders a set of test colours; withdrawn from this tool
D-series daylight (D50, D55, D65, D75)CIE standard daylights reconstructed from a published basis, named for their temperature
DielectricA non-metal; a faint colourless highlight plus coloured diffuse reflection
Diffuse reflectionLight that enters a material, scatters and leaves in many directions, carrying the material's colour
Exposure normalisationScaling so a perfect white diffuser in the same light and geometry reads Y = 1
F0The reflectance of a surface seen straight on, set here by the refractive index
Illuminant AThe CIE standard for incandescent light: a blackbody at 2856 K
Illuminant EA theoretical light with equal power at every wavelength
JzAzBz (Jz)A colour space for high-dynamic-range signals; Jz is its lightness
McCamy's formulaA published approximation for computing CCT from chromaticity
MetamerismTwo materials with different spectra matching under one light but not under another
Microfacet modelA lobe that treats a surface as tiny mirror facets with a spread of tilts
Reference illuminantThe light a source is compared with: a blackbody below 5000 K, a CIE daylight at or above
Reflectance spectrumThe fraction of light a material reflects at each wavelength, from 0 to 1
Refractive index (n)How strongly a material bends light; sets a dielectric's F0
Rendering shiftThe ΔE₀₀ of one material between a source and its reference light
RoughnessHow spread out a surface's reflection is; low is glossy, high is matte
Specular reflectionMirror-like reflection from the surface itself
Spectral power distribution (SPD)A light's power at each wavelength
sRGBThe standard colour space of screens and the web
TM-30 (Rf, Rg)An IES method scoring colour fidelity (Rf) and gamut (Rg); withdrawn from this tool
Un-adapted swatchA colour shown as the light actually leaving the surface, without compensating for the light's colour
