Dye in Solution glossary

Chemistry and colour terms used in the Dye in Solution tool, each explained briefly.

TermMeaning
Absorbance (A)−log₁₀ of the fraction of light transmitted; A = 1 lets 10% through
Acid form (HA)The protonated form of a dye, holding an extra hydrogen ion
Base form (A⁻)The deprotonated form of a dye
Base fraction (f)The share of a dye in its base form at a given pH, from Henderson–Hasselbalch
Beer–Lambert lawA = ε × c × l: absorbance is proportional to molar absorptivity, concentration and path length
CIE 1931 2° observerThe standard set of colour-matching functions describing average human colour vision for small fields
CIE L*a*b*A colour space with lightness L* and opponent axes a* (red–green) and b* (yellow–blue)
CIEDE2000 (ΔE₀₀)The CIE's recommended formula for how different two colours look
Colour inconstancyHow far one sample's colour changes between two lights
Complementary wavelengthFor purples, the wavelength on the opposite side of the white point, given instead of a dominant wavelength
Concentration (c)Amount of dye per volume; shown in micromoles per litre (µM)
Dominant wavelengthThe spectral colour that, mixed with the light's white, matches the sample
Excitation purityHow far a colour lies from the white point towards the spectral edge, as a percentage
Gaussian bandA bell-shaped absorption curve; the tool draws preset bands this way
GHSThe Globally Harmonized System for classifying and labelling chemical hazards, per substance
Henderson–HasselbalchThe relation f = 1 ÷ (1 + 10^(pKa − pH)) between pH, pKa and the base fraction
IlluminantA standard light defined by its spectral power: D65, D50 (daylights), A (tungsten), E (equal energy)
Isosbestic pointThe wavelength where both forms of a dye absorb equally, so absorbance there does not change with pH
Kubelka–MunkA model of colour in opaque, light-scattering layers such as dyed cloth; not offered in the current interface
λmaxThe wavelength at which a band absorbs most
MetamerismTwo samples with different spectra that match under one light but not under another
Molar absorptivity (ε)How strongly one mole of dye absorbs at a wavelength, in M⁻¹ cm⁻¹
Path length (l)The distance light travels through the solution, in cm
pKaThe pH at which half of a dye is in each form
Safety data sheet (SDS)The supplier's document for a substance, giving its hazards (section 2) and controls (section 8)
sRGBThe standard colour space of screens and the web
Transmittance (T)The fraction of light at a wavelength that passes through the solution
UV–Vis spectrumA measurement of absorbance across ultraviolet and visible wavelengths
