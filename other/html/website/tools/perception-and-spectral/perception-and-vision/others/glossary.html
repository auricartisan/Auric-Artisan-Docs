Perception and Vision glossary

Terms used in the Perception Vision tool, each explained briefly.

TermMeaning
Acuity (Snellen)Sharpness of vision written as a fraction such as 20/40: the viewer sees at 20 feet what a typical eye sees at 40
Anomalous trichromacyColour vision with all three cone types but one of them shifted, giving weaker colour discrimination than normal
Bradford transformA published method for predicting how colours shift when the light source changes, used here to relight the scene
CataractClouding of the eye's lens, which scatters light and lowers contrast
CIEDE2000 (ΔE00)The CIE's current formula for how different two colours look; larger is more different
ClampedA colour pushed back inside the sRGB range because the simulation produced a value a screen cannot show
Cone signals (L, M, S)The responses of the long-, medium- and short-wavelength-sensitive cones
DaltonisingRecolouring an image to move information into colour differences a dichromat can see
DeutanRelating to the M (medium-wavelength) cones; deuteranopia is the absence of M cones
DichromacyColour vision with only two working cone types
Illuminant (D65, D50, A)A standard light source: D65 and D50 are daylights, A is tungsten (incandescent) light
Mesopic visionVision in twilight, when both rods and cones contribute
Neutral driftHow far the simulation pulls a grey away from grey, in code values
ProjectionThe operation that removes one colour axis, replacing the missing cone's signal with a combination of the others
ProtanRelating to the L (long-wavelength) cones; protanopia is the absence of L cones
Relative luminanceThe lightness-related quantity Y of CIE XYZ, from 0 (black) to 1 (white)
RodsPhotoreceptors that work in dim light and do not distinguish colour
ScotomaAn area of lost vision in the visual field
Scotopic visionNight vision with rods only
SeverityHow strongly a simulation is applied, from 0 (none) to 1 (full)
Spatial simulationA simulation that depends on neighbouring pixels or position (Cataract, Central loss, Acuity)
sRGBThe standard colour space of web content, with a defined transfer curve and primaries
TritanRelating to the S (short-wavelength) cones; tritanopia is the absence of S cones
WebGL2A browser graphics interface that lets the page compute on the graphics card
