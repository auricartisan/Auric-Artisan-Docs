Perception and Vision shortcuts

Keyboard and pointer actions in the Perception Vision tool.

The tool has no keyboard shortcuts of its own beyond these. Site-wide shortcuts are described in Site features.

ActionWhereResult
Press or dragThe frame, with Compare set to SplitMoves the divider to the pointer
Double-clickThe frameOpens the frame enlarged, filling the window
EscEnlarged viewCloses it
Select the × button or the dark area around the imageEnlarged viewCloses it
Enter or SpaceA focused row on the Data tabExpands or collapses the row
TabAnywhereMoves between controls; sliders then respond to the arrow keys
