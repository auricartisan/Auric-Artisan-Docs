Privacy

What Perception Vision keeps in your browser, how it treats images and the camera, and what uses the network.

What stays in your browser

  • Images you load are read by the page, drawn on a canvas and processed on your own graphics card or processor. They are not uploaded, and they are forgotten when you leave or reload the page.
  • Every simulation, readout and the colour list are computed in the page.
  • Settings files you load are read locally.

The camera

The page has a Camera source. It would process frames locally in the same way as images, but auricartisan.com currently does not allow its pages to use the camera, so the page reports it as unavailable. No camera frames are captured or sent anywhere.

What leaves the page, and only when you ask

  • Save the frame, Download the CSV and Download the settings create files through your browser's normal download. A saved frame contains your image as simulated.
  • Copy to clipboard and Copy a link to this view write to your clipboard. A link contains your settings in its address, but never your image.

The rest of the page

The tool page loads the site's usual pages, styles and scripts, and, depending on your cookie choices, advertising and consent-based analytics, like the rest of auricartisan.com. See the Privacy Policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/privacy/ and the Cookie Policy at https://auricartisan.com/legal/cookies/.
