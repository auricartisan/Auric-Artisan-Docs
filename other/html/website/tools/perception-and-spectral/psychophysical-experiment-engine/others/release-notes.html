Release notes

User-facing history of the Psychophysics Bench.

The site-wide history is at https://auricartisan.com/library/changelog/. The entries below cover what changed for users of this tool.

September 2026 — known issue

  • Only the Lab tab opens; the other five tabs, including Export, do not respond. See Troubleshooting.

September 2026 — rebuilt as the Psychophysics Bench

Changed:

  • Contrast is now Michelson contrast in relative luminance about a stated background, through the sRGB curve. Earlier versions built the grating in screen code values, so a requested 0.02 was drawn at about 0.051. Sessions recorded before this change cannot be converted to the new axis, because their exports did not record the background.
  • Spatial frequency is now in cycles per degree, from a viewing distance and pixel pitch you enter. Earlier versions used cycles across the patch.
  • Staircase steps are ratios in decibels instead of a fixed 0.05, and reversals are averaged geometrically in an even number.
  • The Bayesian and accelerated procedures are named as the tool's own rather than QUEST and PEST.
  • The stage shows the stimulus at the starting contrast between runs.

Added: Background, Viewing distance, Pixel pitch, Envelope σ in degrees, Delivered / asked, At what percentage, the Nyquist and contrast-cap warnings, and the header lines in the trials CSV.

Removed: the m-alternative forced choice, same/different and odd-one-out tasks, which could not score a trial correctly.

May 2026

  • The Psychophysical Experiment Engine User Guide was published in the site documentation.
