Website tools

Every tool on auricartisan.com, grouped by what it helps you do, with its address and a link to its documentation.

Auric Artisan's tools run in your browser at https://auricartisan.com. Most need no account: what you make is kept in your browser, and you can export it as files. Each tool below has its own folder of documentation with a quick start, task guides, a complete reference, troubleshooting and a glossary.

The tools fall into five groups. If you are not sure which one you need, start with Which tool for which job.

Accessibility and vision

Check colour contrast, audit whole websites, work through WCAG, and see colour the way people with different vision do. Group overview: Accessibility and vision.

ToolAddressWhat it does
Analyzer/tool/analyzer/Audits a website's colours, contrast and accessibility, and keeps the reports.
Contrast Checker/tool/contrast-checker/Grades a text and background pair with WCAG 2.x ratios and APCA.
Accessibility Checklist/resources/accessibility-checklist/50 WCAG 2.2 checks with the law by jurisdiction and an inspector.
Accessibility Compliance/accessibility-compliance/Where this address takes you and what it offers.
Vision Lab/tool/vision-simulation/Shows a colour, palette or image through 78 vision conditions.
Ishihara Plate Generator/tool/ishihara-plate-generation/Makes, checks and exports Ishihara-style plates.

Colour workspace

Build palettes and design systems, convert and study colours, and choose fonts and icons. Group overview: Colour workspace.

ToolAddressWhat it does
Basic Color Tools/tool/basic-tools/A panel workspace of colour tools, plus the site-wide Colour Tools.
Color Science Lab/tool/color-science-lab/Workbench, Compare, Gamut audit and Illuminants views with 45 panel tools.
Personalization Generator/tool/personalization-gen/Turns a colour pool and a brief into palettes, type, UI pairs, tokens and a brand studio.
Design System Generator/tool/design-engine/Generates, checks and exports a complete design system.
ASCII Art Generator/tool/ascii/Turns a picture or a word into character art.

Icon Library/tool/icon/Browse, customise and export icons from the Auric icon CDN.

Light sources, adaptation, gamuts, HDR and tone. Group overview: Colorimetry and rendering.

Tonal Steps/tool/general/gamut-and-rendering/tonal-steps-tints-shades/Builds tint, tone and shade ramps.
Tone Mapping/tool/general/gamut-and-rendering/tone-mapping/Compares tone-mapping operators on HDR frames.
