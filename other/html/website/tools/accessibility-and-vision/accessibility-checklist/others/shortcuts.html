Accessibility Checklist keyboard shortcuts

Every key the Accessibility Checklist responds to, and where each one works.

The single-letter keys work only while focus is inside the index (including its filter buttons and area headers) or the pane. They are ignored in text fields, when Ctrl, Alt or Cmd is held, and while the screenshot dialog is open.

Moving between checks

KeysWhereAction
Down / UpOn a rowNext or previous visible check; focus moves with it
Home / EndOn a rowFirst or last visible check
J / KIndex or paneNext or previous check. From the pane, focus stays and the new title is announced.
EIndex or paneOpen the check: the one-check view on a phone, the pane title on a wide screen
EscPaneOn a phone, back to the list; on a wide screen, back to the check's row

Marking

KeyAction
SpacePass (only on a row, the pane or the check title)
FFail
NN/A

Pressing the key for the current verdict again clears it.

Tabs

KeysAction

Printing

Ctrl + P prints the same report as Export the report.
