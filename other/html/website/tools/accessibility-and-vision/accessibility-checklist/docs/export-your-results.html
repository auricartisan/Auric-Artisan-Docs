Export your results

Use the Export tab to print your findings, copy them as Markdown for an issue tracker, or clear your work.

Open the Export tab. Its heading is "Turn the flags into a report".

What the tab shows

  • What the report will contain — four tiles with your current counts: Flagged, Passed, Not applicable and Untouched (which starts at 50).
  • Decided, by level — a bar for each of A (out of 22), AA (out of 13), AAA (out of 2) and Practice (out of 13). Here "decided" means Passed or Flagged; N/A does not count.
  • Evidence — "No screenshots attached", or how many screenshots are attached across how many checks.
  • Preview — the first lines, as they will read — the first lines of the Markdown export, exactly as it will be copied.
  • Three ways out — the three buttons below.
  • A privacy note: "Your work stays on this device."
  • Before you send it — how many checks are untouched, and a reminder that "A worked checklist is evidence of a process, not a conformance claim."

Copy as Markdown

"Straight into the tracker." Best for Jira, Linear, GitHub and similar tools.

  1. Select Copy as Markdown.
  2. The page says "The findings are on your clipboard, as Markdown."
  3. Paste into your tracker.

The Markdown always covers all 50 checks, whatever filter is on. It has this shape:

# Accessibility review

Worked 24 September 2026 · WCAG 2.2 · 15 of 50 checks decided

- Flagged: 3
- Passed: 10
- Not applicable: 2
- Never checked: 35

Source: https://auricartisan.com/resources/accessibility-checklist/

## Flagged (3)

### 2.4.7 Focus is always visible, and never removed without a replacement
Level AA · Keyboard & focus · 2 screenshots attached
outline: none with nothing in its place is the single most common accessibility bug in the wild.
> (the "What it means" paragraph)

## Never checked (35)
These were not looked at. They are not passes.
- 1.3.1 Landmarks wrap every region of the page

A worked checklist is evidence of a process, not a conformance claim.

Details worth knowing:

  • Each flagged check has its criterion and title as a heading, then its level (or "Practice"), area and screenshot count, its one-line summary, and its "What it means" paragraph as a quote. Practice checks have no criterion number.
  • Passed and not-applicable checks appear only as counts.
  • Screenshots are never included; only their number.
  • "Worked" is the date you copy, not the date you did the work.
  • The "checks decided" count includes N/A.
  • If nothing is flagged, the lists are left out and the Markdown says either that nothing is flagged but some checks were never looked at, or simply "Nothing is flagged."

If the browser refuses the clipboard, the page says "Could not reach the clipboard — the browser refused it."

Export the report (print)

The button is described as "One printable document". In the current version it opens your browser's print dialog for the checklist; it does not download a file. To get a PDF, choose Save as PDF in the print dialog.

When you press it, the page announces how many findings it is printing, or "Nothing is flagged — printing the list as it stands."

The printout contains the list of checks:

  • Flagged checks with their full explanation and a heavy left border;
  • Untouched checks collapsed to their title, tags and summary;
  • up to four small thumbnails of the marked-up screenshots per check.

Passed and N/A checks are left out of the printout, and the tab rail, meter, index and pane are not printed. There is no summary of counts on paper; use Copy as Markdown for those.

Tip: Select All 50 and switch off Open only before printing, so that a filter does not limit what prints. Pressing Ctrl + P gives the same printout as the button.

Reset

"Clear this device." This removes every mark and every screenshot from every check.

Important: Although the button's description says it asks first, in the current version Reset clears everything immediately, with no confirmation and no undo. There is no server copy to fall back on. Copy your Markdown or print first if you might need your work.

After a reset the page says "Cleared — marks and screenshots." Filters and folded areas are not changed.

What is not available

  • No CSV, JSON or file download.
  • No import or restore, so progress cannot be moved to another browser or device.
  • No notes field: only marks and screenshots are kept.

The "What a claim actually needs" link

The link at the foot of the Export tab, What a claim actually needs →, is meant to take you to the Reference tab. If it does not switch tabs, select Reference in the tab rail. See Laws by jurisdiction.
