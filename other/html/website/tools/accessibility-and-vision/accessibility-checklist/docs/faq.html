Accessibility Checklist FAQ

Short answers to common questions about the Accessibility Checklist.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

It is free, and there is no account. Nothing to install either: it works in any modern browser.

Is a finished checklist a WCAG certification?

No. The page says it plainly: "A worked checklist is evidence of a process, not a conformance claim." A claim needs a full evaluation against the whole standard, and the involvement of people who use assistive technology every day.

Does it cover all of WCAG 2.2?

It covers the ground most audits hit, in 50 checks tied to 33 WCAG criteria, plus 13 good-practice checks. It does not have a check of its own for every criterion; for example, the WCAG 2.2 additions Dragging Movements (2.5.7), Consistent Help (3.2.6), Redundant Entry (3.3.7) and Accessible Authentication (3.3.8) have no dedicated check. The Analyzer has automated rules for three of those four.

What do AUTO, PARTLY and BY HAND mean?

How much the Analyzer can decide: AUTO checks it decides on its own, PARTLY checks it narrows down for a person to confirm, and BY HAND checks only a person can judge. The checklist does not read Analyzer results; you mark every check yourself.

Which checks are required?

Level A and AA checks, 35 in all, marked Required. They are what laws and procurement documents cite. AAA and Practice checks are optional.

How do I undo a mark?

Select the same verdict again. The check goes back to Not checked yet.

Where is my work saved?

In this browser, on this device. Nothing is uploaded. It survives closing the tab, but not clearing site data, and it does not follow you to another browser or device.

Can I move my progress to another computer, or share it with a colleague?

Not as a file you can import. Share the result instead: Copy as Markdown for your issue tracker, or Export the report and save it as a PDF.

Can I add notes to a check?

There is no notes field. Use screenshots with numbered callouts, or add notes in your tracker after copying the Markdown.

Are screenshots included in the export?

The printed report shows up to four small marked-up thumbnails per check. The Markdown says how many screenshots are attached but does not include them.

Is the legal information up to date?

It was last reviewed on 11 September 2026, and the page notes that dates move. It is orientation, not legal advice; check the primary source linked from each instrument.

Why is the menu entry called Accessibility Compliance?

The site header's Learn menu lists this page as Accessibility Compliance, and the old /accessibility-compliance/ address forwards here. The page itself is titled The Accessibility Checklist.
