Accessibility Compliance

What the Accessibility Compliance address offers a visitor today, and where it takes you.

https://auricartisan.com/accessibility-compliance/ is the address of Auric Artisan's accessibility compliance resource. It no longer has a page of its own: opening it forwards you straight to the Accessibility Checklist at https://auricartisan.com/resources/accessibility-checklist/, which is where the compliance material now lives.

What a visitor gets, therefore, is the checklist: 50 WCAG 2.2 checks explained and ready to mark, the tools to record evidence and export findings, and a Reference tab on what the law asks for in 15 jurisdictions. The site header still calls this destination Accessibility Compliance in its Learn menu.

Who it is for

Anyone following an old link, a bookmark, or the Accessibility Compliance menu entry, who wants to know what WCAG and the law require and how to check a site against them.

Quick start

  1. Open https://auricartisan.com/accessibility-compliance/.
  2. Your browser moves to the Accessibility Checklist at once. If it does not, select the Accessibility Checklist link on the short "Redirecting" page.
  3. Select the Reference tab to read what the law asks for where you work.
  4. Select the Checklist tab to work through the checks.
  5. Select Run the automated pass first → to audit your page with the Analyzer.
  6. Use the Export tab to print or copy your findings.

What you can do

Everything the Accessibility Checklist offers:

  • read 50 WCAG 2.2 checks, each explained, with a test and a fix;
  • mark checks and attach annotated screenshots;
  • export your findings;
  • read the accessibility law for India, the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Japan and eight more countries.
