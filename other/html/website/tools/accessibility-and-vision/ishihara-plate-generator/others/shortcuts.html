Ishihara Plate Generator keyboard shortcuts

The keys the Ishihara Plate Generator responds to.

KeysWhereAction
Enter or SpaceA focused plateOpen it full size
Double-clickA plateOpen it full size
RightFull-size viewNext plate
LeftFull-size viewPrevious plate
EscFull-size viewClose it

All other controls are standard buttons and menus: move between them with Tab and Shift + Tab, and use them with Enter or Space.

For shortcuts that work across the whole site, see Keyboard shortcuts.
