Ishihara Plate Generator privacy

What the Ishihara Plate Generator does with your plates and settings.

  • Everything happens in your browser. Plates are generated, scored and exported on your device. The Export tab says: "Nothing leaves the browser — each file is built here and handed straight to the download."
  • Nothing about your plates is stored. Plates exist only while the page is open. The one thing remembered in this browser is your Plate Size choice.
  • Custom content stays local. A number or word you type into Custom is used only to draw the plate.
  • The ZIP builder. The first time you export a ZIP, the page loads its ZIP library from auricartisan.com. No plate data is sent.
  • The API hint. If you dismiss the "Automate this" note, that choice is remembered in this browser so the note stays hidden on every tool page.

The site-wide privacy policy is covered in Company and legal.
