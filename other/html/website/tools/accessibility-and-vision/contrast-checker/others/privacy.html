Contrast Checker privacy

What the Contrast Checker does with the colours you enter.

  • Everything runs in your browser. The ratio, the grades and the fix are calculated on your device. The colours you enter are not sent anywhere.
  • Nothing is stored. There is no history, no saved pairs and no account state. The only record of a pair is the page address, which your browser keeps in its own history like any other address.
  • Shared links contain the colours. A copied link carries both colours in plain text in the address. Anyone with the link can see them.
  • The API hint. If you dismiss the "Automate this" note, that choice is remembered in this browser so the note stays hidden on every tool page.

The site-wide privacy policy is covered in Company and legal.
