Analyzer keyboard shortcuts

The keys the Analyzer responds to, in the URL field, the Sections picker, the workspace panels and the token meter.

The Analyzer can be used entirely from the keyboard. Tab and Shift + Tab move between controls as usual; the keys below are the Analyzer's own.

Anywhere on the Analyzer page

KeysWhat they do
Ctrl + POpen the Sections picker, in Simple or Advanced; replaces the print shortcut on this page

In the URL field

KeysWhat they do
EnterStart the audit, the same as Analyze

In the Sections picker

KeysWhat they do
Type any textFilter the sections by name
Up / DownMove to the row above or below, as the rows are laid out
Left / RightMove across columns, when the filter field is empty
EnterOpen the highlighted section (or scroll to it) and close the picker
SpaceOpen or close the highlighted section and keep the picker open
EscClose the picker

On a panel

KeysWhat they do
Left / Right on the panel gripMove the panel one place earlier or later in the workspace
Enter or Space on Width, Focus, Collapse, CloseUse that button

On the token meter

KeysWhat they do
Enter or SpaceOpen the token sheet

Disclosures and cards

Folding cards and disclosures, such as Why this score?, Cause chain and the A11y+ cards, open and close with Enter or Space.

For shortcuts that work across the whole site, see Keyboard shortcuts.
