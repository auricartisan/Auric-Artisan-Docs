Tone Mapping limits and accuracy

What the Tone Mapping lab implements exactly, what is its own, and what it does not do.

Exact to the source

  • Transfer functions: the sRGB curve, PQ (SMPTE ST 2084, constants as the standard gives them) and HLG (the OETF and its inverse; the OOTF is not applied).
  • Primaries and white points of Rec.709, Display P3, Rec.2020 and ACEScg.
  • Bradford adaptation between white points.
  • Operators with their published constants: Reinhard (2002), Hable (2010), Narkowicz (2016) and Uchimura (2017).

This tool's own

  • Log (soft) and the custom curve.
  • The grade controls, the low-level operators and the gamut mapping options.
  • Working luminance is read from each working space's own matrix, so saturation and vibrance mix towards the right grey.

Not implemented

  • ITU-R BT.2446 Methods A and C. The two operators once named after them are kept as "Gamma-domain scale" and "Scaled Reinhard" and are not tone curves.
  • The Academy's ACES transforms (RRT and ODT). The Narkowicz fit approximates their look.
  • HDR image files such as OpenEXR. Uploaded images are 8-bit and carry no absolute luminance.

Current-version caveats

  • The operators work per channel, not on luminance, so strongly saturated highlights can shift hue.
  • Soft compress gives the same result as Clip.
  • Display peak and Source peak affect only the two retired operators and the false-colour display; they do not scale the working curves or the PQ output.
  • With PQ output, display value 1.0 is encoded as 10,000 cd/m².
  • The false-colour map multiplies source luma by the display peak, while its note uses the source peak.
  • The link records only operator, exposure, source encoding, display peak, output encoding and target primaries.
  • The Linear axis, Log axis and Film stops buttons set custom-curve presets rather than changing the chart's axis.
  • Install a table on the Data tab does not load files.

Precision

  • The curve .cube has 33 entries; the pipeline .cube is 17 × 17 × 17. Programs interpolate between entries, so very steep curves lose some detail.
  • Output is 8-bit per channel.
