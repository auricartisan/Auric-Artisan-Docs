Tonal Steps shortcuts

Keyboard shortcuts in Tonal Steps, Tints and Shades.

These keys work when the focus is not in a text box, slider or list.

KeyWhat it does
1Ramp engine: HSL lightness
2Ramp engine: Equal luminance
3Ramp engine: Equal contrast (gives the same colours as Equal luminance)
RRandom base colour
EscCloses a full-screen chart
MouseWhat it does
Double-click a chartOpens it full screen
Hover a swatch in The other setsShows its HEX, luminance Y and contrast against black
