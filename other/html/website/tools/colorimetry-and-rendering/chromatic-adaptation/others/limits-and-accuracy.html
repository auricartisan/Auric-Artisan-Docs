Chromatic Adaptation limits and accuracy

What the Chromatic Adaptation lab computes exactly, what it approximates, and what it does not do.

The lab is an educational and research simulator that runs in your browser. It is not a substitute for measured device profiles, calibrated displays, spectrophotometer data or a validated ICC workflow.

What is exact

  • White points of all 12 illuminants are tabulated CIE xy values, held verbatim. Adaptation needs only these, so it is correct even where a spectrum is not held.
  • Transform matrices are the published values; each inverse is recomputed, and the Matrix tab shows the round-trip error.
  • sRGB conversion uses the IEC 61966-2-1 primaries and transfer function. Adaptation happens in linear light; the transfer function is applied only at the ends.
  • ΔE₀₀ is the CIEDE2000 formula (CIE 142:2001).

What is modelled or simplified

  • Time course. The two-phase model uses constants from Fairchild and Reniff (1995). It describes average behaviour in their experiments, not any particular viewer or scene.
  • Degree of adaptation is yours to set. CIECAM02 would compute it from the adapting luminance and the surround; the lab does not.
  • Clipping. Adapted colours outside sRGB are clipped per channel (or compressed first with HDR Tonemap). This is not perceptual gamut mapping. For that, see Gamut Mapping.
  • Spectra. The daylight illuminants are reconstructed from the CIE daylight basis; A is computed from Planck's law. B, C, F2, F7 and F11 are not drawn because their tables are not held.

What is not done

  • Ranking transforms by accuracy. That needs corresponding-colour data sets, which are not held. The comparisons report what each transform does, measured against Bradford or as a colour shift, and say so.
  • Spatial or per-pixel adaptation. The Spatial Adaptation and Per-Pixel Modulation controls do not affect the result in the current version, nor do Display Gamma, Target FPS or GPU.
  • Colour appearance modelling. Only the adaptation step is applied; lightness, colourfulness and hue correlates of CIECAM02 or CAM16 are not computed.
  • The 10° observer. Not held.

Precision

  • Images are processed at up to 480 × 320 pixels, in 8-bit sRGB.
  • Batch results round ΔE₀₀ to two decimals and L*, C* and h° to one.
  • The Lab timeline covers 0 to 5 seconds only; the Dynamics tab covers 0 to 120 seconds.
