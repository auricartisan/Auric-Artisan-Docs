HDR Gamut Plot glossary

Plain-language definitions of the HDR and colour terms used in the HDR Gamut Plot.

Absolute (curve) — a transfer curve whose output is a stated luminance, independent of the display. PQ is absolute.

BT.1886 — the ITU-R reference EOTF for standard-dynamic-range flat-panel displays; with zero black level it is a 2.4 power law.

BT.2020 (Rec. 2020) — the ultra-high-definition television standard, whose wide primaries HDR video usually uses.

BT.2100 — the ITU-R standard for HDR television. It defines PQ and HLG, the HLG system gamma and the ICtCp colour space.

cd/m² (nit) — the unit of luminance.

Display-referred (curve) — a curve whose output depends on the display's peak luminance. HLG and gamma curves are display-referred.

EOTF — electro-optical transfer function: signal to light.

HDR (high dynamic range) — images and displays with a much wider luminance range than standard video.

HLG (hybrid log-gamma) — a relative HDR transfer function (ARIB STD-B67), combining a square-root segment for darks with a logarithmic segment for highlights.

ICtCp — an HDR colour space from BT.2100: I (intensity), Ct (blue–yellow) and Cp (red–green), built on PQ-encoded cone responses.

Jzazbz — a perceptually uniform colour space for HDR and wide gamut (Safdar et al., 2017), with its own PQ-like quantiser.

Line of purples — the straight edge of the chromaticity diagram joining violet and red.

Locus share — a gamut triangle's area as a percentage of the spectral locus area, in the same plane.

Occupancy — how much of a gamut's area an image's chromaticities cover.

OETF — opto-electronic transfer function: light to signal.

OOTF — opto-optical transfer function: the rendering from scene light to display light, including HLG's system gamma.

Peak brightness (L_W) — the maximum luminance of the display the curves are rendered for.

PQ (perceptual quantiser) — the SMPTE ST 2084 transfer function, mapping signal 0–1 to 0–10,000 cd/m² with steps close to the threshold of visibility.

Signal level — a code value from 0 to 1, as carried in the video signal.

Spectral locus — the curved edge of the chromaticity diagram, made by single wavelengths.

System gamma (γsys) — the exponent of HLG's OOTF, 1.2 at 1,000 nits.

Tone mapping — compressing a wide luminance range into what a display can show.

u′v′ — the CIE 1976 uniform chromaticity coordinates.

xy — the CIE 1931 chromaticity coordinates.
