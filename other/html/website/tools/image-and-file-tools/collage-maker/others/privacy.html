Privacy

What the Collage Maker does with your pictures, and what stays in your browser.

Your pictures stay in your browser

  • Pictures are read by the page itself and drawn straight to a canvas in your browser. The tool does not upload them or send them anywhere.
  • The measurements, the arrangement comparison and the exports are all worked out on your device.
  • Exported files (PNG, JPEG, session and reading) are created in the browser and saved by your browser's normal download.
  • The clipboard is used only when you select Copy to the clipboard, and only to place the PNG there.

Nothing is kept after you leave

The tool does not store your pictures, your layout or your history. Reloading or leaving the page clears them. To keep a layout, save a session file; to keep the picture, save a PNG or JPEG.

What a session file contains

A session file contains the canvas settings and, for each picture, its file name, position, size, scale, rotation, opacity, flips and visibility. It contains no image data. If you share a session file, the recipient sees your picture file names.

The page itself

The Collage Maker is a normal page on auricartisan.com. Site-wide features such as sign-in, preferences and any consent-based services follow the site's own privacy policy and your cookie choices; they are covered in the site's help pages at Help.
