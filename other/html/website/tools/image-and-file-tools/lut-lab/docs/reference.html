Looks tab

Each card: name, Stylisation or Transform badge, an 18-colour before and after strip, a description, mean ΔE₀₀, max hue and clipped on the hue ring, and Show it on the pair.

LookKindmean ΔE₀₀ on the ringmax hueclipped
Teal and orangeStylisation1.2+3.5°0/72
Bleach bypassStylisation7.9−12.9°0/72
Film softStylisation1.9−7.7°0/72
Mono contrastStylisation22.6+177.8°0/72
Log-ish liftStylisation16.9−25.2°0/72
Reinhard tone-mapTransform6.6−25.1°0/72

These are the figures at full strength, and for the tone-map at the default exposure of 2×. Mono contrast removes colour entirely, so its hue figure has no useful meaning.

Withdrawn: ACEScct to ACEScg and Export 32-bit float samples, each with why and what it would take, and a table of the ACEScct decode for nine input codes.

Method tab

SectionContents
Sample orderThe index order, and the eight rows of a 2 × 2 × 2 identity
InterpolationThe seven blends of trilinear interpolation; worst identity error at sizes 2, 5, 9, 17, 33 and 65
ClippingSeven test pixels judged by the old test and the clip test
Round tripWrite-and-read-back difference at 17 and 33 of the current transform; identity accuracy at 16, 17, 18, 33, 52 and 65
Linear lightThe tone-map at six input codes, in linear light and in the old placement

Data tab

Status buttons: All (17), Verbatim (5), Computed (5), Stand-in (4), Absent (3). Select an entry to expand it.

StatusEntries
VerbatimThe .cube format, sRGB, CIE 1931 observer and CIELAB, CIEDE2000, Reinhard tone reproduction
ComputedTrilinear interpolation, clipping detection, the hue sweep, what a transform moves, the export round trip
Stand-inThe five stylisations, the 8-bit path, the tone-map exposure, the drawn scenes
AbsentACEScct to ACEScg, 32-bit float export, PQ, BT.2020 and the ACES primaries

Loading your own picture changes the scenes entry to Verbatim, naming your file.
