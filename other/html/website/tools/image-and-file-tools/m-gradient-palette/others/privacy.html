Privacy

What the M-Gradient Palette does with your colours and settings.

Everything runs in your browser

  • The ramp, the measurements, the route comparison, the contrast checks and the colour-vision views are all worked out on your device.
  • The tool sends nothing to a server.
  • Save the SVG and Save the reading create their files in the browser and save them through your browser's normal download.
  • The clipboard is written only when you select a copy button or a step of the ramp.

Nothing is kept after you leave

Your colours and settings are not stored. Reloading or leaving the page returns to the default ramp. To keep a ramp, copy the custom properties or save the reading.

The page itself

The M-Gradient Palette is a normal page on auricartisan.com. Site-wide features such as sign-in, preferences and any consent-based services follow the site's own privacy policy and your cookie choices; see Help.
