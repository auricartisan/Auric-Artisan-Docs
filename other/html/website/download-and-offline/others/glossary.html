Download and offline glossary

Terms used in the download and offline guides.

TermMeaning
PWAProgressive web app: a website a browser can install like an app and run offline.
InstallAdding the site to your device as an app with its own window and icon.
StandaloneRunning in its own window without the browser's toolbar.
Service workerA part of the site that runs in your browser and serves stored files when you are offline.
CacheThe browser's store of site files kept for offline use.
App shellThe small core of pages, styles and scripts stored automatically on your first visit.
Offline profileHow much of the site to store: Auto, Lite, Balanced or Full.
Persistent storageBrowser storage that is not cleared automatically when space runs low.
Best-effort storageBrowser storage that the browser may clear when space runs low.
QuotaThe amount of storage the browser allows the site to use.
AuditA check of which files of your profile are stored and which are missing.
Background syncA browser feature that finishes a task, such as a download, when you are next online.
Share targetAn app that appears in your device's share menu.
