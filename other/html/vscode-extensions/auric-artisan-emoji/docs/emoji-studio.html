Use Emoji Studio

Search, browse and insert with Emoji Studio: views, categories, skin tones, quick filters, layouts, cards, suggestions and the Workspace overview.

Search

Type in Search (placeholder "Search 1,914 emoji — name, mood, :shortcode: or U+1F680"). Press / or Ctrl + K (Cmd + K) to focus it.

You can search by:

  • name or keyword, such as developer or thanks;
  • an exact emoji;
  • a shortcode, such as :rocket:;
  • a code point, such as U+1F680;
  • common Hindi words, such as दिल, रॉकेट or धन्यवाद.

Views

TabShows
01 LibraryEvery emoji matching the search and filters.
02 For youFavorites first, then the emoji you use most, then curated picks.
03 FavoritesEmoji you starred. Press F or the heart on a card.
04 RecentEverything you inserted or copied, most recent first.

In the narrow sidebar, Favorites and Recent buttons at the bottom switch views, and Back to library returns.

Filters

FilterOptions
CategoryAll or one of nine: Smileys & Emotion, People & Body, Animals & Nature, Food & Drink, Travel & Places, Activities, Objects, Symbols, Flags. More / Fewer expands the chip list.
Skin toneYellow (standard) or Light, Medium light, Medium, Medium dark, Dark. A note shows how many emoji it applies to.
Quick filter (full Studio)All, Curated, Skin tone (tone-capable emoji only), Newest.
Density (full Studio)Compact, Comfortable, Showcase.

Clear filters resets search and filters. The results line reads, for example, "42 emoji for "heart"".

Layout

In the full Studio, choose Grid or List. Cards load as you scroll ("144 of 1,914 loaded — keep scrolling"); use Load more if it appears. auricEmoji.showNames shows emoji names on cards when there is room.

Cards

Each card shows the emoji and, space permitting, its name. On hover or keyboard focus (always visible in the sidebar and on touch screens):

ActionWhat it does
InsertInserts into the active or last active editor, even if your click action is Copy.
CopyCopies in your copy format.
…Opens the inspector.
HeartAdds to or removes from favorites.

Selecting the emoji itself (or pressing Enter on it) runs your click action (auricEmoji.clickAction): Insert (default), Copy, or Insert and copy. Shift + Enter copies.

Keyboard in the grid

KeyAction
Arrow keysMove between cards
EnterClick action
Shift + EnterCopy
SpaceAdd to the sequence
Backspace (outside text boxes)Remove the last sequence item
FFavorite or unfavorite
IOpen or close the inspector
EscapeClose the open panel, then clear the sequence
?Open the keyboard map

Tab reaches each card's buttons. Text boxes keep their normal editing keys.

Suggested for you

The Suggested for you rail (headed "For {file}" when a file is open) lists emoji based on the file and your recent use, each with a reason such as In favorites, Used 3× or Curated. Select Refresh for new suggestions.

Workspace overview

Expand Workspace overview · active file & quick tools:

PartContents
Hero"Every emoji. Exactly where you type." with Quick insert, Replace shortcodes and Keyboard map →.
Active fileThe target file and language, line and cursor count, skin tone, completion state and click action.
Active file buttonsInsert {emoji} again, Replace :shortcodes: in selection, Clear history.
MetricsEmoji (with category count), Skin-tone ready, Favorites (kept on this machine only), Recent (ranked by use and recency).

Clear history removes recent emoji and last-emoji recall. Favorites are kept.

Studio settings

  • Customize Emoji Studio (gear) and Settings in the footer open Settings filtered to this extension.
  • auricEmoji.closeStudioAfterInsert closes the full Studio after a successful insertion.
  • auricEmoji.gridDensity sets the default density.
  • auricEmoji.statusBar.enabled shows or hides the Emoji status bar button.

Result

You can find any emoji quickly, in your tone, and insert or copy it with one click or key.
