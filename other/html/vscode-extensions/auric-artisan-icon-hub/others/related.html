Icon Hub or Icon Studio?

You want toUse
Recolour, restroke and resize icons with a full colour pickerIcon Hub
Export an SVG sprite or a ZIP with licencesIcon Hub
Load icons on your site from the Auric CDNIcon Hub
Browse Auric Modern, Drawn and Playful artwork and emoji tooIcon Studio
Work with no network access at allIcon Studio (Icon Hub also works offline if you turn off metadata checks and avoid CDN output)

The two keep separate settings, views, favorites and recents. Icon Hub keeps its own view even when Icon Studio hosts the Auric collections.
